# Shigatsu Youka — jumpscare-free reader

**Contact:** [afrotankrob@gmail.com](mailto:afrotankrob@gmail.com)

## Important: a rough reader with known mistakes

I made this mainly for myself, to get the general story without the scares. It is **not a faithful recreation of the game**. Reported problems include extra or mismatched scene text and scenes or links that do not work. **I do not plan to fix every reader issue.** For the intended scene order, choices and presentation, use the English-patched game.

A static web page that shows the free LiveMaker visual novel 死月妖花～四月八日～ (Shigatsu Youka, by New++) as still pictures and text: one row per text box, next to a reconstructed screen. English (unofficial fan translation) and Japanese. Its approximate scene unlocks and story chart are based on the game's navigator; they do not reproduce every game branch or condition.

Read it here: https://nostramadeus.github.io/shigatsu-youka-reader/

- No sound, no timing or screen shakes; the optional encyclopedia screamer is omitted. Other still images can still be disturbing.
- Scenes with a branch or a hidden route start with a small "Play options" panel, like the game's scenario navigator: read from the start, from just before the branch, the other branch, or the hidden route. An option opens by the game's rule, approximated from the scenes you marked read; until then it is greyed with the reason. Other scenes follow the normal route.
- Progress is kept in your browser only (export / import buttons on the scene list).
- The translation and the playable English patch live at https://github.com/Nostramadeus/shigatsu-youka-english-translation
- The game itself: https://www.freem.ne.jp/win/game/19917

Unofficial fan project. All pictures and text belong to the game's author. Author request for removal: open an issue, it will be taken down.

Everything in this repository is generated (`en/`, `jp/`, `img/`); edits belong in the translation repository.
