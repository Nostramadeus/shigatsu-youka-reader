Shiyo 737.
In a certain small town, it happened.
Sawa Prefecture, Sawa District, Motoki-cho―a small municipality whose population at the time was under 2,000.
In March of Shiyo 737, in this town, a certain uncanny phenomenon is said to have occurred.
The one who found it was a then-resident of Motoki-cho, Katsurako Kanai-san.
In that season, when the cherry blossoms were starting to bloom, Kanai-san found something.
video: 桜.wmv
"Was there ever a cherry tree in a place like this...?"
What Kanai-san had found was a large, old cherry tree.
Larger by far than the other cherry trees, and giving off a dignity beyond them by far: its divine air.
However, Kanai-san was seized by a strange feeling.
video: 桜.wmv
Because, until then, she had never seen a cherry tree like this.
Kanai-san passed along this road nearly every day, going to draw water at a nearby well.
In other words, to Kanai-san, this cherry tree had suddenly appeared.
At once, Kanai-san called the neighboring residents.
video: 桜.wmv
"Hm... was there a tree like this...?"
"Kanai-san's the only one who comes along this road every day..."
"If you say it was here, I feel like it was, and if you say it wasn't, I feel like it wasn't..."
video: 桜.wmv
At the time, Kanai-san lived alone, and the only one who came along this road every day was Kanai-san.
For that reason, the other residents' memories were vague too.
video: 桜.wmv
"But, well, if a cherry tree this magnificent suddenly appeared, shouldn't we treat it as a god and enshrine it?"
"True, it's too magnificent to just leave as it is."
The residents, all saying so, one after another.
They decided to enshrine this cherry tree as the goshinboku of Motoki-cho, and in time it became the symbol of Motoki-cho today.
video: 桜.wmv
And now, the present day―
video: 桜.wmv
The goshinboku is still enshrined in Motoki-cho's Central Park, and perhaps partly thanks to it, Motoki-cho has become one of the most famous cherry-blossom spots in the country.
video: 桜.wmv
But why did the goshinboku suddenly grow in this place?
Was Kanai-san misremembering, or is there a supernatural phenomenon behind it, beyond even our imagining?
Paying no heed to our questions, the goshinboku continues to watch over Motoki-cho even now.
