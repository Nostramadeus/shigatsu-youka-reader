Shiyo 800, April 8, morning, the Central Park murder: record of the statement of its victim Ryoji Kogori-san's eldest daughter, Natsumi Kogori-san (hereafter written as 'the girl').
The victim showed nothing particularly unusual the day before, and when the victim disappeared, she had been told by her mother, Akane Kogori-san, that he was going to Hokkaido on a business trip.
The girl's involvement in this case is extremely unlikely.
However, whether the girl's mother truly believed he was going on a business trip remains in question.
The girl's mental state: good.
She is to go home for now and, if necessary, will be asked to answer questions again at a later date.
