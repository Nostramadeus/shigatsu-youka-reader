First, we would like you to take a look at this.
Pay particular attention to the sound.
The video you just saw is nighttime footage shot in a certain city in Japan.
The video contains footage of a residential area, a man's voice, and a strange roar echoing through it all.
According to the poster, there was nothing near them that could have been the source of this sound.
In fact, this sound is not limited to Japan.
In recent years, around the world, there has been one report after another of a roar like grinding metal, or like a low trumpet blast, ringing out over a whole area.
Air currents in underground caverns, the sound of meteors, air currents high in the sky: many causes have been suggested, but no clear cause is known.
This sound has been given a name.
―The Apocalyptic Sound.
video: 悪天候.wmv
The apocalyptic sound is also said to be a prophecy of destruction.
Each time the trumpet sounds, cataclysms occur, driving humanity toward extinction.
The concept of the apocalyptic sound has been documented since about 2,000 years ago, and it has a religious aspect.
Religious scholar Kevin White puts it this way.
"Religion, along with agriculture, is an element at the very root of human culture. In other words, it's no exaggeration to say culture equals religion. Why could religion become such an element? Because in the past, science was not as developed as it is today. When people encountered something mysterious whose cause they didn't understand, they couldn't help feeling uneasy. Religion was needed to explain it."
"What happens to people when they die? Why does the sun rise? Why does night come? Why do storms happen? And why do sounds like the apocalyptic sound ring out? From familiar things like these to the very rare, people explained them through religion. And when many people believe in that religion, group psychology increases the credibility of the explanation. This is one of the reasons a particular religion spreads widely."
"In other words, this gives us a hypothesis. That is: 'The occurrence of the apocalyptic sound will bring about the destruction of humanity' ―the apocalyptic sound was being observed as long as 2,000 years ago. We can say that people, seeking to explain that eerie, inexplicable sound, turned to religion for salvation."

According to Kevin White, the apocalyptic sound is not something observed only recently; its existence has been recognized for as long as 2,000 years.
Today we live in an information society, where information from all over the world is instantly available.
Phenomena like the apocalyptic sound can also be recorded as video and shared with people around the world.
That is why people all over the world today can know of the apocalyptic sound's existence.
"Until now, science has explained many mysterious phenomena. Surely the apocalyptic sound, too, will turn out to be caused by some natural phenomenon or by a phenomenon of human perception. Once the apocalyptic sound is explained, that video will probably become something people laugh about."
