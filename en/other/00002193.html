We left the child with my mother-in-law, and the two of us talked about the Green-Eye Curse-Killing.
Never before had such tension run between Ko-san and me.
"Kikyo, was that in the storehouse's books?"
Ko-san asked.
"Yes. A number of alarming things were written there..."
"............"
Just as I thought, Ko-san knew something.
He raised his head, and his eyes wandered as though his gaze were writing out the words, Where should I even begin?
Then Ko-san sighed once.
"Kikyo, I want you to tell me how much you know. We've only heard about it by word of mouth. We've never checked it in the storehouse's books."
"Understood."
At that, we both straightened up.
The tension in the room rose even further.
"That a girl born with green eyes is to be killed at once. That's what was written in that book. Apparently it's because she could become a threat to the very existence of the settlement."
"............ I see... I know about that too. A child born with green eyes is killed right away― since I was born, it's happened only once. But I don't know why they're killed."
"Because of the law?"
"Yeah..."
Because it is the law, a newborn baby is killed.
What a cruel law that is, an ordinary person would think.
But I see it the other way around.
The law is not there to bind people.
It is to protect people that the law exists.
If that child had simply grown up―
this settlement might have been struck by a calamity like those of the past.
