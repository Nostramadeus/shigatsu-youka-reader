―Ko-sama did nothing but watch the flow of the river.
I followed his lead and watched the flow of the river too.
"Kikyo-dono, did you know? 　A river flows like this, and you can't go against its flow. But the river joins the sea, and then it flows on toward unknown lands. Nature is very free. Rain and wind often bring disaster on people. But from nature's point of view, that's only because it moved freely. Unswayed by human will or custom, it always acts as it pleases, freely."
"Human beings can't live going against nature. Before nature's freedom, people are powerless. But..."
Ko-sama turned those eyes of his to me.
"But it's precisely in the midst of it that human beings can live strongly. Human beings have always suffered before nature's freedom and harshness. But at the same time, we're kept alive by nature's bounty. We live within nature. That's why I think human beings, too, should be freer."
"Nature is humanity's teacher. We are taught by nature, we learn from nature, and we live together with nature. Wanting to learn, and sending your dreams out toward the future. Isn't having that freedom taken away the same as going against nature?"
"Ko-sama..."
"I want you, Kikyo-dono, to live more freely. To let something like custom crush the passion and dreams you were born with... Don't you think that, of all things, is wicked?"
"............ Is it? I don't really know..."
"If you don't mind my asking... What sort of person is the one you're marrying?"
"............ I don't know..."
"Huh?"
"I don't know him. I'm to meet him for the first time on the day of the wedding."
"Is that so..."
"That's custom. Marrying the person your parents chose is a normal thing."
"But...
But don't you think that's wrong!?"
A shout from Ko-sama, so loud the sound of the river fell away.
My eyes and my ears were wholly taken by Ko-sama.
"Marriage... is that what it is?"
"............"
"I'm sorry... for butting into something like that..."
Ko-sama turned his eyes to the river again.
"Kikyo-dono, what is it you wish for?"
"What I wish for...?"
"Kikyo-dono, how do you want to live?"
"............ Is there any meaning in wishing for a way to live?"
"There is."
Ko-sama declared it flatly.
"If you give up, there's no more hope and no more future. But... But if you don't give up, then surely..."
"............ No, Ko-sama, that's not right."
"Huh?"
"Dreaming is certainly important. But what's more important is... taking action to make the dream come true."
"Dreaming is something anyone can do. But without effort or action, in the end it's just a pipe dream. All I can do now... is live inside that pipe dream. On my own, there's nothing I can do about it."
"............"
"Ko-sama, what do you think? 　What do you think I should do, to keep chasing my dream?"
"That's..."
"Ko-sama, you are irresponsible. Nothing but ideals, without even knowing what I should do. This is the law of this age and this place. By my will alone, I can't do anything about it."
"Then...!"
Ko-sama stood up and looked down at me.
"Then come to the Arata settlement."
