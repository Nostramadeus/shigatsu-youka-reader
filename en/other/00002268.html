Thank you very much for playing Shigatsu Youka ~April 8~ through to the end.
I am Satsuki of the creative circle New++.
It was a very long novel game, and that you played it to the end is the greatest reward an author could ask for.
Originally, I started making this work purely on a whim.
Somehow I felt like making a game of some kind, and thought: well then, with my skills, what could I make?
When I started, I knew nothing of creation tools, or even the very concept of a free game, and I began making it in PowerPoint.
Gradually I learned about creation tools and free games, thought about the UI design, the presentation and how to move things along, and about a month into production the prototype of the present game was finished.
In February 2016 I released a prototype as 'The Witch of April 8', and since it was fairly well received, I kept the system and the flow of the scenario as they were and pushed on to completion.
However, since I started without giving any thought at all to what kind of world or scenario it would have, I myself could not imagine what shape this world of Shigatsu Youka would take.
'I hate cherry blossoms'
That phrasing no longer survives, but I wrote this one line first, the first thing that popped into my head, and added the scenario bit by bit from there.
To be honest I meant to finish it casually in about three months, but before I knew it three and a half years had passed, and it had become a monster of a work beyond anything I imagined at the start.
How I managed to make such a long work is a real mystery, even to me.
Also, I truly intended to complete Shigatsu Youka with the fifth arc, the Saigo no Koe Arc [Last Voice].
That is the arc where Haruka Niimura's grandmother Erika dies and Haruka wavers over whether to live in the Arata settlement.
I thought that would make for a crisp, clean ending, but―
When I thought it over, though, ending on 'and then she heard the voices of the dead' felt...
After denying the supernatural at every turn up to that point, closing on that was a bit...
I could not accept it myself.
So I made major changes to the scenario as a whole.
・The addition of the True Jusatsu Arc [Killing by Curse], the True Meicho Arc [Clear Sign], the Eden no Sakura Arc [Eden's Cherry] and the Eigo Kaiki Arc [Eternal Recurrence]
・The addition of the Droga infection setting
・The setting of Shigatsu Youka as the mastermind
・The backstage of the Megasawa City case
And so on: I changed it from a vague, mysterious tale to an SF-flavored world.
Until I made the fifth arc, then, I had no mastermind called Shigatsu Youka in mind at all.
Incidentally, the word 'Shigatsu Youka' (死月妖花) was born from a typing mis-conversion of 'Shigatsu Youka' (四月八日, April 8).
That was when I thought, "Ah, what if I made Shigatsu Youka a variety of cherry tree?", and at last arrived at the world as it is now.
Seeing it this way, I think I really was making things up as I went along.
Well, as the author, I got to make it however I felt like at the time, so it was fun.
It was almost all solo work, too.
The truth is, I myself am really bad with print, the type who hesitates even to read manga...
And yet the reason I made a novel game was simply 'because it looked easy to make.'
So, hoping for something that would not be painful to read even for me and that had at least a little gameplay to it, I ended up with this kind of system.
Chopping the text into pieces like the Scenario Navigator, building a character-count system so you can tell how much is left, making clickable spots to hunt for like the References.
Though going as far as building a jigsaw puzzle like the report reference was, I cannot deny, a bit much...
It was a lot of fun while I was making it, though.
I would like to close the afterword here, but a word about this game.
The truth is, one important matter has been left unresolved.
'Shigatsu Youka ~April 8~' is a very long novel game, with strong suspense and SF elements, so understanding all of its settings and mysteries is no easy task.
From here on, I will touch on that a little.
Do you remember how the Megasawa City case came about?
The sequence was:
・Chigaya Niimura has her first period and develops the Green-Eye Curse-Killing.
・They arrive at falsificaso as the cure, but doubt whether it is really safe to administer to a human body.
・Therefore they send Droga and falsificaso to Mifuyu Niimura to test their toxicity.
That is how it came about.
Did you notice the decisive contradiction here?
As you can see from 'The Shinigami's Rule' in the Wabigoto Arc [Apology], there is no falsificaso.
The falsificaso never reached Mifuyu Niimura in Megasawa City.
That defeats the purpose of treating Chigaya Niimura.
Why did this happen?
Of course I have the setting and the flow of this in my head, but a certain person is involved.
Yes, that person who appeared by name alone, with no sprite and no lines.
That person, as of Shiyo 800, surely―
A small hint, that.
Making it would have taken an enormous amount of time again, so this time I decided to let it go.
Dragging things on any longer would not be good, either.
If many voices ask to see this unreleased part, I might take it up, though...
'Shigatsu Youka ~April 8~' came together in this rather haphazard mood, but if You-sama enjoyed it even a little, nothing could make me happier.
Thank you so very much.
My thanks as well to everyone who helped with test play and provided the wonderful images, sounds and other materials.
Now, for everyone who has supported 'Shigatsu Youka ~April 8~', I have prepared a small present.
I would be glad if you watched the video that follows.
I ask for your continued support.

April 8, 2024
New++　Satsuki
