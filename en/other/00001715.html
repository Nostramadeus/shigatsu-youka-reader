15 boxes · not part of the scene list (menus, tutorial, system)

A single DVD.
This is top-secret material obtained from a certain informed source.
Surveillance camera footage of two rooms.
These two videos show different rooms filmed at the same time.
In each room, one young woman is sitting.
Note that these women are identical twins.
"Does telepathy exist? That has been, for many scientists― especially neuroscientists and psychologists, a field of great interest. In many of those experiments, identical twins have been the subjects."
―And then, the next moment.
The surveillance camera footage from earlier―
When the older sister, Jessie, raised her right hand, 2 seconds later the younger sister, Angie, raised her right hand too.
Then, a few minutes after that, when the older sister, Jessie, raised her left hand, without a moment's delay, the younger sister, Angie, raised her left hand too.
They could not see each other, nor could they hear each other's voices.
Why did this happen?
"Identical twins are people who were originally one fertilized egg that split in two during cell division― in other words, they were originally exactly the same organism. Therefore, that this kind of sympathy― resonance should occur is something many scientists have put forward as a hypothesis."
"As a result, several cases like Jessie and Angie's have been seen in the past, and there are not a few papers concluding that telepathy exists. However, by what mechanism does telepathy occur― solving that mystery is precisely the important mission of us scientists."
"What was put forward as that hypothesis was the resonance of brain waves. Brain waves are radio waves produced by the electrical signals that flow in the brain. When one person's brain waves are sensed by another person, might that become telepathy? That, too, many scientists have hypothesized."
In India and France, 5,000 miles apart, a certain experiment was carried out.
A joint project of the University of Barcelona in Spain, Axilum Robotics, Harvard Medical School, and Starlab.
One subject in India was fitted with a brain-machine interface― that is, a machine that reads the brain's electrical currents and brain waves, and three other subjects in France, fitted with receiving interfaces, received its output over the internet.
For example, the sending side concentrates on "0" or "1," and can the receiving side sense it: a very simple test.
The result―
"The results of this experiment are extremely interesting. It was an experiment possible only because of modern infrastructure, which uses machines to amplify brain waves and sends them over the internet to distant places. This experiment... succeeded."
―Brain waves, amplified by machine and delivered over the internet.
Receive them with a receiving interface, and one can perceive the will of someone even 5,000 miles away.
Telepathy, until now categorized under supernatural science as a paranormal phenomenon or a psychic power, had been made real on the basis of scientific theory by making use of modern communications technology.
"Telepathy is neither a paranormal phenomenon nor a psychic power. It is a perfectly natural, ordinary ability that humans innately possess. Give it a little push with the power of science, and telepathy becomes possible. I can state with certainty that denying telepathy is now unscientific."
―Then, how should we conclude the case of Jessie and Angie, introduced at the start?
"Unfortunately, at present, the reality is that nothing can be said either way. Is there some unknown synesthesia found only in identical twins? Or... perhaps it was a trick, ha ha ha."
