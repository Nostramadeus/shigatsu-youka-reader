―And now, the segment you've all been waiting for.
"Everyone across the country! Good morning! 　Yukino Shinbashi here, and today I'm paying a visit to this place!"
―Yuki-chan, that's a park with an awful lot of cherry trees.
"That's right! Actually, we're broadcasting from Central Park in Motoki-cho, Sawa Prefecture. Today we're bringing you the show from Motoki-cho, Sawa Prefecture, famous nationwide for its cherry blossoms!"
―Oh, the crowd's really fired up too!
"Yes! Here in Central Park, the Motoki Cherry Blossom Festival is held for about a month every year, and this year it's reaching its 70th!"
―So that means the festival has been going on since Shiyo 730!? 　That's quite a long history for a festival.
"About that, actually, the very first festival ever held was already the 15th!"
―Hm? What do you mean?
"Please take a look at this!"
―Oh, that's a magnificent cherry tree!
"Yes, this is the big cherry tree that's said to be the goshinboku, the symbol of Motoki-cho, you could say."
―It looks like it's about 1,000 years old or something.
"Actually, this tree apparently hasn't even been growing for 70 years yet."
―What!? It's that young!?
"That's right. The cherry-blossom festival originally started as a way to worship this goshinboku, but the residents back then had a question: 'Wasn't this cherry tree here much longer ago?' So, to make it a festival that had been held since much earlier, they deliberately called even the very first one the 15th."
―Even so, if it's been going on for more than 50 years, that means the people of Motoki-cho really do love their cherry tree.
"Yes, among Motoki-cho residents, there are even companies with a system called cherry-blossom leave, rather than paid leave. So at this time of year, even on weekdays, it's as lively as a weekend."
―I see! Are there any fun events?
"Actually, today is the spring eating contest, the main event of the Motoki Cherry Blossom Festival! 　And we've received word that a super-big-name special guest will be competing!"
―A special guest competing means... someone who can really eat, right?
"Yes! The winner of the recent national tournament... If you're curious, please come visit Motoki-cho!"
―Motoki-cho's a long way to go from here right now! 　What dish will they be competing with?
"That would be... this! A food-stall classic, sauce yakisoba! 　The contestant who eats the most of this sauce yakisoba in 15 minutes wins!"
―Oh, that looks delicious! For me, sauce yakisoba and grilled squid are must-haves!
"Of course, there's a grilled-squid stall too! 　Now, I'd like to try some of this yakisoba right away!"
―Ah, that's the classic style, the kind that makes you want a beer. Personally, I'd want mayonnaise on it.
"Mmm! Delicious! 　Yep, that slightly charred touch you only get at a stall, and the smell of the sauce hitting you hard, piping hot and fresh off the griddle, are the best! 　Actually, the winner of this contest since the year before last is said to be a little girl!"
―A little girl?
"Yes, last year she apparently polished off 150 takoyaki in 15 minutes!"
―150!? I couldn't do that even if I had a week...
"She's entering again this year, apparently, so will it be a one-on-one showdown with the special guest, or will a third force come storming in!? It's going to be a very exciting battle! 　Neighbors, people of Sawa Prefecture, please come out to Central Park in Motoki-cho today! 　That's all from the scene!"
―Thank you, Yuki-chaaan!
