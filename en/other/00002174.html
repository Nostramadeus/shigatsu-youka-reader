―My face covered by the bridal hood, I walked with sliding steps.
A great many people were watching me.
I could not tell how many people there were, but I could tell there were many.
Father being Father, he had surely invited a great many guests.
Among them, surely, were people connected to my husband.
How do I look to them now?
With my face hidden and my head bowed as I walked, I could see only my feet, but perhaps from the tension of being watched by so many, my back straightened on its own.
With my head bowed and my back straight, I surely looked as if I had a terrible stoop.
Before long, the seat where I would sit and the go-betweens came into view.
At this shrine, it seemed, the go-betweens entered first, then the bride, and last of all the groom.
Come to think of it, I had never even met the go-betweens.
I glanced sideways at the faces of the two go-betweens, but... neither was anyone I knew.
Were they people from a marriage business Father had hired?
Keeping my face lowered, I took my seat.
There was no one in the seat beside me yet.
Next, my husband would enter and sit in this seat.
But I had been told to keep my head bowed like this, so I did not know whether I would be able to see my husband's face from here.
Just then,
the guests began to murmur about something.
If they were murmuring at my husband's entrance― did he look that startling?
When I strained my ears...
"What's with that getup..."
"Creepy..."
"Kikyo-chan's marrying that?"
Remarks like these, which could hardly be called kind.
I clenched my fists tight.
Whatever sort of person he may be, I will become his wife.
I will support him and spend my life with him.
I am already resolved to that.
His looks do not matter.
It all depends on my own heart.
The man who had to be my husband sat down beside me.
A little of his clothing came into my view.
I had been sure he would appear in a black hakama, but―
What my eyes saw was a dark red cloth.
It was very strange clothing.
No wonder the guests had been murmuring.
But these clothes, I had seen them somewhere―
―!?
No, that can't be...!
Just then, the go-betweens told me to lift my head.
Ordinarily I would have faced straight ahead and shown my face to the guests, but I looked at him from the corner of my eye.
"Ko-sama...!?"
I shouted under my breath.
"K-Kikyo-dono!?"
My husband shouted under his breath too.
And then the go-betweens warned us to be quiet.
