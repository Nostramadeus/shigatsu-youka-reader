That day arrived.
"Kikyo, are you ready?"
This was goodbye to this house as well.
Father had allowed me to take just a few books with me.
But I told him I did not need them.
Because they were no longer something I needed.
On the way to the venue, I kept thinking.
I had resolved never to regret again, yet my heart was not that simple.
I wanted to see Ko-sama once more.
This time I would talk with him properly, and then, with Ko-sama...
All I did was give myself up to that pipe dream.
No, this is no good.
I am a bride now, about to marry into another family.
It is disrespectful to the man who will be my husband.
At least on the outside, I must behave like one.
I arrived at the shrine.
This was where the wedding would be held.
Today, here, I would meet my husband face to face for the first time.
............ Might Ko-sama not suddenly appear here and take me away to Arata?
For a while, I gave myself over to that impossible fantasy.
No, no, I said that's enough.
Thinking about another man at my own wedding is unpardonable conduct.
I took one big, deep breath and passed through the torii gate.
I was immediately shown to the back.
Before I could even rest, my arms were put through the sleeves of the bridal dress.
Then a great many makeup tools were laid out in front of me.
All I did was sit there.
My hair and makeup were all done for me.
All the while, I was thinking.
What is asked of me as a wife?
The man will surely be the son of a merchant, like Father.
A life where my husband runs here and there to scrape money together.
If so, then I must make a home where my husband can relax.
I will take care of the house, and let my husband think only of his work.
For a merchant, trust with his customers is what matters. It is his lifeline.
If it is cut, he will go bankrupt in no time and end up on the street.
Now, dressed like this as a bride, I am at last beginning to see this marriage in a positive light.
Yes, from today I marry into another family.
If I am to marry into a merchant family anyway, I will support my husband with all I have, so that he can become a great merchant who loses to none of the Great Powers.
My dreams and passion depend on how I think of them and how I use them.
Ko-sama, too, is surely living with all his might in a faraway land even now.
If we ever meet again someday, I want him to see how much I have grown, as a woman and as a wife.
And the one who becomes a cornerstone of a country that loses to none of the Great Powers will be my husband.
Come to think of it...
Ko-sama had said something that stuck with me.
He had said it, but... what was it?
A whole month had passed, and I had forgotten.
