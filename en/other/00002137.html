There stood one man, clad in a Noh mask and a red cloth.
At that bizarre sight, I covered my mouth.
"Ah, I'm sorry for startling you. I'd just like to ask the way..."
"Y-yes...!"
"Which way should I go to reach the Emperor's castle?"
"Um, well, um..."
Despite the gentle manner of the man's words and actions, I could not let down my guard.
Had the man noticed?
In one smooth motion, he took off the Noh mask.
The moment I saw his face, I caught my breath.
The gentleman had blue eyes and brown hair, just like a Westerner.
Right before my eyes was a Westerner, someone I had never before been able to come anywhere near.
To think such fine looks had been hidden beneath the Noh mask.
"Uh, the Emperor's..."
"U-um...! 　I'll show you the way!"
"No, no, there's no need for that. I couldn't put you to such trouble..."
"N-no, you see, I'm reeeeeally free right now!"
"Really...?"
"Yes! 　Reeeeeally!"
"............"
"............"
A short silence took its seat between the two of us.
But,
"............ Hahahaha! So you were! 　Hahahaha!"
And the gentleman burst into loud laughter.
"No, no, I'm sorry. I never expected anyone to insist that hard on being free. Ahahahaha!"
"............ Heh heh heh...!"
Seeing his smile, I was finally able to relax as well.
"Then, may I ask you to show me the way? I'm Ko Niimura."
With that, the gentleman named Ko-sama held out his right hand to me.
............ What could he mean by it?
Could this be a Western greeting?
I followed his lead and held out my right hand in the same way.
Then Ko-sama took my hand and shook it up and down.
"Pleased to meet you. What is your name?"
"Oh, my name is Kikyo. I'm 16."
"Kikyo-dono, is it? Um, and your surname...?"
"Uh... my family are merchants, so we have no surname..."
Townsfolk like us were not officially permitted surnames.
So I did not know my own surname.
"No surname...? 　What does that mean?"
"Um... Ko-sama, are you from the West after all? 　If so, perhaps you are not aware that there is such a law."
"Ah, no, I'm Japanese. I only look like this because there's a Westerner among my ancestors. But my home village is in a remote region far from here, so I don't know much about Tokyo."
"A Westerner among your ancestors...?"
"Yes. Among my ancestors is a Westerner who came here about 400 years ago."
"Oh my! No wonder... But why have you come all the way to Tokyo?"
"Ah, that... It's a long story, so shall we talk as we walk?"
It was not very far from here to the Emperor's castle.
