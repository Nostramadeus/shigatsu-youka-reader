"Kikyo, what are you reading this time...?"
For the several days since meeting Ko-sama, I had been reading a certain book.
It was about the geography of this country.
If only I could find a land called Arata in it―
The land where Ko-sama lived had captured my interest.
The Arata settlement: though it lay in a remote region, Western culture had blended into it, with none of that 'because you're a man, because you're a woman' thinking.
I wanted to go there someday.
For several days, I had been in the grip of that childlike curiosity.
"Kikyo, are you listening?"
"Oh, Father..."
Only then did I realize Father had been speaking to me.
Usually he did not look kindly on my reading, but today was a little different.
His expression was somehow softer.
"Kikyo, the man you're to marry has been decided."
"Huh? Marriage, you say..."
"You're already 16, after all. Ideally it would have been better to wait until you had grown a little more womanly, but I believe I've chosen a match worthy of you."
"............"
I had known a day like this would come someday, but to think it would arrive so suddenly.
In this era, it was a matter of course for the parents to decide on the marriage partner between themselves.
There were even cases where one did not know one's partner's face until the wedding day itself.
"I'll introduce you to him next month. Be ready to pack up your things."
With that, Father left.
So that was how it was... Was it all over now, then, sending my dreams into the future like this?
Once I was married, I would not be able to read freely.
―The Emperor's castle, visible from the second floor of our house.
"Haah..."
What sort of view could one see from that castle?
Could one see the Arata settlement where Ko-sama lived?
In the end, the books had not told me where the Arata settlement was.
A remote region, where Ko-sama lived.
My mind was on nothing else the whole time.
Just then, the neighborhood grew noisy over something.
"What's that thing..."
"Creepy..."
"A youkai?"
When I looked down, people were staring at a certain figure.
"Ko-sama!?"
Someone in a Noh mask and the red vestments was walking along, paying no mind to all the attention.
Staying out of sight, I followed that person.
If I carelessly called out here and now, I would draw strange attention myself.
I was soon to be married, so it would not do for odd rumors to start.
I would call out once there were fewer people around.
That person was not necessarily Ko-sama, but had to be someone from the settlement.
Ko-sama seemed to be heading for the castle, as before.
Since I had guided him there before, he seemed to remember the way, and he walked on without any sign of getting lost.
In that case, I would have no chance to speak to Ko-sama.
Should I hurry out in front of Ko-sama and let him see me?
Then he might notice me and stop.
"Huh...?"
Ko-sama was heading somewhere other than the castle.
What was more, that way was a dead end, with almost no one passing through.
If I was going to speak to him, it had to be now...!
I quickened my pace and followed.
"............?"
Strange...
He had surely gone this way, yet there was no one here.
Had he gone into one of the houses?
"So it was Kikyo-dono after all."
A familiar voice came from behind me.
"Ko-sama!"
"Hahaha, did you follow me?"
"Y-yes, I did! I had been hoping to see you once more! 　Are you going to the castle again?"
"Yes. The other day, I was told to come back once more."
"Then why did you come this way?"
"Well, because you, Kikyo-dono, were following me. Looking the way I do, I thought somewhere out of sight would be better."
"I see..."
"But I'm sorry, I have to go to the castle now."
"Huh...?"
"But perhaps I might get to see you again, Kikyo-dono. With that thought, I decided to stay the night in Tokyo today. So once my business at the castle is done... if it's all right with you, Kikyo-dono..."
So saying, Ko-sama looked away a little.
"Y-yes! I'm reeeeeeeally free, after all."
"............ Hahaha! Free again today, were you!"
I saw Ko-sama off and went back home.
At dusk, at the place where we had met earlier, once more...
Um, I should have a new kimono somewhere.
Oh, my hair won't do like this, either.
How do I do makeup again...?
"What's this, Kikyo, got your heart ready to be a bride?"
Watching me try to dress up as prettily as I could, Father remarked so.
"Huh? Yes, I'm going through my things at the same time..."
"Good, good! Honestly, I thought you'd be more against it, so that's a relief!"
I'm sorry, Father.
There is no need to get my heart ready to be a bride.
After all, whatever I may feel, I am someone who will be married off.
Something like that means nothing at all.
More important is getting ready for tonight.
Since my marriage has been decided, I will not say I feel no guilt.
But I only want to talk more with Ko-sama, who knows cultures and learning I have yet to see.
Yes, that is all.
But why was it that, now that I would soon see Ko-sama, my heart raced?
Suddenly, I thought about what lay ahead.
If the marriage offer were to fall through, and I were to go to Ko-sama's homeland― Then I would...
What am I thinking.
I am dreaming far too many strange dreams.
As Father says, I should know my place.
And yet...
