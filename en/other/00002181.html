―After I had finished the mountain seclusion, Ko-san said this to me.
"Kikyo, is there anything you want? It's to celebrate finishing the mountain seclusion!"
"Something I want?"
I had only just finished the mountain seclusion, and perhaps because I had been cut off from the everyday world, I no longer had any desire for possessions.
Just having warm meals and a futon was the greatest luxury there could be.
If there was anything I wanted beyond that―
"............ I'd like books."
"Books?"
"Yes, for my studies."
"............ Hahaha, Kikyo, you really are eager to learn."
So Ko-san entrusted me with a key.
It was, he said, the key to the Niimura family's storehouse.
He said the storehouse held many books from long ago, and I could read them as I pleased.
Some had been written by Westerners who came here long ago, he said, so they were just right for learning about Western culture.
The only problem was whether I could read those books.
They were all old writings, and some were even written in foreign languages, I heard.
These days, it seemed, no one even in the settlement could read them.
He had given me this reward with the work of deciphering those books folded into it.
"Wow... So many...!"
Before my eyes were the storehouse's books, inscribed with several hundred years of history.
How much time would it take to read all of these?
What was more, they held things that almost no one in this country knew.
―At first, even reading a single sentence was hard.
There was no decent dictionary, and much of it was beyond anything my knowledge until then could handle.
Even so, as time goes on, one gradually grows used to it.
Before long I was able to read them to some extent.
And among them, there was a word that caught my attention.
"Ko-san, do you know about Droga?"
"Droga? 　Yeah, I know it. It's a drug that enhances human strength."
It was just as the books had said.
So this settlement did have a mysterious drug that enhanced human strength.
"Ko-san, have you ever used it?"
"No, I haven't. It's supposed to be hard to use, so they won't let you use it until you're 20 or older."
"I see... Then some of the other residents have used it, haven't they?"
"Yeah, it gets used a lot. They say that when you use it for hunting and the like, it makes things really easy. Actually, when I first came to Tokyo, what I tried to present to the Emperor was Droga."
"I see. So that's what lay behind it."
"But... the Emperor wouldn't accept it. I suppose it looked suspicious after all."
"Why did you try to present it?"
"My mother, the Grand Witch, had me do it. In this age of contact with the Great Powers, she thought it was about time we researched Droga's effects too."
"In other words, the idea was that presenting Droga would interest the Emperor, and then funds and facilities for research could be obtained?"
"Yeah, that's it. But I failed... The Emperor asked me if there was something I wanted instead. Refusing the gift and sending me home empty-handed on top of that would have been shameful for the Emperor. So I took the plunge and asked if there was any woman who'd be willing to live in Arata."
"And so I... Thinking of it that way, it's strange somehow."
"Yeah. Kikyo, when I think about how you came to me, it wasn't all bad. So... my mother told me something earlier, and..."
"I know. I'm to research Droga, aren't I?"
"Haha, you're as sharp as ever..."
"After all, I was known as a clever woman! Now that I've come here, I'll give you all the help I can!"
