Er, thank you for taking time out of your busy day to watch the Motoki-cho official channel.
I am Choji Nakajima, mayor of Motoki-cho.
I will get straight to the point: I have an announcement for everyone in Motoki-cho, and in Sawa Prefecture.
I, Choji Nakajima, will be retiring as mayor at the end of this term.
Looking back, it was 24 years ago, when I was 35.
At the time, Motoki-cho's population had begun to grow, and it was in the middle of developing, little by little, into a bigger town.
I wanted to make it a bigger town, a prosperous town second to none among the cities, towns and villages of Japan, and with that in mind I have given my all for 24 years.
Thanks to all of you, I received tremendous support and was able to fulfill every pledge I made, and as a result, Motoki-cho has achieved unprecedented growth.
The economy, tourism, safety. Every one of them is a wonderful strength of Motoki-cho that we can be proud of before all of Japan.
From here on, I intend to leave the future to the younger generation and watch over Motoki-cho's further development.
However, at this age, I have found a new dream.
I want to use the know-how I cultivated in the responsible job of mayor of Motoki-cho for Japan this time, I want to make Japan bigger, like Motoki-cho.
That is how I came to think.
Accordingly, I will be running in the House of Representatives election to be held next month.
I have no experience in national politics, but given my age, I believe this is my last chance.
Townspeople of Motoki-cho, even once it leaves my hands, this town will go on developing.
I wish to entrust Motoki-cho to its young people, and to those with good hearts.
Even if I am elected to the House of Representatives, this is my hometown, and I owe who I am today to all of you.
So that I may come home in glory, from now on I will devote myself to Japan.
I ask for your continued support of Choji Nakajima.
