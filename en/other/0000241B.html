Hidden route of scene 85 (April Sickness)

35 boxes · hidden route (裏ルート) of scene 85

I had to go about things as usual.
That was what Ryoji-kun had told me.
Rather than told, I had been instructed in a letter.
By now, is Ryoji-kun meeting Mifuyu, I wonder.
Can he meet her and negotiate?
Will words even get through to Mifuyu, now that she has become like that?
Natsumi seemed to be home, but it looked like she was laid up in bed.
It would probably be better not to force her awake.
For now, I had to get dinner ready.
Natsumi had no appetite because of her April sickness, but that didn't mean I could make nothing.
I opened the window of the room.
At that, a quiet night wind blew in.
This was insurance. I was opening the window in case the worst should happen.
Bean sprouts... cabbage... carrots... onions...
Then, if I used the frozen pork, a dish of sorts would come together.
I tossed the pork into the microwave and chopped the carrots and onions.
I chopped the vegetables with the blade... and the thawed pork―
"Haah..."
A sigh.
When I went to cut the pork, I put the knife down.
What I saw that time―
"Haah..."
I sighed once more and gripped the knife.
With a grinding scrape, I sank the blade into the half-thawed pork.
I got it over with as quickly as I could.
I cut the meat as quickly as I could.
As quickly as I could― quickly― quickly― quickly.
But, I stopped my hands, wrapped the pork in plastic wrap, and put it in the refrigerator.
Then I pulled out a burdock root that had been freezing in a corner of the vegetable drawer.
With this, I wouldn't have to use the pork.
Instead of meat, I took out dried horse mackerel.
I threw the ingredients into the heated frying pan all at once.
The carrots and the burdock, both at the same time.
Usually I put them in properly, in order, but today, all at once.
Just then, Natsumi came out of her room.
"Morning. How are you feeling?"
"Mmm... better than this afternoon..."
"Think you can eat?"
"Yeah, I'll eat properly today. Dad isn't back yet?"
"He says he's suddenly been sent on a business trip to Hokkaido. He'll be back next week, apparently."
The lie came out so smoothly it surprised even me.
It was by no means a pleasant feeling.
I couldn't tell Natsumi about today's negotiation.
"Dad's business trips are always so sudden. Dad's not here, huh. And I was going to eat takoyaki with him."
"Takoyaki?"
"Haruka gave it to me. As a get-well present."
"We're always, always imposing on Haruka-chan. We'll have to thank her later."
"Yeah... really. I'll thank her properly later."
Haruka-chan always looks out for Natsumi.
That makes me happy too, but sometimes I also feel sorry about it.
For Natsumi, Haruka-chan is someone she cannot do without.
And the reverse is true as well.
No, to me too, both Natsumi and Haruka-chan are precious.
Nothing can replace them.
And not only those two. Mifuyu is the same.
Mifuyu had been with me ever since I was old enough to remember.
And yet...
Where did we go wrong?
Such a foolish question came to mind.
Where we went wrong is obvious, no matter who looks at it, or how.
"Sorry to keep you waiting. Okay, let's eat."
"Oh..."
"Oh..."
On the surface of the takoyaki sat a single cherry blossom petal.
"Sorry, I'll take it off right away..."
I hadn't expected a cherry blossom to slip into a place like this.
For Natsumi, this alone was enough to turn the takoyaki into something revolting.
"I-I'm fine! Not eating it over something this small..."
Natsumi lifted the takoyaki with her chopsticks, but―
She flung down the takoyaki and chopsticks and ran straight into the bathroom.
"N-Natsumi, are you okay!?"
"Hack! Hack! 　I... I'm fine... This always happens... Go on and eat..."
"Natsumi... All right. Call me if anything happens."
"Yeah..."
April sickness...
Apparently it is a little different from trauma.
When Eiichiro-san died, Natsumi was splashed by the spray of blood.
While a strong wind blew, wrapped in a storm of cherry petals.
To begin with, Natsumi doesn't even know that Eiichiro-san died.
Rather than not knowing, she doesn't remember, and it seems she hadn't even understood what happened at the time.
So the doctor had said it was strange to call it trauma, too.
Thinking of Natsumi, we should have left Motoki-cho, with its many cherry trees, early on.
But, because of my selfishness―
"Are you okay?"
Natsumi, back from the bathroom, had a somewhat better color in her face.
"Yeah, I'm fine now."
"I see... If you're fine, then good..."
"What's wrong? Did something happen?"
"Huh? No... nothing..."
"............?"
I had been clutching my old cell phone the whole time.
There was still no word from Ryoji-kun.
Even so, I couldn't carelessly call him from my end.
"Never mind that, Natsumi, you're the one who should at least eat some rice gruel. I'll make it."
"Okay, thank you..."
I went to the kitchen and took the earthenware pot down from the shelf.
For now, all I can do is believe in Ryoji-kun.
Believe in him, and pray that he is safe.
It had been written in Ryoji-kun's letter.
Mifuyu isn't stupid either.
If she were to lay a hand on our family, even Mifuyu would not come out of it unharmed.
"Mom, you haven't cleaned the bath yet, have you? I'll go do it."
"What are you talking about, as wobbly as you are? Sit still and behave."
"But I feel like moving around a little would help. I think April sickness is a matter of mood. I'll go do it."
Yes, what I can do is act as usual in front of Natsumi.
This gloomy air goes against what Ryoji-kun wants.
"Then I'll take you up on it. But just give the bathtub a light wash, okay? Cleaning for more than 3 minutes is forbidden!"
"Okay! I'll go do it!"
With a lively step you would never think belonged to someone who had been shut in the bathroom, Natsumi headed for the bath.
It seemed Natsumi had closed the living room window.
In the end, Natsumi said nothing.
In that case, there was no longer any need to make Natsumi's condition any worse.
I had Natsumi eat the rice gruel and urged her to go to bed early.
Natsumi doesn't know anything yet.
It's better that Natsumi doesn't know anything yet.
This is our problem.
A problem that we, as her parents, must solve.
Before long, the date changed over, and the clock's hands moved unnaturally fast.
Still there was no word at all from Ryoji-kun.
Nor was there any sign that Mifuyu, next door, had come home.
Ryoji-kun...! Please, hurry and get in touch!
Please, just be safe...!
