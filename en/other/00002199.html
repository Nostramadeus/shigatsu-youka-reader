The residents' disappearances had not happened only once.
Several times in the past, the green-eyed and the Goddess had brought the settlement to the brink of disaster.
And to my astonishment, residents had gone missing as recently as about 10 years before.
I asked Mother to show me the letter that recorded what had happened at that time.
I also had her let me keep it among the storehouse's books.
I had it made part of the storehouse's books out of fear that the memory would fade, and so that the document would be passed down to later generations.
Ko-san said:
Is that connected to Droga?
I answered:
Droga exists only here. So the proper course is to look for the cause in the things that exist only here.
In Mother's document there was a passage like this.
'In the spring of the year after the mass disappearance, we noticed that dozens of unfamiliar cherry trees had appeared around the Arata settlement. They had suddenly appeared in places where, until the year before, there had been no sign of any tree at all. As if they had bloomed in full every year, the great cherry trees proudly flaunted their blossoms.'
'The number of cherry trees that suddenly appeared was about 30. By strange coincidence, this is nearly the same as the number of residents who disappeared.'
'We decided to cut one of the newly grown trees and study how it lives. We dug it up by the roots and replanted it within the settlement. As we did so, clothing and a skeleton came up, tangled in the roots. So it is true. These cherry trees grew suddenly, using the residents as a seedbed. This tree is no cherry tree at all. It is some other thing that looks just like a cherry.'
'Since ancient times, it is said, these cherry-tree-like things that suddenly appear have been called 'Shigatsu Youka.' An eerie flower blooms in the month of death. The people of the Arata settlement, unless by accident or injury, almost without fail die in April. That is why it is the month of death.'
'Indeed, if we think of it this way, can we not solve nearly all the mysterious phenomena surrounding this Arata settlement? Until now these inexplicable phenomena have been dismissed as curses or youkai, but this is an age when the Great Powers are pressing in on Japan and Westernization is expected to advance. It is time we broke free of this myth-mindedness and unraveled the mysteries of the Arata settlement realistically.'
'As one part of this, a representative will be sent from this Arata settlement to Tokyo. The representative will carry Droga and present it to the Emperor or to those who hold power. If the right person can be found, research with ample funds and facilities should be possible, but there is no knowing when the mystery of Droga will be solved― Even if it takes 100 years, it ought to be unraveled across the generations.'
It seemed Mother, too, had vaguely noticed a link between the disappearances and Droga.
That was why she had Ko-san carry Droga and seek an audience with the Emperor.
But the Emperor did not accept it, and the research into Droga came to a standstill.
Since I came in its place, I myself am carrying out that research.
I see. So it is 'Shigatsu Youka.'
The 'Shigatsu Youka,' which looks just like a cherry, uses humans in some way, through the Droga made from its roots, to multiply its cherry trees.
For a living thing, that makes sense.
But...
Why is it? Something does not sit right.
If it is suspicious, it is simply a matter of having the use of Droga stopped at once.
Mother has not done that...
"Ko-san, is there a reason Mother permits the use of Droga?"
"Hmm... I can't really say without asking her..."
"I suppose so..."
It is not that I suspect Mother.
Mother permits Droga even though she has noticed that it is dangerous.
Most likely there is a reason she has no choice but to do so.
