"Kikyo Niimura-san, I am glad to see you in good health."
Shinozaki-san said that before anything else.
"Yes, thanks to you."
As I saw that face and heard that voice, my rusty memories came back to life.
Yes. I cannot simply grow old and die like this.
There is still something I must do.
To find out what Droga truly is, and to bring true peace to this settlement.
The peace this settlement has now is a makeshift peace, painted over by Droga.
Surely Shinozaki-san has brought the answer with him.
"I am deeply sorry for the delay. Our research on the drug in question is complete."
With that, Shinozaki-san set down a thick sheaf of papers.
"And... what did you find?"
"To be frank... we have reached the conclusion that the drug is not something human beings should lay hands on."
"............ I see..."
Shinozaki-san's account―
The spores contained in Droga, the horror of the Verde mummy, an evil being called the Goddess.
There lay the answer to 'a drug of cause and effect,' which I had pondered since I was young.
"And also... This is very difficult for me to say, but I told you one lie."
"Shinozaki-san, do you mean that you are not from the government?"
"You were aware of it...?"
"I had no proof of it, though. But now, government or not, I am very grateful that you have come like this to tell me."
―Shinozaki-san offered his apologies once more and left the settlement.
Looking through the report, I found that along with the research results, it set out their purpose.
They were the descendants and comrades of the people who long ago parted ways with the Arata settlement, and it seemed they were now a sect called the Holy Cerejeira Church.
The sect revered Droga as a sacred treasure.
At some point, the leader of the sect began to have doubts about how Droga worked.
It was exactly what the previous Grand Witch had been thinking.
To unravel it, they had needed Droga's raw material, which is to say, the cherry trees here.
Among the sect's believers were people connected to the government, so they had also been able to secure a certain budget.
But now that the true nature of Droga was known, the sect was falling apart.
I cannot die yet.
No, I am not even permitted to step down from the role of Grand Witch.
I have a duty.
A duty to convey the horror of Droga to everyone.
"Ban Droga!?"
"What do you mean by this, Grand Witch-sama!"
"Droga is essential to our way of life! 　Why such a thing...!"
No sooner had I gathered everyone at the meeting hall and issued a ban on Droga than this happened.
I had known it would come to this.
In the past, bans on Droga had been issued in the settlement several times.
But as time passed, the horror of Droga faded, and people would lay hands on this devil's drug again.
That history had kept repeating itself.
That, too, must end here.
I deliberately did not tell everyone the reason for the ban.
Even if I banned it the same way as before, history would only repeat itself.
That was why I would rid this settlement of Droga by a different method.
Shinozaki-san, I had also thought, might be asked to dispose of the cherry tree at the Niimura main house as well.
But the Shigatsu Youka is a fungus, a relative of the strongly parasitic honey fungus.
Removing the fruiting bodies above ground is merely a drop in the bucket.
I shut myself up in the storehouse and composed a new book to add to the storehouse's books.
First, I would change the way the settlement conducted its memorial services.
The falsificaso that appears as a by-product when Droga is refined would be treated as a tool of the rites.
Falsificaso works as a wonder drug for those infected with Droga.
By making it one of the rite tools in this way, falsificaso will go on being made for all time.
Of course, this carries the danger that Droga will be refined as well.
But in any case, everyone knows how to make Droga, and it is written in the storehouse's books too.
There is little point in banning the refining of Droga.
Then one need only make the wonder drug at the same time.
