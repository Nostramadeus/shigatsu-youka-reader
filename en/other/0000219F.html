―That day came suddenly.
I simply knelt, my hands on the tatami.
Unable to lift my head, I could do nothing but regret.
The reason we had gathered at the meeting hall that day.
It was for the funeral of Ko-san and Mother.
Only the day before, Ko-san and I had discussed 'why Mother permits Droga.'
And right after that, Mother and Ko-san had thrown themselves from the cliff at the graveyard clearing.
Could it be that they took their own lives because I had raised such a subject?
If I had not spoken of it, would this not have happened?
In truth, while I was researching Droga and the Green-Eye Curse-Killing, some part of me had thought of it as someone else's affair.
I had not yet lived through any tragedy connected with Droga.
At most, I had heard of the residents' disappearances that happened before I came here, and the story of a child with green eyes being killed.
So somehow it had never felt real.
Seeing the two of them dead before my eyes like this, I became aware of how terrible a thing I had involved myself in.
It was not only the danger to myself.
Might I not end up putting even the people close to me in danger as well―
Such thoughts would not let my hands leave the tatami.
I was powerless.
"Mom, are you all right?"
The child born to Ko-san and me called out to me like that.
"I'm all right."
I forced a smile at the child, who was peering into my face.
If I involved myself any further, might the danger not reach even this child?
What I was truly meant to do was uncover the truth of Droga and the curse-killing.
Yet if, for that very reason, I put those around me in danger too, it would defeat the whole purpose.
Beside the remains of Ko-san and Mother lay one more body, wearing a Noh mask.
It was the mummy called Lady Verde.
The mummy of a girl named Tomi, who is said to have taken her own life about 300 years ago.
Lady Verde is enshrined in this meeting hall.
The goddess that we, the people of Arata, worship.
It seems this mummy, too, is an idol with a dark history of one kind or another, but...
But perhaps that, too, ends here.
Even if I looked any further into the secrets hidden in this settlement...
―A few days later, I took over as the Grand Witch.
The residents did not know that I had been researching Droga.
So I was just one of the brides who had come from elsewhere.
I had only to behave as one.
Would doing just that not keep me, and everyone, safe?
I had lost all will.
Now I would seek nothing more, doubt nothing―
If only everyone here now could go on living in peace as before―
All I could do was seek nothing more.
And so, just as I was, I grew older year upon year.
The residents used Droga, and whenever a child with green eyes was born, they killed it.
I followed the law and did nothing but turn a blind eye.
My child grew up well, and new Western culture gradually found its way into the settlement too.
At the same time I, too, grew old and frail, fated to die just as I was.
―It was at just such a time.
Yes, I was 65 then.
A certain visitor came to this settlement.
