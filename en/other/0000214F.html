―I slipped quietly out of the house.
The sun had already set, and the western sky was faintly tinged with crimson.
Casually covering my face with my sleeve so that the people I passed would see as little of it as possible, I hurried to that place.
But once I met him, what should I talk about?
How long would we be able to see each other?
No, before that, what did he think of me?
If I said something that somehow offended him, this precious chance would be ruined.
Thinking about such things only now, I walked on.
"Kikyo-dono?"
Just a little farther to the place we had agreed on― Suddenly, that voice reached my ears.
"Ko-sama!"
There stood Ko-sama, without the Noh mask, in perfectly ordinary clothes.
"Ah, so it is you, Kikyo-dono. You look like a different person."
"Huh?"
"No, it's just... the lovely kimono, and your hair..."
"Oh... yes, I made myself a little prettier... Y-you know, I'm reeeeeeeally free!"
"Hahaha! So you were!"
I had had the time to prepare, but my heart had been anything but free.
Right up to the very last moment, I had agonized over what to wear.
"But Ko-sama, is it all right to take the mask off?"
"It's this dark already. To the people of Tokyo, my hair and eyes shouldn't stand out much."
"But Ko-sama, you noticed my clothes and my hair right away, didn't you?"
"I grew up in the mountains, so my eyes are good. Do my eyes and hair stand out?"
"............ No, not that much, to my eyes..."
The western sky had gone nearly dark too, and there were only gas lamps here and there.
That, and a mere token of light from the moon.
Unless I came close enough to hear his breathing, I could not clearly tell it was Ko-sama.
The streets of Tokyo, now gone completely quiet― What reached my ears was Ko-sama's breathing.
"Shall we go, then? Uh, how late can you stay out today?"
"Um... I can stay as late as you like. You know me, I'm―"
"Reeally free?"
"Yes!"
Though this was only our second meeting, Ko-sama had gotten ahead of my joke.
Somehow, it made me happy.
―We walked as far as the river, and the two of us sat down on its bank.
But,
"Oh, wait, Kikyo-dono, please use this."
Ko-sama held out the red vestments to me.
"............?"
"You're in such lovely clothes. Please put this down to sit on."
And before hearing my answer, Ko-sama spread the red vestments on the ground.
"Um... Thank you very much. Then I'll accept your kind offer..."
I accepted his kindness and sat down on the red vestments.
A slightly chilly wind off the river.
It cooled, just a little, my body, flushed from the thick kimono I was not used to.
Ko-sama's breathing, too, was drowned out by the sound of the river.
