070What Is a Reference?Allow me to explain the references. A reference is, as I said at the outset, material for coming to know this world.It is not that you must necessarily check them, but if you wish to know this world more deeply, active viewing is recommended.Some of them are difficult and call for advanced knowledge, so if one concerns you, looking it up on the internet would be advisable.Conversely, some references are of no use whatsoever, so the power to judge whether one is needed is also called for.Yet it is not rare for something that looks of no use to be important information later on.In any case, if you wish to know this world deeply, checking every one without omission would be best.An unviewed reference carries a 'New!!' mark.New

452About the FormatsReferences are classified, as a rule, into two kinds: object, scenario, letters and report.The object format comes in various kinds, such as documents, web pages and tangible things, but the operation is the same for all.オブジェクトIn the scenario format a short story unfolds, almost the same as a scenario of the main work.シナリオIn the letters format you can view letters written long ago. However, you must spend points to read all of them.In the report format you can view reports written before the war. Here too, spending points widens the area you can read.

831Higher FormatsI have not confirmed it myself, but it seems there are formats besides object and scenario that are categorized as higher.Their unlock conditions are strict, and broad knowledge seems to be required as well.I do not know whether /VAR/-sama should know this much, but if the chance arises, please look at them.
962The Letters FormatThe letters format consists of horizontally long letters; at first, almost all of each is hidden and cannot be read.As you keep clicking the letter, points are spent and the area you can read expands.However, please be aware that if your points run out, you cannot expand the readable area any further.Also, letters generally have many stains and cannot be read clearly.Once you can read the whole area, the stains disappear and clear letters emerge.For details, please refer to the help when you select a letter reference.
1052The Report FormatThe report format is a report from a certain organization, but most of it is missing and cannot be read properly.By spending points, it is possible to add the missing pieces.クイックメニューBut, those pieces are scattered as in a puzzle, so you must return them to the proper positions with the mouse.Once all the pieces are fitted into their proper positions, the stains on the page disappear and clear letters emerge.For detailed instructions on handling the pieces, please refer to the help within the report screen.

1252About PointsHave you noticed that there are points like these?ポイントThese points are added by the main scenarios, by viewing references and by chatter.ポイントIn the letters and report formats, you must spend these points to expand the area that you can read.ポイントIn the letters format, the more paper you pull away, the more your points decrease.In the report format, points are spent each time you add a piece.
1340Reference LockVery rarely there is a reference that cannot even be viewed unless a set condition is met.Even if it is unlocked, you cannot enter the viewing screen unless the condition is met.The condition for unlocking the lock is not announced, so I am afraid you must search it out yourself.The one thing I can say is that the lock is released by viewing other references.
1441Influence of the References'Reference' is an English word meaning 'reference material', and it is no more than something to consult.However, this is an extremely unstable world. There is no telling what may happen from viewing a reference.If it is possible, I think you would do well to view the references actively.Later on, it may be of help to /VAR/-sama.
1572The OOPArt ReferenceI am terribly sorry. Due to a malfunction in this world, it seems that, very rarely, references that should appear much farther ahead can appear.But, hiding them for that reason would be dull, so I shall leave those that have appeared as they are.Shall we call these 'OOPArt references'? OOPArts are objects thought to be entirely out of place in the location or era in which they were discovered.Of course, there is no problem with viewing them, but since they appear because of such a malfunction, their appearance will probably be extremely indistinct.By spending the points you have earned, I believe their clarity will increase somewhat, but I do not think it will be enough for you to see them clearly.Once you reach the scenario in which they should originally be unlocked, you can view them with their usual clarity, so you may wish to wait until then.Well, they should appear much farther ahead, so there is no doubt that they will be an important hint.
