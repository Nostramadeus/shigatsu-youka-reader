172Jusatsu Arc [Killing by Curse]私にできることNatsumi Kogori senses who the victims of the flayed-body case are and that its culprit is Haruka Niimura, and flees into the mountains to protect her from the police. In the mountains, however, they are attacked by a monstrous figure in a Noh mask and red robes, armed with a nata and a hammer ― a shinigami ― and Haruka Niimura is left near death.\n　Erina Goto rushes to the scene with Daijiro Ise, but the shinigami is gone: only an unhurt Natsumi Kogori and a gravely injured Haruka Niimura. 204Jusatsu Arc [Killing by Curse]葛藤Natsumi Kogori is shown Ryoji Kogori's body. According to Daijiro Ise, the Megasawa City murders and the flayed-body case occur to match the memorial years of the rites for Eiichiro Niimura.\n　Back home, Natsumi Kogori sets about preparing her father's rites, but on seeing the letter from Ryoji Kogori that Erina Goto found, she realizes that something dreadful is stirring behind it all. Then Haruka Niimura is carried off by the shinigami, and they meet on the roof of their apartment building. The shinigami is revealed to be Mifuyu Niimura. Natsumi Kogori apologizes over Eiichiro Niimura, but is answered with "That is not the problem," and Mifuyu Niimura throws herself from the roof together with Haruka Niimura; from then on she is missing.

Haruka Niimura talks her round and she promises on the surface not to investigate, but means to go on alone. Her inquiries suggest the flayed-body case arose from the Niimura-Kogori feud, and she questions Haruka Niimura. Haruka Niimura claims she cursed Ryoji Kogori to death and caused the Megasawa City murders, but far too much of that makes no sense.\n　When the Kogori come home she stays till morning, then goes to their house with Haruka Niimura to prove the curse a sham. There, however, Natsumi Kogori, Akane Kogori and Mifuyu Niimura are already dead, and Haruka Niimura kills herself to protect Erina Goto from the curse.\n　The next day, rescued from the burning Kogori house, Erina Goto despairs of her uselessness and gives up all thought.
303Eden no Sakura Arc [Eden's Cherry]ようこそ、元木町へ！The incidents in the Arata settlement and the thing called Droga were born of Itohime being kept alive by Shigatsu Youka, and her aim was to grow Shigatsu Youka and go to Motoki-cho, where the Prince sleeps. But after Natsumi Kogori reasons with her, Itohime is invited to go to Motoki-cho together. Touched by that kindness, Itohime looks back on what she has done and asks that Shigatsu Youka itself be destroyed. In the end, Shigatsu Youka is wiped out by third-generation blood, and Itohime fades away thinking, "I want to be reborn by Natsumi Kogori's side."
202Jusatsu Arc [Killing by Curse]春花と魔女と死神On Haruka Niimura's advice, Natsumi Kogori hides at Erina Goto's house (not knowing why). Erina secretly contacts Daijiro Ise; it emerges that the Megasawa City murderer, the shinigami, is after Natsumi. The shinigami then bursts into the house, plainly inhuman, leaping abnormally and dodging bullets. Natsumi slips out and is drawn to Central Park by a strange voice.\n　There, Ryoji Kogori, who should be dead, appears as a ravaged corpse, says 'Believe in everyone,' and vanishes. The shinigami arrives, but under its mask is her mother, Akane Kogori. Akane dies by police gunfire; Mifuyu Niimura goes missing.\n　A month later, Motoki-cho grown hard to live in, Natsumi and Haruka move to their parents' family homes. But Haruka, who moved to the Arata settlement, falls out of contact. Two years later, Natsumi and Erina head there to find her.
194Saigo no Koe Arc [Last Voice]最後の声Erina Goto believes Chigaya Niimura is the ringleader of the curse-killings and presses her. Chigaya does not deny it, and intimidates Erina into stepping no further. But Chigaya's single remark, "I wanted to kill Mom too," makes Erina realize that her deduction was entirely wrong.\n　Meanwhile, Haruka Niimura, regretting that people have left the Arata settlement and that the settlement itself is in danger of dying out, resolves to protect it herself and says goodbye to Natsumi Kogori and the others. But just then, she hears the voice of the late Erika Niimura, asking her to protect the Arata settlement from afar.
195Meicho Arc [Clear Sign]過去へ馳せる夢Erina Goto surmises that the Megasawa City murders were an alibi contrivance using the rites for Eiichiro Niimura and the Kogori couple, and that Mifuyu Niimura, the principal offender, used a bug and the GPS function planted in Haruka Niimura's smartphone to bring the harm to Natsumi Kogori and the others as well. Haruka Niimura turns this around, lures Mifuyu Niimura to her home, and succeeds in arresting her while disguised as Eiichiro Niimura's mother. How the vanishing-trick crimes of the Megasawa City murders were made possible remains unknown, but that should come out in the questioning ahead.\n　Natsumi Kogori and Haruka Niimura, who have both lost their parents, come to live together in the Niimura house, and on that first night something impossible spreads out before the two of them.
