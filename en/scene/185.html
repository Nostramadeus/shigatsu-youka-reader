When I looked beside me, Sis was gazing outside with a sullen face.
Sis taking part in a family outing. Rare things do happen.
"Momoko, you're off from your part-time job tomorrow too, right? You can take it easy, can't you?"

Sis's attitude was the same as ever.
Sis used to be kind, but at some point she started behaving badly, and she even dropped out of high school.
These days, I hardly know what kind of life she leads outside the house.
Come to think of it, I had a feeling my phone had rung a little while ago.
―It seemed there'd been a call from Niimura-senpai.
Niimura-senpai was on a trip with the Kogori family.
If she was calling me, she was probably feeling awkward after all.
I should call her back right away.
"Hiii! It's your Goto-chan!"
I was still sleepy, but I greeted her in a high-energy voice.
"Oh, Goto. You at the amusement park yet?"
―Niimura-senpai, from what I heard, was feeling awkward with the Kogori family after all.
When I told Senpai the things she seemed to be losing sight of, she accepted them easily enough.
"You... do you always talk down to your senpai like that?"
"I'm not talking down to her or anything..."
"I don't know what you two are to each other, but you don't talk to a senpai like that."
After that, she shut her mouth.
Sis and I just can't get along, no matter what.
Kana-chan said it at the secret base in Arata.
That Sis and I were just a little out of step.
That it wasn't Sis avoiding me, but me avoiding Sis.
When she treats me like this, my resolve breaks.
―While all that was going on, we reached our destination.
"Amazing..."
Faced with the biggest amusement park in Japan, even Sis let out a sound of admiration.
"Let's drop our bags off at the hotel first."
"Okay!"
With Dad leading the way, we dropped our bags off at the hotel and went straight into the park.
As expected, the crowds were huge.
If I let my guard down even a little, I'd probably get separated from my family.
Come to think of it, I'd promised Niimura-senpai a video call.
On the screen were my senpai, lined up side by side on what looked like a beach.
"Hiii, you two! Enjoying your time with just the family?"
"Hey! You made it to the amusement park? 　Actually, that's some noise... Is the amusement park crowded after all?"
"It's amazing! Here, take a look! 　All these people, people, people!"
I tried to get the rides behind me on camera, but at my height it was pretty hard...
"You are way too short. There's nothing on screen but people. C'mon, forget the people, show us the rides or something."
"I'm stretching my arm out as far as it'll go here! 　Mmph-mph... can't breathe..."
Tossed around by the waves of people, I couldn't even stand still anymore―
"All the rides are... mmph, like hours-long waits... Huh? Dad and the others are gone... Squeeeze!"
All around me was nothing but walls of people.
I'd been careful, but I got separated from my family anyway.
Then, someone gave my hand a sharp tug.
The hand didn't feel like Dad's or Mom's.
"You... You're the smallest shorty in the whole Goto clan, so watch yourself."
The one who pulled me out was Sis.
"Sorry..."
"At times like that, it's not 'sorry,' it's 'thank you,' right?"
Sis quickly stepped away from me.
Dad and the others were right nearby.
At some point, I'd apparently hung up the call.
The popular rides all had waits of several hours, so we could only pick a few we wanted to ride.
Mom and Dad said they'd go along with whatever I wanted to ride.
Sis was standing a ways off from us, but she wouldn't meet our eyes.
"Sis, thanks for helping me earlier."
"............"
"Sis, which one do you want to ride?"
"Any of them's fine."
"Come on, just pick one at least. Today I'll even do my best to try a roller coaster."
I spread the park map open in front of Sis's face.
"This one, then."
Sis pointed at random without even looking at the map.
"Huh...? This one?"
"Ugh, I said this one, didn't I?"
What her finger pointed to was...
"M-Mom and I will wait here, so you two go on in! 　Oh, Mom, let's have a beer or something!"
"H-ho-ho-ho! Let's do that, Dad!"
"What!? You're kidding, right!? 　Wai..."
"............"

"Sis... why'd you pick this one?"
"I-it couldn't be helped! I wasn't looking at the map!"
This was Sis snapping back at me when she was the one in the wrong... or rather, she was rattled.
No wonder. We were about to go into this attraction.
"S-Sis... don't you ever leave my side!"
"You're in high school already, aren't you!? 　Wh-wh-what are you so scared of! What happened to your operation!?"
"The operation... failed the moment we picked this place..."
This place was so high-quality― in other words, the line was apparently short because it was too scary.
Fearfully, we stepped into the haunted house.
There were no cars to ride. It was the kind you walk through on your own feet.
"It's pitch-black..."
"O-o-of course it is! There's no such thing as a bright haunted house!"

"Aaaagh"
"Eeeeek"

"Why'd you suddenly yank my sleeve!? Don't scare me like that!"

"You nearly died at that funeral the other day, didn't you!? 　Something like this is nothing, right!?"

Trembling, we shuffled forward.
Even if someone tells me to go to a haunted spot, I don't find it especially scary.
Because there can't be anything unscientific in this world.
Haunted houses, on the other hand, I really can't handle.
Because in a haunted house, things are guaranteed to 'show up.'
I'm fully aware that whatever shows up is fake.
And they're fully aware that we walk into a haunted house thinking exactly that.
That's exactly why they calculate, down to the last detail, how to scare us.
That's exactly why, however much of a genius girl I am, taking on an operation drawn up by professionals in the field is far too reckless.
That's exactly why... it's so scary...
"Sis... what's that?"
When we'd finally made it 3 meters from the entrance, I pointed ahead, hiding behind Sis.
"Huh!? There's nothing there!"

"You call yourself a genius girl, don't you? Get a grip!"

At the ghost that suddenly appeared, we let out screams like never before.
My knees had no strength left at all, and my legs gave out under me.
At the same time, footsteps echoed, moving away from me.
"S-S-Sis...! 　Wait for me!"
My legs were shaking so much I couldn't stand up properly.
"Waaah! Don't leave me behind..."
It was so scary that I finally burst into tears.
"Sis, you... big idiooot! Waaah!"
Then someone appeared and tugged at my hand.
"I've been right here the whole time, though? 　The one that ran off just now was the ghost."
"Uuugh, Siiiis!"
I couldn't think about anything anymore, and I clung tight to Sis.
In the end, Sis dragged me out through the give-up exit.
Apparently, at a mere 5 meters traveled, it was a record-setting give-up.
"Uh... uh... hnguuuh..."
Even after we came out of the haunted house, my tears wouldn't stop.
"Oh, Momoko! Erina! How was it?"
Dad called out to us in a cheerful voice, without reading the mood at all.
"As you can see."
"What's wrong, Erina? Was it scary?"
"It was scary! Waaah!"
I couldn't let go of Sis, and just kept clinging to her tight.
The families around us were all having a great time, and I alone let my pitiful sobs echo out.
"Honestly, what a hopeless kid..."
