To the police,
"Don't tell my daughter yet,"
she said, but to the police, Senpai was a person of interest.

This is, with high probability, a number calling from a police station.

Senpai's forced smile.

"Don't cry, Haruka... I'm okay."

Kogori-senpai looks weak, but she's strong.
But when she tries to show us that strength, it gets harder for us.

"This incident, when I think about Senpai's dream, I get the feeling it's connected to her April sickness. And then, the incident from more than 10 years ago." [TN: A pun on gogatsu-byo, 'May sickness,' Japan's term for the May blues.]

"Please! Senpai... the person who's precious to me... please don't abandon her...!"
