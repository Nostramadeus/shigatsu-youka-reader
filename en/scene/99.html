Death's Neighbor

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-07 20:45 火 · 101 boxes

So the last one is Chiga-nee.
Going last looks like it'll be a bit tough, though...
"Well, naturally, the last one is Chigaya."
Chiga-nee's handwriting is so messy.
"Mm... So I'm last, then."
Maybe some of the alcohol had worn off. Her way of talking was back to normal.
"Mm... I live alone now. This is a story from after I started university in Tokyo, until I moved from my first room to the next one..."
―That day, I had come to Tokyo alone to look for a room to rent.
What kind of place would become my castle?
Living alone for the first time, I was excited.
A convenient place close to the station and the university.
And, as cheap as possible.
I was walking all over, looking for the next real-estate agency, when...
"Say, you there, miss with the odd eyes."
Suddenly I heard a middle-aged woman's voice.
When I turned toward the voice, there she was, sitting on a chair at a little table set up by the roadside.
She was a street-corner fortune-teller, the kind you might see on TV.
"Mm... Do you mean me?"
"Oh, odd eyes, just as I thought."
"Huh...?"
Hadn't she called me odd-eyed because she'd seen my eyes?
"And a stunning beauty on top of it! 　Let's see, how about I take a look at you, miss?"
"Mm... But I..."
"I don't want any money. I'm not out to push anything strange on you, either. I just got curious about you, miss. Anyway, it'll only take about 10 minutes."
Not knowing Tokyo well and not very wary, I sat down across from her as she told me to.
"Hmm..."
I thought she'd use the cards on the table, but instead she was staring at my face.
"Mm... You don't use the cards?"
"Ah, I meant to practice card reading, but my specialty is face reading. With you, miss, I'd better look properly..."
"Mm... Do I interest you that much?"
But the fortune-teller didn't open her mouth for a good minute.
"―I see. Miss... never make the easy choice. And close to you, there's someone who tells lies. Someone who deceives everyone with clever manipulation. It might even put your life at stake."
That's quite a frightening thing to say...
"You're an honest, straightforward person, miss, for better or worse. Whatever you do, don't let someone close to you deceive you... That's all!"
The fortune-teller really did finish without taking any money.
Deceived by someone close to me...
But I had come to Tokyo alone, so there was no one close to me.
Still, it was only fortune-telling. I just thought I'd be careful about the people I'd meet from now on.
And I kept looking for a new place to live, and―at last I found one.
A 10-minute walk from the university, a 10-minute walk from the station, a 5-minute walk from the supermarket.
And the rent was 20,000 yen.
I did think it was a little suspicious, but when I asked the real-estate agent,
"It's cheap because the building is 80 years old. Students these days don't want places that are too old, either... But it was renovated a little while ago, so there's heating and air conditioning, and a bathroom unit, and I don't think there's any problem living there."
the woman helping me answered with a smile.
"Mm... Then could I take a look at it?"
"Yes, certainly."
The apartment building she took me to did look old from the outside.
But I was used to old houses from Arata, so I didn't mind much.
Cockroaches or centipedes showing up don't rattle me, anyway.
Yes, I could live here without any problem.
Unlike the outside, the inside of the room was very clean.
She'd said it was renovated recently, too. Hadn't I come across a pretty good place?
But there was one thing that bothered me.
I couldn't see anyone who lived there.
How should I put it... there was no sign of anyone living there.
Having grown up in the mountains like this, I'm very sensitive to the presence of living things.
But at the time, I didn't think much of it.
Walking through the crowds of Tokyo had probably dulled that sense too, I figured.
I decided on this room.
The room I'd move into was Room 205.
On either side were 204 and 206, and 206 next door was the end room on that floor.
The building's name was... 'Shirinkan'.
―And then, on moving day...
With most of my things carried in, I thought I'd go greet the neighbors, and I left my room with a gift.
I knocked on the door next to mine.
Just because there had happened to be no sign of anyone that time didn't mean no one lived there.
206 was vacant, so the one I knocked on was 204.
But the door didn't open, and instead a knock came back.
"Mm...?"
It's not as if this were a toilet. Was she telling me to open it?
Was that how things were in Tokyo?
Puzzled, I opened the door myself.
The one who came out was―
A woman about my age, perhaps.
But her hair was a mess, her eyes were bloodshot, her face was pale, and she was terribly thin.
Just seeing her took me aback.
But what surprised me even more was...
that the room inside had not been renovated, and was as run-down as a ruin.
"Mm... I-I'm Chigaya Niimura. I've just moved in next door..."
I greeted her, trying to look calm, but she just kept staring at my face.
When I handed her the manju I'd brought as a gift, she took them quickly.
And then...
"I'll come by later..."
was all she said, and she slammed the door shut.

I was stunned, but I figured Tokyo really did have strange people, and went back to my own room.
The resident of Room 204 was strange too, but what bothered me more was the inside of that room.
It was so dilapidated you'd never think anyone lived there.
The woman was bad enough, but I couldn't stop thinking about the inside of that room.
Besides... there really were almost no residents.
Maybe this was a property with a dark history.
Its name was 'Shirinkan'.
I searched for it on my phone...
No results found for “Shirinkan”.

That's strange. Could that happen with a building's name these days?
I decided to look into it a little more tomorrow.
―That night.
"Mm..."
I woke to a sound like someone knocking on a door.
What on earth was that sound?
Was the neighbor doing something?
It wasn't a sound like knocking. It was, unmistakably, the sound of knocking.
I turned on the light and looked at the clock. It was 3 in the morning.
Did people come by even at this hour in Tokyo?
"Mm... Coming."
Uneasy as I was, I peeked through the peephole.
"............?"
I couldn't see anyone.
No, but I could still sense someone there.
"Eek...!?"
All of a sudden she was there: from the other side of the peephole, the woman next door was peering in at me.
And her whole body was convulsing. She could barely stay on her feet.
With eyes even more bloodshot than in the daytime, she was twisting the doorknob violently and knocking again and again.
I was too scared to open it, but... in those bloodshot eyes was a look, desperate and pleading, that made me feel her loneliness.
As if she were asking me for help...
I made up my mind and decided to open the door.
"Y... You came out... You finally came out...!"
When I opened the door, she forced a twitching smile on a face as gaunt as a dead woman's.
But even making a smile seemed to drain her strength.
She was holding something that looked like the manju box I'd given her as a greeting.
"U-Um...! 　This is... to thank you...!"
The woman pushed the box she was holding at me, almost forcing it into my hands.
"Mm... Thank you..."
Pressed to take it, I accepted the box.
If it was a thank-you for the manju, she could have come in the daytime...
The woman was trembling and staring at me.
At that moment, I noticed.
She had vivid odd eyes, green and blue.
Just like me.
Looking closely, I felt her face somehow resembled mine, too.
"Mm... Um... So you have odd eyes too."
When I said that,
she hurried back to her own room.
Creepy...
Even if this is Tokyo, this is just too much...
When I looked closely at the box I'd received, it was exactly the same as the manju box I'd given her.
I thought she'd returned it, but from the feel of it, the contents had clearly changed.
It was really... creepy...
That exchange had left me wide awake.
What on earth was in this box...
A dead rat, or a box full of grotesque bugs...
When I shook the box, there seemed to be just one hard thing inside.
I steeled myself and decided to open the box.
"Mm...? What's this..."
Inside was an old wooden board.
And on that board...

Shirinkan [TN: Written here 'death-next-door hall', not 'poem-grove hall'.]
had been written with a brush.
What is this...! That's even creepier...!
Bugs or dead animals would at least make sense, since you'd know it was harassment.
Was this a pun on this building's 'Shirinkan' (poem grove) and 'Shirinkan' (death next door)?
"Ugh...!"

For a moment, I was hit by dizziness and nausea.
Maybe because I'd seen something like this, I felt quite unwell.
I decided to rest right away.
I didn't know what the neighbor meant by it, but I'd throw this sign away tomorrow.
I crawled into bed.
My very first day of life in Tokyo, and it's a disaster...
―I woke to the sound of a thunderstorm.
Something was dripping onto my face, drop by drop.
Was the roof leaking!?
I sat up in surprise, and...
The room looked completely different.
It was ruined and falling apart, and the belongings I'd brought had decayed as if decades had passed.
What on earth was going on!? It was just like the room next door...!
When I looked at the floor,
there was that wooden board I'd received.
―Shirinkan
Frightened, I tried to leave the room.
But... I staggered and collapsed.
There was no strength left anywhere in my body...
It felt as if I hadn't eaten in days.
When I looked at my hand...
it was so thin it didn't seem like mine, nothing but skin and bone.
This is the same as that room next door, and the woman next door...!
I had felt unwell ever since I received this sign last night.
Could this be happening because of the sign...
Which would mean that woman gave it to me knowing what it would do?
Leaning on the wall, I made my way to the door on my own and tried to open it.
But it wouldn't budge, as if something were holding it shut with great force.
This is bad...!
With this sign here, won't I rot away just like this room...!
Thinking that, I tried to throw it out the window, but the window was shut tight and wouldn't open either.
I didn't even have the strength to make a hole in the wall.
I have to call somewhere for help...!
When I picked up my phone,
it crumbled away like sand.
There was no way out of this room.
I didn't have the strength to shout.
I didn't have the stamina to search this room.
Unless someone came... I wouldn't be saved...
I lay down on the bed, wet from the leak and covered in stains.
I thought it was a good property, and it had a trap like this...
There's no such thing as a deal that good in this world.
As if I could see time itself, the ceiling boards peeled away and the wallpaper grew older and older.
The room decayed even more than before.
When I looked at my hand again...
it was so wasted it was a wonder I was alive.
It felt as if only this room and I were moving through time faster and faster, heading straight for death.
Along with that passing time, extreme hunger came over me.
But all the food had rotted.
Unless someone brought me food, I would probably starve to death...
A knock...?
Someone knocking on the door...!
Someone came!
I summoned the last of my strength and went to the door.
But the door couldn't be opened from my side.
Instead, I knocked from my side.
Then, with help from the other side, it opened easily.
Now... now I'm saved!
But when I saw the person on the other side, I was shocked.
It was me, the very me who had come with a gift just yesterday.
"Mm... Um... I'm Chigaya Niimura. I've just moved in next door..."
My head couldn't keep up. On instinct, I snatched the gift she held out and shut the door.

I couldn't bear the hunger any longer.
Like a ghoul devouring a corpse, I devoured the 20 manju.
So happy I could hardly stand it, with tears, drool, and snot streaming down...
But even as I stuffed the manju into my mouth, I was seized by fierce regret.
However blinded by food I'd been, why hadn't I just run out of the room then?
But now, the sweet, soft texture of the manju and the way they slid down my throat were irresistible.
At that moment, I was a mindless zombie devouring whatever caught my eye.
Once I had eaten all the manju and calmed down a little, I thought about what to do next.
This time, no mistakes. I'll think only about escaping.
Yesterday's me should be able to open the door in the middle of the night.
And if I hand over this sign...!
No... if I did that, the me in the next room would go through the same thing.
But if this went on, I would certainly die.
I had no choice left.
But I didn't know what to do after handing over the sign.
I didn't even know whether the me in Room 204 had been saved after that.
―When I came to, it was already the middle of the night.
I'd been so exhausted that I must have fallen asleep without realizing.
And I was terribly hungry again.
My body was badly weakened, and the room smelled of nothing but nasty mold.
What time was it?
If it was 3 in the morning, the door might open.
Feeling my way, I put the sign in the box and took hold of the doorknob.
And the door opened easily.
I couldn't let this chance slip by!
Middle of the night or not, I couldn't care about that.
I would push this onto the me next door.
Fighting not to collapse, I knocked on the next room's door.
The room I'd come out of was 205, and the room I knocked on was 206.
This was the end room.
Ngh...! Wake up already, me!
That terrible hunger came at me again, as if it would crush me!
Hurry... hurry...!
I nearly blacked out and slumped against the door, but desperately pulled myself upright.
Then out came someone exactly like yesterday's me.
Yes...! Now I'll be saved...!
A smile rose up in me for the first time in a long while.
"U-Um...! This is... to thank you...!"
I pushed the sign at her, almost forcing it into her hands.
But at that instant, the baton of death passed to the other me.
To live, I was trying to kill this person.
She thanked me with a puzzled look, but
"So you have odd eyes too..."
when she had said that much, I couldn't bear the faint pangs of conscience that welled up in me.
I tried to run away instead of going back to my room, but I could barely even stand anymore.
At this rate, even if I didn't go back to the room, I would die.
With the last of my strength, I went back to my room.
If this saves me... then that's fine...
If I die, then it can't be helped.
I did... all... that... I... could.
―From outside, I could hear a faint hubbub...
When I came to, I was lying on the floor of my room, back the way it had been.
When I looked at my hands, they weren't thin, and my body felt fine.
I was saved...
What on earth had that sign been?
What was... Shirinkan?
Terrified, I moved out of the room right away.
After that, I passed in front of Shirinkan just once.
The outside was as old as ever.
But the rooms were renovated, out of keeping with it.
And the baton of death.
Shirinkan, where neighbor brings death to neighbor.
The other me I had given the sign to had no one left to pass it on to.
What became of her?
Is she alive, or has she rotted away?
Is she still searching for food, like a zombie that has lost its mind?
Maybe... even now...
"That's the end of my story."
"Wow... So it's a time-loop story, in a way... What happened to the Chiga-nee in 206...?"
"Mm... It's just a ghost story, after all. Maybe I made it up, maybe it was a dream..."
