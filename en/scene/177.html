Abandonment

Jusatsu Arc [Killing by Curse] · Shiyo 800-04-09 10:00 木 · 33 boxes

Dazzling light came piercing into my eyeballs.
The glare sent a sharp pain deep behind my eyes, and my eyelids grew heavy on their own.
"Oh, she's awake!"
A voice I knew―
　Where am I? My head was pounding, and the skin all over my body stung.
"Mooom! Erina's opened her eyes!"
Erina... is my name.
This voice... it's Sis.
―Before long, my mom came.
"Ah, Erina! Thank goodness!"
Mom stroked my cheek.
But, I could barely feel Mom's hand.
I realized something like bandages was wrapped all over my face.
Just then, it came back to me with a start.
Kogori-senpai's home and the carnage in it, and the one who stabbed a knife into her throat to try to kill herself: Niimura-senpai.
But, though I remembered up to that point, I had no memory at all of what came after.
Had that really been real?
"Hey, when is it now!? 　What month, what... Cough! Cough... Hk..."
I tried to raise my voice, but the back of my throat hurt, and I couldn't speak well.
"Today is April 9. You slept through the night. It's 10:30 in the morning now."
Mom answered, looking worried but putting on a smile.
"Through the night...? Niimura... -senpai... what about her...?"
When I said that, Mom's face clouded a little.
"Mom, why aren't you telling her? You have to tell her, don't you? At this point..."
Before Mom could answer, Sis started talking.
"Stay calm and listen. Everyone, they're all dead, I heard. The friend who came over yesterday and her mom, that senpai you love so much too, and her mom too..."
So Niimura-senpai really did, with the knife, kill herself...?
To protect me from the curse...?
―Sis went on.
"After that, the Kogoris' home caught fire, and only you, at the front door, got pulled out just in time, they said. Everyone else... the fire had already spread, and even the bodies are in pretty bad shape, apparently."
After that, there was a fire?
What does that mean? I don't understand... I don't understand any of it...
"So, you're in this hospital now, admitted. That's where things stand."
Then, before I even had time to think, there was a knock on the hospital room's door, which stood open.
"Oh, so you've come to."
I knew him. It was the detective who looked like an old man, the one I'd met at the police station yesterday.
"You're Erina Goto-san, right? You had a rough night last night."
The detective spoke to me with a smile.
But even hearing that, no feeling at all welled up.
I felt neither nervous nor annoyed, and his smile didn't put me at ease either.
"Sorry, when you've only just woken up, but there's something I'd like to ask you."
With that, the detective began rummaging around in his bag.
Was this questioning?
"This, we found in your pocket. Any idea what it is?"
Inside the clear plastic bag the detective took out was something white and flat.
"Huh...? 　Huh...!?"
What the detective showed me, it was...
Two paper dolls.
And on the paper dolls―

Haruka Niimura
Mifuyu Niimura

Their surfaces were soiled with something like vomit.
"I-I don't know..."
I was too scared to say I knew.
It was the first time I'd seen paper dolls of those two, but I knew what this meant.
"I see... By the way, this junk stuck to the surface, it's actually Haruka Niimura-san's―"
"I'm telling you I don't know!!!"
Forgetting even the pain in my throat, I shouted.
Mom and Sis both froze at my voice, and the hospital room went silent.
"Excuse me. If that's so, that's fine. Well then, take care of yourself."
The detective left briskly.
What is this... What is all this...
"Erina, are you okay? Want something to drink?"
Mom said that to me, but it didn't register.
"But, Niimura-san's dad, yesterday was the anniversary of his death, right? And that senpai you like never knew that the whole time, that's weird, isn't it? 　You, I'd think, would have noticed ages ago."
"Momoko... that's enough."
"Oh... yeah, sorry, sorry."
But, Sis's words dealt me one last shock.
Come to think of it... that time...
"Yeah, I lived here in Motoki-cho till kindergarten, but my dad's job had us move. 3 years ago my dad finally got transferred overseas, so it turned into a posting away from the family. Just me and Mom came back to this town."
"Our moms were childhood friends, and that's how we've been friends too, ever since we were old enough to remember. When it was decided 3 years ago that we'd come back to Motoki-cho, Natsumi told me, 'The place next door is empty.' I begged Mom, and we moved into the place we're in now."
I see... So that's what it was...
I hadn't noticed at all...
The things I had overlooked, there were still so many more of them.
Something this small, why hadn't I noticed it?
If I had noticed it back then, my senpais might not have had to die.
But, I was done with thinking.
Having a head that turns oddly fast is nothing but torment.
Some genius girl. A title like that, I've had enough of it.
Now, I don't want to think about anything.
It's just as Niimura-senpai said. If everyone has died, then now... none of it matters.
If it's a curse... then now, that's fine...
The Operation... failed.
Through the open window of the hospital room,
cherry petals came fluttering in.
Mom... Sis...
Cherry blossoms, they're beautiful, aren't they.
