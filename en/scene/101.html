Morning of April 8

Jusatsu Arc [Killing by Curse] · Shiyo 800-04-08 06:30 水 · 50 boxes

I couldn't clearly remember what I had dreamed.
I had gone to bed, and it was already morning.
But I had a feeling that some strange noise had disturbed my sleep.
However, a few seconds after I woke up, its source became clear.
A patrol car was racing along energetically with its siren blaring.
Here in this Motoki-cho, the sound of patrol cars is rare.
Had there been an accident or something?
"Might as well get up..."
It was 30 minutes earlier than my usual wake-up time, but I was oddly wide awake.
Since morning, the whole area had had a restless feel to it.
"Mom's... still asleep, huh."
Neither in the kitchen nor in the living room was there any sign of Mom.
I plopped down on the living room sofa and turned on the TV.
"Look at this! Here at this park in Tohoku, too, the cherry blossoms are beginning to open, and they're expected to be at their best next weekend!"
It seemed a famous anchorwoman was reporting live from a big park.
Cherry blossom news first thing in the morning. The worst.
I immediately changed the channel.
"Yesterday's baseball game was a real nail-biter! 　After a fierce back-and-forth it went into extra innings, but―"
I don't really understand sports.
"Morning..."
Mom emerged sluggishly from her room.
"Morning. Something's been noisy since early this morning, huh..."
"Yeah... it has..."
Supposedly a morning person, Mom was looking awfully sleepy.
From somewhere in the living room came a cell phone ringtone.
It was Mom's phone.
"Oh! I did it again!"
Mom hurried over to her phone and picked it up.
"I forgot to charge it again... It's an old phone, so it won't charge much by now... Oh? Natsumi, it's an email from school."
Mom showed me the phone screen.
It seemed to be an email from our homeroom teacher.
Subject: School is closed today.
Body:
Good morning, everyone in Class 2-2.
This is your homeroom teacher, Mukai.
This morning we were contacted by the police and notified that our students are to be placed under a stay-at-home order.
For this reason, school is closed today. Please do not go out, and remain at home.
If there are any developments in the situation, they will be posted on the school website.
That is all.
"So they say. Look after the house, okay?"
"Is it some kind of incident after all? It's been kind of noisy since this morning."
"Who knows... That's not something for us ordinary folk to poke our noses into."
"A whole day under a stay-at-home order, huh. What should I do..."
"You're spending it with Haruka-chan next door, aren't you?"
"Hmm... but..."
The truth was, the day before yesterday, the DVD of a movie I'd been looking forward to had arrived.
When I heard there was no school, I had thought I would enjoy an elegant cinema time all by myself, but...
"She came to visit you yesterday, so go show your face and keep her company. She's probably bored, with school suddenly closed."
"Yeah..."
"Come on, come on, don't just stand there spacing out. Get ready to go over to Haruka-chan's place."
"Wouldn't it be a bother to just barge in... Even if it's Haruka..."
"Then you can just invite her over here, can't you?"
Mom seemed worried about leaving me alone, with the slightly unsettling air this morning.
"Well then, look after the house! And get along nicely with Haruka-chan!"
A little after 8, Mom wolfed down the leftovers from last night's dinner and left for work.
"Maybe I'll try calling Haruka..."
I went back to my room and picked up my smartphone.
My parents had only just bought it for me the other day, as a present for moving up a year.
"No, no, I can see Haruka anytime, and deliberately watching a DVD alone could be nice too."
My brand-new smartphone rang, and the incoming-call screen showed “Mom.”
"Hello? What's wro―"
"Sorry, Natsumi! 　I forgot to take out the trash! Do it for me, please!"
"Really, I was just about to have my elegant DVD time..."
"Sorry, sorry! Thanks!"
Mom left it at that and hung up on me.
Around Mom's age, do people turn into scatterbrained middle-aged ladies like this?
I didn't want the trash left in the house either, so I reluctantly left my room.
When I got to the entryway, a single big trash bag was sitting there.
To leave something this big sitting here and forget it...
"Heave-hooooo!"
Holding the trash bag in both hands, I shoved the door open with my whole body.
And then,
"Ouch!"
The moment the door hit something, I heard a woman's small shriek.
"Ah, sorry, Obasan!"
"Oh my, Natsumi-chan! Good morning, good morning!"
Apparently the door I had opened had hit Obasan.
Her voice was high-energy first thing in the morning, as always.
"Good morning. Sorry, the trash bag was big, so I kind of rammed the door open."
"Hohoho, I'm fine! Natsumi-chan, are you taking out the trash too?"
"Yeah. Oh, you too, Obasan."
"That's right! It's getting warmer, so you have to put the kitchen scraps out right away!"
I headed with Obasan to the apartment building's elevator.
"Natsumi-chan, how are you feeling? I believe my Haruka went to visit you yesterday?"
"I'm feeling good today, thanks!"
"Oh my! That's wonderful!"
Now that she mentioned it, I realized my body was in really good shape today.
The weather was calm too, with no wind at all.
Anyway, I had heard that Haruka's mom here was a childhood friend of my mom.
It was relatively warm today, but even so, she was wearing a revealing combination: sleeveless top and miniskirt.
An outfit even someone my age would hesitate to put together.
It's hard to say out loud, but at her age, I really have to wonder about dressing like that.
She's youthful and beautiful and has a good figure, I admit.
But I feel like it's starting to be a bit of a stretch.
Or rather, the way she talks sounds more like a middle-aged lady than my mom does.
Maybe she gets all hyped up to show off how young she is, but it seems to backfire.
How should I put it... Yeah, it's painful...
"Come to think of it, there's no school today. Maybe some kind of incident happened..."
"Who can say! Us ordinary folk wouldn't know!"
"Ha, my mom said the same thing."
"Hohohoho! Akane and I have been one heart and one body since the day we were born! 　Since birth, XX years together with Akane!"
She doesn't need to hide her age, when I already know she's the same age as my mom.
Apparently they're even closer than Haruka and I are, and partly because of that bond, we live next door to each other.
But why is her personality so different from my mom...
Mom, believe it or not, was apparently quite popular when she was young, as a cool beauty or whatever they call it.
I think Haruka and I are fairly alike, but when I'm told these two have been best friends since they were little, it just doesn't click.
For the honor of Haruka's mom, let me say this: she is a very good person.
But silence with this person is somehow awkward.
Whether I like it or not, if I don't bring up some topic, I can't relax...
Oops. That did nothing at all to restore her honor...
The elevator reached the first floor.
The trash collection area is right in front of the apartment building.
"Oo oh -up ... Haah, that was heavy!"
Suddenly letting out a voice even more middle-aged than her real age, she dusted her hands off, clap, clap.
"Obasan, your bag looked heavy."
"I was cleaning out the fridge, and a ton of expired food turned up!"
Looking at the bag Obasan had thrown out, I could see rotting vegetables, eggshells and such.
"Haruka has a weak stomach! Once it gets warm, we have to be careful!"
"Come to think of it, Haruka gets food poisoning about once a year, but she had an upset stomach the day before yesterday too, and it was still bothering her a little yesterday, right? Everyone at our place has a strong stomach, though."
"Ugh, how awful! It's been noisy since this morning. Maybe something did happen after all!"
Obasan seemed to have noticed the restless air as well.
The two of us got into the elevator again and pressed the button for our floor.
"Hey, hey, Natsumi-chan! If you don't mind, today could you keep Haruka company? She was saying there's nothing to do with school closed and she's bored. And I'm leaving for work soon. We've got some delicious tea at our place too!"
"Oh, and also! I'll leave lunch money with Haruka, so if you like, would you have lunch with Haruka!? That girl may not look it, but she gets pretty lonely, and I think she'd be happy if Natsumi-chan were with her! Oh, but that girl's stomach isn't doing so well right now!"
All of a sudden she had rattled this off at me, like a middle-aged lady's tongue twister.
In short, how about hanging out today at Haruka's house? That was the idea.
My elegant DVD time alone...
But it's not as if I'm choosing solo cinema time out of firm conviction.
"Then I guess I'll come over. The uncut DVD of the movie I went to see last year with Haruka just came."
"Oh, good! Haruka'll be so happy! Come on, come in, come in!"
"Ah, wait a second, Obasan! I'm in my loungewear, so I'll go change."
"Okaaay! I'll let Haruka know. Come over when you're ready!"
She gave me takoyaki yesterday, so showing up empty-handed is definitely no good.
Rummaging through the kitchen, all I found was a teeny bit of chocolate candy.
This is... sakiika.
Other than that, there were only Dad's snacks for drinking.
Well... even sakiika is better than nothing. Dad won't be home for a while anyway.
What else.
I picked up the DVD I had just bought.
There was no need to watch it by myself at all.
If I watch it with Haruka, that's two birds with one stone.
Besides, Haruka is sure to be happy too.
I locked the door, and just as I was about to press the intercom next door,
"Ouch!"
"Oh, sorry, Natsumi-chan!"
This time, the door Obasan opened hit me.
"No, no. Can I come in?"
"Yeah, Haruka's waiting for you! I'm off to work, so please keep her company!"
"Okaaay! Good luck at work!"
Trading places with Obasan, I went into Haruka's home.
