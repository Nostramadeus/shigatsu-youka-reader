Alibi

Meicho Arc [Clear Sign] · Shiyo 800-04-08 13:50 水 · 33 boxes

"Senpai, sorry to interrupt while you're drunk on victory, but Kogori-senpai happens to be away right now. From the look of it, she won't be back for quite a while."
"Uh, yeah... Right."
So one way or another, we'd smoothly gotten Natsumi out of the way.
"Um, where were we?"
"About your phone being bugged, Senpai."
"Right, right. So you think Natsumi's dad planted a bug in my phone?"
"It's a possibility. I don't have proof yet, though..."
Natsumi's dad doing something like that? I couldn't see why.
After all, it would mean Natsumi's dad had been helping the shinigami.
And though it wasn't confirmed yet, the one who'd been murdered was Natsumi's dad.
"Then shouldn't we go to the police after all..."
"That's true... But something's nagging at me."
"Going to the police might mean letting Kogori-senpai know about her dad's death. Then the police would hold Senpai for a while. The shinigami must have predicted that much, too."
"So I think going to the police might be going exactly according to the shinigami's plan. If that happens, I have a feeling Senpai would be in real danger..."
"Huh? Then the shinigami's a police officer!?"
"No, I don't think it's that..."
"Hmm, I don't really get it... Let me sort this out for a sec."
"Got it. Then I'll explain it in order, starting from 12 years ago."
"Yeah, go ahead."
Goto took out her notes and flipped through them.
"April 8, 12 years ago: Niimura-senpai's father died. Then April 8, 10 years ago. The first Megasawa City case, where two second-graders who had been bullying you, Senpai, were killed by someone. I think your 'curse,' Senpai, was probably what triggered it."
"You're not denying the curse?"
"Only as a trigger. Other than their bullying you, Senpai, can you think of any reason second-graders would get murdered?"
A 7-year-old being killed because some adult held a grudge was hard to imagine.
I still couldn't see it as anything but my curse coming true, though.
"No, I can't think of any..."
"Right, it wasn't murder out of a grudge. Strictly speaking, the person who killed them held no grudge."
"Something about the way you said that bothers me..."
"Listen. Everything started with the case 10 years ago. I don't think your dad's accident has much to do with it. It doesn't have much to do with it, that's all. I'm not saying it's unrelated."
True, the first two to die were two kids with no connection to Dad.
The four killed after that shouldn't have had any connection to Dad either.
"And then the Megasawa City cases 3 years ago and 6 years ago. Those were simply imitations of the case 10 years ago, or just the same method used again."
"So that means we can treat the three Megasawa City cases as a set."
"Yes. And today's case is the only one where the victim isn't someone you cursed on purpose, Senpai. There were paper dolls, but if someone else prepared them..."
"Hey, wait a sec. The only one who could do that is..."
Nobody but my mom could. Goto suspects Mom?
"Senpai, you want to save Kogori-senpai, right?"
"Of course I do..."
Goto was pressing me on something that hardly needed asking by now.
This was a warning from Goto: "Face the facts."
"If that's who the shinigami really is, everything links up into one line. Don't you think so?"
"............"
I couldn't say anything back, but I didn't want to think my own mom was the shinigami.
"Do you think it would've been better if the shinigami were someone else? 　Someone appeared three times because you wished for it, Senpai, and did it for you. And you'd rather that had been someone else?"
"I know. It's way too selfish. I really was grateful to the shinigami. But for it to be Mom..."
They say parents will do anything for their child's happiness.
Doting on me as truly as she does... Mom would...
But...
"But... But we have alibis...! Me, and Mom too...!"
"Exactly. So let's try a different angle. Why do the crimes only ever happen on April 8? Leaving the curse out of it, what logic does the culprit use to pick this day?"
"Only the culprit would know that... I think Dad's the shinigami, so isn't it timed to the anniversary of his death..."
Apparently psychopaths and thrill killers will deliberately leave things at the scene, or commit their crimes by a set rule, to leave a trace of what they've done.
If it isn't a curse, that's what I'd think it is...
"Think about it more simply. They targeted only the days of the memorial services for your dad. This is deliberately manufacturing an alibi!"
"So after all, Goto, you're flat-out saying my mom's the shinigami. Say it straight...!"
Goto had been choosing words with no edge to them, but that started to get on my nerves.
"Yes."
Goto gave only that one-word answer.
Goto's reasoning was something I wanted to push back against somehow.
But Goto's words, "Senpai, you want to save Kogori-senpai, right?" came back to me.
"Fine. Let's assume Mom's the culprit. Then how did she kill six people?"
I swallowed my anger and raised it as calmly as I could.
"I'd like to try one more different angle. Senpai, do you want to save Kogori-senpai no matter what?"
Why does she keep repeating this stuff?
"Don't make me say it over and over...! Of course I do!"
"Even if it means killing someone?"
What kind of question is this? Wanting to save Natsumi even if I have to kill someone?
I'd never thought about it, but if killing someone meant Natsumi would be saved...
No, but...
"I don't know... I've never thought about it. Do I have to kill somebody today to save Natsumi?"
"No, that's not it. I just figured the culprit probably felt the same way. Say Kogori-senpai is about to be killed by a murderer right in front of you, and you, Niimura-senpai, are holding a knife. What do you do?"
"Well, obviously...! I'd want to save her, even if I died taking the killer with me..."
"I'm sure Kogori-senpai would say the same thing."
"Goto, that's enough. You don't need to tiptoe around my feelings. We're short on time, so keep going."
Goto was being careful of my feelings as she talked, in her own way, but by now that only got in the way.
"You still don't get it? 　Each time, two people were killed at almost the same time. That means the Megasawa City case had at least two people doing the killing. A pair willing to commit murder for you, Senpai, and for your mom... there's no one it could be but them!"
"No way...!"
"The ones who did the killing in the Megasawa City case are Kogori-senpai's parents."
It can't be...! Those two, for my sake?
"You said your two moms have been childhood friends and best friends since they were born, right, Senpai? Her own daughter was the reason her best friend's husband died― I don't think it's that much of a leap to exploit a weakness like that and make them commit murder by proxy."
"That's going way too far...!"
"Then what about the bug in your phone, Niimura-senpai? How do you explain that?"
"Ngh...!"
It was galling, but thinking of it that way, it did explain the curse.
Natsumi's dad―if he'd lent a hand in the Megasawa City case, it wouldn't be strange for him to be involved in today's case somehow.
Even planting a bug, maybe―
The bug and Natsumi's parents having been the shinigami: it was just one assumption being used to prove another.
But if it wasn't a curse, it was entirely possible.
If it was for our alibi that Natsumi's parents carried it out, they could fool the police without any curse at all.
To begin with, Megasawa City itself had once been a city built on some so-called evil cult, and now, the other way around, people there loathe anything like a curse.
That might be exactly why rumors of a curse spread so easily, why residents' testimony lost its sense of reality, and why the police investigation ran into trouble.
At last, I too was convinced the curse was a sham.
"Goto, I get it... I've finally woken up too. Done your way, Goto, nobody needs a curse. If anything, all that crazy talk about a curse would help fool the people around them."
But those crimes were said to be physically impossible for any human. Could that be explained, too?
