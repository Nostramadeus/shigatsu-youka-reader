Police Officer's Face

Jusatsu Arc [Killing by Curse] · Shiyo 800-04-08 16:00 水 · 43 boxes

The place we arrived at was Zahha.
So he meant to let us talk here at our leisure.
"Welcome. A party of two?"
As always, there were a lot of groups that looked like high-school students.
We took our seats in the smoking section we were shown to.
"Oh, shoot. Um, sorry, out of my usual habit I went and picked the smoking section, but..."
"Huh? Oh, that's fine, a little thing like that."
Ise-san scratched his head, looking awkward, but I flashed him a grin in reply.
Besides, since the customers were almost all high-school students, there was no one in this smoking booth.
It was perfect for talking about the incident.
"Welcome. Here is our new spring menu."
Just as the waitress was about to set down the menu,
"Y-you!"
Ise-san suddenly stood up and, like fireworks, made his cheeks bloom bright red.
"Wh-what is it, sir..."
The waitress opened her eyes perfectly round.
My eyes too went to Ise-san and were nailed there.
And then, Ise-san thrust his police badge at the waitress.
"I-I-I am a police officer! I am by no means a shady individual! 　Just because I have a pretty girl like this with me... by no means... by no means!"
"Huh?"
"Whaa?"
The waitress and I tilted our heads at the same moment, voicing question marks that came out of who knows which hole.
"Listen! Pretty girls, as a rule, are to be admired, and laying a hand on them must never, ever happen! This is the fundamental principle of the Motoki Pretty Girl Appreciation Society! Sexual crimes against pretty girls, all the more so, are beyond outrageous!

"U-um, sir, I understand, so, your order..."
"Oh... yeah... my apologies... I just..."
Ise-san quieted down and took his seat.
"Um, have you had lunch already? If not, order whatever you like."
"No, I've already eaten! Thank you very much!"
Truthfully, I wanted to eat the steak set from earlier and the spring parfait, but I had to hold back at least a little.
Here, like a proper high-school girl, I'd go with the drink bar and a strawberry mille-feuille.
―When I had finished ordering and come back from the drink bar, Ise-san heaved a big sigh.
And then Ise-san, meaning to drink his coffee, put his lips to the cup.
"But, Ise-san, you're pretty much one of those, aren't you? What they call a... hunk?"
When I complimented him with a grin, Ise-san spewed out his coffee spectacularly.
"Cough! Cough!"
"Ise-san, you okay?"
"M-my nose..."
Ise-san wiped his mouth and nose with a paper napkin, and I wiped up the coffee spattered over the table.
It was the first time I'd seen someone spray it out in a mist, like in a manga.
"G-Goto-chan... Don't go saying strange things all of a sudden..."
It seemed Ise-san's way of calling me had become "Goto-chan."
If he sprayed coffee next time, there was a chance it would land on me.
Maybe I should be careful about what I say and do too.
But, there was one thing that bothered me.
Ever since we met, I had intuitively held a doubt about Ise-san.
Whether it was all right to check it or not, I didn't know.
But for the talk we were about to have, I absolutely had to make sure.
"Hey, Ise-san..."
"Hm?"
Ise-san took another mouthful of coffee.
And then, I flashed Ise-san a grin.


"Ise-san, are you a lolicon?"
"Man, I ate and ate! I can't eat another bite!"
"In the end, Haruka, you sampled more than a whole serving's worth..."
Having come to the pizza fair at Motoki Station, we enjoyed the sample pizzas they had prepared in large numbers.
I had wondered what kind of person an Italian chef would be, but when I saw his face I finally understood.
This Italian chef, he was someone who was on TV a lot.
Italian though he was, he wielded fluent Tsugaru dialect; he was the man of the hour, making living rooms roar with laughter with his lively talk.
He always wore a white chef's coat, but he gave off a stronger impression of a variety-show personality than of a chef.
But, since this morning's incident had happened, wouldn't this chef too find it hard to work in the mood Motoki-cho was in today?
Or so I had thought, but he was a pro, just as you'd expect.
As if to smash the gloomy mood, he acted cheerful and showed off his witty talk and his smile.
With such a popular chef coming, the pizza fair's huge success probably goes without saying.
And he was a professional chef, just as you'd expect. Not only his talk, but his pizza too was superb.
Perhaps drawn along by the packed pizza fair, this whole area was noisy.
"I made sure to get take-out ones too, so today's a really good day!"
Haruka looked very satisfied.
Even though there'd been a murder like that, calling it a good day.
Haruka looked very satisfied, but there was one big problem.
"Hey, Haruka... how are you going to carry that home?"
"Huh? By bike, obviously... ...or not. It won't all fit... Haha, what'll I do..."
Haruka was happily at a loss.
"Whoa, a call. Sorry, Natsumi, hold this for me."
Haruka, with that, handed the pizza boxes over to me, with a thump.
Ugh... pretty heavy...
"Oh, it's from somebody in my class.
Yes, hello? Oh, what's up? Yeah..."
At first she was chatting happily, but―
"Huh? Goto...?"
Giddy until just a moment ago, Haruka's voice suddenly changed to a tense tone.
"Natsumi... Goto's... at Zahha with a suspicious person, they said..."
Don't tell me Goto had caught the culprit's eye and been carried off?
But going to Zahha for that would be strange.
Or else, of all things, compensated dating?
No, Goto of all people wouldn't do something like that.
But it was certain that Goto was with a suspicious man.
"Hey, Haruka, can you call Goto?"
"Yeah..."
"Ojisan, sorry, it's a call."
"O-Ojisan...?"
"Hi-iii, it's Goto! What's up, Niimura-senpai?"
"Goto! Are you safe!? Are you with a suspicious person right now!?"
Suspicious person...?
I turned my eyes to Ise-san.
"Ahahaha! This man is a police officer! A lot happened while I was at the park, and it turned out he's going to tell me about the incident!"
"What the heck, scaring me like that... damn, those guys and their weird info..."
"It's really noisy on your end. Are you out somewhere?"
"Yeah! I took Natsumi along, and this time it's the pizza fair by the station! But I bought too much, and how am I gonna carry it home..."
"The moment you hear 'pizza,' you lose all restraint... At times like that, you should use the same-day delivery service at the station. I'm pretty sure they'll do it for 500 yen. And don't drag Kogori-senpai around too much!"
"Ooh, just what I'd expect from the genius girl Goto! You saved me! Thank you!"
Still, so I'm with a suspicious person, huh.
Come to think of it, I'd vaguely felt eyes on me for a while now.
When I looked out past the smoking booth, everyone quickly looked away.
"Sorry, Ojisan! 　Okay, then, let's talk about the incident!"
I flashed him a grin.
But, Ise-san's face was somehow grim.
"Now, now, there are days like that, so please don't be down!"
"The one you were just talking to... Haruka Niimura-san?"
"Huh? That's right, but... Do you know Niimura-senpai?"
"I thought so... Niimura-san's family had it hard, I once heard from a senior of mine."
And so it was from the mouth of Ise-san, showing me a police officer's face for the first time, that I learned of the death of Niimura-senpai's father.
