The sensation of the adrenaline level rising.
The sensation of being filled with noradrenaline.
Abnormal brain waves. A rising heart rate. Curiosity that can't be held down.
Incident, incident, incident, incident, incident, incident, incident, incident, incident, incident!
This is a huge incident, the kind that comes once in a lifetime, if that!
On a global scale, it probably isn't all that rare.
But, that happened right here in Sawa Prefecture, in Motoki-cho!
The patrol cars this morning had me thinking, maybe...? But this went beyond what I expected!
A murder in this Motoki-cho!
And what's more, it happened on this April 8!
There's no way I can keep quiet about this!
For me, greedy for new things, for worlds not yet seen, there is only one way to calm down!
Unravel the mystery!
The method is simple.
Search every last bit of information that's up on the net right now!
The source can be anywhere! It can be anything!
Whether information is a lie or not, that much I can see through myself!
There are people who say relying on the net is laziness, that it's unproductive, and so on.
Those people just don't know how to get useful information from the net.
Otherwise, there's no way the net would have spread through this world as far as it has.
"April 8, just as I thought...! 　Hmmm... I see, I see... Shiyo 790... Shiyo 794... Shiyo 797... And this year, Shiyo 800... Three times in the past, similar incidents happened on April 8 as well! Could there be some kind of rule to it...?"
Not for anyone to hear, the talk to myself spills naturally from my mouth.
"Hmmmn... Information from the net has its limits... I guess I have to do door-to-door questioning after all... Before the questioning, shall I check the school message board?"
High schoolers' information is fast.
But, for all that speed, there are lots of false rumors too.
But, where there's no fire, there's no you-know-what.
Smoke out the truth mixed in among the lies.
For me, that sort of thing is easy.
"The quick ones have already gotten hold of all kinds of information. In Central Park, a brutalized body too horrible to look at. Dumped in the early morning... one victim, it says... One victim, that's quite different from the Megasawa City murders. Maybe the other one just hasn't been found yet..."
"Even so, a murder here in Motoki-cho, rare things do happen. The sky falling in would be far more realistic. This time, not the Megasawa City murders, but the Motoki-cho murders, is that what we'd call it?"
"The Megasawa City murders... Every time one happens, all of Japan is in an uproar, after all. And now this time, it happens in Motoki-cho... Did the killer move over here or something?"
"Hmm, now that my prep on the net is done, I suppose it's finally time to go do door-to-door questioning."
Oh, since I'm going anyway, maybe I should invite those two as well...
But, they're worn down at this time of year, and on top of that, they're bad with unsettling talk like this.
Probably, the two of them are together today.
This time, this genius girl, Erina Goto, shall charge into the scene alone!
Carrying out the Operation!
