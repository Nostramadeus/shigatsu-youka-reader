I'm the Type Who Looks Slimmer in Clothes

Meicho Arc [Clear Sign] · Shiyo 800-04-08 14:50 水 · 59 boxes

"Natsumi! Are you there!? Answer me! Hey!"
Only one stall in the restroom was in use.
It might be a stranger, but I couldn't afford to care how I looked!
"Natsumi! Is that you, Natsumi!?"
I knocked on the door again and again, but there was no response.
Damn...! So Natsumi's already...!
Then the lock clicked open, and the door slowly moved.
Inside, slumped limply in front of the toilet, was Natsumi.
"Natsumi! Hang in there! 　Hey!"
Her breathing was weak, and there was cold sweat on her forehead.
Don't tell me she'd been poisoned...!?
"Ghh... hack... Ugh..."
"Damn...! If it's poison, I've gotta make her throw it up...! 　No, an ambulance!"
"Hi... comrade-in-arms... Sure enough, that spice was the real deal... Did you come to save me? I'm... done for..."
What the heck... She was still dragging out the Russian-roulette takoyaki thing?
"Hey, can you stand?"
"Forgive me, Haruka... How long has it been now, since the BDDP War ended... Ah, growing old is a thing no one should wish for..."
This one thinks she's been holed up in the restroom for how many decades, exactly?
I let Natsumi lean on my shoulder, and we went back to our seats.
"Pwah! Apple juice after something spicy is the best! I feel young again, just like that!"
Natsumi was the only one whose mood was out of sync with ours, but well, she can stay that way.
There seemed to be no need to worry about being watched by people from the settlement.
"So, Kogori-senpai, you'll keep your promise, right?"
Goto's energy was back too.
At a time this serious, how can she switch her face around like that...
"O-o-o-oof course! 　Goto, you're prepared too, aren't you!?"
"Huh? 　Oh... why, yes! 　Ohohoho! But of course! Surely you know that I am a woman who always keeps her promises!?"
Ahh... It's hard to talk in front of Natsumi, and we can't go home either.
"Then, Senpai, let's post from our phones at the same time!"
"Oh, too baaad! Looks like I left my phone at Haruka's place! 　Go ahead, go ahead. If you want to post that badly, Goto, you can go first all by yourself!"
"Ngh...!"
In this situation, we couldn't hand Natsumi a phone.
Goto, for once, had dug her own grave.
"Ohohoho! Then you can just post from my phone. Shall I post them based on my own estimate?"
Goto was going out of her way to make things a pain, too.
Could it be that Goto was scheming up some other event in her own way?
No, this was the pattern where I get dragged into trouble again.
"Um, you two, that kind of thing can come later―"
"Hm!?"
"Hm!?"
The two of them turned their pitch-black glares on me again.
This wasn't some event plan. They were just being idiots...
Goto and Natsumi could go on being idiots while I tried to think up a solution my own way.
The most important thing is that nobody else dies or gets hurt.
The background and motives of the case come second.
Goto had been trying to dig into that side of things too, but the past cases and the background don't matter right now.
If nothing worse happens, that's a passing grade for today.
The one most likely to die today is my mom. There's a high chance she'll kill herself.
But we can't make any careless moves from our side either.
So the best thing is to quietly get close to Mom and restrain her.
If we stay holed up in Zahha or the police station all day today, at least we'll be safe.
Natsumi's mom seems to be at the police station, so right now all of us are safe.
No, if Mom means to kill herself, she might come charging in no matter what, whether it's Zahha or anywhere else.
In that case, the police station would be harder to storm than Zahha, so it's safer.
But then we can't save Mom.
Just keeping ourselves safe isn't good enough...! I can't abandon Mom!
That's the biggest problem...
Goto's stuck too, and I can't come up with any good ideas myself.
Maybe I should cleverly get the opinion of Natsumi, who doesn't know the details of the case.
Fudging the details, I'd fish for something to build an idea on...
"Hey, Haruka, are you listening!?"
"Huh? 　Um, sorry, I wasn't listening..."
"Goto's lying, even now! Goto has a little kid's body any way you look at it, there's no way her figure is that good! 　She's fudging her measurements and her cup size too!"
"Is that so... Maybe she's the type who looks slimmer in clothes?"
With a pointless conversation like this, half-hearted answers are the way to go.
"Haruka, even you're taking Goto's side!? There's no way those are her measurements!"
"Oh my, Kogori-senpai, you are quite welcome to measure me with a tape measure, if you like?"
Natsumi, give it up... Goto never picks a fight she can't win.
But if I said that, it was obvious I'd only be adding fuel to the fire.
I wanted to wrap this conversation up fast, so I'd play along a little and try to steer it back on course.
"Hey, Goto, how do you get a gap between how you look and your actual figure?"
"Oh? Oh? You're asking that, Niimura-senpai? 　Niimura-senpai, you look a whole lot slimmer in clothes yourself, don't you?"
Goto pointed her fingers at me again and started making them wriggle and pulse.
"I-I don't do anything special!"
"Oho, so without doing anything special, you have something that impressive and that springy."
"H-hey, don't look down there! You dirty old man!"
Maybe because we'd had that conversation so loudly, I could feel eyes on us from all around.
There were high-school boys around, too.
"Come on... please. Stop talking about this..."
I was so embarrassed I crossed my arms in front of me.
"Yes, that! That's it!"
"What's 'that'..."
"Being able to do that or not makes a huge difference! If it comes down to it, you can just take your clothes off and that'll fix it!"
She was saying something so nonsensical it wasn't even worth calling her on it.
Apparently, to Natsumi, clothes are something you take off when it comes down to it.
"Niimura-senpai, if you squeeze your arms together like that, your cleavage shows through that low neckline."
"Yes, that! That's it!"
"Looking at you like this, Niimura-senpai, you really are a pioneer of looking slimmer in clothes! 　Fitting, with the 'pai' and all." [TN: Pun: 'pai' (from 'pioneer') is slang for breasts.]
What's with all this slimmer-in-clothes stuff... Ugh, maybe I should just switch all my clothes to large...
"Haruka and Goto both act all slim like that, and then when you're alone you strip in front of the mirror and grin, don't you!"
What the heck is she imagining...
"No, no, Kogori-senpai, Niimura-senpai is so slapdash she doesn't even know her own bra size!"
"Hah! Hiding the real thing like that and walking around with fake ones hanging there!"
Hiding the real thing, a fake... what's that supposed to mean...
Huh...?
At that moment, a stroke of devilish genius descended on me.
"That's it! That's the way!"
I shouted that and stood up.
With this... with this, everyone can be saved!
"What's wrong, Haruka...?"
No, but... For that, I'd have to tell Natsumi everything.
But considering the preparations, there wasn't much time. We'd need the police's cooperation too.
"Goto, once I've told Natsumi everything, we're going to the police!"
"Huh? But..."
"Trust me! I can't keep putting all the weight on you. It'll be okay! I'll take on every dangerous part myself!"
"Y-yes!"
Swept along by my momentum, Goto hurriedly opened her notepad, and beside her, Natsumi was staring blankly.
"Goto, go to the police right now and explain what's going on. Natsumi's mom is sure to be there, so it should go fast. Natsumi and I will meet up with you once we're done talking!"
I snatched Goto's notepad and wrote down the things we'd need.
Goto looked at it and immediately brightened.
"That's Niimura-senpai for you! You don't look slimmer in clothes for nothing!"
Goto grinned.
"Understood! This genius girl, Erina Goto! I'll help with everything I've got! 　Now: Final Operation start! Use the change to buy yourself a new bra or something!"
Goto left 5,000 yen and dashed out of Zahha.
"Haruka, what is this...?"
"Natsumi, there's something I have to tell you. It's about the incident this morning."
I sat down beside Natsumi and took her hand.
―Holding nothing back, I passed what Goto and I had talked about on to Natsumi.
Natsumi's dad had been murdered.
My mom was trying to commit even more crimes.
The Megasawa City case too: I'd skipped over parts, but I believe I explained it with nothing missing and nothing extra.
Hearing all this, Natsumi was shaken, as expected.
But she didn't lose it. Trembling, she still looked me in the eye and listened to the very end.
According to Natsumi, earlier at my house she'd had a dream where a red person appeared and screamed.
Somehow, vaguely, she'd had a feeling it connected to her dad.
"Dad... Dad's...!"
"Natsumi, I know how hard this is for you right now. But right now I really need your help...!"
"But...! Dad's...!"
"Natsumi, listen carefully. The truth is, my dad died 12 years ago too."
"Huh...?"
"That's why I know how much it hurts to lose a dad. So that we don't lose anyone else, I need your help!"
I squeezed Natsumi's hands tight, but there was no strength in Natsumi's hands.
"Natsumi, please! This is something only you can do! 　At this rate, I'm going to be killed!"
"............!!"
Hearing that, Natsumi lifted her head.
"No... No, that can't happen!"
"If you help, nobody else has to die!"
"............"
Then, suddenly, strength came into Natsumi's hands, and she squeezed mine back.
"Tell me. What should I do?"
"All right...!"
It's at times like this that Natsumi's heart is strong. That's exactly why this operation can work.
"We're out of time, so we're leaving Zahha now. I'll explain while we walk."
I lent my shoulder to Natsumi, whose legs still had no strength in them, and we left Zahha.
There was no time for a detailed explanation, so as we walked I only told Natsumi what I needed her to do.
Natsumi, you're really amazing.
Some time ago, Goto said it. A strong heart that can face any hardship without giving in: that's Natsumi.
Because that's who Natsumi is, Goto said she can respect Natsumi from the bottom of her heart.
I feel the same way now.
Goto's brains, and Natsumi's strong heart. And I'm the one who ties what the two of them feel together.
I don't have intuition or a strong heart like Natsumi, and I don't have brains or drive like Goto.
But there was something I could do too! Something only I could do, because I know the Arata settlement and Mom so well!
Now, with this, not just today's incident but the Megasawa City case will really be over!
