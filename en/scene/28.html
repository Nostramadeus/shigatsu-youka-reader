The letter had been left on the table.
I sat down on a chair and looked at the letter.
Akane, it has been a long time.
I am sorry for this sudden letter.
Akane, I have taken up my pen because there is something I would like to ask your advice about.
Haruka is what I am writing about.
Haruka, after she started elementary school, defended a classmate who was being bullied, and for that reason now Haruka is the one being bullied.
Haruka is considerate of me and does not say she is being bullied, and she says school is fun.
I talked to her teacher, but however much the teacher looked into it, no evidence was found.
In other words, as far as the school is concerned, there is no bullying.
It seems the PTA is involved, so they cannot look into it deeply.
I tried to press them, but in my present state of mind, unfortunately, there are limits.
Is there not some way to help Haruka? I cannot bear to watch her any longer.
This letter alone may not convey it, but I am even considering dying together.
Childhood friends or not, I know I am being selfish, asking this after not being in touch all this time.
If you are willing to respond in any way, please come to the return address on this letter tomorrow, April 7, at 22:00.
This is where we live now.
Please do not reply by phone, email or letter.
I do not want anyone else to know about this.
I do not want to leave any evidence at all.
If you refuse, please simply ignore this.
When you come here, I will tell you how it will be done.
I would like a man's help, so if possible, please have Ryoji-san come too.
I will prepare everything you need here.
The targets are the two people in the enclosed photographs.
The targets' names and addresses are written on the backs of the photographs.
Do not worry. Whatever happens, the police will never be able to reach you.
I have made that much preparation.

Mifuyu Niimura
What was this letter...?
Haruka-chan's two bullies were to be stopped. That much I understood.
But then there was the wording: 'leave no evidence,' 'the police,' 'targets.'
Mifuyu-san was telling us to kill the two bullies?
And if we couldn't, Mifuyu-san and Haruka-chan would die together...?
This left us no choice at all!
She was telling us to kill kids about the same age as Natsumi!? Give me a break!
No, my reaction was too rash.
Mifuyu-san had sent this letter knowing we'd think that.
She was that cornered, and there was that much trust between Mifuyu-san and Akane.
And Haruka-chan was our daughter.
Dying together did feel like an exaggeration, but there was no way I could ignore it...

"No, wait. She said no phone calls or letters. That goes for anyone we contact about this. Even this letter, if you look closely, has no postmark."
No postmark meant Mifuyu-san really had delivered this letter without leaving a single trace.
Then how had this letter been delivered?
"Then I'll tell my mom from a public phone tomorrow. That's all right, isn't it?"
Well, that should be fine. There was no direct connection to Mifuyu-san.
Besides, Mifuyu-san had said she'd make sure nothing could be traced back to us.
No, what am I thinking!
We're going to persuade her! Not to kill anyone!
We finished getting ready for tomorrow and went to bed.
Tomorrow, we go to Megasawa.
We're the cause of all this, and we're going to settle it properly.
