The Thirteenth-Year Memorial Service 1

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-08 09:00 水 · 66 boxes

We finished breakfast too and headed to the room on the second floor.
Goto's attitude earlier had frozen the air between the two of us as well.
"Hey, Haruka..."
"I know, I know. Goto acted like that because she's serious. So I'm not gonna snap at Goto anymore."
What kind of face is Goto making on the other side of this sliding door?
If she still has that sharp glare from before, it'll be hard to talk.
"Phew..."
I put my hand on the sliding door and took a deep breath.
"You nervous? 　It's fine, I'm telling you. Goto's not that clueless, right?"
"Yeah..."
In the room stood a tiny person wearing a Noh mask and robes.
"Boooo! 　Hey, hey, look! With this, do I look intimidating too!?"
Goto's tone of voice was back to its usual rowdy self.
No, no... just what you did earlier was intimidating enough...
Still, there was something off about this getup of Goto's...
Her body was too small, so the Noh mask and robes were out of proportion.
I've seen this sight somewhere before...
Ah! I remember!
Exactly like the threat pose of the lesser anteater I saw at some zoo a long time ago!
The way it stood up spread-eagled and threatened with everything it had.
That was so cute...
"Goto... so cute..."
"Huh?"
"Pfft-hahahahahaha! What's with you! You're a big-headed monster that'd show up in a kids' anime!"
"Wh-what kind of way to put it is that!
　Look, look, rawr!"
Goto... that's so calculated...
No, she isn't even aware of it, so she's just plain cute...
"Yeah, that really doesn't suit you! 　Here, how about this? Not a young woman's mask, a hannya mask!" [TN: A hannya is the Noh mask of a horned demon-woman.]
"Ooh! I've always wanted to try one on!"
Goto eagerly swapped her Noh mask for the hannya.
"Ta-da! How's this!?"
Goto spread her arms wide and struck an even more intimidating pose― or so she must have meant to...
"............"
"............ Pfft!"
This is a kindergarten play.
"Wh-why are you laughing!?"
"Ahaha! That's great, Goto! You're prettier than usual with that hannya on!"
"Gyaah! Are you saying my looks score is below a hannya's!?"
"There, there, Goto, you're cute!"
I gave Goto a pat on the head, like soothing a little kid.
"Hnn... when Kogori-senpai does that, I'm helpless..."
"There, there, Goto, you're cute!"
Haruka gave Goto a pat on the head the same way.
"Niimura-senpai saying it? Frankly, that really ticks me off!"
"Ahhahahaha! Good, good, Goto! Go with that hannya!"
"No! I'm switching to the young woman's mask!"
Goto switched her mask back to the original young woman's mask.
But this one looked even more out of proportion.
"Hey, hey, Goto, do that pose from before one more time!"
"Huh? The threat pose?
　Rawr!"
"Okay, we've gotta get changed too!"
"You're right!"
"H-hey! Don't ask me to do it and then ignore me!"
"Also, take your socks off. At the memorial service, you've gotta make sure nobody can tell who you are. I've got wooden sandals ready for everyone at the front door."
Is it because even socks and shoes can give away who someone is?
They're really thorough...
"Goto, how long are you gonna hold that pose?"
"Geez! I haaate you both!"
―We finished getting ready and left the Niimura house.
The meeting hall was supposedly about a 5-minute walk from here, but in the unfamiliar wooden sandals I was a little unsteady.
On the way, I felt a little wind.
But the cherry branch in my hand gave no answer.
At this point it was a question of the premise itself, but did the curse-killings even really exist?
To us, it was all just stories we had heard. We had never actually experienced the curse-killings.
"Niimura-senpai, are there any other rules for the memorial service?"
"Let's see... Probably not to talk with people more than you need to. The shinigami would catch on. Other than that, just copy what everyone does. Basically you just sit there."
"The shinigami would catch on?"
"Yeah, here they call dead people shinigami. You know how they say ghosts try to take living people away with them? 　It's kinda like that. We dress like this so the shinigami can't single out the people they wanna take, or so they say."
"But then, Goto's obviously recognizable... Even if everyone were dressed the same, Goto is the one I'm absolutely sure I could pick out."
"Yeah, you don't see many shinigami this out of proportion."
"Then the shinigami might take me away..."
"Nah, I don't think they'd take a weirdo like you, Goto."
"Grrr!"
Geez, she's so cute!
"Come to think of it, Natsumi, if you're holding the cherry branch, people'll know it's Natsumi, too..."
"Huh? Oh, you're right... But Erika-chan told me to bring it, so that's safe, right?"
"Well, still, only the Niimuras and Shirozaki-san know you've got the branch. So, there's a decent wind. Don't you feel anything?"
"No... nothing..."
The road wasn't paved with asphalt, so what should have been a 5-minute walk took a whole 10 minutes.
The sandal thongs dug in between my toes, and it hurt a little.
The meeting hall, when we got there, was an old-fashioned one-story building.
Even the windows weren't glass, but wooden sliding panels.
"Hey, is this the meeting hall where they said they kept watch on Kotaro-san?"
"Probably. They watched him every April for 3 years, but the curse-killings still happened, apparently."
Thinking about it that way, maybe Kotaro-san didn't do it with his own hands...
"Goto, what do you think?"
―Goto stayed silent for a little while.
With the ill-fitting Noh mask I couldn't see her expression, but she must have been thinking it over with a serious face.
"It's just like I told Kogori-senpai in the bath earlier. There has to be an accomplice. Even if Kotaro-san was being watched, an accomplice could still carry out a curse-killing."
"Maybe the accomplice caused a curse-killing during the watch to prove Kotaro-san wasn't the culprit?"
"I see, that seems quite possible too. But... whoever the culprit is, that's clearly helping the crimes along, isn't it? 　When everyone wants to stop the curse-killings..."
For all the guesses flying around, we were only walking circles around the truth, with no sign of getting any closer.
"Goto, did you figure anything out from what Kotaro-san said earlier?"
"There wasn't anything that looked like a real lie, but... there was one thing that bothered me a little. Earlier I deliberately pressed him interrogation-style, but I got the feeling Kotaro-san wasn't used to it. Kotaro-san, even though he was suspected of being the culprit every time a murder happened?"
"Um, so you mean Kotaro-san never got suspected like that?"
"That's right. Even if people suspected Kotaro-san, I think it was in a more roundabout, softer way."
"Well, yeah, that figures... They were trying to solve it within the settlement, so they probably wouldn't openly suspect someone living in the same place..."
"Goto, do curse-killings really exist...?"
"Hey now, you're asking that now?"
"True, we haven't experienced a single thing about the curse-killings... There's also the possibility it was one huge prank by the whole settlement."
"No, wait, no way. I mean, this settlement's population is under 20 people, right? 10 years ago there had to be at least 30. So where are they supposed to have gone...? And why would anyone spend 10 years setting up this prank?"
It's true, it's far too elaborate for a joke...
Going by what Obasan said, at least there's no doubt that Sakura-san died with her head cut off.
No... at the time, Obasan went home right after Sakura-san ran off somewhere, taking Haruka with her, so Obasan never saw the scene of the beheading or anything after it.
If the curse-killings don't actually exist, then of course I can't hear the cherry trees' voices.
Then what would the purpose be?
I can't believe they'd spend 10 years on some huge setup just as a prank, though...
Well, the head of the settlement is someone who loves pranks like that, so a prank is possible, but this one would be a bit nasty...
"I guess doubting that the curse-killings exist is a stretch after all..."
"Seems so... If there are no curse-killings, that's one way-too-desperate prank..."
"Well, obviously... Truth is stranger than fiction."
For once, Haruka came out with something literary.
"Well, maybe the watch on Kotaro-san was loose because Kotaro-san asked to be watched himself."
"Right, in the end nobody knows who the culprit is, so the rumor goes around that it's Sakura-san's curse."
"Which means the culprit had no intention of pinning the crimes on Kotaro-san. That way, they could make people think it was Sakura-san's curse, or get everyone around them caught up in suspicion feeding on itself. It does add up, more or less..."
In the end, even whether the curse-killings were faked was uncertain.
How can we get any closer to the truth?
"So, I know it's odd after saying all this, but please throw away all our reasoning so far. Kogori-senpai already heard this from me in the bath."
"Do we really need to go that far?"
"Right now, we're completely in the palm of the culprit's hand. Unless we completely change how we think at some point, we'll stay like this forever."
"Look, remember? Chiga-nee's lie."
"So, what kind of incidents were these curse-killings? 　The weapon, how they were killed, all that's known, right?"
"Mm... it seems they were all killed with a single blow. It went straight for the carotid artery, so it was the work of someone very practiced."
"Is that something you heard recently?"
"Mm... no. I'd heard it since the incidents first happened."
"Why is it, Chiga-nee, that you know so much? 　Do adults in Arata tell children the details of murder cases? 　Who was killed when, where, with what weapon, and how, and so on."
"Mm... the thing is... Sorry, the part about having heard it since the incidents first happened was a lie."
"For someone asking our advice, you open with a lie?"
"Chiga-nee was asking our advice, and still, whatever her reasons, she opened with a lie. In the same way, a lot of people's testimony must have lies mixed in."
"I see. If even Chiga-nee told a lie like that, then the others might be lying too... But that doesn't mean everything's a lie, right? Shouldn't we keep some of the information instead of tossing it?"
"I don't think we need to throw out Kogori-senpai's vision or the Droga. As I thought, the problem is everything after Sakura-san's head was cut off. Let's reset everyone's testimony from the beheading on. We have to start by throwing away our own assumptions. While we're at it, try including Kana-chan, Niimura-senpai's mother, and even Kogori-senpai's parents among the suspects."
"Hey now, that far?"
"If we're resetting everyone's testimony, we have to start there. We gather all the information again from scratch and compare it with what we've put in the trash so far. There must have been a gap somewhere."
"Oh, that's what you mean. We collect everyone's testimony and the circumstances and all that again, and check whether what they say is different from before, right?"
"Right. But please don't reset one thing: 'there are several masterminds, and none of them is aware of the others' crimes.' Otherwise these curse-killings don't hold together. The important thing is strictly 'not trusting everyone's testimony so far.'"
"I see, so it's not about pretending all the information so far never existed, it's about not trusting anyone's testimony, right?"
"Right. I put it in a complicated way, but that understanding is OK."
Just then,
someone dressed the same as us came walking toward us from the other way.
"People are gathering. Let's go inside too."
