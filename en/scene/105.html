The Thirteenth-Year Memorial Service 2

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-08 10:00 水 · 104 boxes

So this was the meeting hall where they had kept watch on Kotaro-san―
Unlike the old exterior, the inside had been cleaned spotless.
At the front there was an altar.
One of the things on it was Ojisan's memorial photo, but everything else was stuff I had never seen.
On a table-like stand, red robes the same as the ones we were wearing had been laid out, with a Noh mask placed at the head.
At first glance it looked like a person lying there, but the sleeves and hem dangled limply off the stand, and the body was completely flat.
Next to the stand with the Noh mask and red robes, there was another stand just like it.
Countless knives had been stabbed into the top of it, standing upright.
"Haruka, what's that?"
"For now, just sit anywhere."
As Haruka urged, I sat kneeling on the tatami.
"Well, just copy everybody. And no more chatting in here. The shinigami'll learn your voice."
Okay, so keeping quiet during the service is probably the same as at a regular one.
Instead of answering, I gave a big nod.
But the pot and tripod I had seen this morning weren't there.
Those were supposed to show up as ritual implements too...
―By the time 10 o'clock came around, about 15 people had gathered in the meeting hall.
It looked like everyone in the settlement had come.
Three people dressed the same as us came in through the front entrance.
Then all the attendees, Haruka included, bowed low where they sat, as if prostrating themselves.
Goto and I followed suit.
Only one of them had a different mask and a different robe color.
That one must be Erika-chan, and the other two must be Obasan and Chiga-nee.
The Grand Witch was holding a flask of clear liquid. One witch held the pot, and the other held the tripod.
There was no doubt those were the things carried up from underground this morning.
Then the tripod with the pot on it was set in front of the memorial photo, and the Grand Witch lit a fire.
With that done, the three witches knelt in front of the memorial photo.
............ ............ But no matter how many minutes passed, nothing moved at all.
"Haruka, this is..."
"Quiet. Just a little longer."
"............"
Kneeling like this, which I rarely do, made my legs feel cramped.
―Eventually, the smell of oil began to fill the room.
The smell was drifting from that pot.
I knew this smell.
It was a lot like the fried giant bullfrog I had eaten yesterday.
So was that giant bullfrog oil in there?
Blue flames rose from the pot.
It seemed the oil had reached its flash point.
Then the Grand Witch stood up.
"A morte chegou. Todo mundo vai Shibaro o Grim Reaper."
Was it some kind of incantation? The pronunciation clearly wasn't Japanese, either.
Then the Grand Witch let a little from the flask in her hand drip into the flaming pot.
For an instant, the fire blazed up so high it nearly reached the ceiling.
But in less than a second, it went back to its original size.
Next, the two witches stood up.
They each pulled out one of the knives stuck in the table,
rolled up their sleeves just a little, and lightly stabbed their arms.
And then,
they plunged the knives into the red robes spread out on the other table in one stroke.
Next, they took the flask in turn
and let a little of the liquid drip into the pot.
"Natsumi, Goto, let's go. You can stab the knives anywhere except the head."
With that, Haruka stood up.
Haruka pulled out one of the knives,
"Hss..."
Haruka drew the knife smoothly across her own arm.
Blood began to seep faintly from the cut.
She pulled out one more knife and stabbed both into the robes on the stand.
Then,
Haruka picked up the flask set beside it and let a few drops of the liquid fall into the pot.
It seemed I just had to copy this.
Shirozaki-san had said this pot was used something like offering incense. Was this what he meant?
Even so, cutting your own arm with a knife...
When I glanced behind me, a line had formed of people waiting their turn.
If I dawdled, I would be holding everyone up...
I made up my mind and pulled out a knife before the fear could weigh down on me.
Then I rolled up my sleeve just a little and pressed the tip of the blade into my arm.
"Ngh..."
It was my first time doing anything like self-harm, but it seemed the blade hadn't gone in as deep as I had thought.
All it did was make a little blood seep from the cut.
Then I pulled out another knife and stabbed it into the arm of the robes on the stand.
Next was the liquid in the flask.
This was a little dangerous too. If I messed up, I would get burned.
Inside the pot, something like oil was boiling, with flames rising off it.
When I picked up the flask and let a little of the liquid drip in,
"Hot...!"
a big flame shot up for an instant and hit me square in the face.
I let out a small scream, but neither Haruka nor anyone else reacted.
But thanks to the Noh mask and hood, it didn't turn into anything serious.
But... what was this smell?
The moment I dripped the liquid in, an odor I had never smelled before rose up.
My head was spinning, or rather, my brain picked up a strange sensation.
What on earth was the liquid in this flask...
Once Goto had gone through the ritual the same way, we went straight back to where we had been sitting.
"Nice, you two. Perfect."
Haruka praised us in a whisper.
But ever since I had smelled that liquid, something was off with my body.
I was heating up from the inside and couldn't settle down.
Come to think of it, Ojisan had used that to make Droga, according to Goto's guess.
If so, then what was in that flask... it couldn't be...
Had I just inhaled Droga?
I had heard that burning Droga for smoke brings on a high, so maybe I was in that state right now.
My body was so hot I didn't even notice the cut on my arm, so hot that sitting still was agony.
"Haah... haah... Senpai, something's weird with me... My body's hot... it's weird..."
Goto was swaying where she sat.
Goto was showing the same symptoms as me.
Was Haruka used to it? She didn't seem off at all.
"Natsumi, Goto, you okay?"
Something was wrong...
My pulse was racing, and my body was hot.
My breathing quickened, and I could feel my sweat glands opening.
Then,
one of the witches came over to us.
"Natsumi-chan and Goto-chan, go rest outside."
I heard Obasan's hushed voice.
"You two, go on outside. Sorry, but I can't just slip out..."
"Okay..."
Goto and I took the cherry branch and slipped out between the people waiting their turn for the ritual.
But even when I tried to walk, I was unsteady and couldn't walk properly.
Once outside, we sat down on a rock close by.
At first my body had been hot, but gradually my fingertips grew cold, and even sitting up became hard.
"Senpai, I feel sick..."
"Me too, I feel like I'm going to collapse..."
"Goto, could that be Droga...?"
"Um, I couldn't say..."
Goto picked up a stick lying at her feet.
"Nnn...! Ngggh...!"
Goto seemed to be trying to snap the stick with her hands.
"Haah, no good... It's probably not Droga... Uuu... putting strength into it made me feel even sicker..."
Goto crouched down right where she was.
"Goto, are you okay?"
I rubbed Goto's back.
But she didn't answer. She was breathing hard, her shoulders heaving.
"You two okay?"
When I turned around, someone in a Noh mask was standing there.
But I could tell by the voice. It was Shirozaki-san.
"Goto seems to be in pretty bad shape..."
"So you did breathe in the smell directly... Here, you'd better take off the Noh masks and red robes."
Shirozaki-san helped Goto and me out of our red robes.
―Maybe thanks to taking off the red robes and Noh masks, I could feel the pleasant breeze all over my body, and the sickness gradually faded.
When I took a deep breath, even fresher air ran all through my body.
"Uuh... is everyone else okay with that stuff...?"
"Everyone holds their breath when they put the falsificaso in the pot."
"Falsi... ficaso...?"
"It's the name of the liquid in that flask. Breathe those fumes in directly and you're bound to feel sick... Well then, I'll head back. I just happened to be at the very back, so it was easy to slip out, but it's bad to be away from my seat too long."
"Okay, thank you very much."
"Goto, how are you feeling?"
"Yes, I'm fine now. But the word Shirozaki-san used, 'falsificaso'... That's Portuguese too."
"Really?"
"falsificacao― it means 'fake.'"
"Fake...? So that means there's a real one?"
"Hmm, let's see... Maybe it's a stand-in for some substance, maybe not... If it's a stand-in for Droga..."
"If it is...?"
"Then maybe they used to use Droga as a ritual implement. But using Droga got banned, so they started using a substitute, or something."
No matter how much we thought about it, though, it would never get beyond guesswork.
"Let's head back too. It seems it's not good to be away from our seats for long."
We put the red robes and Noh masks back on and went back into the meeting hall.
The last person was just stabbing in a knife.
"You guys okay?"
"Yeah, I'm fine now."
When I said that, Haruka faced forward without a word.
When the last person finished the ritual, the Grand Witch stood up again.
"A morte foi amarrado. Matar a partir de agora!"
Then the Grand Witch took the last knife in hand, cut her own arm, and
plunged it into the neck.
Then she took up the nata and the mace―
cut the limbs off the red robes with the nata, and smashed the Noh mask apart with the mace.
"Grim Reaper esta morto. Vamos la, ir para casa para todos em casa."
When the Grand Witch said that, everyone stood up at once
and headed for the meeting hall's exit.
Were they going to do something outside next?
"Good work, you two. That's it, we're done."
"Huh? It's over already...?"
The thirteenth-year memorial service, which had looked so elaborate, ended much more simply than I had expected. [TN: Buddhist memorials count the year of death as the first year.]
The fire in the pot had gone out too, and the Grand Witch and the others each took an implement and left by the exit at the front of the meeting hall.
Only the three of us were left in the meeting hall.
Everyone cuts their arm with a knife, stabs the knives in, and at the end the red robes and Noh mask are destroyed with a nata and a mace.
Is this really an offering for the dead?
"Haruka, we can't take the masks off until we get home, right?"
"Yeah, hang in there a little longer."
Honestly, it was pretty stuffy.
The red robes weren't light, either. They weighed heavily on my shoulders.
We headed home in silence.
When I looked at the cut on my arm, it seemed light enough not to need any treatment.
Partly because of the wooden sandals and how tired we were, it took a whole 15 minutes to get from the meeting hall to the house.
Kana-chan and Kotaro-san were probably already inside.
Weren't Erika-chan and the others back yet?
"Ahh, geez, that was rough..."
Once we got home, Goto promptly took off her red robes and Noh mask.
"That really was a strange memorial service. Cutting our arms with knives..."
"That's the only kind I know. Sorry, I should've told you about the arm-cutting and stuff."
"Well, it's not like it's a big cut."
Just then, someone came into the house.
"Mm... Goto-chan and Natsumi-chan, were you all right? 　Looks like you breathed the falsificaso in directly..."
From under the Noh mask appeared Chiga-nee's slightly sweaty face.
"Yeah, I'm okay, but honestly, none of it was anything I'd ever seen before. Ahh, I'm tired."
I sprawled out spread-eagled in the entryway.
"Chiga-nee, good work on your debut as a witch."
"Mm... it's more nerve-racking than I thought it would be. But maybe I did pretty well for a first time. There's still time before lunch, so you should all rest in your room for a bit."
"Yeah, let's do that."
As Haruka and Chiga-nee urged, we headed to the room on the second floor.
As soon as we got to the room, all three of us flopped down on the tatami.
"Whew, but doing it again after so long really does wear you out."
It seemed Haruka had had a hard time of it too.
"Anyway, what do the knives and all that mean? Is that really an offering for the dead?"
"I told you earlier, right? The dead are shinigami, and they try to take us away. So they make the shinigami possess the red robes and Noh mask laid on the stand, pin it with the knives so it can't move, and then finish it off like that, apparently."
"And cutting our arms?"
"Hmm, what's that about? Maybe our blood can defeat the shinigami or something?"
"This is less an offering for the dead and more like exorcising a demon..."
"Well, I don't really get it either. That's supposedly the traditional memorial service of the Arata settlement. It started out as a gathering of outcasts. I don't think it's that weird that they have strange rituals."
A pleasant breeze blew in through the open window.
It cooled my sweaty body just right.
"Kogori-senpai, how long are you going to keep that on? Just looking at it makes me feel hot..."
I still had the red robes on. I'd better take them off.
............?
When I took off the red robes, there was the sound of paper crumpling.
When I reached into the pocket, sure enough, my fingertips felt paper.
What could it be?
............!?
RUNNOW
Does it mean 'Run now'...!?
Why would something like this...!?
"Natsumi, what's wro―
What's with that paper...!"
"RUNNOW... And it's written in blood."
If someone put the paper in, then it was someone other than me who had a chance to touch these red robes―
Did Shirozaki-san put it in earlier, when he took my red robes off...!?
