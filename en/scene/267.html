Splitting Up

Eden no Sakura Arc [Eden's Cherry] · Shiyo 800-04-07 18:30 火 · 10 boxes

There was a strange air over the Arata settlement.
With Chiga-nee gone missing, had this year's curse-killing already happened?
That unease had become an invisible fog, blanketing the settlement.
Only Grandma was at home.
Normally she'd greet us with one of her goofy impressions, but this time, it seemed, she couldn't.
Kotaro-san and Kana were apparently both out walking around, searching for Chiga-nee.
Tomorrow the thirteenth-year memorial goes ahead as planned, they say, but the air already called for words like 'mourning' and 'grief'.
"Natsumi, how about it? Sense anything?"
"No. There was a big cherry tree in the garden, but it looks like it's almost dead."
"Just like Chiga-nee said..."
"Niimura-senpai, where's the storehouse Chiga-nee was doing her research in?"
"Hey now, you're going already? We could rest a bit first..."
"I just want to take a quick look at the storehouse. Otherwise I'll be too worried about Chiga-nee to sit still..."
"All right. Natsumi, you stay here. Nobody else knows we're sniffing around the curse-killings. If too many of us go poking around, they'll get suspicious."
"Y-yeah..."
Natsumi nodded, looking uneasy.
Come to think of it, Natsumi is Necro's target.
The moment she's on her own, just like Chiga-nee...
"Actually, let's all three of us go. Showing only Goto around the settlement out of nowhere would be weird anyway!"
"Y-yeah..."
Natsumi gave the same response as before.
Maybe that was a bit of a tactless thing to say...
"You two go. My intuition doesn't work here, and I'm no use to anyone. Obaasan might be able to tell me all sorts of things if I talk with her, too. There are three of us, so why not split up the work?"
It was certainly efficient, but I could see a hint of loneliness on Natsumi's face.
"So I'll head downstairs. Obaasan looked like she was getting dinner ready, so I'll go help."
"Hey, Natsumi..."
"Haruka... here, I don't have any power at all. So let me do what I can."
With that, Natsumi left the room.
"Did I say something wrong after all...? Natsumi―I went and said something that would leave her all alone..."
"It's fine. Kogori-senpai is just a little on edge too. Let's check the storehouse quickly and hurry back to her."
"Yeah, you're right..."
I was a little worn out from the long trip here, but with this heavy atmosphere, I couldn't bring myself to rest easy.
