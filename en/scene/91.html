To the Cherry Tree

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-07 20:30 火 · 41 boxes

video: 桜.wmv
When I went outside, a wind was blowing, cold yet strangely lukewarm, the way it is only in spring.
This great old cherry tree, found only at the Niimura main house.
To my eyes, it seemed to be blooming in full with countless blossoms, as if to show them off.
But this cherry tree too was near the end of its life, and no longer had the vitality left to carry a voice to me.
video: 桜.wmv
To hear the voice, I would have to leave Arata once and go somewhere with cherry trees.
Following what Chiga-nee had told me, I stood in front of Shirozaki-san's house.
My first impression of Shirozaki-san was... a very ordinary middle-aged man.
Even if I asked and he turned me down, there was nothing to lose by asking.
I knocked on Shirozaki-san's door.
Shirozaki-san came out right away.
"Huh? Natsumi-chan, what's up?"
Apart from Shirozaki-san, there was no sign of anyone else in the house.
"Sorry to bother you so late. Um, I have a favor to ask..."
"............"
Shirozaki-san's brow creased as if in refusal.
He must have thought it was some awful favor to do with the curse-killings.
"Um... I've got something I need to do at the convenience store, but everyone's busy... so if you wouldn't mind..."
I avoided saying it outright.
Shirozaki-san would probably see the real meaning of my request right away.
"If the buggy from earlier is okay with you, I can give you a ride. Strictly speaking, riding double on a public road is illegal, though. It's about 40 minutes one way. That all right?"
"Y-yes! That would help a lot!"
"But Natsumi-chan... what would you do if I were the one behind the curse-killings?"
"Huh...? 　But..."
It was true that I couldn't say for sure Shirozaki-san wasn't the culprit.
"Still, I'm one of the residents who wants to stop this incident too. If I do anything strange, you stab me with this."
Shirozaki-san pressed a knife on me.
"Wh-wait a second...! A thing like this...!"
"That's my condition for giving you a ride to the 'convenience store.' If you can't do it, go on home."
What a drastic thing to do...
"Um... what if I were a witch..."
"I don't know anything about the witch story."
The trust between Shirozaki-san and me lay in never bringing up the witch.
And it was built on this knife.
A truly dangerous, strange kind of trust, but...
If Shirozaki-san was going this far to help, it meant he wanted the curse-killings solved even more than I did.
To put it bluntly, I was an outsider with no connection to the curse-killings.
Shirozaki-san chose a fairly wide road, in a different direction from the one we had come up the mountain by earlier.
Two shallow ruts were worn into the ground, clearly showing where cars had passed.
We were probably supposed to take this road from Arata Station to the Arata settlement in the first place.
In my hand, the knife―
I hadn't even been given the sheath, so I had to be very careful.
Shirozaki-san was driving the buggy very safely.
Still holding the knife in place, I took out my phone.
The internet connection seemed to work, a little.
This, too, was reaching Dad and the others in Motoki-cho.
If anything happened, Dad would contact me.
―38 minutes later, we arrived at the nearest convenience store.
While we rode the buggy, Shirozaki-san and I didn't talk.
"Well... I'll wait here."
Still straddling the buggy, Shirozaki-san lit a cigarette.
"Um, could you hold on to this for me? It's hard to walk into a convenience store with a knife..."
"All right."
Looking around, I saw there were a few cherry trees.
But there was no wind, so I couldn't hear the voice right now.
I had felt wind while riding the buggy too, but even so, I hadn't heard the voice.
Wind felt on a moving vehicle, and wind felt while standing still―
Could I only hear the voice when I was standing still?
Come to think of it, I had never tested it that far.
I went into the store once and bought a towel, some drink or other, and snacks.
I wanted a bag to put the knife in, so I got a plastic bag too.
When I stepped outside, a very faint wind brushed my face.
Shirozaki-san was still straddling the buggy, gazing blankly at a nearby cherry tree.
He must have noticed I had come out, but he didn't try to meet my eyes.
This had to be Shirozaki-san's signal: 'If you're going to do it, now's the time.'
I closed my eyes and took a big, deep breath.
I focused my mind and listened closely for the voice in the wind.
I had never once tried to concentrate on the voice before.
Until now, whenever the wind blew, it would whisper to me on its own.
But now it was different.
I couldn't let the cherry trees' voice slip past me, hidden in this faint wind.
The faint wind stirring my hair brushed lightly against the eardrums deep in my ears.
............ ............ But I couldn't hear anything like it.
The old tree in Arata had been vaguely trying to speak to me, but here I couldn't even feel that.
Come to think of it, my intuition only worked when I was in contact with someone who was going to be in danger.
So I, and Shirozaki-san too, were probably all right.
My phone rang― it was Dad.
"Hello? Did something happen?"
"No, nothing's happened, but... You're outside Arata now, right? How is it? Can you tell anything?"
"No, the wind's too weak..."
"I see..."
Even hearing Dad's voice, I got no intuition at all.
"Hey, how are Haruka and Goto doing?"
"Haruka-chan seems to be doing the dishes at home with Mifuyu-san. Goto-chan's cutting in and out a bit. She must be right in the middle of the hunt."
"Okay. I'll try calling Haruka and Goto. If I hear their voices directly, I might be able to tell something."
"Right, that's a good idea."
I hung up and immediately called Haruka.
"Natsumi! Well!? Did you feel anything!?"
I listened not just to Haruka's voice but to the faint wind too.
............ But there was no reaction at all.
"No, nothing... Haruka, it doesn't look like anything's going to happen to you."
"Then call Goto too. Right now it's her who looks to be in danger. In more ways than one..."
Right now Goto was out hunting with Kana-chan.
But the time limit was about up, so if nothing had happened, they would be heading for the Niimura main house.
"Hello, Goto?"
"Ah― Ko― Senpai?"
Maybe the signal was bad. Her voice kept cutting out.
"―Hup. Senpai, how about here? Can you hear me?"
"Yeah, I can hear you fine."
"Ah, I knew it! 　Right now, um... we've come to a secret place."
"A secret...?"
"Yes, Kana-chan's secret base."

"Hah...! Hah...!"
Before I knew it, a stronger wind had started blowing.
And at the same moment, an intuition more intense than any before...
It always came to me as a voice, but this was the first time I had sensed it through sight.

"Goto, get away from there, right now!"
"What...? 　Senpai, where are you right now!? Outside Arata!?"
"Yes! I just got a really strange feeling! Get away from there right now!"
"Understood! Kana-chan! We're going home!"

"Aww? Let's hang out longer!"
"Just hurry! 　Should we go straight back to the house!?"
"Yes! Get away from there right now!"
And then the intuition I had felt so vividly a moment ago receded as if it had never been.
The wind suddenly died down too.
