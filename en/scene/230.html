The Last Chance

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-07 18:30 火 · 50 boxes

The sun had set completely, and a moon you could mistake for a full one had begun to light up this land of Arata in earnest.
"Sorry, Chiga-nee. Dragging you into a mess like this..."
"Mm... I came to pick the three of you up, so don't worry about it. Besides, it was Kana who brought it up in the first place."
Chiga-nee reassured Haruka with that gentle smile of hers.
"How long until Kana-chan gets back?"
"Mm... the round trip won't take Kana all that long, but getting ready to bring everyone out of here will probably take a little while."
There was nothing here but uncomfortable rocks to sit on.
After the long train ride and then the climb, my stamina gauge had started blinking red too.
"Mm... shall we move a bit? There should be an unused hut nearby. I think it'll be more comfortable than here."
"Ahh, that'd be a lifesaver! My back's aching just from sitting here..."
Haruka and Goto got to their feet fairly lightly.
We were tired, but when it came to somewhere more comfortable than here, our legs moved on their own.
―The distance we walked was about 100 meters.
What waited at that finish line was a ramshackle abandoned hut.
'Abandoned hut' is only my guess, but it was rotted enough that I was sure nobody used it anymore.
"Geez, if there was a place this good, you could've told us sooner..."
"Mm... at times like this, it's really better not to move around much."
"What was this place originally used for?"
"Mm... it's a rest stop for when we go hunting. But we bought a buggy 3 years ago, and it hasn't been used since. The buggy can get as far as that landslide back there."
A buggy was... a small car for driving on rough roads, if I remember right?
When we opened the door, air even damper than outside came gusting out.
It smelled a little musty, and nobody had cleaned it.
But the roof didn't seem to leak, so it would be fine to sit or lie down.
"Ahh, I'm beat!"
Haruka flopped down on the dusty floor.
But,
"Whoops! Oh no!"
Haruka hurriedly sat up and started brushing off the dust on her back.
"Damn...! My precious knit dress..."
It was a present I'd given her last Christmas.
I'd picked it out thinking Haruka should have something at least a little girly.
"Natsumi, sorry. After you went and gave it to me..."
"It's fine, it's fine! Just do whatever's comfortable right now!"
I brushed the dust off Haruka.
"Hey, hey, Kogori-senpai! Next time, give me a knit dress too, please!"
"Goto, don't bother. With your build, it'd never suit you!"
With Goto's build, there were probably styles that would suit her better than a knit dress.
"For Goto, wouldn't something like Chiga-nee's look be good? 　I think clothes that put the weight on the upper body would suit her?"
"Ooh, that sounds nice too! Chiga-nee, where do you buy your clothes?"
"Mm... in Tokyo. I go to university in Tokyo."
"Chiga-nee, why did you go to Tokyo? Did you want to try living in the city or something?"
"Mm... let me see... That's part of it, but mostly it's that I wanted to see the outside world once. Dad and Grandma encouraged me too."
"So Grandma pushed for it too. That cosplay hag'd probably be into Tokyo clothes and stuff!"
"Grandma comes to Tokyo now and then. Last time it was, um... Comiket... was it? She took part in that."
"Comiket... what's that?"
"It's the biggest fan-comics convention in the world. Hundreds of thousands of people come every time, and apparently it gets really lively. There's a cosplay area there too, I hear."
"Grandma took part in an event that huge?"
"Mm... she seems to have been interested for a while. She put a lot of energy into getting ready, it looked like. She dragged me along too, against my will..."
"S-so... how'd it go over...?"
"Mm... surprisingly, it was a huge hit. Surrounded by lots of cameras."
"What!? Grandma's cosplay was that high-quality!? 　Who'd she cosplay as!?"
"Mm... I think... do you know that movie called “Fascination”, the one that got a live-action film last year?"
"Oh, I know it, I know it! Natsumi, we went to see it last year, right!?"
"Yeah, I remember! The director's cut DVD came to my house yesterday!"
"Really!? Then let's have a movie night at my place sometime! I'll serve the fancy tea! 　So who'd Grandma cosplay as?"
"Mm... I don't really get it, but apparently it was the girl who's the main character."
"Whaaat!? Isn't she a high-school girl!? 　A hag over 70 cosplaying a high-school girl and it's a huge hit... There's no figuring this world out..."
"Mm... I know... We were surrounded by so many cameras... Getting out of the venue was a real struggle."
"So that means you, Chiga-nee, were there with your grandma, right? 　The one they were probably photographing wasn't your grandma, it was Chiga― Mmph...!"
I hurriedly clamped my hand over Goto's mouth.
Goto! I know what you want to say!
That the one being photographed wasn't her grandma, but Chiga-nee, there with her in ordinary clothes!
But it was better if the Niimura family didn't find out about this, as far as possible.
If it got from Chiga-nee to her grandma, she'd be hurt for sure.
"Uh, um, Chiga-nee, wouldn't clothes like Haruka's suit you better!? 　Something that shows the lines of your body a little more!"
"What!? 　Mm... um... I suppose... so... B-but I... don't have... that nice a figure..."
Chiga-nee was as tall as Haruka, and quite slender.
So if she says her figure isn't nice...
Hm!? Does that mean... she has the same complex I do!?
Even without the voice of the cherry blossoms, I can tell a comrade by intuition!
I grabbed Chiga-nee's hand tight.
"Chiga-nee! I know exactly how you feel! 　Even when there are cute summer clothes I end up giving up on them, and I don't have the nerve to go to the beach either!"
And Chiga-nee squeezed my hand back, hard.
"Mm... Natsumi-chan! At last, someone who understands me! 　That's right, we're women, so of course we have a complex or two about our bodies! 　The girls my age these days are all so well developed, I'm really scared to stand next to them...! 　That's why I dress like this!"
I knew it! Chiga-nee and I have the same worry.
An inborn worry that no amount of effort can do anything about!
"............?"
"What're they going on about? These two..."
Haruka and Goto didn't seem to really get it.
I was sure the friendship between me and Chiga-nee would last a long time to come.
"Mm... so, Haruka. I haven't told you yet, Haruka, but... I'm going to study abroad starting this summer."
"Study abroad! That's awesome! 　Um... how long'll you be gone?"
"Mm... 4 years. So... once the thirteenth-year memorial service is over, we won't see each other for a while..." [TN: Buddhist memorials count the year of death as the first year.]
"Is that right... We already live apart, but it's still gonna be lonely..."
"Chiga-nee! I'll come see you in any country! Our friendship will last forever and never die!"
"Mm... Natsumi-chan, I'll be waiting! 　I'll make lots of friends in Sweden and wait for you to come, Natsumi-chan!"
"S-Sweden!?"
"Mm... that's right. I want to study welfare. Um, see, this settlement has lots of people much older than me. It's for the settlement's future."
"Ah, I see! Sweden's known as the welfare country, and tuition's free for international students too!"
Kana-chan's going to be sad. They seem like such close sisters.
"Mm... so... This is my last chance... Before I go abroad, there's something I have to..."
Chiga-nee murmured it as if to herself.
"Hey, Chiga-nee, you mean..."
"Mm... Grandma seems to be planning to tell Natsumi-chan and the others too. About Mom."
―In the darkness, we heard about the curse-killings from Chiga-nee.
What Chiga-nee had to do―
It was to stop the curse-killings, and at the same time catch the culprit who killed Sakura-san.
"This is the first I've ever heard of any curse-killings, too... Come to think of it, I did feel like fewer people came to the memorial service every year... But you can't check everyone's face at the memorial service..."
"Can't check their faces?"
"Well, yeah, you wear a Noh mask at the memorial service. Of course you can't tell, right?"
"Are we wearing Noh masks tomorrow too? That's a pretty unusual custom..."
"Huh?"
"Mm... Haruka, wearing Noh masks at memorial services is only an Arata thing."
"What!? Seriously!? I had no idea... If I'd gone to somebody else's funeral without knowing, I'd have totally embarrassed myself..."
"A funeral where only Niimura-senpai's wearing a Noh mask would make a pretty surreal picture."
"Total chunibyo..."
"That aside, the culprit behind the curse-killings and the culprit who killed Sakura-san aren't necessarily the same, right?"
Goto looked raring to start deducing.
She'd left her notepad with Kana-chan, so for once she was reasoning without notes.
"I understand what's happened over these 10 years, but were there no incidents or anything before that?"
"Mm... if there's anything I can think of... over the last 50 years, suicides suddenly started increasing. But sorry... going by memory alone it's fuzzy, so I can't say anything for sure."
"Hmm, then let's set the suicides aside for now. If anything's unclear about the incidents of these 10 years, we can think about the suicides afterward."
"So, what kind of incidents were these curse-killings? The weapon, how they were killed, all that's known, right?"
"Mm... it seems they were all killed with a single blow. It went straight for the carotid artery, so it was the work of someone very practiced."
"Is that something you heard recently?"
"Mm... no. I'd heard it since the incidents first happened."
"Sakura-san's death, if you think about it normally, was suicide, right? I see, I see... There's something a little strange here."
"Mm... really!? What is it?"


Why is it, Chiga-nee, that you know so much?
Goto's eyes, turned on Chiga-nee, filled with suspicion all at once.
"Mm... what do you mean..."
"Sakura-san died when you, Chiga-nee, were 10, and the first of the curse-killings happened when you were 11. So back in elementary school, Chiga-nee, you were actively involved in solving the case? 　Do adults in Arata tell children the details of murder cases? 　I don't know Arata's culture, but normally you wouldn't tell a child the details of a case, would you? 　Who was killed when, where, with what weapon, and how, and so on."
"Mm... um..."
Goto's point was clear to me.
But how did she mean to settle this contradiction?
Surely not that Chiga-nee―
"Mm... the thing is... Sorry, the part about having heard it since the incidents first happened was a lie."
"For someone asking our advice, you open with a lie?"
"Goto, don't come down on her like that. There's gotta be some reason it's hard to say."
"............"
"Mm... I heard it from Auntie. This past New Year's, all of it..."
"From Mom...?"
"Mm... this past New Year's, Kana and I had a huge fight. Hearing what the fight was about, Auntie secretly told me everything."
"Chiga-nee, I've formed three hypotheses. One: the case where Chiga-nee is the culprit. Two: the case where Chiga-nee simply misunderstood something. Three... the case where Chiga-nee worked out who the culprit was on her own. And the culprit is someone very close, so close she wouldn't want to tell anyone..."
"Mm... that's...!"
Chiga-nee fell silent before Goto's reasoning, which you could almost call an interrogation.
But after heaving a big sigh,
"Mm... you're not called a genius girl for nothing. All right. I'll tell you what I heard from Auntie, and what we talked about."
―From here, Chiga-nee began to tell us the conclusion she had reached with Obasan.
