Rubbing the Wrong Way

Wabigoto Arc [Apology] · Shiyo 790-04-08 20:00 木 · 77 boxes

―The time had gone past 20:00.
Natsumi, worn out from playing, had fallen asleep in Haruka-chan's futon.
Akane was out shopping at the moment.
I still hadn't told Akane what Natsumi had said.
Two sets of footsteps came echoing along the outside walkway.
It had to be Mifuyu-san and her daughter coming back.
"Natsumiiii!"
The moment the door opened, Haruka-chan piled on top of Natsumi.
Natsumi woke right up, and they started their wrestling match again.
"Welcome back, Mifuyu-san."
"I'm back. Where's Akane?"
"She went shopping."
"I see..."
Mifuyu-san showed no particular expression.
She looked a little tired, but not as bad as yesterday.
"Ryoji-san, did you pull it off?"
"Yeah, just the way you wanted."
"I see. Thank you. I can't really repay you, but I'm grateful. This was for the best. This way... surely..."
Mifuyu-san's tired face clouded over even more.
I still couldn't believe Mifuyu-san had wanted a murder.
"But for all that, it wasn't very noisy around here. Hasn't the incident come to light yet? That's no good. You had to make sure the bodies were found right away. It's because they're found right away that you can stage an inexplicable incident..."
"Was that method your idea, Mifuyu-san?"
"Yes, I thought it up. Nobody who didn't know the area could pull something like that off. How was that drug? Was it as powerful as I expected?"
"............"
"From the look of it, you managed to use it well without straining your body. Apparently if you push it too hard, it can cause broken bones or heart failure."
"Who left the letter that was sent to Motoki-cho? 　You can tell me by now, can't you?"
"I left it. Haruka was at school, and while she was, I went back to Motoki-cho and slipped it into your mailbox."
"You went to all that trouble so that we'd come here?"
"That's right. If I'd met you two and you'd held me back, and I'd been stuck in Motoki-cho, I wouldn't have been able to go to today's memorial service. Then I couldn't have manufactured my alibi, right?"
"I see... Did 'someone I can trust' think that up?"
"No. That person only sent the supplies, and refused to help any further than that. The rest of the plan―how we'd stay in contact and so on, I thought up all of it myself. Because I'm the one who knows what you two do."
"The dying together... you meant that?"
"Yes. Otherwise I wouldn't have asked you for something like this."
"Then why did you propose a method like this, like people being spirited away? 　Even if it's the Arata settlement's way, if anyone saw you, that getup would stand out way too much."
Mifuyu-san turned her eyes toward Haruka-chan and Natsumi.
The two of them were playing around and didn't seem to pay any attention to our talk.
"I gave Haruka a book. A children's book of curses. Just as I expected, Haruka hid it from me and put a curse on those two to kill them."
"What do you mean...?"
"This Megasawa City, you see, was a religious city until about 50 years ago. And a new religion unique to Megasawa City, at that. But that religious group... I don't know the details, but apparently it caused a scandal. In the backlash, the people of Megasawa City now loathe that cult, and anything occult, too."
"And what does that have to do with Haruka-chan's curse...?"
"Don't you see? 　Loathing something means it's on your mind. In other words, you're conscious of it, right?"
"Um... Haruka-chan's curse becomes the trigger, the two get killed in some bizarre way, and everyone around thinks it's the occult, is that it?"
"That's right. This Megasawa City hates the occult. So if something occult happens, plenty of people should decide that's the cause. What the locals say eventually becomes public opinion."
"I see. But would steering people's minds like that really work...?"
"Who knows. That I can't say. But it can't hurt, and if it works, I'm just lucky."
"Even so, there was no need to have Haruka-chan cast a curse..."
"To check Haruka's real feelings. Haruka's curse, if you watch what kind she casts, shows how seriously she's taking it, doesn't it?"
"Hold on! Then Haruka-chan'll believe she killed them herself...! 　Making her carry something that heavy...!"
"It's what Haruka wished for herself, isn't it? 　Isn't granting that wish a parent's job?"
"Kh...!"
To think she had used even her own daughter's mind.
It's late to be saying this, but Mifuyu-san really isn't in her right mind.
In that case, if I tell her we did nothing today...
No. For now, I should draw out whatever information I can.
"By the way, that cult, what exactly..."
"It's taboo here in Megasawa City, so I don't know the details. But apparently the classmate Haruka protected from the bullying comes from a family connected to that cult. So the cult hasn't disappeared. It still exists today. In other words, the people's minds aren't completely at ease."
"You said that cult had some connection to the Arata settlement..."
"That was something I made up. But there's a story that long ago, a faction that left the settlement took root in Megasawa. I heard it from my mother-in-law once. I just forced a link to that."
"So, to steer the people of Megasawa City psychologically and throw the police investigation into confusion, you used the curse and that drug...?"
"That's right. That way the police can't get any proper testimony. Well, as I said earlier, it's only a matter of being lucky if it works out. And besides, it really is movement no human could make."
"So making the police give up was also the aim..."
"Right. Nobody would think a human could leap 2 meters into the air, would they?"
"Then today's results get reported to that person, I take it."
"I don't intend to report it. That person broke the settlement's rule too... so that person probably doesn't want to be too involved. Even at the planning stage, that person only came to Megasawa once to talk."
"If it had failed... what would have happened? 　Say, if we'd done nothing."
"Nothing would have happened... I'm sorry. Yesterday's was just a threat."
"I see... Then I'm glad..."
"Huh? Glad...?"
Mifuyu-san stuck her face into the box that held the weapons.
"What does this mean!? There's no sign they were used at all! 　Did you do it some other way!?"
"Mom, what's wrong?"
"Haruka, you be quiet!"
Faced with Mifuyu-san's sudden fury, Haruka-chan and Natsumi froze where they stood.
"I'm home."
"Akane! What is the meaning of this!? Why didn't you do what I told you!?"
At Mifuyu-san's words, Akane fell silent for a moment.
"I'm sorry, Mifuyu... We couldn't do anything. No, we didn't do anything..."
"You two...! You understand what you've done, don't you...!"
With a face like a demon's, Mifuyu-san took up the mace and edged closer, step by step.
"Mifuyu-san, wait! Mifuyu-san, you don't really want this! 　Eiichiro too―"
"Shut up! How dare you deceive me...! 　You won't get away with this!"
"Stop it, Obachan!"
Natsumi clung to Mifuyu-san's leg.
"Why are you doing this!? Obachan isn't like this! Go back to being the old Obachan!"
"L-let go! You'll get hurt!"
"But Haruka-chan's crying! 　Haruka-chan doesn't want to see her mom like this!"
"Mom... stop it. Mom, you're like a witch I read about in a book..."
"Wha...!"
"Is it because I made a paper doll that Mom turned into a witch?"
"N-no, Haruka! That's..."
"No! I don't want Mom to be a witch!"
Hearing Haruka-chan's voice, Mifuyu-san quietly lowered the hand holding the mace.
"Haruka... I'm sorry..."
A single tear spilled from Mifuyu-san's eyes.
I knew it. What Mifuyu-san wanted wasn't to kill.
And the reason Natsumi came along.
To show Haruka-chan's heart directly like this, and make Mifuyu-san stop herself.
That was the real purpose all along...!
"Haruka... Heh... Heh-heh-heh... Ha-ha-ha... Ahahahahahaha! 　Heh hahahahahaha heh ahaha!"
"Ahhahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahahaha"
"Khh-heh-heh-heh-heh...! Just what I'd expect of Natsumi-chan... Being a witch, you can even work sorcery like this.
Truly detestable... You die first!"
"Stop!"
Akane leapt at Mifuyu-san.
"Let go...! 　Let gooooo!!"
"Wake up, Mifuyu...!"
But Akane was shoved away by Mifuyu-san.
"Mom...!"
Natsumi ran to the fallen Akane and called out to her.
"Khh-heh-heh-heh-heh...! You really do love rubbing it in my face. That's how you always went on ahead of me, Akane, and got the happiness of having a family without wanting for a thing. Why... why is it always, always only me..."
Then Mifuyu-san took something out of the box.
"I'll kill you all...! 　Me and Haruka too, this is as far as we go. Ahahahaha! I'll put an end to all of it!"
Mifuyu-san's hand was gripping something―
it stabbed deep into Mifuyu-san's arm.
"Mom... I'm scared... What are you doing...?"
"Haruka... It's all right. Over there, together with Dad, let's be happy."
With a dull thud, bits of vivid red flesh scattered across the whole room.
"Ah... ahh...!"
Natsumi's legs gave out, and she fell on her bottom right where she was.
"Heh. A witch, and you put on a show of being frightened like a normal person. You too, soon... No, if you followed us over there―"
"Stop!"
Beside myself, I threw myself at Mifuyu-san.
"Ghaaah...!"
But something slammed into my chest faster than the eye could see, and blood gushed from my mouth all at once.
"Oh my, did that burst your lungs? This drug really is something. Go on, drown in your own blood and die in agony."
"Ngh...! Hack!"
I tried to breathe, but my lungs wouldn't move and no air came in.
"What would you like, Akane? 　How do you want to die?"
"S-stop...! Don't lay a hand on Natsumi...!"
"Don't worry. It'd be a nuisance to have that witch follow us over here too, so I'll let that child live. In exchange, though, she's going to get a good look at you two dying."
Then, this time, she switched to the nata―
Natsumi's futon, where she had been put to sleep, filled with red liquid pumping out in gushes.
And without a pause, Mifuyu-san grabbed the hair of Akane, who was now only a head, and pressed her against Natsumi.
"Come on, Akane, can you still hear me? Your hearing's still alive, isn't it? 　Go on, why not give Natsumi-chan one last kiss?"
With Akane's head ground against her, Natsumi, not understanding anything, was letting out screams that never became screams.
My lungs torn open, drowning in my own blood, I could do nothing but watch it happen.
"Hey, Ryoji-san, can you hear me? 　Heh-heh... I really am such an idiot... Killing you all doesn't mean a thing..."
Then, this time, she lit the gas stove and set fire to Haruka-chan's clothes, which had been hung up.
"Apparently, exposing the drug in my body to high heat destroys the evidence. I can't cause Arata any trouble, so I'll burn to death right here. What about you, Ryoji-san? Burning to death hurts. Do you want me to finish you off?"
I could hear her voice saying that, but I was suffocating, my mind was hazy, and I couldn't respond.
"Well, you look like you'll suffocate soon anyway, so I suppose that's fine. Then― Ryo―ji-san― Good― by― e."
