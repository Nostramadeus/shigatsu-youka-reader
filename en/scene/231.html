Contradiction

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-07 19:00 火 · 50 boxes

―What Chiga-nee told us about was Haruka's uncle, Kotaro-san.
It seemed Kotaro-san was the one Chiga-nee and Obasan believed to be the culprit.
The reason was that he had been pushing Chiga-nee and Kana-chan to be able to fight with a nata and a mace.
Chiga-nee, who hadn't known the outside world, came to think that hunting with weapons like those was strange, and apparently that was what first made her suspect Kotaro-san.
Suspecting Kotaro-san over just that seems a little strange to me, though...
Chiga-nee talked it over with Obasan, who then asked my dad and the others whether they would come and help.
"So you said Kotaro-san might be the culprit, and when you told Kana, it turned into a huge fight..."
"Mm... Kana doesn't want to believe her own father is the culprit. Same here. I want to catch the culprit, and at the same time I want to prove Dad innocent. Actually, it seems Grandma was going to tell the Motoki-cho group herself. But I thought that once you got to the settlement, there wouldn't be much time to spare..."
"Natsumi, is your intuition picking up anything?"
"The thing is... ever since about when we came into these mountains, nothing at all..."
"Oh, right... There's only one cherry tree in Arata right now..."
"In that case, the reason Kogori-senpai ended up coming here doesn't really make sense, does it... Oh, I'm not saying Senpai's useless. It's more, without intuition, what kind of factor could Kogori-senpai be..."
Right. It isn't necessarily only my intuition that's needed to solve the case.
"Mm... Also, I should tell you this too, just in case. The plan was actually for Natsumi-chan's parents to stay in the storage shed at Arata Station and come to the settlement if it came to that, but that's off now too."
"Huh!? They were supposed to come!? And now it's called off...?"
"See, Haruka, your train was late, wasn't it? 　Haruka, the train you all took was the last one that goes to Arata, so if they'd taken it, they'd have run right into you, Haruka, and the others. None of you were supposed to know about the curse-killings, so I hear your parents ended up staying in Motoki-cho."
"So your grandmother meant to tell Senpai about the curse-killings, but she hadn't told anyone else what she was planning to say. Niimura-senpai's mother was planning to keep the curse-killings from us. So you weren't as coordinated as I'd thought..."
"Mm... As long as we don't know who the real culprit is, it's hard for any of us to talk things over with each other, at least where the case is concerned."
"Natsumi, once we get to the settlement, why not try going down to the foot of the mountain in Mom's car?"
"I see, so my intuition will kick in. Sounds like it's going to get busy..."
"Mm... The reason I lied earlier and said 'I'd heard about the case from the very beginning' was that I had to hide the thing with Natsumi-chan's parents. Auntie and I had gotten our stories straight, and I couldn't tell the three of you that. But that plan's off now anyway, so I decided there's no point hiding it."
"Mm... Also, there's something called the succession rite tonight, and I was going to switch places with Auntie then. Because Kana might have attacked Natsumi-chan's parents."
"What!? Kana-chan, of all people, would do something like that!?"
"Mm... Kana... Mom's killer is someone she truly hates. If she found out who it was, I think she'd kill them without hesitation. Dad always used to tell us. If we found out who killed Mom, we should kill them without hesitating... Dad would only have to tell Kana 'Whoever's in the storage shed has to be the culprit,' and then Kana would probably..."
"So, Chiga-nee, after switching places at the succession rite, you'd keep tabs on Kana-chan and make sure Senpai's parents didn't come to harm, right? 　Which means Kotaro-san hasn't given Kana-chan any instructions yet?"
"Right, that's what's scary about Dad. He can apparently manipulate the people around him without ever lifting a finger himself. So even the people who live here might turn against us."
"Mm... Also, there was a time when we all kept watch over Dad in the meeting hall in April. In April, from Shiyo 793 to 795, I think. We watched him the whole time, but the curse-killings happened anyway..."
"Hey now... We're actually heading into a place that scary...?"
"But it can't be helped. Kogori-senpai said that's what we should do, so we have no choice..."
"Kind of... sorry..."
"No, don't apologize! We know full well we're better off doing what you say, Natsumi."
"But I've got no intuition right now. Can I really be any help...?"
"It's fine, don't worry! I bet Dad knew that too when he called you guys out here, Natsumi!"
"Then what reason could there be for needing me...?"
"I see... Approaching it from that angle could be interesting."
"Well, Natsumi obviously isn't as sharp as Goto, but if you're talking about what's amazing about her, it's her nerves of steel."
"Like, she wouldn't talk even under torture?"
"Don't say scary things... So nothing comes to mind after all, right?"
"No, wait just a second! 　Sakura-san had intuition like Kogori-senpai's, didn't she? 　Kogori-senpai's biggest link to all this is right there, isn't it!?"
"Mm... My mom's intuition...? 　You're right, that's one way to look at it. A link between the curse-killings and Mom's intuition..."
"What was Sakura-san's intuition like? Sensing danger when the cherry trees bloom, I'm guessing?"
"Mm... I think so. When cherry season came, everyone used to go hear what Mom had to say. And that's how they decided things like the day's hunting route, apparently."
"Was there something that triggered her awakening to that power? 　Kogori-senpai and Sakura-san both got splashed with Niimura-senpai's father's blood, maybe?"
"Mm... I've never heard anything like that. And if I don't know, Auntie probably doesn't either."
"Chiga-nee, were there cherry trees back then? Sakura-san could use her intuition, right?"
"Mm... There's only one cherry tree, at the Niimura main house. That's probably how Mom could use her intuition, but lately that tree hasn't been doing well... Grandma says it might be near the end of its life."
"Why is there only one cherry tree in the first place? You'd think there would be a few more..."
"Mm... Around 70 years ago, someone dug them all up and took them away, I hear."
"Someone... who?"
"Mm... I don't really know either, but I think it was an outsider, at least. It does seem strange that this settlement, as insular as it was, would give nearly all its cherry trees away to an outsider, though."
"They were a rare variety, apparently. A cherry you only find around the Arata settlement, they say. Though apparently they didn't let go of the one at the Niimura main house."
"Mmm... What a truly strange place... An insular settlement, 10 years of curse-killings... Sakura-san, as a person. Honestly... Que droga of a place..." [TN: 'Que droga' is Portuguese, printed in Latin script in the source.]
"Goto-chan, what did you just say!?"
"Huh? Huh?"
Chiga-nee suddenly raised her voice by an octave.
"Mm... Just now, you said Droga..."
"It's Portuguese, apparently. What's wrong?"
"Mm... Droga is... a special drug they used to make here in the Arata settlement, long ago. Supposedly it lets a person use 100% of their strength. The first batch was made a full 500 years ago, apparently."
―And then Chiga-nee explained Droga in more detail.
"Huh. But that doesn't have much to do with the curse-killings, does it?"
"No, no, Niimura-senpai. Some little thing might turn out to be connected. 　That said, working it out from here is a bit much... We're about to come face to face with the suspect, Kotaro-san, aren't we?"
"Mm... Right. Keep a poker face as much as you can, please. What I want isn't to corner Dad. I want to prove Dad is innocent. Time and again, Dad has been suspected of being the culprit, but no evidence has ever turned up. Even so, I think Dad is suspicious."
Her head may think he's the culprit, but her heart wants to deny it.
Between parent and child, it's only natural to feel that way.
"But there's no proof that Kotaro-san is the culprit, is there? Just that the way he had you hunt was unnatural..."
"Mm... That's right. Because learning to handle a nata or a mace is normal for anyone from Arata, but we sisters are the only ones made to keep using them for real, all this time..."
"Mmm... No proof... But there's something like circumstantial evidence... It's kind of creepy... How do I put it... Kotaro-san being made the culprit no matter what... It's like I can sense someone out there intending that..."
"You mean the culprit is someone else?"
"I can't say for sure, but that's entirely possible. Letting Kotaro-san's shadow flicker faintly into view... No, it's almost as if Kotaro-san himself is playing the part of the culprit..."
What does that mean?
As usual, I couldn't see what Goto was thinking.
"Oh, one more thing to confirm: the police haven't been told about these curse-killings or about Sakura-san's death, right?"
"Mm... Seems that way. Grandma says she doesn't want to hand anyone from this settlement over to the police..."
"Hmm, hmm...
Got it! I don't have hard proof yet, but I can see the general background now, so don't worry, Chiga-nee!"
"Mm... Really!? Dad isn't the culprit!?"
"Sorry, but I can't say any more than this. I'd like everyone to act just the way you are now, as much as possible. If you hear my theory and let something slip, someone might pick up on it. Kotaro-san seems to be a frighteningly smart man."
"Also, the accounts of the people in Arata will probably contradict each other, just very slightly. So in the conversations from here on, watch out for that one thing."
True. If the culprit is hiding among the settlement's people, a contradiction should turn up somewhere.
But if it hasn't come to light in these 10 years, the contradiction must be that trivial and inconspicuous.
I'll keep an eye out for it.
Then the door of the abandoned hut opened.
"Ah, Sis, there you are! 　Geez, not moving when you're lost is the most basic rule there is, isn't it!?"
"Mm... Sorry. Waiting over there is a little tiring."
"Well, that's true! 　The ladder's up, so we can get through now! Hurry, let's go!"
If Kotaro-san put her up to it, this girl would come after us...?
Kana-chan's smile was too innocent for me to believe it right away.
