The New Families

Wabigoto Arc [Apology] · Shiyo 781-11-24 14:00 木 · 71 boxes

"Um... this kind of thing... isn't uncommon."
The doctor delivered the verdict in a calm voice.
Even though I was sitting down, my knees shook uncontrollably.
"No... That can't be true...! 　Please! One more time... Please check one more time!"
"Well, but... we've run every test there is... I'm afraid it's 100% certain..."
I sat on the chair, utterly stunned.
My happiness began to come crashing down.
"Um... given the situation, it would be best to have surgery as soon as possible. If it's left as is, it could affect the mother's health as well..."
"No! Absolutely not! 　Mine... mine and Eiichiro-san's baby...!"
I clutched my belly and sank to the floor.
"And also... I think it's best to tell you this sooner rather than later... I know this is a cruel thing to say at a time like this..."
―What I was told next, like a follow-up blow.
That I would never again be able to get pregnant...
The new family member that Eiichiro-san had been so happy about.
The dream we had talked about, of making our children friends with Akane's.
All of it had turned into an illusion.
My heart was plunged into the abyss.
―Some days later, I had the new family member who had died taken out of my belly.
"I'm sorry, Eiichiro-san...!"
After I left the hospital, I knelt before Eiichiro-san with my knees and forehead on the floor and apologized at the top of my voice.
"Mifuyu-chan... there's no reason for you to apologize... It's a shame about the baby, but I'm glad you're safe, Mifuyu-chan... As long as you're safe, Mifuyu-chan, that's all I need..."
Eiichiro-san offered me every word of encouragement he could.
However,
"Why... How can you say something so carefree!? 　It's fine because I'm safe!? You were so happy with me when we found out we were having a child! Was that a lie!? 　That's... that's just...!"
I ran into the bedroom alone.
"No... I don't want this...!"
I clutched my head and lay face down on the floor.
What an awful woman I am.
Even Eiichiro-san must not have known how to deal with me.
And yet I had said such terrible things.
Eiichiro-san would probably be fed up with me too.
Akane would probably find it hard to see me, too.
Family... the family I had always dreamed of...
It's all... over now...
―For a while I stayed burrowed in the bed alone.
I remembered Akane once telling me I was extreme.
Thinking it was all over had probably been a bit extreme after all.
Akane's words calmed my heart just a little.
Next, I thought about how I could face Eiichiro-san.
Eiichiro-san isn't to blame for anything. Any way I look at it, I'm the one at fault.
"Mifuyu-chan, Akane-chan's here."
I heard Eiichiro-san's voice through the door.
"Mifuyu, I'm coming in, okay?"
I stayed under the covers without moving, but the door opened without waiting for my answer.
It seemed only Akane had come into the room.
I had heard Akane was suffering from terrible morning sickness.
She must have really mustered all her strength to come.
I had told her by phone on the very day we found out the baby had died.
At the time, Akane had just listened to me and hadn't said much.
If she had come despite morning sickness that bad, did she have something to tell me in person?
"Hey, Mifuyu..."
Akane gently stroked my back through the covers.
I was still burrowed in the bed.
"Akane... I'm sorry. I wanted our kids to be friends..."
"What are you talking about... Right now, what matters is you getting back on your feet, Mifuyu."
"They said... I can't have babies anymore..."
"Yeah... I heard that too. And so... um... about that, there's something I want to suggest..."
Hearing that much, I poked my head out from under the covers.
"I talked it over with Ryoji-kun too, and... my child, won't you adopt them?"
"Huh...?"
I slowly sat up.
"Akane... don't say weird things... Something like that..."
"The way I'm doing now, I can have a second one too. Mifuyu, you'd raise them properly, wouldn't you?"
It's a fact that if I were to have a child, I would have no choice but to use a surrogate mother or adopt.
But...
"I'm sorry, Akane... I can't answer something like that out of nowhere..."
"I see... then think it over. Eiichiro-san has to weigh in on this too."
"............"
To be honest, if adopting meant I could have a child, I want to welcome them as family.
And if it were Akane's child, I'm confident I would raise them with all my heart and love.
Akane had been so happy about having a child, and yet she had made this offer.
Not only Akane but Ryoji-san too must have felt terribly sorry for me.
But... even so... stealing Akane's happiness away from her...
There's no way that could make me happy...
Having calmed down somewhat, I left the bedroom with Akane.
In the living room were not only Eiichiro-san but Ryoji-san as well.
From the look of the two of them, they seemed to have been having the same talk as us.
"Well, we'll head home. There's Akane's health to think of."
"Got it. I'll talk it over with Mifuyu-chan and decide."
I couldn't read from Eiichiro-san's expression what he was thinking.
But if he was going to talk it over with me and decide, it seemed he wasn't ruling it out completely, either.
"See you. Akane, we're going home."
Only Eiichiro-san and I were left in the room.
"Eiichiro-san, I'm sorry. For taking it out on you earlier..."
"No, it's okay. Anybody gets like that sometimes."
Eiichiro-san spoke kind words to me, as always.
With Eiichiro-san and Akane both, I really am blessed with the people around me.
"What do you think, Mifuyu-chan? You heard from Akane-chan, didn't you?"
"Uugh..."
I couldn't give an answer right away after all.
No, what that really meant was that I was torn.
I realized I was harboring hopes along the lines of, if everyone would allow it...
"Sorry, you must be tired. Want to rest a bit? Or how about we go for a drive, to take your mind off things?"
Eiichiro-san was trying every way he could to look after my feelings.
But I couldn't answer that suggestion right away either.
"Come on, let's go! Today, you're keeping me company!"
Eiichiro-san was unusually forceful for him, pulling me by the hand and out the door.
The air outside was cold. It seemed winter would soon be setting in for real.
We got into the car.
There was no conversation between us.
Eiichiro-san drove without telling me where we were going.
I could tell it was a road heading out of Motoki-cho, but...
―Gradually the buildings disappeared from view, and we went into the mountains.
"Here we are."
The car stopped at a patch of leveled gravel near the top of Mount Motoki.
When I got out of the car, my cheeks were brushed by mountain air with an even sharper chill.
Over in a corner was a roasted sweet potato vendor's cart.
I stood in front of the sweet potato vendor with Eiichiro-san.
"Excuse me, one roasted sweet potato, please."
"Coming right up!"
There was only one old woman at the stall.
When she opened the lid of the big oven, an intense wave of heat rose up.
We took the sweet potato and sat on a bench nearby.
Eiichiro-san was a big eater, just as his looks suggested, and yet he had only bought one.
"Here."
Eiichiro-san broke the sweet potato in half and handed it to me.
"Ow, hot!"
Held in my cold hands, the sweet potato felt even hotter.
The sweet aroma and warm steam rising from it brought back the appetite I had lost.
I tried gently biting into the potato with my front teeth.
...It was so warm and sweet.
The cold air and the mountain scenery brought out the flavor of the sweet potato all the more.
"This sure brings back memories... Mifuyu-chan, the time I first met you and we talked in Central Park."
Come to think of it, back then too, a cold early-winter wind had been blowing, just like today.
How many years had it been since then?
Once they've gone by, they're over in a flash.
But one thing is certain: by Eiichiro-san's side, I had always been given happy times.
He's truly more than I deserve.
"Mifuyu-chan. I think it's fine for us to adopt."
"Huh...?"
Eiichiro-san said that before taking a bite of his potato.
"Mifuyu-chan, you think it's wrong to lean on her this much, don't you?"
"Yeah... Akane always tells me that if I'm ever in trouble, she'll help me with anything. But... this is the one thing..."
"I think Akane-chan's looking further ahead. Leaving her precious child with us, and then having a second... Then she could raise children of her own blood together with you, Mifuyu-chan. If that happened, Akane-chan and Mifuyu-chan would be tied by an even stronger bond, she figures."
"Uugh... but... even so, I..."
"No, that's not all. If we don't adopt, how will things go from here? Akane-chan could never bring herself to come see you, Mifuyu-chan, with a child in her arms."
"That... may be true, but..."
Bringing her child in front of me was something Akane wouldn't want to do.
Even if I meant not to mind, I might come to envy her.
"You see, I think we should adopt, not only for your sake, Mifuyu-chan, but for Akane-chan's sake too. Mifuyu-chan, you want to stay as close with Akane-chan as you've always been, don't you?"
And then Eiichiro-san took his first bite of sweet potato.
"Hot hot hot!"
Eiichiro-san chewed the sweet potato while letting out little yelps.
I dropped my gaze to my own sweet potato.
Maybe because of the cold air, there was a little less steam coming off it.
I took another bite of sweet potato too.
And again, warm steam and a sweet aroma rose from the sweet potato.
"This sweet potato is delicious."
"Isn't it? Actually, I hear this place is a hidden gem that only people in the know know about. I'd always thought I'd like to come here with you someday, Mifuyu-chan."
Eiichiro-san took a big chomp of sweet potato this time.
"Ogogogogo! Hhot hhot hhot!"
"Hey! You're going to burn yourself like that!?"
Of course it was hot, stuffing that much into his mouth at once.
Every time Eiichiro-san huffed and puffed with his mouth, steam came out of it.
"Haah! So good!"
Eiichiro-san's round face broke into a smile.
Seeing Eiichiro-san's profile like that, I was able to smile too, for the first time in a long while.
"Ma'am! One more sweet potato, please!"
"Whoooo are you calling ma'am?"
"Ah, no no, miss! This sweet potato is delicious! My wife loves it too! 　Right, Mifuyu-chan!"
"Y-yes! It's delicious!"
"Of course, of course. They're potatoes from my own field. Well, I rent the field from the landlord, mind you. Even so, it makes me happy when folks enjoy potatoes I put my heart into growing."
Potatoes grown in a borrowed field...
Even so, that didn't change how delicious they were.
They're this delicious surely because she put her heart into growing them.
"Eiichiro-san... I..."
"You don't have to answer right away. There's still time to think it over. Let's talk about this as many times as we need and then decide. Oh, miss, one more, please!"
"Honestly, Eiichiro-san, you won't be able to eat your dinner."
"It's fine, it's fine! Mifuyu-chan, your cooking's so good I can always eat it!"
"What a red-hot couple... You're going to scorch my potatoes."
"Yes, Eiichiro-san is the best husband in the world!"
"Mifuyu-chan, you're the best wife in the world too!"
"Here, here, take these home with you! It's embarrassing just watching you two."
The old woman gave us a whole five sweet potatoes.
"Eiichiro-san, I'm going to call Akane. I'll tell her to think harder about whether she really wants to give her child up for adoption. We'll talk it over as many times as it takes, and then I want to come up with an answer we can accept."
"That's a good idea. For both your sakes, something like this needs careful thought."
"Hello, Mifuyu? Feeling any better?"
"Yeah... I went out with Eiichiro-san to eat roasted sweet potatoes."
"Pfft! 　That's so Eiichiro-san! 　Oh, right, right. My baby's going to be born next April, right? 　If that one goes to the Niimura family, and I manage to have a second one the very next year, then my kid and yours, Mifuyu, could be friends even though they're a year apart, don't you think?"
"Hey, Akane, you're getting ahead of yourself... We can't decide yet..."
"Honestly, you... Reeeeeeeeally, you never get over that wishy-washy streak, no matter how much time goes by! 　Right now, you just think about what will make you happiest! 　I worked out how I'll be happiest, together with Ryoji-kun!"
"I'm grateful you'd say that. But there's no need to rush, so let's take more time to think it over. Talking it over as many times as it takes, so we can both accept it."
"Hmm... well, I guess you're right. But knowing you, when push comes to shove you won't be able to decide again, will you?"
"Uugh..."
"That 'Uugh...' again... Say that verbal tic one more time and I'll push the second one on you too!?"
"H-hey, Akane...!"
"Ahahaha! Well, that part's a joke. Anyway, I made that proposal not just for you, Mifuyu, but for me too, so don't go saying no right away! 　Prop- er- ly, give it your consideration! 　That is all!"
"Hahaha... Akane-chan's voice was really loud."
"Eiichiro-san... I still don't want to give up on happiness after all...!"
―The following spring, with my husband and me in attendance, a healthy baby girl was born.
The child became our adopted daughter and was named Haruka.
And the summer of the year after that, Akane gave birth to another girl.
That child was named Natsumi.
Haruka and Natsumi-chan are sisters of the same blood, born a year apart.
But we decided not to tell them until they were both adults.
I thought of Haruka as if she were my own real daughter.
Haruka was the precious daughter of Akane and me.
Haruka grew up healthy and strong alongside Natsumi-chan.
