Self-Blame

Jusatsu Arc [Killing by Curse] · Shiyo 800-04-08 19:10 水 · 35 boxes

I kept pedaling my bike at full speed.
That was because I didn't want the people I passed to see my face.
I was about to cry under the moonlight.
No, the tears were already flowing.
As I pedaled, in my heart I apologized to Niimura-senpai over and over.
"For me, Natsumi's important, but you, Goto, are really important too. So, don't do anything reckless."
"I know that! Please trust me!"
Niimura-senpai had worried about my safety and told me to stop investigating.
And in answer to that, I had told her to trust me.
But, I had already decided.
I can't forgive the culprit who made Kogori-senpai sad, no matter what.
I had no intention at all of stopping the investigation.
Right now, I could still hold off on acting and make it so I never deceived her.
Even so, I won't stop.
I'm sorry, I told a lie.
I deceived Niimura-senpai...
This is no small lie.
Niimura-senpai worried about my safety, and yet I'm about to throw that away.
I had held a conviction that I would never do anything like deceiving the people who mattered to me.
Ever since I was little, studying was the one thing I was good at.
Since before I started elementary school, teachers and parents alike had praised me: genius, genius.
I say I was good at studying, but it's not as if I'd done anything special.
It's probably the thing called talent, but I think of it as one of my individual traits.
Just because I was good at studying, I didn't mean to be stuck-up about it, either.
But, maybe people found someone like me hard to approach; I never made anyone I could call a friend.
Compared to getting good scores on tests, or being praised by teachers, my desire to join everyone's circle was stronger.
If my test results were about the same as everyone else's, could I join everyone's circle?
Once, I got a bad score on a test on purpose.
It was a bad score by my standards, a score a little above average.
When I got home and showed her my test score, Mom sadly furrowed her brow.
"Erina, what's wrong? Did something hard happen? You can tell me anything, okay?"
The situation didn't change.
If anything, I had made Mom sad.
I stopped getting bad scores, and on the next test I got my usual score.
Mom was very happy.
Right then, a grin from ear to ear had come out on my face on its own.
"Oh my, Erina, that smile, it's very nice."
I see. Even just this much changes the impression you make quite a lot.
Ever since then, I'd gotten into the habit of grinning from ear to ear.
And then, strangely, classmates had started to gather around me on their own.
I was happy. I was very happy.
But...
Even so, my personality didn't change.
I was bad at reaching out on my own, and somehow I kept people at a distance.
Strictly speaking, I think I couldn't fully trust the people around me.
What part of me were they all seeing?
If it was only the smile, then in the end that was only the surface.
If the person that was me couldn't be accepted, things might go right back to how they were.
That's why I just couldn't lay myself bare to the people around me.
The decisive reason I felt that way was that everyone called me "Erina-chan."
Between friends, people called each other by just their names, or by weird nicknames, and with me there was none of that at all.
It might have been selfish, but for me, with my oddly sharp eye for observation, that was what made me feel the gap between me and the people around me.
I know the reason.
The title "genius girl" that had stuck to me at some point, and the overestimation that came with it: the fear that they would widen the distance between me and the people around me.
For me, "genius girl" was far from a strength; it was a complex.
But, if I stopped that, I would make Mom sad again.
That attitude of mine must have come across to the people around me, too.
I did find a few people I could talk with a little, but not enough to call friends; my personality stayed as gloomy as ever, and only my smile took on a life of its own.
Unable to approach the people around me, unable to back away, the desire for friends and the anxiety of showing myself: I was in agony amid these conflicting feelings.
And then, once I was in middle school, I met Kogori-senpai, who happened to be in the same club.
"So your name's Goto. Nice to meet you, Goto!"
Even the upperclassmen had called me "Erina-chan," but the first to call me by just my name was her.
"Genius girl? Yeah, I've heard that. Hey, hey, more importantly, Goto, I've got a friend called Haruka, and―"
For me, who had only ever dealt with people who got defensive over the "genius girl" reputation, Kogori-senpai's attitude was fresh, and it didn't make me feel any wall.
With Kogori-senpai, who always paid attention to me at the same eye level, I little by little became able to show myself, too.
And also with the one I got to know through Kogori-senpai, Niimura-senpai.
"So this one's the genius girl Goto! Huh, you're a total shorty!"
"Sh... shorty...?"
"Hey now, are you eating properly? Isn't all the nutrition going to your brain, so your growth stopped?"
"I-I do eat properly... I may not look it, but I'm, pretty... um... a big eater..."
"Huh? What'd you say? You're too quiet, I can't hear you!?"
"E... even like this, I'm a big eater!!"
"Oho! All right, in honor of our meeting, I'll act like a senpai and treat you to something at Zahha! Let's see it, then, Goto's so-called big appetite!"
"Goto-sama... I'm sorry. Please, have mercy already..."
"Goto, you're amaaazing! Couldn't you enter an eating contest!?"
"Damn... my allowance for this month...
Hey, Goto! After I treated you to all this, you'd better keep me company when I'm free!"
"Right, right, Haruka's not in any club, and she's got a lot of time on her hands."
"Natsumi...! You guys, show your elders a little more respect!"
"Huh? Niimura-senpai, you're a third-year!? You don't have any composure at all, do you?"
"Oh...! You've got some nerve, Goto...!"
Like this, before I was even aware of it, the chances to lay myself bare kept growing.
I really like who I am now.
I can discover a me I never knew before, and every day is fun.
There are people who accept that, and it's so much fun.
That I could become who I am now is thanks to her.
And being brought together with Niimura-senpai, that is also thanks to her.
People like these, at least, I'll live without ever doing anything like deceiving them.
That was what I had vowed.
I'm sorry... I'm sorry...!
Me right now, I must have the face from before I learned to smile, from that lonely time.
Toward the moon, I grinned from ear to ear as hard as I could.
It's all right... surely it's all right.
The time will come when the three of us can meet with smiles like this again.
For that, I chose this path.
I pedaled my bike hard.
