Whoever eats the most wins!

Even a small amount packs a heat and a smell that are no joke.

Erina Goto... my cute junior... thank you truly for everything up to today...
Now's my chance...!
The moment the two took their eyes off the sushi tub, I loaded two pieces of sushi with a huge amount of wasabi.
And then, never noticing, the two of them grabbed the wasabi sushi at the same moment and crammed each into their mouths in one bite.
