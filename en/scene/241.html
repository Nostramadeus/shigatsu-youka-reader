And hemp cloth would dry relatively quickly, too.

The hemp cloth was as thick as a carpet.
The grating's mesh was fine too, so just lying on it, you wouldn't notice.
Obasan shifted the grating with a crowbar, opening the door to the underground.
A rusted ladder was fixed there, leading down into pitch-black darkness.
When I listened closely, I could faintly hear the sound of flowing water.

"Ugh... this ladder's covered in rust..."
Haruka slowly climbed down the ladder.
"Okay, Natsumi, go ahead!"
She seemed to have made it down safely.
The 5-meter ladder was taller than I'd imagined.
I slowly climbed down the gritty, unpleasant ladder.
Rusted as it was, it seemed firmly fixed to the wall and felt steady.
Even so, I made my way toward the underground space without looking down.
"Haah, that was scary..."
My hands were covered in rust, and my clothes had gotten a little dirty too.
The underground space seemed less like something dug out and more like a storeroom that made use of a cave that was already there, but there were some traces of digging by human hands.
A drainage channel had been dug in the corner, and water trickled along it.
This channel probably carried the rain that fell in from above somewhere else.
Oil lamps were mounted on the walls.
Rather than an underground room, an underground passage might be the right way to describe it.
The space went on far into the back.
"Mom, what's back there?"
"I've been told not to go too far baaack. There was a cave-in decades ago, and it's supposedly dangerouuus!"

"Come to think of it, on the way here there was a rock overhang you could shelter from the rain under. Maybe the ground here is easily eroded by water. If I remember right...

"Hmm... As long as you don't go too far back, that's fiiine! 　There's supposedly a door made of iron bars, so don't go past it, okaaay!"

Somehow I hesitated to go any further in, but I couldn't stop here.
Goto in the lead, I went on into the depths of the cave with Haruka and Kana-chan.
We went on, relying on our phone lights.
The cave was longer than I'd imagined.
Drainage channels ran along both sides of the passage the whole way, and only the quiet flow of water and our footsteps echoed.
