Shigatsu Youka ~Secret Contact~

Eden no Sakura Arc [Eden's Cherry] · Shiyo 800-04-07 20:30 火 · 68 boxes

An eerie roar rang out without warning― This is the same sound I heard at Toyotake Station!
Kana-chan and I ran outside and looked up at the sky.
A beautiful moon hung in the sky, ill-matched to the echoing roar.
And under that sky―
"Goto!"
"Kogori-senpai! Kana-chan too!"
Goto seemed to have come out of the storehouse too, startled by the sound.
"Haruka... I hope she's okay... She hasn't answered my messages either... I guess the signal really doesn't reach underground."
―And then Goto suddenly grabbed Kana-chan by the shoulders and roared at her.
"K-Kana-chan! You told her!? You talked about Niimura-senpai to Kogori-senpai!?"
Goto took Kana-chan by both shoulders, shook her, and pressed her in a loud voice.
"Um... what about Haruka-chan...?"
"Did you tell her that Niimura-senpai was going to the cave!?"
"Y-yeah..."
"Um, Goto... what's wrong!?"
"Kogori-senpai, please stay inside the house! 　And close your eyes, and cover your ears too!"
The roar that had been aimed at Kana-chan was turned on me too.
At that glare, thrust up at me from below, I took a step back.
"Uh, um..."
"Never mind that, Senpai, please just come, quickly!"
Goto grabbed my hand by force and pulled me into the house.
"Kogori-senpai, listen!? 　Do NOT leave this room, abso-lute-ly not!"
"Um... what's this about...?"
"I can't explain that! 　When you need the bathroom, call me! 　Do not leave this room without me, ever!"
"Mm... o-okay..."
"Sorry, Senpai, but right now this is the only way!"
"Okay..."
The fusuma was shut with a snap.
What is this... why won't anyone tell me anything...
Is she that resentful about being shut up in this settlement because of me? Does she hate me?
No, no, that's not it. Goto's attitude wasn't anything so simple.
Goto was panicking.
Panicking because I'd learned what Haruka was doing.
In other words, it's bad for me to know the current situation.
What could the reason be?
The first thing that comes to mind is my precognition.
Chiga-nee apparently has the same ability I do―does it have something to do with her being missing?
A message seems to have arrived.
"Natsumi, for now, just do as everyone says."
It was from Dad.
Through the bugging app, Dad knows everyone's situation.
And yet he sent a message rather than calling, and such a vague one at that, which means...
"Okay, thank you."
All I can do is trust everyone.
What I can do is stop my ears and turn my eyes from everything and devote myself to 'knowing nothing.'
So what I can do is 'do nothing.'
Believe what Goto says, and just stay here―
Lying on my back, I stared blankly at a corner of the ceiling.
The settlement where such a roar had rung out just now was strangely quiet.
A place so quiet you'd wonder whether people really lived here.
I even found myself thinking that maybe I was the only one left in this settlement right now.
That Goto and Kana-chan and Obaasan had all actually gone from here...
I gave myself up to that impossible fantasy and cursed this constitution of mine.
I ended up with this constitution because of me.
I let the balloon fly, got Haruka's dad killed, and got his blood on me.
That was the beginning of everything.
If I hadn't let the balloon fly that day, Haruka's dad wouldn't have died, and nobody would have been dragged into all this.
Regret at this point is useless, but I never imagined it would turn into something this big.
Something as small as letting a balloon fly has grown into a huge case, one thing toppling the next in a chain.
As if it were all a single story, plotted out in advance.
Like a current you can't resist―
A story plotted out in advance...
............ No, surely not.
What if this 'someone' Goto called Necro had plotted out everything, every single bit...
Hmm. Somewhere along the line, Necro's will intervened―no doubt about that.
Some events happened by chance, but Necro didn't let them slip; it intervened, disguised as more chance.
Going by everything I've heard, that's perfectly possible.
Then where does Necro's story begin and end?
What is Necro, to begin with?
Granting precognition, making cherry trees sprout out of nowhere―clearly something not human.
No, let's stop thinking. I mustn't know.
Let me send Haruka a message at least.
She's taking a while coming back, too.
"Haruka, are you okay? You haven't come back for ages, so I'm worried."
I watched the sent message for a while, but... no sign of it being marked read.
She did say she was going to the cave, so the signal might be bad.
And maybe I'd better send this one too.
"I heard from a girl called Kana-chan―you went to a cave with Obasan, right? To look for a mummy"
This is my warning.
What I heard from Kana-chan, passed on to Haruka just as it was.
That lets her know what situation I'm in now.
Haruka's anxiety might get fanned, but if it's dangerous for me to know where Haruka is, I should let her know quickly.
If this brings her back quickly, I'll be relieved, but...
This time, a call from Dad.
"Hello?"
"How are you holding up?"
"Yeah... so-so."
Not that it matters―he already knows all about me crying in the washroom just now, being comforted by Erika-chan, being yelled at by Goto.
"Did you call because you were worried?"
"Yeah, well, sort of..."
"Sort of...?"
"Natsumi, this is how it is."
"Huh?"
This is how it is... what is?
No subject, no predicate, no modifiers―it meant nothing.
"Hang on, I'll put Akane on."
"Hello?"
"Mom... are you two at home right now?"
"That's right. Don't worry, we haven't snuck off to Arata or anything."
"Yeah."
"Natsumi, we can hear the voices. Wherever you are, we can hear the voices."
"Yeah..."
"But we can hear Haruka-chan's and Goto-chan's voices too, clearly."
"???"
What does the 'but' mean?
This isn't a conversation that calls for a 'but.'
"But we can't see what you girls are seeing. All we get is the voices."
"???"
"All we can make out is the three of your voices."
"Um, Mom? What... what does that mean?"
"Natsumi, that's how it is."
Mom too, like Dad, leaving out the subject, the predicate, the modifiers.
Could this be...
"Mom, are you... are you giving me a hint!?"
"Natsumi! That's enough! I'm hanging up now."
"Wait! Mom!"
"Natsumi, it's all right. Trust everyone."
She hung up.
Nobody will give me information. Not the information that matters.
No―think before you panic! That was an important hint from Dad and Mom!
Mom and Dad can hear my voice.
'But' they can hear Haruka's and Goto's voices too.
What a 'but' that doesn't fit the context means― is this.
Something has been left out wholesale.
"Mom and Dad can hear our voices. (Something goes here). 'But' they can hear Haruka's and Goto's voices too."
With a structure like that, it would be natural.
What goes there?
What belongs there is―
Judging from the current situation, my knowing something causes harm.
Regardless of what I intend.
Even if I learned the secret, swearing not to tell would be meaningless.
Come to think of it―
"To stoke our anxiety, it shows Kogori-senpai illusions it passes off as precognition, and steers us into coming to the Arata settlement. It seems to be a tougher enemy than I'd imagined."
In the car on the way to Arata, Goto had said that.
Make it look as if I have danger sense, and feed me information that suits Necro.
And the cherry tree at Toyotake Station appeared the moment I said let's go home.
As if Necro had been watching us.
Watching...?
So that's it...!
Necro can somehow watch me.
So it can also know what I see and hear.
Goto realized that, and is keeping information from me.
In other words, what was left out of that phone call was―
"Mom and Dad can hear my voice. Necro can't hear Haruka's and Goto's voices. 'But' Mom and Dad can hear Haruka's and Goto's voices too."
In other words, they were letting me know what Necro is doing to me.
Putting it all together―
Dad and Mom can hear our voices, but can't tell what we're seeing.
Necro can hijack my sight and hearing, but can't tell Haruka's and Goto's.
With this, what everyone says and does all fits!
What Goto did just now was a message: don't look at anything, don't listen to anything, so that Necro doesn't find out what she's investigating.
By that logic, did Chiga-nee have her senses hijacked by Necro too, and get made to disappear before everything was found out?
I see... Then... then I can see what I can do.
What I can do is keep Necro deceived.
Judging from how Dad and Mom behaved just now, the message was: watch out for your sight and hearing.
Put the other way, whatever I think doesn't reach Necro.
If even my thoughts got through, they'd never have given me a hint like this.
Then again... there's no guarantee that Necro didn't catch what I just caught from that hint.
Dad and Mom probably called because they couldn't bear to watch me being isolated, but...
If Necro has noticed 'that I've noticed,' I could go missing like Chiga-nee.
I can't be too optimistic...
I opened the window and drew a deep breath of the air outside.
The smell of the mountains, of nature.
Only after coming to a remote place like this did I finally understand phrases like 'the air tastes good' or 'a refreshing taste.'
If nothing were wrong, a place rich with nature all around.
But Necro's existence threatens it.
"Trust everyone"―the words came back to me.
Everyone is on my side. I don't intend to doubt that.
So I want to be on everyone's side too.
Every one of them is doing what they can.
I've found what I can do, too.
Once more, I took a deep breath of air.
Fresh, refreshing air, born in the heart of nature―
That's what I meant to breathe in.
My nose caught a faint acrid smell.
An acrid smell I hadn't noticed when I opened the window.
"Hm?"
I could see someone running from the direction of the storehouse.
Who was that? Too big to be Goto―probably a man.
The figure melted into the dark and was gone at once.
And just then―
"Fire! Fiiire!"
That was clearly Goto's voice.
The acrid smell grew stronger and stronger.
No doubt about it―the smell of smoke.
The fire seemed to have broken out quite close by.
It might spread to here.
But was it all right to run?
Goto had said never to leave this room...
"Kogori-senpai! There's a fire! Please get out of the house right now!"
Goto, who should have been downstairs, seemed to have come rushing back.
Shoes still on, badly out of breath.
"G-got it!"
Thank goodness―I'm allowed to run from here.
No, a fire isn't good, but still.
People from all over the settlement gathered and tried to put out the storehouse fire.
Goto and I followed Kana-chan and took refuge near the meeting hall.
Goto and Kana-chan both looked up blankly at the orange sky.
Was this Necro's doing?
No―there was clearly a suspicious person.
The man who ran from the storehouse―he has to be the culprit.
I thought of telling Goto that.
But then I remembered what I have to do.
I have to keep Necro deceived.
To keep Necro deceived, I have to feed false information into my own sight and hearing.
That is, I have to lie.
"Goto... is this my fault too...? 　Did Necro burn the storehouse...?"
At my words, Goto's eyebrows twitched.
What will Goto make of it?
Will she see through the lie, or decide it was Necro's doing after all?
"Kogori-senpai... this is, well..."
Not saying at once that it was Necro's doing means she's at least somewhat torn.
Or else she's keeping information from me.
But for me, this fire may have been a small stroke of luck.
I've just figured out how to keep Necro deceived.
Tell Goto an obvious lie, and let her feel that something's off.
Anyway, next I have to find a way to let her know, indirectly, that I've noticed Necro's nature.
