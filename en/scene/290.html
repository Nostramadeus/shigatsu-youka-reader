The Trumpeter of the Apocalypse ~Betrayal~

Eden no Sakura Arc [Eden's Cherry] · Shiyo 800-04-07 21:45 火 · 43 boxes

The one who appeared there was the missing Chiga-nee.
Maybe she had been watching our exchange. Gripping a knife, she cut in between us.
But what was going on? Chiga-nee was covered in blood, and the bleeding from her face was especially bad.
Maybe her wounds weren't as bad as they looked. Chiga-nee showed no sign of minding the bleeding, but...
"Mm... Auntie and Haruka went to the abandoned hut out toward Toyotake! All of you, take shelter there too!"
"Sis, but that place is..."
"Kana! Show them the way! There's no time! Leave this to me!"
"G-got it! Eri-nee! Natsumi-chan! Hurry!"
The abandoned hut out toward Toyotake―according to Chiga-nee, the two of them are there.
What's more, we're being asked to go there too.
Apparently it's important enough for Chiga-nee to be putting herself on the line, resolved to see this through.
I have a mountain of things to ask Chiga-nee, but our safety comes first.
With Kana-chan leading the way, we'll try to break through.
"W-wait!"
The resident standing at the very front raised a nata high.
But Chiga-nee at once planted herself in front of that resident.
"Mm... Don't get in my way. I mean it. If you lay a hand on those girls... I'll kill you!"
"Eri-nee! Hurry!"
I cast a glance toward Chiga-nee, and Kana-chan forcibly pulled me away.
Chiga-nee, are you really all right?
Did you really do that just to protect us?
"Goto! Pull yourself together!"
The one who barked at me, my steps slowed by hesitation, was Kogori-senpai.
As if her voice had woken me up, my feet started moving too.
I looked back toward Chiga-nee once more.
Chiga-nee, her knife leveled at that group―
Is she going to fight them after all?
Things beyond my understanding are already happening in this Arata settlement.
"Hah... hah..."
Suddenly my field of vision opened up, and we came out into a clearing with a commanding view.
Sprinting 200 meters down a gravel road I wasn't used to had been pretty hard work.
Kogori-senpai and I stopped here to catch our breath.
Kana-chan, without the slightest sign of being out of breath, was looking at the sky.
And carefully, as if feeling out our surroundings, she gazed quietly up at the sky.
In the sky hung the big moon, one night short of full.
From that moon, what do we look like?
Something like a boss mushroom called Shigatsu Youka has taken over this whole area, parasitized human beings, and caused all this.
No... maybe the ones who took over this land were the humans.
Shigatsu Youka had been rooted here since long ago, blended into the natural world, and yet one day humans suddenly came along―
But if I'm asked whose side I'm on, it goes without saying.
If Kogori-senpai's premonition is right, going back to Motoki-cho as we are will not end the calamity.
This has to be settled one way or another, here.
Then again, there's also a chance that Kogori-senpai's premonition itself is a deception by Shigatsu Youka...
"Kana-chan, how much farther to the abandoned hut?"
"About 15 minutes on foot, I guess. But it was pouring around there this evening, so the path is probably a muddy mess. It's easy to get lost, so don't leave my side, okay?"
"Hmm... Going back to the settlement now―better not even think about it, right...?"
"With everyone acting like that? It's probably dangerous."
Just as the residents said, wouldn't it have been safer to go straight back to the settlement?
We, at least, might have been safe.
But we left the settlement to look for Niimura-senpai and the others.
Since that goal hasn't been achieved, going back to the settlement now is questionable too.
"For now, all we can do is go to that abandoned hut, right..."
True, there seems to be no room for choice.
Since Chiga-nee said it like that, doing as she says is―
No, but...
"Eri-nee, we're going, okay?"
"Oh, right..."
This clearing was a graveyard, apparently. From here, the path along the cliff led down to the mountain stream, and going on past that would bring us to the mountain path where the abandoned hut was.
I walked on, lost in thought.
Chiga-nee knew that she herself was being sense-hijacked.
On top of that, if she survived despite being carried off, Chiga-nee's survival skills are formidable.
Maybe Chiga-nee has come up with some kind of plan, I thought.
But as long as she's being sense-hijacked, she can't say it out loud, and we have no way of knowing her true intentions.
Yes―if, just as I'm keeping Kogori-senpai deceived so that Shigatsu Youka is deceived, Chiga-nee is keeping us deceived so that Shigatsu Youka is deceived...
For Chiga-nee to fool Shigatsu Youka, she has no choice but to fool us.
"Huh?"
Wait a minute... something's nagging at me.
"Goto, what's wrong?"
"Eri-nee?"
Without thinking, I stopped walking.
I remembered―how Chiga-nee had disappeared from that storehouse.
The corpses had been moving.
Chiga-nee had clearly said "Mom."
And the name Eiichiro―two corpses had been moving.
What matters is how Chiga-nee dealt with that situation afterward.
In other words, either Chiga-nee was kidnapped but escaped on her own, or, unfortunately, Chiga-nee has betrayed us.
But from Chiga-nee's position, either way, she'll at least act in ways that betray us.
Chiga-nee is being watched by Shigatsu Youka at all times, so she can't help us openly.
So if she does do something, she'll pretend to betray us and act in a way that helps us.
Of course, there's also a chance that Chiga-nee has betrayed us, pure and simple...
Chiga-nee would think that far ahead before acting, but now that things have gotten this complicated, I'd better assume nothing will go according to plan.
"Kogori-senpai, Kana-chan, go on to the abandoned hut, just the two of you. I still have something I have to do!"
"Wait―Goto!?"
Without checking how the two of them reacted, I turned on my heel toward the graveyard clearing.
I need to get far enough away that Kogori-senpai can no longer perceive me.
Because I have to give support from the shadows so that Chiga-nee's plan succeeds 100 percent.
Chiga-nee's intentions, our intentions, and the residents' intentions are all at odds.
I don't know Chiga-nee's plan, but uniting everyone's intentions in a situation like this is impossible―in other words, the chance of Chiga-nee's plan succeeding is vanishingly low.
At this point, I can ignore the possibility that Chiga-nee has really betrayed us.
If Chiga-nee has betrayed us, there's no saving us, however we struggle.
I know full well that walking through a settlement like this alone is far too dangerous.
And I might end up getting in Chiga-nee's way.
But I can't just silently go along with Chiga-nee's 'betrayal.'
For some reason, the residents blocked our way.
I can think of only one such reason.
Someone among the residents has realized that if we head to the abandoned hut, things will go exactly as Shigatsu Youka wants.
My grounds for saying so: far from being glad that Chiga-nee had been found, not one of the residents was even surprised.
In other words, the residents already know both that Shigatsu Youka exists and that corpses move.
Now that it's gone beyond the curse-killings and the mastermind has come out into the open, the residents too will resist desperately.
Even as outsiders, we could still become the residents' targets.
How on earth did the residents find out that Shigatsu Youka exists?
Was it thanks to the wisdom of Kotaro-san or Niimura-senpai's grandmother?
If things turn into a three-way standoff at a time like this, even what could be resolved won't be.
I see. Thinking of it that way... here is my hypothesis.
The residents somehow managed to learn that Shigatsu Youka exists, but they believe the kidnapped Chiga-nee has already been killed.
They're convinced that Chiga-nee has become a corpse controlled by Shigatsu Youka, and is acting to lead us astray.
To prevent that, the residents are doing the opposite of Chiga-nee's aim― in other words, keeping us from going to the abandoned hut.
That's why I have to make sure.
Whether Chiga-nee is really dead or not.
If she's alive, this three-way standoff has to be resolved as soon as possible.
After all, Chiga-nee and the residents have the same goal.
Which means...
Damn! Sending Kogori-senpai and the others to the abandoned hut was a mistake!
If Chiga-nee is a corpse, it means we've walked right into Shigatsu Youka's trap!
I'll go back to Senpai and the others right now and―
No good. Without Kana-chan guiding me, there's no guarantee I can reach the abandoned hut.
Besides, if I let Kogori-senpai hear that Chiga-nee might be a corpse, I have no idea how Shigatsu Youka will move.
Damn it! For me to make a mistake like this―what a blunder!
I have to quickly confirm what Chiga-nee and the others really intend, and rejoin Senpai and the others!
