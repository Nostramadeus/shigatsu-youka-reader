Goodbye

Saigo no Koe Arc [Last Voice] · Shiyo 800-05-04 20:00 月 · 71 boxes

"Heave-ho!"
I put the last of the rite tools back in its place.
"Mm... you must be worn out, Haruka."
"Phew! This is pretty tough work... What tools do we need tomorrow?"
"Mm... for tomorrow, just the things up top will be enough."
"Okay! Let's head back, then!"
"You must both be worn out. Let's go back and rest."
"Mom, you okay? You look pretty, or rather, totally wiped out..."
"Huh?
　Ho-ho-ho! This much is nothing at all!"
Mom's loud laugh is tinged with exhaustion.
Until we got here, she and Shirozaki-san must have had a rough time, just the two of them, with the preparations and the town office and everything.
"Hey, can't I help out tomorrow too? The witch's work takes three people, right?"
"Oh my, no! Haruka, you can't become a witch until you turn 20 and I'm dead!"
"Yeah, I know that... But there's nobody around, either, so isn't it about time you did it more efficiently?"
"Mm... Auntie, I think so too. There are only three of us in this settlement: me, Kana, and Shirozaki-san."
"Huh? Chiga-nee, what about school and studying abroad?"
"Mm... I can't leave Kana on her own. And it wouldn't be good for this place to end up empty, either. Shirozaki-san on his own would have a hard time too."
"Seriously...? But the three of you living here forever, that's not great either... Chiga-nee, and Kana too, sooner or later you'll get married and head outside the settlement―"
"Haruka, you don't have to worry about all that. Things like this are the witch's work."
"But come on..."
"Mm... Haruka, for now, pray for Grandma's soul. Haruka, I'm happy you worry about all this, though."
Dad was born in this settlement, Grandma protected it all these years, and now its survival is on the line...
By blood I'm an outsider, but it still makes me sad to see this place wither away.
Isn't there some way I can make this place lively again?
Grandma's wish: turn it into a place where everyone can live happily...
"Chigaya-chan, go on back ahead of us. I've got somewhere to go with Haruka."
"Huh?"
"Mm... All right. Haruka doesn't know yet, after all."
With that, Chiga-nee left, leaving her cryptic words behind.
"Haruka, follow me. Keep me company a little longer."
"Ah, wait up!"
Where on earth are we going?
This way leads toward the graveyard clearing, but...
Mom comes to a stop, and sure enough, it's at the graveyard clearing.
Tomorrow, Grandma will be buried here.
From close by, I can hear the sound of that mountain stream.
"What's up? Checking out Grandma's hole ahead of time?"
The hole to bury Grandma in tomorrow has already been dug.
In the Arata settlement, this custom of burial in the ground still continues to this day.
Even I, who've only ever known Arata's funerals, used to think it was unusual.
Though apparently, on a worldwide scale, cremation is actually the minority, and burial is still more common even now.
"Haruka, right here is where your sister is sleeping."
"My sister?"
"A child of my blood and Eiichiro-san's. She couldn't be born alive."
Saying that, Mom closes her eyes and puts her hands together.
At times like this, people in Arata wear a Noh mask, but Mom puts her hands together with her face bare.
I follow suit and put my hands together.
The other day, Mom told me― about the sister who was in Mom's belly before I was born.
Mom's body was where she arrived, but she ended her short life right there in the womb.
My sister who never saw the light of day, with no blood ties to me, is sleeping here.
"If my sister had lived, I'd have grown up as sisters with Natsumi, huh."
"That's right. She was due at almost the same time as you, Haruka, so it would probably have been a friendship like the one between me and Akane."
"How many months did my sister get to live?"
"5 months. She was inside me for just 5 months. But for those 5 months, I was very happy. Eiichiro-san and I would talk about our dreams for the future, and I'd talk with Akane about how I'd live as a mother."
"............"
Honestly, I have mixed feelings.
I'd thought I grew up with all of Mom's love to myself.
The sister who should have gotten that love is sleeping here.
Is this feeling jealousy? Anger at Mom? Or disappointment in myself for not even knowing about any of this?
"The truth is, I wasn't going to tell you about this, Haruka."
"Huh?"
"If you saw me like this, Haruka, it wouldn't sit well with you, would it?"
"Th-that's not..."
"To me, Haruka, you matter more than anything. Even so, I can't forget this child. Once you knew that, Haruka, you might feel inferior over blood ties..."
"You idiot... As if I'd ever think that..."
The voice I argued back with was weak and small.
"But today might be the last chance. Haruka, for you to meet Ayaka."
"Ayaka...? 　Is that my sister's name?"
"There are hardly any people left in this settlement anymore. In a few more years, it might disappear."
"No way..."
"So while Arata's memorial prayers still go on, I wanted you to meet her, Haruka. Ayaka is my child by blood. Haruka, you're the child I was given through Akane and the bond between us. Which of you matters more, that's not something I can weigh on the scales. Haruka, you, too, are to me―"
"Mom, don't say any more! I get it! 　I know exactly how you've felt all this time, Mom, raising me. Even if you don't put it into words now, I get it..."
I lied.
I cut her off because I didn't want to hear any more about my sister.
I don't mean to deny that my sister existed.
But if my heart had been even a tiny bit more childish than it is now, I might have cried.
"Hahaha! Right, so I had a sister too! 　Hahahaha!"
"............"
Mom's eyes― they're eyes I saw long, long ago.
Yes, the eyes she looks at me with when I lie.
"Hey, hey, Mom! Natsumi's gonna be in elementary school soon too, right!?"
"I see... Natsumi-chan's starting elementary school already. Haruka, is school fun?"
"............ Yeah, it's fun!"
"Really... is it really fun?"
"............ Yeah, it is fun!"
"............"
Mom's eyes are the same as back then.
My lie, Mom saw right through it with an almost frightening look.
"Haruka, if you're having fun, then that's fine. But if anything hurts, make sure you tell me."
"............ Yeah..."
Mom's words made me burst into tears, and I confessed about the bullying.
"If Haruka is all right, then that's enough. But if Haruka is hurting―"
"You haven't changed."
"Huh?"
"Mom, you haven't changed. Not since way back, and not now."
Those eyes, trying to see through me so hard it's almost frightening, are the best proof there is of her love for me.
The way she lives as a mother, desperate to protect me.
Mom hasn't changed.
I look up at the sky―
"Hey, Sis! Our mom is the best in the world!"
I shouted toward the heavens.
"Mom, thank you for letting me meet Sis."
"............"
Mom's frightening eyes had, before I knew it, filled with tears.
"C'mon, let's go home. We've got the burial tomorrow, so it's gonna be rough."
I led Mom by the hand.
"Hey, it's okay if I come here again and say hi, right? 　This isn't some weak place that'll vanish just 'cause there are fewer people. As long as I'm around, it'll be fine."
"............ You're right, it'll be fine..."
I have a place I need to protect: the Arata settlement.
Grandma worked so hard to change this land. Sis is sleeping in this land.
What I can do to protect this land is...
―By the time we got back to the house, it was already 9 p.m.
I soaked in the tub alone.
What I can do for Arata... What could it be?
Grandma worked this hard trying to solve the curse-killings and to make the settlement easy for everyone to live in, and yet...
Abandoning that halfway through is something I just can't do.
Mom has the company, so she can't leave Motoki-cho, and Chiga-nee has a dream she wants to fulfill by studying abroad.
If someone asked whether I have a dream... for now, not really.
No, just now, I found one.
My dream is to rebuild the Arata settlement.
Grandma―I don't think I can do it as well as she did, and besides, there's hardly anyone here to rebuild it with in the first place.
But at this rate, the Arata settlement will disappear.
That's why this place needs someone who will protect it with unshakable will.
What I can do is stay here and keep protecting Arata.
Living here means being separated from Natsumi and Goto, too.
If I don't live here, Chiga-nee will probably quit school and come back.
But rather than Chiga-nee giving up her dream and coming back, there should be someone whose dream is protecting this place.
Mom―what will she say?
She'll definitely be against it.
But I won't back down.
The fate of this settlement depends on my will alone.
I―will absolutely not back down.
Reasoning this flimsy probably won't convince Mom.
If anything, I won't even be able to convince Chiga-nee, and she'll withdraw from school and come back here.
In that case, I'll make it a done deal, even by force, and move here.
Once I'm back in Motoki-cho, I'll do the paperwork to withdraw from school right away, then tell Mom.
I think I'm gonna get yelled at a lot.
But I'm ready for that much.
If I'm scared of getting yelled at, I can't serve as this place's keeper.
First, maybe I'll try talking to Natsumi and Goto.
Those two are sure to be against it too.
I really hate the thought of being apart from Natsumi and Goto, too.
I hate it, but...
I got out of the bath early.
I'll go back to the room and tell Natsumi and Goto.
Just as I was about to go into the room on the second floor,
I passed Natsumi in the hallway.
"Taking a bath now?"
"Yeah.
Haruka, what's wrong? You look really grim."
"Natsumi... after you're out of the bath, I've got something important to tell you both, Natsumi and Goto."
"Okay... you can tell me anything, all right? 　If anything hurts, you can't keep it all to yourself, okay?"
"Yeah..."
"Oh, and also, Goto says she'll look after Kana-chan in Motoki-cho. Then Kana-chan won't be lonely either, right? 　Shirozaki-san―what's he going to do...?"
"Natsumi... Let's talk about that later."
"............? 　Okay, got it..."
Natsumi went down the stairs.
Kana and Shirozaki-san, taking care of them isn't what this is about!
Do you even get what it means for people to be gone from here!?
No, there's no way she could.
Natsumi and Goto―expecting that much from them would be cruel.
But it makes me really happy, the way you look out for me like that.
I've always been held up by Natsumi like this.
From now on, I have to live without Natsumi's support.
Natsumi... I'm sorry... Thank you for everything until now.
In the futon, Goto is breathing softly in her sleep.
Goto cut her family trip short and came all the way here too, huh.
Grandma must be happy about it too.
Thanks, Goto.
Natsumi―once she's back from the bath, should I wake Goto up?
No, I'll talk to Natsumi first.
I've already decided. There's no need to go out of my way to wake Goto when she's tired.
I opened the window and gazed out at the mountains of Arata.
Down in the garden, a cherry tree that's withered away.
It's as if it symbolizes this settlement.
The Arata settlement has withered away.
But I won't give up.
I'm going to bring Arata back to life, for sure.
And so, for that...

Everyone... goodbye.
