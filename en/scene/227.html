I Came to See You.

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-08 06:00 火 · 79 boxes

―April 8, 6 a.m.
I was at the side of Kana-chan, asleep under anesthesia.
The morning sun of April 8 crept faintly into the hospital room.
―5 hours earlier, April 8, 1 a.m.
Right after that, Kana-chan was rushed to the hospital in Niimura-senpai's mother's car, and the police and ambulances raced to the scene.
The residents, in the middle of fighting back against Kana-chan, collapsed and stopped moving.
Kotaro-san and Chiga-nee died at the scene.
Every one of the residents had a deep gash in the neck, and that was the fatal wound that killed them.
In other words, Kana-chan had finished off every one of them.
Kogori-senpai's parents were badly hurt, but neither had a fatal wound.
"It hurts! It hurts! Eri-nee...!"
On the way to the hospital, Kana-chan screamed from the agony and thrashed around.
Little by little the drug's painkilling effect wore off, and unbearable pain tormented the 10-year-old girl.
I held Kana-chan tight, desperately.
It's okay―there's nothing to be scared of.
I'm right here with you.
The fresh blood pouring from Kana-chan's side dyed my clothes deeper and deeper in the color of death.
I knew.
With bleeding like this, there was no hope of her surviving.
Her breathing grew shallower and shallower, and by the time we reached the hospital, Kana-chan was no longer thrashing, no longer screaming.
Still breathing weakly, she was wheeled into the operating room.
―April 8, 1:30 a.m.
The red light of the operating room came on.
I sat motionless in a chair where I could see the light.
Kana-chan, trying to protect us― to save us by sacrificing herself― had plunged the Droga into her own arm without a moment's hesitation.
Had she been confident she could save us without a scratch?
Or had she already meant, back then, to make herself the sacrifice?
I want to believe it was the former.
If Kana-chan used the Droga out of self-sacrifice―that's just too painful.
Until the surgery was over, sleep never once came for me, and I simply prayed for a miracle to happen.
I'm someone who doesn't believe in God, or ghosts, or the next life.
But this time, of all times, I wanted to believe God existed.
No... it's the opposite.
I wanted, with all my heart, to deny that God existed.
As a trial given to Kana-chan, it's far too unfair.
If God is the one giving it... it's far too unfair.
And yet, in my heart, I kept on praying.
If it's a trial... if it's a trial, then it can be overcome, can't it?
Please, save Kana-chan...!
Inside my heart, those contradictory prayers and denials swirled together.
―April 8, 5 a.m.
The operating room light went out, and Kana-chan was brought out.
They had operated, but the wound in her belly was deep, and there was no hope she would survive, they said...
Instead of being moved to the ICU, she was moved to a private room.
So that we could be at Kana-chan's side at the end...
"Goto, here..."
What Niimura-senpai handed me was a handkerchief dampened with water.
"Wipe your face."
When I did as I was told and ran the handkerchief over my face, a red smear came off on it.
Kana-chan's blood had gotten on my face, it seemed.
I'd been holding Kana-chan in the car, so my clothes were covered in blood too.
Such terrible bleeding.
My clothes were so soaked with blood that it made me wonder how this small body could have held so much.
Even so, Kana-chan was still holding on.
"Goto... get a little rest... You haven't slept at all, right?"
During Kana-chan's surgery, Niimura-senpai had stayed with Kogori-senpai.
Kogori-senpai's injuries were apparently nothing serious, so Niimura-senpai had come over here as well.
Kogori-senpai was now in the same hospital room as her parents.
Erika-chan and Senpai's mother were being questioned by the police.
"Niimura-senpai, you're the one who should rest. I... want to stay by Kana-chan's side to the very end..."
Just then, Niimura-senpai's phone rang.
"It's the police. I'll go take it."
Niimura-senpai left the hospital room.
I stroked Kana-chan's pale face.
At first, I thought she was a bit of a pest.
But that was because of the sadness of not having her mother, and the loneliness of there being no other kids her age in the Arata settlement―
Being without her mother, whom everyone had loved, ever since she was born, is probably loneliest at about Kana-chan's age.
"Mo... Mo..."
Kana-chan spoke...!?
"Kana-chan, can you hear me!?"
I took Kana-chan's hand.
And she squeezed back, weakly.
"Eri... nee...?"
"That's right! Kana-chan, you made it!"
"Eri-nee... were you... by my side the whole time?"
"That's right! I mean, I'm Kana-chan's older sister, aren't I!?"
"Thank you... Mommy... I was dreaming she was by my side the whole time... Eri-nee... you're kind like Mommy, so... that's how I got to have a dream like that."
Did this mean the trial had been overcome?
Or was it a mercy God had granted at the very last moment?
No, right now it doesn't matter which!
Because I'd thought I would never hear Kana-chan's voice again.
Even if it was a final mercy, I quietly thanked God.
"Kana-chan... I'm sorry. For being such an unreliable big sister..."
Kana-chan tried to make some expression in answer to my words, but she didn't seem to have even that much strength left.
How was she taking my words, in that hazy state of mind?
"Eri-nee... I... to the very end... was lonely... But... now... Eri-nee's with me... so I'm not lonely..."
"Listen! There are so many places I want to go with you, Kana-chan! 　Motoki-cho has lots of cherry trees, and it's a really beautiful place! 　When you're better... when you're better...! 　Together... okay?"
Even as I encouraged her, the tears wouldn't stop falling from my eyes.
I'm the one encouraging her... so what am I doing crying...!
To cover it up, I talked to Kana-chan a lot.
When Kana-chan gets better... I told her those fleeting dreams, whatever came into my head.
But maybe my feelings had only spun their wheels, because a drop trickled down from Kana-chan's eye.
Why... why is Kana-chan crying too?
Because I'm crying?
Then I won't cry! I won't cry, so...!
So just once more... show me a smile...!
"Eri... nee..."
"I... lo... ve... you..."
"Goto, any minute now Grandma's―"
Niimura-senpai's voice was drowned out by the flat tone of the heart monitor.
Even after the strength had gone from Kana-chan's hand, I kept holding it.
I didn't want to let go.
While there was still warmth in her, I wanted to keep feeling Kana-chan's life until the very, very last.
"Niimura-senpai... did I... manage to be a proper big sister...?"
"Goto...!"
As I kept holding Kana-chan's hand, Niimura-senpai hugged me from behind.
"You and Chiga-nee both... you were the best older sisters Kana could've had..."
"Being a big sister... is hard, isn't it... I said I'd be her big sister just on a whim... and I couldn't do a single thing a big sister should."
"You... There's something more important than that... Kana's face, look at it. She doesn't look like she's suffering, or lonely, does she... You stayed by her side, that's why, right? You were the only one who could've done that, right...?"
Kana-chan's face was as peaceful as the Goddess's.
Was it because I was by her side?
There's no way of knowing―
But if Niimura-senpai was right... then I'm glad I got to meet Kana-chan.
I'm glad I got to be a big sister.
I gently let go of Kana-chan's hand.
Bye-bye.
Me too... I love you, Kana-chan.
―Shiyo 800, August 1
I had come alone to the Arata settlement, now deserted.
Without telling Kogori-senpai or Niimura-senpai, I visited this place by myself.
I climbed that long mountain path alone―
and came to that place.
Maybe planning to make the round trip in one day was too much after all...
It was still only 15:00, but the evening cicadas had already started singing.
If it came to that, I was thinking I'd clean up the Niimura main house or something and stay there just the one night.
The midsummer sun shone dazzlingly into Kana-chan's secret base.
In a corner of the secret base, empty juice cans and empty snack boxes were still lying where they'd been left that day.
I laid sunflowers beside them and put my hands together.
"Kana-chan... happy 11th birthday."
And beside them, I laid the cogongrass flowers I had picked in May.
"Chiga-nee... if Kana-chan's my little sister, then you're my older sister too, Chiga-nee, right?"
I lay down spread-eagled like I had that day and gazed at the sunlight streaming in through the crack in the rock.
On the empty mountains of Arata, the cries of the early evening cicadas echoed shrilly.
It was very cool here.
Kana-chan had said that when something bad happened, she came here alone.
That she could see her mother here.
Are Kana-chan and Chiga-nee watching me from over there?
I closed my eyes and thought back on that day.
With so many mysteries left unsolved, the Arata settlement had perished.
But a certain hypothesis had come to me.
The blood of Niimura-senpai's father, and the danger sense Kogori-senpai gained from being drenched in it.
An unknown drug called Droga, found only in the Arata settlement.
The cherry trees native to Arata, most of which were said to have been carried off by some group or other.
The dying cherry tree at the Niimura main house.
And―the beheaded Sakura-san.
Thinking about it that way: the reason everyone in the Arata settlement dies in April.
The reason Kikyo-san didn't die in April.
The reason there were so many suicides.
Everything connects, even those.
But even if I form a hypothesis and solve the mystery... neither Kana-chan nor Chiga-nee is coming back.
For just a few hours, I had a little sister not related by blood.
I made a friend as far apart from me in age as a grandmother and her grandchild.
My memories of the Arata settlement... that alone is enough.
It should be enough...
But... I can't help thinking about it.
How that tragedy could have been avoided.
There was no way to stand up to Kotaro-san, with his 'unknown ability' to think ahead of everything―none at all, in that situation.
If the same kind of intuition as Kogori-senpai's really was in Kotaro-san too, avoiding it would have been impossible.
And one more thing.
How Chiga-nee and Niimura-senpai's mother had both come to suspect Kotaro-san as the culprit just didn't sit right with me.
The reason was that it started with him having them hunt with a nata and a mace.
From that, they realized Chiga-nee and Kana-chan were being drilled in killing techniques, apparently.
But to conclude from that that Kotaro-san was the culprit seems like a bit of a stretch, doesn't it?
In other words, I suspect that Chiga-nee and Niimura-senpai's mother were hiding something.
With Chiga-nee dead now, the only one left to ask is Niimura-senpai's mother, but...
Still, even if I asked, it's not as if anything could be done about it now.
Chiga-nee must have known something more.
She must have been hiding something.
No, maybe it wasn't only that.
If I wanted to, I could even form the hypothesis that Chiga-nee was the real culprit―
That too is nothing more than a fruitless thought now.
This is going all the way back to the start, but the moment we set foot in the Arata settlement, it was decided that things would turn out like this.
But Kogori-senpai had made the prediction that we had to go to the Arata settlement.
Something must have gone wrong somewhere.
The cause was probably Kogori-senpai's parents getting close to the Arata settlement.
If that could have been avoided, the ending might have been very different.
No... the ending would definitely have been different.
I just can't believe Kogori-senpai led us to a tragedy like this.
The murder that was about to happen in Megasawa City.
That was where it all started.
―No... the reason Kogori-senpai developed the peculiar condition called April sickness.
That was being drenched in Eiichiro Niimura-san's blood.
No... not this either...
Further back.
Something must have happened long ago to Niimura-senpai's father―Eiichiro Niimura-san.
How Kogori-senpai came to have that ability must be hidden there too.
And surely the same thing―just as with Kogori-senpai, it must have happened to Sakura-san too.
This isn't a hypothesis, it's a hunch...
And there was one more thing bothering me.
In front of Arata Station, Kotaro-san never once said anything like 'I'll kill you' or 'Die.'
What's more, he had been trying not to let Kogori-senpai get away.
Fixated on Kogori-senpai to an abnormal degree as he was, what on earth was Kotaro-san's aim?
In the end, the 'conclusion' I'd been chasing was still in the dark.
With the 'conclusion' vague, there's no way the 'setup' could come into view either.
What should I have done?
At the very least, to avoid this tragedy, something would have had to be done 10 years ago.
Even something tiny would do.
Even just the smallest thing...
Then―
"―"
What is that sound...!
Suddenly a roar loud enough to burst my eardrums echoed across the whole area.
I scrambled to my feet and went out of the secret base.
I looked all around to find the cause of this abnormal sound, but the dazzling sunlight stung my eyes, which had gotten used to the dark.
When I shaded my eyes from the sun with my hand, my surroundings came into view little by little.
"Huh?"
My eyes gradually adjusted, and the scenery leapt into view.
I couldn't believe it was real.
Because―
"The trees... are withered...?"
When I came through here a little while ago, lots of trees had been covered in fine leaves.
And in just 10 minutes or so, the leaves had fallen and the trees had withered, as if this were a wasteland.
The noisy evening cicadas and the roar from before had both vanished before I knew it, and all that sounded around me was a faint wind and my own breathing.
What's going on...? What's happening!?
I hurried, running toward the settlement.
Brown, dried-out leaves were piled up underfoot.
Crunching them loudly with every step, I ran to the settlement.
"Wha...!?"
