Fledgling Heroes

Eden no Sakura Arc [Eden's Cherry] · Shiyo 800-04-07 17:45 火 · 57 boxes

Going back to Motoki-cho was the sensible thing, as far as our safety went.
But one remark from Natsumi had changed all that.
If this keeps up, Motoki-cho is going to disappear―
Both the connection and the sheer scale of it were outlandish.
But in a situation where common sense didn't apply, there was no dismissing that possibility out of hand.
More than anything, Natsumi looked uneasy.
Mom came to pick us up at Toyotake Station in the car, and the four of us headed for the Arata settlement.
For a while the car was silent, but there was a lot we needed to talk about here.
But how to even bring it up...
It seemed as if everyone was stuck on that, and keeping their mouths shut.
"Sorry..."
The first to speak was Natsumi.
She must think it's her fault that we're headed for the Arata settlement.
But even I can weigh the pros and cons.
If we'd just gone back like that, Motoki-cho would...
Then again, if it isn't precognition, like Goto says, and 'someone' is just making her hear what it wants her to hear, then even this stuff about something happening to Motoki-cho is pretty fishy, but...
"But this has made one thing clear. The 'someone' really does exist. And it controls the cherry trees."
"Controls the cherry trees...?"
"To stoke our anxiety, it shows Kogori-senpai illusions it passes off as precognition, and steers us into coming to the Arata settlement. It seems to be a tougher enemy than I'd imagined."
"Goto, what do you mean, controlling cherry trees and showing illusions?"
"Controlling the cherry trees means exactly what it says― there is something that can make cherry trees grow or wither, entirely at will. As for why it grew a cherry tree there, it was to stop the train. On top of that, it makes Kogori-senpai recognize the destruction of Motoki-cho, and so turns our steps toward Arata."
"At this point, that thing's definitely not human..."
"That goes without saying, but it doesn't seem to be an animal either. For now, shall we call the 'someone' 'Necro'?"
"What's this Necro supposed to be..."
"In English, a sorcerer is called a necromancer. Shorten that, and you get Necro."
"Necro the sorcerer, huh... Mom, don't you know anything about this Necro thing?"
"I don't think your grandmother or Kotaro-san knows anything about it either. Necro―the first time it clearly showed that it exists was that cherry tree just now, wasn't it?"
True, everything up to now could be explained away with "it must be some clever trick."
But a cherry tree suddenly appearing was beyond anything you could call a trick.
It was exactly as Goto had predicted: nothing less than proof that something outside common sense had slipped in among us―Necro.
"Come to think of it, I believe something similar happened in Motoki-cho a long time ago. The phenomenon of a cherry tree suddenly appearing."
"S-seriously!?"
"Yes, I think it was before I was born. They say the goshinboku in Central Park appeared out of nowhere one day."
"Oh! Now that you mention it, that's right! 　Hey, Goto! Can you look it up!?"
"At this point, the chance that the goshinboku is just an urban legend has gotten pretty slim. I'll look into it in detail right away!"
Goto whipped out her phone.
She seemed to have started looking up what had happened back then.
"I've read about the goshinboku in some magazine."
"A magazine?"
"Yeah, I just happened to see a magazine article online. I think it said that decades ago, some researchers who tried to investigate the goshinboku were killed, or something."
"Found it! It's a really small article, though..."
Goto handed her phone over.
The three of us peered at the screen.
―A newspaper from March 30, Shiyo 737.
'Giant Cherry Tree Appears in Motoki!'
The headline read like something out of a gossip rag.
For all that, the article took up so little space that it was oddly comical.
A nearby resident, Keiko Kanai-san, discovered a large cherry tree growing there.
The tree was reportedly already about 20 meters tall and in full bloom.
"It must be a god come to make flowers bloom in this Japan of ours," says Kanai-san, smiling fondly.
That was all there was to the article.
"Hey, Mom's maiden name was Kanai, wasn't it?"
"That Keiko Kanai was my grandmother."
"Seriously... what a crazy coincidence. So she'd be my great-grandma..."
"Starting with this year, Shiyo 737, Motoki-cho's population began to grow. Gradually Motoki-cho became a famous cherry spot known nationwide, and from Shiyo 790 on, more and more of the newly rich bought up land in Motoki-cho, and the population grew even further."
"I see, so that's the truth behind how it became a famous cherry spot."
"So that means what I sensed, 'Motoki-cho will disappear,' ties in too, right?"
"Yes. We can't overlook what they have in common: a big cherry tree suddenly growing. But is that really the only thing connecting Motoki-cho and the Arata settlement? I think it's likely Necro grew the goshinboku in Motoki-cho for some reason."
"Mom, do you know anything about that?"
"Nothing really comes to mind. As far as I know, Eiichiro-san moving to Motoki-cho was the first connection. Before that, the two places should have had nothing whatsoever to do with each other."
It was when Dad came to Motoki-cho that the two places were first connected.
And Dad came to Motoki-cho when he was starting high school...
But the goshinboku was there before that, so going by the timeline, Dad wasn't connected after all.
A chain of cause and effect seemed to be starting to show itself, faintly, yet it never quite came into view.
Part of me felt it would sit better to write it all off as coincidence.
But a cherry tree suddenly sprouting up was unthinkable under normal circumstances.
That very thing had happened just now, and it had also happened in Motoki-cho more than 60 years ago.
And Natsumi's intuition: 'the destruction of Motoki-cho.'
No, I'll think positive.
We may still be a long way from the truth, but we can't be headed in the wrong direction.
"Goto, what do you think?"
Goto had been looking down for a while now, apparently lost in thought.
She's probably tying every factor together as she thinks it through.
"Obasan, do you know what variety the goshinboku in Motoki-cho is?"
"The variety? I've never heard anything that specific."
"I see... Actually, I've never heard either. It looks like a Somei-yoshino cherry, but I'd been thinking it was a bit too big for one..."
With that, Goto started looking something up on her phone again.
"You're looking up the goshinboku's variety?"
"Yes. If it's a cherry tree that just suddenly grows, I think it must be a special variety. No scholar would ever leave a cherry tree like that alone."
True, if a cherry tree were that bizarre, botanists would be falling over each other to study it.
In which case, maybe what that cherry tree really is is already known, and how it suddenly grows has been figured out too...
"Huh... 　That's weird..."
Goto, who'd been searching, was tilting her head and glaring at her phone.
"What's up?"
"When I try to look up the goshinboku, it seems an urban legend from Motoki-cho is involved somehow."
"Could that be... Motokizakura-sama?"
"Kogori-senpai, you know about it?"
"It's actually a pretty famous urban legend in Motoki-cho. 　A yokai, a woman in a kimono, who gives whoever meets her either happiness or misfortune."
"Yeah, the usual kind of story."
"We may not be able to laugh that urban legend off. 　This urban legend of Motokizakura-sama seems to have been around for quite a long time."
"It seems that story's been around since my parents' generation, at least."
"So it's an urban legend that old... So what's that got to do with the variety?"
"This is... a pretty nasty story... Are you sure you want to hear it?"
"Don't tell me it's actually a ghost story or something...?"
"Yes, apparently something ghost-story-like really happened. It reads like a more detailed account of Kogori-senpai's story from earlier―the researchers being slaughtered."
"In late April of Shiyo 747, an official team of five researchers was formed to investigate the goshinboku. They apparently started with a field survey of the cherry tree. But then―"
"A few days after arriving in Motoki-cho, all five members of the research team apparently died suddenly at their lodgings. The cause of death: crushed brains from blows to the head. In other words, they were beaten over the head and died."
"What's that supposed to mean... That's a full-on ghost story..."
"The truly frightening part comes next. Due to pressure from somewhere, the research on the goshinboku itself apparently fell through. An official research team, no less. In other words, this is the same as saying the government halted the research."
"W-wait a minute! You mean...!"
"At the very least, the government knows what the goshinboku really is. It's top secret of top secrets, something the general public must never know."
"No, wait, wait. But that's weird too, isn't it? 　Why would a government secret be up on the net? That's got to be another urban legend, right?"
"Most likely, the murder of the research team got swapped out for the urban legend of Motokizakura-sama. Back then the media were newspapers, TV, and radio, so manipulating the public must have been easy."
"Besides, all it says here is that 'the research was halted on orders from somewhere.' Pressure from the Japanese government is just my guess. By painting over the incident with an urban legend, they planted it in people's memories as 'a fishy-sounding case,' and let the incident fade from memory."
"So basically, we never find out the variety after all..."
"Yes. In this country, even knowing the variety seems to be taboo..."
"Damn! Before we knew it, we'd gotten dragged into something unbelievable..."
"But it means someone has known what that goshinboku really is since at least before Shiyo 737. They halted the research before it had gotten anywhere in Motoki-cho, and even went so far as to manipulate the public."
"Most likely, records that reveal what it really is already existed somewhere before Shiyo 737. Considering the period, probably as paper records."
"Paper records... so, books... Wait a second! That means...!"
Records that had existed since before Shiyo 737.
Those had to be paper records―books, without a doubt.
And there was someone who'd been looking into exactly that.
And that person was missing now...
"Chiga-nee... so she ended up reaching the truth...?"
"Those who try to learn the truth get erased..."
"I'm scared..."
Just then, the car suddenly slammed on its brakes.
"Mom, what's wrong?"
"No, I can't do this. Arata is too dangerous. We're going home."
"W-wait a minute! If this keeps up, Motoki-cho's in trouble, right!?"
"Haruka matters more to me than Motoki-cho!"
"What are you talking about! Are you gonna abandon Motoki-cho and the Arata settlement and Chiga-nee!?"
"That's right! We can live anywhere!"
"Then... Then what happens to Natsumi!? 　She has precognition because Necro already has its hooks in her, right!? 　Is there any guarantee Natsumi will be safe if we just go home like this!?"
"That's..."
"Chiga-nee told me everything! In Megasawa, Natsumi saved us, didn't she!? 　And you're gonna abandon Natsumi after that!?"
"Haruka, never mind me. You two go back to Motoki-cho―"
"You keep your mouth shut!"
Just then, it was Mom's phone that rang.
"Akane's calling...? 　Hello?"
It seemed to be from Natsumi's mom.
She must have heard that whole exchange through the bugging app.
They had to be talking about what to do to help Natsumi.
"Yeah... I see... But then...! 　............"
The conversation didn't seem to be getting anywhere.
I took the hand of Natsumi, who sat with her head down, trembling.
"Natsumi, don't worry. I'm right here with you."
At that, Natsumi gave a weak nod.
Natsumi had said she'd go to Arata alone, but she must have been scared after all.
"Phew..."
Mom hung up and let out a breath.
What on earth had they talked about?
"I'll ask just to be sure. Are you really going to Arata?"
"That's..."
"I'm definitely going! 　There's something only I can do, so I'm definitely going!"
"Then I'm going too! 　Besides, I promised Chiga-nee we'd meet in Arata!"
"A-as if I could go home and leave these guys behind!"
That meant all three of us had flatly rejected Mom's proposal.
Hearing that, Mom heaved another big sigh.
"Akane and Ryoji-san were really torn, it seems. As things are, Natsumi-chan's safety can't be guaranteed. Maybe it'd be better, instead, to trust everyone and see it through to the end, they said. Also, Akane told me this. 'Isn't it about time you let go of your girl and let her travel?' she said. So we'll leave it to you to decide. You're really ready for this?"
The three of us looked at one another.
Without hesitation, we nodded to each other.
Natsumi, timid and yet somehow with nerves of steel.
Goto, a cheeky little shrimp and yet more loyal than anyone.
I'm not thinking anything grand, like wanting to save Motoki-cho or Arata.
It's just that I like these two, so I can't leave them be.
So I'm going too.
"Mom! Take us to Arata! And we go home on schedule―April 9!"
"Haruka... That's my girl! Mine and Eiichiro-san's, through and through!"
Mom stomped down hard on the accelerator, and the car lurched off, swaying from side to side.
"H-hey, Mom! Calm down!"
"I'm still plenty young, you know! As if I'd lose to you three!"
With us aboard, the car sped nimbly along the mountain road.
That's right. We're not going in order to take risks, but to protect ourselves.
No risk, high return―we can't afford to be that greedy anymore.
Chiga-nee, hang on. I swear I'll find you.
And... just you wait, Necro...
I'll expose what you are and save everyone!
