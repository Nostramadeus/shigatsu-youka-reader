Suspect's Scream 2

Jusatsu Arc [Killing by Curse] · Shiyo 800-04-08 20:20 水 · 37 boxes

How many years had it been since I'd ridden a bike standing on the pedals with this much force?
If Senpai really was heading to the police station, then if I went to the police station first and headed from there to Senpai's house, I was sure to meet her.
As before, there was no wind, and the moonlight shone brilliantly on the rows of cherry trees.
I reached the police station in 10 minutes. I must have gotten there before Senpai.
From there I turned toward Senpai's house and rode on, careful not to miss her on the way.
In the distance, the lights of a convenience store came into view.
Senpai would definitely come along this route.
I'll lie in wait in front of this convenience store.
"Oh..."
A figure sitting on the bench in front of the convenience store.
It was a girl with short hair.
There's no mistaking her!
"Senpai!"
I tossed my bike aside and ran up to Senpai.
"Goto...?"
I hugged Senpai's head as she looked at me in surprise.
No words came out at all.
"Haha... What'd you come here for... It's too early for visiting hours, you know?"
Senpai's feeble voice―hearing that voice, I pulled away from Senpai.
Senpai, her face a mess and swollen from crying, wasn't even wearing a jacket.
I took off the jacket I had on and draped it over Senpai's shoulders.
"Let's go home, Senpai. It's all right."
"Go home where? Even my mom wouldn't want to see the face of a murderer, even her daughter's."
"Then, let's go home to my house."
I suggested it without taking my eyes off Senpai's face, but...
"Are you an idiot? You might get killed by me, you know?"
"But..."
"............"
It didn't look like we could keep up a proper conversation any longer.
I slowly moved away from Senpai and went into the convenience store alone.
I bought two warm drinks and went back to the bench.
"Please drink this and calm down. It's my thanks for lunch."
I grinned from ear to ear at Senpai.
Senpai, staring blankly at my face, took the piping-hot bottle.
I sat down beside Senpai and warmed my hands, chilled from the bike ride, on my bottle.
"Why would Senpai be a murderer?"
"............"
From Senpai's mouth, a big white breath came out.
It seemed she still hadn't calmed down.
"Why didn't you go to the police?"
I tried changing the question. Would she answer anything?
"I was thinking about how to tell the police. And besides, she's still at the police station, right? Natsumi is. I'm not supposed to go near Natsumi."
Was she trying to say that on April 8, Niimura-senpai transforms?
I took the bottle Senpai was holding and opened the cap for her.
"Sorry..."
With that, Senpai put her lips to the bottle.
"Hot!"
Senpai let out a small shriek.
"I did pick the one that looked the hottest."
I gave her a small smile.
"............"
Senpai hung her head again. She just wouldn't start talking.
"Goto, go home by yourself. You'll catch a cold."
"I'm not leaving Senpai's side until morning."
"Are you an idiot..."
Senpai lifted her face, just a little.
"I'm not an idiot! I'm the genius girl, as everyone agrees, me included!"
"Pfft, are you an idiot..."
Senpai smiled for just an instant.
Matching her, I put on a smile, too.
Not a grin from ear to ear; just enough to lift the corners of my mouth.
"Really, Senpai, let's go!"
I stood up and gave Senpai's arm a hard tug.
"Idiot, what're you doing!"
"Really, you call me idiot, idiot, way too much! I'm the genius girl, I said!"
"Stop it! Are you an idiot!?"
Senpai was resisting desperately.
Not about to lose, I pulled on her arm, too.
"What do you mean, murderer! Does a dithering coward like this have the guts to kill someone!?"
Senpai's butt came up off the seat, and her shoes skidded with a scrape.
"Idiot! You don't know anything!"
When Senpai put even more strength into it, her lifted butt landed on the bench again.
"I don't know anything, that's why I'm telling you to talk to me!"
I didn't give up, and kept pulling on her arm.
"What are you gonna do once you know something like that!?"
"What do you mean, what? You want to save Kogori-senpai, don't you!? You need my help, don't you!?"
"But...! Even if I rely on you...
that's..."
Suddenly, Senpai's resistance stopped.
Matching her, I eased off, too.
"But... I can't go to your house. There's nobody home, right?"
"There is. Right when I was leaving, my sis came home, so."
"............"
Now, I had no choice but to stick with the lie.
But, this is a forgivable lie. A lie not for my own sake, but for Senpai's.
"Come on, Senpai."
I tugged on Senpai's arm once more.
She showed no sign of resisting anymore.
"Goto... You, aren't you scared of me? I... Natsumi's dad..."
"Niimura-senpai, do you want to kill me?"
"N-no! I... I don't want to kill anyone...!"
"Then I'm not scared. Why should I have to be scared of Senpai? 　April 8, is it a day when Senpai, like a werewolf, transforms?"
"That's... not it, but..."
The big moon floating in the sky―today, Shiyo 800, April 8, was a full moon.
Somewhere in a Western country, a werewolf might be out, but here in Motoki-cho, nothing like that was happening.
We each got on our own bikes.
"Senpai, please go first."
"Why..."
"Because I have to keep watch so you don't run away."
"............"
Niimura-senpai started off on her bike without a word.
