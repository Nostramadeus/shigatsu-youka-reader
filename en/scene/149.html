Profile of the Culprit

Jusatsu Arc [Killing by Curse] · Shiyo 800-04-08 18:00 水 · 40 boxes

The sun had sunk quite low.
The crows that had surrounded the police station had vanished, every last one.
"Goto, how did you get all the way here?"
"I got a ride from the police officer who was with me."
"Then, Goto, you've got no choice but to walk too, huh..."
"You kids, I hear you're going home already?"
From inside the police station, Ise-san appeared.
"Yes, we thought we'd hear Niimura-senpai's story at Zahha."
Goto answered Ise-san in a friendly way.
Could it be that Ise-san was the police officer who got mistaken for a suspicious person at Zahha?
"In that case, I'll give you a ride in my car. None of you three have wheels, right?"
At a time like this, if it were the usual Goto, she'd say, "Ooh, just what I'd expect from Ojisan! A dependable police officer, indeed!" but,
"Sorry to trouble you."
she answered, politely, with a bow.
Goto sat in the passenger seat, and Haruka and I sat in the back seat.
As before, Haruka remained sullen.
"Going to Zahha, was that Niimura-san's suggestion?"
"Yes. She said somewhere people would see us as much as possible would be good."
"I get it... That's good."
So Ise-san is in favor of this idea too...
"Niimura-san's story is better heard by her friends than by us police. And also... Niimura-san's story is something you must never make fun of."
"Huh...?"
Haruka spoke up for the first time in the car.
"Why? At the questioning earlier, they said it was ridiculous..."
"Well, as long as it's a questioning, I have to move the investigation forward as a police officer. Besides, there were several others there besides me. I kept my own thoughts out of it as much as I could."
"So, here I'll tell you my own personal thoughts. Not a police officer's, but my own, personally."
Our car was moving slowly.
"According to Niimura-san, a 'shinigami' called up by a 'curse' kills the target it's aimed at, it seems."
"A curse...?"
"A curse...?"
"The truth is, 10 years ago, 6 years ago, and 3 years ago, murders happened in the town where Niimura-san lived at the time. Every time, on April 8, two people at a time, six in all, were killed. By the 'curse' Niimura-san talks about. To curse someone, you write the target's name on a paper doll, cast a little spell, and it's done. If I say the Megasawa City case, you'll understand."
"The victims were all people connected to the school Niimura-san attended in Megasawa City. Among them were children and students who had bullied Niimura-san."
Haruka was bullied? From her letters, I couldn't pick up on anything like that at all...
"And today is April 8, too. If it's the 'curse's' doing, it wouldn't be strange for a murder to happen today around Niimura-san."
"What do you mean, April 8? Is there something special about it?"
"Oh, right... Kogori-san, you don't know, do you? That today is the anniversary of Eiichiro Niimura-san's death."
The anniversary of Haruka's dad's death?
"Eiichiro Niimura-san died 12 years ago. He fell to his death trying to get the balloon you let fly."
"I-I did...!?"
Goto and Haruka stayed silent.
That meant both of them had known about it.
"Sorry, Haruka..."
"Don't apologize. That was an accident. Natsumi, there's nothing you need to worry about..."
12 years ago means I was 4.
I don't mean to defend myself, but maybe it can't be helped that I didn't remember...
Huh...?
That's strange...
That would mean both Dad and Mom have been hiding it all this time.
Maybe it's out of concern for my mental health, but there's no need to hide even the fact that he died, for 12 years...
"Earlier, I said 10 years ago, 6 years ago, 3 years ago, but there's a pattern to it. Can you tell what it is?"
Odd numbers? 　Every 3 years?
No, between 6 years and 10 years there's a gap of 4 years.
What could it be?
"They're the memorial anniversaries of Eiichiro Niimura-san."
Memorial anniversaries? [TN: Buddhist memorials count the year of death as the first year.]
In other words, the third-year memorial, the seventh-year memorial... Um, a tenth-year memorial?
Was there even such a thing as a tenth-year memorial?
"Eiichiro Niimura-san's family home is in the Arata district of Iizawa Prefecture―located in the northwest, within that same Iizawa Prefecture. It's commonly called the Arata settlement, a place deep in the mountains with only a small population. That place... how do I put it, has lots of really odd customs. The Arata settlement apparently has what Shinto calls a tenth-year rite―in other words, a tenth-year memorial. So if you follow this pattern, the murders were carried out in line with Eiichiro Niimura-san's memorial services."
"However, Mifuyu-san, who is Niimura-san's mom, and also Haruka-san, attended every one of the memorial services. That's why their alibis are perfect. The police confirmed the whereabouts of the two of them at the time too, and apparently there's no mistake."
"Niimura-san, there's one thing I want to ask. Why didn't you attend today's thirteenth-year memorial service?" [TN: A Buddhist memorial held 12 years after a death.]
Come to think of it, today it's been 12 years since he died. That is, the thirteenth-year memorial.
If the memorial services had always been held precisely on April 8 every time until now, then this time too it should be held without shifting it to the weekend.
"It's because I was feeling unwell. I got food poisoning the day before yesterday, so to be safe, it was decided that only Mom would attend. And also, I thought that if I went to the memorial service, someone might die from the curse again..."
"And today, at least..."
said Haruka, looking down and putting force into her tone.
"Today, at least, I absolutely don't want anyone to die! That curse must never, ever succeed!"
Haruka ground her teeth with a creak.
"So that means you made paper dolls this time too?"
"Yeah, looks like I made them without realizing, just like before... Natsumi's dad, and her mom... and... Natsumi's, too."
"Oh, but Kogori-san, don't worry. As she said, Niimura-san has no awareness of having made the paper dolls. Someone is using this so-called 'curse' to throw the case into confusion. When she woke up this morning, those paper dolls were on Niimura-san's desk, apparently. From just that alone, Niimura-san thinks she's the culprit."
"Is that true? Haruka?"
After a short pause, Haruka nodded silently.
"Well, that's all I know. Niimura-san knows about this too, and what happened 12 years ago, I told Goto-chan earlier at Zahha as well. Niimura-san, is there anything more you want to say?"
Haruka shook her head, still looking down.
Ise-san stopped the car at a nearby convenience store, and then, opening the car window, lit a cigarette.
"I know what Goto-chan and Kogori-san are thinking right now. That the one who caused this whole series of incidents was Niimura-san's mom... Mifuyu Niimura, isn't that it?"
That's exactly right. Even Haruka herself might be thinking it could be.
"But I don't think so. I don't know how they did it, but somebody put those paper dolls there somehow. Somebody trying to pin the blame on the Niimura family."
"Hey, Haruka, did you go out last night?"
"No, I was home the whole time. But, Mom works late at night too, so she's not home."
I see, then the culprit could sneak into the house while Haruka was asleep and leave the paper dolls.
But, to think that Haruka's mom works into the middle of the night...
She always acts cheerful and bright, but because of me, she's been going through tremendous hardship without anyone knowing.
"Haruka... I'm sorry. Because of me, your dad..."
"Like I said, why are you apologizing! It was an accident when we were kids! And it was 12 years ago... Dad was just a klutz, that's all!"
"But, you kept it hidden from me all this time, didn't you? It must have been hard to say... That he died because of me..."
Now, I understand painfully well what it feels like to lose a parent.
Haruka had kept all that suppressed the whole time, and still called me her best friend.
"Why are you crying! Are you an idiot? We've cried way too much today!"
Haruka hugged me tight.
Unable to hold back, I hugged her back.
"Senpaaai! I'm so glad! I knew it, the bond between you two is stronger than anything!"
For some reason, Goto started crying too.
"Hey now, give me a break... If you cry, people will think I'm a suspicious person..."
With that, Ise-san closed the window and set the car moving.
We were almost at Zahha.
"Considering the past incidents, the Niimura family would not be the ones to bring about today's murder. Most likely, it was set up by the real culprit―the 'shinigami.' So... so, Niimura-san... Don't agonize anymore. Right beside you, there's a precious best friend who's supporting you, isn't there?"
Hearing Ise-san's words, Haruka and I looked at each other.
And then, without even wiping away our tears, we smiled at each other.
"Waaah! I'm so glad!"
"Is the Motoki Pretty Girl Appreciation Society's No. 1 in popularity really this noisy... Maybe I should quit the society..."
When we arrived at Zahha, we got out of the car with our faces bright.
"See you! Get along, now!"
"Thank you very much!"
"Thank you, Ise-san!"
"Ojisan, thanks for everything!"
"But, just to be safe. Don't let your guard down until April 8 is over. The 'shinigami' might still be targeting you. It's certain that somebody placed those paper dolls."
"Also, the fact that she came to the police today, I haven't told Niimura-san's mom. Niimura-san came to the police station on her own and talked on her own. That's all it was, so there's no need to worry about reading her mom's face."
Ise-san went back to his car and drove away.
"Hey, Natsumi..."
"Hm?"
Haruka kept staring at Ise-san's car.
"Ise-san is... so cool!"
"Huh!? 　Ah... ahahaha... you're right..."
"But, Senpai? 　That man is a hardcore lolicon, you know?"
Today, Haruka had been frightened of the curse, and had shed tears out of self-reproach toward us.
But the Haruka of this moment, to a degree surpassing all of that, showed the most tragic expression of the day.
Read the room, Goto.
