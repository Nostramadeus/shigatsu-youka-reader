Suspect's Scream 1

Jusatsu Arc [Killing by Curse] · Shiyo 800-04-08 20:00 水 · 41 boxes

Okay, I'm calling Niimura-senpai! I don't care if it comes out that I lied.
There's no more time to hesitate.
The index finger of my right hand wouldn't move toward the call button.
In my heart I shouted "Hyah," and pressed my index finger onto the button with more force than necessary.
3 seconds... 5 seconds... 10 seconds...
"The person you are calling is unavailable. We apologize, but..."
As if I'd been waiting for those words, I hung up right away.
I hadn't been able to say what I had to say, and yet, this relief.
My loud heartbeat was slowly settling down.
As if to shatter that relief, the phone rang shrilly.
“Niimura-senpai” was displayed on the incoming-call screen.
What do I do... Should I answer, or should I calm myself down a little first?
I stopped thinking and pressed the call button.
"Hi-iii, it's Goto!"
I answered in a voice more cheerful than necessary.
Whether it sounded forced or not, I don't know.
"Oh, Goto. What's up? Find out anything?"
Niimura-senpai's voice was the same as always.
"No, it's not exactly that. But, being alone, I'm scared after all..."
Oh―!
The instant I said that, I thought, oh no.
I had just told her I was alone right now.
"Huh... So Goto, you're home alone right now. You did say before that your parents had a class reunion. I'm alone right now, too. Mom's coming home late today."
The way she put it snagged on me strangely, but Senpai's voice was no different in particular.
"Aren't your parents worried? On a day when an incident like that happened, their daughter alone at home?"
"Oh... no, I called them just now, so..."
"Hmm. Well, make sure you lock up tight."
"Yes... but it's scary after all, isn't it, when something like this happens so close by."
"Yeah, actually, I was wondering whether to call you, Goto, too. And then I got a call from you, so. Natsumi I'm not, but being alone does make me feel uneasy after all."
Was I fated to talk with Niimura-senpai either way?
No, whether I call her or she calls me should make a big difference to how what we say from here comes across.
"Hey, Goto."
Niimura-senpai's tone of voice dropped a little.
"Can I ask you one weird question?"
"What is it...?"
"Why... did you call me?"
"Huh?"
"Feeling uneasy alone, that's not like you, is it? 　Even though earlier you were so fired up, saying you wanted to solve the incident."
"Ah... ah... do you think so..."
"............ Haha... Ha..."
"Niimura-senpai... did something happen?"
"Something? 　Something, like... what?"
"Ah... um... uh..."
"What's with you? 　Could it be, you're scared of me?"
"............"
"You being this scared means... could it be, you found something out?"
"............"
"What, no answer at all?"
"Se... Se..."
"Hmm?"
"Uh... I..."
"Goto..."
"............"
"Sorry..."
"Huh?"
"N-no, sorry! For once you were scared, so I thought I'd tease you, but... Doing that at a time like this was too insensitive, I guess! Ahahaha!"
Niimura-senpai suddenly pitched her voice higher and said it brightly.
"It's nothing! Nothing... Don't worry about it, okay..."
But, my goosebumps just wouldn't go away.
"Sorry! Really, sorry, Goto! I'm gonna hang up. The mood got weird somehow. Tomorrow, let's go see how Natsumi's doing! Good night!"
With that, Senpai hung up on me.
The beep-beep sound from the phone went on for a while.
I slowly put the phone down on the bed.
In the end, I couldn't ask what I wanted to ask.
Even so, Senpai seemed somehow strange―or so I felt...
Had she really just meant to tease me?
Or else...
Suddenly, a short ringtone sounded.
I was a little startled, but it seemed a message had arrived.
............? 　It was from Niimura-senpai.
Why a message right after the call?
I opened the message.
Written there was this.


　Goto, help me
What does this mean? Senpai is asking for help?
Could it be she's trying to tease me again?
No, that's exactly what made the mood so strange just now, so it would be strange for her to go out of her way to press the attack.
In other words, my guess is that this is what Niimura-senpai truly feels.
“What's the matter? Are you all right?”
I replied, pretending I didn't know anything.
I had no idea at all what she intended.
Maybe I was fundamentally misunderstanding something.
But, from the other side of this electronic screen, I could hear an anguished cry.
Before long, a reply came.
“At this rate, I'm going to end up killing Natsumi. Help me. Help Natsumi. I don't want to kill Natsumi. Help me.”
Niimura-senpai kills Kogori-senpai?
I'd suddenly been told something that partly matched my hypothesis.
But...
“Senpai, please calm down. It's all right.”
“Goto, knowing you, you've already figured it out, right? About my dad.”
Really, that man was Niimura-senpai's father...?
Somehow, all the strength suddenly drained out of me. She had confessed the very thing I'd been trying to ask.
With this, you could say Senpai is as good as guilty, but why confess?
If I contact the police and have them come and protect me, my safety is secured.
So if Senpai is the culprit, she has no need to say something like this.
Something doesn't add up.
“I'm sorry. I lied. I looked into Senpai's father.”
I answered honestly, too.
“I figured you'd do that, Goto. That's why I wanted to talk to you.”
“But, why by message?”
“It's pathetic, but right now I'm crying so hard I can't talk. It doesn't mean anything more than that.”
“It's all right, so please take a deep breath. I'm on your side.”
“Because I,

killed Natsumi's dad
Offhandedly, Senpai had said something enormous.
But, when she confessed so head-on like this, the factors that deny it came to mind one after another.
I can tell. Senpai didn't kill anyone.
At the very least, you'd need a car, and you'd have to move the body.
To begin with, skinning someone alive must take considerable skill.
A crime on that scale, there's no way a high-school girl could do it.
“Senpai, calm down. It's all right.”
“I'm going to turn myself in.”
“Wait! You've got it wrong! Senpai!”
“But probably, Natsumi can't be saved anymore.”
This is making no sense at all anymore.
“Senpai, it's hard, isn't it? It hurts, doesn't it? Talk to me. I'll listen to all of it. I'm on your side. Kogori-senpai's on your side too.”
―After that, no more replies came.
Don't tell me she really went to turn herself in?
Niimura-senpai, don't do anything rash...! Niimura-senpai being the culprit is logically impossible!
I hurriedly put on a jacket, rushed out of the house, and got on my bike.
