The Maidens' Holy War ~Apparition~

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-07 19:20 火 · 55 boxes

"Thank you for the meal."
Hands pressed together, Goto and I gave a proper after-meal thanks.
"Come to think of it, is Obaasan still not here?"
In the end, even after dinner was over, Obaasan never showed up.
Obasan had gone to wash the dishes, and Kotaro-san and Chiga-nee were drunk and asleep in the living room.
Then the sliding door of the living room opened.
"The genius girl, as everyone agrees, me included, Erina Goto! Has arrived at the Arata settlement!"
On the other side of the sliding door stood an old woman in a school uniform, with twin tails.
Who is this supposed to be...?
No way...
"How about it, Kogori-senpai! Today's food was delicious, wasn't it! 　Which one, which one did you like best?"
"Oh, uh, um..."
Obaasan dressed up as Goto was painful on a criminal level.
This needed self-censorship.
This far gone, it wasn't an impression, or even cosplay anymore. It was a costume-party act.
Still, her gestures and way of talking were spot on.
"I sent Grandma a video of Goto last week, so she probably practiced with that..."
To copy her this well from a video sent last week, she must have practiced a lot.
"Niimura-senpai, what do you think? My twin tails, seeing them once in a while, don't you think they're pretty nice? 　I heard you were all coming today, so I've been planning my operation the whole time!"
With that, Obaasan spun around once.
Had she really gotten a uniform and a wig ready just for this?
Goto was knitting her brow as if she were looking at weird food.
"Wrong!"
At the sudden shout, the room went silent.
The voice belonged to Chiga-nee, who had sat up.
"Goto-chan isn't like this! 　She's quieter... and lovely... she doesn't talk in such a vulgar way!"
Chiga-nee was trembling all over.
Just then, Goto's mouth twisted into a smirk.
Goto took hair ties from her pocket, quickly put her hair in twin tails, and stood next to Obaasan.
"Here before us is none other than Erika Goto! 　Oh-ho? Don't tell me you're a fan of the genius girl, as everyone agrees, me included, Erina Goto?"
Goto grinned at Obaasan.
Come to think of it, the two names were just one letter apart.
Obaasan was Erika Niimura, if I remembered right.
"Why, it's Motoki-cho's genius girl, Erina Goto! 　I see, so you're afraid this Erika Goto will steal your title of genius girl, and that's why you're competing with me?"
Obaasan grinned at Goto.
"As if the one and only original genius girl could have her throne threatened by a single fan! 　If you like, shall we have a showdown?"
"Shall we have a showdown?"
Kana-chan seemed to be trying her hand at a Goto impression too.
"Oho, challenging this genius girl Erika Goto to a showdown, you've got quite the nerve. Very well! A showdown it is!"
What on earth is this turn of events...
"Wrong... Goto-chan isn't like this...!"
Chiga-nee, faced with reality, was on the verge of losing her reason.
Though she was the type to act on instinct to begin with.
"Goto-chan isn't this vulgar! 　She isn't some cheeky kid who says arrogant things like that and acts all high and mighty!"
At these words, Goto seemed a little miffed.
The everyday Goto was exactly like this, so Chiga-nee's words amounted to rejecting her whole personality.
"What's that, Chiga-nee! Didn't the men you've met so far think the same thing about Chiga-nee!? 　All looks and nothing else, that's Chiga-nee, so I bet the men got disillusioned, didn't they?"
"Got disillusioned, didn't they?"
"Wha...! What's with you, shorty! 　Talking like that to a grown-up 20-year-old!"
"Oh my, you're 20? 　You sure have an old-looking face."
"An old-looking face."
"You said the thing I worry about most...!"
"Besides, I could tell when you hugged me earlier. That big, puffed-up chest of Chiga-nee's is 97 percent padding, isn't it!?"
"Padding, isn't it!?"
"Ngh...! You said the thing I worry about second most...!"
It looked like Chiga-nee was losing.
In this kind of showdown, Goto was strong through and through.
But badmouthing someone's chest didn't sit right with me.
That kind of worry, I understand it painfully well.
"Honestly, it's so unsightly... The old face can't be helped, I suppose, but she should just live proudly with her chest as it is. Even Kogori-senpai has accepted that about herself and lives proudly!"
"Lives proudly!"
Hey, Goto, what did you just say?
"Goto, I can't let that remark pass... Why does this turn into talk about my chest? 　A shrimpy nitwit numbskull like you is nowhere near ready to defy me!"
Sharing the same worry: Chiga-nee. I had no choice but to take her side.
"H-Hey, you guys..."
"Goto, I'm siding with Chiga-nee."
I stood next to Chiga-nee.
"Oh, Natsumi-chan, you're a girl who understands. That's right! As if I'd lose to this vulgar little shrimp and that cosplay hag!"
For some reason, Chiga-nee was burning with rivalry against Obaasan too.
Probably a case of: hate the monk, and you hate his robes too.
"Niimura-senpai. Senpai, you're on my side, right?"
"On my side, right?"
"Huh!? This has nothing to do with me, right!?"
Haruka seemed to be trying to flee this battlefield.
Goto, don't you let her get away.
"Mifuyu-saaan! Get over here this instant! Mifuyu-san, you're on my team."
"U-Um..."
Timidly emerging from the kitchen: Obasan.
Judging by Obasan's manner, she had heard the conversation.
"Mifuyu-san, you know what we're about to do, don't you?"
"Y... yes..."
The old woman in a uniform and twin tails wasn't even doing an impression. She was talking down to her.
"All right, I'm going to go sleep in my room."
Though he was supposed to be asleep, Kotaro-san suddenly got up and left.
That fatso ran away!
Scurrying off like a wharf roach, only at times like this...!
Hmph, fine. This battlefield is a women-only battle stage.
"All right, everyone, sit down. It's showdown time!"
We gathered around the cleared table.
Me and Chiga-nee, Goto and Haruka, Obaasan and Obasan.
Apparently we'd be having a battle royale in these pairs.
"Ooh, a showdown, it's been forever! Who's gonna win?!"
Kana-chan seemed set on being just a spectator.
But what kind of showdown would it be?
"The showdown we're about to have was passed down by Westerners who reached the Arata settlement long ago. A showdown the Arata way, based on a time-honored tradition."
I didn't know what kind of showdown it was, but if it meant crushing Goto here, I'd take on anything!
"Here's the showdown!"
Obaasan brought something down on the table with a slam.

These are... paper dolls...?
Six paper dolls were lined up on the table.
"Some of you are new today, so let me explain the rules. First, write your name on this doll."
Each of us was handed a paper doll and a pen.
Writing my own name on a paper doll felt a little creepy.
Obasan and Haruka looked pale.
Did they have some bad memory involving paper dolls?
"Looks like everyone's done. Okay, hand them over."
Obaasan gathered everyone's paper dolls and stacked them in one pile.
"Next, I'll draw one paper doll from these, like drawing lots. Whoever's name is on the doll goes first. Mifuyu-san, the lights."
"Yes."
On Obaasan's instructions, Obasan turned off the light in the room.
With no streetlights outside, it was truly pitch-black, but after a few seconds, moonlight began to filter in faintly.
At the same time, an orange glow fell softly on Obaasan's face.
She seemed to have lit a candle.
"What we'll be doing is... ghost stories."
Ghost stories?
So, a scary-story contest?
"When one person's story is over, I draw the next paper doll. That's how you'll take turns telling ghost stories. At the end, you vote with your own paper doll for whoever was scariest. The team with the most votes wins. Of course, voting for your own team is against the rules."
I see, so all I had to do was tell a scary story.
The usual me would be bad with this kind of story, but now that the demon of fury had possessed me, nothing could scare me.
"But there's a little jinx to this showdown... They say the story you vote for will befall you someday. Especially around a memorial service..."
"Don't say scary stuff like that..."
Only a candle lit the room, and paper dolls with names written on them.
The atmosphere was perfect.
Now then... on this night before the memorial service, who would tell the scariest story...?
