Goto picked the streets where we were least likely to be seen.
Goto walked the whole way with us, but my legs had already turned to lead.
"Welcome to the Goto home! Come to think of it, Niimura-senpai, this is your first time here, isn't it?"
"Ooh, so this is Goto's place! Yeah, it's my first time."
"Make sure you carry your shoes up with you, okay? You never know who might come by. Come on in, come on in!" Goto's room was on the second floor, and we went up.
"Huh, so this is Goto's room. You keep it pretty neat."
"Senpai, you two must be hungry after all that walking, right? It's a little early, but should we have dinner?"
The pizza we'd had for lunch was long gone from my stomach.
Dinner sounds good too, but we walked a lot, so if I can, I'd like a bath.
"But you walked quite a bit, so maybe a bath first would be better?"
Goto, such a good junior...
"Hey, hey, will it be dinner first? A bath? 　O・ r・ may・ be―"
"Okay, Goto, that's enough! 　Natsumi, which do you want?"
"A bath first would be nice. I'm really sweaty..."
"Then I'll go get it ready, so please wait here!"
Goto said that and left the room with a light step.
"Goto is so reliable, isn't she?"
But Haruka's face had clouded over somewhat.
"Natsumi, you must think it's weird after all... Me saying I don't want to go home and stuff..."
"Mm, well... But if you'll tell me tomorrow, that's fine. Dad and Mom will probably be mad at me, though..."
"Yeah... I'm sure I'll get chewed out too. I'm probably making them worry..."
"Natsumi... today, I really need you to not leave my side."
"............? 　Of course! That's why we came here, isn't it?"
"No, that's not what I mean... I want you to not go anywhere I can't see you."
"Natsumi, I...!"
"The bath is being drawn! Please wait 30 minutes!"
"Oh, uh, Goto, good work!"
Goto was holding two bottles of tea, a Super Soda Bomber, and a towel.
A towel? For wiping off sweat, maybe?
"Sorry about that! I hadn't even served you any tea. Niimura-senpai, this one's yours, right?"
Goto handed Haruka the Super Soda Bomber gently.
"Way to go, Goto! You're so thoughtful!"
However,
"Hey, Goto... you didn't shake this, did you?"
"Huh?"
Haruka was apparently still bothered by the soda that had sprayed out earlier.
It seemed Haruka had been holding a bit of a grudge.
"N-no, it's nothing! I'll gladly take it!"
With that, Haruka opened the lid.
The liquid shot out of the can and hit Haruka square in the face.
"Here, Senpai, towel, towel!"
"Pff, pff, pff... P-pfwah! Pfhuhuhuhu pfpfpfpfpf-pweh...!"
"Y...! 　o...! 　u...! 　guuuys!!"
Haruka pounced on Goto.
"Gyahahaha, that tickles, Senpai! Gyahahahaha!"
"You shook it before you came in the room! Why are you guys always wasting soda?!"
"Gyahahaha! That's what bugs you?! Gyahahahahahaha!"
The two of them started a wrestling match on the bed.
I should take the side of Goto, who'd thought up the same prank.
"Hey, Haruka! What do you think you're doing to my cute junior?!"
I dove onto the bed too and went to pin Haruka down.
"S-stop it! 　Okay! Okay, I get it! Quit tickling! 　Wai-, I'm sorry! Heeelp!!"
At the sound of the intercom, we froze.
"Be right back!"
"It's okay... right...?"
Haruka anxiously watched Goto go.
Someone had come all the way to Goto's house looking for us...?
Maybe I'll take a little peek.
From the top of the stairs, we had a good view of the front entrance.
Together with Haruka, I lay flat on the floor at the head of the stairs and watched the entrance.
Goto was facing a man I'd never seen before.
"Um, excuse me... Could you speak a little more clearly?"
"I-I-I-I am, I-I-I-Ise!"
"Pardon?"
"Hey, who's that guy?"
"No idea..."
"I am Assistant Inspector Daijiro Ise of the Motoki Police! 　N-n-n...Natsumi Kogori-san is the subject of a missing-person report!"
He seemed to be police. So he really had come looking for us.
"What? Senpai is missing!?"
Goto raised her voice, deliberately loud.
Actually, she was keeping quite a distance for someone having a conversation.
That way she was probably making the man talk louder, so we could hear him too.
Though even without that, the man's voice was unnaturally loud.
"A p... police officer...!?"
"Haruka, it's okay. The police are just on edge because of that incident this morning."
"Y... yeah... That's right... yeah, that's gotta be it!"
"N-n-no, it has not been established that she is missing, but there is a request from Kogori-san's mother! 　And anyway, why are you standing so far away!?"
"Well, Ise-san, was it? You're acting kind of suspicious..."
"I am not a s-s-s-suspicious person! Here is my badge, see!"
"That guy... he's all nervous in front of Goto."
Goto's sharp mind was fairly well known, apparently.
Ise-san might have known that, and been nervous because of it.
"Um, so what can I do for you?"
"I will ask you straight out! Do you know a person named Natsumi Kogori-san!?"
"Well, of course I know her. She's been my senpai since middle school."
"N-n-n-no, that is not what I... Natsumi Kogori-san! Do you know her!?"
"Like I said, I know her."
"Uh-uh-uhm... how do I put this..."
"You mean, do I know where Kogori-senpai is?"
"Yes, yes, that is the one! Just what I would expect of the genius girl!"
"What's with that guy? 　His face is bright red."
"I did get a call from Senpai's mother a little while ago. But I don't know either."
"Mm... I-I see... D-don't you have any idea where she could be... maybe...?"
"I got it! That guy's a rookie, just started this spring! That's why he's nervous, right?"
Would a rookie come by himself?
"An idea where she is... If she hasn't contacted even me, it must be pretty serious. But I really don't know. Hey, Ojisan! Senpai hasn't been kidnapped or anything, has she!?"
"O-Ojisan...?"
"Well, has she, Ojisan!?"
"Wh-wh-wh-whoa!"
"Why's he so freaked out? Is Goto making that scary a face?"
"Well, has she!?"
"Ugh..."
Ise-san stood frozen, but then cleared his throat once and straightened up.
"Well, it is true that the possibility of a kidnapping cannot be ruled out... I suppose... Understood! Thank you for your cooperation!"
Ise-san gave Goto a snappy salute.
"Thank you for all your hard work!"
Goto gave a snappy salute too.
"Also... Goto-san... P- p- p...!"
"???"
"I want to take your picture!"
"A picture?"
"F-f-f... for the record, we need a list of everyone we have questioned, so I am photographing all of them! I request your cooperation!"
"Sure! But please wait just a moment. I'll go fix my hair!"
Goto came up the stairs.
"Until I give the signal, you absolutely can't make a sound. Even after that Ojisan leaves. Absolutely!"
She softly whispered that, then went into her room.
"What does that mean?"
Ise-san was fidgeting restlessly, but photographing the people he'd questioned was pretty thorough record-keeping.
―Before long, Goto came out of her room.
"Huh? Goto, what's with that hairstyle?"
"Shh!"
Goto made the 'don't talk' gesture and went down the stairs.
"How's this?"
Goto spun around once in front of Ise-san, swinging her twin tails like soft feathers.
"Nuooooooh! Without a doubt, this is the Motoki Pretty Girl Appreciation Society's No. 1 most popular, Erina Goto! 　Good, good!"
Saying that, Ise-san started taking pictures with his phone.
Ise-san took picture after picture.
In step with him, Goto kept switching poses.
"Whew... I am glad I came... Well then, I will take my leave!"
Ise-san left politely.
After seeing him off, Goto was straightening the shoes in the entryway.
"So what was that all about? Was that really a cop?"
Goto came up the stairs.
At the same time, she had her index finger to her lips, signaling 'don't talk'.
And in her other hand, she was holding something.
Goto came up to us and, finger still on her lips, showed us what she was holding in her other hand.
It was a small device, like a black matchbox with an antenna attached.
This is... a listening device!?
Goto had said, "Don't talk even after Ise-san leaves." That would mean the police left this listening device, but―
