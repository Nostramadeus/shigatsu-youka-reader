Make-Believe Loveliness

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-07 17:00 火 · 82 boxes

"Natsumi, we're almost there."
Someone was shaking me.
A slight pain in my neck.
I realized I'd been sleeping with my head slumped over.
When I opened my eyes, it was dusk outside.
"5 more minutes to the last station. Make sure you're wide awake."
We'd come that far already?
After changing trains so many times, I was understandably tired by the second half.
We talked up a storm, but in the end we never discussed the overnight trip.
"Hey, Goto, wake up. We'll leave you behind!"
Beside me, Goto was sleeping blissfully.
"Not just my brains, but my face and figure too..."
Mumbling nonsense in her sleep, she was drooling from her grinning mouth.
Goto was surprisingly sloppy when she slept.
We seemed to have come pretty deep into the mountains.
The train had only one car, and we were the only ones on it.
―Or so I thought, but there was one more person, on the side-facing bench farthest from us.
Mid-20s, maybe?
A woman was sitting alone, perfectly still.
A foreigner...?
Her hair color and her skin were different from ours.
"Huh? Chiga-nee?"
Haruka spoke to the woman.
"Mm... Haruka, you finally noticed me. I was so lonely..."
The woman called Chiga-nee turned a fleeting smile on us.
"Wait, Chiga-nee, you were hiding your face on purpose, weren't you?"
"Mm... Hehe. I thought I might get all the way to the end without being found out, but it seems that was impossible."
"But Chiga-nee, were you really on from the start? There's no way we wouldn't notice on a one-car train..."
"Mm... I got on partway. And all three of you were sound asleep."
"Partway? Is there even a station between Arata and Toyotake?"
"Mm... If you put your thumb up by the tracks, it'll stop for you. This train is practically a volunteer operation, so it's flexible."
"Hitchhiking a train... What were you doing out there...?"
"Mm... I saw some nice wild mountain greens, so I had them let me off partway. Here."
The woman called Chiga-nee spread open a bag of wild mountain greens.
There really were lots of tasty-looking wild greens in it.
As I was puzzling over it, she turned her face toward me.
Then the woman called Chiga-nee came closer to me.
"Mm... How do you do. I am Haruka's cousin. My name is Chigaya Niimura. You two must be Haruka's friends?"
"Ah, I am Natsumi Kogori. I am always most honorably being looked after by Haruka-san."
I'd greeted her in some bizarre honorific speech...
"So, the one sleeping over here is Goto."
"Mm... So this little one is the famous Goto-chan. Haruka has told me about her. Such a cute sleeping face..."
Chigaya-san said that and brought her face close to Goto's sleeping one.
"............
Hngh...?"
"Mm... Hello, Goto-chan."
"Waaaaah!!!"
Chigaya-san's face closing in startled Goto, who screamed and did two backward rolls on the side-facing bench.
"Wh-wh-wh-wh-wh-wh-who are you!?"
"Mm... Hehe, you really are cute..."
Chigaya-san went up to Goto and stroked her chin.
Goto had no idea what was going on and looked frightened.
"Come on, Chiga-nee... that's taking the prank too far."
"Mm... But this child has such squishy cheeks and eyes like a doll's... she's just so cute. Oh my, you're drooling. Let me wipe it."
"H-help me..."
Goto still didn't seem to grasp the situation.
"Hah! Hah! Wh-wh-who is this person!?"
"Alrighty, we're getting off soon!"
"Hey, Niimura-senpai, please don't ignore me!"
The train started to slow down.
"Mm... Let's go together, Goto-chan."
Chigaya-san took Goto's hand.
"Y... yes..."
Goto always uses her so-called strike-first-and-win operation to drag people she's just met into her own rhythm, but she was being completely outplayed by Chigaya-san.
"Chiga-nee, how are you getting to the settlement? 　Gonna ride in my mom's car?"
"Mm... That was the plan from the start. Didn't you hear?"
"Uh, did I...? Now that you mention it, I think I heard something..."
Odds are, this is just Haruka not remembering...
"Kogori-senpai... what is that...?"
Goto hid behind me and stared balefully at Chigaya-san.
"Oh, she's Chigaya Niimura-san, she said. Haruka's cousin, apparently."
"I... that person, I don't like..."
Well, if that was their first meeting, it was no wonder.
She didn't seem like a bad person, but she had a, how to put it, mysterious air about her.
A pale blue dress with a poncho blanket over it, and long straight hair.
Her looks and her aura alike were like the very concept of beauty given form.
And most striking of all were her odd eyes, blue and green. Her face, too, somehow looked like a fair-skinned foreigner's.
Looking into those eyes, I felt as if I might be flung off to some faraway world, as if I were being lured into another world...
―The train came to a complete stop, and the doors opened.
It was relatively warm today, and even though we were deep in the mountains, it wasn't that cold outside.
This whole area was cut off from the outside world, and I could feel its distinctive air on my skin.
"Mm... Let's go, Goto-chan. This way."
Chigaya-san forcibly pulled out Goto from behind me, where she'd been hiding.
"H... help me..."
Goto went through the ticket gate with Chigaya-san as if she were being kidnapped.
Chigaya-san's aura and the way she acts sure don't match...
"Haruka, Chigaya-san is a beautiful person..."
"Apparently a lot of the Arata settlement's ancestors were foreigners. So every now and then, someone like that gets born."
"I see. But Haruka, you're, how to say, ordinary. You don't seem foreign at all."
"After all these years, the foreign blood's probably gotten thinner, or so Mom says, anyway. I'm the only one who's way different from the others... or rather, people of the Arata bloodline have a pretty unique vibe..."
"Haruka, you seem like someone you could find anywhere, though."
"Well, sure, I was born and raised in Motoki-cho. But if I lived in the Arata settlement long enough, who knows. Maybe I could turn out like Chiga-nee!"
"Not happening."
"That comeback stings a little..."
When we went through the ticket gate, a car was parked there.
Standing next to it was Haruka's mom.
"Harukaaa! Natsumi-chaaan! Over here!"
That high-energy, falsetto-like voice spread unashamedly across the whole area.
Today, too, Obasan was full of energy.
"Finally made it this far... Are the preparations for tomorrow going okay?"
"All that's left is to wait for the big day! The preparations are pretty much done!"
Obasan snatched our bags and loaded them into the car one after another.
She really was an energetic person.
"Goto-chan, sit next to me! Come on, come on!"
"H... hel..."
Chigaya-san and Goto vanished into the back seat.
"Hey, Natsumi, what kind of person does Chiga-nee seem like to you?"
"Hmm, demure and super beautiful, but actually really strong-willed... maybe?"
"Oh-ho, Natsumi, you caught on already. Chiga-nee doesn't look it, but she's quite something."
It was a vague way to put it, but I felt like I knew what she meant...
Still, there weren't any buildings at all around here.
By the station, there was just a small wooden building that looked like a storage shed.
"Obasan, there are no buildings at all around here."
"That's right! This station is more or less here just for the Arata settlement!"
"Do people from the settlement use that shed?"
"............ Who knows. Maybe it has tools for maintaining the station or something!"
"Mm... Whoa! Goto-chan, amazing! You're bigger than you look. Your size is―"
Obasan's voice was shoved aside by an alarming shout from inside the car, Chiga-nee's.
"Okay, okay, Chigaya-chan, Goto-chan, seatbelts on!"
Haruka took the passenger seat, and I sat next to Chigaya-san.
Or rather, Chigaya-san was crowding in, pushing Goto to the edge.
So much that the two of them took up barely more than one person's worth of space...
"Everyone got their seatbelts on? The road's rough. Mom... drive quietly... carefully, okay?"
"It's fine, it's fine! I'm used to this road!"
Obasan's driving, as I recalled, was pretty wild.
And besides, Obasan's answer was slightly off from what would ease Haruka's worries.
"Mm... Goto-chan, let's share my seatbelt."
"............"
Goto no longer seemed to have the will to resist.
Would we really make it to the settlement safely like this?
The car moved slowly, at a safe speed.
Even Obasan apparently didn't push it on a road this bad.
Still, these were really deep mountains.
It felt as though I could see the sun going down and the dark coming on.
"Chiga-nee, how's college?"
Chigaya-san is a college student, then.
Partly because of her aura, she seemed very grown-up.
"Mm... I have lots of friends. And I never miss a party I can make it to."
Chigaya-san was still clinging to Goto, rubbing her cheek against hers.
"Chigaya-san, so you go to drinking parties and things."
"Mm... Natsumi-chan, 'Chigaya-san'? I don't like being called something so distant."
"Then it's Chiga-nee for everybody! 　Okay?"
Apparently I was going to call her Chiga-nee too.
"Mm... On days I have no part-time work, it's parties till morning. Since I have the chance, I should enjoy whatever looks fun."
"By parties... you mean... group dates?"
"Hmm, all sorts. Group dates, just friends... Sometimes I go into town alone and join up with people I've just met, right there..."
Did that mean getting picked up, or picking men up herself...?
Chiga-nee, unlike her looks, really did seem to be quite something...
"Chiga-nee, you're quite aggressive..."
"Mm... Natsumi-chan, I don't like polite speech either. We're not senior and junior, are we?"
"Y-yes, that is... I mean, yeah..."
Chiga-nee was smiling, but the gaze coming from those odd eyes was too intense.
This person had a seriously forceful gaze...
"Ch-Chiga-nee, if you go to parties, you must be really popular. Do you have a boyfriend or anything?"
"Mm... A boyfriend...? 　A boyfriend... are you asking about my history with men?"
Then, suddenly, the look in Chiga-nee's eyes changed.
And she began clenching her fists tight.
"Uh, um... Chiga-nee...?"
"Nnnn...!"
I thought she was angry, but she had her face down and was making a sound like she was about to cry.
Then, suddenly, she grabbed both my hands.
"Mm... Natsumi-chan, will you listen to me!? 　The start of a party is fine, the start! 　Everyone fusses over me, and I'm considerate of everyone and act just right! 　There are lots of wonderful men, and they give me their contact info right away! 　But... but...!"
Chiga-nee's eyes overflowed with big, spilling tears.
"Mm... Before I know it, it's morning, and none of the men answer anymore! 　What do you think of that!? Isn't it awful!? 　The day before yesterday, when I woke up in the morning, I was asleep in a roadside gutter!?"
She was pleading desperately, but for some reason I couldn't bring myself to sympathize.
Or rather, I didn't get what 'before I know it, it's morning' meant.
"Mm... Why could that be... I know I shouldn't say it myself, but I don't think my face is that bad. Could I have done something when I blacked out from drinking...?"
Ah, so drunken mishaps were the cause.
Faced with a weeping Chiga-nee asking herself questions and answering them over and over, all I could manage was a wry smile.
―Just then, a buzzing.
Chiga-nee's phone, it seemed.
"Mm... It's a friend from college. Um... sending the photos from the last party, so... it's about time Chigaya... faced reality...?"
She stared at her phone for a while, then gradually started to tremble.
"Chiga-nee, what's wrong?"
Then Chiga-nee, still looking down, silently handed me the phone.
Did that mean I should look?
Ah, I see...
Chugging beer from a pitcher, Chiga-nee.
Drinking sake from a giant cup (clearly a platter that had held food) like a sumo wrestler who has just won a tournament, Chiga-nee.
Straddling a man and forcing a beer bottle into his mouth, Chiga-nee.
I handed the phone back in silence too.
So this is the kind of person they call a disappointing beauty...
No wonder men keep their distance.
"Right now, the only thing I live for is Goto-chan!
　Goto-chan, I love you!"
Chiga-nee hugged Goto again.
Goto looked like she might foam at the mouth any second.
Haruka was right. She really was quite something...
The only word that came to mind was disappointing.
Would Goto really make it home alive?
Or rather, would she even be allowed to go home?
"Oh my? We're a little short on time. Maybe we'd better hurry?"
"Mom... please, drive safe..."
But suddenly I felt the tires spin, and heard an engine roar that made me want to cover my ears.
"Now... here we go...!"
The huge van started tearing down the narrow mountain road.
If anyone had been on a road like this, they surely would have been sent flying before they could dodge.
In the rear-view mirror, Obasan's face was really scary.
I feel like I saw Obasan with a face like that when I was little, but...
I never imagined I'd be riding a thrill ride this deep in the mountains.
Mom once told me that Obasan has an extreme side, so watch out when she goes wild, and that her true nature comes out behind the wheel.
Twisted into a manji shape, I took to heart just how reliable that information had been.
