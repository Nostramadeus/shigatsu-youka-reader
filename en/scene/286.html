Green Eyes ~Fallen~

Eden no Sakura Arc [Eden's Cherry] · Shiyo 800-04-07 19:50 火 · 57 boxes

Between 7 and 8 p.m.―probably about that.
The sun had set completely, and my second night in this abandoned hut was beginning.
By now the Motoki-cho group must have reached the Arata settlement and seen my memo.
It should be safe to come out of here by now.
I was hungry, as you'd expect, but this was no time for a leisurely meal.
A person can live about a week without eating.
On my way back to the settlement, I mean to at least drink some water from the stream and quench my thirst, but―
"Haha... Hahaha... it's hopeless after all...! 　Better the easy way... than suffering...! 　People all die sometime anyway..."
My negativity act is finally reaching its grand finale.
After blurting out negative things since morning, my own thoughts are about to roll that way too.
The power of words out of your own mouth is frightening.
Even without meaning it, I could end up doing as I say.
I slowly got up, took out my earplugs― and put my hand on the door.
In contrast to my heart, it was very quiet outside.
Trees in fresh bud swayed with a soft rustling, and for the first time in a long while my hearing was stirred.
"I'll tell everyone else too... it's better to die without suffering, after all."
I staggered off toward the mountain stream.
Walking while pretending to be in a daze isn't easy either.
"............?"
After I'd walked a few dozen meters, the ground underfoot suddenly turned to mud.
Had there been a passing shower?
However well my ears were stopped, I'd have noticed rain this heavy beating down on the hut.
The hut, it seemed, had only just escaped the downpour.
"What is this... I have to go tell everyone, and now..."
The road toward the settlement was blocked by a landslide.
Climbing over it would be easy, but even if I knocked out the whole Motoki-cho group, carrying them to the hut would be impossible.
I'll have to think of somewhere else...
"Hurry... I have to hurry..."
My first rock climbing in a long time.
With the hunger on top of it, I couldn't muster much strength, and the climb didn't go smoothly.
No doubt Shigatsu Youka is already aware of what I'm doing right now.
But there was no sign of that eerie sound ringing out.
It may have rung a few times while I had my ears stopped, but at least I haven't heard the sound once today.
―The water of the stream was a little cloudy, maybe from the rain.
I wanted to gulp it down, but for now I made do with rinsing my mouth.
When I looked at my arm, the cut had already scabbed over.
The wound seemed to have closed, but not completely.
If I put too much strength into it, it would probably bleed again.
That sound...!
The apocalyptic sound―the sound that announces the end.
When this sound rings, a corpse comes close by...!
Without thinking, I nearly drew my knife, but stopped my hand just in time.
Right now, I must not be Shigatsu Youka's enemy.
I have given up resisting―that is the line.
Don't oppose them; show them I'm not an enemy.
Once, I plunged my face into the stream.
Water in early April is truly cold.
"Hah... hah..."
Before I ran out of breath, the cold was too much to bear, and I lifted my face out.
But I felt a gaze that made me forget even that cold at once.
Someone... Someone is watching me.
That sound rang out, which means...
"Found you at last. What are you doing in a place like this?"
Had she been watching me all along?
Making no move to run, and no show of fighting― I looked steadily into Mom's eyes.
"Mm... What about Uncle...?"
"As you can see, it's just me. So don't worry."
As if I could feel safe!
I'd like to go on a war footing here, but I mustn't show any will to resist.
I'm going to negotiate―to become one of Shigatsu Youka's own.
"Mm... Mom, I..."
"Chigaya, I know you have a lot to say, but hurry and run from here."
"Huh?"
"Right now, neither Eiichiro nor Shigatsu Youka is watching. You run, quickly, and live!"
"Mm... Mom..."
"Listen, Chigaya. Eiichiro is with Shigatsu Youka right now, regenerating his body. We're dead, so we need Shigatsu Youka to repair our bodies regularly. Right now she can't sense-steal me, so she won't notice!"
I was taken aback as Mom suddenly rattled all this off at me.
"Mm... But Mom, yesterday you..."
"I'm sorry, Chigaya... We've had hostages taken by Shigatsu Youka."
"Mm... Hostages...?"
"If we defy Shigatsu Youka, the people of this settlement will be wiped out at once. Chigaya, you've seen Shigatsu Youka's power for yourself, haven't you?"
"Mm... But doing that would leave Shigatsu Youka unable to reproduce too, wouldn't it...?"
"Shigatsu Youka can parasitize other animals, not just humans. She gives humans priority simply because she can take in their intelligence, that's all."
"Mm... But me, abandoning everyone and running away..."
"Chigaya, you're the only one who can get away now. There's no time to go to the settlement and get everyone out. So... at least you..."
"Mom..."
"I'm sorry, Chigaya... We finally meet again after 10 years, and it has to be like this..."
Meeting a dead person is, to begin with, impossible.
Whatever the form, I got to see Mom.
So I don't want Mom to apologize to me.
Mom... you've been suffering all this time, haven't you...
"'Doubting everything' means asking 'is that really so?' and 'why is that so?'―doubting, and digging deeper."
Time and again, I've mulled over Goto-chan's advice.
I know―what is happening now is impossible.
So it's dangerous to swallow whole anything that stems from it.
That means it's dangerous to take what that book says at face value, and dangerous to believe what I see and hear.
So even Mom's sweet words―
Repeat the 'why'...!
"Mm... But Mom, why haven't you let anyone escape before now? 　The last time Uncle was having his body repaired, you could have let everyone escape like this, couldn't you?"
"That would throw the settlement into an uproar. 'The dead are walking.'"
"Mm... Then why didn't you at least tell Dad? 　If you'd told just Dad, he could have spoken for you to everyone."
"That's dangerous. In this settlement, danger sense is already shunned as 'the witch's power.'"
"Mm... Then...! 　Then..."
But I couldn't think of any more questions.
Mom's answers are perfectly reasonable, and they hold together.
Then maybe it's all right for me to just run...
"Chigaya... whatever happens, stay alive. Go back toward Toyotake and down the mountain path, and you'll be safe."
Toward Toyotake―that's the direction the hut was in.
From there, down the road with the landslide, you reach Toyotake Station.
Huh?
Wait a minute... Isn't that odd?
Go back toward Toyotake?
That's something she couldn't say without knowing that I'd been toward Toyotake until just now― that is, in the abandoned hut.
Besides, when Mom saw me just now,
"Found you at last."
she said.
In other words, she shouldn't know I was in the hut― no, she should be acting as if she doesn't know.
Could this be...
"Mm... Mom... no, I can't... I'm not running."
"Why!? Why...?"
"Mm... I'm sure I can't get away from Shigatsu Youka anyway... So at the end, at least, I'd like to spend time with you, Mom. That's why I'm going to do as Shigatsu Youka wants. I've already been thinking about it―how to talk everyone into staying here instead of running."
"Chigaya..."
Of course, this is a bluff.
There's a chance Mom is deceiving me.
I've been muttering surrender to Shigatsu Youka all this time.
Shigatsu Youka is checking whether I mean it―that's what I thought.
"Mm... Sorry, Mom. I'm glad you're trying to let me escape. But I don't want to be parted from you again."
"Chigaya, never mind me―think of your own future―"
"Mm... A future? 　What future is there if I run away from here alone? 　There's no future for someone who abandoned Dad and Kana. If that's how it is, I'd rather stay with everyone to the end, in the land where I was born and raised..."
"I see... Are you really sure?"
"Mm... Yes..."
"Chigaya... what a shame. A parent, you see, wants her child to live and be well, whatever happens."
"Mm... Mom, that's enough. I don't want to hear any more. Instead, tell me what I should do for Shigatsu Youka."
"You mean it? There's no going back, you know."
"............"
"Then, Chigaya, here and now... you have to die."
"Mm... Die?"
"Yes. Die, and let Shigatsu Youka control you. Then our wills will be one, completely."
Damn...! I didn't think it would come to this.
Dying is the proof.
The condition for becoming one of Shigatsu Youka's own.
"Chigaya, I can't take my own daughter's life, not even now. If you could... do it yourself..."
I have a knife.
I can die with this.
What do I do?
If I show her I'm ready to stab, right up to the last second, will she tell me after all that I don't have to die?
"Chigaya. I'll look the other way."
With that, Mom turned her back.
I understand well enough that taking Mom by surprise here would mean nothing.
Do I really... really have to die?
What happens if I say now that I can't do it after all?
I put the knife to my throat.
One push, and I die and become one of Shigatsu Youka's own.
I've been completely outplayed...
It was never going to go that easily according to plan.
Is this it...
"Hahahaha! Impressive, Chigaya-chan! A wise decision, I'd say!"
A sudden burst of loud laughter.
It was Uncle.
"Congratulations, Chigaya. You pass. I'm sorry. We were testing whether you really meant it."
"Well, after yesterday's demonstration of how far we outclass you... better to think about a peaceful end, really."
"That way of thinking―spoken like a dead man."
That was close...!
It's just as these two said yesterday, after all.
What they said yesterday was... let me think...
"Eiichiro and I are already dead. Surely you understand that this self belongs to the Goddess?"
"The way we talk and think comes from the original owners of these bodies, but what rules the mind now is the Goddess. The original owners' minds died more than 10 years ago."
Shigatsu Youka has simply commandeered the will and intelligence of the two of them.
Honestly, a mushroom trying to deceive us with words...!
"Mm... I don't have to die?"
"That's right. Chigaya, we'll have you do what only you can."
"Mm... What should I do?"
"Chigaya, you'll talk the people of the settlement round. Tell them not to panic, to stay calm."
"Just make sure they don't run from here. That's a job we can't do."
"Now, let's go, Chigaya."
"Mm... Go―together?"
"Of course. We're on the same side now."
This has gotten troublesome.
Of course, I mean to get everyone in the settlement safely away.
But like this, I'll end up having to do it with the two of them watching.
If only I could act on my own...
"Mm... Then there's something to do first!"
At that, the two of them exchanged looks.
They don't have to imitate humans this far...
"Mm... You know four people came from Motoki-cho today, don't you? 　They must already have figured out what Shigatsu Youka really is. If we don't isolate them from the settlement, the residents will learn that Shigatsu Youka exists."
"Chigaya... I'm so glad you grew up clever. Yes―it seems Kana told Natsumi-chan about Verde just now, so trouble is only a matter of time."
"Mm... Then there's the abandoned hut where I was hiding, right? 　I'd like to confine them there..."
"I see. Not a bad proposal."
"Mm... But with that landslide, ordinary people can't get through. Can't it be made passable somehow?"
"Ah, that'll take hands. We'll have to wake a few."
"Let's leave the four from Motoki-cho to Chigaya. We have to clear that landslide."
Yes! Now I'm completely on my own, and I can keep the two of them busy toward the hut.
"Right, let's get started."
"Chigaya, we'll come help as soon as the path is open."
"Mm... How long until it's passable?"
"We'll check on that now. We haven't looked at the landslide properly yet, and even with our strength, hauling earth is hard work. We'll need help, so we'll wake a few."
"Mm... But couldn't Shigatsu Youka clear that in no time? 　It digs tunnels underground in an instant, doesn't it?"
"No, Chigaya, none of that. It would upset the ecosystem, you see? 　We keep things as natural as we can. We didn't want to dig underground yesterday either, truth be told."
"If the living things vanish, the Goddess can't live either, however many cherry trees bloom."
Spoken like a wild creature.
Still, this buys me a good deal of time.
"And Chigaya, whatever you do, be careful not to get hurt."
"Mm... Hurt?"
"Yes. Don't shed any more blood, all right?"
Blood...?
