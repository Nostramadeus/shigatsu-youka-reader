Niimura-san

Jusatsu Arc [Killing by Curse] · Shiyo 800-04-08 19:40 水 · 43 boxes

Play options

Inside the house, it was pitch dark.
Dad and Mom were at a class reunion and wouldn't be home today.
Sis, no doubt, was out running around with guys again.
Sis had probably never thought well of me, fawned over by everyone around me as a genius girl.
Even though my parents, for their part, had never treated us sisters differently because of it.
Before I knew it, a wall had formed between us sisters.
I went into my room on the second floor and picked up my smartphone.
"Oh, hello, Mom? Enjoying the reunion?"
"Erinaaa! How are youuu?"
From her voice, Mom seemed to be pretty drunk.
Behind her, I could also hear drunks making a racket.
The class reunion, or whatever it was, looked to be in full swing.
"Yeah, I'm fine. What about Dad?"
"Wait just a second!"
―Shortly, I heard a familiar voice.
"Erina-chaaan? It's Daaad!"
He seemed even drunker than Mom.
"Listen, Erina! You're a chosen child! You're a genius! Do your studying riiight and grow up to be a fine adult! You and Momoko both, you're smart daughters!"
After that, I kept making agreeing noises at his long, rambling drunk talk, and hung up at a suitable point.
Good, my parents seemed to be fine.
Where the two of them had gone today was Minakami City, the prefectural capital of Sawa Prefecture.
Actually, it had overlapped in my mind with the business trip of Kogori-senpai's father, and I'd been a little scared.
Now then, first I'll sort out what I'm going to do from here, and plan the Operation.
First, an investigation of the accident that happened in Central Park long ago.
Next, look into the Megasawa City case in more detail.
How that links up with this incident: I'll force a connection even if it's a stretch, and test it as a hypothesis.
I went online,

“Motoki-cho Central Park accident”

and when I searched with that...
In the list, there was nothing lined up but the incident from this morning.
I set the period to yesterday and before, and tried searching again.
............ This is it...!
Hanami visitor dies in accident in Motoki-cho, Sawa Prefecture.
When I clicked it, it was a very small article.
Shiyo 788―exactly 12 years ago today, a hanami visitor died in an accident in Central Park.
Fell from a cherry tree.
Got drunk and went tree climbing or something, maybe?
The one who died was...
Motoki-cho resident Eiichiro Niimura-san (30)
Niimura...?
Niimura, as in...!
In an instant, I was seized by the feeling of goosebumps rising all over my body.
"You're kidding...?"
Niimura, as in... Niimura-senpai's, father...?
Age-wise, too, there's nothing that off about it.
No, it could very well be someone with the same surname, or a distant relative, too.
It's not that rare a surname, after all.
If I remember right, a local paper is published in Motoki-cho, too.
If I can find that, I should be able to learn more detailed information.
“Motoki Daily Central Park accidental death hanami”

I searched again with that.
There was just one hit.
I took one breath, and when I timidly clicked it―
Just after noon today, Motoki-cho resident Eiichiro Niimura-san (30) accidentally fell from a tree, violently struck his neck on the park's fence, and died.
Niimura-san had come for hanami with his family, and is believed to have climbed a cherry tree.
According to eyewitness testimony, Niimura-san climbed the tree to retrieve a balloon that a hanami visitor's child had caught in the tree.
Niimura-san had been drinking together with a friend's family, and it is highly likely that his hand slipped.
The article ended there.
With just this, there's no proof that he's Niimura-senpai's father, but
・came with his family
・a hanami visitor's child who had a balloon
from those points, it narrows things down a lot.
But, 'a child who had a balloon,' then.
Letting a balloon fly off by mistake means it probably wasn't that big a child.
Say, a kindergartner back then; about high-school age now, maybe?
And,
“to retrieve a balloon that a hanami visitor's child had caught in the tree”
Judging by this wording, it probably wasn't Niimura-san's child.
But, Niimura-san went out of his way to try to get the balloon back for someone else's child.
A "someone else's child" close enough to him for that.
Which means.
I can't shake the feeling that this victim is Niimura-senpai's father.
And, the child who let this balloon fly away, could it possibly be.
With a hypothesis, you assume it and push ahead, and if it's wrong, you just correct it.
But in this case, if I push ahead like this, everything will probably end up beyond repair.
Because there's someone I have to work up the nerve to ask.
Having come this far, there's only one thing I can do. Ask Niimura-senpai about it.
If Senpai is this Niimura-san's daughter, I can't rule out the possibility that she's involved in this morning's incident, either.
If it comes out that I lied to Senpai, that can't be helped at this point.
But there's still something bothering me.
The accident happened 12 years ago today, on April 8. Today is the anniversary of this Niimura-san's death.
If the two are parent and child, then that means the thirteenth-year memorial service was never held. [TN: A Buddhist memorial held 12 years after a death.]
There are probably times the date gets moved to fit a weekend, but Niimura-senpai's manner had been too normal, in a bad way.
What's more, she'd hidden it, going as far as lying about a transfer overseas.
If, with the anniversary of Niimura-san's death in mind, this morning's incident was brought about―
Besides, in that Megasawa City case, two people were killed every time.
If two are killed this time too, another incident will happen before today is over.
The more I think about it, the more I feel Niimura-senpai is deeply involved, but I know there are more factors that deny it.
But contrary to that, a feeling of urgency wells up, too.
Because before today is over, one more person may be killed.
I don't like thinking about things with intuition or feelings, but...
Speaking of intuition―
At noon, when the three of us were about to leave Zahha, Kogori-senpai said to me, "Stay with me."
Did that mean there was some kind of danger?
If danger is closing in on Kogori-senpai...
This is no good... I really need to sort things out a little...
It's not so much that it's complicated; the things I have to think through are just too close to me, and I can't sort out my feelings.
Unable to take it anymore, I threw myself onto the bed.
The accident 12 years ago. A person named Eiichiro Niimura. The Megasawa City case. April 8. The thirteenth-year memorial. Kogori-senpai's dream.
There are far too many uncertain factors to consider it as a hypothesis.
If I had the time, I'd like to think it over a little more carefully, but...
So, do I call Niimura-senpai, or not?
This is a very important choice.
I decided.
I'll confirm the truth of the matter with Niimura-senpai.
If my hypothesis is wrong, that's fine.
I'm calling to make sure that it's wrong.
That's right, with feelings like these, I can't possibly investigate properly.
I'll rule out the possibility that Niimura-senpai is involved, and get serious about investigating the incident.
It'd be best to start with casual talk, and partway through, ask a question meant to trip her up.
When I lifted my face from where I'd been lying face down,
my smartphone was lying right by my pillow.
My hand was shaking. Could I make casual talk in this state?
I got up, stood in front of the mirror, and tried grinning from ear to ear as hard as I could.
Yeah, I'm okay.
I took one big, deep breath.
Okay, time to carry out the Operation.
