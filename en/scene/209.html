Mirror (Me)

Wabigoto Arc [Apology] · Shiyo 778-11-23 16:00 金 · 63 boxes

"Mifuyu... what happened after that? I heard Eiichiro-san took to his bed."
Eiichiro-san and I had met the day before, and now I was meeting Akane at the usual cafe.
"Uugh... Eiichiro-san..."
In the end, I couldn't ask for Eiichiro-san's contact info, and I couldn't go after him either.
Right away I set up a meeting with Akane.
"So... Eiichiro-san's a no after all?"
"N-no, that's not it! He's just like me... So I understand how Eiichiro-san feels way too well, so much it hurts..."
"And that's why he took to his bed... what's that supposed to mean?"
―I told Akane what Eiichiro-san and I had talked about in Central Park.
"I see... Two of a kind, so you'd think you'd get along, but you're too much alike, so neither of you can make a move..."
"Uugh... what should I do..."
"What are you talking about? You charge in! 　Ch- ar- g- e!"
"What's that..."
"Go see Eiichiro-san, who's laid up in bed, right now!"
"What?!"
"He took to his bed out of self-loathing, because he thinks he's no match for you, Mifuyu, right? 　Then go there yourself and talk to Eiichiro-san properly. Or do you have some complaint about Eiichiro-san?"
"Uugh... no complaints... if anything, I thought he was really nice... But what should I even talk about..."
"You, honestly..."
"Would he really like someone like me..."
"Ugh, enough! Come on! We're going!"
―Dragged along by Akane against my will, I headed to the apartment building where Eiichiro-san lived.
"Eiichiro-san lives in 502, apparently."
"Uugh... Akane, come with me..."
"Haah... Only as far as the door, okay?"
Akane brought me as far as the door of room 502, and without even waiting for me to get ready, she rang the intercom.
"No answer..."
"He must be out! Come on, let's go home, okay?"
"Oh, it's open..."
Akane had opened the door without asking anyone.
"Well then, good luck in there!"
"Whoa!"
Akane shoved me into the room by force.
"Uugh... what do I do..."
Inside, the curtains were closed and it was dim.
"Eiichiro-san, are you here?"
I tried calling in a small voice he surely couldn't hear.
Of course, there was no answer.
I took off my shoes and slowly went inside.
Inside, it was tidy, with a little of a lived-in feel left.
A futon was laid out, and I could see someone sleeping under the comforter.
"Um... Eiichiro-san?"
I went up to the futon and called to him.
"Mifuyu-san's voice... I'm finally hearing things... It's terminal..."
That voice was definitely Eiichiro-san's.
Eiichiro-san's nonsensical words reached my ears.
"Um... Eiichiro-san? It's Mifuyu..."
The moment I called to him again, the comforter flew up so high it nearly hit the ceiling.
"Whoa!"
"M-M-M-Mifuyu-san?!"
In his startled jump he hit the light switch with his back, revealing Eiichiro-san in nothing but his underwear.
"Mifuyu-san, why?! Ah, the way I'm dressed, this is, um...!"
"U-um, uh...! I heard you were sick in bed, so I was worried...!"
The light's pull cord swung as if it were laughing at this whole exchange.
We were both so flustered that it took a while for Eiichiro-san to get his clothes on.
"I-I-I'm sorry for barging in all of a sudden! A-a-are you all right?!"
"Uh-uh-uh-um, I-I-I'm all right!"
We were both so jittery that we couldn't hold a conversation.
"Eiichiro-san, h-h-have you eaten properly?!"
"N-n-not yet today...!"
"Th-then I'll m-make something!"
Carried by the momentum, I headed for the kitchen.
"Uugh... Akane, what do I do..."
I'd ended up making him a meal, but could I make anything that would suit Eiichiro-san's taste...
I cleared the sink as best I could and checked inside the fridge.
There were a fair number of ingredients, but I didn't want to keep him waiting long, so I couldn't make anything that took time.
When I stole a glance at Eiichiro-san...
He was kneeling formally with his head down, looking restless.
Like this, the silence would be too much for him, so something quick to make and filling...
That meant...
15 minutes later, I brought the finished dish over to Eiichiro-san.
"Um, I hope it suits your taste..."
What I'd made was okonomiyaki. [TN: A savory pan-fried pancake of batter and cabbage.]
Making something quickly with whatever was on hand, this was about all I could manage.
"It looks... so delicious... I-is it really okay for me to eat this?"
"Y-yes! I took the liberty of making it for you, Eiichiro-san! 　Um, I don't think it tastes bad, but..."
Eiichiro-san slowly brought his chopsticks to the okonomiyaki and put a bite of it in his mouth.
"How is it...?"
"............"
Eiichiro-san stayed silent.
Then, still silent, he put another bite in his mouth.
Eiichiro-san's movements were slow, yet the okonomiyaki was vanishing at an amazing speed.
I watched him, heart pounding, like a student waiting for exam results.
Eventually, Eiichiro-san finished the okonomiyaki and stared at the empty plate for a while.
"Um... did it suit your taste...?"
"Uh... ungh... hic..."
Eiichiro-san suddenly let out that groaning cry of his.
I had never expected him to cry.
"For me... Mifuyu-san... It's really delicious... I'm so happy I could die..."
"Really?! I'm so glad...!"
"Uuuh..."
Eiichiro-san started crying again.
What should I do in a case like this?
Eiichiro-san thinks the same way I do.
In that case, maybe I should do for him what I'd want done for me.
"U-um! I heard you'd been in bed all day, so would you like to go for a walk? 　I'm sure walking around outside will make you feel better!"
That line was something Akane would say to me.
But Eiichiro-san frowned.
"But... wouldn't you be embarrassed, walking with a guy like me?"
"Huh?"
"I mean... look at me..."
"............ Pff! Ahahaha!"
It was just like a conversation between me and Akane.
Now I understood well how Akane had felt, dealing with me.
Eiichiro-san's feelings when he said something like that, I understood well too.
"I want to walk with you, Eiichiro-san. If I'm good enough for you... together..."
And Eiichiro-san's face turned as red as coal.
"Y-yes! I'll go! If I'll do, I'll be your attendant!"
"Heh... 'attendant'... ahahaha!"
"Ha... hahaha!"
It was the first moment that Eiichiro-san and I laughed together.
Just as I'd thought, if I got cheerful, Eiichiro-san could get cheerful too.
Eiichiro-san's smile made me happy as well.
At that moment, I felt it clearly:
Eiichiro-san had my heartfelt affection.
Today again, a November wind was blowing that made you feel winter.
Side by side, Eiichiro-san and I walked through the early-winter sunset.
Eiichiro-san's face was bright red, as always.
He seemed to be searching desperately for something to talk about, with a face that was shaky but earnest.
Eiichiro-san was dragging one leg as he walked, just like when we'd parted yesterday.
Surely the alcohol was out of his system by now, so it really was because of an old injury.
Then, without warning, our hands bumped for an instant.
"Whoa-whoa! I'm sorry!"
"No, I'm the one who did something improper...!"
With frozen faces, we stared at each other for a while.
"Heh..."
"Hahaha..."
Eiichiro-san's hand had been warm.
Maybe because of that, the winter wind suddenly began to feel cold.
"It's a little chilly... There's a cafe I always go to with Akane. Would you like to stop by?"
"Y-yes! As long as I'm with you, Mifuyu-san, anywhere!"
Come to think of it, this was the first time I'd come into this cafe with anyone besides Akane.
Maybe because today was especially cold, it was more crowded than when I'd come in with Akane earlier.
I could hear people around us whispering.
"Are they a couple, you think..."
"It's like Beauty and the Beast..."
"Maybe the guy's super rich or something?"
"Even if he was rich, those looks would be a bit much for me... Yeah, he must be that good of a person."
"Uuuh... I knew it, I'm..."
Eiichiro-san hunched his back.
"I'm happy to be with you, Eiichiro-san."
I gave Eiichiro-san a big smile.
"Um... me too, being with you, Mifuyu-san... makes me happy!"
Until a moment ago I'd been nervous too, but that feeling was completely gone.
More than that, I was happy just to be with Eiichiro-san.
Oh no! The only drink this place had was coffee!
Eiichiro-san had said he couldn't do coffee either, hadn't he...
"Welcome, Kanai-sama. What would you like to order?"
The usual server came to take our order.
Then Eiichiro-san raised his voice louder than ever.
"Ex-excushe me! Me and Mifuyu-san, we're both no good with coffee! 　Do you have anyshing even we can drink?!"
The moment he shouted that, the customers around us all burst out laughing at once.
"Pfffff...!"
"D-don't! You can't laugh!"
I'd meant not to let it bother me, but even I got annoyed at the people around us.
But it's fine. As long as I know Eiichiro-san's charms, that's enough.
"Yes, we do. The owner here was originally someone who disliked coffee as well, so we have put together a menu that even such guests can enjoy. I shall prepare that for you, then."
What?! There was some kind of secret menu?!
"Phew, thank goodness..."
Eiichiro-san looked relieved.
Embarrassed, and still able to be this bold...
Between what happened yesterday and this, Eiichiro-san really does seem like someone I can rely on when it counts.
My affection for Eiichiro-san grew bigger and bigger.
"Kanai-sama, thank you for bringing your companion to us today. Kanai-sama, we are truly grateful for your patronage ever since we opened. To your companion as well, we humbly look forward to your continued patronage."
"Mifuyu-san, you've been a regular here that long?"
"Huh? 　Yes, back when it had just opened, Akane invited me..."
And I had a feeling the eyes around us were turned our way again.
"She's been a regular since it opened, they said."
"That girlfriend must have a good eye, then."
"Then the boyfriend must be someone amazing."
It was a strange kind of praise, but if it made people think better of Eiichiro-san, I didn't mind.
―The coffee they brought us kept that distinctive aroma, but its sweetness and creaminess matched perfectly, and even I found it delicious.
"This is delicious! Amazing, and coffee was always a total no for me before...!"
"Eiichiro-san, thanks to you, for the first time I could feel that coffee tastes good. Thank you."
I sent him a dreamy look.
"Haah... Eiichiro-san..."
"Yes?"
"............ ............ I like you..."
Eiichiro-san sprayed his coffee everywhere.
The next moment, I realized I had unconsciously said something outrageous.
"Cough! 　Cough!"
"I-I-I-I'm sorry! I said something weird out of nowhere!"
Both Eiichiro-san and I frantically wiped the table, but our hands kept fumbling and we couldn't clean it up properly.
"I-b-b-b-I, um...!"
"I-I-I'm really sorry! Saying something so improper...!"
By this point even the people around us were laughing in spite of themselves.
"Maybe they're a good match in their own way?"
"Yeah... I'm starting to think so. Like, two of a kind?"
I heard talk like that, but I was desperately trying to get the situation under control.
In the end we couldn't bear it anymore, so we hurried to drink our coffee and decided to leave the cafe.
―We walked through the cold wind again.
"............"
"............"
We were more silent than we had ever been.
I'd said something so improper that my face felt like it was on fire.
The wind outside was as cold as ever.
But the faint sunlight still lingering was gently warm.
More than anything, because of the outrageous thing I'd said, my heart was racing and my whole body was burning hot.
Afraid Eiichiro-san might hear my heartbeat, I walked with my hand pressed to my chest.
"Um... Mifuyu-san..."
The first to break the silence was Eiichiro-san.
"Are we... alike after all, do you think..."
"We... are. I think so..."
Laughing together when we laugh. Troubled together when we're troubled.
Embarrassed together when we're embarrassed.
"You might not like hearing this, but... Mifuyu-san, when I look at you, it's like I'm looking at myself... I feel like I'm looking in a mirror."
He said exactly what I had been thinking yesterday.
"So...! 　Mifuyu-san, to make you smile, first I have to be smiling...! 　If you're ever sad, Mifuyu-san, I'll smile! 　So...!"
Eiichiro-san turned toward me, his face redder than the sunset.
"So... please become a family with m-me!"
"............!!"
It was a proposal I never would have imagined, the day after we first met.
"Y... y... Yes! If a hopeless, good-for-nothing woman like me will do...! 　Please make me your family!"
Normally, people would think a proposal the next day was absurd.
But that's fine. It seems I really am extreme.
Eiichiro-san's straight, earnest gaze was, to me, kind and warm beyond compare.
You are my mirror.
I am your mirror.
Not the outside, but my very self, reflecting the heart.
Eiichiro-san... with him, I felt I could make the face reflected in that mirror shine forever.
