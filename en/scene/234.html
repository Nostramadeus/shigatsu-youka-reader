Probing

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-07 20:00 火 · 56 boxes

We feasted on the Arata settlement's unusual food.
Haruka had been saying she was too tired to eat or whatever, but after Goto and I talked her around, she finally came down to the living room.
"Mmm! This brings me back to life! 　Nourishing and invigorating, energy restored! These Arata ingredients are like the world's most delicious medicine! Food is medicine, truly!"
"Right!? Here in Arata it's not just the food, the drinks are good too!"
Holding her drink, Chiga-nee was swaying side to side like a different person.
Probably the bad-drunk type...
I'd watched Dad, who loves his drink, so I could more or less tell.
"Ahh, a drink after a good day's work tastes great!"
"Right!? When it comes to drinks, it's gotta be Arata!"
Kotaro-san and Chiga-nee were happily pouring drinks for each other.
There was quite a gap between what Chiga-nee had said earlier and how she was acting toward Kotaro-san...
Well, deep down she wants to prove Kotaro-san innocent, so maybe it's natural for her to act like this, but...
"Hey, Goto, what do you think of those two?"
"Huh? They seem so close, it's nice! I think a close family is wonderful!"
No, no, that's not what I meant...
But knowing Goto, she's probably observing the two of them.
To find those trivial contradictions or whatever.
Do Kotaro-san and Haruka's grandmother even know about my intuition in the first place?
They probably allowed me to attend the thirteenth-year memorial service because they know, but then there's the warning Shirozaki-san gave earlier: 'No one must find out.' [TN: Buddhist memorials count the year of death as the first year.]
Does it apply to everyone outside the Niimura family, or to the whole settlement, the Niimura family included―
"Oh, Kotaro-san! Kogori-senpai has a psychic power that only works at this time of year! 　Had you heard?"
Goto had suddenly made a move...!
Even drunk Chiga-nee's face tightened a little.
"Natsumi-chan's psychic power? 　Sure, of course I've heard! 　Well... don't take this the wrong way, but try not to mention it in this settlement. Sakura is the reason some folks are scared of it..."
So he knew...
In that case, what Shirozaki-san meant was 'No one outside the Niimura family must find out.'
"Why did Sakura-san awaken to an ability like that?"
"Yeah, that! I wanna hear that too!"
Chiga-nee's tightened face went slack again.
Goto was apparently going to approach it from the reason I'd been called here.
"The reason Sakura awakened to the ability... Hmm, when did she notice the ability... I think it was around high school... No, was it middle school..."
At least it wasn't something she was born with.
Then there must have been a trigger, like with me.
"Uh, so did Sakura-san get sick too? 　Since I started being able to use this intuition, I apparently got sick in cherry-blossom season..."
Now, how will he answer...!?
"Yeah, Sakura did tend to get sick in spring too. Well, it was just that she'd feel unwell if she stayed in a dark, cramped place, so as long as she watched out for that, it was no big deal."
"Come to think of it, I apparently got sick from being out in the wind. But to make my intuition work, I had to be out in the wind."
"Sakura was like that too. Once the cherry trees started blooming, everyone would go to Sakura for advice on how to hunt that day, which route to pick, that sort of thing. Sakura put everything into living up to that. She used a small three-mat room on the second floor, and she'd often push herself too hard and get sick."
The conditions differed, but it was a lot like my intuition.
I hear Ojisan's voice, but whose voice did Sakura-san hear?
"I remember now! Sakura awakened to the ability in middle school! 　Back then Eiichiro was still in Arata, so I'm sure of it!"
"Oh, right... It never felt real, but Dad was born and raised here too. So did Dad hunt too?"
"Eiichiro wasn't all that good at it... One time he pushed too hard and even broke his leg. Eiichiro had a bad leg, you know that, right? 　That's how Eiichiro lost his confidence that he could live here..."
"So that's how it was. That's why Dad left Arata..."
"It was rough back then. Me and Sakura, we tried to talk him out of leaving..."
Kotaro-san married Sakura-san, his childhood friend, so naturally she'd have been Ojisan's childhood friend too...
What kind of relationship did Ojisan and Sakura-san have?
Maybe Kotaro-san and Ojisan ended up in a love triangle over Sakura-san.
Hmm, but maybe that's overthinking it...
No... a high-school girl like me asking this kind of question... would probably be forgiven, right?
"Then, Kotaro-san, you, Haruka's dad, and Sakura-san were all close since you were little, right?"
How will he answer this...?
"Of course!
　Well, I can say it honestly now... Sakura seemed to like Eiichiro better... He was always shy and wishy-washy, but it seems Sakura couldn't leave Eiichiro alone, him being like that..."
Oh... That's pretty critical... [TN: 'Oh' is written in English in the source.]
"Ho-hooh, so with Eiichiro-san gone, Kotaro-san made his move, did he!?"
"No, it's not that I made a move, or anything... See, Eiichiro was gone, and the time I spent alone with Sakura just got longer..."
Kotaro-san was grinning sheepishly.
"Ah, anyway, back to what we were talking about..."
Maybe going any further was too embarrassing. Kotaro-san was trying to change the subject.
"Sakura's reason for awakening to her intuition, right... Sakura herself probably didn't realize she had the ability the moment it awakened, so pinning that down is hard..."
I glanced over at Goto.
"I see, I see. A case of stolen love, then..."
"I-I told you, it's not like that! Me and Sakura were together since we were little, so it wasn't anything like...!"
Smirking, Goto kept gouging at Kotaro-san's heart.
She didn't look like she suspected Kotaro-san much.
―Just then, the room's sliding door opened.
"Oooh-hohoho! Mifuyu Niimura has come to the Arata settlement for the first time in ages!"
"O-ohoho... Mifuyu... Niimura..."
A middle-aged woman dressed far too young for her age.
And an old lady in the same outfit, standing on the other side of the sliding door.
"How do I put it... This is hard to comment on... The choice of who to impersonate is just way too iffy..."
Haruka's expression was frozen.
Goto's face still wore the smirk from when she'd been listening to Kotaro-san.
No... her face had frozen mid-smirk.
I could see lots of ! and ? marks over Goto's head.
"Seeee? Look at this! Staying young forever is the secret to staying lively!"
"M-Mother! I've never struck a pose like that...!"
"Nngh!?"
When the grandmother fixed Obasan with a glare―
"Oho, s-see! I'm forever young!"
Obasan trembled as she struck the same pose as the grandmother.
For once, her voice had no spirit in it.
This really is... embarrassing...
Or rather, it wasn't landing with the audience at all.
Even Kana-chan was frozen by this impression with no visible purpose.
I can't put it well, but... it was a really pitiful situation, or a situation that does nobody any good...
No, maybe the grandmother was the only one getting anything out of it...
"Goto, please! Back up Mom!"
"Wait, this is definitely one of those where I bomb too! No way!"
"No, I'll definitely hype it up! I'll chime in!"
"Then hype it up right now!"
"Please! I'll treat you to anything you want at Zahha!"
"Th-that's dastardly...!"
Hesitation started flickering in Goto's eyes.
Luring Goto with food is the standard move among us.
"If you help Mom now, I bet Mom'll feed you something amazing too...!"
"Grrrr...! D-dastardly indeed...!"
At last Goto made up her mind and stood up.
The genius girl's weakness is surprisingly human.
"W-well, well, what have we here! A head-to-head showdown between the beautiful witches of Motoki-cho and the Arata settlement!? 　No wonder you drew so much attention at Comiket!"
Wait, Goto! Her popularity at Comiket is a landmine, in a way...!
"Ohh my, Goto-chan! Just what I'd expect from the genius girl! You've already heard rumors like that!"
"But, but, surely you can't do an impression of this genius girl!? 　Come on, come on, Senpai's mother, please step back!"
Goto pushed Obasan off into the corner.
"All right, Goto, nice!"
Haruka! You're forgetting to hype it up!
"Ohhohohohoho! Don't you underestimate me!"
With that, the grandmother spun around once.
"Sorry to keep you waiting! The genius girl, as everyone agrees, me included, Erika Goto, has arrived!"
In the blink of an eye, she had changed into Goto's outfit.
No, this outfit is even harder to take...
"Haruka, isn't this situation even worse...?"
"What're we supposed to do about this mood...!"
"Haruka, it's because you egged Goto on...!"
Goto's face had completely frozen.
Not good... this mood is really not good...!
"Hold it right there!
　Don't you go forgetting about this genius hunter girl, Kana Niimura!"
Just then, Kana-chan leapt into this pointless showdown.
Oh, whatever happens, happens...
"Well, well? You mean to challenge me, Erika Goto, the original genius hunter girl?"
"Only when I team up with the genius girl Eri-nee are we sisters perfect in mind, skill, and body! You've got no chance, one against two!"
Ah... so Kana-chan was trying to help Goto in her own way.
But every time somebody did something, the mood grew more chaotic.
I just... want to go home...
"Oho! Very well! Come at me, both of you at once!"
"Come on, let's go, Eri-nee!"
"R-right, little sister! Let us vanquish the evil spirit that infests this Arata settlement!"
Ugh... forget it.
I'll just eat...
