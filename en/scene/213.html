The Succession Rite

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-07 22:10 火 · 82 boxes

Moonlight lit up the settlement.
There were no streetlights, but I could see all the way past the mountains.
"Tomorrow, Shiyo 800, April 8, is a full moon. Tonight is just as bright."
But something about this view bothered me.
Something that should have been in this settlement wasn't there.
"Hey, Haruka. Why aren't there any cherry trees except at the Niimura main house?"
That's right: there was only one cherry tree in this settlement.
"Why was that again... I think somebody took almost all of them about 70 years ago. Some kind of group, apparently."
"70 years ago... But a group that wants cherry trees..."
"Apparently the cherry trees here were a rare variety."
So there was a reason there were no cherry trees.
"C'mon, never mind that, let's go. We're going into the forest, so don't get separated from me."
Following Haruka, we gradually made our way deep into the forest.
It seemed to be the opposite direction from the road we'd taken to get here―from Arata Station.
"You two, don't you dare get separated from me. There's a cliff a little further on. One slip and you're a goner in a second."
―Gradually, I began to hear the sound of water.
There was probably a mountain stream down there, with the cliff above it.
Come to think of it, I'd been told that Kotaro-san's wife died falling from a cliff...
"Hey, Haruka, did Kotaro-san's wife fall from that cliff?"
When I said that, Haruka stopped walking.
"Niimura-senpai, what's wrong?"
"You two... do you believe in curses?"
"Curses?"
"Curses?"
Could Kotaro-san's wife's accident have something to do with a curse?
Erika-chan had said something similar too.
Then Haruka silently started walking again.
We followed her in silence too.
Something was off about Haruka.
Was Kotaro-san's wife something I shouldn't have brought up?
"Senpai, what do you think?"
Goto asked in a low mutter, so Haruka wouldn't hear.
"Kotaro-san's wife's death... could it have something to do with a curse...?"
"It's way too unscientific, though... But in a settlement like this, I don't think it'd be strange for there to be stories like that."
"But even Haruka is saying it. 　I bet Obasan too..."
"It is a little eerie..."
Haruka, not noticing our conversation, walked slowly on.
The sound of the stream gradually grew louder.
Coming out of the forest, we reached a large clearing.
"Okay, we wait here."
Haruka sat down on one of the large rocks.
Even though the stream couldn't be seen from here, the sound of the river echoed through the clearing.
It seemed the steep edge of the clearing dropped off into the cliff.
I quietly went over toward the edge―
Just as I thought. Right beside the clearing was a cliff, and I could hear water from below.
It looked like quite a drop.
I leaned out just a little and peered over.
"What do you think you're doing, you idiot!"
Suddenly my arm was yanked, and there was Haruka, with a frightening look on her face.
"Um... sorry..."
"You know what happens if you slip, right?"
I'd only gone a little close to the cliff, and she was this angry.
"So this cliff really is where Kotaro-san's wife―"
"Well... I'll tell you later. C'mon, come over here."
Completely different from a moment ago, Haruka urged me on in a gentle voice.
I did as Haruka said and moved away from the cliff.
In the clearing stood a single stone pillar that looked like a gravestone.
All around it lay countless stones that looked like pieces of smashed gravestones.
Even the gravestone itself was far too crude to be called one― just a single stone about 1 meter tall, standing there.
"Haruka, this is a graveyard, right? Why is there only one gravestone?"
"Well, you'll see if you watch. Since you're here anyway, it's more fun going in without knowing anything."
"Niimura-senpai, that curse you mentioned earlier...?"
Goto finally brought it up.
I'd been wondering too, but seeing how Haruka was, it had been hard to ask.
"Kotaro-san's wife― Sakura-san was her name. It looks like somebody killed her..."
"What...!?"
Not an accident... she was killed?
"Sakura-san was from here in Arata. Kotaro-san was her childhood friend, and they got married right after high school. She was super kind, the kind of person who cared about others more than herself. She wasn't someone people would hold a grudge against."
Haruka's expression was very dark.
She seemed less afraid of the death than saddened by it.
Haruka must have been treated kindly by Sakura-san too.
"But how do you know she was murdered?"
But Haruka clammed up.
Was it something hard to talk about?
―Eventually, Haruka lifted her head.


Her head had been cut off...
Her head had been cut off?
But what about her dying from the fall off the cliff?
Something didn't add up...
"But it's true, Sakura-san had already fallen from the cliff. Apparently that alone was a fatal injury... and still, her head had been cut off."
"Did you see that, Haruka?"
"No way... The first to find her was Kotaro-san. They say there was no blood at all on top of the cliff. So it wasn't that her head was cut off and then she was pushed. Apparently she was pushed, and then her head was cut off."
"Senpai, what about the culprit? With a crime like that, the culprit has to be someone in the settlement, right!?"
"No, nobody was caught..."
"Why not!? What did the police say!? If they'd checked who didn't have an alibi at the time, they'd have found the culprit right away...!"
"Everyone had an alibi... At first, Sakura-san was seen by lots of people in the settlement, running off somewhere in tears. The first to find the body, Kotaro-san, was one of them too. So they split up and went after Sakura-san... and then..."
"You mean the body of someone who died from the fall had its head cut off?"
"Yeah..."
In other words, everyone had an alibi, and at first glance it was an accidental death, or a suicide.
But because her head had been cut off, it became a baffling murder case.
Hence, a curse.
"Grandma wanted it that way, so they didn't call the police. She never told me why, though."
"But that's..."
"I was first told it was murder about 4 years ago. I've been thinking about it a lot since then... Grandma pulling strings behind the scenes and killing Sakura-san... I wondered if that's what happened... But..."
"But...?"
Just then, footsteps echoed toward us.
It seemed either Erika-chan or Chiga-nee had arrived.
"............!!?"
But the one who appeared there was―
A strange figure in red robes and a Noh mask, carrying a nata and a mace.
Who is this...!
"Natsumi, no need to freak out. That's Chiga-nee."
"Huh...?"
The figure stared at us for a while, then walked over toward the gravestone.

She didn't seem to mean us any harm.
"That scared me..."
"By the way, Grandma's gonna show up dressed like that too."
"Come on... tell me things like that first..."
"Sorry, sorry! Huh, so this stuff really is unique to Arata... I didn't know that till recently..."
Of course anyone would be startled by someone decked out with weapons and a Noh mask.
Then I heard another set of footsteps.
The person who appeared wore black robes and an old woman's Noh mask, different from Chiga-nee's.
This one wasn't holding anything.
This one is supposedly Erika-chan, but it's still scary...
"Here we go, it's starting. Make sure your phones won't make any noise."
I took out my phone and checked that it was on silent mode.
Out this far, it seemed there was no signal at all.
"Bem-vindo, novo bruxa." [TN: Portuguese in the original, kept as printed.]
Erika-chan spoke words in a language I couldn't place.
Yes, from the voice it was clearly Erika-chan.
Then Chiga-nee bowed deeply to Erika-chan.
"Vamos, Destrua a bruxa morta."
When Erika-chan chanted something, Chiga-nee turned toward the gravestone and swung the mace high overhead.
Chiga-nee went on smashing the gravestone apart with the mace, without hesitation.
The sound of the stream was drowned out by the blows, and I could no longer hear it.
I just watched as the gravestone was mercilessly smashed to pieces.
―Eventually, the gravestone was reduced to something no different from the stones around it.
"Return, novo bruxa."
At Erika-chan's command, Chiga-nee disappeared into the forest without looking our way.

Erika-chan watched her go, then walked over to us.
"Whew. Done, done."
When she took off the Noh mask and hood, underneath was Erika-chan's face, sweat beading on her forehead.
"This outfit really is hot. Maybe I'll switch to something a bit thinner."
"Hey, Grandma, is this okay? You're not supposed to join up with us till we're home..."
"Well, let's not be so stiff about it!"
"Geez... the head of Arata sure takes it easy..."
"Well, call it the changing times."
―We got up and went back the way we'd come.
"Natsumi-chan, you girls got to see something pretty rare, didn't you?"
"That thing... what does it mean?"
"They say it means freeing the souls of the dead from under the ground and returning them to heaven. Something about a great being sleeping underground."
"What Erika-chan was speaking earlier, um... was it Portuguese?"
"Oh! Just what I'd expect from a genius girl! That's right! 　Seems a lot of the Arata settlement's ancestors were Westerners who fled here. Western culture's been worked into all sorts of rites. What I said earlier was simple stuff, like 'Destroy the gravestone' and 'Become a witch.'"
I remembered what Haruka had told us earlier.
When Sakura-san died, Erika-chan hadn't reported it to the police.
In other words, she covered the case up.
"Hey, Grandma... it's about time you told me. What happened to Sakura-san..."
"If you're bringing it up here, then Natsumi-chan and the others must know too. Well, that's fine. I was planning to tell Natsumi-chan and the others anyway."
She'd been planning to tell us?
"Once we get home, I'll change and come to the living room. Wait until then."
"Okay..."

Something Erika-chan wanted to tell us.
Does that mean it's something we ought to know?
When we entered the living room, Haruka quickly made us some tea.
Only the three of us were there.
Wasn't Chiga-nee back yet?
"Haruka... should we really be hearing about how Sakura-san died...? 　It's a little scary..."
"I don't know either... What I told you earlier is all I know... oh, I forgot to tell you the rest. Grandma's the culprit, I thought, but... that's where I left off, right?"
"Yeah..."
Haruka placed a photograph on the table.
A woman was looking our way, smiling.
"This is Sakura-san. I think I heard this photo was taken on the way home from her high school graduation."
Her hair was a translucent brown, close to blond.
Clear-cut features.
Skin as white as pearl.
And both eyes blue.
Unlike Chiga-nee and her sister, Sakura-san didn't seem to have odd eyes.
"She's so beautiful. Right, she's Chiga-nee and Kana-chan's mom, after all."
"She looks like this, but she was Japanese through and through. There were lots of white people among the ancestors, so every so often somebody like her gets born, I hear."
A gentle smile, like glass.
Even though it was only a photo, I felt like I'd be drawn into Sakura-san's smile.
"They say this is the only one taken naturally, apart from posed commemorative photos. Sakura-san apparently hated how she looked..."
"Even though she's this pretty?"
"Seems lots of the girls at school were jealous of it. She got bullied because of her looks, which didn't seem Japanese. Sakura-san was timid, or rather, she was just too gentle and kind..."
Bullied because of her beauty and her kind nature.
It's so unfair. Just hearing about it makes me feel sick.
"Sakura-san died 10 years ago―on April 17, Shiyo 790. They say almost all the cherry blossoms in Arata had already fallen by then. From here on, I'll tell you what I actually saw in Arata when I was 7."
