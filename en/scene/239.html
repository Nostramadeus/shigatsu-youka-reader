Branch

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-07 23:50 火 · 68 boxes

Before I knew it, Haruka was breathing softly in her sleep.
No wonder, after today's hard schedule.
If I let my guard down, I'd fall asleep too.
"Eri-nee... are you there?"
"I'm right here. What's wrong?"
"Really... is Daddy the culprit...?"
"...No, he isn't. So don't worry."
Goto was lying beside Kana-chan.
Just now, Goto had clearly said 'No.'
Even though, as far as I knew, she had told Kana-chan earlier that Kotaro-san was the culprit.
"Mm... I'm coming in."
"Mm... So Haruka and Kana fell asleep."
Chiga-nee seemed to have sobered up completely.
The succession rite, or whatever it was, must have put her in a serious frame of mind as well.
"Mm... Natsumi-chan, do you have a moment?"
"Me?"
When I glanced over at Goto―
"Senpai, go ahead. I'll stay with Kana-chan a little longer."
When I went down to the living room, Obaasan was there too.
"Sorry, Natsumi-chan. Were you asleep?"
"No, I've been thinking things over this whole time..."
"Hmm... Sounds like you heard a lot from Shirozaki. A little while ago, Shirozaki came by here."
"Shirozaki-san did?"
"He asked me to give this to you, Natsumi-chan."
Set on the table was a cherry branch bearing many blossoms.
"Apparently he helped himself to a cherry tree nearby while you were shopping at the convenience store, Natsumi-chan. But he went back and forth a long time over handing it to you, he said, wondering if it would mean lending a hand to a witch. But Shirozaki made up his mind. That he'd trust you, Natsumi-chan..."
I picked up the cherry branch.
It wasn't an imitation. It was a real cherry branch.
I felt the warmth particular to wood, and a scent of cherry blossoms that the human sense of smell normally can't detect drifted faintly through my nose.
"Shirozaki, too... lost his wife to the curse-killings..."
"To the curse-killings...?"
"Shirozaki hates the culprit more than anyone else in this settlement. No... he must hate himself too. Maybe he's grown sick of a life where he alone goes on living without a care. That's why Shirozaki must want to catch them no matter what."
So that was it.
If he once accepted me as a witch, Shirozaki-san would hate me, and then, sooner or later, the intent to kill.
So that he wouldn't be driven by that impulse, he had been refusing to listen to any talk of witches.
And then, to hold himself back, Shirozaki-san had left a knife with me...
At last, all of Shirozaki-san's actions made sense.
I had words that Goto had entrusted to me.
'The culprit being unknown is the background,' she had said.
In my own reading, this means not a single person knows the truth.
In other words, there is no culprit.
"Goto told me! 'The culprit being unknown is the background,' she said! 　What does that mean?"
Then Obaasan looked down and fell silent.
If she had only gone silent, it would probably have meant 'What is this nonsense?'...
But looking down meant something must have come to her mind.
"In this incident... there's no such thing as a 'true culprit.' Everyone's a victim, and everyone's a suspect. That's all. Suspicion feeds on itself, and people kill their neighbors. That's all..."
"Wait a minute! Then you're saying you've turned a blind eye to it all this time!? 　The head of the settlement, forgiven for something like that? Is that what you think!?"
"That's right, it's nothing that can be forgiven... No one knows who did the killing. That leaves only one conclusion: I can only think that, with suspicion feeding on itself, people lay hands on whoever seems suspicious. Everyone comes up with reasons and hunts for the culprit, but deep down they must know. 'This time, I might be the one who kills.' I didn't have the nerve to hand the ones who got scared and lashed out over to the police."
"Then...! If everyone kept watching each other the whole time...!"
"Then it would have to be all year, not just April, or there'd be no point. Keeping watch on everyone 24 hours a day, 365 days a year... that's asking too much."
"Then... the only thing left is to break up the settlement..."
"Yes, I've said that too, at times. But nobody will leave this place. To the people of Arata, this land weighs more than their lives."
I remembered what Shirozaki-san had said.
For people rooted in a place like this, the place where they were born and raised matters more than their lives.
Depending on the country or region, it's even an asset that starts wars and gets fought over.
If everyone is staying on this land because they want to, then isn't it impossible to solve the curse-killings in the first place?
"Mm... Natsumi-chan, if you're going to blame Grandma, then I'm just as guilty. I haven't been able to do anything either. I should have just reported it to the police in secret or something... But I thought Dad might be the culprit... so I just couldn't..."
I admit that, because it's such a small settlement, its people have lived like one family, and that under the motto 'One for all, all for one,' it has lasted for hundreds of years until now.
But that's wrong...!
They must have known that leaving it alone would only lead to something even more horrible...!
"Mm... Grandma, tomorrow, once the thirteenth-year memorial service is over, let's go to the police. All right?" [TN: Buddhist memorials count the year of death as the first year.]
"............"
"Mm... It has to end now."
"Yes... You're right. Let's do that..."
"But there must have been something that set it off, right? 　The first person killed in the curse-killings was Shirozaki-san's wife. What on earth happened? Was it because Sakura-san died?"
"If I had to say, Sakura was as good as killed by the rule. Maybe there was someone who tried to change such a behind-the-times settlement in a ruinous way. That person cut off Sakura's head and set off the next year's curse-killings. The rest, Natsumi-chan, is what you heard from Shirozaki. Distrust breeds distrust, and people get killed as if by Sakura's curse. Sakura's head was cut off by someone who may already have been curse-killed, though..."
That's ridiculous! All it would take is avoiding the curse-killing just once...!
To begin with, does distrust really chain together so conveniently that someone gets killed every year?
Residents possessed by the 'cursed thinking' Shirozaki-san talked about might think so.
But to me, a chain of murders that neat doesn't feel real.
"For that, I need your power, Natsumi-chan... You can hear the voices of the cherry trees, Natsumi-chan. If you could just hear how to avoid the curse-killings... But what I didn't count on is that the cherry tree in the garden can't get its voice to you. That's why Shirozaki... this."
The cherry branch Shirozaki-san had delivered―
Everything depends on this and my intuition.
If I can find a way for everyone to avoid danger, there won't be any more curse-killings.
Even the culprits who did the curse-killings don't know who the culprits of the other years were.
No one knows who will be the next culprit.
Caught in suspicion feeding on itself, killing others to protect your own life―
The curse-killing: through cursed thinking, peace can come only from killing someone else.
And the curse called attachment to this land is one of its causes too.
―When I looked at the clock, it had already gone past midnight.
April 8―the anniversary of Ojisan's death.
"Bring that cherry branch to the memorial service tomorrow. Everyone should be gathered there, so you should be able to tell who'll be in danger next."
"All right... I hope the wind blows just right, though..."
"Haah..."
Obaasan's shoulders slumped heavily.
"Mm... Grandma, are you all right?"
"Ah... I feel like it's finally going to end... Finally, with this..."
I sat down beside Obaasan and rubbed her back.
"I'm... not sure I can really be any help, but... I'll do my best. Haruka's grandma is in trouble, so I really can't just stay quiet."
This person is Haruka's grandma.
If Obaasan is in trouble, Haruka is in trouble too.
I can't just leave that alone.
"Heh... Natsumi-chan, you're so kind. Haruka has a good friend. I wanted a friend like Natsumi-chan too..."
Obaasan's face was the gentlest and kindest it had ever been.
I couldn't believe she was lying.
I had no grounds for it.
Needing grounds to trust someone is exactly what's ridiculous.
"Even starting now... let's be friends?"
"Nheh-heh-heh-heh! Are you sure about that? Having a cosplay hag like me for a friend will be all kinds of trouble."
"Ahahaha! That impression of Obasan was pretty close! 　Did you practice a lot after all?"
"For this day, I practiced that pose in front of the mirror for a week. Sure enough, it got to my back."
I imagined Obaasan practicing poses in front of the mirror.
"Uheh-heh-heh! Ahahaha!"
The picture was so surreal that I burst out laughing.
Had she practiced Goto's pose the same way, too?
That's not something I really want to picture...
"Mm... Grandma, how nice for you. You've made a young friend."
"Ha ha ha! Now I'll suck up Natsumi-chan's youth and stay lively for a long while yet! 　Chigaya! I'm going to the next Comiket too!"
"Whaat!? I have to go again too!?"
"Of course! You'd make an old woman walk through a jam-packed place like that alone? 　Oh, how about you, Natsumi-chan!? It's fun!"
"Huh!? Um, well... Go-go-go-Goto might like that kind of thing more... Ahahaha...!"
If there were no curse-killings, everyone in the settlement could live happily.
Whether that comes true or not depends on me.
Can I really hear any voice with just a branch like this?
Besides... there are definitely people in this settlement who have committed murder.
However much suspicion fed on itself...
But Obaasan probably has no intention of catching them.
Rather, how to rebuild this settlement―
That must matter to her more than anything.
Goto would surely never forgive the culprit and would hand them over to the police, but...
"Well then, Natsumi-chan, it starts at 10 a.m. tomorrow, so don't be late."
"Yes. No... Got it! Erika-chan!"
When I answered the way I'd talk to someone my own age, Obaasan's expression grew even gentler.
Chiga-nee looked very happy too.
―I headed for the room on the second floor.
Come to think of it... I hadn't taken a bath yet...
In the second-floor hallway, Haruka was standing there.
"You were awake..."
"Yeah, since you left the room, Natsumi. Um... thanks."
"Were you watching?"
"No, just listening. Grandma's usually like that, but she must've been tormented by the curse-killings for years... Natsumi, I bet you coming here makes her feel a whole lot stronger."
"I hope I can be even a little help..."
"Just getting Grandma to feel that way made it worth coming."
If just my coming had encouraged her, then of course that made me happy.
"Natsumi, wanna go take a bath? You haven't yet, right?"
"Yeah, I'll go, I'll go!"
"After the bath, let's just go to sleep. Way too much stuff wore me out today..."
"Really... Goto's new-student speech this morning feels like it was ages ago."
"Haha, I forgot all about it too!"
"Haah... We moved around so much today, the bath feels great."
"Seriously. It's the best!"
Haruka and I let our tired bodies go limp in the hot water.
Nothing seemed about to happen so far, but we hadn't analyzed that vision I'd seen at the convenience store.
Even without Goto, could Haruka and I manage it, just the two of us?
"Goto ended up crashing without a bath, huh."
"She even got sent out hunting, so of course..."
............ Hunting...?
Now that I thought about it, Erika-chan sending Goto out hunting felt a little off...
The mood at the time was that we were heading out hunting on impulse, but once I calmed down and thought about it, sending a complete amateur like Goto out hunting was a little strange.
Was there some other purpose...?
No... I'll stop thinking like this on my own...
I have to let this bath wash away my cursed thinking too.
"Hey, we still haven't gone over the thing I saw at the convenience store, right?"
"Right, right. More than whatever Shirozaki-san said, that's what I'm curious about. Natsumi, your intuition beats Shirozaki-san's story when it comes to credibility..."
With that, Haruka turned her face toward me.
"Hey, Natsumi, were the people you saw really Kotaro-san and Sakura-san?"
"Huh?"
"It's just, Natsumi, you've probably never met Sakura-san. Kotaro-san's who you thought it was, but it could actually be my dad, right?"
I see. They were identical twins, so if it wasn't Kotaro-san but Ojisan...
"If my dad and Sakura-san were in that cave... what were they doing with that pot...?"
"I guess what bothers me is that the person who looked like Sakura-san ended up covered in blood... If Sakura-san and Ojisan were together..."
"That's it! You mean that's what gave Sakura-san that intuition!?"
"Even if that's so... why would being splashed with Ojisan's blood give someone that kind of power...?"
"I don't know about that... My dad might've had, like... some kind of special power... But I've never heard that Dad had intuition like yours, Natsumi..."
"Yeah... Hey, the pot gets used at the memorial service too, right?"
"It's true, like you said, Natsumi, we do use a tool like a pot set on a tripod. But as for it meaning anything special..."
I feel like our deductions have gotten pretty close.
But at this point, we can't state the cause and effect for certain.
"Wait a sec... Then does that mean Kotaro-san isn't really that involved in the curse-killings after all? 　Natsumi, you thought the person in that vision was Kotaro-san, so that's why you figured he's still tied to the curse-killings, right?"
"Hmm... yeah, but..."
"Natsumi, after the memorial service tomorrow, wanna go check out that place?"
"The cave Goto and Kana-chan were in today?"
"Yeah. If we look into it, we might find something out."
"Hmm, but in the end, the curse-killings were caused by the residents' suspicion feeding on itself, right? 　There's no need to dig any further..."
"That's just what Grandma thinks, isn't it? 　Something about it just doesn't sit right with me."
It's true that it's unnatural for it to go on every year, so conveniently one person at a time, and with the culprit unknown.
Under that theory, I can't imagine the residents would tell the truth even if they were questioned.
The culprit is different every year, and as long as nobody knows who was the culprit in which year, they can talk their way out of anything.
Even Erika-chan must have done at least that much questioning.
But on the other hand, if nobody is the culprit, then nobody will come forward and say they're the culprit.
So either way, there's no getting testimony from the residents.
I can't accept 'the theory that everyone killed each other out of suspicion feeding on itself' either...
Like Haruka said, let's look into that cave.
We'll set the suspicion theory aside and gather the information all over again.
"Okay. Let's look into the cave."
"Right. Instead of after the memorial service, let's check it out first thing in the morning."
"Yeah, the sooner the better. The memorial service starts at 10 a.m., so... if we leave the house at 6 a.m., we'll make it in time for the thirteenth-year memorial service, right?"
"Okay, let's get Goto dragged out of bed before 6 a.m. too."
"What about Kana-chan? She's been glued to Goto the whole time..."
"She can come or not, doesn't matter. If she wants to come, fine."
Even the idea that the residents are killing each other makes sense to a degree.
But we decided to look for a conclusion different from Erika-chan's.
What was in that cave―
