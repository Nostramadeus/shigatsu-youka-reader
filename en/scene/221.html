Those Involved 10 Years Ago

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-07 23:00 火 · 39 boxes

―After our baths, we went back to the second-floor room.
At Haruka's suggestion, we decided to go over everything once more before bed.
Once tomorrow's thirteenth-year memorial service was over, the police would finally be getting involved. [TN: Buddhist memorials count the year of death as the first year.]
If only we could turn up some useful information before then...
"Haruka, how many people live in Arata now?"
"Probably fewer than 20... 10 people died in 10 years. At this rate, the Arata settlement itself is gonna disappear..."
If the curse-killings kept repeating like this, the Arata settlement would vanish in less than 20 years.
Could it be that the culprit's goal is...
"Haruka, I figured out the culprit's goal! The culprit must want to get rid of the Arata settlement!"
"No way... What on earth for..."
Only the culprit would know that, but...
At this rate, the Arata settlement will be wiped out for sure.
"But Senpai, in that case, wouldn't it be quicker to just kill everyone off?"
"Well, that's true, but... Maybe the culprit wants to kill as few people as possible? 　Limiting it to just one person a year is the bare minimum for the culprit, too. Maybe they're waiting for everyone to move away..."
"That's... true... It's exactly as you say, but... How do I put it, something about it bugs me."
Goto didn't seem able to wholeheartedly agree with my idea.
"If that's the case, isn't there an easier way? 　For example, killing off all the settlement's crops, or destroying all the tools they need for hunting... Or burning down all the houses. That way nobody gets killed, and it doesn't need years, does it? The point is to make the place hard to live in."
But Goto's argument didn't sit right with me either.
The trigger was probably the Sakura-san incident, but the curse-killing cases started from there.
For some reason, the culprit must have decided to make it look like Sakura-san's curse.
If so, then killing really is what makes sense for the culprit.
"Why did the culprit try to make it look like Sakura-san's curse... Now some people apparently really believe it's a curse, but at first, wouldn't they have thought a living person did the killing?"
"Hmm... It's not certain yet that the culprit is trying to make it look like a curse, either..."
"I know! Natsumi, how about asking your parents!? 　Natsumi got invited because of everything that happened in Megasawa, right? 　Maybe they know something!"
"But Niimura-senpai, if we tell them something like that, won't they get worried and say 'Come home right away' or something?"
"Come home now? That's impossible this late at night, right? Either way, it'd have to wait till tomorrow..."
Haruka was right about this too.
Besides, the reason I came here in the first place was―
"Okay. I'll call them."
"Good..."
"Be smart about how you put it, okay?"
It was late, but would they pick up?
"Hello? Natsumi, is that you?"
Surprisingly, he picked up right away.
Dad, who usually had his evening drink by now, sounded remarkably sharp.
Had he listened when I told him to watch his drinking?
"Sorry for calling so late. There's something I wanted to ask... Haruka and the others are listening too, so can I switch to speakerphone?"
"Okay. Hang on a sec."
Then I heard the sound of paper being readied on the other end of the line.
He was strangely well prepared.
As if he'd known I was going to call...
Oh, right, I'd said 'there might be danger,' so maybe he was on his guard.
"Okay, go ahead."
"Yeah, so―"
I told Dad everything I knew, and asked whether there was anything I'd missed.
I had him check that for me.
I managed to finish my questions while telling him only about what happened in Megasawa, without touching on the curse-killing cases.
But Dad asked surprisingly few questions.
"It's true that Mifuyu-san was sick and threatened us into killing two children. The instructions and the weapons and all that were prepared by Mifuyu-san too."
I don't know about the instructions, but the weapons must be the ones Sakura-san prepared.
What among all this had we overlooked...
"Um, this is Goto. Were the instructions and the weapons the only things you were given?"
"Hmm... No, that's not it. We were also given Noh masks and clothes like red robes. So nobody could make out our faces or builds."
It was safe to assume those were the same outfits I'd seen Chiga-nee and the others wearing at the ritual earlier.
Since they'd been sent from Arata, that shouldn't be much of a problem.
"Wait a second, please! 　You were given Noh masks? 　That's really strange, isn't it? If you were wearing something like that and someone saw you, wouldn't it give away that you were from the Arata settlement!?"
True, this would be almost the same as revealing who you were.
Besides, a Noh mask is pretty conspicuous for hiding your face.
"I get it... Sakura-san sent stuff like that on purpose, to stop Mom from going through with it. But Mom's not that dumb either, right? 　If she got caught dressed like that, it'd make trouble for the Arata settlement..."
"Then isn't sending weapons a contradiction? 　There's no need to wear the Noh masks and robes, either."
"No, wait, there's more. Something else came in there with them... Actually... we were given a drug too."
"A drug...?"
"Yeah. You inject it with a syringe, and supposedly it lets you move in superhuman ways. It takes the brain's limiter off so you can use 100% of your muscle strength, or something..."
The 'drugs' Erika-chan had mentioned.
If that was the drug, it had probably been made in Arata.
But she'd said it disappeared a long time ago...
"Goto, if a person put out 100% of their strength, what would happen?"
"Let's see... According to one theory, people can only use about 20% of their muscle strength. In an emergency, that limiter supposedly loosens; that's the so-called strength you only find in an emergency. Then, supposedly, a woman can show enough strength to lift a car. So far beyond what we saw Kana-chan do earlier that there's no comparison..."
"Seriously...? Then you could move without anybody seeing you and pull off crimes like people getting spirited away?"
"No... I don't think that's possible. If you did that, your bones and muscles would break down in an instant. They say that limiter is there to protect the body, too..."
"Actually, there were written directions on how to use that drug too. Moving at full power is dangerous, so move only a little, at 80% at most."
There were instructions that detailed...?
So Sakura-san really did mean to make Obasan commit murder―
But from everything Haruka had told us, she didn't seem like that kind of person at all.
Beforehand, Obasan had asked Sakura-san to help with the murders, and been refused.
And yet she sent Obasan the tools for murder? That's far too big a change of heart for Sakura-san.
Besides, if there was a drug like that, there'd be ways to disguise yourself without dressing like the Arata settlement.
Did Sakura-san send them for some other purpose?
Then the one who prepared the instructions and the drug was―
"What's with you people! 　Akane, run, quick!"
"Ryoji-kun, no! They've got the back surrounded too!"
Suddenly I heard Dad and Mom screaming.
"Natsumi! Get out of there, quick! Get out of Arata right away!"
"Dad, what's wrong!? Dad, hey!"
But instead of an answer came the sound of nata and blunt weapons hacking flesh apart and smashing it.
That was when I heard it.
In voices I didn't know...
"Chibana's revenge...!"
"Sakura-chan... how dare you...!"
Everything went white before my eyes, and I couldn't think at all.
Dad and Mom, back in Motoki-cho, were... killed...?
"Natsumi, hang in there! Goto, go get Grandma!"
"Y-yes!"
A small figure―Goto, it seemed, went out of the room.
But I didn't have the strength left to follow it with my eyes.
"Dad and... Mom...!"
"It's okay, Natsumi! I'm right here!"
"What!? What's going on!?"
Goto had brought Erika-chan.
Chiga-nee and Obasan came along too.
"On the phone... Natsumi's parents...!"
Haruka pointed at the phone, and Erika-chan picked it up.
"Hey! Is somebody there!? Are you one of Arata's people!? Answer me!"
Just then, Kotaro-san and Kana-chan showed up.
"Geez, what's all the commotion..."
"You gotta be quiet at night, or Grandma'll get mad at you, won't she? 　Wait, the one making a racket is Grandma..."
"Grand Witch-sama. The culprit behind the curse-killing cases has been dealt with."
That was the voice that came back over the phone.
Dad and Mom were the culprits behind the curse-killing cases...?
What on earth is going on...
"Who is this!? Who did this!?"
"............ ............"
But there was no answer.
Instead, I heard the phone's busy signal.
"Kotaro, that voice just now..."
"Yeah... Shirozaki-san, no doubt about it...!"
