Spattered Bloodstains

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-08 14:00 水 · 86 boxes

The fluorescent light was dazzling―
"Natsumi! Are you okay!? Do you know who I am?"
A girl with short hair was shouting.
"Haruka...?"
"Oh, thank goodness...! Really, thank goodness!"
It was Haruka. Haruka was calling to me.
"Senpai! Can you hear me?"
"Goto...?"
Goto was there too.
Where am I?
I have a feeling I was dreaming just now.
Wandering on and on, alone, through some place I didn't know...
No, I was talking with someone.
But... I can't remember who it was.
I clearly remember the two of them calling me back.
"Haruka... Goto... You were holding my hand the whole time, weren't you? 　You were calling me, right?"
"Yeah! I held your hand, and I called out to you too!"
"Uuu! You came back to life!"
That's right. Even after taking an attack like that, I'm alive.
The wound on Haruka's forehead was still raw, so no more than a few hours could have passed since then.
"Haruka... what happened after that? 　Ngh!"
When I moved, the left side of my chest gave a sharp throb of pain.
"Hey, I told you, no. Don't go moving around."
Looking closer, I saw that this was a hospital room.
I was lying on a bed.
"Hey, tell me. What happened?"
"Yeah. So, at that moment..."
"Ha-haa, what a scary face. Honestly, what an annoying brat!"
"Damn...!"
At that moment, I couldn't do anything but watch the nata swing down.
A loud metallic clang that didn't sound like a person being cut, and a few drops of red liquid that spattered onto my face.
When I opened my eyes, Natsumi was lying collapsed in front of me.
There was a faint cut across her chest, but for all the force of that nata, the wound was shallow.
"Damn...! It's no good if you die!"
"Senpai! Over here, quick!"
Before I knew it, Goto had opened the barred door.
"R-right!"
We hurriedly dragged Natsumi through the door.
"W-wait right there!"
We quickly shut it and locked it before the rattled Kotaro-san could get to it.
"Damn! Why!? I had the key...!"
Kotaro-san looked at the key he was holding.
"You little brat! You swapped them!"
Just then,
"Can you hear me, all of you!? It's all right now!"
It was Grandma's voice.
We headed for the entrance, carrying Natsumi and Kana.
"Wait right there! I'll smash this door down!"

Kotaro-san started hacking at the battered door with the nata.
The door grew more and more warped.
Bits of the crumbling ceiling began to patter down.

"Hey, I said stop! There's gonna be a cave-in!"
"Shut up, shut up, shut up!"
"Wh-what is this...!"
Suddenly, with a great rumble of the earth, the cave shook violently.
Something like a huge pillar came falling from above.
I thought it was a rock, but it wasn't.
It was a big tree.
And the tree right above this spot would be― No way... the cherry tree at the Niimura main house!?
"Ngh...! Damn! Stop it, Eiichiro! Let go...!"
Kotaro-san had fallen to the ground and was pinned down by the tree's roots, most of them cut short. He couldn't move at all.
The timing was far too good for a coincidence.
No, this place was prone to cave-ins to begin with, and then there was that shaking just now.
The tree had lost its roots to make Droga, and it looked like the cherry tree had finally given out.
"Goto, you carry Kana!"
"Y-yes!"
"Grandma! Pull Natsumi and Kana up! Hurry!"
"Daaamn!!! Wait...! 　Wait!!"
We ignored Kotaro-san's voice and headed for the exit.
When we got outside, Mom was sitting slumped on the ground, out of breath.
Grandma didn't seem to be badly hurt either.
Near them, about 10 residents lay collapsed, unable to move.
"Grandma, did you do all this by yourself?"
"Chigaya held them up for a little while at the entrance to the settlement. She cut at their legs to slow them down."
"Where's Chiga-nee now?"
"She went back to the entrance. Said a few of them went down at the entrance."
"I see. So Chiga-nee's safe too."
"Most of all... these people are fools. The side effects of Droga got them."
"Side effects...?"
"It pushes human muscle to its absolute limit. When Droga starts to wear off, all the screaming the body held back until then comes at you as pain. By the time they got here, they were already battered all over. Torn muscles all over their bodies, most likely."
"None of them are gonna die or anything, right...?"
"No need to worry about that. We didn't go for any vital spots. But Kana surely wouldn't have shown these people any mercy, so I sent her underground with you."
"I get it... Kana just might do that... Oh, more importantly, Grandma! Natsumi...! Kotaro-san slashed her with the nata!"
Natsumi was still limp.
"Her wound looks awfully light for that, though..."
Then Grandma thrust her hand into the front of Natsumi's clothes.
"I see. Looks like I was of some use too."
What Grandma pulled out was the Grand Witch's Noh mask that Natsumi had tucked into the front of her clothes.
The hard, sturdy metal Noh mask bore a large gash from the nata.
"But it looks like her heart took a strong impact. We can't be too optimistic."
"Seriously...! Can't you do something!?"
"I'll give her first aid, so get an ambulance, quick!"
"G-got it!"
Using Mom's car, we took Natsumi and Kana down and met the ambulance at Arata Station.
At the same time, the police entered the settlement too, and the other residents, still unable to move, were taken away for attempted murder.
Before long, Kotaro-san was rescued from underground too.
"Eiichiro, he... Eiichiro, he..."
His body had apparently ended up right in a gap between the roots, and he didn't seem badly hurt.
But Kotaro-san, pale and trembling the whole time, kept repeating Dad's name in a mutter.
"What's the meaning of this!? 　Sakura! Did you betray me!? 　I did it for you, all this time, all this time for you...! 　Damn! You monsters! 　Don't you forget this, all of you! Don't forget!"
Was he delirious? He was railing at someone, spitting out abuse that made no sense.
Kotaro-san's state of mind probably wasn't sound anymore either.
"So yeah, that's how it went. A whole lot happened, but this year we pulled it off and stopped the curse-killing!"
"You shielding Niimura-senpai was actually a good thing too, in a way, I think. Kotaro-san didn't seem to want to kill you, so he probably eased up right before the blow."
True, one wrong step and there would have been a mass murder.
But a whole string of coincidences piled up, and not a single person died.
Then Haruka gently hugged me.
"Natsumi...! Really, thank you! 　If you hadn't shielded me, I'd definitely be dead."
Why did my body move on its own back then?
Of course, I don't want Haruka to die.
But suddenly, a scene from long ago came to mind.
A scene of Haruka shielding me from someone―
"Haruka, that thing Kotaro-san said about how we're related..."
"Hmm, well, it does bug me a little. But I'm fine either way. I love my mom, the one named Mifuyu Niimura, and whether you're my sister or not, nothing changes from how it's always been."
"Me... I'd be a little happy. If you were my older sister..."
"Hey now, that's embarrassing. Even if we really are sisters, don't go calling me 'Older Sister'!"
"Got it, Older Sister!"
"Hey!"
For a little while, the three of us laughed together.
"And also, also, this genius girl's key-swapping trick paid off too, didn't it! 　I swapped the key Kana-chan was holding for my own house key!"
"Ahh, so that's what it was! If it came down to it, you were planning to lock Kotaro-san in there. Yeah, without that, we'd all have been done for..."
That's right. Because everyone worked together, we were able to get through this tragedy.
"Hey, is there no way to see Erika-chan? I guess she's busy right now, isn't she?"
"Grandma's probably gonna be held by the police for a while..."
"I see... I was saved thanks to the Noh mask, so I want to thank her properly. This coming Golden Week... would that be too soon?"
"Then let's go again over summer vacation or something! Chiga-nee'll probably be leaving to study abroad around then, so it'll double as her farewell party. I bet Grandma'll be happy too! 　Okay, then our summer vacation plans are set!"
A fresh spring breeze was blowing in through the window.
I could no longer sense any danger at all.
No... even if there were danger, I felt like I wouldn't hear anything anymore.
In the dream I'd had earlier, it seemed someone had told me something like that...
Memories of dreams get hazier the more time passes, until eventually you forget them. But this dream alone, I would soon be able to remember clearly― that was how it felt.
"Ah! Found you, found you!"
Suddenly a small figure burst into the hospital room.
"Natsumi-chan, you got better!"
Kana-chan spoke to me, full of energy.
"Yeah! I'm fine! 　Did you get hurt?"
"Look, look! See? The checkup found nothing wrong either. Healthy, healthy!"
Even though her own dad, Kotaro-san, did something like that, she's holding up really well...
"Ohh, little sister! Safe and sound is all that matters!"
"Ohh, Eri-nee! I'm a healthy, bright and bouncy 10-year-old!"
Goto's expression was a smile that seemed just a little bit forced.
She must want to cheer Kana-chan up, even a little.
"Aww, man! So Daddy was the culprit after all... And I went and got strong for Daddy's sake and everything..."
"Um..."
"But listen, I still don't think Daddy killed Mommy, okay? 　'Cause Daddy always left flowers for Mommy!"
"I see... Then Kotaro-san must not be the culprit, right?"
"Yeah! Daddy always picks daphne flowers around this time of year! To leave for Mommy, he says!"
"Daphne... Yeah...
Yeah! I'm sure that's it! Kotaro-san can't be the culprit!"
"Then Daddy'll come back soon, right!?"
"Of course! Your genius-girl big sister here will catch the culprit properly, just you watch!"
I'm not sure why, but as she cheered along with Kana-chan, Goto's face seemed to cloud over even more.
I don't know what in that conversation was strange, though...
Goto says she'll catch the real culprit, but to catch the culprit is― to know the truth.
Why is that...? I feel a resistance to knowing the truth.
Knowing the truth is a very sad thing.
"Hey, Goto?"
"Yes?"
"The truth is fine and all, but... right now... right now... let's just think about happy things, okay? 　The truth... there are times it's better not to know it."
"Senpai...
You're right! Right now I'm just happy everyone's safe!"
Goto paused for a moment, then grinned.
What was it I saw in that dream earlier...?
A very important promise―
A promise with someone, which I've remembered just now, only a tiny bit.
A promise that I 'must not uncover the truth'―
That much alone came back to me, vaguely.
