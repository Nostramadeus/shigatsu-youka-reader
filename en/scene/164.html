Genius's Agitation

Jusatsu Arc [Killing by Curse] · Shiyo 800-04-08 20:00 水 · 21 boxes

Kogori-senpai: I'll try sending her a message.
She must be worn down mentally, and I hope it encourages her, even a little.
“Niimura-senpai and I parted ways, and I'm home now. When it looks like you can go home, please get in touch again.”
If possible, when Senpai goes home, I want to be the one to go get her.
If Niimura-senpai is involved in the incident, going home is dangerous.
Then, a reply came right away.
“Sorry about everything today. I'm waiting for my mom's interview to finish.”
Senpai's still at the police station, then. It's probably the safest place there is.
But, next door to Senpai's home, there is Niimura-senpai.
If Niimura-senpai is involved in the incident, I absolutely must not let her get close.
“You must be tired. Senpai, why don't you come to my place? Waiting alone must be hard, right?”
“Thank you, Goto. But, I'm fine. If I did that, my mom would be home alone.”
True, I also have to make sure Senpai's mother is safe.
But, how should I explain it?
Niimura-senpai might be the killer, so don't go home, or something?
I'm sure Senpai wouldn't believe something like that. If I don't find a clever way to hold Senpai back, there's no telling what will happen.
“Then, couldn't you ask them to let you stay the night at the police station? That way, your mom would be with you too, right?”
Senpai, realize it somehow...!
“Goto, you're kind. But, it seems the police don't have anywhere they can let us stay. Besides, I have to hurry and get ready for my dad's funeral. Dad, too, can come home tomorrow morning, they said.”
Senpai, no, you can't go home! I have to stop her somehow.
“Senpai, this is a strange question, but isn't your intuition sensing any danger?”
“No, for now I'm fine. What's wrong, Goto? You're acting strange?”
This is so frustrating! When it might be dangerous! What happened to her usual intuition!?
Just then, an incoming call came in on my phone.
Kogori-senpai's calling.
"Hello!? Senpai?"
"Oh, Goto... You seemed kind of off, so I called."
Senpai's voice was unusually gentle. Had she regained a little of her composure?
No, never mind that now; there's no telling when her mom's questioning will end!
"S-Senpai! I'm scared! Right now, by myself..."
On the spur of the moment, I had blurted out something strange.
Trying somehow to make a situation where I could be with Senpai, I had said something this needy.
Even though right now Senpai is going through such a hard time.
"Heh, really, Goto. You really are my cute junior. Then, Goto, come over to my place. That way we can be together, right?"
True, that way we could be together, but in the apartment next door lives Niimura-senpai.
Spending the night at Zahha would probably be impossible too, in Senpai's situation right now.
"All right. I'm sorry, please let me do that."
In that case, I'll be her bodyguard.
I may not be much to rely on, but if I stay on guard at all times, I can manage somehow.
If I stay awake and keep watch, we'll surely make it to morning.
I'll lock up perfectly too, and never open the door, no matter who comes.
No, first, a thorough check of the inside of the apartment.
Someone might already be hiding in there.
"Senpai, when can you leave? I'll come pick you up."
"If it's just me, I can leave anytime. It looks like the police will drive my mom home."
All right, the sooner the better. Once we meet up, I'll have Kogori-senpai send Niimura-senpai a lie, a message saying she'll be late.
The later it gets, the harder this trick is to use.
"Then I'll head over now. Please wait for me."
Just in case, I'll bring anything that could work as a weapon.
A hammer... a kitchen knife... a frying pan...
A box cutter, plus... a small saw... a wrench...
I don't see any weapon that would be much use in my hands.
Well, it's still far more reassuring than nothing, I suppose.
But, if a police officer on the lookout stops and questions me, I might get taken in for violating the weapons law.
Well, I can't be worrying about that kind of thing.
I can just say I'm carrying them for self-defense, and I really am carrying them for self-defense.
That kind of excuse probably wouldn't work.
If, while I'm going home with Kogori-senpai, I get stopped and questioned, I might be the only one taken to the police station.
If that happened, I'd end up putting Senpai in even more danger.
For now, I'll wrap just the kitchen knife, the dangerous-looking one, in a towel and tuck it inside my clothes.
Splitting up my things should make it easier to fool people's eyes.
Oh, I also have to pack a change of clothes.
And also, my phone charger. I'll put these in my backpack, so I won't stand out while I'm on the move.
I looked at my reflection in the mirror, backpack on my back.
It's a little big for me to be carrying...
Anything smaller than this wouldn't fit everything, anyway.
If I'm getting around by bicycle, well, it's probably fine.
I jumped on my bicycle and headed for the police station.
I'm moving this fast. I should get to Kogori-senpai first.
Niimura-senpai might be lying in wait near the police station, though.
No, that can't be. If she's going to lie in wait, home should be more convenient in every way.
It's fine. My back is packed full of weapons.
Now then, Operation start.
