Proxy Sisters

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-07 20:30 火 · 73 boxes

The moment I put my shoes on, my body refused.
Shoes were things you wore to walk outside.
So my body refused.
I was already worn out from all the walking today...
And yet, what is this situation!?
Why am I holding these things!?
In my two hands, a nata and a mace.
What are they for, you ask!? Hunting! Hunting!
We're hunting in the mountains, with the sun already down and not a single streetlight!
And on top of that, me, a total hunting amateur, going hunting with gear like this!?
That was the commentary I kept running through my head.
It all started when Obaasan began her impression... and then Niimura-senpai egged me on...
That's right! It's Niimura-senpai's fault!
If she hadn't lured me with food back then, none of this would have...!
But at the same time, I began to feel that I was partly to blame too, for taking the bait.
"Eri-nee, you okay? You look all out of energy."
"Huh? Oh, yeah..."
Of course I'm out of energy!
Now I fully understood why Niimura-senpai had called her a monkey.
"Hey, hey, Eri-nee! I'll just do the hunting any old way, so you go rest, Eri-nee!"
And with that, Kana-chan snatched my nata and mace away from me.
"Rest where...?"
"I know a good place! It's real close to here, so come on!"
Ugh... Is it really a good place...?
Don't tell me she wants to drag out a bear that lives in here and hunt it...
"Come on, Eri-nee! Get in!"
"............"
My legs were completely refusing to walk.
Then I noticed Kana-chan was hugging something like a big bag.
"Kana-chan, what's that?"
"Oh, this?
　It's a giant bullfrog! You ate some earlier, remember?"
"Ugh...! A f-frog...!?"
Kana-chan thrust out at me a frog like a big stuffed bear.
It was clearly bigger than my face.
"Wh-what are you going to do with it...?"
"Ehehe! With this, our hunt's over! That's okay, right?"
"Huh...?"
"Come on, the hunt's over now! Eri-nee! Get in!"
And with that, Kana-chan went briskly off into the hole.
Being left alone in a place like this was more than I could take.
I went after Kana-chan too.
It seemed my body had chosen going into the hole over being alone.
"Kana-chan, are you there...?"
"Of course I'm here! Come on, hurry!"
From under the moonlight outside, into the dark hole.
The contrast made it feel even darker.
But my eyes soon adjusted, and my vision began to clear.
"Amazing... It's like you can see the light...!"
Maybe because of the rain earlier, a faint mist hung in the air.
The mist raised pillars of light inside the cave, which should have been dark, and made a natural neon sign.
"This place is my secret base!"
And with that, Kana-chan crouched and touched the rush mat spread on the ground.
"Hmm, it's still a little wet, but it's probably okay. So it rained a bit around here too."
I see, the rain from earlier must have come in from above.
But then, that's a little strange.
Where does the rain that falls in here go?
Is there a gap somewhere?
"Kana-chan, are you sure about the hunt? Your grandma might be catching a big one."
"It's fine, it's fine! Forget that old hag, forget her! 　I'm not so mean I'd work a tired Eri-nee to the bone!"
Could she be looking out for me?
Letting me take it easy here, so I wouldn't get any more tired―
"Thanks, Kana-chan."
"Don't mention it, don't mention it! It's finally just me and Eri-nee, so I wanted to talk about all kinds of stuff! 　Let us talk as sisters!"
"Kana-chan... But you've got Chiga-nee."
"Sure, I've got my real sister too. Eri-nee, you're going home the day after tomorrow, right? So unless it's a chance like this..."
Suddenly I thought of my own sister.
Sis and I hadn't properly talked in the last few years.
And yet we used to be close sisters...
"I've got a real sister too."
"Whoa! So does she have big boobs!?"
"Huh...!? 　That's... I don't know..."
"You don't know...? 　Could it be... you two don't get along...?"
"Yeah..."
"I see... Sisters who don't even know each other's boob size, that's pretty bad."
What's with that yardstick...
"So, Kana-chan, I'm happy you said we're sisters. I want a sister I get along with too. If you'll have someone like me, let's stay sisters."
I offered, once again, a bond as sisters.
But―
Kana-chan's face was stern.
"Eri-nee... If you're gonna say that, I quit being sisters."
"Huh?"
"Eri-nee, that's wrong! 　Your real sister and you don't get along, so you'll be sisters with me? 　That's weird!"
"But..."
"You've gotta get along with your real sister too! 　You can't get along as real sisters, so it's sisters with me? Am I a stand-in for your sister!? 　Once you get along with your sister, you won't need me anymore!?"
"Th-that's not it..."
"I like my real sister. But I like Eri-nee too. I want it to be like that for Eri-nee too... You'd hate staying like this forever, never making up, right?"
"Well... sure, but..."
"Sis and I had a fight. This past New Year's."
If I remember right, they argued over whether or not Kotaro-san was the culprit.
"Sis says Daddy's the culprit behind the curse-killings. I hate that..."
"Kana-chan..."
"But... But I thought it over again. Then who would the culprit have to be for me to be okay with it? If the culprit's someone from this settlement, I'd hate it no matter who it was. So if Sis, feeling the same, says Daddy's the culprit... Sis must be hurting too..."
Faced with Kana-chan saying something so unexpectedly grown-up, I couldn't say a thing.
About sisters getting along, about the culprit, she had thought far harder than someone like me.
No, I had all but given up on the sister thing, and with the curse-killings, all I'd thought about was pinning down the culprit.
But Kana-chan is different.
Her sister matters, the people of the settlement matter.
And still the curse-killings don't stop.
It was the moment I realized how presumptuous my thinking was.
"I'm pretty sure the culprit is someone in the settlement. That can't be changed anymore, so it can't be helped, right?"
"Kana-chan, do you want to find the culprit?"
"I dunno... If it's like Sis says and Daddy's the culprit... I bet I'd never forgive Daddy."
"Never forgive...?"
"Because all this time he'd have been fooling everyone and killing people, wouldn't he? 　Someone like that, even if it's Daddy... No, because it's Daddy, I can't forgive him."
And with that, Kana-chan tightly gripped the nata she was holding.
"I've made up my mind. If I find out who the culprit behind the curse-killings is, I won't forgive them, whoever it is."
"Kana-chan..."
"I don't remember Mommy. But everyone still cares about Mommy even now, and that makes me happy. But... somewhere in their hearts, they must think the curse-killings are happening because of Mommy's curse. If people think that about my dead Mommy too... I could never forgive that...!"
"So... you've decided to kill the culprit?"
"............"
After a short pause, Kana-chan slowly nodded.
It's just as Chiga-nee said. Kana-chan is going to attack the culprit.
That is Kana-chan's justice, her duty to the settlement, the shape of her love for her mother.
"Kana-chan, no matter what happens, killing is not okay."
"Why?"
"Killing people is wrong."
"Why? Can you say the same thing about this frog?"
"Huh?"
"It's not like we absolutely had to hunt this frog to survive, right? 　It got turned into a tool for a contest, didn't it?"
"That's..."
"In morals class... they told me, 'Let's think about why we must not kill people.' But the rule that you can't kill people is weird in the first place. Eri-nee, what's the reason animals are okay but people aren't?"
What am I supposed to answer in a case like this?
Human rights violations? Not doing what you'd hate to have done to you? Because humanity would be wiped out?
Most likely, reasoning like that won't work on Kana-chan.
If it's human rights violations, then what about the Animal Welfare Act?
If it's what you'd hate to have done to you, animals must hate it too.
If it's because humanity would be wiped out, animals would be wiped out too.
Besides, I'm no good at philosophical stuff...
"I've got a rule when I hunt animals. If I find a parent and its baby, I hunt them both. 'Cause the one left behind would be pitiful. The reason you can't kill is probably 'cause the one left behind is pitiful. So then you just kill both. The ones that get killed aren't aware of anything, so they can't feel sad or suffer."
In other words, murder is wrong because the people left behind grieve.
The only ones against killing are, in the end, the living.
The dead can no longer think about it or even perceive it.
She's saying it's the selfishness of the living.
"So I think it's okay to kill people nobody would be sad about if they died. Isn't that why there's the death penalty? If everyone wants somebody dead, it's fine for them to die."
A view of life and death far too dry and detached.
A view of life and death that could only come from hunting so many animals and taking so many lives at her age.
"But, Kana-chan... There's one more reason you can't kill."
"Huh?"
"I don't want you to kill people, Kana-chan."
"............"
"I don't want you to become a Kana-chan who kills people on nothing but her own judgment."
"That's just... Eri-nee's own selfish opinion."
"Then Kana-chan's opinion is a selfish one too."
"............"
"If I say I don't want you to, will you not kill the culprit?"
"Why don't you want me to kill?"
"I hate it when people die. That's why I want to solve the curse-killings too, and, Kana-chan, I don't want you killing anyone either. Is that not good enough...?"
"............"
"Kana-chan, haven't even the animals you hunted sometimes attacked to protect their families?"
"They have, but..."
"It's the same thing. I don't want to see my family or friends die. No, I don't want my family or friends to kill, either."
"Because Eri-nee hates it, you're telling me not to kill?"
"Yeah..."
"............"
"Is that selfish...?"
Kana-chan gave a slight nod.
"But... Okay. I won't do anything Eri-nee hates. 'Cause I don't wanna do anything that makes Eri-nee sad."
"Thank you..."
"But... But if I have to kill the culprit to protect Eri-nee, I'll kill."
At that point the line is hard to draw, but could it count as self-defense?
"In that case, I won't go as far as saying 'Kill the culprit.' But if you protect me, I'll be happy."
"Yeah! Leave it to me!"
For Kana-chan, living this deep in the mountains and thinking of animals and people as so close, it can't be helped that her view of life and death has drifted from the usual one.
If I could fix that even a little― No, if her view of life and death came closer to mine, that would be something to be glad about.
Though if someone called that 'correction' just my own selfishness, there'd be no arguing with it.
"Hehe!"
"Hm?"
"I really do like Eri-nee!"
"Kana-chan..."
"I bet your sister likes you too, Eri-nee, right?"
"Hmm, you think so...?"
"You two just got your wires crossed somewhere! When you get back to Motoki-cho, try talking to your sister! I bet you can make up!"
"............"
"Maybe it's not that your sister hates you, Eri-nee, but that you, Eri-nee, hate your sister?"
"Hmm, it's less hate than... being scared, maybe. She seems a bit like a delinquent."
"Huh. Has she been in trouble with the police or something?"
"Nothing like that so far, it seems..."
"Then, has she been called in by the school or something?"
"Never heard of that either. Sis did drop out of high school partway, though."
"Is your sister really a delinquent? Isn't it just a misunderstanding on Eri-nee's part?"
Hearing Kana-chan say that, with the talk going the way it had, I felt like I was the one in the wrong.
"You really should talk to your sister properly. Me and Sis had a fight too, but we made up just fine."
"Yeah..."
My phone buzzed. It was Kogori-senpai.
Senpai's dad and the others might have heard what we were just talking about...
"Hel―hel―, Go―?"
"Ah, Kogori-senpai?"
The signal was bad. Was it because we were inside this cave?
"Hup. Senpai, how about here? Can you hear me?"
"Yeah, I can hear you fine."
"Ah, I knew it! Right now, um... we've come to a secret place."
"A secret...?"
"Yes, Kana-chan's secret base."
"― ―"
"Senpai?"
"Ah... Ugggh...!"
"What's wrong!? Are you okay!?"
"Hah...! Hah...! 　Goto, okay? Listen carefully! Get away from there, right now!"
"What...? 　Senpai, where are you right now!? Outside Arata!?"
"Yes! I just got a really strange feeling! Get away from there right now!"
"Understood!
　Kana-chan! We're going home!"
"Aww? Let's hang out longer!"
"Just hurry!"
I dragged the protesting Kana-chan out of the cave by force.
"Should we go straight back to the house!?"
"Yes! Get away from there right now!"
"Eri-nee, what's wrong!?"
"Kogori-senpai says it's dangerous! Let's get away from here, now!"
"Oh! Is it that danger precognition thing!? 　Okay! Let's go back!"
