Goto's Suspicion

Meicho Arc [Clear Sign] · Shiyo 800-04-08 12:00 水 · 44 boxes

Natsumi came over, and after that I made tea, and we watched a DVD together.
Just like this. If we stay together like this the whole time, it'll be fine.
But today of all days, Natsumi seemed to be feeling well.
That's a good thing, but this way, even if I call Goto over, I can't talk to her about it.
Natsumi never out of my sight, and me able to talk about the curse with Goto... a situation like that...
As I thought, setting up the ideal conditions was hard.
After that, I learned from the online news about an incident that had happened at Central Park.
It couldn't be... the curse can't have anything to do with it. Probably...
I let a frightened Natsumi lie down on the sofa, and checked the high school's message board.
What I found out was,
・The victim was in a gruesome state
・The victim was still alive when found
That was about it.
What bothered me most was that the body had been in such a gruesome state.
Leaving someone alive and half-dead was entirely possible.
Once she was awake, I asked Natsumi about her dad, just in case.
Luckily, he was away on a business trip to Hokkaido right now.
In that case, whoever was killed must be someone else.
In that case, who had been killed?
Natsumi was right here, and Natsumi's mom had called to say she'd be home late.
And her dad was away on business.
Questions kept welling up one after another, and I wanted Goto's help all the more.
But with Natsumi right in front of me...
Even as I sat here, time was slipping away.
No, let's look at it differently. There are two things I can do right now.
One is to take Natsumi to Central Park, see the scene for myself, and look for a way to protect Natsumi.
Natsumi would hate it, but that's better than having her die.
The other is to call Goto over here and consult her on the very question of 'how can I set up a situation to consult her.'
Natsumi might go to the bathroom, say, and then,
"Natsumi can't leave my sight, but I need to talk to you without her hearing."
I just have to say something like that. This would only take a moment.
I can't tell which situation would work better.
This is bound to be an important choice.
Now, which do I pick?
Natsumi to the park, or Goto over here.
Okay... Goto's the one I'll call.
Knowing her, she's sure to be sniffing around about the incident.
I can also get information about the incident out of Goto.
I'll lure Goto over with pizza.
"Hey, Natsumi, since we're ordering pizza anyway, why don't we invite Goto too?"
"Huh? But wouldn't Goto just eat all the pizza by herself?"
That was Natsumi's joke. I picked up my phone right away.
"Hello, Goto? What are you doing right now?"
"Ooh, Niimura-senpai! What do you think I'm doing right now?"
"Sniffing around about the incident, I bet. Come over to my place and I'll feed you pizza!"
"What! You guessed not just what I'm doing but my appetite too! 　Aha, I see, I see. Kogori-senpai on her own wasn't enough company, so you called on this genius girl, Erina Goto, too. Is that the way of it? 　But I can probably only stay about an hour, okay? I've got cram school after this!"
"Got it! Natsumi's here too, of course. Okay, I'll be waiting!"
―15 minutes later, Goto showed up.
"Senpais! Good morning! I heard a rumor that things are lonely without Goto, so I have come flying over!"
"Oh, glad you came! Sit anywhere."
"Pardon me!"
Goto said that and plopped down on the sofa.
"So? So? Where's the pizza?"
Goto was looking all around, her nose twitching.
"Haven't ordered yet. Figured we might as well get your opinion too, Goto."
"No need to be so considerate! I'm not picky, I'll eat anything!"
"Heh heh, you were thinking, 'If they were going to order anyway, they should've just ordered anything already,' right?"
"Oh...! Just what I'd expect from Kogori-senpai! You know me well indeed!"
Goto said that and grinned.
"But Goto, you've got cram school after this, right? If we order pizza now, it won't get here in time, will it?"
"Ah...! Now that you mention it..."
"Y-you tricked me...!"
"Goto, sorry... I honestly just forgot..."
"............
Well! In that case, I'll let you off with three Premium Seafood Sets at Sakura Sushi next time!"
"Are you trying to bankrupt the Niimura household!?"
Goto coming over on her own was enough to make it this lively.
Up until a minute ago Natsumi had seemed anxious, but as far as the mood went, we were okay.
"Haruka, I'm borrowing your bathroom."
All right, my chance came already! While she's gone, I'll just tell Goto, "There's something I want to talk about, just the two of us."
"Hey, hey, Niimura-senpai?"
"Yeah, what's up?"
"Why did you call me here?"
"Huh?"
Goto was still smiling, but her eyes weren't.
Whenever she's like this, Goto has some kind of suspicion about me.
She must have picked up on something...!
If she has picked up on something, that works in my favor.
"Goto, hang on a sec."
I stood outside the bathroom door.
"Natsumi, sorry, but could you bring over some DVDs, whatever ones? I'd like something to kill time with after all."
"Rogerrr!"
Good, that should buy me some time.
It means taking my eyes off her for a bit, but only for 1 or 2 minutes.
Having come this far, I had to be ready for a bit of a tightrope walk.
"Sorry, Goto. You asked why I called you?"
"Yes! Senpai, you actually want to ask me something, don't you? Something you can't ask in front of Kogori-senpai."
A smile that never slipped from Goto's face, and a piercing gaze.
Words that seemed to see right through me.
"Um, well..."
"Niimura-senpai, for you of all people, pizza's the last thing on your mind, right?"
Here was my chance to get information straight out of Goto, and I hadn't even thought about what to say.
Even if I said that I'm a witch and might have put a curse on Natsumi...
"Senpai, I won't dismiss anything you say. So please, ask what you want to ask, just as it is."
What on earth did Goto see to say a thing like that...! Did she get some information at Central Park?
"Okay, I'll go home for a bit. What kind of DVDs do you want?"
"Oh, let's see... um―"
"For me, one action movie and one romance! Oh, and three anime too! 　If possible, some snacks would be lovely."
"Sorry, Goto, we don't have any decent snacks at home right now. Want me to go to the convenience store?"
"Just what I'd expect from Kogori-senpai! Please get whatever with this!"
Goto whipped a 1,000-yen bill out of her wallet.
Wait a minute! If she goes shopping, I'll have my eyes off her for a long time!
"Natsumi, you okay going outside? How are you feeling..."
"Yeah, I'm fine. I'm feeling good today!"
"Hey, Natsumi, I'll come with you!"
"Ahaha, it's fine! I'm not a kid anymore!"
"Let's leave it to Kogori-senpai. We have something important to talk about, don't we?"
Aimed my way with a smile, Goto's piercing gaze.
As if that gaze had run me through, I was at a loss for words.
"Something important? Then all the more reason for me to step out, right? 　Okay, I'm off!"
"Ah, Natsumi..."
Natsumi had gone out...
I have to explain the situation to Goto fast and bring Natsumi back!
