Rubberneckers

Jusatsu Arc [Killing by Curse] · Shiyo 800-04-08 10:40 水 · 20 boxes

When I came yesterday, it was much livelier than this.
Everyone was in high spirits for the hanami, and the mood was a very happy one. [TN: hanami = cherry-blossom viewing]
It was lively now as well, but a little different from yesterday's.
Rubberneckers who had caught the rumor of the incident were ringed all the way around this park.
All the same, it is really something how many of them gather like this.
Though I am one of those rubberneckers myself.
"Hmm, I can't see..."
There were especially many high school students among these rubberneckers.
They are all under the stay-at-home order, so they probably came to kill the boredom, but this is the wrong way around.
They closed the schools and issued the stay-at-home order for the sake of safety, and yet if it comes to this, then not closing the schools and having us go in as usual would have been far more appropriate as a safety measure, would it not?
But, they surely do not have the burning enthusiasm that I do.
They all probably gathered out of idle curiosity, but I came to investigate the incident!
―That is the excuse I come up with, but who am I planning to say it to? Me...
Between the rubberneckers, the goshinboku of Chuo Park was visible. [TN: the sacred tree of a shrine, held to house a god]
From the great cherry tree towering high in Chuo Park, cherry blossoms were fluttering down.
That goshinboku, too, has quite a curious legend attached to it.
The goshinboku is said to be about 70 years old.
However, to look at it, it seems to be centuries old.
Motoki-cho must have soil well suited to growing cherry trees.
As proof of that, there are a great many cherry trees in Motoki-cho.
The one thing that Motoki-cho, which has nothing else to recommend it, can boast of nationwide is these cherry trees.
People who came sightseeing were captivated by the cherry trees of Motoki-cho, and over the last 10 years or so the population has risen sharply.
It is Motoki-'cho', but its population is apparently already enough for it to become a city. [TN: -cho = town; the next size up is a city]
More to the point, the question is how to find out what is going on inside the park.
Everyone was stretching their arms up, and taking photos of the inside with their smartphones, which made it all the harder to see.
"Oh, that's it!"
If stretching up on tiptoe does not let me see, then I should crouch down and look.
If I crouch down, and look through the gaps between legs thinner than shoulders, it will be perfect.
"Yeah, Operation successful!"
It was easier to see than standing up.
Apparently, several police officers were examining the area near the goshinboku.
Which means the body was somewhere under that goshinboku?
Blue tarpaulins had been put up like a tent, and sure enough, the pinpoint spot I wanted to see seemed to be hidden.
About all I can do after this is probably the door-to-door questioning.
The source is the internet! I would never say anything that embarrassing.
Of course the internet is an important source of information as well, but subjective information from people is also very important.
If you collect subjective information and distill it, then what is produced out of that is pure objective information.
"Hey, you! What are you doing!"
At a woman's voice I had heard before, my body gave a jolt.
When I timidly looked behind me...
Who is she, this woman?
But, I have seen her somewhere before...
"Oh my? Goto-san?"
"Ichimura-sensei!"
"It really is Goto-san! It's been such a long time!"
"It's been a long time, Sensei! A little over 3 years now, isn't it!"
I grinned from ear to ear at Sensei.
This Ichimura-sensei was my homeroom teacher back when I was in elementary school.
Probably, she saw my build and took me for an elementary school student.
"Goto-san, something about your air has changed."
"Huh? Is that so?"
"The Goto-san I knew was, how should I put it, more sort of―"
"Was I a gloomy, morose, dark pupil?"
"Hmm, well..."
Ichimura-sensei scratched her head, looking awkward.
"Ahaha! People really can change! The genius girl Erina Goto, reborn this cheerful, is overcome with emotion at being able to meet Ichimura-sensei, ma'am!"
"My, my, Goto-san, so you have met good people. And I hear you had a perfect score on the high school entrance exam the other day. Goto-san has always had a good head, and yet you were a pupil whose relationships with people worried me so..."
"Ehe, I have been truly blessed in my relationships with people! I have senpai who are really friendly with me! 　Oh, Sensei! It's a moving reunion and all that, but I'm doing door-to-door inquiries about the incident right now..."
"Goto-san, even if you are a high school student, I can't approve of you hanging around here. For what it's worth, such as I am, I'm still a teacher..."
"Now, now, don't say such stiff things!"
"I don't know any of the details at all myself. Knowing Goto-san, I'm sure you already know more about the incident than I do. Come on, come on, Goto-san, you stay at home as well."
"Honestly, Sensei, you're so se- ri- ous!"
"Goto-san... you really have grown cheerful, and I'm so glad."
Ichimura-sensei began to tear up.
Well, it is true that anyone who knows only the me of back then would be startled at the sight.
All the same, the door-to-door investigation looks like it will be rough going.
Without rushing, slowly, thoroughly, let me press on with it.
