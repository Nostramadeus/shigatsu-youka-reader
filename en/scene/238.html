A Correct Contradiction

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-07 22:00 火 · 83 boxes

"Thank you very much."
I got off the buggy in front of the Niimura main house and gave back the knife.
"Who you can trust and who you can't. What matters is telling them apart carefully. Even I don't know who I should trust, but if this advice helps you even a little..."
Even after he's said this much, I still can't fully trust Shirozaki-san.
I must have fallen into this so-called cursed thinking.
But it's also a fact that here, doubting is the way to survive.
"Well, good night. See you at the memorial service tomorrow."
"Good night. Thank you for everything!"
Shirozaki-san waved with his back still turned.
I headed for the front door of the Niimura main house.
"............!?"
Who is this person...!
A Noh mask and red robes, and a nata and a mace...
Earlier, Shirozaki-san said the culprits in the curse-killings were everyone in the settlement.
Then could this person be...!
I lost the strength in my legs and sank to the ground right there.
"S-stay, stay away!"
"Mm... Natsumi-chan, it's me."
Chiga-nee...? That scared me...
But Chiga-nee walked off somewhere without saying anything more.
Dressed like that... what is she going to do?
Don't tell me she's going to do a curse-killing now!?
I have to tell Haruka and Goto, fast!
"Haruka! Goto!"
In the living room were Goto and Kana-chan, lying down and looking tired, along with Haruka and Obasan.
"You must be beat, Natsumi. Goto filled me in. Looks like you really did sense something..."
"Haruka! Chiga-nee was dressed all weird, and she went off somewhere...!"
"Chiga-nee? 　Oh, that's―"
Haruka explained the succession rite to me.
Chiga-nee was apparently about to perform the succession rite for the witch.
Even so, just now I suspected, even a little, that Chiga-nee might be the curse-killer.
Ever since I heard Shirozaki-san's story, I can't shake this anxiety.
I should report what I saw and heard, quickly.
"Goto, can you move? There's something I want to talk to you about..."
"Oh, yes, I'm fine!"
Goto got up right away.
For someone who'd gone out hunting, she didn't look all that tired.
"Eri-nee, where are you going?"
Kana-chan tugged at Goto's clothes.
"I'm going back to my room. Want to come too, Kana-chan?"
"Okay..."
If anything, Kana-chan seemed to be the more tired one.
However used to it she was, the fatigue must have been building up.
Kana-chan will hear what we talk about too, though. Will that be okay?
Knowing Goto, I think she's already thought about that.
"Mom, sorry, but I'm counting on you."
"All right. I'll keep a close watch."
Obasan was apparently staying on the first floor.
Did she say 'keep watch' because I'd told Goto earlier that it was dangerous?
But that intuition earlier still fits better as a danger that has already passed.
Still, just to be safe. Obasan should probably stay on the first floor.
Come to think of it... Kotaro-san was nowhere to be seen.
"Hey, where's Kotaro-san?"
"Oh, he said he was gonna sleep in his room."
In his room...?
Is that really true?
What if he said that so he could sneak out of the house and do something...
"Natsumi, if you want, how about Kotaro-san's room? Wanna peek in?"
Peeping isn't my thing, but there's no choice this time. I have to make sure I'm safe.
We went up to the second floor, and Goto and Kana-chan went into our room.
"Okay, we're going in."
We stood in front of Kotaro-san's bedroom.
Haruka slid the door open without even knocking.
"Snrrrk..."
From the pitch-dark room came Kotaro-san's loud snoring.
Could it be recorded snoring?
Making it look like he's asleep, when actually he's already outside the house plotting something...
"Haruka, is it okay if I call out to Kotaro-san?"
"............"
Haruka sat down beside the futon where Kotaro-san was presumably asleep.
"Hey, Kotaro-san! If you're gonna sleep, change your clothes."
"Snrrk... Mm...
Huh? Is that Haruka-chan?"
It wasn't a recording, then. Haruka was carrying on a conversation with him.
"Fwaaah... just going to the bathroom..."
Kotaro-san sluggishly got up and left the room.
Sure enough, Kotaro-san was here.
"Natsumi, what do you wanna do? Wait till he gets back?"
"Yeah..."
There was no telling that Kotaro-san wouldn't just take off somewhere.
My suspicion really is feeding on itself.
............
"Huh? You're still here?"
After about as long as a trip to the bathroom would naturally take, Kotaro-san reappeared.
"Ah, no, just..."
"Don't tell me you're keeping an eye on me because you think I'm the curse-killer? Ha ha ha!"
Kotaro-san went into his room without seeming to mind.
"Doesn't look like there's any problem..."
"He won't escape out the window or anything, right...?"
"He's not Kana..."
Once I start doubting this much, there's no end to it.
That exchange with Shirozaki-san earlier had made my suspicion of the settlement people grow even bigger.
But if I keep watching only Kotaro-san like this, I can't report to Goto.
Obasan watching only Kotaro-san is one option, but then this house's defenses would be stretched thin.
For now―I guess I'll go back to my room...
Kana-chan had burrowed into her futon, and Goto was sitting beside her.
Goto seemed to have gotten Kana-chan to sleep.
"Sorry to keep you waiting."
"Senpai... I heard everything from Kana-chan at the secret base. She said if she found out who the culprit was, she'd kill them without hesitating... And I told her what we think, too. That Kotaro-san seems like the culprit, but that even that's a little doubtful... Senpai's bad premonition, too. And then, Kana-chan..."
Kana-chan's pillow had two knives lying beside it.
Did that mean she'd protect us with them?
No―maybe she'd suddenly attack us with those knives.
Right now, I can't trust a single person from Arata.
"Is it okay to talk here...?"
"It's fine. I guarantee it."
Goto said that and gave Kana-chan's head a pat.
Goto's certain she can trust Kana-chan...?
Or could it be that even Goto has started taking part in the incident―
What am I thinking...!
That I turned suspicion on Goto even for a moment shocks me.
If I go that far, there's no one at all I can trust.
"Hey, Natsumi, you okay?
　You've had a scary look on your face this whole time..."
I know I'm acting strange.
All Shirozaki-san did was tell me about the state of mind of the people here in Arata.
If I don't draw the line there, I won't be able to move.
I finally sat all the way down, after staying half-raised the whole time.
Along with me, Haruka sat down too.
"Natsumi... I don't know what you saw, but we're on your side."
"That's right. I know you're scared, but so am I."
"Yeah... I'm okay. I'm just scared too..."
My so-called nerves of steel are surprisingly fragile...
―I told them everything I'd seen and heard, just as it was.
"How do I put it... it's totally different from your intuitions before... You didn't hear a voice, you saw clear images, right?"
"A lot about what Shirozaki-san said and did bothers me, too. We got lots of hints, but it's like the mystery of these curse-killings just keeps getting deeper because of them..."
As I thought, even if their viewpoints differed, both of them had sensed something off.
"First, let's examine what Shirozaki-san said."
Goto stared at the notebook where she'd written down what I told her.
She was even more serious than usual today.
"Let's suppose Shirozaki-san's story is all true. The biggest thing here is that Shirozaki-san is, without a doubt, one of the culprits in the curse-killings. In other words, he's killed someone before."
So that's what it comes to after all.
No, if she said 'suppose it's true,' is there some contradiction?
"Kogori-senpai, there's no doubt you experienced suspicion feeding on itself firsthand. Wasn't it so intense that everyone in the settlement seemed suspicious, us included?"
"Yeah..."
"Seriously..."
"But please keep just one thing in mind. People are quick to believe a 'true-sounding' story that comes after a lie."
"Huh? What's that supposed to mean..."
"Shirozaki-san told one lie. The lie that there's an 'oracle.' Or so Shirozaki-san said. But was it really a 'lie'?"
"You mean the 'oracle lie' was a lie?"
"Yes. In other words, the oracle really exists. If the residents don't follow the oracle, they die. The reason they die if they don't follow it is that they get suspected of being the curse-killer and killed."
The oracle really existing? That's not like Goto to say.
While borrowing the metaphor of an oracle, can Goto see the truth behind it?
"Goto, what is the oracle? 　The words of the witch, Sakura-san, is what Shirozaki-san said, but..."
"That's probably the biggest focal point. This is only if we do 'not' trust Shirozaki-san's story that the oracle is a lie. Since it's being called an oracle, I think it's someone's tip-off, but... Going by this flow, would it be from Kotaro-san...?"
"So you're saying Kotaro-san hears the witch's voice and passes it on to everyone...?"
This really isn't like Goto.
The usual Goto would flatly deny occult stuff like this.
"But their real goal is 'killing the witch,' right? 　Then wouldn't everyone in the settlement make Kotaro-san tell them who the witch is, by any means necessary?"
"But he says the witch is really Sakura-san, so that's already clear."
"No, that's not what I mean. Someone's using Sakura-san's name so that Kotaro-san gets egged on, right?"
"Why do you think that?"
"Huh? Don't tell me you're saying Sakura-san really is letting Kotaro-san hear oracles?"
"That's right."
"Hey, Goto, what's gotten into you? Normally you'd shoot down talk like this..."
"Senpai... can you really deny it? I sure can't. 　Because denying the oracle means denying Kogori-senpai, doesn't it?"
"Hey, Goto, quit stringing us along...!"
"Because... it means being able to hear the voices of the dead at this time of year, right?"
...! So that's it...!
That's exactly the same as my intuition.
The oracle is just my intuition by another name.
In other words, she's saying Kotaro-san can hear the voice of the cherry trees too.
"But there's a contradiction here too. In the mountain hut, Chiga-nee said so, didn't she?"
"Mm... Also, there was a time when we all kept watch over Dad in the meeting hall in April. In April, from Shiyo 793 to 795, I think. We watched him the whole time, but the curse-killings happened anyway..."
"Even if Kotaro-san had been hearing the oracle, he couldn't have done anything, and one suspicious remark would have confirmed him as the culprit. But the curse-killings kept happening. In other words, whether Kotaro-san hears the oracle or not has nothing to do with it. So the possibility that the oracle doesn't exist gets bigger too."
"So Shirozaki-san's 'the oracle is a lie' is actually true? 　Um... we can say the oracle really doesn't exist, right?"
"I think so. But the only one who thinks 'there's no oracle' might be Shirozaki-san."
"Come on... I don't get it anymore... None of this makes any sense at all..."
"Yes, that's the biggest point. I said it in the mountain hut, didn't I? 'I can see the general background.' That's what I meant."
"Um, you mean the truth is that it doesn't make sense...?"
"No, not the truth. The background. It was clear the moment we heard everyone out in the mountain hut that their stories contradicted each other."
"Ugh, I don't get it. Taking a break...
I can't keep up with this anymore..."
Haruka said that and lay down on her futon.
"Goto, um... what do you mean, it not making sense is the background?"
"Hmm... how should I put it... Okay then, Kogori-senpai, who do you think the curse-killer is?"
There's no way I could know that.
But she must be looking for the answer 'I don't know.'
"Goto, the culprit... I have absolutely no idea."
"............ Thank you... Not knowing who the culprit is has to be the right answer."
Not knowing the culprit is the right answer...?
Even I was getting a little tired by now...
"Goto, sorry, I need a break too..."
"You're right... I'm a little tired too."
We lay down on our futons too.
Maybe we'd be better off not getting involved in an incident like this...
But there must have been a reason I had to come here.
If I could just figure that out...
