Not that I trust Chiga-nee's research blindly...
Even so, I can't bear to keep Kogori-senpai shut up in that room forever.

What Chiga-nee found, I have to find as well.

Obasan is with her, so I think she's fine, but even if Kogori-senpai sent a message, it'll be hard to reach them underground.
