Only Fun Things for Now.

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-07 12:40 火 · 49 boxes

We boarded the 12:40 train and sat down in facing seats.
At this hour on a weekday, there were only a scattered few other passengers.
"Senpai, the lunch, quick, quick! Goto's stomach is itching to carry out the operation!"
"You're such a glutton, geez..."
"Between me and Senpai, there is no need for restraint!"
"Heh... fair enough."
When we opened the three lunch boxes, one held nine big rice balls, and the other two were packed tight with a lively assortment of side dishes.
"That's Senpai's mom for you! It all looks delicious! 　Well then, I'll start with these rice balls after all..."
Goto picked up a rice ball bigger than her own hand and took a huge bite.
"Mmmhoo, this is irresistible! 　The saltiness, the shaping, the cooking of the rice, truly exquisite!"
"Goto, can you even taste anything? 　I mean, you eat anything..."
"No, no, these rice balls are packed with a mother's love! 　This genius girl Erina Goto can tell the care that went into making them taste even a little better!"
These big rice balls had obviously been shaped by my dad, though...
Well, Goto looked happy, so I'd leave it at that.
"But aren't you two being inappropriate, Senpai!?"
Goto took her second bite, then suddenly gave us a long, suspicious stare.
"Ah, guess we're getting a bit too carried away?"
Then Goto stood up, rice ball in hand, and pointed sharply at our bags.
"Kogori-senpai's bag has a travel guidebook to Iizawa Prefecture sticking out of it! 　Niimura-senpai's bag clearly shows the outline of five cans of Super Soda Bomber! 　You're both totally in sightseeing mode, huh..."
Goto chomped into her half-eaten rice ball.
"Wh-what are you saying! 　If we're talking about that, Goto! You have nothing to do with the memorial service, and you tagged along anyway! 　You're the one who's totally... I mean, it's nothing but sightseeing for you!"
"Urk..."
Haruka is family of the deceased, and I was the cause of his death, so I think it's only right for us to attend.
Goto has her own reasons for coming along too, but there's no doubt she's a complete outsider.
More on those reasons later.
"W-well, I mean, you see, you senpai would be in trouble without me when it really counts, right? 　After all, I'm the genius girl, as everyone agrees, myself included!"
Tagging along is fine, but that attitude of hers, like she's saying she alone isn't getting carried away, is not okay.
I sent a damp, sullen stare back at Goto, who was puffing out her chest and acting all important.
"Wh-what...?"
"Goto, if you don't come clean, I'm confiscating not just the lunch, but the fireworks in your bag too."
"H-how did you know there were fireworks in there!?"
"Goto, I knew it! You said before that you wanted to do fireworks while looking at the cherry blossoms at night!"
"Y-you were bluffing!? That's so mean..."
Goto's eyes welled up with tears.
But not even Haruka was going to be fooled by fake crying at this level.
"So, are you gonna come clean? Or not?"
"I mean, like, with you senpai, like, it's our first time staying over together, like, so, like, even just a little, like, I wanted to have fun or whatever, like..."
Goto started sulking, poking her index fingers together.
Even so, one hand still held tight to the quarter of a rice ball that was left.
"Ahahahaha! My bad, my bad! I just felt like teasing you a little!"
Faced with Haruka's smile, Goto made a sulky face and tossed the rest of the rice ball into her mouth.
"I'm glad you came, Goto. I'm sure Haruka's dad is happy too. Right, Haruka?"
"That's right, Goto! Pray hard for Dad to rest in peace!"
Hearing that, Goto gulped down her rice ball and grinned.
"Roger, sir! Since I'm going, I'll pray properly! 　I've planned all kinds of operations for that― Ooh, this looks tasty!"
"Hey, wait, Goto! Your senpai haven't even touched it yet!"
Haruka, not to be outdone by Goto, reached into the lunch with her chopsticks too.
"Still, that speech Goto gave for the new students was so funny. Tearing up the written speech right there on the stage."
"Ahh, that was 'cause I almost cracked up myself. No matter how I looked at it, those just weren't my words."
"But it looked like everybody loved it. It's way more fun to listen to than reading off the usual stuff, and your feelings come through!"
With the three of us chatting like this, we'd be at the Arata settlement in no time.
"Oh, the other day we got these phones as presents, all matching, right! 　But Kogori-senpai's dad sure is generous, huh. Even as a present for moving up a year and starting school, just casually handing over the newest model like this..."
"Apparently the Niimuras and the Kogoris split it as a present. Well, apparently they got them cheap, practically free, through Natsumi's dad's connections."
"Who knows, maybe they're bugged."
"What for...? Mom's even telling me it's about time I got ready to stand on my own feet. 　If anything, I'm the one who's gotta grow out of needing my mom. If anybody's getting bugged, I'd rather stick one on Mom."
"Haruka, you're always glued to Obasan..."
"Well, yeah. Ever since Dad died, Mom's had it hard the whole time. She's cheerful as anything now, but sometimes she pushes herself way too hard..."
Obasan is always energetic and cheerful, but she dresses way too young for her age―
And she and I get along well enough that the two of us sometimes go out to eat together.
Every time, we get carried away with Mom's old stories and stories about the late Ojisan.
Dressing young does feel like she's straining a bit, but I think Obasan is fine the way she is.
"Speaking of which, I only just heard the other day, but Niimura-senpai, you left Motoki-cho once, right? 　Did something happen?"
"Uhh, why was that again... Dad died and... yeah, I think it was so Mom could recover. She was in a bad way after Dad died. And then, uh... why did we come back again... Natsumi, do you remember?"
"Hmm... That was 10 years ago, right? 　I think it was because Obasan got better?"
"Right... why was it again... Apparently I hit my head and got hospitalized around then, but I don't remember that at all... Whether I don't remember because I hit my head, or..."
You can't tell unless you part her hair, but there's a faint old scar on Haruka's head.
Whenever I see it, for some reason I feel a little sad.
"And look,
our phones have the same strap, right? I don't remember at all when I got this either."
"Hmm... The design's too simple, so there's no clue."
"This strap, I feel like I've seen it somewhere..."
"Goto, you know it?"
"Somewhere, way back when I was little... I think a girl around my age had one..."
"What, Goto, you had friends when you were little?"
"What's with treating me like a loner..."
"Well, you're usually a loner, aren't you?"
"Nngh... I can't argue with that..."
"Well, I think I only met that girl once or twice, though. I don't know where she is now."
Come to think of it, maybe something similar happened to me too.
I guess everybody goes through things like that.
"Aww, still, I'm jealous of you two. You grew up like sisters, huh. Sometimes you actually look like a real older sister and younger sister."
"Ha, well, Natsumi's practically family, maybe. I'm counting on you from here on too, little sister!"
Haruka said that and patted my shoulder.
"Geez... If I really were your little sister, I'd get you over spicy food, Haruka! 　Isn't it about time you joined me for some Russian-roulette takoyaki?" [TN: takoyaki: fried batter balls with octopus inside]
"How does it come to that!? 　Oh yeah, Mom said so! Said to please give the Russian-roulette takoyaki a rest already!"
"Aww? But it's fun... So then it's back to Haruka getting over spicy food and―"
"Not- in- a- mil- lion- years!"
With that, Haruka and I shared a little laugh.
But Goto's face had clouded over a little.
"Sisters who get along... are nice, aren't they... When I was little, I ate Russian-roulette takoyaki with Sis too..."
"Ah... sorry, Goto..."
"Ah, ahaha! Why are you apologizing! I was just remembering old times a little! 　Oh, it's the ocean! Looks like we've crossed out of Sawa Prefecture into Iizawa Prefecture!"
Goto pointed outside, as if to kick away the gloomy air.
Beyond the cherry blossoms, a vast ocean spread out.
It sparkled in the spring sun, making the pretty pink of the cherry blossoms stand out even more vividly.
I remember it faintly.
I used to hate cherry blossoms.
I think it was because Haruka's dad died on account of me.
Apparently it was called things like April sickness or cherry-blossom phobia.
Even now, when I see Haruka's mom around this time of year, the guilt comes back.
But Obasan reads my face and right away launches into stories about Ojisan, gushing about him.
"Oh, Natsumi-chan, come on, are you stiiill worrying about that? 　If you're gonna make a face like that, then pray for Eiichiro-san to rest in peace instead! 　A man that good, there's nobody else like him!"
Obasan cheering me up by saying this has become an annual ritual.
"Thank you, Obasan... Obasan, you're a really strong person..."
"Oh my, oh my! It's all because Natsumi-chan, Akane, and Ryoji-san are always helping us out! 　Eiichiro-san died, but everyone stays with us to make up for it, so Haruka and I are doing great!"
That distinctive, falsetto-like, old-lady way of talking is a little painful to hear, but to me it's really comforting.
Now I love cherry blossoms.
Getting to gaze at the best view like this, the three of us together, I thought, was true happiness.
