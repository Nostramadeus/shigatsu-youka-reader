Shigatsu Youka ~Unity~

Eden no Sakura Arc [Eden's Cherry] · Shiyo 800-04-07 21:30 火 · 73 boxes

The storehouse fire burned only the second floor and didn't spread anywhere else.
The stench of water-soaked embers hangs over the whole settlement.
I was sent back to my room again.
Right now, Goto and the others should be investigating the storehouse.
Now that I know why I'm being isolated like this, I have no particular complaint.
This is the best method, and my way of actively cooperating.
Even so, Haruka and the others are late getting back.
Still no reply from Haruka to my message―it isn't even marked read.
Haruka, you're safe, right? You're okay, right?
I kept staring at the unchanging message screen.
"Um, Natsumi-chan?"
A knock from the hallway, and Kana-chan's voice.
"Come in."
Maybe because my heart was calmer than before, I managed to raise my voice more than I'd expected.
"Good, you look well."
"Do I?"
"Yeah... Eri-nee doesn't like keeping you shut up in here all the time either, so..."
"Hey, why am I being kept shut up in here?"
"Th-that's, um, well, um..."
Kana-chan is openly flustered by my question.
A slightly mean question, but I have to keep up the pose of not knowing why I'm shut in.
Keep it up toward Kana-chan, and toward Necro.
That said, keeping up this stance is anything but pleasant.
"U-um, Eri-nee's going to look for Haruka-chan, and she wants you to come too, Natsumi-chan!"
"What!? Really!?"
"Yeah, can you leave right away?"
"Um... yeah, I'm fine!"
That was unexpected.
Will it be all right for me to go with them?
But I'm just as worried about Haruka. There's no reason to refuse to go.
Even if going out is scary, Haruka not coming back is scarier.
"Eri-nee! I brought her!"
"Goto, you're letting me help too!?"
"Yes! We're going to search for Niimura-senpai!"
Here's one place I'd better make sure Goto, too, sees my pose of not knowing.
"Then just Kana-chan and I will go there! 　Goto, shouldn't you be looking into the storehouse!?"
Goto must not want to leave Kana-chan and me alone together.
Kana-chan might let something slip again.
"No! We have to go, all three of us...!"
"Natsumi-chan! Right now you should do what Eri-nee says!"
"O-okay..."
That settles it. The rest is going along with whatever Goto says and stating my own thoughts.
All of it to keep Necro deceived.
Still, it really doesn't feel good...
Is keeping Necro deceived really the right thing?
Keeping Necro deceived means keeping everyone deceived.
Does that really lead to a solution?
"It's about 10 minutes into this forest."
"You call it a forest, but the trees are almost all dead..."
"Yeah, they weren't like this a little while ago..."
The trees around here were almost all dead where they stood.
The trees inside the settlement had been the same way.
What on earth has happened...?
I'm sure that when I saw them this evening, the trees were sturdy and putting out new buds.
At first I thought they only looked different because it had gotten dark, but that doesn't seem to be it after all.
"Kogori-senpai, this is apparently the work of Necro―or Shigatsu Youka, as it's called, a fungus that covers this whole area. It seems to strip this mountain of its nutrients, root and all."
"Shigatsu Youka...?"
But Goto fell silent at that point.
I see. So she can't say any more than that.
Goto referred to Necro as Shigatsu Youka, but is that something she learned from the books in the storehouse?
Shigatsu Youka is a fungus breeding around here― and a fungus means something in the mold-and-mushroom family, right?
And Shigatsu Youka sucked up these trees' nutrients, so they withered.
Beyond Shigatsu Youka being a fungus that steals other plants' nutrients, I don't know.
That's all the information I can get from Goto.
The rest is for me to keep deceiving Shigatsu Youka by pretending to know nothing.
I'd like Goto to notice how much I know, if she can, but with my gift for talk that's a tall order...
"Wait."
Suddenly Kana-chan stepped in front of us.
Turning her back to us, she watched our surroundings intently.
"What's wrong?"
"Quiet... someone's there."
"Could it be Niimura-senpai?"
With that, Kana-chan took a knife in each hand.
"Who's there!? You're there, aren't you!? Come out!"
When Kana-chan, knives raised, shouted at the top of her voice―
From behind trees and out of the dead grass, people in Noh masks and red robes crept into view.
The big moon in the sky lit the nata and maces in their hands.
"What? These people..."
"Probably people from the settlement..."
Residents of the Arata settlement!?
The people who were helping put out the fire until just now―why would they dress so menacingly...
"Kana. Our guests. Turn back at once. You must not go beyond this point."
"Move! I have to find Haruka-chan!"
Are they blocking us from looking for Haruka?
Dressed like this, with weapons, even.
Something must have happened to Haruka.
"Eri-nee! Natsumi-chan! Get behind me!"
Goto pulled me by the arm, and we stepped back behind Kana-chan.
A girl as small as Kana-chan taking on a crowd of adults―that's reckless!
Isn't there some way to slip past them and get to Haruka?
"Mm... Everyone, wait!"
Just then, a woman's shout came from beyond the residents.
Haruka? Obasan?
No, it's neither of their voices.
Who could it be?
"Sis...!"
"Chiga-nee!"
"............ Who's that?"
The one who appeared there was a woman in a dirty dress, wrapped in a mud-caked poncho blanket, her hair a mess.
What's more, her face was bleeding and there was blood on her clothes.
From her air and the shape of her face she must be a beauty by nature, but the mismatch between that beautiful air and her state was eerie.
And above all, she was gripping a bloodstained knife.
There's no doubt she's Kana-chan's missing sister.
But part of me can't simply be glad Chiga-nee has been found.
It's not that intuition, but I feel something ominous.
"Mm... Auntie and Haruka went to the abandoned hut out toward Toyotake! 　All of you, take shelter there too!"
"Sis, but that place is..."
"Kana! Take care of those two! There's no time! Leave this to me!"
"G-got it! Eri-nee! Natsumi-chan! Hurry!"
As if ignoring my suspicion, Kana-chan grabbed my hand and tried to force her way through.
"W-wait!"
The resident standing at the very front raised a nata high.
But Chiga-nee at once planted herself in front of that resident.
"Mm... Don't get in my way. I mean it. If you lay a hand on those girls... I'll kill you!"
"Eri-nee! Hurry!"
Worried, perhaps, Goto kept looking only toward Chiga-nee, and her feet were slow.
For a moment what I felt toward Chiga-nee was hostility, but there's no doubt she came here to protect us.
Better not be caught by first impressions; better to trust Chiga-nee.
"Goto! Pull yourself together!"
To keep her out of Chiga-nee's way as much as I could, I made Goto face forward and barked at her.
Kana-chan and Chiga-nee both―do they really mean to face them alone?
Reckless sisters, or confidence backed by real ability?
"Hah... hah..."
Suddenly my field of vision opened up, and we came out into a clearing with a commanding view.
Sprinting 200 meters down a gravel road I wasn't used to had been pretty hard work.
Goto and I stopped here to catch our breath.
Kana-chan, without the slightest sign of being out of breath, was looking at the sky.
Chiga-nee told us to go to the abandoned hut, and Kana-chan seems to be obeying without question.
Haruka and Obasan are both there, she says.
That saves us the trouble of searching, but why do we have to head there too?
Rather than us going to the abandoned hut, wouldn't it be better for Haruka and the others to come back to the settlement?
"Kana-chan, how much farther to the abandoned hut?"
"About 15 minutes on foot, I guess. But it was pouring around there this evening, so the path is probably a muddy mess. It's easy to get lost, so don't leave my side, okay?"
"Hmm... Going back to the settlement now―better not even think about it, right...?"
"If everyone's acting like that, it's probably dangerous."
Hasn't Goto noticed the danger of going to the abandoned hut?
Or is she saying to go to the abandoned hut with that already in mind?
It's a hard call, but I'm worried about Haruka and the others after all.
Should I put joining up with Haruka and the others first?
"For now, all we can do is go to that abandoned hut, right..."
Chiga-nee seemed to know more about what was going on.
Goto and I understand 'what causes the curse-killings,' but not much of 'what is happening right now.'
After all, from that angle too, there's nothing for it but the abandoned hut...
"Eri-nee, we're going, okay?"
"Oh, right..."
Goto started walking, her head bowed a little.
She seemed to be racking her brains.
That clearing was a graveyard, apparently. From there, the route went by the path along the cliff to the mountain stream, then the road toward Toyotake, and on to the abandoned hut.
Did Haruka and the others take this path to the abandoned hut too?
What happened with the search for the mummy underground?
And to begin with, how does Chiga-nee know where Haruka and the others went?
What's the point of going to the abandoned hut?
At this point, all sorts of questions are welling up.
Is it enough for just us to go to the abandoned hut, or should we bring Erika-chan and Kotaro-san too?
I'd like to think it through in my own way, but I can't exchange views with Goto.
Will I have to settle it inside my own head first, and when the time is right, compare notes?
Or rather, how long am I supposed to go on pretending not to know...
If we can't even communicate like normal, it'll be hard for everyone to unite.
Keeping Shigatsu Youka deceived by keeping everyone deceived―is that really right?
Is my judgment right?
In the end, even Goto never noticed.
If so―
Just then, Goto, who had been walking with her head down, suddenly stopped.
"Goto, what's wrong?"
"Eri-nee?"
"Kogori-senpai, Kana-chan, go on to the abandoned hut, just the two of you. I still have something I have to do!"
"Wait―Goto!"
Goto turned 180 degrees and ran off toward the graveyard clearing.
"Natsumi-chan, wait here! I'll go catch Eri-nee!"
"Wait!"
I grabbed Kana-chan's arm as she started to run.
"Wait, Kana-chan. Goto decided she couldn't go on like this. So, right now..."
"But! If Eri-nee gets attacked by those guys...!"
"It's okay. If that happens, Chiga-nee will protect her, won't she?"
"............"
At that, the strength drained from Kana-chan's arm.
No, not just her arm―from her shoulders, from her whole body.
"What's wrong?"
"Natsumi-chan... Sis might be done for."
"Kana-chan, it's okay. Chiga-nee looked hurt, but she won't go down that easily―"
"No! I don't mean everyone's going to get her... It's not that... Never mind, let's just hurry to the abandoned hut."
Kana-chan took my hand and started walking.
Kana-chan's grip was strong.
Is she holding on tight so I don't run off too?
Or is she, after all, a little scared?
"Natsumi-chan, I... actually, this morning... Ah!"
"Hm?"
"N-nothing! Nothing! It's nothing!"
Kana-chan, breaking off so suddenly.
She started to say something and shut her mouth as if in a hurry―could it be...
"If you tell me, Goto will be angry with you?"
"U-um, um, well, um..."
"Because what I hear gets out to Shigatsu Youka?"
"You knew...?"
"Yeah, more or less."
What I just said also tells Shigatsu Youka what I've realized.
At first I meant to keep silent the whole time.
I'd hoped to mix in lies and keep Shigatsu Youka deceived to put us at an advantage, but that means deceiving everyone too.
Yet I thought that would make our actions and thoughts fall out of step and only invite needless confusion.
Even now we don't know what everyone is doing or thinking; stirring that up further is dangerous.
Unify our will properly and band together to face it, and a thread toward a solution might show― that's what I thought.
It may be a selfish judgment, but I can't see us standing up to something as big as Shigatsu Youka in this state.
Try to face it alone and you end up missing like Chiga-nee, or fighting covered in blood.
Everyone told me―"Trust everyone."
"Then I'll tell you... um..."
Is this the rest of what Kana-chan started to say and broke off?
"This morning, I... Sis―I'd found her."
"What?"
"Sis was at the abandoned hut."
Chiga-nee was at the abandoned hut this morning?
What is this girl saying...
"Then when you said she was missing...?"
"I'd found her. But Sis kept saying weird things."
This morning, when I was searching around the abandoned hut by myself, I smelled blood.
Fresh blood, from inside the hut.
I thought maybe Sis was there...
I went in, and sure enough, Sis was there.
But she was lying curled up in a corner, facing the wall, with her hands over her ears...
When I listened closely, she kept muttering weird things.
"I don't want to die... I don't want to die...! There's no way we can win! 　We should just surrender...! Everyone should just become cherry trees!"
"Um, Sis―"
"Everyone, die instead of me...! 　At least save me...!"
She kept muttering things like that with her hands over her ears.
I got a little scared...
"Don't tell me you left Chiga-nee there..."
"I didn't know what to do. Sis―I thought I should bring her along, but..."
She couldn't bring herself to speak to her, and left her there in the abandoned hut, she said.
Even if it's your sister, seeing someone trembling and muttering "everyone die" would be a little creepy, sure.
And for a little kid like Kana-chan, all the more...
"But... but to tell the truth... Sis coming home scared me."
"Because she was saying scary things?"
"That too, but... Sis thinks Daddy or Grandma is doing something bad. If Sis came back, Daddy and the others might get arrested, so..."
"So that's how it was..."
"I'm sorry! I've been scared of Sis since yesterday! 　She kept saying Daddy and Grandma were the culprits and poking around, and this morning she was saying everyone should die... Sis had gone strange, I thought...!"
Suspecting her family, and muttering to herself that she wished everyone dead.
How frightening it would be to have someone like that in the family― asking a grade-schooler for a calm judgment was too cruel, it seems.
Kana-chan seems to regret it, but even now she probably doesn't know what she should do.
"For now, Chiga-nee was alive, so that's good. More important, let's think about what we do from here."
"Yeah... I won't do anything cowardly anymore! 　I won't lie anymore...!"
Everyone's hearts, it seems, were more scattered than I'd thought.
Like this, nothing that could be solved gets solved.
All the more so, since the curse-killings have the people of this settlement distrusting each other.
Maybe we have a huge misunderstanding about the people of the settlement.
Just now they looked like they were attacking us, but it can't be ruled out that they had an entirely different intent.
Going to the trouble of dressing like that and telling us to go back to the settlement.
It could be read as concern for our safety.
If we, Chiga-nee included, had misread that―
Fearing Shigatsu Youka's shadow and deceiving each other is wrong, after all!
Secrets, deceptions―as ways to solve this case they're utter nonsense.
If anything, couldn't you say the curse-killings so far were fed by mutual distrust?
That negative chain has to be cut, here and now.
"Kana-chan, what we have to do is hold everyone's hearts together. I'm sure everyone's misunderstanding each other. If we talk it through properly, I'm sure we can stop the curse-killings too!"
"Natsumi-chan... You really are amazing, Natsumi-chan! 　Everyone said you never give up, Natsumi-chan!"
"That's not true. I'm not that strong at all..."
"Then you have something important that nobody else has, Natsumi-chan. That's why everyone loves you so much!"
People often say my strong point is my mental strength.
But as when I cried on Erika-chan's chest just now, I'm not that strong.
Still, I feel Kana-chan's one remark gave me an answer that fits.
When everyone is about to fall apart, I can hold them together.
I'm not doing it consciously; maybe I just hate seeing everyone face different ways and fall apart.
At times like that, I work to hold everyone together, even a little.
What I can do isn't keeping Shigatsu Youka deceived.
Shigatsu Youka has surely planned ahead for something like that.
Uniting everyone's minds to face it―that is the best plan for taking Shigatsu Youka by surprise.
