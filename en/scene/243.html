Intimidation

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-08 08:20 火 · 36 boxes

Erika-chan, Obasan, and Chiga-nee left the house early to prepare for the memorial service.
The rest of us gathered around the breakfast table.
Kotaro-san showed me a photo of Sakura-san from high school when I asked.
Her hairstyle was different from the one in the vision, but this was definitely the person I saw.
"Aren't there any photos from middle school, or from after she grew up?"
"Hardly any photos of Sakura are left. Sakura hated her looks, so she hated having her picture taken, too. She got bullied at school because of her looks..."
"Bullying, huh..."
Haruka muttered quietly.
What happened in Megasawa started with the bullying of Haruka.
That's why Sakura-san sympathized with it so deeply, but she still tried to stop the murder plan.
"Hey, Sakura-san, Kotaro-san, and Dad were in the same grade, right? Couldn't you do something?"
"Bullying isn't something you can fix that easily. Of course, Eiichiro and I were always on Sakura's side. But Sakura told us not to have anything to do with her at school... Said otherwise we'd end up in a bad spot too..."
Of course. It was bad enough to make Obasan consider even murder for Haruka's sake.
It's become a social problem, so I can imagine it can't be solved easily.
"Kotaro-san, you know about Droga, don't you?"
"Wha...!?"
Goto had suddenly started asking questions that went right to the heart of it.
"Y-yeah, I k-k-know it... It's a drug that was made in this settlement..."
"Is the raw material cherry roots?"
"H-how do you know that much...!"
Kotaro-san was visibly shaken.
Goto's hypothesis seemed to have been right.
"Is it really not still being made today? 　Isn't the Niimura main house's cherry tree dying because its roots are being damaged and harvested?"
Goto seemed to be checking her hypothesis against the answers right here.
Or she was trying to see whether Kotaro-san would lie here.
"Impressive, genius girl... It's true, we do make Droga. This many curse-killings keep happening. You need it to protect yourself, don't you?"
"If it was made for self-defense, then naturally everyone in the settlement knows about it, right?"
"Yeah, that's right... But Mom, Chigaya, and Kana don't know. Partly because they were still kids, and Mom would never allow anyone to make Droga..."
"............? 　What are you talking about...?"
Kana-chan seemed to find the talk hard to follow...
"May I check with Erika-chan and Chiga-nee?"
"............ All right... Mom's going to be angry..."
That probably wasn't the answer of someone who was lying.
Even so, Goto's gaze was awfully sharp.
"Hey, Goto... does Droga have anything to do with this?"
"No, I don't think it's directly connected to the curse-killings, but I've got an idea why Niimura-senpai's father made Droga, so..."
"Even if the one in Natsumi's vision was Dad, do you have proof he was making Droga?"
"I don't. It's an assumption, after all."
"Tch...! Yeah, okay..."
Her own dad had been making Droga, and hearing that had put Haruka in a pretty bad mood.
"Your mother often says it, doesn't she? That they were a husband and wife who were just alike."
"So what about it..."
"Your mother tried to commit murder to protect you. In the same way, couldn't your father have tried to stop the bullying with Droga to protect Sakura-san, even if he wouldn't go as far as killing?"
"You...!"
"Niimura-senpai, what would you do? If Kogori-senpai were being bullied and only violent means were left."
"Geez... Fine, I get it! It's just an assumption anyway, right? Reason it out however you want..."
It seemed Haruka was completely out of sorts.
Even hypothetically, nobody would feel good hearing their own parents talked about like that.
"Eiichiro, huh... Well, he groveled a lot, but he was the type who valued bonds... Sakura, he used Droga to help her? Put that way, I can't say I don't buy it... One for all, all for one."
"That motto's getting seriously fishy, too... Fine, whatever. Dad was using Droga to protect Sakura-san, right? Then that makes sense."
"No, not using it. I think he got hurt when he was testing it. Probably while trying to show Sakura-san what Droga could do at the secret base..."
"Haah... What a klutz Dad was... But is Droga really something you can use that easily?"
"When we use it, the plan is to inject it into the muscle. Syringes are easy to buy online."
"Kotaro-san, is there a book or something that describes how to make Droga?"
"Yeah, it's in this house's storehouse. Eiichiro must have found it, gathered roots underground, and made Droga."
"Is it something even Eiichiro-san, a middle schooler at the time, could make?"
"It's not that hard a method. If you boil freshly dug roots in water, you get a liquid that separates into layers. One of those layers is Droga. Boil off more of the water and you get solid crystals. Burning it for smoke uses those, and while that works on lots of people at once, it puts them in a pretty intense high. If it stays a liquid, that side effect is kept down."
"Kotaro-san, you know a lot about it. Haven't you used it yourself, maybe?"
"Of course not. Nobody knows what side effects might show up, so using it is a last resort. The book covers those effects, too."
"I see. Please go on."
"From the contents, it looks like it was written about 50 years ago. I'd guess the Grand Witch of the time asked for research data in exchange for providing the cherry trees."
Goto's hypothesis lined up with it almost exactly.
There may have been an institution researching Droga that traded research results for the cherry trees.
Haruka's dad happened to find that text and ended up making Droga.
"Then, Kotaro-san, now that we've come this far, please tell us. Who sent the Droga to Megasawa City?"
Up to now, though his eyes had been darting around, Kotaro-san had answered smoothly, but now his mouth stopped.
He looked down and stayed silent, as if lost in thought.
"Sorry, I really don't know about that... To begin with, Sakura would never tell even me that she was sending Droga."
"Then who do you think sent it, Kotaro-san?"
"Mmm... who could it be... Sakura confiding something like that in anyone... I can't even think who that would be..."
"Then what do you think the Droga was sent for?"
"What for...? 　To make Mifuyu-chan kill elementary school kids no matter what, right?"
Goto's manner was an interrogation by now.
Goto was truly trying to use the tiniest contradictions to dig out Kotaro-san's lies.
In the same way, Kotaro-san must have understood what Goto's questions were after.
Unrecognizable as the rowdy, endlessly cheerful little kid she usually is, at times like this Goto has a gaze that is abnormally intimidating.
If Kotaro-san is really innocent, he'll choose his words so as not to look suspicious.
If Kotaro-san is not innocent, he'll choose his words so as not to look suspicious.
Either way, the behavior is the same. Right now, Kotaro-san is in a really tough spot.
"If you're going to keep quiet, you don't have to say anything more."
"I'm sorry..."
"Thank you for the meal! I'll go get ready for the thirteenth-year memorial service, then!" [TN: Buddhist memorials count the year of death as the first year.]
Goto left the living room without eating even a tenth of her usual amount.
"Haah..."
Kotaro-san heaved a big sigh.
"Don't you think Goto went too far just now?"
"Yeah... she was a little scary..."
"Hey, is Eri-nee actually like that?"
Kana-chan, faced with a look on Goto's face she had never seen before, was frightened.
"No, that's not it. She really wants to solve the curse-killings, so she just got serious."
"Oh... right, yeah!"
―But I know.
When I'd only just met her, Goto would sometimes let that kind of look show.
The gloomy Goto who couldn't trust people and shut herself away in her own world.
With someone she can't trust, she probably takes that attitude without holding back.
Since she kept that attitude to the very end, it means Goto's guess has gotten pretty close to certainty.
