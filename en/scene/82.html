Haruka's Hometown

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-07 11:00 火 · 38 boxes

It had been a while since I'd come to the area around the station. The cherry blossoms were in pretty bloom here too.
"Senpaaai!"
At the bus stop 50 meters away, a small figure.
Carrying a big bag, little Goto came running.
"Kogori-senpai! What kind of lunch is it!? I came good and hungry! 　I've planned all kinds of operations for conquering that lunch!"
"Hey now, worrying about lunch right off the bat...? Natsumi gave orders to show up only 70% full, didn't she?"
"Ehh? Did she now? 　Isn't that just your imagination? I'm pretty sure I never got it."
"Playing dumb about something you'll get caught on in two seconds... Anyway, we don't have much time. Let's hurry to the platform!"
To get to the Arata settlement from here, we had to change trains three times and then drive 30 minutes up a mountain road.
It was a fairly long trip, but with the three of us, we probably wouldn't get bored.
Would we be able to reach the Arata settlement by 17:00?
"Hey, hey, Goto, I was just talking with Haruka, and how about the three of us go somewhere over Golden Week?"
"Ooh! Golden Week! 　Hmm, there might be a family trip. I can keenly sense Dad and the others getting all sorts of things ready..."
"Oh, I see..."
"But you don't really have to stick to Golden Week, do you? 　I think even an overnight trip on a weekend would be fun!"
"Oh, right, when you think about it, that's true!"
"An overnight trip on a weekend, huh. Where would be good..."
"Then let's plan that operation too as we proceed to the Arata settlement!"
“The express train bound for Megasawa will arrive shortly.”
It seemed the first train we'd ride was approaching.
First we'd go as far as Megasawa in Iizawa Prefecture, then head north from there on local trains.
Now then, the opening skirmish of our trip, here it begins!
It had been a while since I'd come to the area around the station.
The cherry blossoms were in pretty bloom here too.
There was no sign of Goto at the meeting spot yet.
"No Goto yet, huh..."
"Haruka, are we okay on time?"
"We leave in 15 minutes. If we miss this one, we'll get to Arata way later..."
"Goto of all people wouldn't be late, though..."
Just then, Haruka's phone rang.
"Goto, you're still not here?"
The caller seemed to be Goto.
Then Goto's loud voice carried over to me too.
"Senpai, I'm so sorry! I was spacing out, and then it was this late...! 　I'll be there in 20 minutes!"
"Hey now, then you won't make the train..."
"Go on without me! I entrust the rest to you...!"
"Natsumi... what should we do?"
"Let's wait for her... It can't be helped if we're late."
"Hey, Goto! Be grateful to Natsumi! Archangel Natsumi-sama says she'll wait for Goto!"
"Waaah, Kogori-senpaaai! I, the genius girl Erina Goto, will follow you for the rest of my life!"
"Quit talking nonsense and get over here already! If you don't make the next train, we won't get to Arata today!"
"Geez..."
"But Goto being late because she was spacing out is rare, right? Did something happen...?"
"Well, we'll give Goto a nice long interrogation about that when she gets here."
Haruka flashed a slightly creepy smile.
Yesterday was the opening ceremony and today was the entrance ceremony, and both days school let out in the morning.
For curing my spring-break daze, a schedule this relaxed was a help.
But now we had important business ahead, important enough to blow that spring-break daze away.
"See ya, Goto. Don't be late!"
"Understood! The genius girl Erina Goto shall race toward the success of the operation!"
Goto gave a crisp salute.
"What time's the train again?"
"12:40. We can't take it too easy. If we're late, we won't make it to Arata today."
Tomorrow was the thirteenth-year memorial service for Haruka's dad. [TN: Buddhist memorials count the year of death as the first year.]
This time, as a special exception, we outsiders had been allowed to attend.
At last I would be able to put my hands together in prayer for Haruka's dad, who died trying to save me.
Maybe I'm not supposed to be glad about it, but I still am.
"Okay, here at 12:00, then."
Since birth, Haruka and I have been childhood friends, and for the past 10 years we've been next-door neighbors.
For just the 2 years before that, a lot happened and we were apart, but Haruka came back to Motoki-cho.
But why Haruka came back is hazy to me.
If I remember right, Haruka's dad died, and they went to Megasawa so Obasan could recover, and came back once she was well again... was that it?
"I'm home! Mom, lunch, quick!"
"Oh, you're back."
"Huh? What about work? Skipping?"
"Hey, what do you mean, skipping... Yesterday you told me not to go to the office, remember? Said to go in the afternoon..."
"Oh! Right, right!"
That's right, yesterday I heard Haruka's dad's voice, so I told Dad to stay home for the morning.
"Mom left lunch for you over there."
In the kitchen, three huge tiered lunch boxes were stacked up.
"Um... I can't eat this much...?"
"Natsumi, it's so you'd be covered even if you got back late. As a boxed lunch you can take it with you, and the three of you can eat it, right? 　Those lunch boxes are cheap ones, so you can toss them when you're done."
"I see, that makes it easy! 　So, where's Mom?"
"She should be back soon. She went to buy a gift to bring to the memorial service."
"Right, we can't show up empty-handed when they're putting us up."
Then the door opened, and I felt the air in the house stir.
It seemed Mom had just come home.
"Welcome back, Mom."
"Oh, Natsumi, take this as a gift, okay?"
I was handed yet another enormous thing, a box of sake.
"That's a bit much to carry..."
"Want me to drive you to the station?"
"No, no! Stay home today! 　We're taking a taxi, and Haruka'll help me carry stuff. Oh! I need to tell those two not to eat lunch!"
I headed into my room, pulling out my phone.
While composing a message to the two of them, I did a final check of my luggage.
―Um, messages, check. Change of clothes, check. Everything else, check!
Looked like I hadn't forgotten anything.
"Natsumiii! The taxi's here!"
"Okaaay! Coming right now!"
I carried my luggage out of my room.
"Okay, Dad, Mom, I'm off!"
"Be careful!"
I left the house with barely a goodbye.
The wind that suddenly blew up felt so good against my whole body.
Oh, that's right!
"Dad! It's about time you cut back on drinking! Ojisan says so!"
"Ah, r-right... Damn that guy..."
Dad had been drinking a little too much lately.
Actually, the warning wasn't from Ojisan, but from me.
Well, it's a white lie in a good cause. It's for my family's health, so it's forgivable, right?
"Harukaaa! The taxi's here!"
The moment I said it, the Niimuras' door opened.
"Perfect timing! I figured it was about time. Whoa, that's a lot of luggage..."
"They made me take all sorts of stuff..."
"Okay, I'm in charge of this one!"
With that, Haruka took the sake for the gift.
Then Haruka put her hand on the Kogoris' doorknob.
"Ojisan, hey there!"
Apparently Dad and Mom were still standing in the entryway.
"Yeah, be careful. Come to think of it, what are you doing once you get to the station over there? It's pretty far, isn't it?"
"Mom's coming to pick us up in the car. She went on ahead 'cause she's busy with the preparations, but she says she should be free by the time we get there. See ya!"
Obasan was going to meet us at the station closest to the Arata settlement.
Obasan had gone ahead to the Arata settlement the day before yesterday to prepare for the thirteenth-year memorial service.
"To Motoki Station, please!"
Normally we got around by bike or car, so we hardly ever took taxis.
So just from getting into a taxi, our excitement was already building.
I knew it was inappropriate, but it felt a little like going on a trip.
"Hey, Haruka, how about the three of us take a trip somewhere over the next holidays?"
"Hmm, a trip, huh... Come to think of it, just the three of us have never left Motoki-cho together. Ah, but... I'm taking entrance exams this year. Mom might not let me..."
"Ahh, tough break... Then how about going before exam season really kicks in?"
"Then I guess Golden Week. But won't everywhere be crowded... Actually, won't it all be booked up already?"
"Right, it's less than a month away. Then... maybe find some hideaway inn?"
"I bet everybody's got the same idea. 　One way or another, I get the feeling we'll end up at some guesthouse in Motoki-cho..."
"Ha! But wouldn't that be fun in its own way? 　In May, Motoki-cho doesn't get many tourists, and as long as the three of us can go somewhere, anywhere's fine with me."
"Well, yeah! No objections here either!"
