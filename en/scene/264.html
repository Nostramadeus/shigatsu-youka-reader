The Baton of the Heart

Eden no Sakura Arc [Eden's Cherry] · Shiyo 800-04-05 16:00 火 · 69 boxes

Clusters of cherry trees came into view now and then.
The view from the train window is the same year after year.
Pass by enough times, and you end up memorizing the buildings that serve as landmarks.
A tower whose purpose I can't figure out.
A house with an outlandish color scheme and structure, as if designed by an artist far too avant-garde.
Neither the tower nor the outlandish house will stand forever.
In a few decades, a few centuries, it will all become a different view.
This year, the Arata settlement has to change too.
I decided to go to the Arata settlement on April 5―three days before the thirteenth-year memorial.
I'd originally planned to arrive on April 7, but I could no longer afford to take my time.
Goto-chan, who amid all that innocent, childish behavior had given me such pointed advice.
I can't thank her enough.
I think I understand now why Auntie brought her all the way to Tokyo and treated her at an expensive ryotei.
That day, we feasted on the ryotei's lavish cooking.
I'd only gone over the outline of the curse-killings with Goto-chan, but even that took a great weight off my mind.
"Wow! Chiga-nee, you're so slim, but you eat so much!"
"Mm... Like I said, I can eat as much as I want when I put my mind to it..."
And then Goto-chan deftly plucked a piece of my fried fugu with her fingers.
"Ah! Hey, Goto-ch―, you―!"
"Mmm, delicious! My set didn't have any fugu, so I just had to try some! 　Operation successful!"
Without a hint of remorse, Goto-chan flashed a big grin.
I decided to keep an eye on Goto-chan's movements while I ate.
"Chiga-nee, if you keep staring at me like that, you won't taste a thing!"
As if I'd fall for a trick like that...!
"Why, Chigaya-chan! You can add a side of fried fugu if you like!"
"Mm... No, thank you!"
That line was meant for Goto-chan, not me.
Besides, it's not that I mind having less fried fugu. What stings is that it got stolen.
But even as I thought that, it had been a long time since I'd had such a lively meal.
Whether I'm eating with college friends or at parties, I'm mostly putting on my good-girl act.
Or else I get drunk and show a side of me even more embarrassing than the real me.
Eating a meal while showing the perfectly ordinary me like this is rare for me in Tokyo.
I watched Goto-chan closely, in case she tried something funny again.
Goto-chan kept smiling at me the whole time.
Less a big grin than a soft smile.
Looking at that smile made me a little embarrassed, and I lowered my eyes a bit.
And then―
"Ah! Hey, again, you―!"
"Yes, operation successful again! 　Ooh! So this is the beef nigiri! I hear that raw, lean tastes better than marbled! 　What will the one here taste like?"
Goto-chan tossed my beef nigiri into her mouth, just like that.
So Goto-chan had been smiling at me to make me look away...!
And anyway, if she wasn't full, she could just ask Auntie...!
When I looked at Auntie, she was watching as if it were on the other side of a TV screen, like it had nothing to do with her.
Taking neither side, she was happily enjoying her meal with a smile.
"Obasan, gochisosama deshita!"
"Ohohohoho! Did you enjoy it?"
"Yes!"
Goto-chan flashed Auntie a big grin.
In the end, we didn't talk about the curse-killings again after that.
She'd said we would continue once we finished eating, but...
"Obasan! Since we're here, I'm going to have Chiga-nee come sightseeing around Tokyo with me!"
"Huh?"
"Why, how nice! 　Then I'll go show my face at the company's branch office! 　Well then, see you next time in the Arata settlement!"
Without even waiting for my reaction, Auntie headed off in the opposite direction from the station.
"Come on, Chiga-nee, let's go!"
"Mm... Goto-chan, is there somewhere you want to go?"
"Yes! Tokyo Skytree, of course!"
"Mm... Are you sure? I hear the wait for the Skytree is really long."
"If anything, the longer it is, the more we can talk, right?"
Apparently Goto-chan meant to talk about the curse-killings now.
But in that case, we could have done it over the meal, or somewhere more relaxing.
From Takaracho Station we took the Asakusa Line and headed for Oshiage Station.
"Wow...! It's really tall up close...!"
Little Goto-chan looked up at the tallest building in Japan.
In front of the Skytree, crowds of people were lined up.
Now we were going to join this line too and head for the top.
"Uuugh... at my height I can't see a thing..."
"Mm... Goto-chan, are you really sure? We could go somewhere easier..."
"No, I want this place! Please, just humor me a little!"
"Mm... I don't mind, but..."
"Chiga-nee, have you ever been up the Skytree?"
"Mm... Today's my first time."
"I thought so. I've heard that people from Tokyo don't go to either the Skytree or Tokyo Tower."
"Mm... Now that you mention it, that's true. Maybe it's because we can go anytime we want?"
"So I think there are lots of cases where people from outside Tokyo see Tokyo from a different angle. 　Maybe the Arata settlement is the same."
"............?"
"I live in Motoki-cho, but I think the Motoki-cho I see is probably different from the Motoki-cho you see. When the person looking changes, the place changes too, and when the angle changes, it should look different as well."
"Mm... Is going up the Skytree meant to cultivate that kind of thinking too, not just to see the sights?"
"Hmm, that's not really what I had in mind. I just wanted to go to the Skytree, that's all. But when you think about things by tying them together like this, unexpected hints can turn up. You never know where a hint might be lying around, so I wanted you to come somewhere you had never been. Choosing the Skytree was purely for my own fun!"
I see. Go somewhere you've never been, anywhere at all, to find hints.
With Goto-chan along, I feel like I could pick up those hints smoothly.
"Hmm... a 30-minute wait for the elevator... Well, Chiga-nee, that's plenty of time for us to talk!"
And here Goto-chan took out a notebook and pen.
It was crammed with notes on what Auntie and I had told her and on the Arata settlement.
"About the curse-killings, I've heard most of it from Obasan. I think I probably know about as much as you do. But I think there are things you saw or heard that you haven't put into words, or that you've forgotten even though they're important. Let's start by digging those up."
"Then I'm going to ask you six questions, so answer with whatever comes to mind. It may get a little like an interrogation, but I mean no harm by it, so bear with me."
"Mm... An interrogation... All right."
"Then, first question. What happened in the Arata settlement?"
"Huh? Huh?"
"The question is what happened in the Arata settlement."
That's perfectly obvious, isn't it...
"Mm... After my mom killed herself, her head was cut off by Dad. Then every year after that, I'm supposed to have killed one person, five in all. For the last four years, even though I haven't laid a hand on anyone, one person a year has been killed just the same."
"Thank you. Now, second question. Who was killed, and who did the killing?"
Another question like that. That's something we already know.
"Mm... I killed the first five. The others, I don't know. The ones killed were all people who suspected Dad. But considering how they were killed, I hadn't killed anyone at all. That's what Auntie said."
"How were they killed? 　Oh, this is the third question."
"Mm... Apparently all of them were killed by having their heads smashed in. I thought I'd stabbed them to death, so that's a contradiction."
Goto-chan didn't so much as nod along. She just kept taking notes.
What she was writing was exactly what I'd said.
"Then, fourth question. When did the incidents happen?"
"Mm... It started on Shiyo 790, April 17, the anniversary of Mom's death. Then every April after that. The dates vary, though."
"Fifth question. Where did the incidents happen?"
"Mm... The Arata settlement."
"Last question. Why did these incidents happen?"
"Mm... Why?"
I finally noticed the pattern in the questions.
'What' happened.
'Who' killed, and who was killed.
'How' they were killed.
'When' it happened.
'Where' it happened.
'Why' it happened.
It's all built on the 5W1H (What/Who/How/When/Where
/Why).
The only one I couldn't answer right away was 'Why.'
If you ask why the incidents happened, it's because I killed the people who suspected Dad.
But the way they were killed doesn't match what I remember either, and I don't know why Mom killed herself.
"Chiga-nee, not ready yet?"
"Huh? Oh, um..."
Goto-chan had still been waiting for my answer to 'why.'
"Mm... I don't know. Or rather, I can't even tell what started it all in the first place..."
And Goto-chan closed her notebook without writing down my last words.
"Chiga-nee, this is the 'root' of the truth of the curse-killings."
"Mm... What do you mean, the 'root' of the truth?"
The moment she said 'root' of the truth, I was seized by the sense that everything around us had suddenly gone still.
"Now, Chiga-nee, listen carefully. 'Why' is the only one of the 5W1H that you know nothing at all about. As for who, when, where, what, and how, even if some of it is fake, you have a grasp of those to some extent. But why it came to this, that is something nobody knows. But in fact, this is also the biggest hint. Because digging into 'why nobody knows' is what works."
I sort of understood what Goto-chan was getting at, but I couldn't quite grasp what she actually intended.
"Mm... Sorry, but what should I do?"
"What you should do― is to doubt everything. Chiga-nee―what you saw, what you heard, what you arrived at― doubt all of it."
"Mm... Is that what you meant earlier by 'a contradiction is the truth'?"
At that, Goto-chan lowered her eyes a little.
"That's a little different. 'A contradiction is the truth' means that the cause is something common sense says is impossible. 'Doubting everything' means asking 'Is that really so?' and 'Why is it so?'―doubting, and digging deeper."
"Mm... For example, say we found out A-san was the culprit... Do you mean I keep asking 'Is it really A-san?' and 'Why A-san?'"
"Exactly. The more you dig, the clearer the 'why' becomes. But you must be very careful not to be fooled by false appearances. And something common sense says is impossible should be flickering in and out of sight there."
"Mm... All right."
"I'm sorry, but for now, this is as far as my advice goes. You and Obasan both have too many gaps in what you know, so there isn't much I can tell you."
"Mm... No, you've given me lots of hints. The people of the Arata settlement have been thinking about it for 10 years, and yet you gave me a foothold toward the truth in such a short time. I'm really grateful."
"Ehehe! I'm glad to hear you say that!"
Goto-chan flashed a big grin.
"Now then, to solve the curse-killings, there's something I'd like you to do from here on."
"Mm... Whatever it is, tell me."
"Please go to the Arata settlement as soon as you can. And there, I want you to look into whether there's anything in the Arata settlement that nobody knows."
"Mm... All right. I just need to look into things based on the advice you've just given me, right?"
"Yes! You can ask people, and if there are any of the storehouse's old books or the like, please look through them."
"Mm... I have a few ideas along those lines."
"If there's anything you don't understand once you get to the settlement, I'll help too. If you send me photos of the books and such, I'll translate them."
"Also, just to be safe, I'll say once more what you should do. Behind these incidents is an unknown power― there is a 'someone' who misleads precognitives."
"Mm... Misleads...?"
"Oh, didn't I tell you? The reason 'someone' lets precognitives hear voices is that it works in that 'someone's' favor."
"This precognition is the ability to receive signals sent out by 'someone.' The accuracy of the danger sense, and making Kogori-senpai hear a voice imitating Eiichiro Niimura-san― there is clearly intelligence at work. And a living thing with intelligence acts in ways that work in its own favor."
"So from now on, even if you do get a danger sense, you had better not trust it. Even with the danger sense, keep asking 'why,' and doubt everything."
―That was the advice I got from Goto-chan.
Illusions disguised as precognition.
The pursuit of 'Why.'
Elements outside common sense.
The existence of someone manipulating them.
I'd turned it over idly since then, but still nothing came to mind.
Even lately, whenever I'm about to run into danger, I can still foresee it as always.
By Goto-chan's logic, does that mean it's bad for 'someone' if a precognitive dies?
In other words, precognitives get used at some point...?
But I can't deduce any further than this on my own.
Come to think of it, Goto-chan had said this too.
"Wow! It really is high! 　Paying extra to come up to the 450-meter observation deck was worth it! Operation successful!"
"Mm... It really is... You can see all the way to Mount Fuji."
At times like this, Goto-chan got as excited as a kid her age, or even younger.
"Hey, hey, Chiga-nee, how does Tokyo look from up here?"
Right. How does the place I live look when I see it from here?
To be honest, I'd seen the view from here online, so it didn't strike me as anything in particular.
The only thing I hadn't expected was how long the wait in line had been.
"Mm... Honestly, the view is what I expected... but getting up here was a real ordeal."
"I completely agree! I never thought there'd be so many people... Which makes the view from up here all the more amazing!"
"Mm... Goto-chan, are you glad you got to go up the Skytree?"
"Yes! Thank you for coming with me!"
"Mm... I should be thanking you, for listening to me."
"That's all because you told me everything honestly. If you hadn't told me what you did and about your danger sense, I couldn't have thought it through like this."
"Mm... Then I'm glad I told you. Thank you, really, for helping a total stranger like me."
"No, no! Niimura-senpai is always looking out for me! 　When Senpai's cousin is in trouble, this genius girl can't let a treasure go to waste!"
And Goto-chan flashed a big grin.
What an interesting girl.
She looks like she's fooling around, but there's sincerity and heart in every single thing she does.
I'm sure Haruka is living happily in Motoki-cho too.
"I'm sure that once the curse-killings are solved, everyone will be happy. You, of course, and Niimura-senpai, and Kogori-senpai, and Obasan―everyone, everyone! 　And then I'll be so happy!"
I'll solve it not just for me, but for Haruka, and for Goto-chan herself.
Goto-chan may not look it, but she seems very loyal.
It's a corny way to think, but if we all join forces like this, I'm sure we can reach the truth.
Thank you, Goto-chan.
I will get to the truth of the case, without fail.
The train entered a tunnel.
Once we're through this tunnel, there'll be almost no cherry blossoms to see.
Three days―before the thirteenth-year memorial begins, I'll solve every mystery.
That's the baton Goto-chan handed me.
