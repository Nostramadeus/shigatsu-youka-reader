Shunned

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-07 19:20 火 · 35 boxes

Our rescue turned out to be a bigger production than I'd thought.
Not only had they put up a ladder, every buggy in the settlement had been sent out.
"Well, well, you've all had a rough time. I'm sorry my Kana dragged you into this."
This was the prime suspect, Kotaro Niimura.
He really did look just like Haruka's dad, whom I'd seen in the memorial photo.
"Ohoho, Haruka! That's one more good experience under your belt!"
Obasan was as energetic as ever...
"All right, then, let's give these young ladies a ride to the Niimura house! 　Oh, and I haven't had a drink yet today!"
This man of about 50 was apparently named Shirozaki-san.
He wasn't directly related to the Niimura family, apparently, but came from a family line that went back a long way in the settlement.
From here on, they would take us riding double.
Haruka rode with Obasan, Goto with Kotaro-san, and I climbed onto the buggy Shirozaki-san was driving.
Kana-chan and Chiga-nee were apparently walking home from here.
"All right! Then it's a race to see who reaches the finish first, me or the buggies!"
"Hey, Kana! We've got guests aboard, so we're driving safely over here!"
Kotaro-san scolded Kana-chan, but she'd already taken off running.
"The roads are bad today, so hold on tight to your driver!"
When Obasan called out, the buggies all set off at once.
Shrugging off the rain-soaked mud, the buggy moved nimbly along.
Every time we went through a big puddle, muddy water sprayed up spectacularly, and it felt amazing.
It was my first time riding a buggy, but the springs on the tires? Or rather the cushioning worked better than I'd expected, so the ride wasn't bad even on the rough road.
It was like being on an amusement park ride, and pretty fun.
"Um, Natsumi-chan, was it!?"
Shirozaki-san called out to me loudly.
"Yes! Haruka's childhood friend, Natsumi Kogori!"
I answered loudly too, so as not to lose to the buggy's engine.
"Boy, you ran into some real trouble right after getting here! 　But the settlement has proper electricity and running water, so you'll be fine!"
"Really!? I'd gone and imagined somewhere a lot less convenient!"
"Up until a few years ago, cell phones didn't even work, though! 　But Grand Witch-sama― um, the elder here has been changing all sorts of things!"
"Uh... Aren't there any mysterious things in a settlement like this!? 　Like ghosts showing up!"
"Ghosts, huh! When I was a kid, I used to do tests of courage and such with the other boys, but we'd almost always run into animals partway through, and before we knew it, it'd turned into a hunting contest!"
Hunting?
Looking for the small contradictions Goto had mentioned earlier.
Could I draw some contradiction out of Shirozaki-san here?
"How do you hunt!?"
"Adults use guns! Kids use homemade bows and arrows! 　Nowadays nobody uses bows and arrows anymore, though!"
"Then how do people like Kana-chan hunt!?"
Now, how will he answer...!?
"A nata and a mace, and maybe a knife at most! Hunting bears with things like that, Kana-chan's really amazing! 　A hunter with a bright future!"
Just as Chiga-nee had said, she really did hunt with a nata and a mace.
No contradiction there... not really...
"Then, Shirozaki-san, do you use a nata and a mace too!?"
"No, no, I'm nothing that grand! 　Aside from Kana-chan and Chigaya-chan, everybody's only a hair better than your average person with them! 　Oh! But I'm good at cutting flies out of the air with a nata! I taught Mifuyu-chan once!"
"That's still plenty amazing!"
"Hahaha! Cutting flies with a nata won't fill your belly!"
Hmm... Hunting talk wasn't getting me any new information...
Wasn't there some way to bring up the curse-killings naturally?
"Hey, Mom! Drive more safely, will you!"
"Ohohohoho! If you're a Niimura woman, you'd better get used to this much!"
Haruka's buggy shot past at a ridiculous speed, trailing loud laughter.
"Senpaaai!"
Then the buggy carrying Goto caught up from behind.
The road had gotten wide enough to ride side by side.
"Aww, I really can't beat the buggies!"
Beyond them, Kana-chan and Chiga-nee were leaping around among the trees.
Slower than the buggies or not, being able to move like that is really amazing.
"Shirozaki-san! This settlement seems like a fun place!"
"............ That so!? I'm glad if you think so! 　If you can take home nothing but fun memories, that'd be the best!"
There was something a little loaded about that reply.
"Is something going on!? Something bad, maybe!"
"Hmm... Well, yeah. But it's nothing you girls need to worry about, Natsumi-chan!"
If there's no lie in that answer, Shirozaki-san doesn't know why we came here.
The only ones who know are the people of the Niimura family.
I see... Everyone in the settlement is a suspect, so they aren't carelessly spreading the word about us...
"Oh, right! Same as the late Sakura-san, I have a precognitive ability, only at this time of year! 　If you ever have any trouble, come talk to me!"
The buggy I was on braked hard.
The others didn't notice and went on far ahead.
"Natsumi-chan... I don't know whether what you just said is true or not. But you must never, ever tell anyone else what you just said. Understood?"
I knew it...! He reacted to my being able to hear Ojisan's voice!
"Uh... Is there something about it...?"
"That power... is a witch's power... If anyone else found out, it would turn into a serious matter. So you must never tell anyone, all right? I'll forget about this too..."
With that, Shirozaki-san got the buggy moving again.
Sure, if you call it a psychic power, maybe it is that sort of thing.
But here in the Arata settlement, it seemed it was not so much rare as shunned.
"Uh... In a little more detail..."
"I've already forgotten what you said."
"Sorry..."
Still, Shirozaki-san had given me a small hint.
I should probably tell Goto and Haruka about this.
As for Chiga-nee... I'll decide after I hear what Goto thinks.
Earlier I told Chiga-nee about my danger sense too, but now that I know it's a shunned power, I have to be careful about telling anyone.
