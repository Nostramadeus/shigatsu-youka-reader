The Arata Settlement

Meicho Arc [Clear Sign] · Shiyo 800-04-08 14:30 水 · 39 boxes

"Goto, let me sort this out one more time. The shinigami this time is Mom. The shinigami in the past were Natsumi's parents. The real target this time is me. Natsumi's dad got caught in the crossfire. Right?"
Goto, still holding her head, nodded.
"But there's still a lot I don't get. The shinigami must figure we might go to the police station, right? 　But then the shinigami can't lay a hand on us. The plan should end in failure."
"That's... I'm sorry... I don't know..."
"And then there's the reason for killing me. That Mom would hate me enough to kill me..."
"That... I don't know either..."
Goto, of all people, was throwing up her hands. Had our reasoning hit a dead end here?
"Senpai, do you know what the shinigami looks like, or anything?"
"This is my guess, but a nata and a mace, and it's wearing big red robes. And it should have a Noh mask on."
"A Noh mask? Why a Noh mask, of all things..."
"Maybe it wanted to dress in something to do with death? 　Besides, I thought the shinigami was really Dad, so a Noh mask and red robes, a nata and a mace―"
"No, um, w-wait a second! Things have gotten all kinds of strange here, haven't they?"
Did I say something strange?
"Senpai, tell me more about the Noh masks and red robes!"
"No, what's there to tell... Of course you wear a Noh mask at a memorial service, right? 　And the dead person, as a shinigami, has a Noh mask, red robes, a nata, and a mace. Following that, the people at the funeral dress the same as the dead person."
"Huh...?"
"I don't really get it, but that outfit is something really important in this case. I've never heard of a custom of wearing Noh masks at memorial services!"
Come to think of it, I'd never been to anyone else's memorial service. Were the Niimura family's services special?
"When you say shinigami, the usual image is a skeleton with a big scythe. A Noh mask and red robes..."
"No, I know that much. But Dad's family home is in the Arata settlement, and there a shinigami is a Noh mask and red robes. In the settlement, they call the dead person a shinigami, and the people at the memorial service dress the same as the shinigami. Sometimes on TV I see these lazy-looking funerals where people just wear suits, but if you do it properly, it's gotta be a Noh mask and red robes, right?"
"So, Niimura-senpai, at your family's place, everyone at a memorial, the attendees and the dead person alike, wears a Noh mask and red robes?"
"Yeah, that's right. You end up with a whole row of Noh masks."
"It all clicks now... At last I can see the right angle..."
Goto gathered up the pen and notepad she'd tossed aside.
"Senpai, this case may have much deeper roots. I thought your mom was the ringleader, but something felt a little off to me. How could she have pulled it off so smoothly? Tell me all about the Niimura family!"
"Uh... 'all about' it? I don't know that much either."
"It's called the Arata settlement, a remote backwater where Dad's family home is. It's in the northwest of Iizawa Prefecture, and only about 30 people live there. Beyond that..."
I'd never lived in the Arata settlement, and I only went back now and then with Mom.
"No... come to think of it, when my mom goes back to the settlement, she gets a surprisingly warm welcome. Apparently the ancestors were a bunch of outcasts to begin with, so women who marry into the Arata settlement are really appreciated, or so I hear."
Goto kept taking notes without a word.
"This is just my own assumption, but I feel like settlements like that are really closed to outsiders. But if brides who marry in get welcomed, it must be a really big-hearted settlement. Aren't there any unusual conditions for marrying in, or anything?"
The settlement itself was insular and didn't try to have much to do with anyone else.
So marrying in might have been a hard thing, but I'd never heard any stories like that.
Mom probably wouldn't talk about hardships like that anyway, being who she is.
"Sorry, I don't know of any conditions for marrying in. Maybe there's something, but as far as I..."
True, there was the Noh mask thing―and for something Mom did, it went too smoothly, like Goto said. That bothered me.
Which means, as a possibility―
"Goto, could people from the Arata settlement be helping out?"
If so, stopping a crime this big would be impossible...!
"I don't think they're directly involved. Otherwise, there'd be no reason Kogori-senpai's dad had to be killed. Kogori-senpai's parents could become this time's victims because they did the killing in the Megasawa City case. If people from the Arata settlement had done the killing in the Megasawa City case, there'd be no need to go out of the way to use the Kogori family, and the Kogoris would have nothing at all to do with the Megasawa City case."
That was true. If the crimes in the Megasawa City case were done by people from the Arata settlement, the Kogori family had nothing to do with them, or with today's case either.
For one thing, everyone in the settlement had attended the memorial services so far, so going to Megasawa on April 8 would have been impossible.
In an insular settlement like that, missing a wedding or a funeral hardly ever―
No, hold on...?
In that case, there was no way Mom would be allowed to skip the memorial service today because of work.
And it was the memorial for her own husband, at that.
Me being sick, like this time, was one thing, but Mom being allowed to miss it?
So if the Arata settlement also knew that Mom was going to commit some kind of crime today...
"So people from the Arata settlement are helping indirectly, is that it? Even if they aren't directly involved, I think they'd probably cooperate somehow if Mom asked them to."
"I thought so..."
Goto set down her pen and held her head in her hands.
No wonder. This had gotten way too big.
"Sorry, let me take a little break..."
"Yeah, sorry for putting you through all this."
If we were up against the whole settlement, even Goto would have no way to deal with it.
The least I could do was remember anything I could about the settlement and come up with a plan...
The Arata settlement―
It has about 30 people, and you only reach it by going even deeper into the mountains from the last station on the line, already far back in the hills.
This being the modern day, there are cars and electricity, but no convenience stores or supermarkets, and a lot of the food comes from hunting and farming.
People do go down to the foot of the mountain to shop sometimes, though.
But it's true: outside the Arata settlement, I've never seen the shinigami outfit.
I've seen it ever since I can remember, so it doesn't feel strange to me, though.
Well, the settlement's culture doesn't matter right now.
Goto thought the chance of the settlement being involved in the Megasawa City case was slim.
Because then there'd be no need for Natsumi's parents to do the killing, and they wouldn't have become victims in this case either.
If anything was possible, it was that the settlement was involved in this case.
Say that for some reason, to kill Natsumi's parents, today Mom made her move.
If the settlement was moving to help Mom with that―
If Mom got down on her knees and begged, the settlement might well move.
That's how welcome a bride from outside is.
Damn... I want to reject the theory that Mom's the culprit, but no matter what, everything just makes sense.
But if 30 people were on the move...
Hm? So that means... people from the settlement... are here in Motoki-cho?
Before I knew it, I'd stood up and was looking all around.
"Senpai, what's wrong?"
"Goto, don't you get it!? People from the Arata settlement could be all around us!"
Even here in Zahha, somewhere, secretly watching us...!
Where!? Where are they!?
Arata settlement faces, I know every last one! Where the heck are they!?
............ But there was no one who looked like that.
"I think that's fine. In a small settlement, you'd know their faces, Senpai. Even if it's possible, it'd only be indirect help."
"Yeah, that's right..."
"Besides, didn't I say this case was unexpected for the culprit too? Suddenly moving a big group down from the north of Iizawa Prefecture would stand out, for sure. And if a big group showed up on the day something like this happened, even the police would keep a sharp eye out."
"That's true. But there are a lot of tourists right now, right? A big crowd coming without knowing about the case wouldn't look unnatural, would it?"
"That's... true. I can't completely rule it out after all. Come to think of it... Kogori-senpai's taking a while, isn't she?"
Hey...!
That means!
Natsumi should be in the restroom!
No matter how you look at it, Natsumi was taking way too long to come back...!
"Hey, Natsumi! Natsumiii!"
No answer.
It can't be... Natsumi...!
