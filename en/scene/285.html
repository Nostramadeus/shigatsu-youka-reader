Green Eyes ~Silence~

Eden no Sakura Arc [Eden's Cherry] · Shiyo 800-04-06 21:00 火 · 45 boxes

"Hah, hah..."
"Damn...!"
The two who had fallen staggered to their feet.
I was still sprawled on the ground.
"Chigaya... you've got some nerve..."
"I went a little easy on you because you're my niece... but you're getting carried away."
"A bad girl needs to be punished. Don't think you'll die easily."
Uncle picked up my knife.
Once again, hyphae coiled around my arms and legs and bound them painfully tight.
Standing or falling, as Shigatsu Youka wills― I'm nothing but a carp on the cutting board.
"First, let's drain the blood from that body of yours―right up to the edge of death."
"Eiichiro, don't kill her, all right?"
"I know, I know."
No...! I can't dodge, I can't block...!
Is this it...!
―And there I woke up.
"Hah... hah..."
Sweat had beaded on my forehead.
Mom and Uncle killing me: that was the dream.
Not that the reality I'd seen with my own eyes just before was so very different...
Where... is this?
Not the smell― not the scenery― not the sounds―
Nothing told me where I was.
I could hear the wind, so at least this wasn't that tunnel.
Putting together the direction of gravity and what came into my field of vision, what was in front of me was... a ceiling...?
It seemed to be a building somewhere.
Why was I here?
I thought back to what had happened.
When I'd swung the knife around with my arms and legs still bound, it had slipped out of my hand and sliced my arm like a razor.
At that, the two of them began to writhe in pain, the hyphae on my arms and legs came loose, and I was given a chance to escape.
Yes, in a blind frenzy I'd made for the tunnel exit, and the moment I was outside, I'd headed toward where there was no one, toward where no one would come.
And I'd reached this place, wherever it was― and, worn out, I'd been asleep until now.
When I looked at the cut on my arm, it still hadn't fully dried.
Only a few hours since I escaped―it must be around 9 p.m. now.
I don't know why the two of them suddenly writhed back then, or why the hyphae came loose.
Did something happen that struck at Shigatsu Youka's weak point?
I remember the storehouse's books I read earlier describing a way to drive off Shigatsu Youka.
Let me think... yes, I remember. They drove it off by sacrificing the green-eyed one.
Green eyes―in other words, Shigatsu Youka can't be defeated without using my life.
The moment I got cut, those two began to writhe in pain.
Which means... in other words...
My suffering reaches Shigatsu Youka too.
By that logic, if I die, does Shigatsu Youka die too?
If so, my dying would let everyone escape from Shigatsu Youka's grasp.
How awful... So the way to solve the curse-killings was for me to die.
And because that was never done, so many people have died until now.
Then, here and now, I...
In my hand was the knife. A faint trace of blood was still on it.
If I drive this into my throat, everything ends.
What would happen if I drove this knife into my throat?
Normally I'd die, but would that really be all?
Might I, by some mistake, become a walking corpse?
Or a seedbed for the cherries―
Why is it me?
Why should I have a fate like this...
............ Why...?
Why is that?
Why that is, I can't tell.
"Chiga-nee, what you saw, what you heard, what you arrived at―please doubt all of it."
Suddenly, Goto-chan's words flashed through my mind.
Repeat the 'why'... Doubt everything...
Yes, I mustn't jump to conclusions yet.
The green-eyed who exist now are me and Kana.
So my dying alone won't necessarily settle it.
Could it be that Kana's life has to be sacrificed too?
Besides, it was the instant I cut myself that those two suffered.
If it was my pain getting through to them, then being hit by Uncle in the storehouse did far more damage.
That doesn't quite add up.
At this point, it seems, the digging into 'why' has hit bedrock.
To begin with, the idea that my life is tied to Shigatsu Youka's makes no sense.
My provisional guess is that even my death won't defeat Shigatsu Youka.
Arriving at that thought, I let out a big sigh.
Still, I'd probably managed at least to deal Shigatsu Youka some kind of blow.
But to think that just that much wiped out Shigatsu Youka would be far too convenient.
I'd better assume it only flinched for the moment.
Shigatsu Youka will surely try to carry me off again.
Then the next thing I should do isn't to die.
It's simply to seal off my five senses.
If only what happened earlier had been a dream.
But this wound on my arm proves it wasn't a dream.
I'm no longer allowed to see, or hear, or even smell.
Mom or Uncle might sense-steal me, and if I give them any hint to this place, they'll appear in front of me again.
No, if I was sense-stolen even before that, while running in here, then it's hopeless anyway.
I closed my eyes.
Are those two the only corpses that are moving?
Maybe everyone who's ever been buried in the ground is moving.
There might be hundreds of them, thousands.
No, many of them―at least, nearly everyone who died before the Grand Witch two generations back should have become a cherry tree.
Even so, there must be no fewer than dozens of corpses that might move.
If so, the ones who might appear as moving corpses would be the people buried after the Holy Cerejeira Church carried off the cherry trees.
The people who've died in these last 70 years... at a rough count, there must be at least 30.
Against an immortal army like that, for us humans to stand and fight would be a drop in the ocean.
Even when I took on those two with all my strength, I couldn't do a thing.
Right now, the only one in this settlement with precognition is me.
So as long as I'm not sense-stolen, Shigatsu Youka shouldn't be able to get any information either.
The problem is Natsumi-chan.
She has precognition too, so she's a target of sense-theft.
If I try to stop Natsumi-chan from coming, something will surely get in the way again.
Still, it nags at me a little that I have no danger sense, even with the Niimura main house's cherry there.
Without danger sense, there shouldn't be any sense-theft either.
I see. So the Niimura main house's cherry tree looked withered because Shigatsu Youka made it that way on purpose.
It activates the Niimura main house's cherry tree and puts it to work whenever it needs to.
To break out of this worst-case situation, all I can do is think.
And even if I do come up with something and become able to act on it, I myself must never know where I am.
The time limit is 7 p.m. tomorrow, when the Motoki-cho group is due to arrive.
First, I'll isolate myself.
By tomorrow morning, the people of the settlement will probably start searching for me.
If anyone finds me, those two will learn where I am through my sight and hearing.
A corpse controlled by Shigatsu Youka might even slip in among the search party.
It could imitate someone living and get close to me.
So whoever it is, for now all I can do is lie low so I'm not found.
Is there anywhere in this building I can hide?
Lying there with my eyes still closed, I moved my arms and legs to feel out my surroundings.
The floor here seemed to be made of wood.
And it was damp in places, just like some abandoned hut―
An old hut...?
Could this be... the old rest hut on the mountain path toward Toyotake?
Without thinking, I opened my eyes.
No mistake. This was the abandoned hut that used to be the rest hut.
It had been built for hunters to rest in, but once buggies came in, it had fallen almost entirely out of use.
Thinking back, I have a feeling I fled into an empty building without even meaning to.
But at the same time, a terrible anxiety came over me.
My having just recognized it as the abandoned hut means Shigatsu Youka may have learned where I am too.
It's only a possibility.
It can't sense-steal my thoughts, so just because I realized it doesn't mean Shigatsu Youka knows this is the abandoned hut too.
Having come this far, all I can do is bet that Shigatsu Youka hasn't recognized this place.
Otherwise, whatever I do now, I'll just be caught.
No, there's a way to tell whether Shigatsu Youka has pinned down where I am.
Shigatsu Youka apparently makes a hole near its target when it moves corpses.
And it brings the corpses in and out through it.
In return, whenever a hole opens, that eerie sound rings out―the Fierce Sound.
In other words, as long as that sound doesn't ring, I'm safe.
I hid under a table set in a corner and closed my eyes.
It was the only place in the hut where I could hide.
Then, all at once, drowsiness came over me.
It seemed I'd been unconscious until a little while ago, but my dreams hadn't been restful ones.
Maybe I should sleep while I can...
I have to work out a plan before the Motoki-cho group comes, but right now I can't think of anything.
If I go down under the secret base and dose myself with falsificaso, I probably won't be sense-stolen, but getting there is next to impossible.
In the meantime, they might pin down where I am and get there ahead of me.
The secret base was right on my way here, and I let that go to waste.
Even as I thought about such things, my eyelids grew heavy on their own.
I didn't feel particularly hungry, and I could probably sleep right through until morning.
Still, sleeping out here was bound to be cold.
I had my poncho blanket on for working in the unheated storehouse, so I won't freeze to death, but I'd hate to catch a cold.
If I were about to freeze to death, would Shigatsu Youka notice?
Those two had said they would take me to meet the Goddess.
If killing me isn't their goal, then at worst, I won't have to die...?
But what would they use me for after that...
Then I really would be better off dead...
I curled up like a pill bug, huddling to keep in what warmth I could.
I should hurry and fall asleep...
Because even just my perceiving the real world is a risk.
