The Head

Meicho Arc [Clear Sign] · Shiyo 800-04-08 17:00 水 · 56 boxes

The time had gone past 17:00.
A light car was parked beside the apartment building where the Kogori and Niimura families lived―Revo Heights.
In the driver's seat was Mifuyu Niimura.
She was wearing earphones connected to a brand-new smartphone.
The sounds of the Niimura home―were coming to her through the bugged smartphone of Haruka Niimura.
"Almost time..."
On the passenger seat lay a syringe filled with a pink drug. Mifuyu picked it up.
"Whew..."
Mifuyu took a shallow deep breath.
That sigh just now had not been nerves.
She had gone over once more, in her head, what she had to do next.
It happened just as Mifuyu was about to stick the needle into her arm.
"Who is this!?"
"Run! It's the shinigami!"
"Senpai! Help me!"
Dying screams, and the sounds of flesh being cut and bones being crushed.
"What's going on...!?"
Mifuyu flung the syringe onto the passenger seat before it went in.
Then she put on the red robes and the Noh mask, and took up the nata and the mace.
Taking the stairs so as not to be seen, she came to stand in front of the Niimura apartment.
It would be bad to be spotted, so this time Mifuyu took no deep breath and opened the door quickly.
From inside, she could sense neither sound nor presence.
"The smell of blood...!?"
Earlier she had concentrated on her hearing, but this time she relied on her sense of smell to search out the state of the room.
Even had she tried to rely on her eyes, it was dim inside.
A movie was playing without sound on the room's TV, and thick curtains were drawn over the windows.
And on the floor lay the brutally murdered bodies of the girls.
Their clothes and white skin were stained with blood, and they lay in cramped positions clearly unlike sleep.
"Who on earth..."
She had used her hearing, used her sense of smell, and then used her sight, yet she could neither grasp nor understand what was happening.
Did she mean to touch their skin next, and confirm the situation by touch?
"I have been waiting for you, Mifuyu-san..."
"Who!?"
Without warning, a hoarse voice reached Mifuyu's ears―
Yet Mifuyu froze at that voice, one she knew.
She had said 'Who?' because she doubted who the voice belonged to.
When Mifuyu turned around, there stood one more shinigami, dressed just like her.
Unlike Mifuyu's young woman's mask, this shinigami's was the Noh mask of an old woman.
Mifuyu knew well what this Noh mask meant.
"Mifuyu-san... I have waited a very long time..."
"I knew it. That voice...!"
The voice was muffled by the Noh mask, but on hearing it Mifuyu stepped back one step, and then another.
Then she laid the nata and the mace on the floor, and put her knees and hands down on the bloodied floor.
"It has been a long time, Grand Witch-sama."
Mifuyu, still kneeling, gave a respectful greeting and went on speaking.
"For my absence, and that of my daughter Haruka―from my husband's memorial service, I offer my deepest apologies. You have come all the way from the Arata settlement... But why? Why would you... these girls..."
Mifuyu, her head still lowered, asked the Grand Witch.
"One for all, all for one. It is the slogan of the Arata settlement; even elementary school children understand it. Have you forgotten?"
"No, that is not so. But Grand Witch-sama...! I do not recall asking Arata to do anything like this! 　This is... a matter between me and Haruka."
"Why? Why Ryoji Kogori?"
"That is... But how is it that you know his name?"
"About 20 years ago, Mifuyu-san, for your wedding ceremony with Eiichiro, you said you wished to invite the Kogori couple to Arata, did you not? 　We could not permit an outsider to attend, but I remember the name. Besides, his name is now famous all over Japan as that of a pitiful man who was horribly murdered."
"That is true..."
"Lift your head, Mifuyu-san."
Mifuyu lifted her head.
But her hands, resting on the floor, moved as if hesitating over doing something.
"What are you doing? When I tell you to lift your head, I am telling you to show me your face."
"............"
Mifuyu did as she was told, took off the mask, and set it on the floor.
"Mifuyu-san... you are crying, are you not..."
"Because... Haruka...!"
"I heard that in this town where you live, a brutally murdered body had turned up. The thirteenth-year memorial service, on April 8. How could I not know?" [TN: Buddhist memorials count the year of death as the first year.]
"I am so very sorry..."
The Grand Witch turned her face toward the bodies lying on the floor.
"Natsumi Kogori. Erina Goto. And my granddaughter Haruka. I did it in your place, but was this all right?"
"............"
Mifuyu fell silent.
"Answer me, Mifuyu-san."
The Grand Witch shouted and struck the table with the mace, and Mifuyu's body gave a jolt.
"A... at least... I never planned to kill E-Erina Goto!"
"I see. Then that was a pitiful thing I did."
The Grand Witch stroked Erina Goto's head gently.
"Why Haruka?"
The Grand Witch began stroking Natsumi Kogori's head as well.
"Because I do not want to let Haruka... go. Ryoji Kogori told me. That he could no longer... leave Haruka in my care..."
"What do you mean by that?"
"Huh...?"
"Well... very well. But why even Natsumi Kogori?"
Mifuyu clenched her hands where they rested on the floor.
"She is a real witch...!　Only in this season, when the cherry blossoms bloom, can she foresee the future. And... she can also curse people to death."
"A curse, meaning what?"
"She can kill people not by summoning a shinigami, but with a curse in the true sense of the word. Eiichiro-san, that is how...! 　And... Haruka is my daughter!"
When Mifuyu lifted her head―
"Huh...?"
The Grand Witch had taken off the old woman's Noh mask.
"Haruka...?"
"Mom, sorry... My Grandma impression got better than last time, right?"
"Grab her!"
Police officers came stomping in from the other rooms and pinned down Mom, still on her knees, without any trouble.
"Mifuyu Niimura! You're under arrest on suspicion of murder and abandoning a body, and incitement to murder!"
In no time at all, Mom was in handcuffs.
Then the policewomen who had been posing as us wriggled and got up.
"Operation success... right..."
The two who had been hiding in another room also appeared in front of me.
Goto wasn't smiling. Natsumi didn't say anything.
"I figured if I put on a Noh mask and did Grandma, we could catch you before you did anything weird..."
"All right, take her away!"
Mom was taken away before she even had time to answer.
"Mom...!"
I couldn't stay on my feet, and sank down onto the floor.
"What is this... My plan actually worked... Hey, tell me. This is all a prank, isn't it? 　You all tricked me so you could laugh at me, right?"
Natsumi and Goto both had their lips pressed tight.
"Haruka..."
Natsumi wrapped her arms around me where I knelt.
"I'm sorry. Maybe it's me who's the witch after all... If I hadn't let the balloon go, this...!"
Why is Natsumi the one apologizing... Natsumi's the one who's always held me up.
"Mifuyu-san's been transferred. Thank you for cooperating with the investigation."
"Hey, Ojisan! What's going to happen to the Senpais!?"
"O-Ojisan...?"
"The Senpais won't get driven out of Motoki-cho just because their parents are like this, will they!?"
"Well, don't worry about that part. They worked themselves to the bone to get this case solved. The school and the local authorities should look after them properly."
"I can believe you, right...?"
Goto gazed at Ise-san with teary eyes.
"Nn-nn-nnohh! O-of course! D-D-Daijiro Ise! Even if I must sacrifice myself!"
"Thank you, Ojisan! Want me to give you a smooch on the cheek!?"
Goto flashed him a big grin.
"Ab-ab-ab-absolutely not! 　Pretty girls are to be cherished, and one must never, ever lay a hand on them!"
He was saying things I couldn't follow, but Goto was also trying to act even a little cheerful.
"Ahem! Also, Niimura-san, Kogori-san, I know this is all hard on you both, but I'd like to hear from you. Can you come with us?"
"Come on, Haruka..."
Natsumi pulled me to my feet, my legs still too weak to stand.
Natsumi's dad is dead too, so where is this strength coming from?
"Natsumi... Why are you so... so strong?"
"Not really... I'm just doing what I can right now. Haruka said she was absolutely going to save everyone, so I can't be the one losing heart, can I? 　Because if I lose heart, I won't be able to save everyone..."
"Geez... Natsumi, and Goto too... I've really been leaning on you two the whole time, haven't I... Even though I'm the oldest..."
"One for all, all for one. That's the Niimura family motto, isn't it?"
"Hahaha... At a time like this... Are you an idiot...?"
Because those two were there, nobody had to die.
Mom was arrested, and it was probably only a matter of time before Natsumi's mom got caught too.
More than anything, I had done something truly inexcusable to Natsumi's dad.
But even if I can't call it the best, this is the most decent ending there could be right now...
