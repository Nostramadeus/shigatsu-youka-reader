Moonlight on the Rafters ~Generations~

Eden no Sakura Arc [Eden's Cherry] · Shiyo 800-04-07 19:10 火 · 28 boxes

We changed our original plan and decided to read a few promising books here in the storehouse.
That's because meeting up with Natsumi right now is dangerous.
There's a chance Shigatsu Youka is watching me through the precognitives.
The promising books had already been picked out, and Goto was reading through them.
The book Goto had singled out seemed to have been written about 70 years ago: a report on the Droga human experiments.
That was as much as I knew.
Only Goto was going through the books. I wasn't doing a thing.
The reason I wasn't doing anything was that the audio from earlier had been too much of a shock.
Goto never knew Sakura-san or my dad.
So part of her can write it off as someone else's problem― In a situation like this, that's worth a great deal.
Because it keeps her from ending up like me.
Dad, whom I'd loved so much, and Sakura-san, who'd been like a goddess, had teamed up and carried Chiga-nee off.
That alone had crushed my spirit.
In my head, I know. This is Shigatsu Youka's doing.
Dad and Sakura-san probably didn't do it because they wanted to.
But... even so...
"Senpai, are you okay? I've mostly finished reading over here."
"I see... What'd you find?"
"Mostly what we expected. A religious group calling itself the Holy Cerejeira Church came to this land of Arata to research Droga, and carried off every cherry tree except the Niimura main house's. In exchange, the Grand Witch of the settlement at the time asked them to disclose the research report."
"They did succeed in making Droga, but its side effects were so severe that in the end it was apparently never put to practical use. The side effects: when the user's blood touches the mucous membranes of someone's head, that person gains precognition, or falls into depression. And since muscle control is difficult, there were apparently many cases of serious injury or death."
"So that means Dad looked up how to make it here and used Droga. And that's why Natsumi..."
"This Droga seems to change its effects with each generation.
Uh... Senpai, should we leave this for later?"
"Yeah, do that... I can't concentrate on it right now..."
I don't really get the logic, but if Goto has grounds she's satisfied with, that's good enough.
What matters to me, more than the logic, is what to do about Chiga-nee.
And who I'm up against is the pair who've become Shigatsu Youka's puppets: Dad and Sakura-san.
The shock had settled down somewhat, but what remained in my heart was a sense of helplessness.
In a case like this, what is there that I can even do?
"Goto... where do you think Chiga-nee was taken?"
"Where...? 　Hmm, I don't know the geography around here, so..."
"Right..."
"Senpai, please don't get any funny ideas, okay? 　Like charging straight in to wherever Chiga-nee is..."
"............"
If I was helpless, I wanted to do whatever I could by brute force.
That was what Goto had seen right through―going to wherever Chiga-nee was.
I know Arata's geography a little, but not well enough to pin down where Chiga-nee is.
To begin with, the whole settlement is out searching, and they still can't find her.
I know it's a reckless idea, but...
"Niimura-senpai, is there an underground space in this settlement? 　A cave, for example."
"No, never heard of one... Why?"
"About 450 years ago, the residents of this settlement killed one another. The last to die then was a girl named Tomi. After her death, her body was preserved as a mummy, they say."
"Where's that mummy? Is that what you're getting at?"
"Yes. If you wanted to preserve something like that with the techniques of the old days, it would be in a cave or an underground space where the temperature stays the same all year. Somewhere in this settlement there should be a place that sunlight, wind and other living things have a hard time getting into. You don't know about it either, and a cave like that might be just as convenient for hiding Chiga-nee."
"Just because I don't know about it doesn't mean nobody else does. 　I'd never even heard of any mummy till now, either..."
"They may have searched the cave, but it is doubtful they searched as far as where the mummy is. Apparently the mummy was used as a ritual implement in the old days, but if you don't know about it, that means it hasn't been used for a long time, right? 　In that case, the mummy is somewhere very hard to find. In other words, it may be well suited to hiding Chiga-nee."
"Hmm, a secret place like a cave... Is there anywhere like that...?"
"I know that place!"
A voice rang out of nowhere, and my whole body jerked.
"Haruka-chan, long time no see!"
"Kana...!"
"Um, so this is Goto-chan? 　Nice to meet you! I'm Kana Niimura, Haruka-chan's cousin! 　And my type of guy is―"
Kana launched into a cheery self-introduction that didn't fit the mood at all.
"Um, nice to meet you, Kana-chan..."
Even Goto seemed thrown by Kana's energy.
"So what are you doing in a place like this? I sensed someone in here, so I came to take a look."
"Oh, just doing some research. We figured we'd go back over what Chiga-nee was looking into."
"Hmm. So, find anything?"
Should I really tell Kana what we found?
I stole a glance at Goto.
"Um, Kana-chan, right? 　Kana-chan, do you have a strange power, by any chance? 　Like seeing things in the future..."
Of course. If Kana has already had her first period, everything goes straight through to Shigatsu Youka.
"Nope, I don't have any powers like that. 　Mommy did, apparently!"
It seemed Kana was safe.
"Listen, I think Chiga-nee might be somewhere like a cave. Kana-chan, you said a minute ago that you knew one, right?"
"Yeah! I know one! 　Actually, I only found out today. There's a cave under my secret base!"
"Goto...!"
"Right...! Hey, Kana-chan, could you show us the way there?"
"Sure, but we already searched there."
"Then, um... do you know about mummies?"
"Mummies? 　The ones in the pyramids, all wrapped up in bandages?"
"Yeah, well, pretty much..."
A place where you could hide a mummy―if there's a place nobody has set foot in for a long time...
It might be a wasted trip, but should we check it out...?
"Niimura-senpai, please go with Kana-chan. I'll keep looking into things here a little longer."
"No, no, come with us. Being alone is dangerous. 　Besides, Kana is strong in the regular sense."
"No, the danger is being with a precognitive. As long as I'm alone, I'm safe."
Well, that's true enough, but... this is where Chiga-nee was kidnapped.
I really can't leave Goto on her own...
"Kana, is Mom back already?"
"Yeah, they've already called off today's search."
"Kana, actually, you stay here with Goto. I'll ask Mom to show me the cave."
"All right. Just to be safe, in front of Kogori-senpai or Chiga-nee..."
"I know already. I won't say anything I shouldn't."
