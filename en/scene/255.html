Green-Eye Curse-Killing

Saigo no Koe Arc [Last Voice] · Shiyo 800-05-05 04:44 火 · 11 boxes

There's no way to explain it.
There's no way I could explain it.
Stop Niimura-senpai?
Why would I need to do that?
There's no point panicking over something that happens in a dream.
Maybe this is real?
A reality where Niimura-senpai stabs Kogori-senpai with a knife over and over can't exist.
Honestly, it's just that my body won't move.
It isn't as simple as being horrified, or being scared.
If I don't deny what's happening in front of me― my sense of self will surely collapse.
But... there's one thing I've figured out.
There's a hint that this might be the curse-killing.
Niimura-senpai's eyes were green.
The cursed eyes Chiga-nee talked about.
Hey... why Kogori-senpai?
Ha... Hahaha... Hahaha...
What a strange dream...
A dream― A dream― A dream― Hey― Tell me it's a dream!
The last thing I heard of Kogori-senpai's voice was― a perfectly ordinary― death scream.
Stabbing Kogori-senpai over and over, Niimura-senpai was― smiling happily.
Seeing that smile, I... smiled along with her.
When Niimura-senpai moved away from Kogori-senpai and smiled at me... I smiled too.
Because... all I can do is laugh.
Haha... Hahaha...!
