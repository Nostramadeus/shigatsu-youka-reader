Two Years Later

Wabigoto Arc [Apology] · Shiyo 790-04-06 07:30 火 · 41 boxes

Shiyo 800, April. Eiichiro's third-year memorial service was coming up soon. [TN: Buddhist memorials count the year of death as the first year.]
Having lost the man of the house, the Niimura family had moved away to who knew where.
Mifuyu-san hadn't told even her best friend Akane where they were going.
With no family of her own, the only place I could think of for Mifuyu-san to go was the Arata settlement, where Eiichiro's family home was.
We had tried somehow to get in touch with Arata before, but nothing much came of it, and we didn't even know where the Arata settlement was.
Mifuyu-san and Eiichiro's wedding had also been held in the Arata settlement, but we couldn't attend.
Apparently, by the settlement's rules, outsiders basically weren't allowed in.
Instead, a reception for friends was held in Motoki-cho, but not one person from the settlement came.
So our only connection to the Arata settlement was Eiichiro.
By rights, we were the ones who should go there and pray for Eiichiro's soul.
But it seemed that wouldn't happen this year either.
All we could do was put our hands together right here, the two of us.
We weren't having Natsumi do it, and we hadn't told her the facts either.
It was far too heavy a burden for Natsumi.
Should we tell her once she's around middle-school age, at least?
"How long have Mifuyu-san and you known each other again?"
I asked Akane, who was making breakfast.
"Since right after she was born. When my mother took me in for a regular checkup, Mifuyu had just been born. But Mifuyu's mother died giving birth. My mother was looking through the glass at Mifuyu, who'd just been carried into the nursery, and it seems that's when she learned what had happened to Mifuyu's mother. We've known each other ever since."
"I see, so you two go back that far. I thought Eiichiro and I had an old friendship we couldn't shake, from high school on, but yours runs a lot deeper than that."
"Yes. That's why I want to go to the third-year memorial service..."
Even right after Eiichiro died, we hadn't been able to attend the memorial service.
"All we can do now is not forget Eiichiro and pray for his soul in our hearts. That's all we can do, but we mustn't forget to do it."
But Mifuyu-san aside, Haruka-chan was on my mind too.
Haruka-chan is our own daughter by blood. We may have given her up for adoption, but there's no way I couldn't worry about her.
"All right, Natsumi, I'm off.
Do what your teacher says, and be a good, proper elementary schooler!"
"Okaaay!"
Akane and Natsumi saw me off at the front door as I left for work.
From today, Natsumi was an elementary schooler―today was her school's entrance ceremony.
It was cherry-blossom season, though. Would she manage to get to school all right?
When I opened the front door, the outside air blew in for just a moment.
"Hmm? 　Dad, don't ride in the company car today."
"............ All right, Natsumi. I absolutely won't ride in the company car."
"Dad's so goood!"
Natsumi seemed to be feeling well today.
She had to stay out of the wind as much as possible, but Akane would see to that properly.
At the office, it was another day of prototyping a new phone.
From the design, through choosing the parts, it becomes a real object, even if only a prototype.
As always, when the finished thing worked properly, it moved me.
Well, prototype or not, this time it was only a change to the earlier device's design, so no big machinery or full-scale prototype was needed.
The semiconductors could be reused too, so as long as I fit the machinery neatly into the casing, it would be fine.
"All right, that'll do."
The prototype was working to spec.
All that was left was to humor the client's demands.
The seat next to mine had been empty for nearly 2 years.
Eiichiro used to sit here.
He and I had known each other since high school.
And after graduating, as friends who couldn't shake each other, we joined the same company.
"Kogori-san, we gotta head out soon."
My junior called out to me.
Today was the day we took the prototype to the client.
I'd tested it thoroughly earlier, so I'd be able to show them something good.
We headed for the usual company car.
The wind was a little strong today.
The wind... come to think of it...
"Dad, don't ride in the company car today."
I remembered Natsumi's words.
"Let's take a taxi today. Finding pay parking around there was a pain."
"Huh? But that'll cost a lot. Accounting's gonna chew us out!"
"Then let's take the bus. One should be coming about now."
"Kogori-san, couldn't you make it a little lighter?"
The client said it so casually, but this was actually a pretty tough problem.
"But as you know, this version only changes the design from the previous one. If we tried to make it lighter, we would need to change the parts, so..."
"Ah, then you'd need a full-scale prototype..."
Normally, a phone prototype is a machine bigger than a refrigerator.
Turning that into semiconductors costs hundreds of millions of yen in development.
This design-variant model had been planned to avoid that enormous development cost.
In other words, since the semiconductors were being reused, changing the parts was difficult.
Or so an amateur would think.
For what it's worth, I had a professional's pride, so I meant to meet the client's requests as far as I could.
"But hmm... I really think a difference in weight from before, when you hold it in your hand, would make quite an impression..."
"That's certainly true..."
To tell the truth, I had already predicted the client would make that request.
There was one more prototype sitting on my desk at the office.
It was exactly the piece I'd been improving in anticipation of the client's request.
"Understood. How about the day after tomorrow? I could bring you a lighter prototype by then."
"That soon!? You can change the parts!?"
"No, it's just a small improvement. Well, please leave it to us."
"Wow, that's Kogori-san for you. You're a lifesaver!"
"Then I'll come by again the day after tomorrow, Thursday, April 8."
"Kogori-san, you're amazing! You predicted what they'd say and made the next prototype already!"
On the bus home, my junior was buttering me up.
"What matters is always thinking from the user's point of view. It's pretty hard, though."
"Yeah, when you think like a developer, you always end up wrapping it up with 'it can't be helped.'"
"Hm...?"
Through the window, I saw a car that had crashed into a utility pole.
That was...!
It was the company car we'd been about to take.
It looked like a single-car accident. The driver's seat was bent a little, the passenger seat badly.
The driver seemed to be all right, but one wrong move and it would have been worse than a serious injury.
Natsumi's words came back to me again.

An ability to foresee danger to people. It seemed to work when she was in the wind.

But we hadn't told Natsumi about the wind.
Because if she was in the wind for more than 15 minutes, she would fall ill.
If we told her, she might even come to fear the wind.

What on earth was that sound―
"Kogori-san, what's up? You're spacing out."
"Ah, no... it's nothing."
It seemed my junior hadn't noticed the crash into the utility pole.
"It's rare for Kogori-san to space out. Well, it's spring, so you get sleepy."
"Yeah, you're right. Haha, spring sure makes you sleepy."
"All right, done!"
The prototype to take to the client the day after tomorrow was finished.
I'd made it even lighter than the client had asked.
Even if they said they needed new parts, I'd show them I could finish it to their request in no time.
"Whoa... Kogori-san, you really are amazing!"
"What are you talking about! You're the one who made the base design, right? I just adjusted it to match."
"No, no, I could never customize it like this... For the software side too, you don't need to rely on the software department anymore, do you?"
"Haha, no way. Programs, apps... just looking at a coding screen makes me sick."
Software is an important selling point of phones these days, but this was as far as I could go.
But the people in the software department were good. Apparently they designed things to run without program changes even if some of the hardware was swapped out.
I was just riding on that and getting fussed over by my junior.
"Hey, you take this by yourself the day after tomorrow."
I handed the freshly finished prototype to my junior.
"Huh? M-me, by myself!?"
"You've got the design in your head, right? Just explain it slowly and carefully, and that'll be plenty."
I'd send this guy alone to deliver the prototype the day after tomorrow.
It would be his first time, but it was something he'd need sooner or later.
"G-got it! I'll do my best!"
"Good, that's the spirit!"
With that, my junior began checking the design and the specs again.
He had designed the basics himself anyway, so it wouldn't be much work.
It looked like everyone could go home on time today.
