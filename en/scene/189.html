The Families Reborn

Saigo no Koe Arc [Last Voice] · Shiyo 800-05-03 22:30 日 · 94 boxes

―The time had reached 10:30 p.m.
After the haunted house, we rode things that were as tame as possible, like the merry-go-round and the teacups.
They were run-of-the-mill attractions, so it was nice that the waits weren't that long, but today's lineup was way too unbalanced...
The two of us sisters were in the same room.
Even after we got into the hotel, we didn't really talk.
"I'm going to bed. Don't make any noise."
That was the only conversation we had in the room.
No, she burrowed into bed before I could even answer, so it wasn't even a conversation.
How many years' worth of talking did we do in the haunted house today?
It really had been a long time since I'd had an actual conversation with Sis.
Maybe we'll never talk like that again...
My phone rang.
"I told you not to make any noise, didn't I?"
"Sorry, just for a bit..."
It was an incoming call from Niimura-senpai.
I pulled the covers over my head so my voice wouldn't carry.
"Helloooo, Goto heeere."
"H-hello! Goto...!?"
Even though I'd answered in a whisper, I heard Niimura-senpai's loud voice.
"What's wrong?"
"Sorry to call so late. I need you to stay calm and hear me out..."
"Senpai, what's wrong!? Senpai, you're the one who needs to calm down!"
I sat bolt upright and answered, forgetting to whisper.
"Hey...!"
"Sorry, it sounds like something serious."
"Geez..."
Sis turned away and pulled the covers over her head.
"Sorry for being loud..."
"No, it's fine. What happened?"
When I said that, Niimura-senpai took a big, deep breath.
"Grandma... passed away."
―I was sitting in the passenger seat of the car, heading home.
This was no time to be having fun at the amusement park anymore.
As soon as tomorrow, I would meet up with my senpai and head to Arata.
To attend the funeral being held in the Arata settlement.
Dad and Mom had been drinking, so they couldn't drive.
And at that hour, there were no trains either.
So the one who drove was...
"Sis, I didn't know you had a license... Thanks."
"Not like I had to tell you."
Dad and Mom stayed behind at the hotel, and the two of us set out on our own.
Sis kept driving in silence.
Was the air going to stay like this for the whole 2 hours until we got home?
The expressway's streetlights lit up the inside of the car on and off.
Golden Week or not, at that hour it was nothing but trucks on the road.
Sis drove neither fast nor slow, keeping pace with the flow of traffic, matter-of-fact.
According to Niimura-senpai, Erika-chan had passed away a little while ago.
I suspected it was connected to that incident, but according to Niimura-senpai, the cause of death was apparently a heart attack, with no sign of foul play.
I found myself thinking back on what happened at Arata.
The only friend I ever allowed to call me 'Erina.'
A friend more than 50 years older than me.
A cute friend with a bit of a love for mischief.
A friend who risked her life to save us.
The person who called me her last friend.
"Uuu..."
Tears spilled over.
At her age, maybe a sudden death like this couldn't be helped.
We'd been planning to all go see her this summer vacation...
"How'd that friend die? 　An accident?"
"A heart attack, they think..."
"A heart attack!? 　Just how old is this friend of yours, anyway...?"
"Around 70... I guess."
"Haah... You're seriously a mystery when it comes to stuff like that..."
Sis's exasperated voice, and a sigh.
"Why'd you become friends with that person?"
"Why...? Just 'cause it seemed like we'd get along..."
"That's it?"
"Yeah..."
For a while, Sis stayed quiet, her face still sullen.
"Hey... Sorry to be selfish, but can I talk about myself?"
"Talk about yourself...?"
"Just a little... just a little, to anyone, doesn't matter who... there's something I need to get off my chest..."
Sis wanted to tell me about herself...?
She said anyone would do, but she'd gone out of her way to pick me...
"Okay... I'll listen."
"Thanks... Sorry. I know you've got a lot going on inside right now..."
Sis, thanking me and apologizing to me...?
For the past few years, we'd barely even said hello.
"I... got my first boyfriend 5 years ago, in my first year of high school... and less than a month after we started going out, he died in an accident."
"Huh...?"
"He was sweet, but a seriously rigid, straitlaced guy... You're smarter, but he was smart too, and his grades were good. And yet, even though we were going out, he couldn't ask me on a date himself, couldn't even hold my hand."
"............"
It was all so delicate that I couldn't even nod along.
"After the accident, I went to his funeral, and his relatives took one look at my hair color and made a face. That whole family was a highly educated bunch, all of them even more rigid than he was, and it was so uncomfortable I couldn't even pay my respects properly. Geez... back then that color was my natural hair color."
"So that happened... I didn't know..."
"Whatever... I never meant to talk about it anyway."
These days Sis's hair was obviously dyed, but it had been a little light from birth.
So she'd gone through all that because of it...
"So, like, after that, studying hard and going to school just started to seem stupid... I didn't want to become the kind of person who looks at someone like that just because their hair's a little different. I got sick of living smart and proper. So I dropped out of high school. I just... I don't really know, I got sick of it."
Why was she telling me this?
Sis had always been smart, and her grades were always near the top.
It was true my test scores and deviation scores were higher, though.
Even so, Dad and Mom always praised Sis.
My parents aren't the kind of people who compare sisters.
"So... I figured even you, the so-called genius girl, would end up a rigid, prejudiced person too. Surely Dad and Mom too..."
"So... that's how it was..."
"I wanted to get out of a house like that. I'm a dropout, so there aren't many good jobs, but I figured I'd work part-time, save up money, and stand on my own feet soon... I worked my part-time jobs every day."
"So you were never home because you were working... I was sure you were out playing around with guys."
"Guys?"
"Yeah, you're always on the phone with some different guy."
"............"
"............"
"Heh"
"?"
"Heh heh heh..."
"Hm?"
"Ahahahahaha! Guys...! 　Me, playing around with guys!? Ahahahahaha"
"Hahahahaha... Oh, my stomach hurts... Guys? As if! I don't have time to play around with guys! 　Forget guys, do you have any idea how much I held off even hanging out with friends so I could work!?"
It was the first time in years I'd seen Sis smile.
But somewhere in it, there was an air of sorrow.
"Sometimes, Sis, I'd hear you on the phone with men, so I just assumed. And it seemed like it was someone different every time."
"Ah... I do customer-service work too, so there were quite a few people coming at me with 'give me your number' and 'let's go out sometime.' They're customers, so I have to be careful not to be rude, right? 　You probably heard me talking to them."
"Aren't you supposed to say no when people ask for your number?"
"You probably don't know this, but it gets pretty complicated. Some were friends of regulars, and some even had my number and I don't know where they got it. I finally finished turning them all down the other day. And I've saved up a good amount of money, so I came today thinking of it as our last family trip, but..."
"Last trip... So you're leaving home soon?"
"That's the plan. That's why I've worked these 5 years without having any fun."
"............"
"Now I can finally get away from the family. Like I said before, I don't get along with smart people."
"That's..."
"But... but, you've changed, huh. In elementary school you were gloomy the whole time, and I figured you'd turn into a rigid person. To be honest, part of me was relieved. That I'd guessed right. But... once you got to middle school, you suddenly got cheerful, and you had more friends... And on top of that, you were still the genius girl, same as ever. And now, a 70-year-old friend? 　Makes me laugh..."
"I thought smart people were more biased... but like today, even your senpai rely on you, you're always cheerful, everyone likes you, you cheer up everyone around you..."
Sis got that far and then, still gripping the wheel, started crying, tears streaming down.
"I... what have I been doing all this time... the one who's prejudiced and rigid... is me..."
"Sis..."
Sis turned off toward the rest area we had been about to pass.
Then she picked a spot with few cars around it and parked.
"Uu... uu..."
Sis was slumped over the wheel, crying.
"This might sound bad, but... Sis, I thought the way you turned out was because of an inferiority complex about me. So I thought you hated me."
"If I hated you, I wouldn't be driving you home in the middle of the night!"
"Sorry I never noticed till now..."
"Whatever... I didn't want you to notice anyway..."
So that was why Sis had been avoiding me.
Worried that I'd become a rigid person, and that Sis and I would have a real falling out.
It was just like Kana-chan said.
We'd only been a little out of step, yet I'd been afraid to make the first move, and avoided her.
But I've never once looked down on Sis or made fun of her.
I'd just thought she seemed like a delinquent, and that she lived in a different world from me.
I should have noticed.
"I'm... not being fair, am I? Blaming other people for how I turned out. Blaming my dead boyfriend's family, trying to convince myself it's not my fault. But... no, that's not it... His family had nothing to do with it. I was scared you'd abandon me."
"Abandon...? What are you talking about..."
"Back when we were little, I thought you were so cute. Except for school, I was glued to you the whole time, right?"
"Yeah, I remember."
"But... you got smarter than me, little by little. I was supposed to be the one teaching, and that started to flip around. We're a whole 5 years apart, and it got to where you were even teaching me my schoolwork... I stopped knowing what my reason to exist as your big sister even was... That maybe you... Erina didn't need me anymore..."
"Nothing like that...! 　I've never once thought anything like that!"
"I know that...! 　I know, but... I was scared. Of being abandoned... of not being needed, I was scared..."
So my idea that an inferiority complex toward me was the cause had apparently been half right.
I'd thought it was a question of who was smarter, but that was where I was wrong.
Sis had been afraid that I would reject her very existence.
At her dead boyfriend's funeral, it all blew up. A rift opened between us.
And I'd convinced myself that Sis, feeling all that, was a badly behaved person who lived in a different world.
This is the 'prejudice' I held against Sis.
A blunder of mine, one I have to admit.
In that case, the rest is up to me.
I have to let her know that I'm not her enemy, that I'll always be Sis's little sister.
"I'd never, ever abandon you, Sis. The bond between sisters isn't something that can be cut, no matter what. No, even if it comes close to being cut, I'll protect it to the very end."
"............"
"It's not too late, even now. Dad and Mom are always worried about you too, Sis."
"That's a lie...! 　They're both always going 'Erina, Erina'... doting on nobody but you..."
"That's because, Sis, after you quit school, they didn't know how to act around you. So once they knew you were coming today, Sis, they were both really looking forward to this day."
"Uu, uuu...!"
I'd meant to comfort her, but Sis only started crying harder.
"Sorry, at a time like this... when the one who wants to cry right now is you..."
Sis got the car moving again, even though she hadn't even stopped crying yet.
"Erina, you hate me, don't you?"
"Huh? 　Um..."
"You don't have to hide it. I can tell that much."
It wasn't so much that I hated her; I found her hard to deal with.
No, I'd thought Sis had an inferiority complex about me, and hated me because of it.
That was why I'd found her hard to approach.
"The haunted house was a little bit fun. It reminded me of when we were kids. The two of us used to go to Zahha all the time and eat Russian takoyaki... I was always the one who lost, though."
Sis seemed to have calmed down a little.
Like Sis said, we were really close when we were kids.
Maybe things started changing little by little around when Sis started high school.
"Do you know why you were born, Erina?"
"The reason I was born...?"
"I... when I was little, I pestered Mom. I told her I wanted a little sister."
"Sis, you did?"
"It was back when I was little, right around when I was turning 2. But I remember it clearly. I saw a baby that belonged to one of Mom's friends. It was super cute."
―It was on the way home that day.
At a cafe we stopped in at―
"Hey, hey, Mama! I wan' a baby sister!"
"Momoko, we'll talk about that later, okay?"
"No! Wan' a baby sister! 　I picked a name! 　Erina, that's her name! 　Isn't it cute!?"
"All right, all right... Later, okay?"
"Really!? I'm getting a baby sister!? 　Yaaay! Erina-chan's comiiing!"
"Hey, Momoko...! Don't say it so loud...!"
"So this name... Sis, you're the one who gave it to me..."
"Well, yeah. I guess technically that makes me the one who named you."
I hate my own name.
Erina: a name way too cute for me.
Knowing that Sis thought it up when she was little, maybe I can come to like it a bit.
A name Sis thought up for me when she was small.
A name she gave me because she wanted me to come.
"Sis... me coming along... did it make you happy...?"
"You expect me to say something like that, the way I am right now?"
"No, never mind!"
I flashed Sis a big grin.
"Been a while since I saw that face. You always looked away whenever you saw me."
"Well... of course I looked away..."
"Yeah, figures..."
"Hey, I won't look away anymore. So... so stay with me. Don't run away from home."
"I'm already 20, so it's not like it matters if I leave..."
"I want to get back all that time. The 5 years we spent apart, I want them back."
"Hey, you. You're just after my savings, aren't you."
"No way...!"
"I'm kidding. Aw, man, if I quit running away from home, I'll just be a rich girl."
"You've got that much saved?"
"Ah, if you're curious, then you really are after my savings."
"............"
"I said I'm kidding..."
"Come on... But Sis, you don't have any reason to run away from home now, right?"
"............"
"Hey, stay with me a while longer, please."
"Ugh, fine, fine, I get it. I thought I'd get to live as a NEET for a while, and instead I end up spending my money on my little sister..."
"Was that a joke too...?"
"Half serious. I mean... You―if I can spend it on you..."
"Huh? What'd you say?"
"Ugh, forget it! It's nothing! 　More importantly, I'm stopping at the next rest area again. You can't go to a funeral empty-handed, and you won't have time to buy anything tomorrow, right? 　Anything's fine, just get a gift to bring..."
"Yeah... thanks, Sis."
"Whatever..."
Tomorrow I had to work out a time with my senpai and go to Arata.
There was hardly any time to prepare, and I still didn't really know the schedule.
―Before long, we reached Motoki-cho, where our house was.
It was already past midnight.
"Thanks. Be careful going back."
"Whatever..."
Sis said she was heading straight back to the hotel.
She was as prickly as ever, but I had a feeling we'd soon be back to how we used to be.
"Erina, don't cry too much tomorrow. Your senpai are counting on you, right? 　If you go to pieces, everyone goes to pieces."
"Yeah... I'll do my best."
"Also, grab me a Super Soda Bomber from the fridge, would you?"
"Okay."
I took a bottle of Super Soda Bomber out of the fridge.
Niimura-senpai had said she might hit it off with Sis over this.
"Here."
"Hey, bring it more carefully. The way you're carrying it, the soda's gonna spray out."
"Huh? You can tell? 　Niimura-senpai always falls for this prank..."
"The color on the bottom of the bottle tells you what's going on inside. Blue if the pressure's normal, yellow if it's a little risky, red if it's dangerous. That senpai of yours is still a total beginner when it comes to Super Soda Bomber. I'll have to give her a thorough lecture. So..."
"So... introduce me to that senpai later. The other senpai too. I want to meet them."
With that, Sis drove off before hearing my answer.
I watched Sis go until the car was out of sight.
