Secret Base

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-07 22:10 火 · 47 boxes

Moonlight lit up the settlement.
"Tomorrow, Shiyo 800, April 8, is a full moon. Tonight is just as bright."
"Goto-chan, there's lots of bumps around there, so watch your step! 　Over there, tree roots are sticking out! 　And around there, there's lots of puddles from the rain the other day!"
"At this rate, looks like I don't even need to show the way."
Kana-chan bounced along ahead of us.
"Hey, Kana! Don't get too far ahead!"
We gradually made our way into the deep forest.
Kana-chan had gone off somewhere before we knew it.
"Where'd Kana get to...?"
"Boo!!!"
"Whoa-whoaaa!"
Suddenly Kana-chan appeared right in front of us.
"Ahahahaha!"
"Y-you damn brat! Where'd you pop out from!?"
"The mountains of Arata are my playground! I know my way even with my eyes closed! 　Like this!"
Kana-chan leapt around among the trees just like a flying squirrel.
Trying to follow those movements with our eyes, we swiveled our eyes and necks round and round.
"There we go!"
"Kana-chan, that's amaaazing! You're like a ninja!"
"Ehehe! A lady ninja, a lady ninja! 　Aaah! Please, unhand meee!"
When Kana-chan laughs, she really is just a kid her age.
But to be able to move like that, she must be pretty well trained.
"Um, to perform a movement like that, at the very least the strength of the latissimus dorsi would need to be― Of course, parkour! On the same principle, what she just did is possible too, with enough training!"
"Hey, can everyone in Arata do stuff like this?"
"Ahaha! No way, no way! Besides me, the only one who can is probably Sis? 　Everybody else is just old geezers and old hags!"
"I see. So Kana-chan's in first place!"
"Of course! I'm the fastest runner at school, too!"
Right, even a kid from a settlement like this would still have to go to school.
"Kana-chan, how do you get to school?"
"I walk, of course! Like an elementary schooler would get the luxury of riding to school in a car!"
"You walk to school from this deep in the mountains?"
"Yep! It takes about an hour from here, though! I know shortcuts, so I can probably get to school faster than a car!"
Arata's elementary schoolers... formidable...
"Oh, I know! Let's go to my secret base!"
"Secret base?"
"Yeah! Sis used it when she was a kid! And it's right near here!"
"Haruka, are we okay on time? Will we make it to the ritual...?"
"Grandma'll probably be about 20 minutes late, so I think we're fine for about that long."
"Ooh, you're game! 　So, right here, we veer off onto a side path!"
A side path, she said, but I couldn't really tell where any path was.
Even with the moonlight, everything except this narrow path was too dark to see.
Kana-chan ran off into that darkness.
"Hey, Kana, wait!"
"Over here, over heeere!"
"Geez..."
We slowly followed the trail Kana-chan had left.
"Haruka, do you know the way...?"
"I've never been anywhere around here..."
"It's dark..."
With Haruka in the lead, we made our way through the lightless forest in a centipede-race line.
"How far'd she go...?"
"There aren't any pitfall traps or anything, right...?"
"With that kid, that's entirely possible..."
After about 30 seconds, we saw a break in the forest ahead.
Moonlight was shining in through it.
When we went on further...
In front of us, a cave appeared, gaping open in the rocks.
"Is this what she meant by secret base...?"
"Probably..."
"What if a bear lives in there or something..."
Still in our centipede-race line, we stood in front of the hole.
"Heeey, Kana, you in there?"
"Nobody's home!"
Another half-baked prank...
Crouching, we went into the hole, and for a moment the darkness left us blind.
But I could see light shining in further back.
The finish line of our centipede race seemed close at hand.
"Welcome!"
At the very back of the hole, Kana-chan was waiting.
The back of the hole was about four and a half tatami mats in size, high enough for an adult to stand up.
Dazzling moonlight was spilling in through a crack in the ceiling.
"So this is the secret base."
"Ehehe! Pretty great, right!"
In the corner, empty snack boxes and juice cans were lying around.
"It's your big secret base, so at least clean it up a little..."
"Haruka-chan, not you too! Don't go sounding like Grandma!"
As she said that, Kana-chan flopped down on the ground, spread-eagled.
Something like an old rush mat was spread over the ground.
"Whenever something bad happens... I come here and spend the time looking at the sky like this!"
The moonlight fell gently on Kana-chan's face.
"Let's see, I'll give it a try too!"
Haruka copied Kana-chan and lay down spread-eagled too.
Goto and I followed suit.
Our heads close together like the hands of a clock, we gazed at the dazzling moon.
It was my first time seeing moonlight so bright it was blinding.
"Kana, you said Chiga-nee used this place, but did Chiga-nee find it?"
"Nope, apparently Mommy passed the place she used as a kid on to Sis. Mommy probably took it over from somebody else too, I think."
"I see..."
Kana-chan's mother, then.
We were going to her grave after this, but this felt like a kind of grave visit too.
"When I'm here... I feel really safe. I don't remember Mommy, but it's like Mommy's watching over me gently from the sky... That's how it feels."
Kana-chan's mother died 10 years ago, and Kana-chan is 10.
Kana-chan must have lost her mother right after she was born.
Kana-chan's mother, too, must have deeply regretted passing on and leaving her two daughters behind.
"Niimura-senpai, what kind of person was Kana-chan's mother?"
"Sakura-san, huh... Even now I... think she was the Goddess."
"The Goddess how?"
"In the ghost story I told earlier, I said the Goddess showed up in my dream, and I kinda felt like she looked a little like Sakura-san. So... her face looks a lot like Chiga-nee does now. She was super kind, and just looking at her face made you feel really safe... Really, just like the Goddess..."
"Everybody says that, so I'm happy! It means everybody loved Mommy. When I come here, I get to see Mommy. So... this is my very own secret base. The place where I belong..."
There was a little loneliness mixed into Kana-chan's words.
Kana-chan probably wants to see Sakura-san.
And to be hugged tight, and to try being spoiled a little.
Trying to ease that loneliness even a little, here in this secret base...
Was it because she was lonely, after all, that Kana-chan got so attached to Goto?
"I come here a lot lately. Sis is in Tokyo, so I hardly ever see her either... Daddy and Grandma are here, but they're grown-ups, so... So, I'm really happy you all came today. But... you're all going home the day after tomorrow, right? It's like I got a whole bunch of big sisters, and I'm happy, but... I guess it's still a little lonely..."
"Hey, Kana-chan. You might think it's weird if I say this, but..."
"Hm?"
"If you're okay with me... Chiga-nee's around, but... just for now, how about I be your big sister?"
"Huh!? Goto-chan... would be my big sister!?"
"Yeah... I'm not a beauty like Chiga-nee, and I'm a far cry from the Goddess, but... if it means Kana-chan won't be lonely anymore―"
Just then, Kana-chan flung herself on top of Goto.
"Yaaay! I like my real sis, but I love Eri-nee too!"
"Eri-nee... Heh, there, there."
Goto, still lying down, gave Kana-chan a pat on the head.
"Chiga-nee and Eri-nee, huh! Kana, you're surrounded by good big sisters!"
"Yeah! And of course, I like Haruka-chan and Natsumi-chan too! 　Ehehe... thanks for coming all the way to Arata, everyone!"
As she said that, Kana-chan pressed her face against Goto's chest.
At first I'd thought she was a kid with no sense of distance and no manners, but it was because she'd always been lonely.
Thinking of it that way, I really couldn't be cold to her.
Haruka and I were both only children, but what would it have been like if I'd had a big sister?
Gazing at the dazzling moonlight, I was lost in daydreams like that.
