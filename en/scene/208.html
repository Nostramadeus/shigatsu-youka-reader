Mirror (You)

Wabigoto Arc [Apology] · Shiyo 778-11-22 18:00 木 · 107 boxes

The day of the group date―
The place I was brought to was an Italian bar-restaurant that had recently opened in Motoki-cho.
New shops like this one were starting to open one after another in Motoki-cho.
As promised, I was going to be introduced to a colleague of Ryoji-san's, but...
"We got here a little early. Let's wait inside."
"Uugh... Is he even going to like someone like me..."
"You've come this far. You're not going to say you're going home, are you?"
"I'm not going to go home, not at this point, but... Will I be able to talk properly..."
The table we were shown to was set with glasses and plates for four.
Even if it was a small one, this still counted as a group date, didn't it?
I'd been told I didn't need to worry about the money, but I was nervous all the same...
"Hey... what am I supposed to talk about... Talking properly with a man..."
"We'll steer things along, so you'll be fine. Ryoji-kun's going to help out all the way too. Let's practice a little."
"Uugh... go easy on me..."
"Okay, introduce yourself."
"What?! What do I even say in a self-introduction?!"
"You can at least say your name and your age, can't you?"
"Uugh... um, my name is... Mifuyu... Kanai..."
"What are you, a foreigner..." [TN: In Japanese the family name comes first.]
"I hate this...! I can't even say my own name in practice...!"
"Honestly, you're hopeless... Here."
Akane handed me a sheet of paper.
"I wrote a self-introduction list on that for you, Mifuyu. Just look at it and talk slowly."
The paper had not only my name on it, but my background, my hobbies and so on.
"Listen. You can look at the paper, but always keep your eyes on Eiichiro-san."
"Uugh... I'll try..."
These were awfully detailed notes.
This was the first time we'd met since making plans for the group date, but Akane had seemed busy lately.
That was because Akane and Ryoji-san had decided to get married, and she was starting on the preparations.
The ceremony was planned for 10 months from now, and she had found time in between all that to write these notes for me.
Besides, only Akane, who knows me so well, could have written this.
After seeing this, I really couldn't treat this match lightly.
"Oh, looks like they're here."
By reflex my whole back straightened, and my breath stopped.
They're finally here...! I can't drag Akane's name through the mud.
It'll be fine! Akane and the others set this up, so he has to be a wonderful person!
"Oh. I thought we'd get here first, but you two are pretty early..."
"I wanted to get Mifuyu used to the place, even a little."
The one next to Ryoji-san was...
"Oh... uhh..."
Was this man Eiichiro Niimura-san?
Just as I'd heard, he was short, and his build was clearly over the standard.
His face was hard to call handsome, but...
But looks don't matter at all.
What matters is whether he fits my weighty ideal: whether he could become my family...
"Come on, Eiichiro, sit down."
"Phew... phew..."
This Eiichiro-san person was sweating a lot and breathing hard.
Maybe he was nervous too...
"Ow!"
Eiichiro-san caught his foot on empty air and fell over.
"Hey, you okay?"
"I-I-I-I'm fime...!"
Ryoji-san held him up, and at last he made it to his seat.
If he's that nervous, it's going to make me even more nervous...
―Before long, the drinks for the toast arrived.
"This time it's supposed to be about introducing Eiichiro and Mifuyu-san, but let's just have fun and keep it easy."
"Right, let's keep it easy. Okay, cheers."
"Ah... ah, wait!"
"U-um, hey... Ryoji and Akane-chan... you're getting married, right? 　Then I'd like the toast to celebrate that as well..."
Eiichiro-san sure is considerate. There's no doubt he's a good person.
"R-right! This time let's celebrate the two of you getting married!"
"Thank you, both of you. But never mind that. You two are the stars today."
"Right, right. We're the supporting cast."
"Okay, cheers!"
"Gulp gulp gulp gulp...!"
Eiichiro-san drained his beer in one go.
When I looked closer, his face was bright red and his hands were shaking.
His eyes were a little sharp, and the muscles in his face were twitching.
Maybe, rather than nervous, he was angry?
Had he seen through me, seen that I'm a mopey, good-for-nothing woman...
"S-s-sorry! Just going to the restroom!"
And Eiichiro-san shot a look at Ryoji-san.
"Oh, I'll go too."
The two of them got up from the table at the same time.
Could this be...! The thing that always happens at group dates, where they go off and trash the other side behind their backs?!
"Hey, Ryoji...! Give me a break...!"
"Huh? Mifuyu-san's not to your liking?"
"That's not it! 　Why'd you have to bring a beauty like that, of all people! 　Any way you look at it, I'm no match for her!"
"Don't strain so hard. I think you're a good match for her."
"Damn it. I hate this face and this body..."
"Come on, Eiichiro, ready yet?"
"R-right...! Now's the time to show them the soul of the Arata settlement! 　Fighting spirit! Fighting spirit!"
"Hey, your eyes are way too sharp..."
"Fighting spirit... fighting spirit...!"
"Eiichiro-san... is he angry... He must've seen right through me, that I'm a good-for-nothing woman who's all looks..."
"No way. He's just nervous with you, Mifuyu, right in front of him."
"Uugh... that can't be..."
"Mifuyu, want another?"
"Mm-hm..."
Because of my nerves, I was going through my drinks faster and faster too.
Then the men came back.
"You two were gone so long that Mifuyu got bored, and she's already on her fourth."
"Sorry, sorry."
"Fighting spirit...! Fighting spirit...!!"
Eiichiro-san's eyes were even sharper than before.
So he really was angry...
"Mifuyu, Kanai-san!"
"Y-yes!"
"Pfft!"
Suddenly Eiichiro-san shouted my name― and with my given name and family name the wrong way around, too, so my back snapped straight.
I thought I heard Akane burst out laughing.
"Wh-what are your hobbies?!"
H-hobbies...?!
What are my hobbies again...!
That's it! They were in Akane's notes!
I sneaked a glance at the notes I'd hidden under the table.
"M-my, Mifuyu Kanai's, hobbies are coffee and spicy food!"
Then Akane whispered in my ear.
"W-wait, Mifuyu! You're looking at the wrong part! Those are the things you hate, aren't they?! 　And besides, Eiichiro-san can't do coffee or spicy food either!"
"What?!"
I looked at Eiichiro-san, and―
Whoa, he was so obviously crushed.
He hung his head, all doom and gloom.
Seeing that, I hung my head, all doom and gloom as well.
"Ha, hahaha! Mifuyu-san, your jokes are hilarious! 　M-Mifuyu-san can't do coffee or spicy food either! 　Right?! Right?! Akane?!"
"That's right! Honestly, Mifuyu, you're so nervous!　Ahahaha! 　Haha..."
This was the worst! Our one precious thing in common, and it had turned into this weird mess...!
"I'm going... to the restroom."
"Ah, wait, Mifuyu!"
I couldn't take it anymore and fled to the restroom.
"Akane... I really am a good-for-nothing woman...!"
"That's not true! Have more confidence in yourself!"
"Uugh... what should I do..."
"For now, you need to loosen up more..."
"Maybe I'll order a stronger drink..."
"Turning to booze, huh..."
Meanwhile, the men...
"Ryoji... I'm just no match for Mifuyu-san..."
"Hey, have more confidence in yourself!"
"What should I do..."
"For now, just loosen up more..."
"Maybe I'll order some stronger booze..."
"Turning to booze, huh..."
―When we got back to our seats, a big bottle of vodka was sitting on the table.
Eiichiro-san was gulping it down on the rocks.
Exactly the strong drink I wanted right now.
"Um... could I have some of that too?"
"What?!"
Eiichiro-san looked surprised, but right away he made me the same vodka on the rocks.
"This is pretty strong, though..."
It was true, I'd never drunk anything that looked this harsh.
But... who could get through this without a drink!
"Pwah!"
I knocked back the vodka on the rocks in one go.
"Mifuyu... don't overdo it, okay?"
"Uugh..."
No matter how much I drank, it wasn't going to change the situation.
Besides, drinking barely changes me. I'm ridiculously strong with alcohol.
Like Akane says, I'm extreme in strange places.
In the end, all Eiichiro-san and I did after that was trade vodka in silence.
And so things dragged on in this sloppy way...
and it was time to leave the restaurant.
This group date. A total failure...!
"Hey, Eiichiro, you okay? You drank a bit too much."
Eiichiro-san's eyes were glazed, and he was unsteady on his feet.
Only now could I be sure that Eiichiro-san had acted that way because of nerves.
Then Akane whispered to me.
"Can you look after Eiichiro-san?"
"Me...?!"
"Or is Eiichiro-san a no after all?"
"I can't tell that much yet. But I'm not confident I can do it right..."
"Then give it a try. It's your chance to come out of your shell, isn't it? 　Eiichiro-san isn't the type to suddenly jump on you."
"Uugh..."
"If you run away now, you'll probably stay like this your whole life. Is that okay with you?"
"Uugh... I-I'll try..."
Urged on by Akane, I went and stood beside Eiichiro-san.
"Eiichiro-san, are you all right?"
"Y-yes... I'm fiiine... fat..."
"Well then, we'll leave the rest to you two young people."
"Do it right, now!"
Akane and Ryoji-san went off and left the two of us behind.
"Eiichiro-san, Central Park is right nearby, you know? Shall we take a rest there?"
"Y-yes..."
I pulled Eiichiro-san by the arm into the deserted Central Park, and we sat down side by side on a bench.
Eiichiro-san, maybe because he was drunk, seemed less nervous than spaced out, gazing up at the sky, lost in thought.
It was November now, and a wind with a feel of winter was blowing through Central Park.
I was wearing a thick coat, but Eiichiro-san was... dressed a little thin for the cold.
"Mifuyu-san, I'm sorry... for causing you all this trouble..."
"No, no, it's all right. Do you want some water, or something warm to drink? Aren't you cold?"
I draped my coat over Eiichiro-san's shoulders.
Before I knew it, my own nerves had faded.
"Ugh... uhhh..."
Suddenly Eiichiro-san let out a groan and hung his head.
What could be wrong? It doesn't look like he's angry, at least...
"Sniff... uhhh..."
Was he crying?
"Eiichiro-san, what's wrong? 　It's okay, I'm here, so tell me anything!"
I said that, but Eiichiro-san kept his head down for a while, groaning.
"I'm... pathetic... I could barely even talk, and on top of that I've caused you so much trouble, Mifuyu-san..."
"What are you saying? I'm the one who got all nervous... Being with me wasn't any fun at all, was it..."
I rubbed Eiichiro-san's back as he cried.
"Someone like me is no match for you, Mifuyu-san... Ryoji recommended it, so I came, but someone like you, Mifuyu-san, with someone like me..."
Unlike the men who had approached me until now, Eiichiro-san put himself down.
It was the first time I'd seen a man like this.
Mopey, with no confidence in himself...
"I have a twin brother. But even though my brother looks the same as me, he's always overflowing with confidence, and he's smart too... Ever since I was a kid I wanted to be like him... I thought I was trying hard, but..."
I, too, had always been following in Akane's footsteps.
Looking up to Akane, wanting to be like Akane...
This man... is like me...
"So from high school on, I left my hometown... I wanted to be a different me, even a little... I thought, if I'm going to do it, I want to build a happy family, the kind my brother and the rest would envy... So today I came because Ryoji and the others recommended it, but... You must be disappointed after all... I'm ugly, I'm fat... I'm mopey..."
It was as if he were talking about me.
I was only covering it up with my looks, but inside...
"Mifuyu-san... you're so kind, looking after someone like me. Mifuyu-san, I envy the man who gets to be with someone like you..."
"Eiichiro-san, that's not true. I'm the same... I've never had any confidence, and I wanted to be like Akane... But... all I changed was my looks. I can't do anything on my own... I'm a mopey woman..."
"Mifuyu-san, you really are kind... Being so considerate, matching yourself to me... Thank you for the coat."
Eiichiro-san said that and handed the coat back to me.
"Um... won't you come rest at my place? It's right nearby..."
"No... once I've seen you most of the way home, Mifuyu-san, I'll head home too. Thank you for today. I was really happy to meet a woman like you, Mifuyu-san, lovely right down to her heart."
"............"
No more words would come out of me.
On the way to my apartment, we didn't talk.
I kept stealing glances at Eiichiro-san's profile.
His head down the whole time, his back slouched, Eiichiro-san was the very picture of me that Akane had pointed out.
If I showed him a cheerful side, could Eiichiro-san cheer up too?
If I showed him a sad side, would Eiichiro-san be sad along with me?
If he was like me, couldn't we share the happy things and the sad things both?
I, with someone like that, forever...
"Hey, hey, miss."
Out of nowhere, a voice like that came from behind me.
I turned around, and there were two young men.
"Whoa, miss, you really are gorgeous!"
"Man, you're so gorgeous it's... blinding!"
"We came from Minakami City. Wanna go for a drive right now?"
Ah, this pattern again.
I run into pickups like this now and then, but guys this flashy aren't even a question of whether they're my type.
"Oh, no, I'm in a hurry, so."
"Oh, is that right? Hey, is this guy your boyfriend?"
"Um..."
"C'mon, we're more fun than this fatso, right?"
Well! How rude!
"P-please stop it... Can't you see she doesn't like it..."
Eiichiro-san took half a step forward and was saying something in a small voice.
"Huh? What'd you say?"
"P- pwease stop it, I'm telling you!"
And the two of them burst out laughing at the same time.
"What's with this drunk, short, fat, ugly guy?"
"Something about your face ticks me off."
This time they closed in on Eiichiro-san.
"M-Mifuyu-san...! Hurry and run!"
"I c-can't do that...! Eiichiro-san, you too...!"
"My... legs are shaking... they won't move..."
"Ugh! Whatever, I don't even care about the girl anymore. Hey, let's take this guy out. He's got an annoying face."
"Yeah."
What do I do?! In a situation like this I should yell and call for help...!
But would anyone really come to help for a scream that frightening?
"F-, f-, f-,
fire! 　Fire!"
Suddenly Eiichiro-san shouted that.
The two of them stared with dumbfounded eyes at Eiichiro-san.
"What's with this guy..."
"Did he get so scared he lost his mind?"
Then people came running out of the houses around us.
And little by little it got noisier―
"I think it couldn't be helped this time, since it was an emergency, but please be very careful from now on, okay?"
"Yes... I'm terribly sorry..."
The firefighter who had rushed over said that and left.
Someone must have called it in at some point.
The men had slipped away at some point, as soon as people started to gather.
The people who had gathered understood the situation as well, and went their ways with friendly words, glad that nothing had happened.
"Mifuyu-san... I'm sorry..."
"Eiichiro-san, there's nothing to apologize for. That couldn't be helped. The firefighter said so too, didn't he... More than that, I'm the one who should apologize for dragging you into this. And... thank you for saving me."
"............"
Eiichiro-san kept his head down.
I felt anger toward those men, but my sense of owing Eiichiro-san and my gratitude were bigger than that.
If Eiichiro-san hadn't been there, who knows what would have happened by now.
"After all... a wimp like me... You'd hate being with such a lame guy, wouldn't you..."
"No, that's far more admirable than settling it with violence. If anything, you got us out of a tight spot with quick thinking like that. Eiichiro-san, you're really someone I can rely on."
"Not at all. It's just like they said. Me, I look bad, and the things I do are―
Ah, no, thank you for being so considerate. Mifuyu-san, hearing that from you makes me really happy."
Eiichiro-san finally met my eyes.
That was when I saw it for the first time.
After hours together today, Eiichiro-san had finally shown me a small smile.
No, that wasn't the only thing I noticed.
I hadn't properly smiled at Eiichiro-san even once today either.
"Eiichiro-san, only if you don't mind, but... My place is close now, so would you walk me home?"
It might have been a slightly forced smile, but I raised the corners of my mouth as far as I could and asked Eiichiro-san that.
And on Eiichiro-san's pale face, a smile bloomed like autumn maple leaves.
"Y-yes! I'll take full responsibility for seeing you home!"
This smile of Eiichiro-san's―it looked less happy than relieved.
He hadn't been confident about what he'd done, and only with my words could he finally accept where things stood.
We walked shoulder to shoulder again through the cold air.
Shorter than me and with his head bowed, Eiichiro-san had an expression I couldn't see well.
And even though we were shoulder to shoulder, he kept a slight gap between us.
Did Eiichiro-san really have so little confidence in himself?
I may be repeating myself, but I truly don't care about the looks of the opposite sex.
As long as there's a basic level of cleanliness, short, fat, ugly or bald, I honestly couldn't care less.
So what filled my head were all the things Eiichiro-san had shown me through how he acted.
He was considerate of me, he praised what was inside me, and he saved me with everything he had.
For someone like me, Eiichiro-san had done everything he could.
I have no confidence in myself.
That's why I understand how Eiichiro-san feels, keeping his head down like this and keeping his distance.
Because I understand... that's why I want us to understand each other more.
Still, the way Eiichiro-san walked was awfully unsteady.
Couldn't he walk properly yet?
"U-um, Eiichiro-san, I'm glad you're walking me home, but... Can you really get home on your own? You're unsteady on your feet."
"Oh, this is... Hahaha, I hurt my leg a long time ago, and it's still like this."
I didn't know whether that was true, but was it really okay to let him go home alone like this?
And right then,
we arrived in front of my place.
"Thank you for walking me home. Um... I can at least make you some tea, so if you'd like―"
"Well then, good night!"
Eiichiro-san ran off before I had finished speaking, dragging one leg.
I wanted to go after him, but I felt rejected, and my feet wouldn't move forward.
No, I wasn't being rejected.
He was the same as me.
He couldn't say "Wait," or "Stay with me longer."
If our places had been reversed, I would have run off the same way.
It was like looking at myself―at a mirror.
Eiichiro-san... I'm the same as you.
Wanting to become the strong person I look up to. But having no confidence in myself...
Agonizing over how I could ever change. I understand that so well it hurts.
And you are the first person who ever looked at what's inside me.

Eiichiro-san... I don't want this to be the end...!
