"Kogori-sama! Niimura-sama! For this humble servant's many blunders, I most humbly beg your pardon! 　I, the genius girl Erina Goto, have committed the blunder of a lifetime...! Sob... sob...!"
Goto, the moment she joined us, performed a magnificent sliding kowtow.
"Goto, forget it and get to the platform already! We absolutely can't miss the next one!"
"Niimura-sama! Allow this humble servant to carry your luggage!"
"Forget it and just walk!"
"As you command!"
Goto started walking half a step behind, like a retainer.
"Kogori-sama! These rice balls, made by your honored mother! 　They seep into this humble servant's very innards...!"
"Those rice balls were made by my dad, though...?"
"What! Your honored father! 　Well, well, your honored family are omniscient, omnipotent gods who bring peace to this land of the rising sun! 　Beings enthroned upon the endless clouds of heaven, watching over all that lives, so far beyond human understanding as to be practically a concept"
"Goto... you're starting to get annoying..."
"Que droga! How could this humble servant!" [TN: in Latin letters in the source.]
"What's that supposed to be, 'Ku Droga'..."
"Niimura-sama! The words this humble servant spoke were―"
"Okay, talk normally again already! 　And 'humble servant' is something men say!" [TN: Goto's word for 'I' here, shosei, is used by men.]
"Ha! Just what I'd expect from Niimura-uji! All right! Then I'm switching back to normal! [TN: -uji: a quaint, mock-formal honorific.] 　That just now was Portuguese, and it means 'What the heck!' 　Droga's a word that originally means narcotics and drugs! It's real close to the English Drag!"
Completely broken, that's what Goto was.
It had to be because she was really sorry about being late... probably...
"Goto, we're not mad, so go back to talking like you usually do..."
"If you talk weird any more than this... I'll make you chug a Super Soda Bomber."
"Ugh...! N-not that...!"
Haruka brought the bottle near Goto, and like a magnet, Goto's face moved with it.
"So why were you late? Goto, isn't this the first time you've ever been late?"
"Actually... Remember how the other day we got matching phones as presents? 　While I was getting ready to leave, this turned up in my closet, so I put it on!"
Goto thrust the strap dangling from her new phone at us.
"Huh? It's the same as ours, just a different color. Where'd you buy it?"
"Who knows... Seems it was a looong time ago, and my mom says she doesn't remember either. I kept thinking about when it was, and..."
So that was why she was late.
Goto stays fixed on a thing once she starts thinking about it, so before she knew it, it had gotten that late.
"Haruka, where did we buy this strap?"
"Hmm, when was it... I already had it when I came back to Motoki-cho... Mom and the others probably just bought them somewhere around here, right?"
"I see... I take good care of mine too, so it feels like there's some memory attached to it, but... I've forgotten it now."
I gazed at the faded strap with its fraying fabric.
"And so... I am... truly... sorry...! 　This genius girl, as everyone agrees, me included, has committed a blunder worthy of ten thousand deaths...!"
"Well... hey, they say there's a fine line between genius and idiocy, don't they?"
"Huh!? Am I an idiot!?"
"Hm? At this point, you've got something to say back?"
"Que droga..."
Either way, leaving Goto behind had never been an option.
"Ehehe! But I'm happy! Me and you Senpai even have matching straps! 　Maybe we were friends, and we just don't remember!?"
"Goto as a friend, huh... You were a cheeky, logic-obsessed brat even back when you were little, weren't you?"
"That's not true! Ever since I was little, I've been a dainty, demure pretty girl, just like you see!"
"Yeah, yeah, got it, got it."
Come to think of it...
I feel like, a long time ago, I went to Zahha with a little girl, and to a zoo somewhere.
Haruka wasn't the one... so was it Goto?
Maybe I'm overthinking it.
―Eventually, we reached the last transfer station.
This was apparently called Toyotake Station. We would have to wait about 30 minutes for the next train.
"Ahh, man! If a certain somebody hadn't been late, I bet we'd be at Arata Station by now!"
"Grrr...! I can't say a thing against that..."
It was away from the city, and though it wasn't an unstaffed station, it was pretty rural.
Waiting a whole 30 minutes was a bit of a drag.
"Hey, want to step out the ticket gate for a bit?"
"Goto's in favor! I'll go negotiate with the station staff!"
Goto hurried off to the ticket gate.
"That girl's full of energy... Even I'm a little tired by now..."
I was starting to find it hard to keep sitting, too.
"It's fine, I'm telling you! Let's go, let's go!"
Though she'd made the same trip as us, Goto was full of energy.
Being young must be nice...
Past the ticket gate there was only a small roundabout and a kiosk.
Come to think of it, I feel like Motoki-cho used to look like this too, a long time ago.

Then, a girl was walking along by herself, looking all around.
"Huh? Is that Kana...?"
"Oh! It's Haruka-chan! What are you doing here!?"
"We've got time to kill till the next train, so I figured we'd take a little walk around. Kana, what are you doing here?"
"It's just me, by myself? I came here on an errand, but... the usual ice cream stand isn't here."
"You came all the way here by yourself!? How'd you even get here..."
"For me, running over the mountain is way faster than the train! 　The train goes all the way aroooooooooooooooooooound that mountain, so it's a really long way around!"
"Well, for you, a mountain like this is probably no different from flat ground..."
The 'mountain like this' right in front of the station.
What is a mountain...?
"Hey, hey, Haruka-chan, walk home with me! That way's shorter, isn't it!?"
"Y-you're joking! Don't lump me in with you, you monkey! 　Besides, it's not just me. There's two more of us!"
With that, Kana-chan turned her eyes toward us.
"Whoa...!"
Kana-chan ran up to us, her eyes sparkling.
"You're both super cute! Um, the small one is Goto-chan, and the big one is Natsumi-chan, right!? 　Nice to meet you! I'm Haruka-chan's cousin, Kana Niimura!"
"Huh? Oh, uh... nice to meet you..."
"N-nice to meet you..."
"Haruka-chan, do you always walk around with pretty girls like these attending you!? 　I'm pretty sure... with a trio of high-school girls, usually two are cute and one's just so-so, right?"
"Don't talk like I'm the so-so one, you damn brat!"
"Haruka-chan, did you know!? When you hit on a trio of girls, they say you should talk to the most so-so one! 　Then, out of ugly female jealousy, the two cute ones'll tag along too! 　Wouldn't Haruka-chan be the one who gets talked to most?"
"Gahh! Still the same un-cute little brat! Nobody's ever hit on me!"
"Ahh, I see... Haruka-chan always intimidates guys like that, so maybe they get scared off... Tonight I'll coach you, hands-on and hips-on, in the secrets of being popular!"
"Shut up, you pervy dirty-joke girl!"
This kid isn't cute...
"Kogori-senpai, that Kana-chan girl... she seems really happy to see Niimura-senpai."
"All right, everyone, let's set off on a fun mountain-climbing trip!"
"I keep telling you we're not going! We're taking the train!"
"Whaaat!? I wanna go for a walk with Natsumi-chan and Goto-chan!"
"You two, don't worry about her. If you keep up with a wild child like that, you'll be dead before we reach Arata."
"True... that mountain's a bit much..."
"Not with this luggage, I can't..."
Just then, a strong wind blew between us.
