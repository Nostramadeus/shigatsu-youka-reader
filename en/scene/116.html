Little Detective

Jusatsu Arc [Killing by Curse] · Shiyo 800-04-08 12:20 水 · 109 boxes

"What a crowd..."
It was called a park, but it wasn't a grand one like you'd see in a big city, just a children's park about 100 meters on each side.
The playground equipment was clustered in the middle, with cherry trees lined up all around it.
The outside was enclosed by a fence about 1.2 meters high, so you could see into the park from anywhere.
Naturally, the park was off-limits.
Quite a few police officers were still at work.
The blue tarp I'd seen in the photos online had been removed.
Around it was a group that looked like the press, and farther out still, dozens of rubberneckers had gathered.
Mixed in among them, there had to be some of the denizens of that message board.
"Geez, every last one of them... Don't these guys have anything better to do...?"
That was hardly something Haruka was in a position to say.
"With this, the food stalls'll probably be shut down for a while. Good thing I went yesterday..."
"Kogori-senpaaai! Niimura-senpaaai!"
No sooner had a girl's shout rung out than someone slammed into us from behind.
There was only one girl who called us Senpai and greeted us like this.
"So you were both here!"
"Huh? It's Goto. So you came too?"
"Yes! Did you both just get here, Senpai?"
Goto grinned from ear to ear.
"I was way too bored, so I dragged Natsumi along."
Incidentally, Kogori is my family name, and Niimura is Haruka's.
And this noisy little shrimp is my junior, one year below me, Goto.
She was the one Haruka had mentioned in passing yesterday, the very culprit who'd eaten her way through the park's food stalls.
Though she's as small as a child, she has a bottomless stomach.
"Hm, but are you okay, Kogori-senpai? The cherry blossoms don't bother you?"
"Yeah, I'm feeling weirdly good today."
"Is that so! Then that is splendid news indeed! 　Hey, hey, Senpai, do you two have a minute? Actually, you do, right? You came here because you were bored."
Goto's words made Haruka's eyes sparkle.
"Oh! You got some good info or something?"
"Just what I'd expect of Niimura-senpai! Your nose for this sort of thing is something fierce! Truth is, Boss, it's exactly as you say, gesu!" [TN: "gesu" = a stock servile underling verb ending from period dramas]
Goto rubbed her hands together and sidled up to Haruka.
"Who're you supposed to be...?"
"I've been here since this morning gathering all kinds of information. I'd wager I'm the civilian best informed on this incident, you know!"
"Goto! Have you had lunch?! This Niimura-senpai's treating you!"
"Oooh! Just what I'd expect of the Boss! Operation successful!"
"Hah! So you were after lunch all along. Well, I've got a war chest today anyway."
Haruka and Goto hit it off when it comes to things like this.
Goto and I met first, but ever since I introduced her to Haruka later on, it's always like this.
It's a fine thing that they get along, but getting dragged around by it is exhausting.
"Come on, Natsumi! Let's go! We're moving to Zahha!"
"Oh, wait up!"
Before I knew it, the two of them were already on their bikes.
In the end, we were only at the park for 2 or 3 minutes.
But thanks to running into Goto, it looked like we'd learn quite a lot.
We arrived at Zahha.
We come to this family restaurant every so often.
A lot of what looked like school bikes were parked outside.
"Welcome. A party of three?"
Zahha was very crowded today.
From the look of it, it was nothing but groups of high schoolers.
High schoolers overflowing from the park and the family restaurant both. So much for the stay-at-home order.
"Now, Goto, don't hold back! Eat whatever you want!"
"Boss, you sure are generous today, same as always."
Goto gleefully opened the menu.
Once we'd finished ordering, we each came back from the drink bar.
"Ah, Goto, just watch out for one thing."
"Yep?"
"If it was just me, I wouldn't mind, but today Natsumi's here too. Anything that'll ruin Natsumi's food for her, save it for after we eat."
"As you command, Boss! Understood!"
Goto snapped off a salute.
"Well then, on to carrying out the Operation! Where should I start..."
With that, Goto took a notepad out of her pocket.
Then, with a suddenly serious face, she began staring at the notepad.
"Ummm... you already know a body was found, right? 　The body was found at around 5:10 a.m. It was discovered by a 68-year-old man. Apparently he'd gone into the park to set up his food stall."
"At that point, the victim was apparently still alive. Calling for help in a moaning sort of voice, it seems. The man was startled, but he called the police and an ambulance right away..."
"Didn't make it, huh..."
"Yes. But that much is in the news already, so it's not particularly good info."
"Yeah. We read that much in the online news too."
The whole time, I listened in silence.
"So, from here on it's information I got going around asking―"
Goto had gotten that far when the sizzle of cooking meat came closer.
"Thank you for waiting. Special steak lunch?"
"Oh, yes! That's me!"
Goto's serious face suddenly switched back to her earlier troublemaker mode, and she raised her hand energetically.
Before long, everyone's food had been brought out.
"Ooh, amazing! A special steak 5 centimeters thick! 　Well then, Niimura-senpai! Thank you for the meaaal!"
Goto began cutting into the hunk of meat, cutlery clinking.
"So, what's this info you heard, Goto?"
"Uhmm, fo, bevowe, a fimihar infihenh, a fimihar infihenh haffened, affawenhwyUhmm, fo, bevowe, a fimihar infihenh, a fimihar infihenh haffened, affawenhwyUhmm, fo, bevowe, a fimihar infihenh, a fimihar infihenh haffened, affawenhwy
"Swallow the meat before you talk..."
Haruka said so, and Goto grinned from ear to ear and swallowed with a gulp.
"Mmm! 　 Delicious! 　This flavor, this volume, at this price! Well now, is this truly beef?"
"Don't say weird stuff..."
"Ahahaha! Sorry."
Goto said so and took a sip of her miso soup.
"Haah... Okay, um. Apparently it happened before, too. A similar incident."
"Huh?"
"Huh?"
"A similar incident, like today's?"
"That's right. Well, I'll give you the details after we eat, though. The Megasawa City murders, do you know about them?"
"Megasawa City... murders?"
"Yes. In neighboring Iizawa Prefecture, there's Megasawa City. There, every few years, and on April 8 no less, brutalized bodies are found."
I'd heard of it.
No, it was more than just having heard of it.
If I remember right, for about 10 years now, every 3 years or so, there have been murders in the next prefecture over, in Iizawa Prefecture's Megasawa City.
・They occur every few years, always on April 8
・The bodies are found in an abnormally gruesome state
・Two people are killed each time
・Crimes physically impossible for a human
・They have happened three times so far
What's more, the culprit reportedly still hasn't been caught.
I didn't remember the details, but every incident was supposed to have had a method so uncanny it could only be thought of as a spiriting-away.
Things that would call for instantaneous movement or a leap of several meters, or else monstrous strength no human could produce.
"Th-that sounds familiar somehow..."
Haruka's expression stiffened, and she answered in a low voice.
"Actually, I thought this incident and the Megasawa City murders might be connected, so I was looking into it. But then I made another new discovery."
Here, Goto took a sip of cola and caught her breath.
"The first Megasawa City case happened 10 years ago, on April 8, Shiyo 790. But on April 8, several years before that, right here in Motoki-cho, in Central Park, one hanami visitor died, apparently."
"Huh? At the Central Park we were just at?"
"Yes, that Central Park. In other words, counting today's incident, people have died on April 8 five times."
I got goosebumps.
In Motoki-cho, where even an incident was rare, in the park, in hanami season, someone had died.
"So, twice in Motoki-cho, three times in Megasawa City, there were murders on April 8..."
"No, that's not quite right. The first one, at Motoki-cho's Central Park, was purely an accidental death. There were plenty of witnesses as well. An off-duty police officer was apparently at the hanami too, so the chance it was an incident is zero. Nom nom..."
"This incident... the Megasawa City case... and an accident at Central Park more than 10 years ago... Is that accident at Central Park more than 10 years ago connected to the other four incidents?"
"It isn't."
Goto declared it crisply.
"Senpai, your hands have stopped. Let's save the rest for after we eat. Oh, can I add an order of the spring-only parfait?"
"Huh? Oh... eat whatever you like."
Haruka brought pizza to her mouth as if she'd just remembered it.
I likewise brought spaghetti to my mouth.
"Oh, by the way, to change the subject, do a lot of people from our high school go to universities outside the prefecture?"
"Hm? No, pretty few. Apparently it's mostly the same bunch from middle school through high school and college."
"I see. I'm still torn about which universities to apply to."
"Goto, you're smart, so you can go wherever you want. I'm sure of it."
"Eheheee! I'm the genius girl, as everyone agrees, me included!"
Goto grinned from ear to ear at me.
"But leaving Motoki-cho would be lonely... Come to think of it, Niimura-senpai, you left this town once, didn't you?"
"Yeah, I lived here in Motoki-cho till kindergarten, but my dad's job had us move. 3 years ago my dad finally got transferred overseas, so it turned into a posting away from the family. Just me and Mom came back to this town."
Haruka started dumping loads of Tabasco on her pizza as she said this.
"............? 　Huh...?"
"Huh? You two have been friends since kindergarten? That's news to me!"
"Didn't I tell you? 　Our moms were childhood friends, and that's how we've been friends too, ever since we were old enough to remember. After I moved, it was letters and email. When it was decided 3 years ago that we'd come back to Motoki-cho, Natsumi told me, 'The place next door is empty.' I begged Mom, and we moved into the place we're in now."
Haruka spoke in a soft voice, as if reminiscing.
"I see! 　Oh, did you know? Motoki-cho is famous for its cherry blossoms, and its population is growing fast! 　Seems lots of people are moving here for the cherry blossoms. The population's already over 40,000, so it's only a matter of time before it becomes Motoki 'City.'"
"Huh! I knew Motoki-cho was famous for cherry blossoms, but so that's why the population's grown so much! 　Come to think of it, all kinds of buildings are going up all over the place! 　And the goshinboku at Central Park especially, that's really something."
"It sure is! Oh! Speaking of cherry blossoms―"
"Uh... Goto... let's drop the cherry blossom talk about now..."
"Oh... I'm sorry, Kogori-senpai..."
"Huh?"
Oh, right, they probably thought it wasn't good to talk about cherry blossoms too much in front of me.
"Ahaha, just talking about them doesn't bother me. It's fine."
Making the people around me tiptoe like this is apparently one of the traits of April sickness, too. [TN: A pun on gogatsu-byo, 'May sickness,' Japan's term for the May blues.]
"Haruka, I was really happy when you came back! But still, not having your dad around must be lonely... My dad has only been on a business trip to Hokkaido since yesterday, and even that's lonely..."
"Honestly, Natsumi... You really are a daddy's girl..."
"Huh? But if they're family, of course you want them close by, right?"
Having said that much, I happened to glance over at Goto, and―
"............"
Goto wore a solemn look for an instant, but
"How nice, being close since way back. I'm jealous."
Goto immediately grinned from ear to ear.
That look on Goto's face just now...
Had we said something strange?
"Thanks for the fooood! That was deliciouuus!"
"You satisfied?"
"Not by a long shot! The limited-edition spring parfait has yet to arrive!"
Goto grinned from ear to ear, her whole face beaming.
"Haha, that's right."
"Now then, since everyone's done eating, back to the story."
"All right, go ahead."
"Ahem. Well then..."
"This time of year, by 5 in the morning, a fair number of people come to the park. Since it's gotten warmer, elderly people for walks, students for morning practice. And of course setting up the food stalls."
"So being found at 5 in the morning isn't that unnatural, right?"
"Right. The problem is that between the discovery and the police arriving, the body― to be precise, the victim on the verge of becoming a body, was seen by a lot of people. But..."
"But...?"
"But...?"
I gulped, and so did Haruka.
"No one could make out the face, or the sex, or the age. Do you understand what this means?"
"But that was in the online news too. That they couldn't tell the sex and stuff."
"That's the police's point of view. What matters is that 'the witnesses are ordinary people.'"
"What's that supposed to mean?"
"Listen, okay? The police are strictly a public body. They don't put out opinions with anything subjective in them. They don't make public anything but objective information."
"Uh-huh..."
Somehow Goto's talk was getting complicated.
"But what about ordinary people? Don't they talk about things only subjectively? 'Probably a man,' 'probably someone young,' I think they'd say at least that much."
"Well, true..."
"But if there's none of that, it means the damage to the body was so severe you couldn't even tell the sex, doesn't it? And yet! When found, the victim was still alive! 　I think there's only one condition a body could be in for that to happen."
Not a trace remained of the Goto who'd been grinning from ear to ear just a moment ago.
We were hanging on Goto's every word.
And then Goto took a sip of water and said this.


The skin of the whole body had been peeled off.
"The skin...?"
I froze with the glass of tomato juice I'd been about to drink.
A flayed body? The whole body, at that?
"That's right. It's no figure of speech. I believe the victim was literally dying, skin peeled off."
"Hey now... that's way beyond bad."
Haruka's face twitched.
Apparently she hadn't expected anything this gruesome.
"I also examined other angles, like burning to death, or dissolution by acid, but in the end I think this is the most likely. It's a way of killing every bit as cruel as the past Megasawa City case. After all, it means having the skin of your whole body gouged off with a blade while you're alive, while you're conscious."
"Whoa... I've heard of dismembered bodies and awful lynching incidents, but the whole body flayed..."
"As if to back that up, the witnesses all said the same thing."


That it had been a bright-red body.
"Thank you for waiting. The spring strawberry parfait."
A gigantic parfait was set down in front of Goto.
"Oh-hooo! Here it is, here it is! Sorry about this, Niimura-senpai!"
Goto's expression changed instantly, and she was happily admiring the parfait.
"Huh? Oh, no, no... Have as much as you like."
Goto went for the cookie on the very top first.
Being able to switch how she acts in an instant, whatever the mood around her, is what's amazing about Goto.
"Mmm! Crispy, crunchy! A parfait's cookie must indeed be eaten before it goes soggy!"
Ignoring the two of us, frozen after hearing about a flayed body, Goto plunged her long spoon to the bottom of the parfait in one go.
"Ha, haha... Glad you're enjoying it..."
But my head was taken up with something else.
A bright-red body, dying beneath a cherry tree.
That's exactly the dream I had earlier...!
Could there be some connection between that dream and this incident―
"Senpai, are you okay?"
Goto sank her teeth into the bright-red strawberry with a big chomp.
Red strawberry juice trickled from Goto's mouth.
"Sorry, I need the restroom..."
"Natsumi, you okay? Want me to come with you?"
"No, I'm feeling good today."
As I stood up, I forced a smile their way.
I went into the restroom and let out a long breath.
A bright-red body, and still conscious even so...
Goto has an innocent, lively face, but she's very smart.
Haruka is a bundle of curiosity and drive, but more thoughtful and kind than anyone.
And me...
I probably have no personality to speak of, apart from hating cherry blossoms.
Grades, dead middle; sports, lower middle.
But I do have a strange trait.
It's intuition.
I have abnormally sharp intuition.
To be more specific, I'm eerily good at sensing danger.
So good you could practically call it precognition.
Many times before, I'd sensed all sorts of dangers and escaped harm.
For example, last spring.
I stopped Haruka when she was about to step into some tall grass.
If Haruka had taken one more step then, she'd probably have stepped on the pit viper there and been bitten.
Other times too: Mom's car accident, and the train accident on Dad's business trip.
It happened a few times with me, too.
If you call it coincidence, it can be written off as coincidence, and calling it a trait of mine is an exaggeration.
But Dad, Mom, Haruka, and Goto all know about my intuition.
So they take my warnings at face value.
And the other strange thing: this intuition only works at this time of year, while the cherry blossoms are out.
Though that doesn't mean I can always sense danger just because the cherry blossoms are out.
There are times I can sense it, and times I can't.
And then that dream earlier.
Somehow it seemed like it had to be connected to Goto's reasoning...
I tried to remember more of that dream.
A bright-red person lay collapsed, asking me for help.
Then blood began raining from the cherry tree, and my ankle was grabbed.
At the end, that person screamed at me, and the dream ended.
............
No, that's wrong. I'm misunderstanding something.
Something just doesn't fit.
"Whew..."
I took another big, deep breath.
I'll try asking Goto about the dream.
She might be able to analyze it.
