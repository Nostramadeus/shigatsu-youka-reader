The Worst Resolve

Wabigoto Arc [Apology] · Shiyo 790-04-07 22:10 水 · 23 boxes

"You came, so that means you're ready for this, right?"
Mifuyu-san briefed us on tomorrow.
A Noh mask and red robes, a nata and a mace, and a strange drug.
Mifuyu-san apparently really meant to have us commit murder.
"I'm going to the Arata settlement tomorrow. It's Eiichiro-san's third-year memorial service. I'm going to bed now." [TN: Buddhist memorials count the year of death as the first year.]
In the end, we couldn't talk Mifuyu-san out of it.
On top of that, if we betrayed her, the 'someone I can trust' in the Arata settlement would put our lives in danger too.
When I woke up in the morning, Mifuyu-san and Haruka-chan were already gone.
Had they already left for the Arata settlement?
"Mm... Oh? Where are Mifuyu and the others...?"
"I just woke up myself..."
"I see... So Mifuyu really means it..."
Akane started going over the instructions Mifuyu-san had left behind.
It had been too dark last night to check what they said.
Did she really intend to do it?
"Ryoji-kun, stay here. I'll handle it on my own."
"D-don't be stupid!"
"There's no other choice anymore... Mifuyu and the others... if we abandon them and go home, it won't just be us. Even Natsumi will be in danger...!"
"There's still time! We have to think of another way!"
"That's why this is the best way. Only one person gets killed. The fewer victims, the better."
"Then you'll just get Mifuyu-san's distrust all fired up!"
"This is between me and Mifuyu. So, Ryoji-kun, stay here."
"It's not just between the two of you! It concerns everyone in the Kogori and Niimura families!"
"I... I'll go try the drug. It said to practice with half the dose. I'll scout the location too."
"Hey, Akane!"
―Akane didn't answer my call, and left.
Damn...! I can't let Akane carry the guilt all by herself!
Akane... If it's come this far, I'm going down with you...
Mifuyu-san's instructions, if we followed them, would let us live peaceful days in Motoki-cho again.
But that guilt would weigh on us forever.
Was there really no other choice...?
Some other way, where we wouldn't have to kill two children...
I caught myself thinking: if only we had brought Natsumi along again...
No, but we couldn't contact Akane's family home.
Talking by phone was off-limits, Mifuyu-san had warned us.
Or else, going back to Akane's family home and bringing Natsumi here...
A round trip would just barely make it in time, but there was no guarantee she'd be home, and if we got caught in traffic, that would be the end of it.
The risk was too great...
Was this... the end of the line...?
This all started because of Natsumi's balloon.
The responsibility for that lies with us, her parents.
The misfortune suffered through the loss of a life, repaid with a life...
Something like this... can't possibly be forgiven.
No, who would I even be asking forgiveness from?
God? The police? The victims' parents?
―No matter how many times I went back and forth with myself, I couldn't reach a conclusion I could accept.
Akane showed no sign of coming back.
I started to get a little worried.
"I'm back."
Akane came back as if nothing had happened.
"This drug... It's really amazing. It's only for 10 minutes, but you can move so fast you'd hardly think you were human, and jump too. But if you don't hold back, it feels like your body would break down fast... Even though I thought I was going easy, my body hurts a little."
I could no longer sense any hesitation in Akane's eyes.
Whatever we did now, there was no time left.
"Akane... I'll go try the drug too."
"Just me is enough. Ryoji-kun, you don't need to do this too."
"I can't let you carry the guilt alone. If we're doing this, I'm in it with you..."
"Are you really sure...?"
"It's... all right now... All we can do now is protect ourselves. If it comes down to choosing between my family and the lives of kids I don't know, there's nothing to hesitate over."
"............ I'll take the girl. Ryoji-kun, you take the other one."
"Got it."
I know it's the worst possible decision.
The reason murders never disappear from the world―I finally understood it.
Not everyone commits murder out of greed.
It's just that―some murders are committed only to protect oneself...
Yes, in a sense, you could call this self-defense.
If I don't think of it that way, I feel like I'll lose my mind.
No... I've probably lost it already...
