Humiliation

Saigo no Koe Arc [Last Voice] · Shiyo 800-05-04 19:00 月 · 68 boxes

Kogori-senpai is on the phone with her parents in a room on the second floor.
Niimura-senpai is at the meeting hall, preparing for tomorrow's funeral.
And I'm just sitting here.
Just swinging my legs on the veranda.
The Niimura main house's cherry tree, grown so much shorter.
It's shorter because the tree itself fell down underground.
Droga was being made from this tree's roots.
This Arata cherry... what is it?
There are countless plants that drugs can be refined from.
The raw material of opium: poppy seeds.
The raw material of marijuana: cannabis.
And the raw material of ephedrine and methamphetamine: the cherry.
No, the idea that cherry trees contain ephedrine is nothing more than a popular belief.
But there's no smoke without fire.
How did a popular belief like that come about?
These days it has spread everywhere, told so plausibly that it passes for fact.
I won't be deceived by something like that.
I... won't be deceived.
The truth is already right in front of me.
It's already right in front of me.
There's no need to overthink it.
The truth was simply right under the lighthouse.
"Mm... Goto-chan?"
The one who appeared on the veranda was Chiga-nee.
"What's wrong?"
"Mm... I forgot the matches."
"............"
I go on staring hard at Chiga-nee.
"Mm... where were they..."
Chiga-nee rummages through the chest of drawers and the little storage boxes.
Chiga-nee's search is for something small, but mine is not.
Something invisible, and far too big.
And it's―right in front of me.
So I keep staring hard at it.
"Mm... Did I do something wrong to you, Goto-chan?"
"............"
"Mm... Goto-chan, I've felt like you've been glaring at me this whole time... You were looking at me just now too, weren't you?"
"That's just what I'm thinking, showing all too plainly."
"Mm... What do you mean?"
"I can't let the truth in front of me slip past."
"Mm... Goto-chan, what are you saying?"
"It's just the two of us here. There's no point playing dumb, is there?"
"Mm... I can't find the matches. Maybe they're on the second floor."
"The goal of the culprit of the curse-killings. It was to draw suspicion away."
"............"
"Kotaro-san went on being suspected as the one who killed Sakura-san. Then all it took was for there to be no one left to suspect him. That way, the family would never lose Kotaro-san. Kotaro-san being the real culprit or not didn't matter. Kotaro-san being treated as the culprit and gone for good was what couldn't be borne."
"Mm... Goto-chan, what on earth are you―"
"I still don't know who cut Sakura-san's neck. But the curse-killings after that happened in order to draw suspicion away from Kotaro-san."
"Mm... So what you're saying is, someone wanted to protect Dad even if it meant killing people."
"With exceptional athletic ability, killing would have been possible as well."
"Mm... I'll be going now."
"Chiga-nee! There's no one but you!"
At my throat, a cold sensation.
At my throat, the cold sensation of a blade.
At the same time, my thoughts are cold too.
No fear, no panic.
I only glare at the truth in front of me.
"I can take this as a confession, right?"
"Be quiet..."
"I don't think you did it purely out of selfish desire, Chiga-nee."
"Be quiet! 　Or else...!"
"Or else?"
"That neck of yours..."
"This neck of mine?"
"Goto-chan... Please understand. Dad and Kana―I can't let them grieve any more than this."
"I see. If the truth is known, some people will end up unhappy. That makes sense."
"............"
"Kotaro-san had been judged the culprit by some people. Kotaro-san getting caught would mean losing family again."
"If you understand... then just stop..."
"It was decided that Kotaro-san would be watched at the meeting hall. If you, Chiga-nee, killed someone while that was going on, Kotaro-san would be cleared of suspicion."
"Don't spell out every detail..."
"Until the suspicion was cleared, you kept killing every year."
"I'm telling you to stop...!"
"But because of the curse-killings that happened every year, the residents came to distrust one another. Chiga-nee, you went to Tokyo, and the curse-killings started happening even with you doing nothing."
"Be quiet!"
Chiga-nee presses the knife harder against my neck.
"Someone among them committed the curse-killings, and then, the other day, tried to kill us. Chiga-nee, were you even counting on Kotaro-san killing us?"
"No!"
"If the curse-killings didn't end now, this time it might be you, Chiga-nee, who came under suspicion. So this time, to protect yourself, you needed Kotaro-san caught. And for that, you brought us here―"
"No!"
"Chiga-nee, you can't do anything. If you lay a hand on me, you'll be arrested too, Chiga-nee. Then what happens to Kana-chan? She'll be all alone."
"Please, just be quiet...!"
"Then who was it that killed Sakura-san?"
"Huh?"
"Kotaro-san came under suspicion, and from then on, it all adds up with you as the culprit, Chiga-nee. Then, the one who killed Sakura-san― no, even if it was suicide, who was it that cut her neck?"
"That's what I want to know too...! 　If I'd known, I could have cleared Dad of suspicion...!"
"............"
"............"
For a while, I stay locked in a glaring match with Chiga-nee.
"I... Dad as the culprit―I still don't believe it, even now...! 　Because...!"
"Because? 　Because what?"
Chiga-nee puts one more knife to my neck.
Opening those blue and green eyeballs wide, she glares at me.
This is clear proof that Chiga-nee has no murderous intent.
If she meant to kill, one knife would be enough, and the work would be done in a single second.
She drew a second one only because she thought there wasn't enough intimidation.
Chiga-nee doesn't want to kill me. She wants to shut me up.
Chiga-nee kills me, and the family collapses completely.
Kana-chan is left all alone.
Chiga-nee can't kill me.
This glaring match is nothing but a farce.
―Perhaps realizing what I intended, Chiga-nee put the knives away.
"Chiga-nee, how did you carry out the curse-killings?"
"With a knife, something like that is easily managed..."
"You were skillful, then, for no one to ever find out. Did you have accomplices?"
"I had no accomplices..."
"Really?"
"Really... If anything, it's easier on my own."
But... even so, it's far too convenient.
Chiga-nee leaves for Tokyo, and after that, the residents kill one another.
Would something that convenient really happen every year?
It's far too neat.
"Chiga-nee, you're hiding something important, aren't you?"
"Something important...?"
"Chiga-nee, there must be something only you know. Please tell me. What are you hiding?"
"............ Nothing..."
"That's a lie! 　Spit it out! 　Don't you hide it!"
As if pushed down by my shouting, Chiga-nee dropped to her hands and knees.
Then her whole body starts to tremble, and a grating sobbing, like nothing I've ever heard before, echoes out.
Just like a child's, Chiga-nee's crude, innocent sobbing shakes my eardrums at an unpleasant wavelength.
"I...! 　I...! 　Since the day I was born... I've had a cursed body."
"Huh?"
"Hey, Goto-chan, look closely. My eyes―"
Chiga-nee's tears reflect the color of those eyes.
Tears and eyes, shining like a star at the far end of the universe.
When she blinks and her eyelids cover those eyes, colorless tears run down her cheeks.
"I was born with the fate of killing people. These eyes kill people― the proof of the curse-killing."
"............?"
"Shiyo 790― When I was 10, Mom died. Whether it was suicide or she was killed, I still don't know. But... if she was killed― Mom had more than just one person trying to kill her."


"I wanted to kill Mom too."
"Chiga-neee! Still not done!?"
"Mm... H-Haruka...! 　Sorry, I was just talking with Goto-chan for a bit..."
"Hey, hey, everyone's busy, so hurry it up. We can't start the work without fire, right?"
"Mm... All right, I'm coming right now. Well then, Goto-chan, let's pick that talk up again tomorrow."
Chiga-nee took the matches without even hearing my reply and went back to the meeting hall with Niimura-senpai.
Niimura-senpai came and went, and the whole time, my body was frozen stiff.
I punch the tatami again and again.
"Dammit! 　Dammit Dammit Dammit Dammit!"
Simply giving in to my anger, I ignore the pain in my fists and keep punching the tatami.
"Dammit! Dammit! Dammit! Dammit! Dammit! Dammit!"
I've never known humiliation like this...!
I press my fists harder into the tatami and grind them, twisting my wrists.
I've been had.
Completely had.
To think someone would go to such thorough lengths.
It seems my thinking was more naive than I imagined.
I've been deceived all along.
My hypothesis has been spectacularly overturned.
Wherever we went, wherever our thinking led, a cunning misdirection had been laid, and the tracks leading to the truth had been warped and lured off toward fiction.
Come to think of it, there's still far too much I don't know.
This case is so deep and so dark that I can't even sort those things out.
I looked up at the cherry tree once more.
The road to the truth still seems a long, long way off.
That road is probably being twisted by misdirections called countless forks.
Someone is trying to deceive me.
It isn't Kotaro-san, and it isn't Chiga-nee either.
A completely different will.
A devil-like presence lurking in this Arata settlement keeps deceiving the entire settlement.
We were only dancing in the palm of that one's hand.
You mustn't learn the truth?
That's just someone using Kogori-senpai's intuition to block the road to the truth.
Chiga-nee's eyes are the proof of the curse-killing?
That's just some coincidences piling up and making it look that way.
Kogori-senpai's precognition, and Chiga-nee, supposedly cursed since birth.
Those too must be mysteries I can solve, as long as I get hold of a clue.
But at this point, it's starting to look like the realm of the supernatural.
In the first place, the moment Kogori-senpai has danger sense, it's already supernatural.
Kogori-senpai's precognition... what is it?
It looks like I need to rethink things from there.
But there can't be anything like the occult or the supernatural in this world.
That's nothing more than putting on a label named the occult or the supernatural.
What I want to see isn't the label.
It's what's inside.
Someone is using the label of the supernatural and pulling the strings from inside.
What I call the supernatural is a cunning trick dressed up as an inexplicable phenomenon.
There must always be causality there.
I won't give in to the temptation to write it off as the supernatural.
Besides, Chiga-nee is still hiding far more than I imagined.
I won't be deceived.
I will reach the truth, without fail.
But right now, it's hard to think calmly.
I need time to cool my head.
Enough―that's it for today.
For today, I'll put the curse-killings out of my head.
Yes, there's one more thing I should think about.
Kana-chan.
Kana-chan has no family left but Chiga-nee.
Rather than chase the truth, first I want to put Kana-chan's mind at ease.
That's right, I mustn't give in to my anger.
There are things more important than the truth.
After all, if nothing else, we acknowledged each other as sisters.
