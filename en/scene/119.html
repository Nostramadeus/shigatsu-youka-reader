"Oh, welcome back. You okay?"
"Yeah..."
Goto had already finished her parfait.
At the bottom of the dish, some red strawberry juice was left.
"Hey, Goto, there's something I want you to hear."
"What's the matter?"
Goto was gulping down her umpteenth drink-bar cola, glug glug, without even using a straw.
"Earlier, at Haruka's place, I dozed off for a bit... and I had a strange dream then."
When I said that, the two of them started and looked at each other.
"Whoa... Natsumi, that thing of yours is scary. And you had it at my place, of all places?"
"I don't believe in stuff like the occult or psychic powers, though. But Kogori-senpai's famous intuition, that I do believe in!"
Goto grinned from ear to ear.
"Somehow, I feel like it's connected to today's incident... It might just be my imagination, but will you hear me out?"
When I said that, Goto opened her notepad.
―I told the two of them what the dream had been about.
Haruka listened with a serious face, and Goto listened while taking notes.
"That's creepy, or rather... it's a bit much to be a coincidence... And it's your intuition in this season, right? Can't exactly laugh that off..."
"Hmm, hmm, it might have to do with Kogori-senpai's famous intuition. Suppose it was a dream about being attacked by the red person. In that case, going by your reading, Senpai, it doesn't quite add up. A few points bothered me, so I'll ask some questions."
"Yeah."
"Um, was that person a man? A woman? Couldn't you tell from the moaning?"
"The voice sounded like a man's, maybe. But I think a woman could make that voice too."
"Even the scream at the end?"
"Ah, that one, I think it was probably a man. Probably."
"Then for now, I'll go ahead treating that person as a man."
Goto was scratching her head with her pen, glaring at her notes.
"Senpai, you said he was asking you for help, but was he really?"
"Huh?"
"It was moaning, right? He didn't say anything like 'help me,' did he?"
"Well, that's true, but..."
"So it's not certain he was asking for help. After that, your ankle got grabbed, right? Right before that, what did you do, Senpai?"
"At that point, I was looking at the blood falling from the tree, and when I looked down, my eyes met that person's... I think."
"When your eyes met, he grabbed your leg?"
"Hmm..."
Goto and Haruka were watching me intently.
Let me try to remember a little better.
Goto's questions, held up against my memory, did turn up a spot that didn't sit right.
"That's not it... After our eyes met, I said 'I'll get help' in a small voice. Then I got grabbed."
"I see, I see..."
Goto was smoothly jotting more notes.
"And at the end, when you said 'stop,' the man got up and screamed right in front of you. What was he screaming?"
That horrible scream was still burned into my ears.
"I don't know... Just something like 'Aaaaaah.'"
"I see, I see..."
Goto added still more to her notes, then put down her pen with a sigh.
Apparently she'd gathered the information she needed.
"Senpai, this is dream analysis, so please listen as though half of it's made up."
"Yeah."
"That person, it seems, wasn't asking you for help, Senpai, and wasn't trying to harm you either."
That wasn't asking for help, and wasn't attacking me either?
"Senpai, he was trying to tell you something. The danger that's going to fall on you, Senpai."
A chill ran through me.
Earlier I'd only felt on instinct that it was dangerous, but now it matched Goto's logical analysis.
"Why do you think that? Natsumi's in danger from here on?"
"Yes. First, he wasn't clearly asking for help, right? But at the very least, he was trying to get something across."
"Yeah."
"And when you, Senpai, tried to go for help, he grabbed your leg with tremendous strength. Do you get what that means?"
"I get it. So it meant 'Don't go.'"
"Exactly. 'Don't go. I still haven't been able to tell you of the danger that will fall on you.'"
"But you can't say for sure that it was about warning of danger, right?"
Goto's deductions, I knew, were pointless to interrupt.
But Haruka was oddly impatient.
"Right, that's the mystery. At the end, the man got up and screamed. It probably wasn't just a scream. If he meant to harm you, he wouldn't just scream. He'd strangle you, or hit you. But that person chose to scream."
"............"
Haruka finally stopped butting in, too.
"He was trying to get something across. You see, Senpai, you have an intuition that senses danger. If it comes from that, then 'he was trying to warn of danger.' That was that person's purpose. Well, I'm explaining a hypothesis with a hypothesis, so it's not logically sound, though."
"So if we knew what that person screamed..."
"Senpai, can't you remember? What that person screamed."
"Hmm, no, I really can't..."
"I see..."
"Hey, Goto, is it connected to this incident...? The situation's a little similar, and that bothers me..."
When I said that, Goto began writing something, as if hiding her notepad.
But she stopped her hand right away and made a motion like erasing what she'd written.
A silence settled for a while...
"Dooooooh!!!!"
Suddenly Goto threw both hands up and started shouting, not caring who was looking.
"What's wrong, Goto?!"
"You scared me..."
Goto grinned from ear to ear.
"There's no point thinking about this stuff anymore! Come on, come on, the three of us are here together, so let's talk about fun things! 　Okay, okay, that's it, Operation complete!"
For a moment we were dumbfounded, but
"O-ohh, Goto, well said! Even you say good stuff once in a while!"
Haruka jumped in on it with a smile, too.
"I always say good stuff! Niimura-senpai, you're so ruuude!"
"Oh yeah, earlier, Natsumi here sees the online news, and it's scary, scary! Mom's home late today, scary, scary! 　That's what she was saying! And she's in high school!"
"Wow! Kogori-senpai, how cuuute!"
"H-hey! Don't tease me!"
"Hahahaha! Sorry, sorry!"
"Niimura-senpai! You mustn't pick on Kogori-senpai so much! Unlike Niimura-senpai, she's very delicate!"
"What was that, Goto! Are you saying I'm crude?"
"Huh? Huh? Any way you look at it, you're crudeness walking around in clothes. About as far from ladylike as it gets!"
"Why, you...!"
"See, see, that's what I mean! H-hey, don't tickle me...! 　Gyahahahaha!"
Watching the two of them go back and forth, I couldn't help giggling.
If that dream was one of the nasty intuitions peculiar to this season, I'd had a feeling it would come down on me in some form.
But Haruka and Goto were both on my side, encouraging me.
Getting it out and talking it over like this had definitely lightened my heart.
―After chatting for a while, we left Zahha.
"Goto, what're you doing now?"
"I've got cram school now."
"Isn't cram school canceled?"
"I checked the cram school's website, and it said my course is running."
A big truck roared past on the road, and a violent wind swept over the area.
And the cherry petals lying on the asphalt were swept up into the wide, overcast sky.
"Don't!"
"Natsumi?"
"Senpai?"
"Don't! Goto, don't go!"
I was sensing danger on instinct. This situation was dangerous...!
I'd had this feeling many times before: that sense of detecting danger in the season when the cherry trees bloom.
How do humans tell smells and tastes apart―
Explaining this sense is as hard as explaining that in terms of what you feel.
Yes, this is my intuition―a danger sense I can use only at this time of year.
Like this, it comes suddenly, without any warning.
"............"
Goto put a hand to her chin and stared down intently.
Then, looking at my face this time,
she slowly came closer and grinned from ear to ear.
"Seeenpai!"
She stood on tiptoe and put both hands on my shoulders.
Then she craned up to look me full in the face.
"Guess I got found out? There's no cram school that starts at this hour, is there! Operation failed..."
Now that she mentioned it, cram school for a high schooler starting at 2 in the afternoon was way too early.
"The cram school was a lie! So, I'll stay with you, okay?"
Goto was gazing at me with a bright, clear face.
"Niimura-senpai, may I come over to your place too?"
"Yeah! If you're free, I was thinking of asking you over."
"Ehehe, yay!"
Goto happily got on her bike.
Somehow, I felt really relieved.
I felt like this would let us avoid a great danger.
It only felt that way.
Whether we could really avoid danger, whether any danger had been about to happen in the first place, there was no proof of any of that anywhere.
But what was it I'd seen a moment ago?
A mask... maybe?

"No, we promised from the start the rubbernecking would only be for a bit. I'm satisfied! Sorry for dragging you along."
"No, I felt good today, so it's fine! I got to see Goto too, and like always, when I go somewhere with Haruka, I run into fun things!"
"So, uh... Natsumi..."

"What's wrong? Bathroom? Is your stomach acting up again?"
"No, no... it's not that. It's just, since we're out anyway... Natsumi's feeling good... school's closed... and the weather's not bad..."
Seeing Haruka like that, I couldn't help giggling.
"Heh... Haruka, where do you want to go?"
When I said that, Haruka scratched her head sheepishly.
"Well, actually... today by the station there's this special pizza fair with an Italian chef... It's just... I was thinking I'd like to go..."
I see, so that was why Haruka had pushed delivery pizza for lunch.
Since going out was hard with me along, she'd been trying to make do with delivery pizza.
"Heh heh, just what I'd expect from the woman who loves pizza most in the world! Sure, let's go!"
Haruka being selfish with me like this makes me happy.
"Ohh, Natsumi! You're seriously my angel!"
Haruka threw her arms tight around me.
"Geez, you're overdoing it. But Haruka, you just ate, didn't you? Aren't you full?"
"No, I was thinking of getting takeout. Pizza for dinner too sounds good."
"All riiight! In that case, we sortie at once!"
"Yeah!"
I, too, caught Haruka's mood and started pedaling my bike.
