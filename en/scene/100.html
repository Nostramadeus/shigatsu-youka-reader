Another Rite

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-07 22:00 火 · 40 boxes

Play options

"Now then... it's 10 p.m. Chigaya, time to get ready."
"Mm... Right."
"Are you going somewhere?"
"Mm... I become a witch tonight. A witch is something like the settlement's priest."
Chiga-nee's face no longer showed any trace of the drink.
Her expression was serious, like a knight heading for the battlefield.
"Natsumi-chan, when I asked you to come with me earlier, it was because I want you to watch that succession rite."
"Mm... In the Arata settlement, a grown woman who has lost her mother performs the rite of becoming a witch on the day before the next memorial service."
"Natsumi, it's a good chance, let's get them to show us! An outsider getting to watch has gotta be a first since the Arata settlement began! 　Goto, you're coming too, right?"
"Is it that big a rite? Is it really okay...?"
"Uh... Why are we being allowed to watch? Even if we're here because Senpai introduced us..."
"Curious?"
Erika-chan smiled as she began to speak.
"That's because... it's Natsumi-chan I want watching it. And the one who trusts that Natsumi-chan, Erina-chan, as well."
"Because it's me? 　Why me? Even if I'm friends with Haruka..."
"I'll tell you that after the rite is over. It has a little to do with the curse I told you about earlier."
With that, Erika-chan left the living room.
"Mm... The footing is bad on the way to the graveyard, so be very careful."
"The graveyard?"
"Mm... It's held in the settlement's graveyard clearing. There are no streetlights either, so the moonlight is all we have to go by."
I hate to say it, but a graveyard right after a ghost story is a little creepy.
"Chiga-nee, what about Kana?"
Kana-chan was nodding off sleepily.
"Hey, Kana, you seriously need to go to bed now."
"Nnyaah... Don't wanna..."
"Mm... If Goto-chan's going... I'm going..."
"Ehh..."
At this rate, she's probably planning to cling to Goto all over again.
"Kana-chan, it's already 10 o'clock, so shouldn't you go to bed?"
Goto, in an unusually big-sisterly tone, gently admonished Kana-chan.
True, however used she is to the place, it does seem dangerous.
Goto, though, may be sensing a danger to her own person even more than that.
"Goto's right. You stay here."
"............ Okay... Night..."
Kana-chan tottered out of the living room.
"Thank goodness..."
As I thought, Goto had apparently been dreading getting pestered by Kana-chan.
"Mm... Then, Haruka, please show the two of them the way."
"Yeah, leave it to me!"
"Huh? Aren't we all going together?"
"Grandma, Chiga-nee, and the witnesses each take a different path. We meet up in front of the grave."
"Is this a tradition too? Why would you do that?"
"Um... why was that again?"
"Mm... It's so the shinigami won't come after us."
"Shinigami...?"
"Grandma draws the shinigami to her along the way, so that they don't take the witnesses with them. And I, the one becoming the witch, go alone, so as not to bring impurity to the grave."
So the three of us go together, but Erika-chan and Chiga-nee each take a separate path alone.
"What do you mean by shinigami?"
"Mm... In Arata, we call the dead shinigami. It's been said since long ago that the shinigami try to take the living away with them."
I've heard of evil spirits trying to take living people away with them.
"Mm... It's a tradition from long ago, so why not go in the mood for a little test of courage?"
"Uh-huh..."
Still, we're headed to a graveyard, so it's even creepier than the ghost story earlier.
"H-hey, Goto, are you okay? Aren't you scared?"
"Huh?
　Ahahaha! 　Like I'd ever believe in something so unscientific―"

Having gotten that far, Goto made an 'oh no' face.
She had probably realized she'd said something thoughtless in front of someone who had, after all, grown up in that kind of settlement.
"Mm... It's fine, Goto-chan. If you come along with that kind of lighthearted attitude, it's easier on me and Grandma too. Goto-chan... it's a real shame I can't walk with you and protect you, though..."
Chiga-nee smiled as she stepped up to Goto.
"Uuh..."
Chiga-nee was throwing Goto wicked looks again, but things didn't seem as awkward as they had at first.
Just then, Obasan came back from the kitchen.
It seemed she had finished all the cleaning up.
"Mm... What will Auntie do? It looks like everyone except Kana and Dad is going to the succession rite?"
"Let's see! I'll take a bath! No need for me to go along too!"
So the ones going to the succession rite are Erika-chan, Chiga-nee, and the three of us from Motoki-cho.
"Okay, let's head out first. We gotta stagger when we leave, too."
"We do?"
"Yeah. Same as with tomorrow's thirteenth-year memorial, the rite starts the moment you step out of the house." [TN: Buddhist memorials count the year of death as the first year.]
"But earlier, Chiga-nee was drinking, wasn't she? Is that all right? When it's such an important rite..."
"Mm... Sacred sake is required. Inside me, the one who inherits the role of witch."
"So you always drink before the rite?"
"Mm... That's right. Drinking so much you can't walk is strictly forbidden, though."
"Somehow... that sounds like an excuse to drink..."
"Mm... Heh. Alcohol is used in all sorts of rites, after all. I'm sure people long ago did the same, finding some reason or other to drink."
But it seemed almost all the alcohol had left Chiga-nee's system.
"Okay, we're heading out first!"
―But there was no answer from inside.
"Alright, let's go! The rite's already started!"

"Kana-chan... aren't you sleepy? Are you okay?"
Goto, in an unusually big-sisterly tone, spoke to Kana-chan.
Come to think of it, I've never seen Goto talk with anyone younger.
For all her fussing, Goto might actually be happy to have someone younger take to her.
"I'm sleeping with Goto-chan tonight..."
"Uuh... Who knows what she'll do to me... Uh, is it okay if we bring Kana-chan along too? It seems sad to leave just her behind..."
Of course. Goto had said she'd been lonely at about Kana-chan's age.
That's probably why she doesn't like the idea of leaving her alone.
"Geez, fine. Kana, we're not going there to play, got it?"
At that, the sleepy Kana-chan's face lit right up.
"Yay!"
"Mm... Then, Haruka and Kana, please show the two of them the way."
"Yeah, leave it to me!"
"Mm... What will Auntie do?"
"Let's see! I'll tidy up the kitchen or something! No need for me to go along too!"
"Okay, we're heading out first!"
―But there was no answer from inside.
"Alright, let's go! 　The rite's already started!"
"Come on, come on, a test of courage at the graveyard!"
