The New Students' Representative Address

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-07 09:30 火 · 15 boxes

Shiyo 800, April 7

Thank you very much for holding such a grand entrance ceremony today for us, the new students of Motoki Prefectural High School. To the principal, to all the teachers, and to our honored guests, I offer my heartfelt thanks.
Here in Motoki-cho, famous for its cherry blossoms, the petals are dancing everywhere today as well.
The scenery surrounding us seemed almost to shine, and on the way to this hall, walking with my mother, each time I found cherry blossoms or other spring flowers, I felt as though every one of them was celebrating us new students.
The goshinboku that symbolizes Motoki-cho also seems to be celebrating and encouraging our new lives with its flurry of petals, and I once again feel the joy of being able to go on to a school in Motoki-cho, Sawa Prefecture.
Having received such warm words from the principal, our honored guests, and the upper-year students, I am truly deeply moved.
Until now, I have followed the rails my parents laid for me without a single question.
However, upon entering this school and receiving words from all of you, I have come to wish to gain independence and learning at this school, and to find, without fail, what I myself... want to become in the future. So strongly... Pff... have I come to feel.
We have... infinite possibilities...
What lets us... believe so... is flexibility...

Pfft...
Um...
Pffhehe...!
"Everyone, I'm sorry! A greeting like this just isn't me after all!"
With that, the new student up on the stage tore up the paper with her speech on it.
The new student slammed both hands down on the lectern, and the sound boomed through the microphone.
"I love Motoki-cho! 　The cherry blossoms are pretty, and the people are all kind. And I'm happy I got into a school in Motoki-cho again! 　All of you feel the same, right? 　We've entered a wonderful high school in this wonderful town. The teachers, the parents, the upper-year students, and our honored guests. I'm sure all of these people must be wonderful people too! 　I'm sorry the new students' representative is someone this off-the-wall, but I want to say it my own way after all, in my own honest words!"
"I don't know what hardships there will be. I don't know what tough things there will be. But that's exactly why I think it'll be interesting! 　The only reason I'm standing here is that my entrance exam score was good. I have an amazing senpai who I truly respect, who took me out into a world I didn't know."
"Let's find our new selves! Let's find new possibilities! 　Let's find a new world we don't know yet, one test scores alone can't measure! 　Here, there must be wonderful people who will make that happen!"
"I'm a troublemaker like this, but please, look after me!
　Representative of the new students, Erina Goto!"
The hall was filled with loud applause.
Just then, a message arrived on my phone.
Haruka had sent it.
“That amazing senpai? Bet that means Natsumi.”
Goto was grinning at me when I looked her way.
Goto's a high school student now too, starting today.
She was as tiny as ever, but since the first time we talked, Goto had changed a lot too.
When we first talked, I thought she seemed like a gloomy girl who mumbled and couldn't speak clearly, but now she could express herself this openly.
