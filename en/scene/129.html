Small Yet Great Heartbeat

Jusatsu Arc [Killing by Curse] · Shiyo 800-04-08 14:00 水 · 40 boxes

"Huh?"
Haruka's eyes were on a patrol car, stopped in front of the apartment building.
"Hey! We're hiding!"
Haruka got off her bike and quickly ducked into the shadow of the bike shed.
"Haruka, what's wrong?"
"You don't get it? High schoolers are under a stay-at-home order today. If we get spotted, we'll get in trouble, right?"
Haruka's example guiding us, I and Goto both huddled down in the bike shed.
"But Niimura-senpai, in that case, all the high schoolers who were at the park are getting in trouble?"
"............"
Goto's retort got no reaction at all from Haruka.
"The police, in a place like this... What on earth did they come here for?"
"That'd be door-to-door questioning, wouldn't it?"
"Oh, right. Door-to-door questioning, huh."
Before long, the patrol car drove off in the direction opposite from us.
Without even sounding its siren, it slowly disappeared toward the T-intersection at the far end.
We went into the entrance and pressed the elevator button.
"Door-to-door questioning, huh... Which means they must've come to our place too..."
"Senpai, you seem awfully bothered by that."
"Huh? Oh, no, 'cause with the police, we could maybe get all kinds of new info, right? Too bad..."
"Then why did you hide just now?"
"Like I said, we'd get in trouble for being out, didn't I? Running into the police outside and meeting them at home are totally different."
"Well... I suppose so."
"Sorry to intrude!"
Goto bustled eagerly inside the moment she got to Haruka's place.
"I'll put the tea on now, so have a seat."
"Okaaay!"
Goto answered brightly and
plopped down on the sofa with a whumpf.
"Natsumi, you sit down too."
"Okay."
I slowly sat down.
Goto turned on the TV without asking.
It looked like the midday news was on.
"Yes, we're coming to you live from Motoki-cho."
They seemed to be reporting on the incident at Central Park.
"According to information we have just received, the victim is believed to be male. However, the victim was found with the skin stripped from the entire body, and the victim's identity is still unknown."
"Ooh! My deduction hit the mark! Operation successful! 　Munch munch..."
Goto cried out happily.
In her hand, the sakiika I had brought along.
Amazing, how she can be happy hearing about a flayed body...
On the TV, a personality in the studio and the announcer at the scene were talking.
"There's information that the victim was still alive when first found, but is that true?"
"Yes. According to the person who found the body and other witnesses, the victim was still breathing when first found. However, it appears the victim died before the police arrived. That said, the estimated time of death and the time of discovery almost coincide, so I think it is quite possible that, just as the witnesses say, the victim was still alive."
There didn't seem to be anything particularly new.
No, maybe I should say it just hadn't been announced yet.
"............?"
It was from a number I didn't know.
It seemed to be coming from a landline somewhere in town.
The area code was a familiar number.
"Senpai, aren't you going to answer?"
"Hm, it's from some number I don't know."
For a moment I hesitated to answer, but I took one breath and slowly tapped my smartphone.
"Yes... hello?"
"Natsumi, are you getting along with Haruka-chan?"
"Oh, it's just Mom. I wondered who it was."
"My cell's battery died after all. A little while ago, Dad called. He said it looked like there was an incident over your way, and were you okay."
"Ahh, so it made the news in Hokkaido too."
"Well, he was worried, so I let him know the two of us here are both fine."
"Yeah, got it, yeah, bye."
She must have called from the phone at her work.
"Mom, geez. She never charges it properly..."
When I looked up, Goto was standing right in front of me before I knew it.
Her expression was awfully grim.
And within it, something terribly sad―
I could see a face on the verge of tears.
"Senpai... could you show me the number that called?"
"Huh? It's this one?"
I handed my smartphone to Goto.
"Natsumi, sorryyy, can you carry the tea over?"
"Yeah, coming."
Just as I stood up,
"Senpai!!"
The short Goto threw her arms tight around me.
With a strong grip, as if she were clinging to me.
"G-Goto? What's wrong? Huh?"
What surprised me even more was that Goto's heartbeat was frighteningly strong.
"Senpai, it's all right. I'll go too. I'll be with you."
Goto never once loosened her hold, pressing her heart against me.
"Huh? Huh?"
"Senpai, no matter what happens... please keep a firm hold on yourself."
"What's wrong? Go where?"
"Natsumiii? What's wr... H-hey, what are you doing..."
"Senpai, let's go, quickly... To the police!"
Goto, still holding on to me, pleaded in a forceful tone.
"To the police? Hey now, Goto... why would Natsumi go to the police?"
"Senpai... it's something you'll find out sooner or later, so I'll tell you. Please brace yourself and listen."
"Uh, okay..."
With that, Goto held me even tighter.
