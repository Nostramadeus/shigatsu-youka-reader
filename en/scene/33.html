Apology

Wabigoto Arc [Apology] · Shiyo 790-04-09 00:00 金 · 78 boxes

―The time had gone past midnight.
We were shut up in a room that smelled of disinfectant.
On the bed in the hospital room lay Haruka-chan.
Natsumi was asleep in a chair in the room.
"Haruka-chan's going to be okay, isn't she...?"
"They said it's not a fatal wound. Probably, since Haruka-chan got between them, Mifuyu-san tried to stop at the last second."
Mifuyu-san was not in the room but out in the hallway, and had been the whole time.
Had I been right to stop Akane?
Natsumi's words had suddenly come to mind at that moment.
Wasn't this what she had meant about not getting in the way?
Thinking that, my body had moved on its own.
"Mm... Where am I...?"
"Haruka-chan, can you hear me? Can you see?"
Haruka-chan slowly nodded.
"Natsumi... what about her...?"
"Natsumi's fine. She's not hurt."
"Thank goodness..."
Haruka-chan smiled, though her face still showed the pain.
"What about Mom...?"
"All right, I'll go get her now! Akane, you call the doctor!"
I dashed out of the room and ran to the chair in the hallway where Mifuyu-san was.
―The hallway lights had already been turned off.
A little way from Haruka-chan's room, a figure sat with head bowed.
Mifuyu-san sat without shifting, her face bowed down as if her soul had left her.
"Mifuyu-san, Haruka-chan's come to! 　She can see fine, and she can talk!"
But Mifuyu-san didn't move a muscle.
"What was I... doing...? All I... wanted was to help Haruka..."
"It's over and done with. More importantly, hurry and go to Haruka-chan...!"
"Ryoji-san... you don't blame me? 　I really tried to kill Natsumi-chan... your own real daughter, Haruka, I put through something like that..."
"Of course... Of course, I won't say I'm not angry at all. But right now, more than that, we should do whatever we can to cheer Haruka-chan up. And for that, Mifuyu-san, you've got to..."
"How am I supposed to face her? 　Haruka nearly got killed by me. How am I supposed to face..."
"But...! Haruka-chan wants to see you, Mifuyu-san!"
"Please... leave me alone a little longer... Right now I'm too scared to show Haruka my face... Haruka... if she came to hate me... then I... I couldn't live anymore..."
"All right..."
Leaving Mifuyu-san there, I headed for the room.
Mifuyu-san's heart was wavering considerably too.
If only there were something more... something...
In the room, Haruka-chan was sitting on the edge of the bed, answering the doctor's questions.
Natsumi was still asleep.
"She's fine. There's nothing wrong with her brain or her bones."
"I see. Thank you very much."
"Um, are you her father?"
"Uh, I'm..."
"My dad died. Natsumi let a balloon fly away, and he tried to get it and fell out of a tree..."
"Haruka-chan, does it hurt? Are you okay?"
"Yeah! It hurts a little, but I'm okay now! 　All that's left is... Mom..."
"............"
A stiff air hung between me and Haruka-chan.
"Ah, uh, I'll be going, then."
As if to escape that atmosphere, the doctor left the room.
But,
beside that door stood Natsumi.
"Hey... Haruka-chan's dad died?"
Oh no...! She heard us!
"Did he die because of me...?"
"Natsumi, Dad dying was an accident. Natsumi, it's not your fault―"
When Haruka-chan had gotten that far, Natsumi ran out of the room.
"Natsumi!"
I chased after Natsumi.
Dammit! Of course she'd react like that, hearing about Eiichiro at a time like this!
I have to tell Natsumi right away! Tell her it's not her fault!
Where...? Where did Natsumi go...!?
―Where I ended up was the hallway where Mifuyu-san had been earlier.
There I found Mifuyu-san and Natsumi.
Beside the panting Natsumi, Mifuyu-san, as expressionless as ever, was staring blankly at Natsumi's face.
"What is it..."
"Uh... Uh...! 　I'm sorry!"
Natsumi bowed her head.
Mifuyu-san's blank face changed to one of surprise.
"It's because of me that Ojichan died, isn't it?"
"Huh...?"
"So I'm sorry!"
"What are you saying... Saying that now isn't going to save Haruka..."
"I... heard Ojichan's voice! 　Telling me to help Haruka-chan!"
"Eiichiro-san did...?"
"But... Haruka-chan can only be helped if Obachan gets better too, right?"
"What... Natsumi-chan, what do you think you can do... Eiichiro-san died, and how much we suffered for it..."
I wanted to say something to Mifuyu-san, who gave back nothing but cold words.
But I felt that would get in the way of Natsumi's apology.
"I... I'll go to the same school as Haruka-chan too! Then I can help Haruka-chan! 　And then Obachan can get better too, right!?"
"Do you mean that? 　What will you do if your dad and mom say no..."
"If Haruka-chan says that'll make her better, then Obachan, you tell Dad and Mom too! 　I'll stay right by Haruka-chan's side, always!"
"Why go that far..."
"Because... Haruka-chan... Obachan... because of me... Uu... ngh... I'm sorry!"
"Oh... do whatever you want... But I might do something to Natsumi-chan again..."
"Obachan won't ever do that again! 　'Cause then Haruka-chan would be so sad! 　Haruka-chan loves nice Obachan so much! 　Obachan won't do anything Haruka-chan doesn't like anymore! 　'Cause Obachan loves Haruka-chan so much too!"
"What is all this...! 　After all this, Natsumi-chan... what do you think I can do...! 　Uuh... Eiichiro-san...!"
"Mom!"
It seemed Haruka-chan had walked here with Akane supporting her.
"I'm... sorry too!"
In front of Mifuyu-san, Haruka-chan bowed her head too.
"Trying to fix things with a paper-doll curse just because I was being bullied... that's wrong, isn't it... If that's going to turn Mom into a shinigami... I don't need curses anymore! 　So... so please be the old Mom again!"
"Haruka, you too... What are you saying, Haruka? I did it for Haruka...! 　Haruka, you called the shinigami because you wanted the classmates bullying you killed, because you wanted help, didn't you!?"
"No! 　No!　No! No!"
"That's not it! That's not it! Mom, you don't understand anything! 　'Cause... if I'm being bullied, Mom can't get better! 　I didn't want Mom to become a shinigami! 　If I'm being bullied, Mom won't ever get better! 　So... Mom, I wanted you to get better... Mom, because I want you to get better...!"
"Why...? Mom's fine."
"Then when can we go back to Motoki-cho!? 　Mom, you loved Motoki-cho so much, but you never try to go back to Motoki-cho! 　Dad died and you got sick in your heart, so you can't go back to Motoki-cho, right!?"
"Haruka, but if the bullies died, Haruka, you could get better, couldn't you?"
"Me getting better doesn't mean anything! 　I want to go back to Motoki-cho! 　I want to go home to Motoki-cho together with Mom! To the Motoki-cho where Natsumi is! 　If Mom doesn't get better, nothing means anything!"
"What is all this... I... what on earth was I doing it all for...!"
"I just want Mom to get better. But... I'm sorry, Mom... I think I... put a curse on Mom to become a shinigami. If Mom gets better like before, I'm not scared at all, even of being bullied or being all alone at school. I can put up with that as much as it takes."
"I made Mom a shinigami. Mom, listen. This time it's not a curse. I'll do a charm that makes Mom better."
"A charm to get better...?"
"Yeah. Not a curse, the angel's charm."
"Shinigami inside Mom, our angel, please save Mom. Mom... please make her better. If Mom gets better, I'll put up with anything."
"............"
"That was the charm that makes Mom better. The charm that saves Mom from the shinigami's curse. See? Mom, you're not a shinigami anymore, right? 　Now... you won't think you killed anybody anymore, right? I'm sorry. I'm the one who made Mom a shinigami. I won't use curses ever again. Dad died, but as long as everyone's here, I'm not scared of anything."
"Why are you apologizing, Haruka...! 　I'm the one who gave Haruka the black magic book...! 　I'm the one who egged Haruka on...! 　Haruka... I... I tried to make Haruka a witch. I'm the one who tried to make you a witch who calls the shinigami. So... Haruka did nothing wrong. It's all my fault. Akane... someone like me can't possibly go back to Motoki-cho...! 　After doing something like this, there's no way I'm allowed to go back!"
"Mifuyu... I'm... sorry too..."
"Why? Why are you apologizing too, Akane...!"
"I said I'd help you with anything, and then all this time I abandoned Mifuyu. If I really wanted to help, I should have searched for Mifuyu no matter what... Mifuyu, I let you get this bad and just left you alone..."
"Uugh...! 　Even Akane...! 　When I'm the one... who's to blame for everything...!"
"Mifuyu... You've always been... a big baby, wishy-washy, and extreme on top of that... You longed for a family more than anyone. And because of that, you push yourself too hard. So this time, rely on me. You don't have a single enemy. Everyone's going to help you, Mifuyu. So... let's go home to Motoki-cho, okay? 　I'll never abandon you again, Mifuyu. After all, a life without Mifuyu... I don't want that."
"No! After dragging everyone into this, there's no way I can go back...! 　But... Uugh... but still... Uugh...!"
"But still, what? 　You can at least say what you want yourself, can't you? 　Mifuyu, you're a full-fledged mother now, after all."
"I finally understood. How to make Mom smile. When I'm sad, Mom looks really sad too."
If I showed him I was cheerful, would Eiichiro-san be able to cheer up too?
If I showed him I was sad, would Eiichiro-san be sad along with me?
If he really is like me, couldn't we share the happy things and the sad things too?
"So, to make Mom smile, I just have to smile. I can always smile as long as I'm by Natsumi's side."
"So...! 　Mifuyu-san, to make you smile, first I have to be smiling...! 　If Mifuyu-san is ever sad, I will smile! 　So...!"
"So, Mom... together..."
"Come on, Haruka-chan's waiting for you. Mifuyu, what do you want to do?"
"Uugh...! 　I...! 　To Motoki-cho... to Motoki-cho...! 　I want to go home... I want to go home...! 　Uugh! 　I want Haruka to smile! I want Haruka to be happy!"
"Heh... you still haven't broken that habit of yours... If you ever say 'Uugh...' again... I'll make you look after everything for Natsumi in Motoki-cho, all right?"
"Akane...!?"
"Just kidding. But I'm not only saying this for Mifuyu's sake. I'm saying it for ours, too. I'm proposing the way I can be happiest, so... so don't just say no right away. Properly, give it your consideration."
"Akane... I... once more... Uugh... just one more time... be happy... is that okay...?"
"You... Are you going to have me decide even that for you? 　You can at least decide for yourself whether you'll be happy or not, can't you? 　How to make Haruka-chan happy, Natsumi already showed you, didn't she?"
"Mom, let's go home to Natsumi and the others, okay?"
"I want to eat Russian-roulette takoyaki with Obachan!" [TN: takoyaki: fried batter balls with octopus inside]
"............ Heh... What's that... Is it tasty?"
"Come on, Mifuyu. Say what you're going to do, right here, clearly, in your own words."
"Haruka... let's... be happy once more... Dad died, but... as long as we have everyone... we'll be okay, won't we?"
"Ehehe... Mom, I love you most in the whole world after all!"
"Yes, Eiichiro-san is the best husband in the world!"
"Mifuyu-chan, you're the best wife in the world too!"
"Eiichiro-san... I still don't want to give up on happiness after all...!"
"Heh... I love you too, Haruka. Mine and Dad's, the best daughter in the world!"
"I finally realized. I'd given up on my own happiness... Eiichiro-san died, and I'd convinced myself I could never be happy again. Even though there are so many people supporting me like this... I could have grabbed happiness, but I looked away from it."
"I won't give up anymore. I won't run away anymore. Whether I stay unhappy is up to me. If I can't feel happy, Haruka will stay unhappy too. In Motoki-cho, I'm going to find a new happiness with Haruka. Because that's what I can give Eiichiro-san... what I can do now. I can't let Eiichiro-san see me in such a shameful state any longer."
"Thank you, Natsumi-chan. For letting me hear Eiichiro-san's... Haruka's father's voice."
The four of them looked at one another, as if confirming to each other how precious each one's presence was.
I quietly slipped away.
For now I shouldn't intrude. I should give the four of them a true reunion.
―Outside, the hospital lay in the cold air of early April.
But there was no wind, and the sky was full of stars, calm and clear.
When I let out a big breath, the white of it was drawn up into that sky.
Coming to Megasawa had been the right choice for us.
And bringing Natsumi along, too.
Eiichiro, it's thanks to you.
Because you let us hear your voice through Natsumi―
that's why we were able to get Mifuyu-san's heart back.
I couldn't attend the third-year memorial service, but this is the best offering I could give you, right? [TN: Buddhist memorials count the year of death as the first year.]
You don't have anything to worry about anymore.
Please, rest in peace...
Just then, two small figures came out of the hospital.
"Dad!"
"Ojisan!"
"What's up? Haruka-chan, you shouldn't be walking around too much, okay?"

"What? What's the matter?"

"Hey, Akane..."

"I see..."

"............ I'm sorry..."

"Yeah..."

"Hey, Akane..."
"Hm?"
"Thank you for coming to save me."
"Sorry for being 2 whole years late."
"Heh, Akane, thank you... We really are best friends after all."
"Always, from now on too..."
"Hey, hey, Natsumi! When we go back to Motoki-cho, I want to live near Natsumi's house!"
"Oh! Our next-door neighbors moved out the other day! It's empty right now!"
The children, their dreams racing ahead to the life to come.
Are Akane and Mifuyu-san looking at this starry sky too?
There must be memories in this starry sky that someone like me could never understand.
And these two little ones will surely carve many memories into the starry sky too.
"Okay, time to head back. It'd be bad if you caught a cold."
"Okaaay!"
"Okaaay!"
Come on, let's go home.
To Motoki-cho.
