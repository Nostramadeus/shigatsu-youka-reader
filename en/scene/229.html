Detour (Avoidance)

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-07 17:45 火 · 80 boxes

"Hello, Mom? At Toyotake Station, we ran into Kana... Yeah... yeah..."
Goto was worn out before we'd even set off.
"I understand that following Kogori-senpai's intuition is best... But climbing that mountain..."
"Sorry, I guess..."
So in the end, we followed Kana-chan.
"Um... Kana-chan?"
Goto called out to her hesitantly.
"Yes! Kana Niimura here! What's up, Goto-chan!"
Kana-chan latched onto Goto's arm.
"How long do we have to walk...?"
"Um... for a normal person, about 2 hours?"
"T-two hours!?"
This wasn't a stroll. It was full-on mountain climbing.
And with all this luggage, too...
"Hup!"
With that, Kana-chan grabbed our bags and slung them over her shoulders.
"I'll take full responsibility for these bags! 　And if I lose them or anything...
I'll make it up to you with my body... Be gentle with me..."
I was struck speechless along with Goto by the 10-year-old's blatant dirty joke.
"Okay! I let Mom know! Kana, you sure you're okay carrying all that?"
"I'm fine, I'm fine! I've carried heavier stuff lots of times! I'll take Haruka-chan's too!"
She was going to climb that mountain carrying luggage for three...?
"Hah... hah..."
"Wheeze... wheeze..."
"This is rough..."
30 minutes in.
As the sky burned orange, the slope began to wear on my legs and back.
"Come on, everyone, hang in there, hang in there!"
Kana-chan was at the back, shouting encouragement.
Her cheering was so hearty you'd never think she was carrying all that luggage.
"L-let's take a break..."
Haruka sat down on a rock by the path.
"Haruka-chan, won't sitting down just make it harder?"
"Just be quiet a sec... Let me sit for a little..."
Still, neither Haruka nor Goto had complained once since we started climbing.
It's probably because it's my intuition, but I still feel bad...
"But... what a nice view!"
Goto looked out at the scenery visible between the trees.
The sun was cut in half by the horizon, and from its half circle it sent us light that was dazzling yet gentle.
And far off, I could see a mountain stream flowing.
"The finish line is around that stream!"
It looked like there were still 3 km to go.
A distance that would take over 30 minutes even on flat ground, and we had to climb it on this mountain path...
"Kana-chan, are you okay? Isn't it heavy?"
"Tooootally fine! This kind of thing is my job, since I'm the youngest in the settlement!"
Kana-chan hadn't broken a single sweat.
I could never live here...
"Come on, Haruka-chan, up you get! Let's go, let's go! It'll be bad if it gets pitch dark!"
"Daaamn... guess we're going..."
Haruka pushed herself up in one go.

Just then, something cold hit my face.
―Rain.

The rain grew stronger in an instant, and as if the clear sky of a moment ago had been a lie, everything was wrapped in darkness.
"Everyone, over here, over here!"
We hurried after Kana-chan.
The mountain path had turned into a muddy torrent, impossible to walk on properly.
video: 雨1.wmv
"Ugh, what the heck is this!"
"I haaate mountain weather!"
―Where Kana-chan led us was a rock overhang.
It was about 3 meters deep, and a little spray from the rain blew in, but it kept the rain off better than an umbrella.
The ground inside was a bit raised, too, so there was no worry of the muddy water flowing in.
"Rain like this'll stop soon!"
Kana-chan showed no sign of being tired as she gazed up at the murky black sky.
"Damn... I'm soaking wet..."
"Can't we make a fire? We'll catch colds like this..."
"I don't have anything for that... Oh, Goto! You've got that fireworks set, right!? Got matches or a lighter!?"
"Niimura-senpai's so smart!"
"It doesn't make me happy when you praise my brains... Anything we can burn?"
"I've got a guidebook to Iizawa Prefecture. That alone won't last very long, though..."
"Hmm, something that burns a long time... Oh! Come to think of it!"
Haruka tore the wrapping paper off the sake we'd brought as a gift.
"I knew it! This box is made of wood! We can burn this!"
"Niimura-senpai, that's paulownia. 　Paulownia gets used for chests of drawers, so it's a wood that doesn't burn easily..."
Other than that, the only things we had that would burn were our changes of clothes.
Burning those would really be... wait― if we have a change of clothes...
"Um... couldn't we just change our clothes?"
"Natsumi's so smart!"
"Senpai's so smart!"
That's... a compliment, right...?
―Just as Kana-chan predicted, the rain stopped after about 10 minutes.
We changed and waited for the torrent on the path to die down.
"Kana, are you okay not changing? Even you can't stay wet like that, right?"
"Ahaha! I'm all right, a... a... ah...
Achoo!"
"............ My clothes... want to wear them?"
Goto held out dry clothes to Kana-chan.
"What!? I can wear Goto-chan's clothes!?
　Yaaay!"
Kana-chan seemed less happy to be out of her wet clothes than to be wearing Goto's.
"Ahaha! I guess it's a little big? 　But I'm so happy! Thanks, Goto-chan!"
"Y-yeah..."
Kana-chan hugged Goto's arm, delighted.
Goto looked put out... or not really, actually. She didn't seem to mind it one bit.
And just then―
"Mm... I knew you'd be here."
"Oh, Sis!"
―We went on along the mountain path, led by Chigaya Niimura-san, otherwise known as Chiga-nee.
As if the downpour had been a lie, the sky cleared completely, and beautiful moonlight lit up the mountain.
Chiga-nee and Kana-chan each carried half of our luggage, which our wet clothes had made a little heavier.
"Hey, hey, Sis! She's my new big sister!"
Kana-chan grabbed Goto's arm and pulled her out in front of Chiga-nee.
"Huh? Huh? Big sister!?"
"Mm... my, my. Then Goto-chan is my little sister as well. Goto-chan, let's sleep together tonight."
"Um... I..."
"No! Eri-nee is mine! 　Look! Eri-nee lent me these clothes!"
"'Eri-nee'..."
"Mm... Goto-chan, shall I give you a piggyback? 　Someone as little as Goto-chan is no trouble at all."
"N-no, I'm fine, thank you..."
"See! Eri-nee likes me better! 　Right, Eri-nee?"
"Mm... Goto-chan was just holding back for my sake. Right, Goto-chan?"
Goto kept glancing our way, asking for help.
"Ah, Chiga-nee! Sorry! Making you come all the way out to get us..."
"Mm... heh. If Kana dragged you along, I figured it'd be something like this."
"Well, no... if anything, it was more like Natsumi's advice, or something..."
"Mm... Natsumi-chan's...?"
"Natsumi can hear her dad's voice. Only in this season, when the cherry blossoms bloom."
"Huh?"
"Huh?"
The smiles vanished from Chiga-nee's and Kana-chan's faces.
"Sis, isn't that..."
"Mm... the same as Mom..."
Did that mean their mother had intuition, too?
"Hey, hey, is she at the Niimura main house, too!?"
"Mm... Natsumi-chan, I'm sorry. Mom passed away 10 years ago."
Chiga-nee answered with a smile, not looking sad at all.
"Oh, I didn't know... Sorry..."
"Mm... Natsumi-chan, does your intuition work when you go into dark, narrow places, too?"
"Dark and narrow...? 　Mine works when a strong wind hits me."
"Mm... that's different from Mom, then. My mom's intuition seems to have worked when she went into dark, narrow places."
That was certainly completely different from mine.
It seemed each person had their own conditions.
"Chiga-nee, is there something about dark, narrow places?"
"Mm... I don't know that much... Dad might know."
But if the person herself was dead, there was no telling whether what Chiga-nee's dad knew was reliable.
"Mm... oh?"
Chiga-nee stopped walking.
Maybe there had been a landslide from the heavy rain earlier.
Blocking the mountain path, rocks large and small stood in front of us.
"Hey now, we can't get through like this..."
"This is a problem... Even going back down to the foot of the mountain would be hard on a path this bad..."
"Mm... there's no way the three of you can get through here. Kana, go back to the house and tell everyone."
"Yup yup, roooger!"
Kana-chan climbed the pile of rubble with ease, still carrying the luggage.
"Huh? Huh? What was that...! Kana-chan, that's amazing...!"
"Mm... when you live in Arata, this is nothing."
"I told you she was a monkey, didn't I?"
"But climbing that with the luggage takes more than muscle. You'd have to be really good at using your whole body, wouldn't you?"
"Mm... Kana can hunt a bear all by herself. She's no ordinary elementary schooler."
"Eek...!"
Either way, we had to wait here for a while.
Walking was hard in its own way, but sitting still here looked just as hard.
I lightly brushed off the top of a knee-high rock and sat down.
Its rough, lumpy feel was really unpleasant.
"Huh? There are flowers blooming."
Goto's eyes were on some pale pink flowers.
The plant was shorter than Goto, with its branches spread out like a hydrangea's.
"Mm... this is daphne. The daphne here blooms late."
"Daphne blooms from February to March, after all. If I remember right, in the language of flowers it means―"
"Mm... immortality, imperishability, eternity."
"Ooh, Chiga-nee, you know a lot!"
"Mm... Mom loved this flower."
Sakura-san's favorite flower, then, had been daphne, not cherry blossoms.
And, contrary to what daphne means in the language of flowers, Sakura-san had died young.
How ironic.
