The Shinigami's Rule

Wabigoto Arc [Apology] · Shiyo 790-04-07 22:10 水 · 62 boxes

A single fly had been buzzing around for a while.
We laid Natsumi down next to Haruka-chan.
By tomorrow morning, these sisters would have their own moving reunion too.
"You came, so that means you're prepared for this, right?"
Gradually my eyes adjusted to the dark, and I could make out Mifuyu-san's face.
For Eiichiro's sake she had always dressed beautifully and never neglected her looks, but there was no trace of that Mifuyu-san now.
Her face was gaunt, and her clothes were so worn I couldn't tell how many days in a row she'd had them on.
The clothes hanging on the wall, which were Haruka-chan's, were neat and well kept.
Her striking long black hair was the same as before, and she didn't look any thinner.
Mifuyu-san was probably spending as much of their living money as she could on Haruka-chan.
They seemed to be living even more poorly than I'd imagined.
Just because of that accident, Mifuyu-san was going through hardship she never should have had to.
Poverty with no end in sight, and bullying at school.
I understood all too painfully how she wanted to save Haruka-chan no matter what.
No, now that I'd seen this situation, I wanted to save Haruka-chan too! Absolutely...!
"I've memorized the faces and addresses of the two doing the bullying. Tell me the time, the place and the method."
There was no hesitation in the words Akane suddenly spoke.
"Hey, Akane...!"
"Ryoji-kun, there's nothing wrong with just listening, is there?"
Akane didn't turn her face to me, but kept her eyes on Mifuyu-san the whole time.
I let out a heavy sigh and sat down without a word.
We were only going to listen, nothing more.
If I could find even the slightest way to get through this without killing, no, I meant to push this toward not killing at all, no matter what.
"Thank you, Akane. Tomorrow, use these."
Mifuyu-san brought over a big cardboard box she'd kept in the corner of the room.
It looked pretty heavy.
"First, these. Hide your builds and your faces with the red robes and the Noh masks."
These red robes were awfully heavy.
There was something metal inside them.
They were small knives, with blades about 10 cm long.
Lots of them were sewn in.
"But Mifuyu, you can't go as far as killing with knives like these, can you? 　A real weapon, or a way not to get caught―"
"Those are spares. What you'll really use is this."
What Mifuyu-san took out of another box was a large blade and a dully gleaming blunt weapon.
"A nata and... what's this?"
"A mace. Do it with these. Use the spare knives as needed."
"Hold on. With gear like this, even a man would be too weighed down to move freely. Even a kid could get away."
I played along for the moment, but of course I had no intention of using any of it.
"When the time comes, this."
What Mifuyu-san took out were two syringes.
"With this, you'll be in a state of excitement for about 10 minutes, and your physical ability goes up dramatically. A mace will be as good as cotton candy."
"What is this, some kind of doping drug?"
"A drug that takes the limiter off your muscles and lets you call up the strength you only find in an emergency at will, apparently."
I'd heard of drugs that improve athletic ability, but a drug strong enough to make tomorrow's crime possible?
Suspicious. Hadn't someone sold her something shady?
"Mifuyu-san, how did you get your hands on all this? 　I hate to say it, but living like this, there's no way you could put together this much. And the drug too..."
The nata was polished clean, and the red robes weren't cheap either.
This must have cost a lot of money.
"The Arata settlement, where Eiichiro-san's family home is, sent it to me."
So it was the Arata settlement after all...
Only about 30 people lived there, and its character was extremely insular.
Because of that, the settlement's bonds and solidarity were strong.
Eiichiro used to say it often. One for all, all for one.
That was the Arata settlement's slogan.
"The Arata settlement is a village built 800 years ago by people fleeing the wars of the world. Over hundreds of years, people with nowhere else to go, like drifters and Westerners, gathered there. It's an insular settlement, but once you're accepted as one of them, they'll protect their own by any means necessary. That's the people of the Arata settlement."
"Long ago, warlords and bandits appeared who plotted to invade Arata, with its rich nature and plentiful food. But the residents fought to protect the only place they had, and they armed themselves as well. That's why, even now, there's a lasting tradition that the residents stay ready to fight at any time."
"You remember I went to Arata for a year, right? Back then, I was given that kind of training. Hunting animals without guns or any firearms, being left alone in the mountains for ages... Once, I was even taught how to fight with this nata and mace.
Like this."
When Mifuyu-san swung the nata, there was a faint snip in the air.
What dropped onto the tatami was a fly, cut in half lengthwise.
She'd swung a heavy nata in this dark room and cut a fly in midair?
"Being able to do this doesn't make me invincible. If I got into a fistfight with Ryoji-san, there's no way I'd win. This is within the range of what a human can do with training."
"This drug is what breaks through that limit. For 10 minutes, it lets a person use 100% of their muscle strength. I've never used it, but its effect has been proven over hundreds of years. Apparently in the old days they used it by burning the drug's crystals and breathing the smoke."
"They say that as they resisted the armies that attacked the Arata settlement, this drug was eventually invented, and they could fight more efficiently."
"But there were times when they had no choice but to leave the settlement to get supplies. So when they fought, they wore masks, so that in peacetime they could go outside the settlement with their faces bare without arousing suspicion. At some point, the masks settled into Noh masks, I hear."
"Through the Westerners who drifted into the Arata settlement, the 'witch' and the 'shinigami' came to be known. What the Arata settlement calls a witch is what we'd now call a priest. The shinigami are, literally, those who have died. [TN: shinigami is written with the characters for death and god.] When someone in the settlement dies, a witch presides over the memorial service, and the mourners wear Noh masks that hide their expressions and faces, so the shinigami won't mark them."
"Around that same time―a certain Westerner, who was also a doctor, took an interest in a certain plant in the Arata settlement. What was extracted from that plant is this drug. I don't know exactly how it works, but thanks to it, the Arata settlement has kept its autonomy right down to the present day."
"The shinigami, the god that brings death. The nata and the mace are what the sword is to a samurai. The drug that aids the fighting. And the knives sewn inside are both weapon and armor. This is the shinigami's―the Arata settlement's formal battle dress."
"That's why things like this come from the Arata settlement. One for all, all for one. If a comrade outside the settlement is in trouble, they lend a hand like this."
It was a hard story to follow, but it should be enough to grasp that the Arata settlement made a tradition of fighting for its land and its people.
"But, Mifuyu-san, wait a second. If the settlement would go that far for you, why don't you move there?"
"The rules don't allow it. When an outsider like me doesn't live in the settlement right after the marriage, she isn't allowed to put down roots there until the child turns 16. And arming yourself outside the settlement is forbidden too. Arms are strictly for protecting the settlement, not for invading. Helping outsiders is forbidden too, so I can't get any help with living costs either."
"One for all, all for one. You might think what I've said contradicts that slogan... But the people of Arata are all timid. So even with a slogan like that, it takes a good amount of time before an outsider like me is trusted. Even in this day and age..."
In other words, the Arata settlement wouldn't lend a hand directly, and Haruka-chan couldn't live there until she turned 16.
In that case, it was a contradiction for things like this to reach Mifuyu-san.
Whatever the form, they were helping her, weren't they?
Did that mean someone inside the settlement was a secret accomplice?
"Right now it's all I can do to somehow keep Haruka from going hungry. Haruka is all I live for..."
Saying that, she ran her hand over Haruka-chan's head, stroking it.
"My parents have passed away, and I have no brothers or sisters. There's no one left I can rely on... The truth is, I could do it myself tomorrow, but if I got arrested, this girl would have nowhere to go... But if you two lend me a hand, it's easier to fool the people around us."
What would I do if I were in Mifuyu-san's position?
To protect Haruka-chan, surely even I might...
No, I mustn't think like that! There are things you can do precisely because you aren't the one in it.
"Then let me meet Eiichiro's mother, the head of Arata! I'll talk to her directly!"
"That's impossible. Eiichiro-san's mother―Grand Witch-sama is against this kind of violence. Besides, even as the head of the settlement, she isn't allowed to break Arata's rules... So I haven't told my mother-in-law about this. I asked 'someone I can trust' in the settlement."
"Someone you can trust?"
"Yes. Someone I can trust..."
It seemed Mifuyu-san had no intention of saying who this 'someone I can trust' was.
To sum up, the settlement couldn't openly help Mifuyu-san.
But someone was secretly in league with Mifuyu-san and supporting her.
A money trail might be traced, so it came as supplies like these...
"I don't mind being punished for breaking the rules. But then 'someone I can trust' would be punished too. So this can't be made public in the settlement."
"Then why not come back to Motoki-cho? 　I don't earn much, but I think I could manage to support two more."
"Mifuyu, yes, let's do that! That's the way!"
"............ I can't do that... I... being near Natsumi-chan is torture for me. Even if I put up with it for Haruka's sake... I might do something to Natsumi-chan on impulse."
"Hold on. I'm not trying to dodge responsibility, but that was an accident!"


"Witch..."
"Huh?"
"Natsumi-chan is a witch!　Not in the Arata settlement's sense. A real witch. Eiichiro-san was cursed to death by this child!"
I flared up and nearly got to my feet.
As if to cut me off, Akane spoke up at once.
"Mifuyu, tell me more. Why a curse..."
"Akane, didn't you see? 　When Eiichiro-san fell, an invisible force twisted his body and he was impaled on the fence. If he'd fallen normally, at least he wouldn't have hit the fence."
"But then Natsumi would have been crushed under Eiichiro, so he tried to avoid that!"
"And you heard that sound, didn't you!? 　A sound that loud can't come out of nowhere! That was the witch's power! 　The proof is that since that day Natsumi-chan's had some strange power, right? 　Isn't that the witch's power?"
"No...! That was Eiichiro―"
"There's no point talking any more. Either way, you have no choice but to do as I say. I told you things that are strictly secret―the hidden side of the Arata settlement... You understand, don't you? 　If you betray me after coming this far, it won't just be you two. Natsumi-chan won't get off lightly either."
"If I wanted to, I could set 'someone I can trust' or the settlement on you and make you disappear. The people of the Arata settlement are all timid, after all. You know there was no postmark on the letter I sent, right? I had 'someone I can trust' put that letter in your mailbox. The people of the settlement know where you live."
"Ngh...!"
"One more thing. Long ago, a faction that split off from the Arata settlement founded a cult in Megasawa, I hear. That cult still exists. Haruka's classmates include a believer too. So here in Megasawa, there are plenty of people connected to the Arata settlement too. Don't even think about running."
Damn! Was this what she was after!?
No matter how we struggled, unless we did as Mifuyu-san said, not just us but even Natsumi...
Things had gotten even worse. It was as good as having my family taken hostage...!
I see. Now I understood what Akane meant when she said Mifuyu-san wasn't stupid.
When Mifuyu-san, once so simple and honest, did everything she could for her daughter, she sank this low, into a cunning criminal.
She had always been a step ahead of us. From the moment we came here, we'd had no choice but to do as Mifuyu-san said.
"Now, let's get some sleep. Early tomorrow morning, I'm going with Haruka to the Arata settlement. Eiichiro-san's third-year memorial service..." [TN: Buddhist memorials count the year of death as the first year.]
With that, she turned her back and lay down.
"Then what if you left Haruka-chan with us for a while...!"
"You mean to take Haruka from me...? 　If you take Haruka from me, my reason will snap, and Natsumi-chan might end up like that fly. Haruka is my family. I'll never hand her over to anyone."
"............"
Akane was kneeling formally, head bowed.
Mifuyu-san's wretched life, and the Mifuyu-san who had changed so much, had come as a real shock to her.
She was no longer the Mifuyu-san she used to be.
Natsumi and Haruka-chan slept soundly, not knowing they were right next to each other.
So that these two could always rest peacefully like this, I still wanted to stop it.
There's still time...!
