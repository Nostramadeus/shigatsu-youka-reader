Mom and Kotaro-san were both back at the house.
Kana was right. Today's search had apparently been called off.
By rights Chiga-nee's succession rite would be happening too, but with her gone there's nothing to be done.
"―So that's how it is. I'd like you to take me to Kana's secret base."
It was a bit late to start now, but I dodged Grandma's and Kotaro-san's eyes and talked it over with Mom.
It'd be especially bad for Natsumi to hear.
"All right. We'd better hurry."
"Sorry. I know you must be tired."
"Mother, I'm sorry, but I'm going out with Haruka for a little while."
"Mifuyu-san... Haruka... You be careful out there."
"Yes..."
"I know."
It seemed Grandma had a pretty good idea of what was going on.
Of course, like this, it's totally obvious we're going out to look for Chiga-nee.
"Mom, any results from the search earlier... Well, probably not, but did you find out anything new?"
"How should I put it? Apart from the Niimura family, nobody really seemed to be searching in earnest."
"Is that because they figure Chiga-nee has already been curse-killed?"
"That's probably part of it, but if it's true, it means there won't be another curse-killing this year, right? 　Somewhere in their hearts, they might be hoping Chigaya-chan has been curse-killed."
So much for 'one for all, all for one.'
Open the lid and it's every man for himself, huh...
I guess that's normal, but it's a little disappointing.
But in that case, there might be more room to redo the search than I'd thought.
"It's dark, so be careful."
First Mom climbed down, and I waited for her signal.
So this was the underground space.
Goto was right: no wind, no sunlight, and it didn't look like any other creatures could get in.
A channel had been dug along the edge, and water welling up from somewhere ran through it.
"But Haruka, Kana-chan and I already searched here a little while ago."
"Mom, what you two were looking for was Chiga-nee, right? 　Goto is talking about Tomi's mummy. If there's a secret space where you could hide a mummy, Chiga-nee might be there too."
"Don't go too far in, okay? It's dangerous."
"Dangerous?"
"Apparently there was a cave-in farther in, decades ago."
Which means nobody's searched around there, right?
"Mom, I want to check around where that cave-in happened."
"Haruka... were you even listening?"
"That's exactly why. You and Kana won't even go near it, so it's the perfect place to hide something, right?"
"But..."
"Besides, the cave-in happened decades ago."
"Don't spend too long looking, okay?"
The lamp oil had only been set out around the ladder, and it gradually got darker.
I went on by the light of my phone, but water started getting into my shoes, and it felt gross.
The icy-cold water made me walk faster without thinking.

"Amazing. Could this be the water source for that fungus you mentioned earlier? 　An organism that big must need plenty of water."
"I see. So if we mixed poison or something into this, could we take down Shigatsu Youka?"
"Maybe, but then it would poison people too."
"Right..."
"What's this..."
"The cave-in happened past this point, they say. As you can see, it's closed off now."
An old iron grate, but firmly locked.
A lock...?
Come to think of it...
I'd been carrying the bunch of keys, storehouse key and all.
Goto should have gotten them, and I'd completely forgotten to hand them over.
I picked a key at random, jammed it in, then switched to the next one.
"Hold on, Haruka, it's really dangerous past here."
"I'm not turning back after coming this far! What if Chiga-nee is past here!?"
"Honestly... Haruka, I'd have liked you to have a little of Natsumi-chan's ladylike grace."
"Haha... The girl who's always putting you through hell with Russian-roulette takoyaki, Natsumi―ladylike? 　That reckless girl with nerves of steel? 　When it comes to something like this, she's more trouble than I am, you know?"
A satisfying click came from the keyhole.
"Haruka, don't go touching everything, okay?"
"I know. Hm...?"
Just now, in the light of my phone, I'd caught a glimpse of something brown.
It definitely wasn't rock. Something long and thin was clinging to the rock face.
"What's this..."
"Looks like a tree root. If its roots reach all the way down here... could it be the Niimura house's cherry tree?"
Of course. The Niimura house is probably right about directly above here.
Without thinking much of it, I touched the root.
............!?
A squelchy feel.
Definitely not like a tree root.
It was slimy, like touching sludge.
It was so gross that I washed my hand right away in the water at my feet.
"So the roots really are rotting..."
"Come to think of it, Natsumi said this cherry tree was dying. So the roots rotted too, and ended up like this?"
That would explain it, but something feels off.
Chiga-nee's memo said something about this, I think.
Well, I've left figuring that out to Goto, so we should keep going.
"Huh? It ends here?"
"So it seems..."
Just past the tree root, a wider space greeted us.
But there was no space beyond it that looked like it led anywhere.
"There's oil on the walls. I'll light it."
As they were lit one after another, it got bright enough to clearly make out Mom's face.
I put my phone away and looked around at the ceiling.
Nothing seemed especially off, but...
Still, in the corners of the room were pots and some fairly big nata.
And they didn't look all that old.
"Haruka, this room is strange. The oil is too new. Someone's been in here recently."
"The stuff in here is all pretty new too..."
Clearly, something was fishy.
You couldn't tell at a glance, but a closer look might turn something up.
Me and Mom started doing the same thing without exchanging a word.
Mom seemed to have a hunch there was something in this room too.
Pots and what was in them, the nata, even the faint shadows among the rocks: we went over all of it, every inch.
"Hm?"
A big stone about 70 centimeters across, more than an armful, sat in the corner.
Maybe it could be moved?
I gave it a light touch, and―
It shifted with a heavy rumble.
It moved a little, but there was no way I could shift it on my own.
"Mom, give me a hand!"
"This stone... Haruka, on 'heave,' we push it to the right."
"Okay."
Together, we put our hands on the left side of the stone.
"Heave!"
"Heave!"
On the call, we pushed the stone with all our might.
The stone rumbled the same way as before.
It moved a little, but it didn't feel anywhere near ready to roll over.
"This one's pretty stubborn..."
"I see..."
With that, Mom took up one of the nata lying in the corner of the room.
A nata noticeably bigger than the rest.
"Haruka, stand back."
Mom wedged the nata into a gap under the stone and tipped it hard like a lever, and the stone rolled over with a thud.
Right―leverage, that's what this is.
"What is this..."
What appeared there was a hole just a little smaller than the stone.
It was about 60 centimeters across; a child or a woman could crawl in easily.
"Haruka, I'll go in. When I give the signal, pull me out by the legs."
Mom started into the hole headfirst without even waiting for my answer.
Mom's upper body slid smoothly into the hole, until she was in up to about her knees.
Hopefully she doesn't get dragged in by someone in that hole...
"Mom, you okay?"
"I'm fine! Just a little longer!"
This time, a white artificial light leaked out from deep in the hole.
Mom seemed to have turned on a light.
"This is...! 　Haruka, okay, pull me out!"
Mom's signal came, and I grabbed her ankles and slowly pulled.
Pulling her out without leaving her with scrapes was no easy job.
"Phew..."
Once her hips were out, Mom came out of the hole on her own.
"Was there anything?"
"There was. Trust Goto-chan."
Then Mom thrust one arm into the hole and pulled out what looked like red cloth.
"Whoa..."
Out came something in old, rotted red robes, wearing an old Noh mask to match.
It seemed to be in human shape, but...
A dead body? No way...
Even if it was a body, Mom seemed to have pulled it out with no effort at all.
Which means―
"Is this... Tomi's mummy?"
"Let's see for ourselves, right now."
Mom slowly put her hand to the Noh mask.
So we're about to come face to face with a mummy...
No time now to whine that mummies are gross and I don't want to look.
"Ugh..."
What appeared from under the Noh mask was a dried-up human being that looked like a prop―a mummy.
Goto, you really are something...
I don't know if this is Tomi or not, but I do know now that there's still a lot we don't know.

No. In the end, Chiga-nee wasn't here either.
Dad and Sakura-san: are they still at Chiga-nee's side even now?

The moon tonight was big, like a full moon.
You could see a fair way even without a light, but that wasn't the only reason it looked bright.
The trees in the forest had withered...
Why? Just a while ago every one of them had been a fine, healthy tree, and now so many were dropping the leaves they'd only just put out.
Goto's words came back to me.
"The other trait: it grows by drawing nutrients from the plants around it, killing them as it goes. This kind of nature is called 'plant-parasitic fungi.'
