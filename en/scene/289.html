The Great Sawa Earthquake, which struck 85 years ago, in Shiyo 815.
Right after it, the giant cherry forest that suddenly appeared in the former Motoki City.
Since then, the former Motoki City has been covered in deadly gas. It became a ghost town overnight.

Attempts were also made to take aerial photographs from airplanes and satellites, but the fog is thick and the images are extremely unclear.
Radio noise is also strong, and neither X-ray imaging nor electromagnetic imaging is possible.
Beyond even the deep sea and unclimbed peaks, it has become a realm more unknown to us humans than Mars or Venus.
For more than 80 years, a 50 km radius around the former Motoki City has remained a no-entry zone, and Minakami City, the former prefectural capital, now retains only its function of observing the former Motoki City, with no more than a small number of researchers stationed there.
