Harmony

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-07 21:25 火 · 33 boxes

"Hah... hah..."
"Eri-nee, are you okay!?"
"Y-yeah..."
Somehow, we made it to the front of the Niimura house.
We should be fine now that we've come this far―the scary part is that I can't say that.
"Kana-chan, where did the frog go?"
"Looks like it woke up on the way, and it got away..."
"So it wasn't dead."
"If you're just hunting, you don't need to kill it. But..."
Kana-chan looked back down the path we had come.
"There wasn't any sign of people at all. And it didn't feel like anybody was after us. Natsumi-chan really does have precognition, right?"
"Yeah, that's for sure."
"That's weird..."
Now that she mentioned it, I did feel there should have been at least a little sign of danger.
"Eri-nee! Someone's coming!"
Swiftly, Kana-chan stepped in front of me and checked our surroundings.
She wasn't only looking around. I could hear her breathing in little sniffs.
She seemed to be searching the area with her sense of smell as well.
"............
Oh, it's only Grandma..."
"Oh, well, well, if it isn't the genius hunter girl...
Wait, doesn't look like the mood for that."
Kana-chan's eyes seemed to have told her this wasn't a friendly mood.
If so, I wish she'd noticed back at dinner...
No, wait a second!
Kogori-senpai's premonition of danger can't be about running into Obaasan, can it!?
...Yeah, probably not.
If it were, the cave would actually have been the safer place.
For now, does that mean we avoided the danger?
"W-well, it's nothing that serious! We were hunting with plenty of breaks!"
"Yeah, yeah! Eri-nee was super good even though it was her first time! She really is a genius girl!"
"Oho, I'm looking forward to that."
"Grandma, did you catch anything?"
"This is what I got."
Obaasan held up a fish like a cutlassfish, one that had to be a good 80 centimeters long.
"Wow! That's a fine shinigami fish!"
"Ha ha ha! Even at my age, something like this is easy!
　How'd it go on your end?"
"Ah... um..."
"So, Eri-nee caught a giant bullfrog, but I messed up and let it get away..."
"Oh! Erina-chan, that's amazing for your first time!"
"Uh... um..."
Credited with catching the frog, and on top of that called by my given name, I got visibly flustered.
"Now Erina-chan's a real hunter too! 　We're comrades-in-arms!"
"Comrades-in-arms, comrades-in-arms!"
Ah, Kana-chan did it to get me and Obaasan on good terms...
Then I can't let that go to waste.
"A-ahahahaha! Genius girl Erina Goto! Once I get serious, even hunting is this easy!"
When I grinned at her, Obaasan copied me and grinned right back.
Then we shook hands firmly.
"Welcome to Arata. Erina-chan, you're a fine warrior too!"
"Ehehe! Let's get along, Erika-chan!"
It came about in a strange way, but I feel the bond between me and Obaasan― no, between me and Erika-chan, has grown deeper.
For Erika-chan, whether I really caught the frog probably doesn't matter at all.
More than that, gaining a comrade-in-arms more than 50 years older than me is the greatest trophy of all.
And with that, the hunting contest is over! The result: a draw!
The Operation is a huge success!
I could hear dishes being washed in the kitchen, but there was no one in the living room.
"Well, I've got a little something to take care of."
"Erika-chan, where are you going?"
"Just off with Chigaya for a bit. There's a ritual we do the day before the memorial service, which is tomorrow."
"A ritual..."
"Actually, I'd planned on having all three of the Motoki-cho group watch it too... Erina-chan, how about you, at least?"
"Hmm, I'm really interested, but... I'd like to wait for Kogori-senpai. She's probably bringing some important news. She's gone outside the settlement right now, and it seems that danger sense of hers kicked in."
"I see. Then it's no time to be watching a ritual. I'll get the ritual done fast and head back myself."
Obaasan left the living room, and from the kitchen came the squeak of a faucet being turned off.
"Huh? So you got back. Phew, I'm taking a break too!"
Niimura-senpai dropped down onto the tatami with a thud.
I followed her lead and sat down.
"So, how'd the so-called contest go?"
"Ahh, a lot happened, and it ended in a draw!"
"A draw, huh? Well, that's the most peaceful outcome! 　Oh yeah, Natsumi called me. Didn't she call you too, Goto? I told her to call you, Goto."
"She did. She told me to get out of there right away, but nothing happened."
"Huh? Natsumi's intuition misfiring? That's rare..."
"Right? So I'm planning to ask her all about it when she gets back."
Just then, Kana-chan tugged at the hem of my skirt.
"Eri-nee... I'm sleepy..."
It's already 10 o'clock at night. That's probably a little late for an elementary schooler.
―Then, the sound of the front door opening.
Was Kogori-senpai back, or were Erika-chan and the others heading off to that so-called ritual?
Either way, Kogori-senpai would be coming into this living room before long.
