Couldn't this, perhaps, be turned around and used as a way of keeping Shigatsu Youka deceived?

"I can't beat Shigatsu Youka. So everyone should surrender to Shigatsu Youka," and so on.
I'll mumble it as if mentally worn down.
At the very least, through my hearing, I can let Shigatsu Youka hear my voice.
And Shigatsu Youka, for its part, must want a living human spy.
Shigatsu Youka's goal is probably to cover this settlement in fruiting bodies once again.
That way, an ecosystem forms around the fruiting bodies, and mammals that can be made into embryos are drawn in too.
It's not only humans that can be made into embryos, but other wild creatures as well.

"Let's all become cherry trees!"

If I can, I must keep my own eyes and ears from taking in that evacuation or the decision behind it.
If Shigatsu Youka learned that everyone was evacuating, who knows what it would do...
The problem is whether something like this can keep Shigatsu Youka deceived to the end.
So, to raise the odds of success even a little, I'll start the game of bluff right now, from this moment.
"I can't... I just can't... there's no getting away..."
