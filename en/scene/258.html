Woman to Woman

Eden no Sakura Arc [Eden's Cherry] · Shiyo 800-03-29 11:00 火 · 76 boxes

―My head hurt...
The buzzing of the alarm was so loud.
Why is my phone alarm even going off...?
College is still on spring break, isn't it?
Then the alarm on my phone stopped by itself.
Haah, a little more sleep...
I was a little hung over...
Just as I tried to go back to sleep, I realized the buzzing hadn't been the alarm but an incoming call.
I sluggishly reached for my phone.
From Aunt Mifuyu?
What could she possibly want?
"Why, Chigaya-chan! 　Morning, morning!"
Auntie's shrill voice, far more cheerful than it needed to be, hit my inner ear head-on.
That was pretty rough on a hangover... I held the phone a little away from my ear.
"Mm... Auntie, what is it?"
"I'm in Tokyo on business today! I thought we might have lunch together!"
I looked at the clock: 11 a.m.
My memory was hazy, but I'd probably gotten five hours of sleep.
I wanted to sleep a little longer, but I didn't feel right turning down an invitation from Auntie when she'd come all the way to Tokyo.
"Mm... Where should we meet?"
"Chigaya-chan, there's a cafe called 'Primavera' near your place. Do you know it?"
"Mm... I think there is one. That place, maybe...?"
"Then I'll be there a little after 12!"
"Mm... All right..."
Auntie's voice the moment I woke up, and a meal with Auntie on top of that: my stomach already felt heavy.
I let a shower pour over my head, as hot as I could just barely stand.
I'd have liked a long soak in the tub, but there was no time to fill it.
And I looked back on last night.
Last night...
we'd gathered at 7 p.m. at a party bar for students.
Through a friend's introduction, I'd been let in on a drinking party with a tennis club of 30 or so.
The ratio of men to women was a perfect one to one, and every guy there was quite the clean-cut type.
My losing streak has hit triple digits.
All right, all right... today I'm going to catch at least one gentleman I can stay in touch with!
Putting on my good-girl act, I made my approach, making full use of the looks I'd inherited from Mom.
But gradually the guys got into the college mood and grew rowdy with chugging and drinking chants.
When I see that kind of thing, my Arata settlement blood stirs.
As if I'd lose to these city-bred beansprouts!
I'm a 100% natural mountain girl!
Even the guys who'd been fawning over me gradually began to stiffen their cheek muscles at my wild streak.
That is irresistibly irresistible.
I don't know how to put it, but it's irresistibly irresistible.
After that I leave the rest to the alcohol, drag the men around until morning, and drink the night away.
And when I come to, sometimes I'm asleep in my own bed like today, and sometimes I'm asleep in a roadside gutter.
Last summer I even found myself floating in the middle of the Sumida River.
Anyone other than me would definitely have died.
The one thing I never get used to is the moment this morning arrives.
When morning comes, I'm hit by fierce self-loathing and regret.
If I kept up my good-girl act the way I started, one or two of the men would probably see me as a woman.
But partway through I start having fun, and fooling around with everyone suits me better than sneaking out of the party with a guy.
That's what I think at the time, but come morning it's always like this.
Because of all this, not one man so far has even asked me out a second time, let alone pushed me down.
Truly, not a single one.
The most physical contact I've had with the opposite sex was probably putting a certain man in a figure-four leglock during the King Game.
Just to be clear, my face shouldn't be all that bad.
I take care of my figure, too.
At this point, I'm almost tempted to suspect the men just have no eye for women.
Well, I'm getting to see Auntie, after all.
She can at least listen to me grumble.
I threw on whatever clothes, dried my hair any old way, and ran a comb through it any old way.
Then, after brushing my teeth and nothing else, I left home.
As for makeup, I probably haven't put any on since Coming-of-Age Day.
The place Auntie had picked was a cafe I walked past almost every day but had never once gone into.
It looked very expensive.
I go to red-lantern bars a lot more than places like this.
If I'm going to polish myself as a woman, maybe I ought to know places like this too...
Just in case, I checked what was in my wallet once more.
Auntie would probably treat me, but I didn't want to be the kind of woman who always expected that.
Yes, this was where polishing myself as a woman began.
Just then, I felt a hand land on my shoulder with a pat.
"Long time no see, Chigaya-chan!"
"Mm... Auntie, you seem as full of energy as ever today."
It had been about three and a half months since I'd last seen Auntie.
Not since we met in Arata this past New Year's.
"Oh my!? Chigaya-chan, did you stay up late, by any chance? You look sleepy, and you've got dark circles under your eyes!"
"Huh? Um..."
"Being young is no excuse to be so mean to your skin and your insides! 　They say aging starts in your teens!"
Hearing Auntie's shrill voice in person was rough on a hangover, as I'd thought...
Inside, fine ladies with an unmistakably wealthy air were enjoying their lunch.
This place must be expensive after all...
"Come on, come on, Chigaya-chan! Order whatever you like! Everything at this place is delicious!"
"Mm... Auntie, have you been here before?"
"Their main shop is in Motoki-cho! Back in the day, I used to go there with Akane all the time!"
"Mm... Akane...?"
"Oh, Chigaya-chan, you wouldn't know her! She's been my best friend since the day I was born! 　She still lives in the apartment next door!"
Come to think of it, I'd heard from Haruka that her best friend since birth lived next door.
Could it be that two generations, parent and child, have built that kind of friendship?
There's nobody around my age in the Arata settlement, so I'm a little jealous...
"Welcome, Niimura-sama. We hear you are a valued patron of our Motoki-cho main branch. Please, take your time and relax."
A young, pretty waitress greeted us respectfully.
I looked over the menu the waitress handed me.
"Ugh..."
Even with Auntie saying she'll treat me, there's one more zero than I imagined.
Canned coffee sells for 100 yen or so, and even the cheapest thing here is more than 10 times that.
The lunch menu costs about as much as the first round of a drinking party.
Whoa... this one with the cakes and sandwiches stacked into a tower costs 5,000 yen.
Whoa, whoa...!! This even more amazing tower one is 9,000 yen...!
Something cheaper is fine!
This... the 3,000-yen beef curry set will do.
Though 3,000 yen for curry is pretty steep, too...
"Chigaya-chan, you don't have to hold back! 　Relatives only treat you while you're a student!?"
I know there isn't the slightest ulterior motive in Auntie's urging.
But I was thrown by a world where that much money changed hands for bread and curry, not even a kaiseki course.
A cold sweat trickled down me on its own.
"Then, Chigaya-chan, let's get this! One of these is enough for two!"
Auntie pointed without hesitation at the giant tower one.
"O-okay..."
Even if it was for two, that was 4,500 yen a head.
Apparently you could order any drink you liked, with refills.
But for 4,500 yen, I could get all-you-can-drink at the ryotei-style izakaya I went to last month.
As long as I'm thinking things like that, my girliness is at middle-aged-man level.
"Chigaya-chan, don't you go to places like this?"
"Mm... I've hardly ever been to one..."
"Oh, really? I'd think a beauty like you, Chigaya-chan, would look right at home in a place like this!"
"Mm... Would I suit it...?"
"But maybe you still prefer the Arata way of having fun? 　Red-lantern bars, places with all-you-can-drink."
It seemed Auntie had seen right through me.
Right, I'd meant to ask her advice about that today too.
"U-uh, Auntie! Can I ask you for advice about something that's bothering me!?"
I took a sip of water and lightly clenched my fists.
Auntie met my serious eyes without losing her smile.
"Hmm? What is it?"
"Mm... Uh... Well... Um..."
My serious gaze was shot down by Auntie's and sank lower and lower.
"What's wrong? If something's troubling you, you can tell me anything."
Auntie had switched to a voice as warm and accepting as a mother's.
Auntie and my mom are completely different types, but even so, I felt a warmth that put me at ease.
"Mm... Am I... a failure as a woman...?"
"Huh...?"
Auntie's reply was serious, but mixed into it, just barely, was a snort like she was holding back a laugh.
I'm being fairly serious here...
"I see. Everyone worries about that. When I was about your age, Chigaya-chan, I often went to Akane with the same kind of worries too."
"Huh!? Auntie, you had drinking disasters too!?"
"Drinking...??"
This time she seemed to genuinely have no idea what I was talking about.
But she quickly seemed to catch on to something and gave me a composed smile.
"Chigaya-chan, do you want to be popular with guys? 　Or do you want a boyfriend?"
"Mm... Um... Which is it, I wonder..."
"............ Pfft!"
"Mm... Is that so weird...?"
"Hee hee... sorry, sorry. It's like looking at myself back then."
"Mm... You used to talk to this Akane-san about things like that?"
"That's right. If anything, I was much worse than you, Chigaya-chan. Far from deciding what to do myself, I'd even ask Akane what I was thinking."
With that, Auntie clasped her hands in front of her face and lowered her voice a little.
"If you want to be popular, take a good look at yourself as you are now and start doing things men like. But if you want a boyfriend, you're fine just as you are. Whether or not someone shows up who accepts you just as you are, and whether that happens sooner or later: that's all it comes down to."
I couldn't tell whether that was agreeing with me or disagreeing, or even whether it was advice at all.
For that matter, I don't even really know what I'm after: being popular, or a boyfriend.
"Actually, Chigaya-chan, why are you so worried about your own flaws?"
"Mm... I can't really put it into words, but... I want to fall in love... maybe..."
I didn't know if that was the right answer, but it felt the most fitting.
Rather than being popular or getting a boyfriend, it's feeling my heart flutter and race through romance.
I've never had that kind of experience, so maybe I just long for it.
Ahh, somehow, having come this far, I was starting to feel embarrassed.
To think I'd lay bare worries like a grade-schooler's to a relative.
I'll be 21 soon. Am I a little too starry-eyed about romance?
"Mm... Ah... it's no good after all, is it... Asking for such childish advice..."
"Age has nothing to do with things like that. No, it's a very good thing to have worries like that."
"Mm... It's a good thing?"
"People grow by piling up worries and failures. A life with no worries or failures would be surprisingly boring, I think."
"Mm... Failures... I seem to be the type whose flaws stand out... So if I want to fall in love, I have to fix those things..."
"My, my, Chigaya-chan, you really are a maiden at heart. Is it that you're in love with love?"
"Mm... I don't really know..."
"Right. With things like that, sometimes you only understand them when you look back later."
Just then, the tower in question was brought to us.
Beside the fragrant coffee, a tower as lively as an amusement park.
So this kind of thing is called an afternoon tea set.
"Oh, come to think of it, Chigaya-chan, you eat quite a lot, don't you? Maybe this won't be enough?"
"Mm... It's fine, it's fine. I don't usually eat much..."
I couldn't bring myself to really eat in a place like this.
But it's true that I normally eat little.
It's just that I can eat as much as I want if I set my mind to it. A normal amount is enough to satisfy me.
Once, when a friend talked me into trying the eating-challenge menu at a ramen shop, I polished the whole thing off with more than half the time limit to spare.
That was the moment I realized my stomach was beyond the range of ordinary people.
I couldn't forget that sense of accomplishment, and ever since, I've ended up taking on the challenge at every place that has an eating-challenge menu.
Ugh... a mean drunk and a big eater...
On top of that, arm strength to put a man to shame, built from growing up in the mountains.
I'm a far cry from the woman I'm aiming to be...
Mom, I'm sorry.
You left me such good looks, and I end up being a contrast of the bad kind.
"Now, eat up first. Trying things you're not used to is good experience too."
"Mm... Well then, itadakimasu."
I reached for a cake on the top plate, which looked easy to grab.
"Oh my, Chigaya-chan! With these, you start from the sandwiches on the bottom! 　The top is the dessert plate, right?"
"Mm... I see..."
Thrown by the mystery rule, I nonetheless picked up a sandwich from the bottom and put it in my mouth.
............ ............?
It didn't strike me as especially good.
I think the food in Arata is much better, though.
"Hee hee hee. Maybe you can't really taste it until you're used to it?"
"Mm... No, it's really good."
"But it's no match for the food in Arata, is it?"
"............"
Auntie saw right through what I was thinking.
I put the sandwich down on my plate and sighed.
"Mm... I really do need to start polishing myself with things like this. Popular or not, if I don't know at least basic table manners like this, I'll embarrass the man I'm with..."
"I don't know about that! 　If you're interested and want to learn, that's fine, but if not, it'll just tire you out, Chigaya-chan. Finding someone who shares the values you have now is much faster, and easier from here on, too."
"Haah... is there even a man out there who'd suit a woman like me...?"
After all, my losing streak is in triple digits, with a 0% win rate.
I'm not mentally tough enough to stay upbeat after that, and I'm even tempted to suspect I'm actually ugly.
Then Auntie gazed at me with a sly little grin.
"Chigaya-chan, looking at you reminds me of when I met Eiichiro-san. Me and Eiichiro-san, we were both like you― no, even more pessimistic, or rather, we had no confidence in ourselves."
"Mm... You said that earlier too. I can't picture you like that at all, Auntie..."
"When you meet the person you're meant to meet, you'll change naturally. There's no need to force yourself to change. 　Chigaya-chan, what is it about yourself that you're so unhappy with?"
"............ How I get when I drink..."
"............
W-well... that part may need a little work... Hohoho..."
For a moment, Auntie's face seemed to freeze.
Then, as if to cover it up, Auntie sipped something called a Beginner Coffee and hid her face behind the cup.
"Mm... Maybe I should quit drinking... It's not like I can't get by without it..."
"Now, now, don't be so tense about it. You're still young, so take your time thinking it over."
"Mm... You think so...?"
"Sure, if you're attractive, you'll have more chances to get to know men, and your odds of meeting someone you click with will go up. But, Chigaya-chan, aren't you forgetting the most important thing?"
"Mm... What would that be...?"
"Chigaya-chan, results aside, are you enjoying meeting people?"
"Mm... Meeting people? 　I don't know... Once the drinks get me going it's fun, but when morning comes, it's more like self-loathing..."
"Self-loathing isn't fun, is it?"
"Mm... Well, no..."
"It's fine to go looking for someone with everything you've got, but while you're young, above all, don't forget to enjoy yourself. Good memories and bad memories are both life experience. If you can't enjoy it, it all turns into bad experience, and there's no way you can stay positive, right?"
"Mm... If I can't enjoy it, I can't be positive..."
"Chigaya-chan, you're probably just trying too hard. People naturally gather around someone who can enjoy anything, even with a few flaws."
"Mm... You're right, I try way too hard every time. Okay, I'll try to take it a little easier."
"Good! Come on, come on, eat up!"
Nothing in particular had come of the talk, but getting it off my chest made me feel a little lighter.
And with that, my appetite welled up on its own.
When I tasted the sandwich again, the bread was fluffy, the vegetables crisp, and the meat juicy. It was delicious.
My complaint about the taste must have been down to my state of mind.
"Chigaya-chan, the curry and the omelette rice here are really good too! 　Eat as much as you like!"
Curry and omelette rice.
Hm, just hearing the lineup made me want to eat.
At a place that serves sandwiches this good, I can expect a lot.
"Mm... Then, both!"
"Hee hee hee hee! That's it, that's it! You're young, so you have to eat lots! 　There are more men than you'd think who like girls who eat a lot!"
Yeah, I'll get the bad stuff off my chest to someone who'll listen, eat lots of good food, and forget it all in one go!
A little reflection and a lot of positive thinking.
This must be how people grow.
"Chigaya-chan, watching you eat reminds me of Haruka's junior, Goto-chan!"
"Mm... Goto-chan...?"
"Here, this is Goto-chan. She eats a ton too!"
Auntie showed me a photo on her phone.
She was very petite, with big round eyes, like a doll.
Cute... so cute...
She was a girl so delicious-look... no, so cute that I was jealous of Haruka.
"This Goto-chan is really smart. People call her a genius girl. She's going to come to Eiichiro-san's upcoming thirteenth-year memorial service."
In that instant, the air around me and Auntie froze.
The way Auntie had just put it― was the same as saying 'a smart kid is coming to the Arata settlement.'
In other words―
"Mm... So it means the curse-killings might be solved..."
"Chigaya-chan, the reason I asked you here today is that I wanted to talk about that."
It seemed this was where the real subject began.
This year makes 10 years since Mom died.
A curse-killing is expected this year, too.
Which means this girl is going to help solve it.
