The Maidens' Holy War ~Duty~

Wabigoto Arc [Apology] · Shiyo 790-04-06 18:30 火 · 55 boxes

"I'm home!"
"Dad, welcome home!"
Natsumi came pattering out to meet me.
"Dad! I did it with lots of energy! 　Um... self... self-intro...?"
"Self-introduction? You got to tell your teacher and your friends about yourself, right?"
Natsumi got scooped up into my arms. She sure had gotten heavy.
"Yeah, self-introduction! Teacher said I had a big loud voice and lots of energy!"
"There, there, good job, Natsumi."
"Eheheh."
"Hm? Where's Mom?"
Always the one to come greet me, Akane showed no sign of appearing.
There was no sign of dinner being made either.
"Mom's reading a letter."
Just because of that?
"Akane, what's wrong?"
In the living room, Akane turned toward me with the letter still in her hand.
"Oh, sorry. Welcome home..."
She looked gloomy. Was it not just an ordinary letter?
"This came from Mifuyu..."
There was no sender's name on the envelope.
The letter itself had Mifuyu-san's name and address written on it.
Megasawa City, Iizawa Prefecture... The next prefecture over.
Wasn't she living in the Arata settlement...?
"Ryoji-kun, sorry, but could you and Natsumi eat dinner out?"
"Is that letter about something that serious?"
"Sorry, I just... want to think by myself..."
"All right..."
I was curious about what the letter said too, but for now it was best to do as Akane asked.
"Hey, Natsumi! Mom's praising you for doing a great self-introduction today too! 　Let's go to that place today. Um, that restaurant that opened in March..."
"Zahha!"
Natsumi answered cheerfully, but the word Zahha― an H-row sound after a doubled consonant (the small tsu) is a bit hard for me to pronounce.
Like sutaffu, or Gohho, or byuffe. [TN: Japanese forms of "staff," "Gogh" and "buffet."]
I'm no good with foreign words like these...
Natsumi rode on my shoulders as we headed for Zahha.
"How come Mom can't come?"
"Hmm... Mom has to write a reply to her letter, apparently. So let's go, the three of us, on our next day off!"
"Mom's writing back to her letter, so good!"
It was clear today, with no wind. It made me glad that Natsumi was feeling well.
"The flowers... are blooming..."
"Hm? Yeah..."
With her on my shoulders I couldn't see where Natsumi was looking, but she probably meant the cherry blossoms.
"Gross..."
That was Natsumi's impression of cherry blossoms.
Plum and peach blossoms look like cherry, but she apparently had no particular dislike of them.
Maybe it was about time we thought about moving away from here.
By rights, we should have done it before Natsumi started elementary school, but...
The truth was, Akane had asked me to wait just one more year.
Because if we moved away from Motoki-cho, we would completely lose any way of getting in touch with Mifuyu-san.
Akane worried about Natsumi's health too, but if she held out a little under a month, she bounced right back.
Even so, staying on in Motoki-cho, with all its cherry trees, might be reaching its limit.
If that letter meant we could get in touch with Mifuyu-san again, we might not be here anymore by next year's cherry-blossom season.
Maybe I should put in a transfer request at work soon.
If that didn't work, I'd have to look for another job.
"Wow, so many people!"
Lots of people were lined up at the newly opened Zahha.
Natsumi-aged kids and their parents made up most of the crowd.
Celebrating starting school at Zahha. Every family thinks alike.
"Oh, that girl's in my class!"
Natsumi ran off toward a girl about her age.
It looked like Natsumi would do fine at elementary school.
―After waiting about 15 minutes, we got a table.
"Natsumi, today we're celebrating you starting school. A parfait, anything, eat whatever you like! 　Oh, but you have to eat the stir-fried spinach and the salad."
"Yaaay!"
Natsumi was happily looking over the menu.
Whether family-restaurant vegetables were good or bad for you, I didn't care.
The habit of eating vegetables is what matters, Akane had told me once.
"Dad! I want to eat this pretty one!"
"Let's see."
This was... takoyaki...? [TN: takoyaki: fried batter balls with octopus inside]
But strange takoyaki, colored red, yellow, green and black.
"Excuse me! Eight of this Russian-roulette takoyaki, please! 　Only one winner! We'll order the rest later!"
The waitress took Natsumi's order and left.
And what did she mean, winner?
When I managed to check the takoyaki on the menu...
Let's see... Only one contains an extra-hot spice.
Thrills and excitement for everyone...
Who'll draw the loser... maybe it's you...?
Reading that far, I felt the blood drain from my face all at once.
Natsumi was actually planning to eat this!?
My brand-new elementary schooler of a daughter, eating this!?
What's more, Natsumi had even specified the number of losers.
That was proof Natsumi understood the rules!
Natsumi calling the loser the 'winner' made it even scarier...
Natsumi, did you have that kind of gambler's streak!?
No, wait, how did she even understand it when it's written in kanji!?
And Zahha, if a kid orders it, stop them!
"N-Natsumi... Do you know what kind of dish this Russian-roulette takoyaki is?"
"Yeah! I want to play a game with Dad using these!"
Akane...! What have you been doing with Natsumi every day!
"N-Natsumi! Not this one! 　Not this, look, a parfait, or pancakes, or a hamburger steak..."
At that, the excitement drained from Natsumi's face and it started to droop.
"Sniff... You said I could order anything..."
"H-hey now..."
Having said she could order anything, I'd need a proper argument to make her give this up.
What's more, forcing her to give it up at her own school celebration would just make her sad.
On top of that, the baffling situation that she understood the rules better than I did.
To make her give it up, I'd need a lot of persuasive power!
"Sorry for the wait. Here's your Russian-roulette takoyaki."
While I was agonizing over it, the thing in question was set down in front of us...
F-first, reconnaissance of the enemy...
Gah...!
A tasteless dish, and dry ice for effect...! Zahha meant business...
I had already checked the detailed rules.
Four kinds of spice―habanero, wasabi, Japanese mustard and something-or-other―one of them was in there at random.
The color of the takoyaki had nothing to do with the spice.
The black spice, the something-or-other, felt especially dangerous.
"Dad, why are there so many waters lined up on this table?"
To be extra safe, I had gotten 10 glasses of water.
If Natsumi got the spicy one, I'd have to give first aid right away.
Of course, I couldn't let Natsumi draw the loser.
I should be the one to draw the spiced takoyaki!
That was my duty as a parent!
"Oh, Russian-roulette takoyaki!"
A little girl was peering over at our table.
"Want one?"
Natsumi picked up one black takoyaki...
"Aaah!"
The girl had it popped right into her mouth.
"Ah! Wait, you dummy, y-yo...!"
But it was too late.
The grotesquely glistening black takoyaki was tossed into the girl's mouth.
"Yummy! There's chocolate inside!"
Phew... so it was safe.
If it had been the loser, I'd have had to get down on my knees and bow to her family right away.
"So now the chance of getting it next is one in seven!"
The one who said that was clearly a girl even smaller than Natsumi.
Why was a kid like this calculating probabilities?
I guess you find them everywhere, these genius girls.
"Which one are you going to eat?"
At some point the little girl had sat down next to Natsumi.
Huh? 　Huh!? She's still planning to join in?
Hold on, this is no joke! This lowers the chance that I get the loser!
I had to send her back to her family right away!
"Dad, aren't you eating?"
Wait, before I knew it, only three takoyaki were left!?
"Do you two understand...? 　There's one in here that's really spicy, you know that?"
"I know that! That's what makes it fun!"
"When I did it with Sis before, it was fun! 　Sis ate the winner and didn't come back from the bathroom for ages, though."
Damn! So they were doing this with a full grasp of the rules!
Even if I tried to shoo this kid away now, Natsumi was having fun too.
What was I supposed to do...! I didn't want to let Natsumi down at her school celebration.
But then, if this kid drew the loser...
No... this kid seemed to be a Russian-roulette takoyaki veteran.
Her parents must have known that when they let her have a match with her older sister, but...

If it's come to this... there's nothing for it but to go through with it...!
I have to draw the loser, whatever it takes.
This is... This is the duty laid on me!
"All right, then I'll eat first. That way I'm more likely to draw the loser."
"Dad, it's not the loser, it's the winner! 　And going for the winner on purpose is boring!"
"If everyone eats them all, then whether it's in turns or at the same time, the chance of getting it is the same either way, isn't it?"
What is with this genius girl! Explain it so an adult can understand!
Damn... I never thought these little gamblers would have me breaking into a cold sweat like this.
The takoyaki left were red, yellow and black...
Of these, would the one that looked like the 'winner' be the black, after all?
Right, I'd pick black.
"Okay, all three of us pick the one we want at the same time."
"Okay!"
"Chance of survival: two in three..."
Good, all three of us picked different colors.
"All three of us eat at the same time... Ready!?"

"Ready, go!"
"Ready, go!"
"Ready, go!"
