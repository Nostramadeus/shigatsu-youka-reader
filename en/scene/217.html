'Setup'

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-07 23:50 火 · 57 boxes

"Sorry, Kotaro-san! I need a little help."
"Mm... mmm?"
Kotaro-san looked at Goto's notes.
"Aw, man... Mom dragged even you kids into this...?"
Kotaro-san scratched his head roughly, looking exasperated.
"Oof, there we go."
Kotaro-san plopped down heavily in front of Goto.
"Here, let me have a look."
Kotaro-san snatched Goto's notes away.
"I see. The time of Kikyo-san's death in 735, the time the suicides started, and the time the curse-killings started... Sakura's cause of death, huh..."
Kotaro-san showed no particular surprise as he stared hard at Goto's notes.
"First, let me teach you kids a way of thinking things through. Haruka and Natsumi-chan, sit down over there."
Kotaro-san opened his eyes wide, suddenly sharp.
"'Kishotenketsu.' You know this one, right? It's the structure you need when you make four-panel comics or stories. [TN: Kishotenketsu: four parts, ki 'setup', sho 'development', ten 'twist', ketsu 'conclusion'.] But it's also necessary when you're reasoning something out. Not that I'm much good at deduction myself..."
There's a beginning, then events that follow from it, then the twist that surprises the audience, and finally the wrap-up.
I think that's roughly how kishotenketsu goes.
"If we think about the curse-killing case with this, which part of kishotenketsu do you think is the most important? 　Tell me, one at a time."
Which is the most important? It's a simple question, but a pretty hard one.
If I apply it to the curse-killing case...
"I'd still say the 'conclusion.' What purpose the culprit has in causing these incidents. Figure that out, and the other parts'll come into view too, won't they?"
"I see. Goto-chan, what do you think?"
"Umm... what do I think..."
Goto rarely struggled over a simple question.
"I think it's the 'development.' The 'development' is the present, so if we can solve it, there won't be any more curse-killings, maybe..."
Goto's point of view was all about stopping any more curse-killings.
From that point of view, I could understand what Goto was saying.
"Natsumi-chan, what do you think?"
"Hmm..."
At this point, should I pick the 'setup' or the 'twist'?
"Then... I'm just going by feel, but the 'setup,' maybe. After all, you have to get a firm grasp of the beginning..."
"I see..."
Kotaro-san heard the three of us out and smiled slightly.
"Natsumi-chan, congratulations! The answer is
the 'setup.' That's only true this time, though."
Goto took back the notebook Kotaro-san had snatched and was taking notes as if listening to a lecture.
"Why is the 'setup' important? What happens if there's no 'setup'?"
A state with no 'setup'...?
No 'setup'...
What would happen...?
"It means nothing happens..."
"Right. Without the 'setup,' the case doesn't even exist. Or it hasn't come to light. And if your understanding of the 'setup' is off, then the 'development, twist and conclusion' after it will all be off too."
True. If the 'setup,' in other words the premise, is off, the story after it turns into a total mess.
"Okay, next. What is the 'setup'? The 'setup' means the start of the case. So, can you tell me when the case started?"
Obviously, the incident of Sakura-san's death.
No, when the suicides began?
Or the curse-killings that began the year after Sakura-san died?
"I can't... say for sure..."
"Do you see what I'm getting at? In this case, the 'setup' is unclear. Where does the case begin? And is this string of curse-killings really a case at all? There are people in the settlement who honestly believe it's a curse."
So that was it. We'd rushed to answer and hadn't properly read the actual question.
When the 'setup' is spelled out, like in mystery novels or movies, the story after it is easy to follow.
But this time, we have to pin down the 'setup' ourselves.
"By the way, I looked at Goto-chan's notes just now... Looks like the idea came up that I might be the culprit..."
"Um... that's..."
"No, no, I'm not blaming you. Everyone in this settlement is a suspect. And the one most likely to be suspected is me, the man who lost Sakura. This is a good chance, so let's assume I'm the culprit and try reasoning it out. Go ahead, start. No need to hold back."
That was a pretty bold thing to say.
If this makes Goto sure that Kotaro-san is the culprit, what is he going to do...?
"Then, um... if Kotaro-san is the culprit, the 'setup' is when Sakura-san died, right?"
Kotaro-san listened in silence.
He didn't seem to intend to cut in.
"No, Goto, hang on a sec! 　That means Kotaro-san can't tell where the 'setup' is either, let alone who the culprit is, right!?"
Haruka said that, and Kotaro-san scratched his head with a wry smile.
"Well, of course... If I knew, the culprit would probably be caught already. I want to know who cut off Sakura's head too. That's why I want you to assume I'm the culprit and think that part through as well."
Kotaro-san, too, was a victim who had lost Sakura-san.
Kotaro-san, like Erika-chan, needed our help.
In that case, the events that Kotaro-san and Erika-chan never came face to face with― what happened in Megasawa 10 years ago might hide an important clue.
If so, it's something Kotaro-san couldn't possibly know, so it's only natural he's stuck.
But something from 10 years ago like that...
"Kotaro-san as the culprit... if we assume that... a whole lot of things add up!"
Kotaro-san being right there didn't seem to make her hold back at all.
"Sakura-san jumped off the cliff, but she actually hadn't suffered a fatal injury. So, since she'd broken the rule, he couldn't forgive Sakura-san, and he finished her off. But then he himself might be suspected of being the culprit. So, calling it a curse, he killed one person every year and made it look as if Sakura-san had died from the curse too."
Goto's theory certainly added up.
Kotaro-san had said he had people's respect and trust, so if he used that position...
"I see..."
Kotaro-san murmured that, but said nothing more after it.
Had he gone quiet because she'd hit the heart of it, or was he waiting for the next deduction...?
"No, but... that's kinda weird. If that's how it is, then my mom was the cause. If he can't forgive that, then my mom should've been killed too. Even if not right away, I don't think he'd leave her alone for 10 whole years."
Haruka had a point too.
Kotaro-san wouldn't open his mouth.
He was just silently waiting for us to speak.
No, it felt like he wanted to say something but was deliberately saying nothing.
"Natsumi, what do you think? Taking Kotaro-san as the culprit..."
"Hmm..."
Goto and Haruka were reasoning from Kotaro-san's motive.
Maybe I'll try coming at it from a slightly different angle...
"Kotaro-san, could I ask you something?"
"Sure, go ahead!"
"The person who first found Sakura-san after she went off somewhere was you, Kotaro-san, right?"
"Yeah, that's right."
"At that point, was Sakura-san really dead?"
"I see, so you're coming at it from that angle..."


　　She was alive.
"Th-that means...!"
Sakura-san was still alive when he found her...!?
Then cutting off her head to finish her off, that was...!
But Kotaro-san kept his mouth shut.
"You're kidding, right!? Why'd you kill Sakura-san!? 　And for 9 years on top of that...!"
Haruka grabbed Kotaro-san by the shoulder.
"Haruka-chan, are you done with your deduction?"
Kotaro-san didn't look particularly shaken.
"Quit messing around! Why'd you do this!?"
"Senpai, wait! Just because she was alive doesn't mean he finished her off..."
"'Cause no matter how you look at it, the only one who could've finished her off is this guy!"
Haruka was enraged, but Kotaro-san still wasn't trying to say anything.
"Kotaro-san, may I keep asking questions?"
"Natsumi, what are you talking about!? What more is there to ask!?"
"Please tell me a little more about when you found her."
"Natsumi...!"
"Haruka, the culprit being Kotaro-san is just a hypothesis. We haven't settled the 'setup' yet."
"Damn...!"
Haruka let go of Kotaro-san and sank to the floor.
"When I found Sakura, huh... It'll take a little while, okay?"
Kotaro-san said that and sat up straight.
"At that moment, Sakura was standing at the top of the cliff. When I found her, Sakura was just about to jump off the cliff."
"Sakura! Come back here, now!"
When I called out, Sakura turned her tearful face toward me.
"Kotaro, stay away! I... broke the rule... So...!"
"Don't say such stupid things! Mom isn't that narrow-minded!"
"No...! I've failed as one of the Arata people! There's no way I can be forgiven...!"
"Mom was talking with Mifuyu-chan! Mifuyu-chan, and Sakura too, Mom doesn't mean to blame either of you!"
"Really...? 　Really?"
"Yeah! So come over here, now!"
I slowly approached Sakura.
That was when it happened.
Suddenly, Sakura swayed and toppled over.
As if drawn by something unseen.
Sakura slipped smoothly over just like that, and vanished below the cliff.
Frantic, I headed for the bottom of the cliff.
Haruka-chan probably knows this, but it takes at least 5 minutes to get from that cliff down to the bottom.
When I reached the bottom...
Even from a distance, I could tell Sakura was already gone.
Because Sakura's head had already been severed from her body.
"That's a lie! 　There's no way something that convenient happened! Sakura-san just swayed and toppled over right at that moment!? Quit messing around!"
Haruka stood up and lit into Kotaro-san.
"Senpai, this story probably isn't a lie."
"What, even you, Goto...!"
"If this were a lie, he could just say she was already dead when he found her. Or say she jumped without being talked down. There's no need for Kotaro-san to go out of his way to lie here..."
"Damn...!"
Haruka sank to the floor again.
"Natsumi-chan... mind if I ask you a question too?"
