"Hello?"
"Ah, um, this is Ryoji Kogori-san's phone. I'm with the Motoki Police―"
Hearing those words, I hung up right away.
It was the moment my last hope was cut off.
Dad's phone: the one who answered it was, for some reason, someone from the police.
So that's it. It really is so, after all.
My mouth trembled, and the backs of my eyes grew hot.
In my hand, my phone, and Haruka's cold hand.
The feel of that cold hand gave my heart something to brace against.
I mustn't cry, I mustn't cry now.
I absolutely mustn't cry in front of Haruka.
Just for now...
"Natsumi, it's fine, okay? Going back, even now. This is just me being selfish..."
Hard to make out and without inflection, Haruka's voice.
It seemed she had no strength left even to raise her voice.
"I've already decided. I won't leave you, Haruka. It's okay, you don't have to worry about anything."
I turned off my phone and squeezed Haruka's hand tight.
Before long, the train came.
We didn't have anywhere in particular to go.
Getting on the train had been my suggestion. Surely, to escape the eyes of the police, we had to leave Motoki-cho.
Motoki-cho had the bare minimum of facilities needed for daily life, so not many people rode the train.
In the car we got on, there was no one.
Wherever we went, we probably wouldn't be seen by anyone.
Haruka wouldn't sit down next to me.
She just stood there, her face turned down.
"Come on, Haruka."
Haruka's hand, I took it and pulled her down to sit.
As before, Haruka kept her face turned down―
The sun was sinking, and the inside of the car was turning red.
The view soon changed to mountain scenery, and the cherry blossoms began to be lit by the setting sun.
How far we'd go, I wasn't thinking about.
Haruka: anywhere, as long as it was somewhere she could feel safe.
"Natsumi... I..."
Haruka's hand, I squeezed it hard, but far from squeezing back, Haruka had no strength in her hand that I could feel.
"Haruka, if you don't want to talk, you don't have to. But, even if you do tell me, nothing will surprise me. And I won't start hating you, Haruka, either."
"............"
Haruka said nothing.
Instead, from Haruka's eyes, big tears came spilling out.
"I... I'm sorry...! 　Natsumi...! I'm sorry!"
Somehow, I had sensed it.
Hearing the police mentioned and frightened, Haruka.
Telling me to be on my own right away, Goto.
At the police station, Mom.
And, that dream.
I don't want to imagine it, but maybe―
When I thought the one who died in the incident might be my dad, I was thrown into a panic.
I know I have to go to the police right away.
But, right in front of me, the frightened Haruka, I just couldn't leave her alone.
Together with me ever since we were born, Haruka― crying and suffering, Haruka, as if I could abandon her.
Let them think I'm a bad daughter.
Let them think I'm stupid.
Let me be in danger right now.
Haruka, if I can set her mind at ease, then that's fine.
"Natsumi... your dad..."
I see, so she's going to talk about my dad after all.
If I panicked here, what would happen?
Surely Haruka's mind would be thrown into even more turmoil, and she'd go mad―
Even at a time like this, all I can do is whip my own heart on and keep calm.
There's nothing for it but to endure.
All it takes is for me to endure.
"It's okay, Haruka. I'm on your side, no matter what."
When I said that, once again from Haruka's eyes, tears came spilling out.
Without any resistance at all, I had defended Haruka, and I was surprised at myself.
My dad was killed, and yet.
Haruka killed Dad, and yet.
There must have been some deep reason.
I had already decided to run away with Haruka.
"Right now... Natsumi, you're the one in danger... Being with me, Natsumi, you're in danger..."
"Yeah, maybe so... But, I won't leave you alone, Haruka."
"Natsumi... you... are a real idiot..."
"Yeah, maybe so... But still, I won't leave you alone, Haruka."
Could these be the words of a murderer?
To begin with, a crime like that, could Haruka even have done it?
"Haruka, I'll be honest with you. I just can't believe that you, Haruka, killed my dad. Calling a grown man out, flaying him, leaving him alive in Central Park: that's impossible with your strength, Haruka. Aren't you misunderstanding something?"
"............"
Haruka's sobbing grew gradually quieter.
Had she been able to feel a little relieved?
"Natsumi... What I'm about to say, I want you to hear it without being shocked. Probably... you won't believe it."
"Okay."
The train entered a tunnel, and the car went dark all at once.
Whether to save power or because of some malfunction, the lights in the car didn't come on.
"I'm... a witch..."
"Huh?"
"A witch who kills people..."
A completely unexpected statement.
I never thought she'd say something this far-fetched...
"A witch... meaning?"
"I can curse people to death."
I didn't know how to answer.
Something that stupid...
"Haruka, did you curse my dad to death?"
"On a paper doll, you write a name, and cast all kinds of spells on it. Then, that person dies on April 8..."
"I-I see. So you, Haruka, wrote my dad's name on a paper doll and put a curse on him."
"Probably..."
Probably, she says... Why give such a vague answer after coming this far?
"How come it's 'probably'?"
"I don't remember it, making the paper doll. When I woke up this morning, it was on my desk. So I got scared and put it away right away. If I threw it out or something, Mom might find it."
"I see, so you didn't do it because you wanted to either, Haruka. But how do the cursed people die? Surely it's not that their skin just peels off on its own and they die, right?"
"............ Shinigami..."
"Huh?"
"When a curse succeeds, the shinigami appears where the target is. The shinigami cruelly kills the one I cursed, by every means there is."
"Hey, Haruka, why do you think you're a witch?"
I tried changing the question.
Goto could do it better than I probably can, but I have to draw the story out bit by bit and keep piling up deductions so that I can prove Haruka isn't the culprit.
Just then, Haruka suddenly lifted her face and stared hard at me.


Up to now, I've killed six people.
It wasn't the face of someone telling a joke.
Haruka had cursed people to death before.
"I... see..."
I noticed my own voice was trembling.
And then Haruka went on.


You die today too
As you'd expect, my face went stiff.
This meant, in other words, Haruka had put a curse on me.
"Ha, hahaha...! 　That's right, Natsumi! The person trying to kill you is right there beside you, after all! 　That's the face you've gotta make!"
It was the first time I'd seen Haruka with a face like this.
Her bloodshot eyes wide open, she was grinning broadly.
If I had to compare it to something, yes... it was a witch's face.
"You're really such an idiot...! You came with me knowing I'm a killer, didn't you? 　You came all this way trusting me, didn't you? 　Just how full of daydreams is your head...! 　Hahahahaha heh hahahaha heh hahaha...!"
"Th-that's not true! Haruka didn't do anything like that!"
"Heh-hahahahahaha! 　So, what are you gonna do!? 　On a train like this, there's nowhere to run! 　Natsumi! 　You're going to die! 　You're going to die today...!! 　HAHA-HE-HAHAHAHA-HYA-HAHA...!"
This isn't Haruka!
This is Haruka, turned into a witch...?
No! Don't let yourself be pushed back!
By now Haruka is confused too, frantic, and just doesn't know what to do anymore!
I absolutely mustn't back down a single step!
"Haruka isn't going to kill me...! 　Because... Haruka's been my best friend since we were born...!"
For an instant, the phrase 'make-believe friendship' rose in my mind.
Besides, I didn't think such a cheap line would work.
But, I absolutely must not give in here.
Haruka, still wearing the face of a "witch," kept staring at me.
I won't lose to something like that.
But,

From Haruka's eyes, swollen from crying, tears came flowing again.
"Natsumiii... I don't want you to die... Please don't die..."
Haruka cried out in a sorrowful voice, tears spilling again.
This is the best proof there is.
Haruka has no intent to kill. All she has is the delusion that she's a witch, and a paper doll.
"Haruka... calm down."
I reached out to rub Haruka's back.
"Don't touch me!"
"I have no right to be comforted by you, Natsumi..."
No! Right now, I absolutely must not accept what Haruka's claiming.
"I'm staying with you, Haruka. You, Haruka, didn't kill my dad or anything. And you won't kill me either."
"But... but, this morning, for real―"
"Don't take me for a fool. That kind of trickery, I'll sweep it away for you."
"I'm a witch! Why are you with me!?"
"The one who said 'stay with me' was you, Haruka, wasn't it!?"
"Yeah, that's right! I thought if I sent you somewhere far away, Natsumi, you'd be okay! 　But I realized that if I'm with you, it's no good in the end!"
"Really, what you're saying is a total mess! I bet your curse too, Haruka, is just some messy, sloppy story!"
"It's not sloppy! Everyone I've put a curse on up to now has died!"
"As if I'd believe a story like that!"
"Shut up, shut up, shut up! 　What would you know about me!? 　Someone like you isn't my friend anymore!"
My open hand struck Haruka's cheek.
Haruka's hair clip came off, and her bangs fell softly over her face.
It was the first time I had ever hit anyone.
My hand, from Haruka's tears, got wet, and it hurt more than I'd imagined.
"Haruka... What was that? What you just said. Not my friend?"
"............"
"Come on, say it."
"Sorry..."
"I'm telling you to say it one more time! 　Not my friend anymore!?"
"That's not it...! I didn't mean it for real...!"
"Hah, I can't believe you, saying something like that on impulse... Haruka, what do you want? What are you going to do?"
"I don't know... I don't know...!"
"Then what am I supposed to do?"
"Sorry..."
"What's with that... What's with that! You cry and apologize, you say you don't want me to die, and that's all!? 　Why won't you say you'll save me?"
"Save you...?"
"If our places were reversed, no matter what happened, I would absolutely protect you, Haruka. Or those words, 'not my friend,' did you mean them?"
"Th-there's no way I did! I... Natsumi...!"
"Then save me! Haruka, protect me from the curse you put on me!"
"Natsumi... I'll save her..."
"I can't hear you. Say it more clearly."
"I'll... save Natsumi from the shinigami."
Hearing those words, I could finally let the strength go out of my shoulders.
"Yeah, if the shinigami appears, save me. Please, right now the only one I can rely on is you, Haruka."
"Okay..."
"Thank you. But I still can't believe in your curse, Haruka. But, maybe the 'shinigami' that killed six people does exist. That thing, what's it like?"
At my question, Haruka took one big, deep breath.
"The shinigami always kills the one I curse. In a really cruel, horrible way... Natsumi, you know too, right? About the Megasawa City case..."
"Yeah... I know."
"Whenever I put a curse on someone, without fail, the Megasawa City case happens on April 8. Without any exception, when I put a curse on someone, on April 8 the one I cursed always dies..."
"Don't tell me the culprit of the Megasawa City case is... that shinigami?"
"That's right... That's why it can kill in ways no human could... I'm a witch and I called up the shinigami, so I know its true identity."
I see, by Haruka's logic, Haruka is the mastermind and the shinigami is the one who does the killing, then.
Haruka had known the shinigami's true identity―in other words, the true identity of a violent criminal―and kept quiet all along.
Why hadn't the police been able to pin it down?
Whatever the shinigami is, the one inside is probably human anyway, and the spiriting-away-like methods must be some kind of trick.
Maybe Haruka knows about how it's done, too.
"The shinigami's true identity, I'll tell only you, Natsumi. It'll surely show up in front of you, Natsumi."
"Yeah."
"The shinigami's true identity is... my dad."
Huh? I couldn't hear well over the horn just now...
"Sorry, Haruka, I couldn't really hear..."
"It's my dad...!"
Haruka's dad is the shinigami?
The one who's supposed to be overseas, Haruka's dad?
"Haruka, your dad came all the way back to Japan for this day?"
"I'm sorry I kept quiet till now, but my dad passed away a long time ago. When I put a curse on someone, he appears on April 8, the anniversary of his death, and my wish― he kills the one I cursed, for me. It's exactly because he's a dead person that such spiriting-away-like crimes are possible."
Haruka's dad had passed away.
Even a confession that should have been a huge deal was, by now, only one piece of information.
Even so, a dead person being the culprit, I couldn't believe that right away.
"Your dad who became the shinigami, Haruka, have you ever met him?"
"I've never met my dad as the shinigami either, so I don't really know what he looks like... But..."
"But... when I lived in Megasawa City, I heard it as a rumor... Apparently there was someone who witnessed the moment of a crime, just barely..."
Which meant that, at least, they probably hadn't been killed by something invisible.
"My dad... Nata and a mace, in other words a metal club: he kills with those, apparently. And..."
"He wears a Noh mask."
"A Noh mask? You mean one of those masks?"
Haruka nodded silently.
When I hear shinigami, I picture a scythe and a skeleton, but...
Nata and a mace... and a Noh mask.
It was far from the image of a shinigami, but the tastes of a culprit posing as the 'shinigami' don't matter right now.
If it's only at the level of a rumor, it's hard to call it accurate information, but right now, with no information at all on the shinigami, even a small thing like this is highly significant.
"I see... then that makes it easy to recognize. Haruka, if it shows up, protect me, okay?"
"Natsumi, you're my best friend, and nothing can replace you. I'll... absolutely save you. Even if it costs my life, I'll absolutely save you. Without fail, Natsumi, I'll protect you to the very end."
"Thank you. But... I want to survive together with you, Haruka."
"............"
After that, no conversation arose between us.
