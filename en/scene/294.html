Chiga-nee had told me which path to take, and as I followed it, the footing got worse and worse.
Maybe there'd been a passing shower; the path was muddy.
In contrast to the state of the path, a big moon hung in the sky.
Thanks to it the path was easy to see, but that was exactly what slowed my steps.
Because the moonlight showed me strange things.
This path was covered in footprints.
Chiga-nee―had the residents come through here when they searched for her?
But for that, they were, how do I put it... strange footprints.
They had to be human, these footprints, but every one of them was barefoot.
And besides...
As if there'd been a landslide, big rocks and trees lay across the path ahead.
But every one had been shoved to the side of the path so people could get through.
Someone had clearly moved these rocks and trees on purpose.
There was no way a human could move anything this heavy.
Could this, too, be Shigatsu Youka's―
If so, this path was one Shigatsu Youka had made so that we could get through.
Was it really okay to walk a path like that?
What with the way Chiga-nee had been acting, this was clearly a trap―
I'd better hurry and find Mom and get back to the settlement.
"Haruka?"
Mom's voice...?
"Mom...!"
"Haruka, you came. Did you see Chigaya-chan?"
Mom seemed to be fine, but there was blood smeared thick around her neck.
"Haruka, are you all right? There's blood..."
"Huh? Oh, this is Chiga-nee's nosebleed. I just found Chiga-nee."
"I see..."
Come to think of it, I had blood smeared on me too.
Which meant the blood on Mom was Chiga-nee's too, probably.
If so, Mom must have met that messed-up Chiga-nee.
"Mom! Did you see Chiga-nee!? Chiga-nee―wasn't she acting weird!? Did she hit you or anything?"
"H-hold on, Haruka, calm down..."
"Because Chiga-nee threw me on my back out of nowhere!? 　Then told me to get to the abandoned hut, like, now! 　Chiga-nee, maybe because of Shigatsu Youka..."
"I said calm down. I saw Chigaya-chan too. But Chigaya-chan hasn't lost her mind. Look, Chigaya-chan is being watched by Shigatsu Youka, isn't she? 　So she couldn't put it into words, but don't you think Chigaya-chan was trying to leave us a message somehow?"
"Oh... oh, right, Chiga-nee's being watched by Shigatsu Youka..."
True, Chiga-nee knows she's being watched by Shigatsu Youka.
So she can't say or do anything that helps us.
So all she can do is help us indirectly, with actions that make no sense―is that it?
But Mom's reading is weirdly generous...
"Hey, can we really trust Chiga-nee? There's still a chance she's gone off the deep end, right?"
"Well... but once you start doubting, there's no end to it. Haruka, can you say for certain that I haven't lost my mind right now?"
"No, well... I mean, sure, but..."
"Chigaya-chan and I talked, just a little. She wouldn't tell me her purpose, but I think Chigaya-chan was in her right mind when she said 'go to the abandoned hut.' But..."
"But...?"
"This path is a little strange, isn't it? The landslide's been cleared, and the footprints..."
"Yeah, I thought it was weird too. Means a lot of people were here until just now, right?"
"Haruka, we're not going to the abandoned hut. Let's watch how things look around here for a bit. The hut is a short walk from here, but... how should I put it... it's just..."
"It's just, what..."
"There are no animal calls. At this time of year you'd always hear them. It means they're wary of something."
"Aren't they just wary because we're here...?"
"Then we'd hear the animals farther off."
"Hmm... Fine, I'll do what you say, Mom. So, we just stay here?"
"Haruka... you matter more to me than anything."
"Huh? What's that, all of a sudden..."
"You remember what happened at Toyotake Station at noon, don't you?"
"The cherry tree showing up on the tracks?"
"Right. That happened because Natsumi-chan's five senses were taken over by Shigatsu Youka."
"So what about it..."
"If you follow this path all the way, you'll reach Toyotake Station. Cars pass there now and then, so you, at least..."
"W-wait! You're telling me to run off alone!?"
"You alone can slip past Shigatsu Youka's eyes and get away! 　There's no need for all of us to hole up here..."
"No, I know, but... Natsumi and Goto―leaving them behind..."
"Then is there anything you can do by staying here?"
"I've got no comeback to that, but... still..."
"Haruka, you're dead weight."
"Wha!?"
"If you stay, everyone will just be looking out for you. Better you get away from here instead."
"Don't... don't say that..."
"I'll tell Natsumi-chan and Goto-chan myself. Nobody will blame you. When you get down there, call out the police, the riot police, anyone."
"Nothing's even happened yet, and you want me to call them out... Chiga-nee―we even confirmed she's safe..."
"Never mind that, just go! It's now or never!"
"............"
"I'll take care of Natsumi-chan and Goto-chan somehow..."
Running away alone, here of all places...
Sure, honestly, I'm scared, and if I could get away I'd go right now...
"Haruka, don't hesitate. If something happens, we need someone outside who can help. You understand, don't you?"
"Mom..."
"Now, don't dawdle. Go, quickly."
What I can do... is help the settlement from the outside, not the inside.
The one thing I, useless as I am, can do...
"Mom... Natsumi and Goto... I'm counting on you..."
"Leave it to me. I'll see you meet them again safe and sound."
Am I running away?
No, I'm going to get help from outside.
Natsumi and Chiga-nee aren't here now, so while Shigatsu Youka isn't watching me, this is something only I can do.
To call the police for help, I don't need to tell the truth.
If I say "There's been a murder, come right away," the police will have to move.
But I could do that over the phone.
Damn... no signal...!
The signal doesn't reach unless I go back to the settlement.
I thought of going back to the settlement to call―but there are things I can only do from outside.
Forcing myself to believe that, I run from Arata.
Yes. Right now, I'm running away.
A miserable thing to realize.
I'm running and leaving everyone behind.
Even realizing that, I couldn't stop my feet.
Why? Why don't I try to go back?
Natsumi and Goto, left behind while I run away...!
I know. Mom's first goal is getting me away.
Calling for help from outside is just a pretext to talk me into it.
Knowing that, I'm still running.
The more I went over those thoughts, the more the misery won out.
I sat down under an overhang of rock I came to, and waited for my breathing to settle.
Outside the overhang it was still mud, no state to sit in.
My shoes were already soaked through.
Why... why did I run...
Natsumi said, "Let's go home to Motoki-cho," and in that moment I was relieved from the bottom of my heart.
I wouldn't have to go somewhere dangerous. I wouldn't have to be scared.
That relief really did feel good.
But it was smashed to pieces right away.

On the surface I put on a brave face, but inside I was shaking.
Why do I have to be the one in danger...!
It doesn't have to be me in danger!
I... want to run...!
"Damn...!"
Seeing my own ugly insides, I ground my fist into the ground.
As if to cover up those ugly insides, I made my fist hurt on purpose.
I'd thought Natsumi and Goto were people I needed in my life, and I threw them both away and ran.
Somebody once said:
Running isn't shameful.
When an animal senses danger, it runs first thing.
It's the humans who treat running as shameful who've got it wrong.
So what...
Running is easy. I'll survive, sure.
But... even an animal, if its young or its pack are in danger, tries to help, doesn't it?
Never mind animals―even honeybees will take on a hornet bigger than themselves to protect the hive.
Running now is wrong...
I know I'd be no use even if I stayed.
The wish to run and the wish not to run mixed together and were about to crush my heart.
I... want to be useful...!
I want the power to be useful...!
Why don't I have a single thing going for me!
At a time this important, why can't I be any use at all...!
Thud, thud, I slammed my fist into the ground again and again.
The pain started to go numb.
Blood must be seeping out by now.
"Damn!"
One last time, I punched the ground hard.
Doing this changes nothing―not fooling my heart, not punishing myself.
I have no choice but to run.
―I'd stopped my fist, and yet the sound of something hitting the ground went on.
No, it's not quite a hitting sound.
This is... footsteps...?
Who would be out here...?
............ Huh?
For a moment I nearly said that person's name, like it was nothing.
But I swallowed the words right away.
Because that person is...
"It's been a long time, Haruka."
On that face, unchanged from 10 years ago, was that goddess-like smile.
But... Impossible, impossible...!
Because this person is...!
"Sakura... san...?"
No way... why would Sakura-san be...?
"Haruka, may I sit next to you?"
"Ah..."
The person who looked like Sakura-san sat down beside me as I stayed there speechless.
Wait, what is this, a dream?
Or had I actually died somehow without noticing, and could see dead people now?
No... there's one thing I can think of.
Chiga-nee left that phone behind, with the audio on it.
Chiga-nee seemed to have been taken by Sakura-san and Dad.
Chiga-nee was either mistaken, or the two of them had shown up in that storehouse.
"Haruka, I know what you're thinking. It's all very confusing, isn't it?"
"Th... that's..."
It was all so much that the words wouldn't come.
"Chigaya―did you see her?"
"I... I did meet her..."
"Hee hee, you don't have to be so formal with me. I see... so Chigaya made it back safe. I'm glad... I should explain things properly to you, too. Otherwise you'll just think the dead are walking around."
"Y-yes..."
"It's all right, Haruka. I'm not going to gobble you up out of nowhere. Calm down. It's all right."
"I died 10 years ago, you know. You know that, don't you?"
"Yes..."
"Since then, my body has been in a certain someone's keeping."
"Keeping?"
"Shigatsu Youka―a person who has been in this land since ancient times."
"A person... is it?"
"Hmm, it's hard to explain. Chigaya has it right: she can sense what Natsumi-chan and Chigaya sense, and that comes through to me too. So I know what you all saw. Shigatsu Youka really is a fungus, and she has fruiting bodies disguised as cherry trees. And she parasitizes mammals, humans included, and goes on growing and reproducing."
"But why are you here, Sakura-san...?"
"She has the power to bring the dead back, you see. No―to be exact, the power to move the dead."
"Is that... a necromancer?"
"Your friend Goto-san said something like that, didn't she. Yes―you could well call her a necromancer. Which would make me a zombie, I suppose?"
Having said that much, Sakura-san laughed gently again, hee hee hee.
This is... a dead person? A zombie?
To me she looked like nothing but a living human.
And her gestures, her whole air, were just as they'd been in life.
The only thing different was her clothes.
Her hair and skin were beautiful.
And those blue eyes, too.
"She uses those who died here to protect herself, you see. She just wants to go on quietly in this land. She's afraid of her existence becoming known. So, Haruka, when you go home, please don't tell anyone about her."
"............ Okay..."
"Good. As long as I've heard that, I can rest easy."
"Um, Sakura-san..."
"Hm?"
"Why did you take Chiga-nee away...?"
At that, Sakura-san hugged her knees tight and brought her face down to them.
And, just like a living person, she drew a deep breath and sighed.
Everything about her was impossible to take for a dead person's behavior.
"As things were going, Chigaya was in danger."
"In danger...?"
"People have been killed every year for the last 10 years, haven't they?"
"The curse-killings, right..."
"Yes. Those are the people who learned too much being killed."
"Learned too much...?"
"As I said, all she wants is to go on quietly here, with no one getting in her way. Which means she sees anyone who threatens her peace as an enemy. Shigatsu Youka―anyone who gets close to her, every one of them..."
"What the hell... so the curse-killings were just Shigatsu Youka killing people, in the end...?"
"That's about it. Shigatsu Youka uses the dead to kill her targets."
"She, in her heart, finds the people of the Arata settlement a nuisance. There's no telling what might give her away. That's why, for decades, there were times she killed people who hadn't long to live and made it look like suicide. She thought she could drive the humans away that way, but... driving off residents rooted in the land isn't so simple."
"Don't tell me, Sakura-san, you killed someone too..."
"I was never made to serve. I only woke yesterday. Chigaya―to rescue her, Eiichiro and I... No―should I say she rescued her? 　Before Chigaya learned too much, she called us. She doesn't kill people without reason either."
"So... that's how it was..."
Even if she doesn't kill without reason...
The curse-killings―that doesn't change the fact that they were selfish murders by Shigatsu Youka.
She just wants to go on quietly, in peace.
For that alone, she went on killing―nine people.
And not just that; apparently she's killed old people and made it look like suicide, too.
There's no way that's forgivable...
But... what is this...
Something's nagging at me...
If that's the case, then Chiga-nee, back there, was―
"Say, Haruka, Chigaya was all right, wasn't she?"
"Huh? 　Oh, yeah, though she threw me on my back out of nowhere..."
"I'm sorry. I expect that was so Shigatsu Youka wouldn't catch on."
"Mom said the same thing. But won't what you're doing get found out? Shigatsu Youka's got you serving her, right?"
"It's fine for now. Shigatsu Youka is busy regenerating Eiichiro's body."
"Regenerating his body?"
"We're dead, after all, so what we can do is limited. Once we've been active for a certain time, our bodies start to rot, so Shigatsu Youka repairs them regularly. At those times, Shigatsu Youka doesn't notice anything I do."
"What a complicated creature..."
"So, Haruka, the next time we meet, I may be your enemy. Shigatsu Youka doesn't forgive anyone who learns she exists."
"What!? Then all of us...! 　Goto and Kana know too! And Natsumi, and Mom...!"
"So that's how it is. The people of the settlement don't seem to know yet, but... I see, so everyone from Motoki-cho knows, and so do my children."
"Hey, Sakura-san! Can't you keep it secret!? 　So Shigatsu Youka doesn't find out...!"
"I could... but if I do anything unnatural, I'm sure she'll kill every one of you."
"Kill us... you mean we'd be turned into cherry trees...?"
"No. Like the curse-killings―no argument, no delay. Turning into a cherry tree takes a little time."
"I'm going back after all! I have to tell everyone this!"
"But it's dangerous. You finally got away..."
"Mom told me to run because I'm no use even if I stay! 　But... I don't want to be the only one who runs! 　I've got a duty to tell everyone what I just heard!"
"But... as I said, the next time we meet, I'll probably be your enemy."
"Then, Sakura-san, do what you can! 　You don't want to see Chiga-nee and Kana done in by Shigatsu Youka either, right!?"
"Haruka... I don't know what I can do, but..."
"I finally found something I can do! 　I'm sure you can too...!"
"Haruka... don't do anything reckless, all right? 　Be careful of Eiichiro especially. He must be somewhere around. By now, her will should be starting to work again, too."
"Dad, huh... Got it! Thanks!"
