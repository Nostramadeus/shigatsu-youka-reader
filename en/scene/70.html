Tampering

Meicho Arc [Clear Sign] · Shiyo 800-03-27 19:00 金 · 63 boxes

That time, in the end, we left Natsumi behind.
There was no way we could take our daughter to a place where we might commit murder.
We met Mifuyu-san, and sure enough, she asked us to kill the two kids bullying Haruka-chan.
And then that horrifying incident...
At the same time, it meant Mifuyu-san now had a hold over us.
After that, at every memorial service, there were orders to kill from Mifuyu-san.
Mifuyu-san also told us not to move away from Motoki-cho.
If we ever tried to move, the distrust of Mifuyu-san's 'someone I can trust' would be stoked as well.
If we ended up out of contact with Mifuyu-san, we'd make an enemy of the Arata settlement.
To let Mifuyu-san know where we were on a regular basis, we had Natsumi and Haruka-chan write letters to each other.
Eventually, Mifuyu-san came back to Motoki-cho.
Apparently people around Mifuyu-san's family found them creepy, and they couldn't stay in Megasawa City anymore.
When I looked into it, Megasawa City had once been a religious city, just as Mifuyu-san had said.
So quite a few people truly believed it was a case of being spirited away, and things had apparently gone far beyond bullying at school.
Coming back to Motoki-cho can't have been what Mifuyu-san wanted, either, but it must have been convenient for keeping watch on our movements.
―And then, that day came.
"A bug in the phones?"
"For Haruka and Natsumi-chan."
"What on earth for..."
"Haruka's too kind, it seems, so she's easy to bully... Natsumi-chan or someone else could turn against Haruka any time. So I need to be able to keep watch on Haruka and the others at all times. Protecting your child is only natural for a mother, isn't it?"
Anger welled up in me, but Mifuyu-san was no longer someone you could talk sense to.
"Then you could just install a bugging app, couldn't you?"
"What are you saying? If we did that, there's no telling when Haruka might find out about the app. Haruka's friend, that girl named Goto... That girl only has to get the least bit suspicious, and it'll come out. All it needs is a function that sends audio data, nothing more. Or are you going to make me a special app from scratch now?"
Given how the phones my company makes are built, I could probably fit a bug inside and send the audio out over the network.
But I didn't have the skills to make software, like an app.
"Let me see... Have it done by April 3, please. If they're presents for moving up a year, it'll seem natural."
"That's only a week from now... Counting the time for testing, two of them is impossible."
"Oh, is that so... Then the bug only needs to go in Haruka's. And please get a phone for that girl Goto as well. Goto, that girl, seems to be very curious, and I want to pull her interest away from Haruka's phone, even a little. If she has the same thing, that takes care of it."
With that, Mifuyu-san set down an envelope with something inside.
"Well, I'll be going. Let me know when it's ready."
With those words, Mifuyu-san left the room.
Inside the envelope was 300,000 yen in cash.
"Damn...!"
I brought my fist down on the envelope.
What we did 10 years ago was a mistake after all! Things are only getting worse and worse!
If this keeps escalating, what will she come out with next?
"I see... So that's what she said..."
That night, I told Akane about what Mifuyu-san had asked of me.
Maybe Akane's senses had gone numb as well. She barely reacted.
"Haruka-chan is the reason I've put up with it, but I can't take any more...! 　It's not just about how I feel. If this goes on, something even more horrible is going to happen...!"
"You're... right."
Again, Akane barely reacted.
Ever since 10 years ago, things between Akane and Mifuyu-san hadn't seemed very good to me.
We've been used however Mifuyu-san pleases.
She holds Haruka-chan and Natsumi hostage and makes us commit murder.
"Ryoji-kun, what do you want to do?"
Then again, it wasn't as if I had a good plan.
And refusing Mifuyu-san's orders was, as they say, out of the question.
From the next day on, when I got home from work, I set to work putting a bug in the phone.
It was my first time doing that kind of work, but once I tried, it wasn't all that hard.
With a small bug fitted in the phone, it could send audio over the network, and the phone could supply the bug with power as well.
But there was one big problem.
It's a technical matter, but if you attach an external device like this, naturally the software side has to support it, too.
The guys in the software department wouldn't have planned for anything this far outside the spec, so new device drivers and the like would have to be prepared.
Unfortunately, I didn't have the skill to make them.
"―Hey, Section Chief Kondo."
"What is it, Section Chief Kogori?"
After a meeting one day ended, I called out to the section chief of the software department.
"Um... Say we built an off-the-shelf bug into the next model. How long would it take the software side to support it?"
"A bug...? 　Ha ha ha! Section Chief Kogori, that's another interesting project from you!"
"Well, uh... I just thought if you could record audio data to an outside server, there might be demand for it as a service..."
"Hmm... I see... But in that case, couldn't you just take one of the bugging apps out there, rename it 'Recording App' or something, and release it? 　If you go to the trouble of building something like that, counting the testing, it'll take more than a month! Ha ha ha!"
"Ha... ha ha ha... Yeah, of course..."
"Besides, even if you can't send the audio in real time, wouldn't it be simpler to record the audio data on the phone and send it off by email?"
"Real-time sending is impossible...?"
"That's right. Whether you need that or not makes all the difference!"
Mifuyu-san probably wouldn't be satisfied unless she could hear the audio in real time.
3 days left until the deadline.
Should I explain the situation to Mifuyu-san and ask for more time...
Even so, for an engineer, nothing could be more humiliating.
Phones made to build a richer society, being used for something like this...!
It seemed my patience, too, was about at its limit.
Our family is being held hostage.
If we could leave Motoki-cho―
If we could get Haruka-chan back―
In that case... what I can do is...
"Just what I'd expect from Ryoji-san. I can't believe how clearly it comes through."
On April 3, Mifuyu-san seemed pleased with the bugged phone she had received.
Just as she'd asked, she could listen to the bugged audio in real time.
The model Mifuyu-san had specified could also be checked from other phones, so you could tell where your child was.
With this, both their whereabouts and their conversations were laid bare.
"But if she turns the GPS off, I won't know where she is, will I?"
"Mifuyu-san, your phone can control the settings remotely. Only on Haruka-chan's, though."
"Is that remote control an app? 　It's on this phone, and it's not something Haruka could find out about, is it?"
"On Haruka-chan's end, I made it a hidden app."
"Hold on, then there's no telling when it'll be found out. That's why I said no bugging app, put the bug itself in―"
"Sorry, but with this deadline, this is the best I can do."
"I see... But if there's any problem, I'll have you modify it."
"............"
"Heh! Some development-department section chief I am!"
"Ryoji-kun, that's enough now..."
That night, I was drowning my sorrows.
Some parent I am! Some engineer!
All I'm doing is being an accomplice to a crime!
Then something rose from the pit of my stomach into my mouth.
I rushed into the bathroom and spewed vomit, still reeking of alcohol, into the toilet bowl.
To escape this vicious cycle, I had to get Haruka-chan back after all.
If we could bring Haruka-chan back and run―
"Ahh, geez! Damn..."
At least so as not to wake Natsumi, I muttered it quietly to myself.
"Ryoji-kun, I have a suggestion..."
"Huh...?"
I turned my unfocused eyes toward Akane.
"Let's take Haruka-chan in, here with us."
"............ Huh?"
"No, even if we have to force her, let's take her with us and run away from Motoki-cho..."
"............ Ha ha ha! Ha ha ha ha ha ha..."
"Ryoji-kun..."
"Akane... so you were thinking the same thing after all."
It seemed the matter was already settled.
As long as Haruka-chan was by Mifuyu-san's side, we couldn't leave Motoki-cho.
If she started talking about dying together again―
This is a woman who has had six people killed so far.
I couldn't see even dying together as a mere bluff anymore.
To do that, we had to pull Haruka-chan's heart away from Mifuyu-san.
The truth was, I had tampered with the phone I gave Mifuyu-san for Haruka-chan.
It was a trick I came up with precisely because I couldn't make it to her specs.
What Mifuyu-san had asked for was not a bugging app but a built-in bug.
Because with a bugging app, that genius girl might find out.
A bug, being hardware, would never show up on the surface.
But unfortunately, that was impossible with my skills.
So I went back on Mifuyu-san's order and planted a bugging app, one of the kind going around out there.
Of course, it's a hidden app, so the chance Mifuyu-san feared, 'the genius girl finding out the bugging app exists,' is low.
So I did some tampering.
First, I swapped the battery for an old one.
That model boasts a high-capacity battery, but Haruka-chan's phone will probably run out of charge in about a day.
Even if I had actually built in a bug, the same thing should happen.
Because the bug would steal power from the battery.
I did one more piece of tampering.
Inside the casing, I put about 10 grams of insulating metal.
Just a piece of metal, with no function whatsoever.
In other words, what I'm aiming for is―
"Hey, Goto, my phone's battery runs down weirdly fast."
"Mine lasts 2 days with no trouble, though. Maybe you've been watching too many dirty videos?"
"A-are you an idiot!? I watch that kind of thing on the computer!"
"What!? You seriously watch dirty videos!?"
"No! I mean when I wanna watch videos, I use the computer!"
"It's the same model as mine, just a different color. And a battery can't suddenly get old... Is something eating the battery...?"
"What's that supposed to mean..."
"Senpai, please let me weigh your phone."
"160 grams. Being 10 grams heavier than normal can't be a margin of error."
"What's that mean... There's a 10-gram machine inside?"
"In a case like this, the likely answer is... a bug."
"A-a bug!? Who'd do something like that...!"
"It means someone wants to watch Niimura-senpai's every move, all the time."
"No way! Someone like that... Could it be..."
"Anyone come to mind?"
"My mom... Mom kept pestering the teacher at the parent-teacher meeting about whether I was being bullied at school, so..."
"Even so, a bug is going a bit too far..."
"Even if it is Mom, a bug really puts me off... It's creepy..."
"The way things look, it might not be just a bug. Security cameras in your room, maybe even sneaked into the homes of friends you hang out with..."
"You're kidding, right...?"
"With this kind of thing, the more you do, the more anxious you get. It's bound to keep escalating."
"Hey, Goto! Let me stay at your place for a while!"
"But maybe my house has one, too..."
"Give me a break...! How am I supposed to get away from Mom?"
Even if I had actually built in a bug, nearly the same thing should happen.
The bug eats power, and the phone gets about 10 grams heavier.
Even if Mifuyu-san weighed the phone just in case, she'd think there really was a device inside.
With this, I can make Mifuyu-san believe a bug really has been planted.
Besides, Mifuyu-san shouldn't notice the poor battery life right away.
It's a flaw only someone who has used it for several days would notice.
This should make Haruka-chan feel that something is off.
A warning from me, made possible by giving Natsumi and the genius girl the same model as well.
I took Mifuyu-san's order and turned it against her to rig this trap.
Even so, whether they'll pick up on tampering like this is doubtful...
It's a truly trivial, very slight hint from me.
All I can do is bet on the genius she showed me 10 years ago.
"Well... guess I'll get to bed..."
It was a small hope, but once I could see it, my heart felt suddenly lighter.
I straightened my still-unsteady legs like sticks and stood up.
"Akane..."
"Hm?"
"You're the best wife in the world to me."
"Huh?"
"Good night."
I whipped my drunken head into action and sat down at the desk.
I got out paper and a pen and wrote down what I intended to do tomorrow.
Tomorrow, I'll meet with Mifuyu-san.
I'll negotiate about Haruka-chan.
I don't expect to get results right away just by talking tomorrow.
I'll negotiate little by little, over a number of days.
In the meantime, the tampering on that smartphone will come to light, and―
But the one I'm up against is Mifuyu-san.
She shows a cunning like a born criminal's and always makes her move a step ahead of us.
Maybe my life is in danger, too, but...
But for us to break out of this life, this is all that's left.
I'll do everything in my power that I can do now.
For Akane, and for my two daughters.
But...
Who on earth...
