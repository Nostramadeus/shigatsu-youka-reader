The Charm

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-07 20:00 火 · 24 boxes

"Now, things are really heating up. Next person, please."
Obaasan picked the doll at the left end.

Haruka Niimura

"Me, huh..."
"Uh, what should I talk about...? It's tough going after Goto..."
Everyone was staring at a wryly smiling Haruka.
"A-ahem. Then I'll begin."
"When I was little, I got really into charms. I got especially interested in curses. Back then I felt like a black magician."
―I think it was before I started second grade.
I was looking through the charm books in the school library when I found a book I'd never seen before.
A big book with a hard cover that didn't belong on an elementary-school bookshelf.
When I opened it...
Pictures of an old woman in a robe taking care of frogs, pictures of scenes where magic was being used.
I couldn't read the kanji, but I figured this was a book of curses.
I was having fun looking at the pictures, but on one of the pages, I found a piece of paper tucked in.
On that paper, something was written in a kid's handwriting.
'A charm to help someone you want to help,' it said.
I thought maybe there was a way to help people.
When I had the teacher in the library read me the page the paper was tucked into,
"If there is someone you want to help, do what is written on this page. But if you cannot help that person, you may die 10 years later."
is apparently what it said.
If I did this charm, Mom, who was sick back then, might get better.
But I might die before I grew up.
I was scared, but I came to want to help her no matter what.
I hesitated, but I decided to do it.
The method was simple. Picture your best friend's face, draw the emblem from the book on a piece of paper, and keep it with you the whole time.
The emblem was something even a kid could draw.
That night, I had a strange dream.
The Goddess appeared, wrapped in dazzling light.
I gazed at the sight, entranced.
And then the Goddess said:
"First, help the friend you love. Then your mother will be saved as well."
That was the strange dream.
But that friend lived far away.
I got a little disappointed.
But a few days after that, when I woke up in the morning,
that friend was sleeping next to me.
I was surprised, but happy.
But my memory of what came after is kinda patchy.
What I vaguely remember is my friend getting attacked by someone, and me shielding my friend and getting hit on the head.
Dream or no dream, I was just desperate to protect my friend.
After we came back to Motoki-cho, Mom got better and better, my friend is right nearby, and I'm really happy now.
I decided I'd use charms to help people rather than to curse them.
"That friend, of course, is Natsumi. Thanks to Natsumi coming to me back then, Mom got better, and I get to be here like this. Thanks, Natsumi."
Obasan was shedding big tears.
Everyone else was sniffling a little too.
I barely remember it myself, but it seems I was of some help to Haruka, and that honestly makes me happy.
"Uuu... Niimura-senpai... I'm so glad for you..."
Goto, not to be outdone by Obasan, started crying too.
But wasn't this just a nice story, not a ghost story?
"Zzz... zzz..."
Kana-chan had fallen asleep at some point; maybe it was boring for her.
"I've heard a lot about how close Natsumi-chan and Haruka are, for a long time now. You two stay good friends from here on, too."
Obaasan said it with a smile.
