The Empty Scales

Eden no Sakura Arc [Eden's Cherry] · Shiyo 800-03-29 19:00 火 · 44 boxes

"Mm... Auntie, there's still a lot I need to tell you. I'll tell you everything I've seen and everything I've done."
I was gazing steadily at Auntie, a complete change from before, and Auntie looked a little taken aback.
Then Auntie stood up and sat down again right across from me.
And she drank down her lukewarm tea.
"What you did, Chigaya-chan, might be just the tip of the iceberg when you look at the curse-killings as a whole. Chigaya-chan, if you did it all on your own, it's just too big a story, somehow..."
"Mm... I think so too. It feels like everything connects, somehow... And like there's someone pulling the strings behind it all..."
"Someone pulling the strings?"
"Mm... I can't really explain it..."
"Chigaya-chan, even if there were someone like that, you mustn't go chasing after them right off. First, start with what we know, one step at a time."
"Mm... You're right..."
"Then... I know it's hard to talk about, but Chigaya-chan, could you tell me what you know, in detail?"
I took a deep breath and called up those hateful memories.
The curse-killings I committed from Shiyo 791 through 795.
Mom's death, and the old man's suicide: to make them look connected to those, I carried them all out in April.
"Mm... It started with that time 10 years ago when Mom ran off somewhere crying, remember? 　I saw the person who cut off Mom's head that day."
"What!?"
"Mm... The one who cut off her head was Dad. Because Grandma couldn't save Mom when she was about to fall off the cliff. Dad cut off her head to cover up Grandma's crime, so it would look like an obvious murder. Dad seems convinced that Grandma pushed her, but Grandma never said a word in her own defense."
"W-wait a minute, Chigaya-chan! 　Are you... really sure about that?"
"Yeah... I clearly remember what the two of them said, and I saw the moment Mom fell, and the moment her head was cut off..."
"Then, Chigaya-chan, how did you find Sakura-san and your grandmother?"
"Huh? What do you mean, how... That time, I was walking along carrying Kana, and..."
"That mountain stream runs for kilometers, doesn't it? 　Wouldn't it be pretty hard to walk that cliff-like riverbank carrying Kana-chan?"
"Mm... But the place was right by the graveyard clearing, so I don't think it's strange that I just happened to find them..."
No, wait a minute.
Did I find them by chance...?
I don't think that's quite it.
How did I find them...?
That time...


"She's over that way."
"That way!!"
Maybe because I'd shouted so suddenly, Auntie's body gave a jolt.
"W-what's wrong?"
"Mm... I was told 'that way'! 　Someone told me Mom was over that way!"
"Someone, meaning who...?"
"Mm... I don't know that part, but..."
"Listen, Chigaya-chan, stay calm. I've still only heard a little of what you've told me, but from where I stand, it's full of strange things. Will you answer just a few more of my questions?"
"Mm... All right..."
"Kotaro-san never asked your grandmother why she pushed Sakura-san off?"
"Yeah..."
"Kotaro-san still believes your grandmother pushed her, then?"
"Yeah..."
"So the only ones who know about it are Kotaro-san, your grandmother, and you?"
"Yeah..."
Having said that much, Auntie stopped talking and reached for her water, its ice all melted.
"Don't you think that's strange? 　At the end, your grandmother grabbed Sakura-san's hand and tried to pull her up, right? 　How could that look like pushing her off?"
Now that she mentioned it...
It really was strange.
Pushing someone off and trying to pull them up should be completely different motions, however you look at it.
No matter how you look at it, that doesn't look like pushing.
Misread it...?
Dad would have been watching it from below the cliff, so it was possible he'd misread it.
But back then, Dad told Grandma without a moment's hesitation that she'd 'pushed Mom off.'
Besides, if he was watching, he should have been able to shout before she fell, to stop Grandma or something.
And yet, after Mom fell, he appeared as if he'd timed it, and cut off her head.
To me, more baffling by far than the chance he misread it is what Dad did.
Almost as if he'd known Mom would fall―
"Maybe this incident really is 'something like a curse'..."
"Mm... A curse? But that's..."
"But Sakura-san and Natsumi-chan have the ability to hear impossible voices, don't they? 　It's only normal to think something's off right there, isn't it?"
"Mm... Impossible voices... Oh, right, Auntie, the truth is... I have the same ability."
"What!? Chigaya-chan, you too!?"
"Mm... I'm sorry I never told you..."
"I see... If you're Sakura-san's child, it's no wonder you'd awaken to a power like that..."
"Mm... Natsumi-chan isn't Mom's child too, is she...?"
"That's not possible. I was there at the birth."
That I'd inherit Mom's power, I can still understand, but is there something else in common that lets people gain the ability?
At this point there's far too little information.
"Mm... In that case, trying to push the deduction any further might just be a waste of effort."
"You're right. At times like this, it's so reassuring to have Goto-chan around. But just in case, I'd like to hear everything you know."
"Mm... Okay. Then let's set the deduction aside for now and put sharing what we know first."
"I'll just nod along, then. If I butt in, the story will stall again."
At 10, I tried to kill Mom, and a few days later the murderous intent suddenly vanished.
A few days after that, Auntie and Haruka came to Arata, and that same day Mom died.
The next year, I stabbed to death Mrs. Shirozaki, who'd suspected Dad.
After that, I killed one person each year, five in all.
This proved that confining Dad and watching him had been pointless.
And as for the curse-killings of these last four years, I had nothing at all to do with them.
Who did them was completely unknown.
I wanted to stop the curse-killings that had taken on a life of their own just as much.
Though that was presumptuous, coming from the one who'd killed first...
"Thank you, Chigaya-chan, for telling me. But, Chigaya-chan... No matter how I think about it, this story is strange."
"Mm... You're right. For Dad to swear Grandma pushed her..."
"That's not all. You didn't do the curse-killings of these last four years, right? 　Even if someone else did them, how did they do it?"
"Mm... What do you mean?"
"You could sneak up on someone without them noticing and use a knife... that much you could do, but is there anyone else in the settlement who could do the same?"
Now that she mentioned it, she was right.
About the only one who could move like me was Kana.
But Kana had no motive to cause a curse-killing.
Could it be that Dad noticed my curse-killings and had Kana do them...?
In that case Kana would have done her first curse-killing at 6, and that's surely impossible.
Was there anyone else who could copy my methods?
Grandma was athletic too, but she couldn't move the way I could.
Besides, by that logic, Grandma would have to know that I'm the curse-killer.
Grandma is always racking her brains over how to solve the case, though.
Grandma covering for me would be a different story, but then it would all just be a put-up job, and that would be the end of it.
Things shouldn't be that simple.
"Chigaya-chan, do you know how the people who were curse-killed died?"
"Mm... They were stabbed with a knife, weren't they?"
Why was she asking something so obvious?
There was no way I, the one who'd done it, wouldn't know that.
No way I wouldn't know...
She was going out of her way to ask me that.
"Chigaya-chan, no matter how I look at it, it's strange. The truth is, the bodies of the curse-killed―"
W-wait, wait...!
Auntie was trying to say something, and I knew what.
I stabbed them to death with a knife.
And yet―
"The bodies of the curse-killed―



had their heads smashed in with a blunt instrument."
My mind went blank.
For nine years I'd carried the sin of killing― scales tipped by a rigged trial of my own making.
I was a slave to those scales.
The scales whispered to me on the sly.
"Hey, why are you carrying me? 　But you've come this far―keep carrying me forever, why don't you."
Like a cuckoo's egg left in another bird's nest, the scales just kept brazenly riding on my back.
Without a shred of shame, it whispered to me, grinning, with its foul breath.
"You've always believed you killed five people, haven't you? 　Did you really kill them? Wasn't it a dream?"
That was a dream...?
That can't be...
"Then is your aunt lying? 　Which is the lie―that you killed them, or her story? Which do you think?"
Auntie had no reason to lie.
Then had I just been mistaken...?
"Chigaya-chan, are you okay?"
"............"
Inside my heart, I checked my answers against myself.
The scales inside me were reading out their own charges.
And the charges were― empty.
"Uh... this is going back to square one, but are you sure you killed five people...?"
"Mm... Then who else but me could have secretly killed five whole people? 　Besides, smashing heads in with a blunt instrument is harder than stabbing with a knife. Who on earth would kill like that..."
"You're right... Stabbing a vital spot with a knife is easier, and kills for sure."
"Mm... Auntie, let me meet this Goto girl as soon as you can."
"Goto-chan?"
"Mm... The thirteenth-year memorial begins in nine days. Before then, I want to gather as much information as I can and sort it out."
"You're right... From here on, it looks like we'll need Goto-chan's help."
"Mm... But... Natsumi-chan and I have to be kept from ever meeting face to face. If that murderous intent welled up again..."
"All right, don't worry about that. Goto-chan―I'll explain the situation to her and try to arrange a meeting."
How far could this Goto girl widen my view, with nothing but my vague memories and subjective information?
The battle had already begun.
