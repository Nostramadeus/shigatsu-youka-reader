We put down roots in the land named Arata and worked day after day to clear it.
Fleeing that terrible war, months crossing steep mountains―
we wanted, whatever it took, to make this land we had finally found rich.
That feeling spurred us on.

Nii-sama, the Emperor's son, had given up his rank and worked day after day to make this land richer than any other.
He took up farm tools himself and led the way in the hard work and the dirty work alike.
Everyone came to trust Nii-sama, and day by day the land grew rich.
In the same way, I felt my own heart growing richer day by day.
I was 11 then, and in time I grew toward womanhood.
There were times Nii-sama treated me as he had when I was his attendant, and times he suddenly treated me as a woman.

Old enough that there would be nothing strange about marrying.

They said my family had once been of some standing too, but from my grandparents' generation on, it had fallen into complete decline.

"Itohime, are you all right? Your face is red."

Why was I so flustered?

The voices of small birds threaded their way through the new spring buds.
This silence was so long.
In time with my heartbeat, white breath rose through the gaps of the hands covering my mouth.
The cold air chilled the back of my throat, but inside, my body was so hot.
So hot, so hot―it hurt.

"Well, I'm leaving the rest to you."

The voices of Nii-sama and Tsunamune-sama.

Before dawn, the sky still dark.
A cold snap, I suppose you would call it; it was as cold as midwinter.

There was only one road leading out of this settlement.

Freezing air, nothing like spring. A thin skin of ice had formed over the puddles.
