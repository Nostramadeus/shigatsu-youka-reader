Jusatsu Arc [Killing by Curse] · Shiyo 800-04-08 08:20 水 · 77 boxes

"Welcome! Miss April Sickness!" [TN: A pun on gogatsu-byo, 'May sickness,' Japan's term for the May blues.]
Obasan had probably only just been seen off. In the entryway, already standing there, was Haruka.
"Really, Haruka, don't drag me back to reality."
"Ahaha, sorry, sorry! But you seem to be in pretty good shape today."
"Yeah, I'm feeling great today!"
"Sit wherever, I'll make tea now. It's supposedly luxury stuff from la France."
"Ooh, hospitality, huh. And this is from me, a care package!"
"Let's see, let's see, chocolate and sakiika. I see... With fancy tea, sakiika is the obvious pick, right― Like that makes any sense!"
"That's one dead-straight play-along retort...!"
"Also, look! A DVD!"
"Oh! Isn't this the one we went to see together last year? You bought it?"
"Yeah. Apparently the DVD has lots of the parts that got cut from the theater version."
"Oho, well, well. It has been a long time since I last had the pleasure."
Haruka bowed deeply to the DVD.
Today Haruka was awfully high-energy.
But I know this is Haruka being considerate.
In this season, my mood tends to sink.
So that I can feel even a little happier, Haruka treats me to a hospitality that goes beyond the tea.
"Oh, Natsumi, can you grab the teacups?"
"Okaaay!"
I took two teacups and set them next to the gas stove, where the kettle sat.
"By the way, Haruka, what did the school-closure notice you got say?"
"The email said it was a notice from the police."
"Same as my class. I've always wondered, but isn't this kind of thing usually sent out to the whole school?"
"Ah, my mom said something about that. Apparently it's to spread out the points of contact for inquiries."
"Spread-out-the-points-of-contact...? What's that?"
"If they sent the email to the whole school, we'd all try to ask the sender questions and stuff, right? 　Then handling it would be a pain, so they send the email class by class and have us contact our homeroom teacher directly, supposedly."
"Is that... efficient...?"
"Beats me..."
"Seems like every teacher's email would say something different, though. Is that really okay..."
"If it works out fine this way, isn't that good enough? 　Oh, right, you asked what the email that came around to my class said."
"Mmm, for safety's sake, don't go outside, it said."
"For... safety's sake?"
Mom's email had no such wording as for safety's sake in it.
"So that means 'it's dangerous outside,' right?"
When I said that, Haruka's hands stopped dead.
"Yeah... guess it does. So it's not an accident or something, it's an incident after all?"
The voice of Haruka, so upbeat until then, dropped an octave.
"And the danger is still out there, which means..."
My own voice naturally dropped too.
"Terrorism or something? Like poison gas getting spread around."
"In a little town of only 40,000 people? Even then, they'd surely put out an evacuation order or something... Maybe a wanted criminal is hiding out here?"
"Then maybe they'd take a hostage to get away?"
The fun mood from a moment ago was growing subtly stiff.
"Sorry, Natsumi, just in case, check that the front door's locked."
"Yeah..."
I went to the entryway, checked that it was locked, and then, to be safe, put the chain on too.
"It was fine. The locks are all set."
"Thanks! Natsumi, did you lock up properly when you left your place?"
"Yeah, our place is fine."
"I see."
Haruka picked up the hot kettle and poured the water into the teapot.
How can I put it, the atmosphere was strange.
Earlier, when I went out with the trash, I had sensed a slightly tense air too.
In this town, what was happening―
"Here! Carry the tea over for me, please!"
Haruka spoke in a cheerful voice, as if trying to break the stiff air.
I set the tea on the living room table and sat down on the sofa.
"Okay, let's watch it!"
Haruka had my DVD in her hand before I knew it.
There was a laptop next to the TV, and the DVD went into its disc drive.
It was set up to show the picture from the computer on the TV.
Haruka's house is where, whenever we watch DVDs or surf the internet, we always use the computer on the big screen like this.
I looked at the clock: 8:30 a.m.
Normally, this was when I would be getting to school.
Haruka, once she had finished setting everything up, sank deep into the sofa and snuggled right up against me.
While we watched the DVD, we didn't talk at all.
It wasn't that today's strange air had swallowed us. We were simply absorbed in the DVD.
Though Haruka was sucking on sakiika the whole time...
―3 hours later, the end credits on the screen.
Right to the very end, both of us had been able to immerse ourselves in the world of the DVD.
"Ahh, that was fun! It really is the best!"
"Seeing the parts that got cut, it finally feels like everything adds up!"
"I know, I know! Why'd they cut them from the movie?"
"Still, the girl who was the main character really was cute."
"Ha! Right?! 　'Be reborn as a pure devil! Devil Justice!'"
With that, Haruka imitated the heroine and snapped into a pose.
"Ooh, Haruka, you're good! Were you practicing, maybe?"
"Huh!? 　N-n-n-no way! Ahahahahaha!"
Haruka is easy to read at times like this, or rather, simple.
To put it kindly, she's very honest.
"Then, then, do the rival girl! Come on, do the Dark Seraph!"
"O-okay, fine! 'My darkness... Sleep within my bosom... See, it feels so very good, doesn't it...?'"
"W-wow, Haruka! That's exactly like her! Why have you been hiding a talent like that all this time!?"
"It's not like... I've just got a relative who likes doing impressions, that's all."
"Then my next request is..."
"Gaah! That's enough already! M-more importantly, Natsumi, what do you want for lunch?"
"My mom left me money. Said to have lunch with Natsumi-chan."
Haruka fluttered a 5,000-yen bill.
"5,000 yen's worth? That much?"
"Since we've got it, let's get some gorgeous pizza delivered! It'll go better with tea than sakiika, for sure!"
"Haruka, you really do love pizza... Pizza, huh... I haven't had any in a while."
"All right, it's decided!"
Haruka went online on the computer.
It looked like she meant to order the pizza online.
"Um... Huh?"
Holding the mouse, Haruka's hand stopped moving, and so did her eyes.
One look at the screen and I understood why.
"H-hey, isn't this... this town?"
"Yeah..."
At the very top of the search service's page was the headline of the top news story.
Sawa Prefecture, Motoki-cho: Sex-Unknown Brutalized Body Found

A chill went through me, as if an icicle had been stabbed down my spine, and goosebumps covered my whole body.
Motoki-cho as a name might exist in a few places across the country, but in Sawa Prefecture, this is the only one.
"Natsumi... I'm gonna look at the news."
"Yeah..."
Haruka slowly moved the mouse pointer to the news link and gently clicked.
The linked page had lines of text and a single photo.
The photo showed many cherry trees, with the center covered by a blue tarp.
It looked like it had been taken from above, by helicopter or drone.
I read the article...
At around 5 a.m. on April 8, the body of a brutal murder victim was found in Central Park in Motoki-cho, Sawa Prefecture.
The person who found the body reported that
"Someone is calling for help in Central Park,"
and when paramedics and police rushed to the scene, a person in cardiopulmonary arrest was found lying there.
Because the body is severely damaged, identification is expected to take time.
The cause of death has not been determined, but the possibility of suicide is considered extremely low.
"A brutalized body asked for help?"
Before I knew it, Haruka was gripping my hand tightly.
"So the person was badly hurt but not dead yet, and died around the time the police got there?"
"It doesn't even say the victim's sex. Severe damage means... so bad you can't even tell the sex...? 　And still alive, even so..."
―For a while, silence flowed between the two of us.
An incident like this, in a neighborhood close enough to walk to...
I was gripping Haruka's hand tightly too.
"Just a sec, okay?"
Haruka opened her bookmarks and went to a different page.
The page title was "Motoki High Board."
Our high school's message board.
"We might find the latest info here."
"Hey, stop it... It's scary..."
"Huh? Oh, sorry."
"Eep!"
At the sudden ringtone of a smartphone, I let out a short shriek.
"Natsumi, you okay?"
"Yeah..."
It was my smartphone ringing. Mom was calling.
"Hello...?"
"Ah, hello, Natsumi? Getting along okay with Haruka-chan?"
Unlike the mood on our end, a cheerful voice came back.
"Oh, yeah, we were watching a DVD together at Haruka's place."
"I see. Sorry, but I'll be home late today."
"Huh...!? Why...?"
The thought of being alone tonight filled me with a tremendous anxiety.
After such a frightening incident, to be alone tonight...
Reacting to my voice, Haruka looked at me with worry.
"We decided to go out drinking with people from work. Sorry, but please look after the house till tonight! Look forward to a souvenir!"
"Okay. But..."
"What's wrong?"
"Don't you know about the incident?"
"Incident? 　Oh, you mean this morning? Sorry, my job keeps me on my feet all day, so I haven't heard a thing. Oh! My phone's about to die!"
"O-okay... I'll look after the house."
The moment I finished saying it, the call cut off.
The truth was, I wanted her to come home soon.
I couldn't tell her to come home because I was scared.
"Natsumi... you okay?"
"............"
I couldn't answer, and could only hang my head.
"Mom says she'll be home late..."
"I see. Well, grown-ups have all kinds of stuff going on. You can stay here till she gets back."
"Okay, thanks..."
"There, there, nothing to be scared of."
Haruka gently hugged my head.
It's always like this. I'm always leaning on Haruka, and I can never give anything back.
At times like this, even though there's only a year between us, I feel that Haruka is the Onee-san.
"Sorry. Always, always."
"Don't worry about it... We all get in a pinch sometimes."
"Yeah..."
Someone, who and from where I didn't know, had been killed.
That kind of thing is on the news sometimes.
But does an incident happening nearby really make you this anxious?
Or am I just prone to anxiety because it's cherry blossom season?
If so, then everyone else is living normally, and I'm the only one this anxious.
I'm causing Haruka an awful lot of trouble right now.
"Natsumi, you don't have to worry about a thing. I'm here, so it's okay."
Haruka said it gently and stroked my head.
Between fear, happiness and feeling pathetic, I was about to cry.
"Come on, Natsumi... lie down for a bit."
Still holding my head in her arms, she laid me down on the sofa.
"Natsumi, can I watch that DVD again?"
"Huh? Yeah, sure."
"Yes! It's fun no matter how many times you watch it."
Haruka quickly closed the browser and started the DVD.
"Oh, and if you get bored, you can just go ahead and sleep a little. You're tired, right?"
Oh, I see... She said she wanted to watch the DVD again so that I could rest.
That way, she must have thought, I wouldn't have to feel indebted for Haruka's consideration.
"Okay... thanks..."
I did as Haruka said and closed my eyes right away.
