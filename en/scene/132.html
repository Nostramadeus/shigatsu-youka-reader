An Unplanned Murder

Meicho Arc [Clear Sign] · Shiyo 800-04-08 14:10 水 · 37 boxes

"Senpai, this is where it gets hard. Now we have to sort out today's case and the paper dolls."
She was right. Reasoning out the past cases meant nothing if we didn't solve today's.
"There are still a lot of things that don't make sense. Who the shinigami really is, the reason for killing the Kogori family, the bug, and how the Megasawa City case was pulled off like a spiriting-away. If we don't look into these fast, we probably won't be able to escape the culprit."
"Huh? Isn't the shinigami in this case Mom?"
"That's very likely, but not necessarily, is what I mean. But if we follow that assumption through and hit no contradictions, I think we can say it's almost certain."
Goto was objective through and through.
She brought feelings in only where the culprit's motive was concerned.
Even when she called something a fixed assumption, she wasn't 100% fixed on it.
She kept building on her thinking while always doubting her own reasoning.
That was Goto's way.
"Then:
the shinigami this time = Niimura-senpai's mom,
the shinigami in the past = Kogori-senpai's parents.
That's the premise we'll work from. The spiriting-away method must be some kind of trick, so I'll set it aside for now."
"Got it. No objections."
"What I think is... isn't it possible that this time, Niimura-senpai's mom wasn't planning to commit a crime at all?"
What is she saying all of a sudden? Now she's defending Mom?
"If the killing had been planned from the start, wouldn't she have made the paper dolls at an earlier stage? No, to be precise, wouldn't she make sure Niimura-senpai 'noticed' the paper dolls?"
"Hard to say... If it were like that, I'd take Natsumi away before April 8, somewhere with nobody around... No, maybe somewhere with lots of people around would be better instead... True, if it followed the Megasawa City case, the paper dolls would show up in front of me at least a week before the crime. So does that mean the culprit got into a situation where the paper dolls had to be made in a rush?"
If the paper dolls had shown up sooner, it might have been useless, but I'd have prepared carefully to save Natsumi no matter what.
But the shinigami had always seen its crimes through in Megasawa City, so doing that might have been useless anyway...
In that case, the shinigami would be confident of seeing it through this time too, so making me aware of the paper dolls days in advance should be the right move.
To begin with, showing me the paper dolls at all was a big risk for the culprit.
What need could there be to show me the paper dolls, even at that risk...
Was it simply to make me believe it was a curse?
"Goto, you're right. If the paper dolls had been there earlier... how do I put it... it'd feel more real. For me, at least..."
"But there's something that contradicts this. A bug was planted in your phone. That was probably preparation for the crime. We were given these phones 5 days ago, on April 3. So the preparations were underway at least 5 days ago."
It did show planning, and that contradicted the paper dolls that had suddenly appeared this morning.
"And then declaring the Kogori family the targets. Still, I think there's a motive. The parents are in the way because they know about the past cases, and Kogori-senpai is your dad's enemy."
"Ugh, it's all tangled up, I don't get it..."
Planning, motive, targets―none of it matched up.
"The Megasawa City case was that carefully prepared, so why has it come to such an inconsistent situation― or rather, a situation that's fallen apart? Isn't it likely that something the culprit didn't expect happened, and the plan had to be changed?"
I see. Not everything goes according to plan. If we assume some kind of trouble came up...
"On this, I'd like to hear your opinion too, Niimura-senpai. The culprit is your mom, the ones who did the killing in the Megasawa City case are Kogori-senpai's parents. On that premise, where on earth did the trouble come in..."
"Let's see... Natsumi's dad being the only one killed bothers me a little. Natsumi's mom staying alive means she could confess the past to the police at any time."
When I said that, Goto silently took notes.
"I see, killing only one of them is far too risky. Kogori-senpai's mom, to protect her daughter, probably wouldn't even balk at confessing."
"Right? Hm? No..."
If Natsumi's mom told the police, what would happen to Natsumi?
Daughter of a sick killer: that brand would be publicly stamped on her.
Then society would look at her strangely, and life might get hard for her.
If I were Natsumi's mom, even if I went to the police, I wouldn't want to say anything until the very last moment.
Instead, I'd do whatever best made sure Natsumi stayed safe.
For example, keeping her with someone trustworthy―with me―the whole time.
The victim still wasn't confirmed, so even if she wanted the police to protect Natsumi, it'd be hard to give a reason.
If the victim wasn't Natsumi's dad, she'd just be stepping on a land mine.
Getting the police to protect Natsumi would have to wait until the victim was confirmed.
"Hey, Goto... Killing just one of them makes sense after all. Natsumi's mom wouldn't want to confess, right? Not when she thinks about Natsumi... Isn't the culprit counting on that too?"
Goto didn't answer, just added to her notes.
That was also her answer: it was very useful.
"I see, I see... There's a limit to how far this angle can take us."
"Like you said earlier, Goto, if the culprit never meant to lay a hand on the Kogori family... The bug was already prepared, right? So the three of them did have a plan to team up and kill someone, didn't they?"
Goto wrote down what I was saying partway, then her hand stopped dead.
Then she set her notebook and pen down on the table, almost like she was tossing them.
"Goto, what's wrong?"
"Senpai... that... what that means... do you understand...?"
"Huh...?"
Someone they wanted to kill besides the Kogori family...
In my phone... a bug...
No, that can't be...!
The real target... no way!
"Don't be ridiculous! 　Mom and the others... me...!?"
Goto didn't move, still holding her head, her shoulders trembling.
"Senpai... this is... This is all... just my own reckless reasoning, so... there's a real chance I'm wrong. So..."
Clenching my fists, I sat back down in my chair.
"Goto... Sorry for yelling. After you thought so hard about it..."
I felt like I was going to lose my mind too.
Mom might be trying to kill me.
"Senpai... can I say one more thing?"
What now... What more could she possibly have to say?
"Say it's just as we reasoned, and the culprit achieves every goal... What do you think the culprit will do after that...?"
Goto's voice was still shaking.
Something that terrible was what Goto was predicting.
Even high-school girls like us could guess who the culprit was.
With crimes this patched-together, being traced was only a matter of time.
If the culprit had gone this far to force the murders through...
"Suicide... huh..."
Goto slowly nodded.
How could this happen... If Natsumi and I try to save ourselves, Mom ends up dying...!
"Goto, let's go to the police!"
"We can't!"
"Why not!? We need the police to catch Mom, fast!"
"We can't!　Your mom's already prepared to die! 　You'd better assume that if she's about to be caught, she's ready to die on the spot!"
"Then what do we do!?"
A ringtone sounded.
Goto pulled out Natsumi's phone.
"Yes, yes, this is Goto! 　Oh, Senpai's mom!? Kogori-senpai's in the restroom at Zahha right now, pushing as hard as she can! 　Yes, yes, understood! I'll pass it on!"
Goto answered cheerfully and hung up.
"It was a number from the police station, last four digits '0110.' [TN: 110 is the police emergency number in Japan.] They've probably identified the victim as Kogori-senpai's dad... And they want Kogori-senpai to come to the police station right away."
Dammit! That had just proven our reasoning right!
Which means Mom killing herself could really happen.
What do I do... What do I...
"Let's meet your mom. Even if the original target was you, Niimura-senpai, the three of them have definitely fallen out among themselves. It's better to assume the case is moving on your mom's say-so alone."
So the idea is to talk her out of it.
Already, the kind Mom I had inside me was gone.
I remembered the face Mom had when she left this morning.
That blank, emotionless face she'd shown me last thing this morning.
And one baffling remark.
"You and Natsumi-chan, get along."
What could that have meant?
