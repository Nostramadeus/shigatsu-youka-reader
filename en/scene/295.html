Moonlight on the Rafters ~Substitute~

Eden no Sakura Arc [Eden's Cherry] · Shiyo 800-04-07 22:10 火 · 68 boxes

My resolve is different from before.
I have something I must do.
I have to tell them.
What Shigatsu Youka is thinking―
If this keeps up, we're going to fall prey to Shigatsu Youka for sure.
Knowing what Shigatsu Youka really is puts the Motoki-cho group, Chiga-nee and Kana in particular danger.
I know full well how dangerous it is to head back down this path.
But I don't want a single one of them to die...!
If I'm the only one who survives, then I...!
Will Mom yell at me when I get back?
I'm not scared of that one bit.
Everyone vanishing is way scarier.
If I get yelled at, I'll just yell right back at Mom.
What I'm doing is right―my selfish justice is surely right.
"Hm?"
When I reached the spot where the landslide had been, I could see figures far off ahead.
Just in case, I ducked out of sight.
If it's Chiga-nee, I'll just cause her trouble again.
Besides, I don't want to get thrown again...
That's Mom, Natsumi, and Kana, is it...
What happened to Goto? Isn't she with them?
Is she still reading books in the storehouse?
From the look of it, Chiga-nee must have steered Natsumi and Kana this way.
Then Goto should be coming this way soon too.
If things go well, maybe all of us can escape down this mountain path.
I'd have to force a blindfold and earplugs on Natsumi, though...
At times like this, Natsumi being watched by Shigatsu Youka really is the sticking point.
As I'd expected, the three of them took the fork toward the abandoned hut.
Now, what should I do?
If what Sakura-san said earlier is right, there are corpses wandering around.
On top of that, Sakura-san said that next time she'd be our enemy too.
We in the Motoki-cho group, plus Chiga-nee and Kana, will be Shigatsu Youka's first targets.
If corpses really are wandering around, I absolutely can't let them find me.
Being found by a corpse is the same as being found by Shigatsu Youka.
What happens if I do get found?
Will the corpses come after me?
Or does it pull some physical attack, like suddenly sprouting a cherry tree under your feet?
Well, either way, nothing good's going to come of it.
At the very least, this is a thing that kills one person a year without anyone catching on.
Killing us would be no trouble at all.
So there's no way I can let Natsumi hear what Sakura-san said.
Two points in Sakura-san's words matter:
・The Motoki-cho group, Chiga-nee and Kana are the top-priority targets
・Shigatsu Youka shows an opening when it repairs the corpses
In other words, we have to wait for an opening from Shigatsu Youka and get out of Arata fast.
But there are two problems.
We don't know when Shigatsu Youka will show an opening.
And how to get Natsumi and Chiga-nee out.
No matter how far those two run, they'll never get clear.
Because Natsumi's and Chiga-nee's danger sense works even in Motoki-cho, even in Tokyo.
In other words, wherever there are cherry trees, Shigatsu Youka's eyes can reach.
Fleeing the Arata settlement isn't enough―until they reach somewhere with no cherry trees, the two of them aren't safe.
But if it's going to kill us, Arata, with so few people around to see, has to be easier.
Isn't there some way to neutralize Natsumi's and Chiga-nee's intuition?
If Goto's still in the storehouse, she might've found a way by now, but...
No... wait a second...?
Chiga-nee said, I'm sure, that 10 years ago she had a symptom of wanting to kill Sakura-san.
From Goto's story, that's the thing called the Green-Eye Curse-Killing...
Why did that get cured?
The Green-Eye Curse-Killing, I'm sure, means that when a green-eyed woman has her first period, she wants to kill anyone with precognition.
But Chiga-nee's precognition and her green eyes never went away.
Which means...
Damn it! I never asked Sakura-san the thing that matters most!
Why Chiga-nee's murderous intent disappeared, Sakura-san has to know for sure!
Because it's possible that it wasn't Chiga-nee they treated―they erased Sakura-san's precognition and so erased Chiga-nee's murderous intent!
Is Sakura-san still under that rock overhang from before?
Is she still on my side?
If she's an enemy, I might get killed.
But if I don't find that out, Natsumi and Chiga-nee will never get out of here again...!
Do I go back to Sakura-san, or bet that Goto already knows a way to neutralize precognition―
The one who's sure to know the answer is Sakura-san.
But the one that's sure to keep me safe is Goto...
Going back to Sakura-san is way too dangerous.
Not much of a choice here...
I hurried toward the settlement.
Sakura-san said that being found by Shigatsu Youka was only a matter of time.
Meeting Sakura-san again = being found by Shigatsu Youka.
Which means being killed by Shigatsu Youka.
And that's not all. I don't even know how many corpses are walking around here.
I climbed the cliff along the mountain stream and stepped into the graveyard clearing.
At the very least, besides Sakura-san, Dad's corpse is apparently out there.
There's no saying there are only two of them.
Maybe it's just these two, or maybe there are 100 walking around.
No, but hold on...?
I reined in my legs, which had been running flat out, and slowed to a walk right away.
Those trees that should have been so lush still bare their exposed trunks, as if ashamed.
If getting spotted by a corpse― getting spotted by Shigatsu Youka means being killed on the spot, then the three who went to the abandoned hut just now should already have been killed too.
Because Natsumi is always being watched.
But they headed off to the abandoned hut like nothing had happened.
It seems finding us doesn't mean it kills us right away.
"Hm!?"
I could see a figure straight ahead.
I hid behind a withered tree.
If it's a corpse, my life's in danger, no question.
But can I really stay hidden like this?
In moonlight this bright, it's a sloppy game of hide-and-seek that even I, no mountain hand, could win at once.
But maybe there was no need to hide so desperately.
I know that figure.
"Haruka!?"
"I knew it―Grandma!"
Grandma, walking this forest alone.
No one with her...?
"Haruka, what happened to Mifuyu-san!?"
"Grandma! Never mind that, it's bad! If we don't hurry, we're...!"
"Haruka, I know what's going on. I talked with Erina-chan just now, and I finally understood what's happening. We have to bring Kana, quickly."
"Kana...?"
"Kana is the one human being with the power to destroy Shigatsu Youka. Only a woman with green eyes has the power to destroy Shigatsu Youka."
"Then what about Chiga-nee!? Better to have Chiga-nee along too, right!?"
"Chigaya isn't green-eyed anymore..."
"Huh...?"
What happened while I was away from the settlement?
Chiga-nee no longer green-eyed, and Kana the one who destroys Shigatsu Youka...?
New events and information I can barely grasp are all tangled together, but Grandma has no reason to lie.
It sounds like Goto was in on the talk too.
"Grandma, what are you doing out here?"
"I heard Kana is at the abandoned hut. Erina-chan―I sent her back to the settlement, and Chigaya is heading for Shigatsu Youka. There's a path in the underground cave that leads to Shigatsu Youka. She should be checking that path as she goes, so those following behind can get through smoothly."
I see. There are two ways for us to survive.
One is to neutralize Natsumi's and Chiga-nee's intuition and escape when we see an opening.
The other is to defeat Shigatsu Youka itself.
Grandma seems to have chosen defeating Shigatsu Youka.
And Chiga-nee is heading for Shigatsu Youka to prepare for it.
"Hey, then can't you fix Natsumi's precognition too!? How did you cure Chiga-nee!?"
"Ah, I hadn't told you yet, Haruka. But it isn't that simple, you know. Well, let's talk as we walk. Kana's at the abandoned hut, isn't she?"
I went back the way I'd come and headed for the abandoned hut.
What Grandma told me was about the effect of falsificaso.
It neutralizes Droga, apparently, and 10 years ago, giving it to Sakura-san ended Chiga-nee's madness.
Right around then, Mom was trying to kill a classmate who'd been bullying me in Megasawa.
She was threatening Natsumi's parents into manufacturing an alibi and plotting the perfect crime with Droga.
But the Droga was only bait to make Mom act; what Sakura-san and the others really wanted was for her to use the falsificaso.
In the end, the Kogori family talked her out of it, and no murder was done.
So Sakura-san took a gamble and used the falsificaso on herself, and as a result Chiga-nee's madness ended without any treatment done to her.
"Hey, Grandma, you can tell me now, right? About Sakura-san killing herself..."
Grandma's and Kotaro-san's behavior really is baffling.
I can still understand why Sakura-san killed herself.
Why did Kotaro-san cut off Sakura-san's head...?
"Kotaro believes I pushed Sakura off. The rest... you can work out, can't you?"
"Grandma, that was to keep everyone from thinking you killed her, right?"
"Kotaro said as much. By the time I saw Sakura's body, her head had already been cut off."
"............"
The Sakura-san I met just now― Sakura-san made no attempt to hide her presence, or that she was a corpse.
A look no different from the living, and the air and gestures she had in life.
Of course, there was no trace of a severed head.
And yet she fell from the cliff and then had her head cut off...
But... it really is a little strange.
Grandma caught Sakura-san's arm at the very edge of the cliff and was trying her best to pull her up.
How could that look like pushing her off?
"Hey, Grandma, you didn't push Sakura-san off, did you?"
"............
No. In the end, I couldn't save her. Even if I say I killed her―"
"No, I mean, you tried to save her, right?"
"Well, yes. I tried with everything I had, but..."
It really is strange.
Unless Grandma shook off a Sakura-san clinging to her arm, you can't call it a deliberate push.
Or rather, the very phrase 'pushed her off' is strange.
And Grandma pushing Sakura-san off is strange as a motive in the first place.
I can't put it well, but all sorts of things are strange.
But there's one thing I can think of.
Something only I would understand― something I understand because of the impossible experience of meeting Sakura-san just now.
"Grandma, don't tell me the one who cut off Sakura-san's head was..."
At that, Grandma stopped walking.
Head down, as if she'd sensed something, her mouth twisted hard.
"Haruka... This Arata settlement has been, for 10 years now― Sakura died, and ever since... no, perhaps since long, long ago, taken over by something we can't identify."
"I knew it. The one who cut off Sakura-san's head was..."
"It must have been... Eiichiro. Shigatsu Youka's will―following it, to spread the rumor of a curse through this settlement."
"So that's it... Grandma, you'd figured it out?"
"I realized it just now, when I talked with Erina-chan. Shigatsu Youka's existence, and Shigatsu Youka's purpose―when I learned them, it finally came together."
"I see. No wonder we never found any evidence, however hard we looked into Kotaro-san. Hey, what did Dad look like, back then?"
"He looked exactly like Kotaro always does. I can't remember his clothes, of course, but nothing felt off."
The Sakura-san I'd just met was barefoot, in something like white robes.
If the walking corpses dress in the same white robes as Sakura-san, then the one who cut off Sakura-san's head, Dad, must have worn the same.
Then even Grandma would surely have felt something off.
Which means... Dad's corpse got into the Niimura house to steal Kotaro-san's clothes?
When Sakura-san died, there was no one at home, so if he meant to, that was possible too...
"So cutting off Sakura-san's head was to make a mess of the settlement's relationships, and it used Sakura-san's suicide?"
"I'd been careful, I thought, so that no one would overhear Mifuyu-san's confession about Megasawa."
"Well, with something that delicate... Grandma, can you tell me that conversation in detail?"
"Mifuyu-san's confession?"
"Yeah..."
"I don't remember it word for word, but―"
That day, Mifuyu-san and I were in the little three-mat room on the second floor.
"Sakura-san did her precognition in that room, right?"
Yes. Mifuyu-san said she had something to tell me.
"Mother... No―Grand Witch-sama. I have come today to receive judgment."
"Judgment? Isn't this about telling me you're moving from Megasawa to Motoki-cho?"
"There is that too... I have broken a taboo."
"............ Let's hear it."
"Grand Witch-sama, you know of Droga, do you not?"
"Droga, is it... It's been a long time since I heard that name."
Mifuyu-san held out several syringes to me.
Syringes filled with a cherry-pink liquid.
"For certain reasons, there was someone I absolutely had to kill. With these, I..."
"Did you kill them?"
"No, I stopped myself. My best friend and her family, and Haruka, talked me out of it... But it does not change the fact that I broke a taboo. That is why I have come today to receive judgment."
"Which means the one who gave you these, Mifuyu-san, was―"
"Sakura-san...! 　Sakura-san only took pity on me! 　So Sakura-san's sin―"
"Mifuyu-san... you do understand what you have done, do you not? 　Breaking the rule is subject to punishment."
"Yes. I will take all the punishment upon myself... So, as for Sakura-san..."
"............ Well... I'd say that's fine! 　Nothing happened, right!?"
"What? Oh, yes..."
Making Droga and sending it to Mifuyu-san was certainly not a good thing.
But, you see, after she had stopped herself and confessed honestly, I couldn't bring myself to blame Mifuyu-san.
Soft of me, for a Grand Witch, you might think.
"Mom and you didn't talk about anything else?"
"Hmm... it should have gone about like that. It didn't even take 5 minutes."
It really is strange that this alone would convince Sakura-san she'd be "blamed."
Most likely, Sakura-san was in the closet of the three-mat room.
It was daytime then, so was she in the closet to do her precognition?
Then the two of them came in with that grim air, and she couldn't come out...
Shigatsu Youka set its sights on that, made Sakura-san hear voices, and tricked her into believing a harsh punishment would come down.
"Grandma, Sakura-san―I'm sure Shigatsu Youka pulled a trick on her! 　If it made her hear voices right then, she'd think she was the one being blamed, right!?"
"No, that's not it. Chigaya's madness stopped because Sakura gave herself the falsificaso. By then, Sakura no longer had any precognition."
"Oh, right... My head wasn't working for a second..."
So the theory that Sakura-san was hiding in the closet of the three-mat room is out.
Was she eavesdropping outside the three-mat room?
That day Mom and I had come to Arata out of the blue, so Sakura-san must have wondered what we'd say to Grandma.
But something, somehow, still doesn't fit.
Mom was sent not just Droga but falsificaso too, wasn't she?
Where did the falsificaso sent to Megasawa go?
"Come on, hurry. No time to dawdle."
I was full of regret.
Instead of sneaking around searching the storehouse, instead of keeping Natsumi in the dark again―
Maybe we should all have sat down face to face, pooled what we knew and talked it over.
Put together what everyone knows like this, and the mystery comes apart, bit by bit.
Maybe we were too afraid of Shigatsu Youka watching.
It's too late to say that now, though...
Beyond the muddy path, the abandoned hut came into view.
Natsumi, Mom and Kana are here.
I have to take Kana and go after Goto and Chiga-nee.
