An Incomprehensible Will

Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-07 23:15 火 · 37 boxes

I called the Motoki-cho police right away and asked them to check Natsumi's apartment.
I put Natsumi to bed on a futon.
At first she was shaking all over, but as I kept holding her and stroking her head, she eventually fell asleep.
Grandma and the others went straight to the Shirozaki house, home of the voice from earlier.
Left in this house were the three of us from Motoki-cho, Kana, and my mom.
Goto got the frightened Kana to sleep with her head in her lap.
Even Kana was scared after coming face to face with a murder.
"Why Natsumi's parents...! Those two have nothing to do with this...!"
So this is a curse-killing... Every year in April, someone dies.
And this time, two people with no connection to the Arata settlement.
"But this has made one thing clear. Judging from what that Shirozaki person said, the curse-killing cases were the residents killing each other out of suspicion of one another..."
Suspecting each other, killing each other.
I see, so that's what the curse-killings really are...
"But why Natsumi's parents... those two shouldn't have had anything to do with it."
"Maybe it has something to do with what happened in Megasawa. Not that there's any way of finding out now..."
"No, it's not settled that Natsumi's parents are dead. Let's wait to hear back from the police..."
"You're right... Um... what kind of person is this Shirozaki-san?"
"Shirozaki-san's a good-natured old guy who loves his drink... He's really the kind of guy you'd think 'no way' about..."
"I see... From the sound of it, Shirozaki-san wasn't the only one who attacked them. It felt like there were several others there too."
Like Goto said, there'd been the sense of several people on the other end of the phone.
Their distrust must have exploded, and they'd all banded together in the worst way.
"Goto, if Shirozaki-san lashed out because of paranoia... then that'd mean the one we've been calling 'the culprit' cut off the head of Sakura-san, who died in an accident or by suicide, but never killed anyone, and hasn't done anything since?"
"No, then there'd be no reason for a curse-killing case to happen the next year. For a while, the culprit must have been acting too. But at some point the culprit achieved the goal and stopped the curse-killings, while the suspicion all around kept going, and that turned into the serial curse-killing cases... Isn't that it? And this time, people from outside the settlement finally became targets too."
A cell number I didn't know. Was it the Motoki-cho police?
"Hello!? This is Niimura!"
"Ah, Niimura-san. My name is Ise, with the Motoki Police Station. A short while ago, we searched Kogori-san's residence."
"And... what did you find...?"
"Well..."
Goto and I swallowed hard.
That one short pause felt terribly long.
"Well, at Kogori-san's home... there was no one. No signs of a struggle at all, either..."
"No one... was there...?"
I'd thought it would be one or the other: either they'd find bodies, or Natsumi's parents would be there as if nothing had happened.
But neither of those...
"Was the car there!? 　Natsumi's... no, Kogori-san's car!?"
"The car was there. Isn't it possible they went out on foot?"
"Both of them going out on foot together, right in the middle of a conversation like that!? 　Please search the whole Motoki-cho area right away! I'm somewhere far away right now, and I can't get over there!"
"Hmm... Let's see. I understand how you feel, but when nothing has happened, going any further than this is..."
"It already has happened! They were abducted, I'm sure of it! So please, search right away!"
The police won't move unless something has happened.
No, they won't move until they've recognized that something has happened.
How else am I supposed to explain it...
"............ Understood. It is a fact that Kogori-san and her husband are actually missing, so we will set up a cordon in town and stop every passing vehicle at checkpoints. If we have not found them by morning, we will request assistance from the Sawa Prefectural Police."
"Huh...? Th-thank you very much!"
Against my expectations, the response I got was a kind one.
"No, no, there's no need for thanks. Motoki-cho is so peaceful that we at the Motoki police have time on our hands. Besides, the role of the police is to protect everyone's safety and peace of mind. Giving people peace of mind is part of our job as well."
"I really do appreciate it."
"Well then, that's all. I have to get back to the search myself. I'll be in touch again."
"Goto, the Motoki police said they'll search the whole town top to bottom right away!"
I was happy with how the police were handling it, but Goto's face stayed dark.
"Even so... I still think something has happened to Senpai's parents..."
"Yeah... you're right..."
Far from being reassuring, it had only become more likely that Natsumi's parents really had been attacked.
The Kogori home had been empty. That alone was strange enough.

Just then, the door of the room slid slowly open.
It was Mom.
"Haruka... I went and looked around a little too."
Her expression was somehow gloomy.
"So...! What about Shirozaki-san!? Did that guy really do it!?"
"Everyone was gone... Shirozaki-san too... the people from the other houses, all of them..."
"All the residents vanished...?"
Which meant that at some point, they'd all gone over to Motoki-cho?
But... that would be way too bold a move.
If that many people went and committed the crime, they'd stand out.
"Hey, Goto. Then that means Natsumi's parents were abducted and killed somewhere else, right!?"
When I said that, Goto seemed to sink into thought a little.
She probably couldn't agree with what I'd said.
"Couldn't it be that they weren't abducted, but went to the crime scene of their own free will? Senpai's call got answered right away, and from the way he talked, it didn't sound like they'd been abducted, did it?"
True. If they'd been abducted, they probably couldn't have made a call, and if they could use a phone, they'd have asked for help right away.
"Haruka... Goto-chan... the truth is..."
Mom began to speak, struggling to get the words out.

Then Mom's shoulders began to shake.
"Mom, what's wrong? Are you okay?"
And then Mom sank to the floor as if she'd crumpled.
"I'm sorry...! 　It was me...! 　It's my fault...!"
"Mom, calm down...! 　Tell me what happened... take it slow, it's okay."
When she heard that exchange, Goto picked up her notepad, still letting Kana sleep in her lap.
It sounded like she was saying that because of Mom, Natsumi's parents had been killed.
But it was obvious that Mom hadn't done anything directly.
Right now I had to calm her down and get her to talk.
"Akane and the others... were killed in the storage shed at Arata Station..."
"Huh...?"
Natsumi's parents had been at Arata Station?
Why not Motoki-cho?
"I told them to hide in the storage shed..."
Mom told them that...!?
Could Mom have been driven by distrust too, and done that to Natsumi's parents...?
"Mom, you didn't give orders to Shirozaki-san and the others, did you!?"
"No! Of course not!"
Mom lifted her head and denied it desperately.
For a moment I'd suspected Mom, but I'd been hoping she would deny it like this.
Goto said nothing, taking notes on the conversation.
"Haruka... I can't take this anymore... Because I'm here, Akane too... Sakura-san too...!"
Mom suddenly stood up and left the room.
"Wait, Mom!"
I ran out of the room after Mom.
