A quiet bathroom. Rising steam.
I was staring blankly at the steam rising from the tub.
Goto had hidden the listening device away somewhere.
The police must think I might be here.
The bathroom door opened, and cold air came in, trading places with the damp steam.
Along with that air, Haruka came in.
She'd said she wouldn't take her eyes off me, so of course we were bathing together too.
Haruka silently rinsed off with hot water and got into the tub.
"That police officer earlier was funny. He got all flustered in front of Goto. Maybe he's into that kind of thing?"
I'd meant it as a fun topic, but Haruka's expression didn't change.
Ever since she'd heard about the listening device, she'd looked even more serious.
"Natsumi... you must think it's weird after all... Me suddenly saying stuff like I want it to be just us two, and the police coming and leaving a bug..."
"Haruka, I don't know what you're thinking, but as long as I stay with you the whole time, it'll be okay, right?"
"............"

"Th-that's dirty! Goto! Daaamn it!"

"Nahhahha! You're a hundred years too early to defy the genius girl, as everyone agrees, myself included! 　Oh? 　Niimura-senpai... Huge! 　Ho ho... So you look a lot slimmer in clothes than you are."

Goto suddenly turned on the tears, but they were obviously fake.
"D-don't cry... I was kidding..."
All of a sudden Haruka got flustered and went over to Goto.
"Gotcha!"
Goto jumped into the tub again and blasted cold water from the faucet.
"Gyaaah, I'm sorry, I'm sorry!"
"Goto, forgive me―!"
"Nahhahha! As long as you understand, all is well!"
Haruka and I, just the two of us, had no chance against Goto, whatever we did...
Or so I thought at the time.
