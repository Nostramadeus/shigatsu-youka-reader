Those Who Appeared and Those Who Vanished

Eden no Sakura Arc [Eden's Cherry] · Shiyo 800-04-07 16:00 火 · 53 boxes

Tomorrow, Dad's thirteenth-year memorial service would be held in the Arata settlement.
The ones with me now were my best friend Natsumi and my cute junior Goto.
This was the first time the three of us had ever gone outside the prefecture together.
At first, I'd meant for them to come visit Dad's family home just for fun, like a little trip.
But―
"Hey, Goto, heard anything from Chiga-nee?"
"I still can't reach her at all. Up to then she'd been keeping me posted, point by point, on what she'd looked into and what she'd found out, but..."
"I see..."
"Chiga-nee's family doesn't know where she went either?"
"Yeah, Kotaro-san and Grandma both say the last time they saw Chiga-nee was yesterday evening. I know she'd been in the storehouse the whole time, poring over the books, but vanishing all of a sudden like that..."
"Is it my fault...?"
"Huh? Why would it be your fault...?"
"Because I asked her to go to Arata early and look into things..."
Hearing that, Natsumi and I sighed at the same time.
"You... Taking all the blame on yourself? You idiot."
"That's right, Goto. You gave Chiga-nee the best advice there was."
"............"
Goto must know that much herself.
But what Goto is feeling isn't so much responsibility as plain worry for Chiga-nee.
Chiga-nee has been missing since last night.
The day before that, the day before yesterday, April 5―when Chiga-nee got to Arata, we talked on the phone for the first time in a while.
"Yeah... hello...?"
"Mm... Haruka? It's been a while."
"Oh, it's just Chiga-nee. I was wondering who it was..."
"Mm... You didn't have my number saved?"
"No, I just switched to a new phone the other day. My numbers didn't transfer over right..."
"Oh, didn't you ask Goto-chan how to do it?"
"Huh!? How do you know about Goto...?"
That was when I first learned about the curse-killings.
And also that Goto had gone to Tokyo.
But even hearing all that, I couldn't believe it right away.
Kotaro-san had cut off Sakura-san's head, Chiga-nee had killed five people, and then that turned out to be a misunderstanding.
Goto apparently thought the cause was 'someone' lurking in Arata.
It took an hour to hear everything Chiga-nee had to tell me.
The whole time I was listening, I paced around the house.
It just wasn't something I could listen to calmly.
Pointlessly wiping things down and cleaning the toilet as I listened.
And the moment Chiga-nee finished talking, I sank deep into the sofa.
"Hey, Chiga-nee, I figure that's all true, but... Was that okay? Telling me all this..."
"Mm... What I was worried about was whether that urge to kill would be aimed at Natsumi-chan. But it looks like my precognition really doesn't work in Arata this year. So I think it'll be fine even if I meet Natsumi-chan."
"I see. So there's no need to hide it anymore."
"Let me fill you in on how things stand in the settlement right now. No one knows we're investigating the curse-killings. The only ones who know are Auntie, Goto-chan, and you. Oh, and Natsumi-chan's parents."
"Oh... now that you mention it, something about it being dangerous for Natsumi's parents to come..."
"Apparently, Auntie originally asked Natsumi-chan's parents for their help. Then it seems Natsumi-chan said, through her precognition, that it would be dangerous for her parents to go to Arata. So it was settled that Natsumi-chan and Goto-chan would come instead."
"So for now, once we're in Arata, I just play dumb about the curse-killings, right? 　But there's something weird about that too..."
"Weird?"
"You were convinced you'd killed five people, but you're trying awfully hard to solve it, or how do I put it... Something just doesn't sit right with me about the way you're acting."
"Mm... Well, that's because I only started moving to solve it at the start of this year. By then, the curse-killings were already out of my hands, you know?"
"No, that's not what I mean. At the start of this year, at least, you still thought you'd killed five people, right? 　Solving the case would be the same thing as you turning yourself in, right? 　Isn't that kind of weird?"
"Mm... I see. Setting out to solve a case I caused myself certainly is a contradiction."
"Right? 　Chiga-nee, if it's a settlement where that many strange things keep happening, maybe you should be a little more..."
"............"
"Chiga-nee?"
"Mm... Am I myself a contradiction...?"
"Hey, what's wrong?"
"Mm... Goto-chan told me. That this case would always run into a contradiction, and the cause would be something common sense couldn't conceive of. And that I should keep asking 'why' over and over to dig into why it turns out that way."
"Hey now, what are you talking about...?"
"Mm... Haruka, sorry, there's something I want to look into. I'm hanging up."
All that came from the other end of the line now was a flat electronic tone.
As for that conversation with Chiga-nee, I'd told Natsumi, Goto, and Mom about it.
After hearing it, Goto had apparently been getting in touch with Chiga-nee every few hours.
She said the last time she'd gotten through to her was last night.
"Goto, was there anything different about Chiga-nee up through yesterday?"
"She seemed a little tired. I think she was probably going through the books without sleeping. What's more, the books were apparently all written in classical Japanese and Portuguese, so deciphering them seemed to be giving her a lot of trouble. She sent me pictures of some of the pages, and I tried deciphering them on my end too."
"Did you figure anything out?"
"I found out about 'the Green-Eye Curse-Killing.' Girls born with green eyes, it says, feel a powerful urge to kill toward girls with the same green eyes, or toward anyone who has precognition. It apparently happens to green-eyed girls once they have had their first period."
"What's that supposed to mean... That's a full-on supernatural phenomenon..."
"So Chiga-nee wanting to kill me could really happen?"
"Yes. Over 400 years ago, the residents began killing one another. At first it was apparently a conflict born of mutual distrust, but gradually more and more people turned green-eyed, and they began killing one another―"
"By 'they,' you mean men too?"
"Yes. It is not limited to a first period. Even men are apparently driven by the urge to kill when bleeding."
"I get that that's what Chiga-nee's urge to kill really was, but... That alone still doesn't show us the whole picture at all."
"Yes. We still need some big pieces. The pieces we need to solve the case."
―And then the train began to slow down gently.
It looked like we'd reached Toyotake Station, our last transfer station.
There was a little time before the connecting train came.
We got permission to go out through the ticket gate, and I looked up at the mountains with their cherry trees in bloom.
From here on, there would be almost no cherry blossoms to see.
If Natsumi could foresee anything, this was the last place for it.
"............"
Natsumi had her eyes closed and seemed to be concentrating on the voice of the wind.
But what we'd thought was Dad's voice might actually be the voice of someone else, as Goto saw it, and that bothered me too.
This time, of all times, Natsumi's foresight and advice aren't something I can swallow whole.
"............ No good. I can't hear anything."
"I see... So does that mean there's no danger?"
"No, that's not it... I do hear something, but... would you call it noise? 　It's like something's interfering, and I can't make it out at all."
"So that means there might be danger..."
Even Natsumi's intuition, which we'd relied on until now, seemed to be off.
The way Natsumi had just put it, there might be danger.
Even just knowing that made a difference in how much we braced ourselves.
"Uh... I hate to bring this up, but... Isn't turning back to Motoki-cho from here an option?"
"Turn back to Motoki-cho...?"
"We can't rule out the possibility of danger, right? 　Is going all the way there really the constructive thing to do? And this is late to bring up too, but the danger sense telling Kogori-senpai to come to the memorial service is pretty suspicious, or..."
There's nothing wrong with Goto's argument.
There's no guarantee that this year's curse-killing won't fall on us.
If the danger is going to fall on Natsumi or Goto, I don't feel right going on from here.
I agree with what Goto said, too.
However bad things are in the settlement, taking Natsumi and Goto there...
Just then, Natsumi's phone rang.
"It's Dad. Hello?"
Awfully good timing for a call.
Come to think of it, our phones had a bugging app hidden in them, didn't they?
To make sure we were safe, Natsumi's dad, a mobile-phone engineer, had planted it.
It didn't feel good, but our lives came first.
"He says come home, don't push it."
"............"
"............"
At those words, neither Goto nor I could answer right away.
If we went home like this, we'd probably be safe.
But it would also mean abandoning the settlement and Chiga-nee, who had vanished.
"Haruka, Goto... I'll go to Arata alone."
"What!?"
"Senpai...!"
"I want to know. What this intuition of mine really is. If I go to Arata, I'm sure I'll find out what it is, right?"
"You idiot! Who cares what it really is!"
"That's right! I'll deduce all that for you later, as much as you like!"
Natsumi's nerves of steel really left me dumbfounded.
Natsumi had always been like this, but I felt she was forcing herself a little.
As proof, Natsumi's hands were shaking a little.
I squeezed Natsumi's trembling hands tight.
"In that case, Natsumi. Let's go once cherry season's over. No reason we have to go during the most dangerous time of all, right?"
"I... If Kogori-senpai goes, I'm going too! 　You're definitely going to need my skills!"
"Hold on, you guys..."
When they put it like that, even I...
Then Natsumi, who'd been looking down, lifted her head.
And at some point, her hands had stopped shaking as well.
Natsumi smiled at us.
"Let's... go back to Motoki-cho."
"Natsumi...!"
"Senpai!"
Goto threw her arms around Natsumi, and Natsumi hugged her back.
I really didn't want to drag even these two into it.
By rights, with Arata blood in me, I should probably go, but then these two would likely end up coming along...
To save the two of them, I had no choice but to turn back with them.
I had to convince myself, even by force, that this was the best decision.
Just then, a tremendous sound rang out across the whole area.
At this awful sound, which seemed to shake us from the pit of our stomachs, our smiles froze in an instant.
"Wh-what's that sound...!?"
A sound like someone blowing an eerie trumpet, and a sound like metal grinding against metal.
There was no telling where it came from, and it plunged our hearts into dread.
"It's dying down..."
"What was that just now...?"
"Strange sounds... Apocalyptic sounds..."
And just then, a station attendant with a megaphone began shouting toward the front of the station.
"Ahem, the Toyotake Line is suspended in both directions due to track trouble. The Toyotake Line is suspended in both directions due to track trouble."
So the trains have stopped...?
Then we can't get back to Motoki-cho by train!
"Excuse me, what happened!?"
I ran up to the station attendant and pressed for an answer.
"Well, you see... How should I put it... It just suddenly grew, right over there. A cherry tree."
"Senpai! Come over here!"
At some point Goto had gone through the ticket gate, and now she was beckoning frantically from the platform.
"Sorry! I've got a ticket, so I'm going through!"
With a token flash of my ticket, I went through the unattended ticket gate.
What Goto was staring at was―

Even if we'd somehow missed it, no train could possibly get through with this thing in the way.
Natsumi, Goto, and I, along with everyone else around the station, could do nothing but stare blankly at that blizzard of cherry petals, one you might even call eerie.

"Natsumi, pull yourself together! There are plenty of ways to get home!"
When I shook the shoulders of Natsumi, who was looking up at the cherry tree, she turned her face toward me with a geometric, puppet-like movement.
