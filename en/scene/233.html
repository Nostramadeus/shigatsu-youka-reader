Shigatsu Arc [Death Month] · Shiyo 800-04-07 19:40 火 · 34 boxes

When we arrived at the Niimura house, Haruka's grandmother, Erika Niimura-san, gave us a cosplay baptism.
For us, worn out as we were, this baptism was hard to know how to react to.
No, it would be hard to react to even if we weren't tired...
"Agh, I can't anymore! 　I'm beat! 　I don't want dinner!"
Haruka threw herself onto the floor like a judo breakfall.
"But the buggy was fun!"
Who knew Obasan's reckless driving and Kotaro-san's safe driving would make this big a difference...
"Goto, did you talk about anything with Kotaro-san?"
"Hmm... It wasn't the kind of mood where you'd bring up the case in small talk. Senpai, what about you?"
―I told them about the witch's power Shirozaki-san had mentioned.
Along with his warning: "Don't tell anyone else about the danger sense."
"I see, I see. That's quite a solid lead. What we can infer from this is that Sakura-san's danger sense was shunned too..."
At that, Haruka sat up.
"So what, then? Sakura-san got killed because she had that kind of power?"
"No. Sakura-san's power was used to make hunting safe. Chiga-nee told us so, didn't she? So up to that point―10 years ago, it wasn't shunned."
"I've got it! Sakura-san's danger sense would've gotten in the way of the curse-killings, so the culprit killed her! Isn't that it!? 　See, like killing Sakura-san was the groundwork for the curse-killings."
"If so, Sakura-san trying to kill herself is far too much of a chance event. Besides, there'd be no need to go out of the way to cut off her head..."
"Huh? I thought I was onto something. That's a no in Goto's book?"
"I can't say it's zero, but it's a no, with high probability. A limited causal relationship holds, but everything else is left to chance. After all, by chance, Niimura-senpai's mother would have to hold back from murder, by chance confess to your grandmother about Megasawa City, by chance, Sakura-san would have to overhear it, by chance, Sakura-san would have to try to kill herself, and on top of that, by chance, the culprit would have to be the first to find Sakura-san after she ran off."
"Hmm, so then the people here did come to hate Sakura-san's ability after all...?"
"But in that case, I don't think your grandmother would have Kogori-senpai come here..."
"Then maybe Haruka's grandmother is mixed up in the case somehow?"
"That's possible too... But as long as the causal relationship is unclear, it's too early to suspect her. The precognition isn't necessarily connected to Sakura-san's beheading incident. Even if you hypothesize a connection, please don't ever forget to think, 'It might be wrong,' okay?"
"I know, I know. So first, how about we use the process of elimination? The ones who absolutely can't be the culprit."
For once, Haruka was the one proposing a line of reasoning.
She must be more than a little unhappy about her own grandmother becoming a suspect.
"Um... Kana-chan, who was 0 years old at the time, is ruled out."
"What about Chiga-nee...? She was probably around 10 at the time, right?"
"The thing she mentioned earlier... um, Droga, was it? 　With that, even a kid could pull it off, right?"
"If Chiga-nee's athleticism back then was on par with Kana-chan's now, she might have been able to commit murder even without Droga. But Chiga-nee is probably a miss too. Coming from Tokyo and pulling off the last 2 years of curse-killings without anyone knowing... I think she could do it on the sly, staying overnight holed up in that abandoned hut from before, but then going out of her way to attend college in Tokyo doesn't quite fit, or..."
"And I can't see someone who keeps up something as big as the curse-killings deliberately moving to Tokyo and making it harder to commit the crimes..."
"So Kana and Chiga-nee are out for now. Just to be safe, my mom's out too. At least after Sakura-san ran off somewhere, she went back to Motoki-cho with me."
That meant the names we knew right now were Haruka's grandmother, Kotaro-san, and Shirozaki-san.
I'd like to go around the other houses asking questions, but that would just be outsiders butting in...
Besides, the people of the settlement had probably already done that years ago.
"So it's Kotaro-san who's the most suspicious after all..."
"That's true, but... Even so, how should I put it... It's like we get to Kotaro-san awfully easily..."
"Then, Grandma?"
"That's way too simplistic too... I just don't think a case nobody could solve for 10 years can be solved this simply..."
"Maybe we should work from Shirozaki-san's hint after all... Oh, I know! What about asking your parents, Natsumi? They already know why we came here, so they won't tell us to come home, right?"
Yeah, that really is a great idea.
We can probably also ask about the things we've forgotten about Megasawa.
"Hello? Dad?"
"Looks like you've heard everything. The truth is―"
Then Dad explained the bugging app.
It didn't feel good, but it had been set up for our safety.
"Uh... Where are you now?"
"I'm at home in Motoki-cho, like I should be. We'd have run into you on the train, so we gave up on going to Arata."
Thank goodness for that.
Who knows what would have happened if Dad and Mom had come...
―What we heard was about killing the two kids who bullied Haruka: that Obasan had asked my dad and mom to do it.
We also heard about Droga.
But Sakura-san had sent not just the Noh masks and red robes, which might be one thing, but weapons, and even Droga and its instructions.
It's hard to read it as anything but Sakura-san trying to get them to commit murder...
"Uh, can I ask something? 　Was the shipment one box? Or two?"
"Two boxes, I think."
"Which means it's not certain that one person sent both boxes, right? 　Maybe they were split up, like Noh masks and red robes in one, and weapons and Droga in the other?"
"Hmm, how was it... First we were handed the Noh masks and red robes... Yeah, Goto-chan's right. They were sorted exactly that way."
"Is there a chance the one who sent the box with the weapons was someone other than Sakura-san?"
"Someone else sent just the weapons...?"
"There must be someone who, for some purpose, tried to make Niimura-senpai's mother commit murder in Megasawa."
"Then what were the Noh masks and red robes sent for?"
"Hmm... What would they be for..."
"Well, either way, it's not certain that Sakura-san sent both of them. But why try to have kids with no connection to Arata killed...?"
"If I had to guess, maybe someone wanted to test what Droga does?"
"True, testing a drug on your own body isn't easy... Mifuyu-san, or else us, put to use to test its effects... What a horrible person, this real culprit..."
"Though the person who sent Droga and the person behind the curse-killings aren't necessarily the same..."
In other words, what happened in Megasawa was a massive human experiment dressed up as the murder of elementary schoolers...
If so, then someone with intentions that terrifying is here in the Arata settlement.
"Also, was there anything that bothered you back then? 　Anything at all is fine, however trivial..."
"Even if something bothered me, it was 10 years ago... If I remember anything, I'll get in touch."
"Got it. Thank you very much."
"And another thing: try to stay where your phones can get an internet connection. I know it won't feel great, but it's so we can always know what's happening there. When you were in the mountains earlier, we couldn't hear a thing, and we were worried..."
"Roger, sir! This genius girl has received the honored father's orders loud and clear!"
"Honored... father...?"
That seemed to be about the limit of what we could get out of Dad.
Someone had sent weapons and Droga to Obasan for some purpose.
Whether it was a human experiment was unclear, but that person had definitely tried to make a murder happen in Megasawa.
"For now, Motoki-cho needs to be able to hear our conversations too, so let's carry our phones as much as we can."
"Got it. But... Goto, can you deduce anything more?"
"Hmm... I'm hungry!"
"You... how can you have an appetite while talking about stuff like this..."
"The brain burns energy! 　We climbed a mountain too, and Goto's stomach demands fresh energy, sir!"
In short, even Goto couldn't deduce anything more.
Come to think of it, my stomach was a little...
Just then, someone knocked on the room's sliding door.
"Mm... Haruka, dinner's ready, Grandma says."
"Yeah, coming! 　Wait... this feeling... It's that old hag!"
Haruka flung the sliding door open.
"Don't pull weird stuff when I'm this tired! You cosplay hag!"
"Eek! 　Mm... Haruka, don't scare me like that..."
"Ah, sorry, Chiga-nee... So it was the real one..."
"Mm... I could hear what you were saying out in the hallway, so you'd better keep it down a little. It'd be bad if anyone heard, wouldn't it?"
"Oh, right... Uh... Natsumi's intuition... not to anyone..."
"Mm... I know. I've already told Kana to keep quiet too, so don't worry."
