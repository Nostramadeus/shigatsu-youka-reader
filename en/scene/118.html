Cursed Thinking

'No matter what, we reach Kotaro-san so easily that it's actually strange.'

On my own, I'd have a hard time examining that and working out an answer.
Without a doubt, this is something I should talk over with Goto.

No... before that, maybe I should ask Shirozaki-san...
If it was something that happened in the past, he might know something.
"Excuse me! Shirozaki-san!"
I shouted so the buggy's engine wouldn't drown out my voice.
"............ What is it!?"
After a slight pause, his voice came back.
"When I called Goto earlier, she said she was 'in a secret place.' Do you have any idea where that might be!?"
"A secret place...? 　No... I can't really say from just that..."
I guess that's true. If it's a secret place, maybe even the residents don't know about it.
Though it's also possible Shirozaki-san is playing dumb―
"Then do you recognize a tripod like the ones you use in science experiments, and a pot?"
With something this specific, he might take it for a witch's power instead.
But if so, that's fine.
I have to draw out any information, however little, that Goto can use for her analysis.
"Oh, I know those! 　They're ritual implements we use at memorial services! You'll probably see them tomorrow!"
Ritual implements used at memorial services...?
What were Kotaro-san and Sakura-san doing, taking those out?
"How are those ritual implements used at the memorial service?"
"How do I put it... it's like offering incense. The mourners just pour a little liquid into the pot. Tomorrow, just copy everyone else and it'll be easy."
"Can just anyone take that pot out?"
"Hmm, hard to say... Basically, only whoever holds the post of 'witch' can touch it, but I guess anyone could take it out if they wanted to. But if you only wanted to use a pot, I don't see any need to go to the trouble of taking out the ritual implements..."
It's hard to say anything for sure...
So does that mean the pot used as a ritual implement isn't anything special either?
Still, Shirozaki-san is being awfully talkative...
Does that mean he'll answer plain questions, as long as they don't involve my premonitions?
"Uh, Sakura-san had a witch's power―the danger-sense ability, didn't she? 　Since when?"
"Natsumi-chan, about that..."
"But this is something everyone knows, right? Isn't it okay for me to know that much too?"
"............"
Shirozaki-san stopped the buggy.
Was this a station?
This was probably Arata Station, where we'd originally planned to meet up with Obasan.
There was no one around at all, not even a streetlight.
"You know what that is?"
What Shirozaki-san pointed at was something like a shed, like a small storehouse―
At least, it wasn't anyone's home.
Could this be the storage shed they'd mentioned?
I tried opening the door.
Inside were lumber, blue tarps and other things you'd expect in a storage shed.
So Dad and Mom had meant to spend the night in a place like this...
"Shirozaki-san, does this place have something to do with it?"
"Today... there was an oracle..."
An oracle...? Suddenly the talk was turning occult again.
"That today, the curse-killer would be lurking here... that's what it said."
The blood drained from my face all at once.
Which means... someone tipped them off that Dad and Mom are the culprits!?
"Sorry, could you tell me a little more...!"
"Let's get a little away from here. If the curse-killer shows up, we're in danger too."
Shirozaki-san got on the buggy.
Judging from this, Shirozaki-san must believe in this so-called oracle too.
It still didn't sit right with me, but I got on the buggy too.
"Shirozaki-san, what do you mean!? The culprit, there...!"
"I know. Even if someone does come, they probably won't be the culprit. An oracle... something like that..."
Huh...? He was saying the opposite of what he'd said before.
Shirozaki-san doesn't believe in the oracle...?
I was losing track of what was going on.
At least, there's someone out there who has my dad and mom pegged as the culprits.
And I can't believe Shirozaki-san said 'oracle' as a joke...
"Who is it!? The person who gave that oracle!"



　　The witch.
The word 'witch' again...
One time it's the priests of the Arata settlement, another time it's my intuition being called a witch's power...
And now it's a witch who gave an oracle about the curse-killings...
This one word has far too many meanings, and it's confusing me.
"Who's the witch!?"
"The witch means Sakura-chan. Sakura-chan tells us the culprit who'll cause the curse-killing. So whoever appears at the place named in the witch's oracle, the people of the settlement 'offer up'..."
That's crazy...!
Then, could it be that the curse-killings... are cases where the people of the settlement killed the 'culprit' just as the witch's oracle said!?
They're committing curse-killings to prevent curse-killings...!?
"Shirozaki-san! That's not right! 　Then that makes everyone in the settlement a culprit in the curse-killings!"
"I know that. Everyone knows it... But if you were one of the settlement's people, what would you do? Would you refuse to offer up the culprit? 　And then could you go on living in the settlement?"
I was left speechless.
That's what the curse-killings really are: distrust breeds distrust, and to wipe it away, people kill whoever has become the target.
He hadn't said 'kill,' but 'offer up' = 'kill' must be what he means.
All they'd have to do is stop the curse-killing just once...!
Could it be... that Shirozaki-san has 'offered up' a culprit too?
"That's why, in the settlement, anyone with a witch's power is shunned. Our aim isn't to catch the culprit in the curse-killings. It's to kill the 'witch' who brings the oracles of curses...!"
Wait, hold on...!
Is that a death threat from Shirozaki-san against me...!?
If things go wrong, who knows what the residents will do to me in the settlement.
Going back to the settlement like this is dangerous...!
I could even be killed as a witch, just like that!
"Let me off!"
I put the knife to Shirozaki-san's throat.
I had to get away from here, fast...!
But Shirozaki-san made no move to slow the buggy down.
"If you don't hurry...! 　I'll really stab you!"
I pressed the blade hard against Shirozaki-san's neck.
It might have cut his skin a little.
But... if he still won't let me off, I'll really...!
"Natsumi-chan, that's what the curse-killing really is."
"What!?"
"That's what's causing the curse-killing."
What is he suddenly saying...!?
Is he trying to confuse me by saying strange things...!?
"Like that, you stop being able to trust your neighbors. You get possessed by paranoia that the person in front of you might kill you... and given any excuse, you try to wipe out that fear―in other words, you try to strike first. Which is exactly what you're trying to do right now."
Suddenly the buggy stopped.
"Natsumi-chan, if you're still worried, you can get off here. There's no such thing as a curse-killer. People shake off whatever threatens them so they won't become victims. It's just self-defense... no, excessive self-defense, I guess..."
"Then... then why doesn't everyone stop, when they know! 　There's no curse-killer, right!? You just kill people who might be the culprit because you're scared, right!? 　Then...!"
"Don't you see how dangerous it is to say that out loud? 　In a way, that's covering for the curse-killer. Either you kill whoever might be the curse-killer, or you get killed yourself..."
"But, I mean...! There's Sakura-san's oracle, isn't there!? 　Whoever's faking it is the real culprit, right!?"
"Sorry, the oracle story was a lie. I wanted you to experience for yourself what state of mind you end up in when you're caught up in the curse-killings."
Suddenly telling me it was a lie...!
I can't tell whether even that is a lie...!
Lying twice over like that, when the truth is!
"Natsumi-chan, I know what you're thinking. You must be tangled up in cursed thinking you can't break free of. After only a few hours here, a person ends up like you. So even you can imagine what becomes of people caught up in this incident for 10 years..."
Sure, their suspicion would feed on itself far more than mine.
Even now, I can't trust Shirozaki-san with all my heart.
But the fact that he's telling me all this... could also be taken as an SOS from Shirozaki-san.
"Then... why don't you run away, Shirozaki-san...? 　If it's this dangerous here, then right away―"
"You might not understand... Everyone in this settlement was born and raised in this settlement. How much loss comes with giving that up, how much humiliation."
"Though I guess that doesn't really click for you. For example, the trouble over evicting residents when a railroad gets built, territorial disputes between countries, indigenous peoples rejecting civilization, wars of conquest to expand territory. Places, back then and now, are assets that give rise to a lot of conflict and fighting. For rooted residents like us, Arata is a place we have to protect even if we die for it."
"............ Honestly, it doesn't feel real to me..."
"I figured. I know just how you feel, Natsumi-chan. But... even so, we can't forgive them. Anyone who threatens Arata. No matter what happens, I'll protect this settlement to the end."
"I'm on my own now, and the only thing I have left to lose is this place called Arata. Everyone else is a lot older than Chigaya-chan too, and they've probably decided to stick it out here. So I'll protect this place our ancestors handed down to us, even if it costs my life."
There was an attachment to this place that I couldn't understand.
I understand that much, but― do I really need to step into a place like that too?
"So, what'll it be? Head on to the settlement, or get off here?"
If I run away here, at least I might be saved.
But... that would be the same as leaving Haruka and Goto to die.
There's no way I could...
Just then, my phone vibrated.
It seemed there was still a faint signal.
Keeping the knife on Shirozaki-san, I took out my phone.
It was a message from Dad.
“You must be uneasy, but trust your own intuition. You should be in no danger.”
Dad had been listening to this whole conversation too.
I didn't want to think Dad would throw me into a dangerous place.
But I was the one who said I'd come here, even overruling Dad and Mom.
"Shirozaki-san... To the settlement, please..."
"I see... You got it, missy! I'm counting on you!"
The 'nerves of steel' Haruka and Goto had talked about were pointlessly kicking in now.
Honestly, I'm scared and I want to run away, but...
Besides, there were the figures I'd seen earlier who looked like Kotaro-san and Sakura-san.
I have a duty to pass this on.
