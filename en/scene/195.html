Dream Racing to the Past

Meicho Arc [Clear Sign] · Shiyo 800-05-05 23:59 火 · 18 boxes

Play options

Hidden route
―Echoing inside a dream, the sound of a clock.
When I opened my eyes, the time had gone past midnight.
Past midnight meant that today was May 6.
From tomorrow, it was school again.
When I opened my eyes, right in front of me was the TV.
The movie we had been watching had gone to the menu screen.
When had I fallen asleep?
Maybe because I had slept sitting up on the sofa, my body ached a little.
When I looked to my right, Haruka was leaning on my shoulder, sleeping soundly.
Left like this, Haruka would end up sore too.
Besides, late at night in early May, there was the worry of catching a cold as well.
"Haruka, wake up. If you're going to sleep, let's go to your room."
I shook Haruka's shoulder lightly.
"Mmh..."
Had she been sleeping lightly? Haruka lifted her head right away, and slowly.
"Huh? I fell asleep... Damn... it was just about to get to the good scene."
Haruka stood up and gave a long stretch.
"No good... Slacking off like this on the very first day of our new life..."
Scratching her head, Haruka delivered a statement of remorse.
"Right now, there's only the two of us, so..."
"Just us, huh. Yeah, that's right... There's no one to wake us up when we oversleep, like there used to be... and no one to look after us anymore, either..."
Haruka's eyes were still sleepy, but her mouth twisted sadly.
Now there is no one in this home but me and Haruka.
My parents and Haruka's parents are never coming back again...
"Hey, Haruka, remember? That I'm a real witch."
What I had brought up was something I had not been able to forget, however hard I tried, this whole month.
"A witch... that's..."
Haruka said that much, and then fell silent.
―A silence of a few seconds passed between us.
"Natsumi, don't think about that anymore."
For a month now, Haruka had been saying that to me every time.
But, that person died.
I as good as killed that person.
I remember almost nothing of that time.
But it seems like Haruka knows something―that's the feeling I have.
"Natsumi, that was an accident. I know that feeling really well, getting it into your head that an accident was a curse or whatever. But, that was..."
"You're right... But, lately I think about it a lot. If only those two had had a little more courage... If they'd had a little more courage and held themselves back, today would probably have been a completely different May 6. No, but there was nothing they could have done, I'm sure. There was no other way."
"Hey, Natsumi."
"But... but, it's all right just to dream, isn't it? Of what it would be like if there were a life like that."
I turned to Haruka with the biggest smile I possibly could.
"Well then, good night, Haruka!"

I opened the door to my unlit room―
And I let out one sigh.
The smile I had turned on Haruka vanished the moment there was no more light falling into this room.
And in the dark room, alone, I thought.
I know. Regret is not going to change anything.
Never mind regret. The incident had been moving beyond my control.
There was nothing I could have done.
My room was still lined with lots of boxes.
Things I had brought over from my old home.
Those, too, had to be sorted out soon.
If those two had had more courage.
If that person had had the courage to endure.
If Haruka had had more courage to fight back.
If even one of those...
Then how would things have turned out?
I thought back to a month ago, when that incident happened.
The sound of the clock announcing midnight reached me inside a dream.
"Haruka, wake up. If you're going to sleep, want to go to your room?"
I heard Natsumi's voice, and my shoulder was shaken.
"Huh? I fell asleep... Damn... it was almost at the good scene..."
I had been watching the DVD that I had watched with Natsumi a month ago.
Back then, all I could think about was how to help Natsumi, and I couldn't properly watch it.
So I had gotten Natsumi to sit through watching it over again with me.
"No good... Slacking off like this on the very first day of our new life..."
"Well, there's only the two of us, so..."
"Just us, huh. Yeah, that's right... There's no one to wake us up when we oversleep, like there used to be, and no one to look after us anymore, either..."
Mom always used to wake me up.
From now on, Natsumi and I have to do everything ourselves, just the two of us.
"Hey, Haruka, remember? That I'm a real witch."
It was true: Mom had said something like that, in this room.
"Natsumi, don't think about that."
The truth is, there's a memory I have kept asleep at the very bottom of my memory.
When Dad fell from the tree, the line of his fall shifted.
If not for that, he wouldn't have been impaled on the fence, and he wouldn't have died.
Mom must have been calling Natsumi a witch because of that.
But if he had kept falling straight down, Natsumi would have been crushed underneath Dad.
So Dad twisted his body with everything he had, and shielded Natsumi.
Too kind for his own good. So like Dad.
But, I can't tell Natsumi that. If I did, she would probably feel even guiltier about Dad.
I'll keep this in my heart alone.
"Natsumi, that was an accident. I know that feeling really well, getting it into your head that an accident was a curse or whatever. But, that was..."
"You're right..."
But Natsumi kept talking.
"But, lately I think about it a lot. If only those two had had a little more courage... If they'd had a little more courage and held themselves back, today would probably have been a completely different May 6."
"Hey, Natsumi..."
"But... but, it's all right just to dream, isn't it? Of what it would be like if there were a life like that."
Natsumi turned a smile on me.
"Well then, good night, Haruka!"
And Natsumi went back to her room.
If Natsumi's parents had had more courage.
If Mom hadn't become a shinigami.
If I had had the courage to stand up to the bullying.
If even one of those...
Then how would things have turned out?
Long ago, when I became a witch, what happened...?
