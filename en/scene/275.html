Moonlight on the Rafters ~Turn~

Eden no Sakura Arc [Eden's Cherry] · Shiyo 800-04-07 20:30 火 · 49 boxes

The one at Toyotake Station was the first; the one I'm hearing now is the second.
Goto's whatever-sound, the one she talked about, is ringing out eerily here in Arata too.
The trees that had been lush with new leaves were withered.
There's no way these dozens of trees could die in a matter of minutes.
"Haruka, hurry back to the Niimura house! Get everyone somewhere safe!"
"What's that supposed to mean? What are you going to do...?"
"I'll go look somewhere else."
"No, no, come on. Let's go back together."
"Now that it's come to this, we have to find Chigaya-chan as soon as we can. Besides, if Eiichiro-san and Sakura-san really did take Chigaya-chan, I can talk to them."
"That's crazy! Dead bodies are walking around here!? 　Even if the voices sound exactly the same, they've got to be somebody else! 　If you're going, then so am I!"
"It's dangerous!"
"I came here knowing it was dangerous. And you know what happened at Toyotake Station, right? Shigatsu Youka has no intention of letting us get away."
"............ We come back as fast as we can. I have an idea where. Follow me!"
Mom broke into a run through the forest, where you could now see clear through the trees.
I went after Mom, but with all the fallen leaves and dead branches piled up, it was hard to run.
Every so often I saw small animals dashing past as if in a panic.
It seemed the animals had noticed this abnormal situation too, not just us.
Goto has probably heard that sound and checked out the situation too, but what does she make of it?
So far, the only real damage is some dead trees, but...
The place Mom brought me to was the graveyard clearing.
I could faintly hear the stream.
And a single tall stone stood in place of a grave marker.
By rights Chiga-nee was supposed to do the succession rite here tonight.
"Is Chiga-nee here?"
"No. Eiichiro-san and Sakura-san were both buried here. If their bodies turned out not to be here..."
"You're not saying we should dig up the buried bodies..."
"If you don't like it, go back to the house."
Mom was studying the ground around the gravestone closely, as if she really meant to dig.
"O-okay, I get it, I'll help..."
After the mummy, buried bodies, huh...
"But how are we going to dig them up? 　They can't be that shallow, and we don't have any tools."
"We're not digging them up. We're checking the ground around here. For any sign that it's been dug up, or that something has crawled out."
I see. A burial pit would be deep and big, and not something you could fill back in so easily.
A hole somebody came out of can't be completely covered up in just a few days.
"Haruka, you check from the mountain side. I'll go around from the stream side."
"Got it!"
I headed in the exact opposite direction from Mom.
The clearing is 100 meters square, and there's moonlight, so the two of us could finish checking it in 10 minutes or so.
I walked along the edge of the clearing, staring at the ground at my feet.
But most of it was covered in short, dead grass, and nothing looked dug up.
I looked about 10 meters ahead, but the ground was the same everywhere.
If a body had come out, the grass in that spot would be gone.
No. If it were that easy to spot, the search parties in the daytime would have noticed something.
I have to find any spot that feels even slightly off.
But even if we're talking about bodies crawling out, they were buried more than 10 years ago, weren't they?
They should have been bare bones long ago, and yet the recording Chiga-nee made clearly caught voices of flesh and blood.
I'm no Goto, so there's no way I could know how something like that is even possible.
Come to think of it, maybe I should update Goto on the situation.
I have to tell her we found the mummy, too.
Damn, of course―no signal out here.
Wait, two messages from Natsumi had come in.
"Haruka, are you okay? You haven't come back for ages, so I'm worried."
Right, I haven't told Natsumi anything.
And even if I wanted to get in touch, there's nothing I can do with no signal...
I checked the other message.
"I heard from a girl called Kana-chan―you went to a cave with Obasan, right? To look for a mummy"
So Kana had gone back to the house.
I'll meet up with Natsumi, explain the situation, and―
Wait, hold on! How much does Natsumi know!?
We're supposed to be keeping information away from her...!
Then everything we're doing is leaking straight to Shigatsu Youka too!
Instead of straining to hide things any longer, I'll have to get Natsumi to shut her eyes and plug her ears...!
I'll leave the search here to Mom after all and hurry back!
"Mom! Something urgent came up, I'm going back to the house!"
No answer.
Maybe she couldn't hear me over the stream.
I strained my eyes, but there was no sign of anyone.
The gravestone and the dead standing trees were clear in the moonlight, yet I couldn't see Mom anywhere.
She couldn't have pushed herself too hard and collapsed, could she!?
I ran the way Mom had gone, calling her name again and again.
Racing around a clearing 100 meters square was too much, even for me.
I soon slowed to a walk and called for Mom even louder.
Turning from my hips up to my neck, I looked around a full 360 degrees.
"Mom! Mom, where are you! Answer me!"
Is she just out of sight behind something?
Or is the stream drowning out my voice after all?
Or did Mom go down toward the bottom of the cliff?
I ran through every possibility, but no reason that made sense came to mind.
I didn't want to admit it.
I didn't want to admit that Mom had been kidnapped too.
While I'd been walking with my eyes on the ground, had Mom been carried off?
No. Come to think of it, right near here there's...!
This was the cliff Sakura-san is said to have fallen from.
It was possible that Mom had slipped and fallen from here, too.
But which was more likely: that Mom had been carried off, or that for some reason she'd left the clearing and stupidly slipped off the cliff?
Even I know that heading toward the stream right now is a waste of time.
No. Maybe Mom was pushed off the cliff by someone.
From here, it'll take me at least 10 minutes on foot to reach the bottom of the cliff.
Even while I stand here, Mom might be getting farther and farther away.
Or she could be stuck at the bottom of the cliff, unable to move, suffering, and at worst...!
"Mom! Mooom, where are you!!"
The refreshing babble of the stream nearby was really grating at a time like this.
If it weren't for that babbling, my voice would reach Mom so much more easily.
Still, I tightened my stomach and called out for Mom.
No matter how loud I yelled, the babbling got in my way.
The people in the settlement believe it.
That Chiga-nee must have been curse-killed.
That's what they're hoping for, Mom said.
If they find out Mom has disappeared, will everyone be glad again?
That's... that's something I'll never allow...!
I'm bringing Mom home, no matter what!
"Whoa!"
On the way, my foot slipped and I nearly fell.
I managed not to go down, but my feet stopped right there.
To think Mom just vanished...!
I swear I'll find her!
Just as I was about to take another step―
"Mm... Haruka?"
............?
Did someone just call me?
A voice that sounded familiar somehow.
A spaced-out voice, as if it didn't understand how frantic I was.
I'd been searching for her the whole time, and yet I couldn't believe the sight in front of me.
If anything, it seemed like something that couldn't be happening.
As hard as everyone had searched, they hadn't been able to find even a trace of her.
About the only trace you could call one was that audio of her being carried off by Dad and Sakura-san.
As if even that had never happened, Chiga-nee gazed at me curiously.
Those eyes were really creepy.
In place of Mom, who had vanished, Chiga-nee had appeared.
As if she'd come from another dimension, as if laughing at this whole terrible case―
"Ch... Chiga-nee... You're... safe."
"Mm... What's wrong? Why that face... Actually, what are you doing out here?"
"Chiga-nee, where have you been all this time! 　Everyone was worried about you!"
I put a hand on Chiga-nee's shoulder and spoke to her, but something was off about her.
She was covered in blood and swaying badly.
"Haruka... What on earth are you talking about?"
Even in this state, it was as if Chiga-nee didn't understand anything about herself...
Even now, her nose was dripping blood.
For a moment I didn't know what had happened.
The world spun, and then a hard blow to my back.
Then a body pressed down on top of me.
This time my mouth was clamped shut, and a sudden suffocating feeling hit me.
It took me a while to understand the situation.
Chiga-nee had knocked me down, climbed on top of me, and was covering my mouth with her hand.
It was so hard to breathe that I tried to push her hand away, but her grip was so strong it didn't budge at all.
"Mm... Listen, Haruka. Keep on up this mountain path and you'll come to the abandoned hut. Auntie is there, so go straight there―no detours."
Mom is at the abandoned hut?
If I just keep following this path?
Why would Chiga-nee say something like―
"Mm... Haruka, do you understand?"
Chiga-nee brought her face close, pressing me to understand.
Blood dripped heavily from Chiga-nee's face and spattered onto mine.
What's happened to her?
Has she lost it under Shigatsu Youka's influence?
Anyone with these green eyes is apparently driven by a powerful urge to kill under certain conditions.
If it's gone out of control, or if she's being manipulated...!
"――――"
All I could do was nod.
Defying Chiga-nee here would be pointless.
Whether Chiga-nee had lost control of her mind or was perfectly sane, going along with what she wanted was the best move.
Once she'd confirmed my small sign of agreement, Chiga-nee quickly got off me and ran toward the graveyard clearing.
What... What was that? Just now...
―For a while I lay there spread-eagled, gazing at the round moon in the sky.
Less gazing at it, really, than staring straight up without a thought in my head.
What was with Chiga-nee just now? Has she lost her mind?
What happened while she was missing...?
And on top of that, Mom is at the abandoned hut on the mountain path...?
I slowly sat up.
My whole face was covered in Chiga-nee's blood.
I'd like to wash it off, but if I have time for that, I need to be finding Mom.
According to Chiga-nee, if I take this path there's an abandoned hut, and Mom is there.
I don't know whether Chiga-nee is sane or has gone crazy, but at least I know she's alive.
So now what I have to do is make sure Mom is safe!
