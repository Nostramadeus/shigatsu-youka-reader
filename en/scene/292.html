The Trumpeter of the Apocalypse ~Focus~

Eden no Sakura Arc [Eden's Cherry] · Shiyo 800-04-07 22:30 火 · 86 boxes

This case is far more complex and baffling than I expected, and it overturns common sense.
Even someone who would normally be worth trusting has to be approached with doubt.
Yes―right now, I harbor no small doubt about this Erika Niimura.
Not distrust so much as doubt.
For the head who rules this settlement, she knows far too little about this case.
The lore this person passed on to me is certainly valuable information.
But it is merely something handed down across generations, not information specific to these curse-killings or to Shigatsu Youka.
Conversely, her son Kotaro-san seems to have gained a certain amount of knowledge from the books in the storehouse, but...
The relationship between this person and Kotaro-san somehow doesn't add up.
No doubt they're mother and son, but the difference in what they know is far too great.
No, that isn't necessarily the case.
When I compare this mother and son, the gap in what they know about the curse-killings is glaring, just as I reasoned a moment ago.
Then what if I compare the two of them with the people of the settlement?
Suppose, for instance, that it isn't Obaasan's knowledge that is lacking, but Kotaro-san's that stands out far too much.
If so, that changes things.
I can understand Kotaro-san poring over the books in the storehouse to cure Chiga-nee's madness 10 years ago.
That much I can understand, but... What is this feeling that something's off?
Something's wrong.
I can't see it... I, of all people, can't see it...
Which means maybe I'm overthinking this after all...
No, there's something crucial I still don't know.
Or rather, there's something nobody has asked yet.
"Um, Obaasan, there's something I'd like to ask."
"Hm?"
"Chiga-nee's madness ten years ago―how was it cured?"
"If I remember right... they gave falsificaso to Sakura. Do you know about the green-eye curse-killing?"
"Yes. Girls born with green eyes feel murderous intent toward people with precognition..."
"That's right. Kotaro was looking into all sorts of things to cure it, and he came across a drug that cures those infected with Droga."
"But why give it to Sakura-san...?"
"Whether it was safe to give Chigaya falsificaso― After all, those documents were written before I was born. Could we trust something that old? Because of that worry, Sakura apparently used the falsificaso on herself, without telling anyone. Her own constitution―by changing it, she cured Chigaya."
"If I remember right, there was a plan to test in Megasawa City whether falsificaso was harmful, wasn't there?"
"Ah, I heard about that from Mifuyu-san. But in the end the Droga was never used, so I didn't hold Mifuyu-san to account. But it seems Sakura had been listening to Mifuyu-san and me... Sakura heard only the part about being found out by me, and jumped from the cliff."
I had heard about that part.
But that changing Sakura-san's constitution was what cured Chiga-nee's madness―that was news to me.
Huh?
Wait a minute...
That... What does that mean?
It can't be... That person...!
"Obaasan, please tell me!"
I grabbed Obaasan by both shoulders and shook her as I pressed her.
"Why does Chiga-nee still have odd eyes!? 　Once Kana-chan has her first period, they'll try to kill each other, won't they!? Why isn't Chiga-nee being treated!?"
This was what had felt off.
No, maybe I noticed it at the very start, but it had long been buried somewhere far back in my memory.
"That was Kotaro's decision. Kotaro apparently means to give Kana falsificaso once Kana's first period starts, but..."
"But that's so inefficient!? 　I mean, worst case, if Chiga-nee and Kana-chan came face to face between her first period and the dose, they'd kill each other...!"
"That's why Kotaro sent Chigaya to Tokyo. That way, they only see each other a few days a year."
"Were there any side effects from using falsificaso on Sakura-san!?"
"As far as I know, there was nothing of the sort, but..."
The safety of falsificaso must already have been established.
If so, just sending Chiga-nee to Tokyo without curing her is far too roundabout a way to go about it!
Waiting for Kana-chan's first period, going out of the way to send Chiga-nee to Tokyo...
If the point were to use Chiga-nee's precognition right up to the limit, I'd understand, but sending her off to Tokyo defeats the whole purpose.
No... the people of the settlement don't know about Chiga-nee's precognition, do they...
But at least Kotaro-san, who read the books in the storehouse, must have realized.
Or is it because the residents would fear it as 'the witch's power'?
Then the timing is strange too.
The first curse-killing happened a year after Sakura-san died, and by the time Sakura-san died, Chiga-nee already had precognition.
For at least a year after Sakura-san died, there was no reason to shun precognition as 'the witch's power.'
So it's clearly strange that Chiga-nee wasn't given falsificaso during that year.
Even if Chiga-nee hadn't told anyone about her own precognition.
Because Kotaro-san must have known that Chiga-nee had precognition.
Kotaro-san is deliberately leaving Chiga-nee green-eyed.
Even though that is extremely dangerous...!
Which means...
The suspicious one isn't Obaasan.
It's Kotaro-san! That man is hiding something!
"Well, I understand your doubts, Erina-chan. But what Kotaro feared most was whether the residents would get suspicious of Chigaya's eye color."
"What do you mean?"
"Hardly anyone knows about the green-eye curse-killing, but you can't say no one does. It's an old legend, after all. Wouldn't be strange if someone had heard it from their grandparents, would it?"
"But then, wouldn't people have gotten suspicious when Chiga-nee was born...?"
"Nothing like that happened back then. Maybe since it's such an old legend, even those who knew didn't care... But eye color isn't something that just changes. If that drew suspicion, then how would Kana be looked at next?"
"Honestly, I'm not convinced... If there was falsificaso, I think you could have explained that there was a proper treatment..."
"It'd be nice if things went that smoothly... But if it failed..."
"............"
"Here, we've arrived."
Between the withered trees, a cave gaping wide―
According to Obaasan, it is a hole made by the movements of Shigatsu Youka.
Before I press her about Kotaro-san, let me look around in here.
If I hurl my suspicion of Kotaro-san at her openly, she may well cover for him, since they're mother and son.
I want a little more to go on.
"Erina-chan, sorry, but could you shine the light down from up here? 　I'll go down first."
"Oh, yes."
But before I could shine the light, Obaasan had already climbed down the ladder.
Has she simply gotten used to this ladder by now?
After a smooth patter of footsteps, a light blinked on down below.
"Come on down. Slowly, now."
At Obaasan's signal, I set my foot on the ladder too.
The rusted ladder, the sheer wall and the dim bottom stoked my fear.
My footsteps on the ladder were probably spaced about twice as far apart as Obaasan's.
"This is..."
Paulownia boxes lay scattered about.
Along with them, two or so sets of red robes and Noh masks had been carelessly thrown aside.
Had someone ransacked the place?
"This is where the red robes and the Noh masks were kept..."
I see―if that group from before were corpses, they would have needed disguises so no one could tell who they were.
So they stole the robes and masks from here and passed themselves off as residents.
"The corpses that attacked Chiga-nee ransacked this place..."
"Looks like about ten sets were taken."
With that, Obaasan shone her light on the spot right beside it.
"Erina-chan, she's here."
She's here?
If she's here, that means― one look at the person lying collapsed in front of Obaasan told me at once.
"Chiga-nee..."
Chiga-nee, her face and clothes covered in blood, lay fallen in this dim, cold cave.
A wavering, unsteady light shone on Chiga-nee.
Was she only unconscious, or...
Obaasan ran her hands over Chiga-nee's whole body.
"It's all right. She's only fainted. No serious injuries, either. She has a heartbeat. She's no corpse."
"Thank goodness..."
It seemed certain that that horde of corpses had carried Chiga-nee in here.
"Mm..."
Chiga-nee let out a faint sound and stirred.
"Ch-Chiga-nee! Can you hear me!?"
"Goto... chan...?"
She seemed lucid, too.
But she made no move to get up right away.
Even when she tried to get up, she seemed to have no strength.
"Chiga-nee, will you eat this?"
I took dorayaki and other Japanese sweets out of my pocket.
Emergency food I had prepared in case anything happened.
Seeing them, Chiga-nee nodded slowly and sat up with Obaasan's help.
Chiga-nee must have been starving. She wolfed down the dorayaki without a word, tearing open the wrapper of another sweet at the same time.
In a situation this extreme, she must be exhausted, body and mind.
I have to make sure Chiga-nee gets plenty of rest.
"Hah... Thank you, Goto-chan..."
Chiga-nee lifted her head and turned toward me.
Huh?
This... Is it the flickering of the light?
Chiga-nee's eyes...
"Uh, Chiga-nee..."
"Hm?"
"Your eyes..."
"My eyes?"
"Your eyes... they're both blue...?"
"Huh?"
I took a close-up of Chiga-nee's face with my phone and showed it to her as it was.
"Mm... With this... Shigatsu Youka's threats―one of them is gone."
"Threats...?"
"Mm... Goto-chan, we're going to rescue everyone who went to the abandoned hut! 　Shigatsu Youka can't read my five senses anymore!"
"Rescue them...!? But Chiga-nee, you led them there because it was safe..."
"There should be lots of corpses wandering around the abandoned hut! If we don't hurry, everyone will be killed!"
"Then why did you send them to the abandoned hut...?"
"I'll explain while we walk!"
Chiga-nee stood up and took hold of the ladder.
"Hold it, Chigaya! There's something else I need you to do!"
"Mm... Something else...?"
"I've figured out where Abaddon―the main body of Shigatsu Youka―is. I want you to head for Mount Itohime."
"Obaasan, but isn't that a mountain path two hours on foot...? And Chiga-nee's strength..."
"Follow me."
Just like that, Obaasan vanished into the depths of the cave.
"Chiga-nee..."
"Mm... Goto-chan, go on ahead. I'm feeling a little lightheaded, so I'll rest just a bit before I come."
"Then I'll wait..."
"Mm... It's fine, go ahead. Don't worry, I won't go off to the abandoned hut on my own."
At Chiga-nee's urging, it was settled that Obaasan and I would go on ahead for now.
―Footsteps echoed from up ahead.
Obaasan seemed to have gone a good way ahead.
"Hm?"
A space opening out beside the passage.
A few drops of water were trickling down the rock wall there.
The rock wall seemed to have been eroded by water, and even where I stood there were marks of splashed droplets.
It looked as though there had once been a big waterfall here.
Then I heard a grating creak, metal scraping against metal.
For now, I'd better hurry on.
A barred iron door stood open.
Had Obaasan opened it?
What could lie beyond it...
"A dead end...?"
"Looks that way. But this cave was made by the monster that makes the Fierce Sound―the monster they call Shigatsu Youka, or Abaddon. This cave and Shigatsu Youka must surely be connected."
Shigatsu Youka is a fungus, so a hollow like this forms wherever its hyphae have been.
It is a creature of unimaginable size, so its hyphae could be outlandishly large too.
But where in this apparent dead end is there a way through to Shigatsu Youka?
"But Obaasan, once we get to Shigatsu Youka, what are we supposed to do? 　We still have no countermeasure..."
"Do you remember the lore from before? 'The dead come forth from the underworld, and uncanny bewitching flowers rise in rows. Those who have become corpses also become bewitching flowers and come forth from the underworld.' ―That lore has a continuation."
"Only the green-eyed prevail. Those who have become green-eyed wither the bewitching flowers and return the dead to the underworld. The descendants shall without fail don masks and blood-red garb, and resist."
Green eyes...?
Which means―someone with green eyes can subdue Shigatsu Youka...?
So that's why she's sending Chiga-nee to Shigatsu Youka...
But Chiga-nee's eyes, as I saw just now...
―And just then, footsteps echoed.
It seemed Chiga-nee had caught up with us.
"Mm... All right, Grandma. I'll go. Goto-chan, you go back to the house."
"Please wait! Your eye color is..."
"Mm... Grandma, please, bring Kana from the abandoned hut. Kana should still have green eyes. I'll head on from here toward Shigatsu Youka. I have to make sure the way is actually passable."
"Kana is at the abandoned hut, is she?"
"Mm... Haruka and Auntie and Natsumi-chan should be there too. But be careful. The corpses may be keeping watch."
"All right. I can't let Erina-chan do something this dangerous, after all. Well then, I'll be quick about it."
"Um... Obaasan..."
"Hm?"
"Please be careful... Erika-chan..."
"Mm... Erika-chan...?"
"Don't you worry, Erina-chan. Old as I am, I'm more than a match for any man around here!"
With those parting words, Erika-chan was gone.
I don't know how much of a threat the corpses are, but Chiga-nee must have seen it firsthand.
Will Erika-chan really be all right on her own?
"Chiga-nee, the corpses―what are they like?"
"Mm... The ones I met were no different from ordinary people, at the very least. They wear white robes, and their speech and gestures are just like the real person's, but their will belongs entirely to Shigatsu Youka. And above all, they have terrifying physical strength. Far more than someone like me could ever stand up to."
"Ugh... I thought they'd be something like zombies, but it's the exact opposite... Erika-chan―will she be all right on her own...?"
"Honestly... it'll be tough..."
"Then―"
"Mm... Goto-chan... we have no other means left. With the strength I have now, I'd be no use at all. Besides... you've noticed too, haven't you? About Dad."
"............"
"Mm... I hate to say it, but Dad is hiding something."
Chiga-nee, it seemed, had been thinking the same thing.
I had suspected Kotaro-san or Erika-chan, but Erika-chan's knowledge was simply skewed in amount and direction.
But Kotaro-san, while knowing all that, has kept it hidden the whole time.
There's no doubt he's hiding something.
"Mm... I think Dad is...
Oh?"
And Chiga-nee glanced toward the wall.
A large stone had been placed against the wall.
Beside it, a hole just a little smaller than the stone.
It looked as if the stone had been blocking the hole until recently.
There seemed to be something inside the hole.
"Mm... There's something in there. I'll pull it out."
"Be careful, okay?"
Chiga-nee thrust her hand into the hole and dragged out whatever was in there.
"Ugh..."
With a small cry, I covered my mouth with my sleeve without thinking.
A mummy, a Noh mask over its face, dressed in tattered red robes.
Could this be...
"Mm... So this is... Verde's mummy..."
Chiga-nee kneels and puts her hands together.
"Mm... She is one of the settlement's ancestors, after all..."
I follow suit and put my hands together.
This was Tomi, the 14-year-old girl who, driven by an unbearable murderous intent because of her green eyes, took her own life.
I can't even imagine what she felt as she took her own life.
But Chiga-nee is one of the rare few who have felt that murderous intent too.
Chiga-nee must understand only too well what Tomi felt.
This Tomi, too, must still be waiting for the moment she is freed from Shigatsu Youka's rule.
But we can't afford to linger like this for long.
"Chiga-nee, let's go. There must be a passage leading to Mount Itohime further in, don't you think? 　And... what on earth is Kotaro-san hiding...?"
I could feel a faint breeze from the hole the mummy had been in.
The hole seemed to be deeper than it looked.
"Chiga-nee?"
Chiga-nee stayed perfectly still, hands together, eyes closed.
Isn't that a bit long for a prayer at a time like this?
"Goto-chan... Dad―I'll tell you about him later. Right now, we have to hurry. Come with me."
Me, come along too...?
Just before, she had been telling me to "go back to the house"...
Is Chiga-nee uneasy too, with her strength worn down?
Or did Chiga-nee decide I'd be safer with her than alone in the settlement?
Oh well. I've come this far.
Niimura-senpai, Kogori-senpai, I'm going on ahead.
