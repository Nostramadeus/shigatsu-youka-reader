Slumber ~A Spring Phantom~

Eden no Sakura Arc [Eden's Cherry] · Shiyo 800-04-08 10:40 火 · 68 boxes

―Uncle's thirteenth-year memorial service ended without incident.
Everyone had spent the whole night dealing with Shigatsu Youka, so some people were dozing off, but no one blamed them for it.
Will Arata's own custom of wearing Noh masks and red robes come to an end now, too?
It had already been hollowed out to a mere form, but the Noh masks and red robes helped us.
It is the precious wisdom of our ancestors.
Even if it is hollowed out further still, I want to keep at least its form.
"Aah, I'm done! I'm going to sleep!"
"Mm... Good work, everyone."
On my back, Kana was breathing softly in her sleep.
Because everyone truly did their best, we were able to bring the curse-killing case to an end.
The Motoki-cho group, I heard, would stay tonight as originally planned and go home tomorrow.
Everyone will probably fall asleep now, but I wonder about their body clocks.
Waking at night and not being able to sleep will make going home tomorrow hard.
"Kana..."
I laid Kana down on the futon and gently stroked her face.
Painful-looking wounds remained all over her body, but these, too, would soon heal.
The herbal medicine Grandma made should work well.
What had Kana thought, meeting Mom?
She may have gotten to see Mom, but it was a painful sight, a body rotting and crumbling away.
I was glad I got to exchange words with Mom once more at the end.
But it was painful.
Mom... all this time, it must have been so painful for you.
For so long, so very long, controlled by Shigatsu Youka―
But... I had realized.
The curse-killing case had not yet reached its true solution.
There was still something that had to be pursued.
"Mm... Yes?"
"Excuse me, coming in."
The one who came in was Goto-chan.
Her eyes looked sleepy, but the light in them was strangely sharp.
She turned those eyes toward Kana.
"Kana-chan really did her best to save us, didn't she. She pushed her injured leg to pull Kogori-senpai out, and when Chiga-nee was strangling me, she honestly tried to help."
Is she saying this to blame me?
It was only an act, but I put Goto-chan through excessive fear and pain.
"Mm... I'm sorry, Goto-chan..."
"Huh?
　Oh, I'm sorry, I didn't mean it that way. I know you had no choice."
Goto-chan said this while gently holding me by both shoulders.
Even so, that doesn't change the fact that I made Goto-chan suffer.
"Mm... Still, back then it was only all right because Dad injected me with falsificaso. If he hadn't..."
"If you start on that, there's no end to it. Whatever the case, if any one thing had gone wrong..."
"Mm... You're right. But still, what I did to you, Goto-chan..."
"Then next time, treat me at that ryotei in Tokyo!"
"Whaat!!? 　That's... a bit..."
"Ahahaha! Just kidding! If I did that, Chiga-nee would be up to her neck in loan-shark debt!"
"Mm... Just how much do you plan to eat..."
"Eri-nee...?"
Kana, who had been asleep, seemed to have reacted to Goto-chan's voice.
"Ah, sorry, Kana-chan, I got carried away..."
"Ehe."
"Hm?"
"I've got... two big sisters..."
At that, Goto-chan and I looked at each other.
"Then I guess Chiga-nee and I are sisters too?"
"Mm... Hee hee, I suppose so."
And we laughed together.
It was a calm, warm atmosphere that made us forget even our sleepiness.
Still, why had Goto-chan come to this room?
I still hadn't heard what she came to say.
Goto-chan stroked Kana's head, trying to get her back to sleep.
She kept gazing at Kana with gentle eyes, and Kana, too, smiled peacefully, as if looking at her mother.
The sharp light from before was nowhere in Goto-chan's eyes now.
This means... Goto-chan is thinking the same thing I am.
Something we can't discuss while Kana is awake.
Something hidden underneath, that we still haven't finished pursuing.
―Before long, Kana fell asleep again and began breathing softly and peacefully.
"............"
"Mm... Goto-chan, you came here because there's something you want to tell me, didn't you?"
"Come with me a moment. There's somewhere we need to go."
That light came back into Goto-chan's eyes.
"Mm... All right."
"Mm... What is... this..."
"You may not have known, Chiga-nee, but this storehouse burned yesterday."
The second floor was heavily blackened with soot, clearly showing there had been a fire.
"Only the second floor burned in this fire."
"Mm... The second floor...?"
"Yes. If a fire breaks out in a storehouse with no source of flame, it's a short circuit or arson. But there was nothing wrong with the wiring, and the only things that burned were the books on the table."
"Mm... Was it Uncle, or Mom, I wonder..."
"No. The books in the storehouse were what it needed to lead us to the summit. Kogori-senpai and one other person were meant to become divine messengers, part of Shigatsu Youka."
"Mm... Then someone who noticed Shigatsu Youka's ambition burned them?"
"That's not it either. We already knew about Shigatsu Youka before the fire, so it was too late for that. Quite simply, someone didn't want us reading any more of those books."
"Mm... A person...?"
"Yes. The arsonist is someone from the settlement."
"............"
I see. So that's how it is.
Then it ties in with what I want to pursue, too.
"Mm... Goto-chan, there's something I want you to see too. Will you come with me?"
"Yes."
―The place we came to was underneath the secret base, in front of the iron-barred door.
"I see..."
Just by standing before the door, Goto-chan seemed to have sensed something.
"This place was off-limits to begin with. But there are strangely few old cobwebs on this iron-barred door. Which means someone went in and out often."
"............"
―!!
It was unlocked.
We came back from the mountain through here this morning, and I had definitely locked it.
That meant someone was up ahead, right now.
"Mm... So you were here after all."
The one there was―
"Chigaya..."
Holding daphne in his hand was Dad.
"Kotaro-san, would you tell us? 　What happened here?"
"Mm... There's still so much I don't understand. Dad getting treated as the culprit so often, and every now and then having precognition like Mom's."
Dad crouched down and set the flowers before the hole that led to the summit.
Then he put his hands together and offered a silent prayer.
"Mm... When Uncle dragged me here, I was injected with falsificaso, and then Droga. Dad injected me with falsificaso again and cured me, but how did Dad know I was here?"
"............"
"Mm... You knew Uncle and the others would come here eventually, didn't you? 　You said you were going around the settlement to thank people for helping put out the fire, and all the while you were hiding here. What does it mean?"
"............"
"Kotaro-san, if you had spoken up sooner, wouldn't the curse-killing case have been solved years ago?"
"............"
"Mm... Dad... Say something..."
"............"
Kotaro-san opened his eyes and slowly stood up.
"I'm proud that everyone in the settlement relies on me. But... open the lid and this is what's inside. I still couldn't accept that Sakura was dead, and like a weakling I let Sakura's phantom lead me around."
"So that's how it was. Kotaro-san, you... were always meeting... the dead Sakura-san here, weren't you?"
"............ Yes..."
―Ten years ago, to treat Chigaya's Green-Eye Curse-Killing, I read every book in the storehouse.
So I had known about Shigatsu Youka, and about the Holy Cerejeira Church, for 10 years.
"Sakura..."
A few days after Sakura died.
I laid the daphne Sakura had loved on the riverbank where her body was found.
At that moment―
"Wh-what...!?"
That 'Fierce Sound' rang out―
"Kotaro..."
The moment I heard that voice, the Fierce Sound and the sound of the stream both faded from my ears.
"Sakura...!? How..."
"I came back through Shigatsu Youka's power."
"It can't be...!"
"But you know, I came back, but I'm not alive again. I'm dead, but Shigatsu Youka regenerated my body."
"Even so, can't you live with everyone again!?"
"I can't. I can only meet very few people, in very few places. Really, I shouldn't even be meeting you here."
"Then where can we...!"
"You know the place we made our secret base when we were children? 　At the very back of it."
"G-got it...!"
"There was a place like this here..."
"It seems it was made off-limits long ago. There's a path here, you see, that leads to the Goddess."
"The Goddess... You mean Shigatsu Youka?"
"Yes. So this is an important place. And a place no one will notice."
From then on I met Sakura here regularly.
Without a word even to Mom, I alone kept meeting Sakura in secret.
And then, two years after I met Sakura again, in Shiyo 792― it was right after the second curse-killing case.
"Curse-killed...?"
"Yeah. Again this year, someone was killed. Nobody says it openly, but it seems everyone thinks I'm bitter about Sakura's death and taking revenge on the settlement. Even though I absolutely didn't do it..."
"It really wasn't you who did it, Kotaro?"
"Of course it wasn't!? Me, do something like that...!"
"............"
"Sakura?"
"Then I'll help, so that you won't be suspected, Kotaro. Next year, have everyone keep watch on you, at your own request. If a curse-killing happens during that time, it will be plain that you're innocent."
"What do you mean? 　Don't tell me the curse-killing case... it was you...!"
"Kotaro, you know how Shigatsu Youka lives, don't you? 　Shigatsu Youka―the Goddess is afraid of humans. Afraid they'll lay waste to the Goddess's land again, like that church did."
"Even when she killed the old people and made it look like suicide, the people of the settlement kept stubbornly guarding this place. So this time, she decided to kill the people outside the Niimura family, little by little. If you don't like it, I'd be grateful if you'd tell everyone to leave here soon..."
"I see. Killing alone would only stir up distrust among the residents. Then the police―outsiders could come at any time. So that was what the old people's suicides were about. She wanted to drive the humans out of a settlement where the old killed themselves, but that didn't work either."
"Well? Could you tell everyone to leave?"
"Handing over your own land with nothing in return isn't something people can easily do. Even less so for residents rooted in the land the way Arata's are... After all, they didn't run even after being made to witness the old people's suicides."
"You're right... I understand that."
"Besides, if I left here too, I wouldn't be able to see you anymore!"
"That's true... But... every year from now on, it will be painful, you know? 　One resident will die each year."
"It's better than losing you..."
"I see... Also, you remember that promise, don't you?"
"You mean leaving Chigaya's green eye as it is?"
"Yes. You may cure Kana's. But Chigaya's―"
"So you still won't tell me the reason?"
"I'm sorry..."
I was weak.
Unable to bear the fear that Sakura would truly vanish, I kept silent, knowing the curse-killings would happen and knowing the culprit.
I knew that leaving Chigaya's green eye untreated was dangerous, too.
As for the storehouse books, there was no telling when someone might catch on, so I thought it best to get rid of the clues.
But throwing them away would have looked suspicious, so eight years ago, on the pretext of keeping Chigaya's Green-Eye Curse-Killing from the residents, I put an electronic lock on the archive room.
But...
But yesterday, after I set fire to the storehouse, I finally realized.
No, I should say I woke up.
Already, a number of corpses were walking through Arata.
What I had been trying to hide, Shigatsu Youka no longer even meant to hide.
In other words, Sakura meeting me had not been Sakura's will, but Shigatsu Youka's.
Even though I had known... Even though, somewhere in my heart, I had known it was Shigatsu Youka's doing.
That was why I hid here last night.
They were bound to come through here, taking everyone to Shigatsu Youka.
Hoping I could somehow cure Natsumi-chan, who was infected with Droga, I prepared the falsificaso meant for the memorial service.
In the end I could only use it on Chigaya, but even so it was enough to have Shigatsu Youka fooled.
It was the least I could do to strike back.
Shigatsu Youka had been deceiving me all along.
